## Pehli Baat: Punjab Arms License Kya Hai Aur Naya Digital System Kya Badal Gaya Hai

**Punjab arms license** aik official ijazatnama hai jis ke baghair Punjab me hathyar rakhna qanoon ke khilaf hai. Pehle ye kaam kagaz par hota tha — form DC office se lena, file jama karwana, daftaron ke chakkar lagana — aur is me mahine lag jate the. Ab ye tareeqa badal gaya hai. **April 2026 se Punjab ka arms license system mukammal tor par digital ho gaya hai**: apply aap official portal **pal.nadra.gov.pk** par online karte hain aur biometric verification aap apne mobile par **Pak ID app** se kar lete hain. Punjab Home Department ne November 2025 me notification jari karke manual aur purane tareeqe se license ka kaam rok diya tha, is liye ab naya license, renewal aur duplicate — sab kuch isi online system se hota hai.

Is guide me aap ko naye digital tareeqe se apply karne ka step-by-step tareeqa, eligibility, zaroori kagzaat, fees aur renewal ka mukammal tareeqa milega. Aik baat pehle se saaf kar dein: arms license sirf **non-prohibited bore (NPB)** hathyar ke liye milta hai, aur sirf us shakhs ko milta hai jo qanoon ki shartein poori karta ho. License milna aap ka haq nahi, ye licensing authority ki manzoori par hai — aur manzoori se pehle police verification lazmi hoti hai.

Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jo Punjab ke rehne wale hain aur apni hifazat ya sports shooting ke liye qanooni license lena chahte hain. Agar aap ka taluq Punjab se nahi hai to ye guide aap par laagu nahi hogi — har sube ka apna alag system hai.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Eligibility Ki Shartein

Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke mutabiq naya personal arms license sirf us shakhs ko mil sakta hai jo ye shartein poori kare:

- **Pakistani shehri** hon aur aap ke paas **valid CNIC** ho. CNIC ki muddat khatam ho to pehle usay renew karwayein — [CNIC banane aur renew karne ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) is guide me dekhein.
- **Punjab ka domicile** ho. Domicile aap ke Punjab se taluq ka saboot hai — agar domicile nahi bana to [domicile certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh lein.
- **Umar 20 saal se zyada** ho. Punjab Arms Rules 2023 me ye had 20 saal rakhi gayi hai. Purane forms aur checklists par kabhi 25 saal bhi likha milta hai — woh purani policy thi, maujooda qanoon ke mutabiq 20 saal hai. Phir bhi apply karte waqt portal par maujooda shart zaroor check kar lein.
- **Active taxpayer (filer)** hon. Filer hone ka matlab hai ke aap ka naam FBR ki Active Taxpayer List (ATL) me ho. Filer nahi hain to pehle [FBR filer banne ka tareeqa](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) parhein aur apni ATL status [yahan se check karein](/guides/fbr-atl-status-check-ka-tareeqa).
- **Koi criminal record na ho.** Police verification me aap ka record check hota hai — kisi bhi qisam ka jarm ka record application reject hone ki bari wajah banta hai.
- **License ki wajah saaf ho.** Punjab Home Department ke mutabiq hathyar rakhne ki wajah genuine honi chahiye — jaise jaan ko sabit khatra hone par hifazat, sport shooting ya fasal ki hifazat. Sirf "hifazat ke liye chahiye" kehna aksar kafi nahi hota — khatre ya zaroorat ka saboot dena parta hai.

Aik aur aham baat: license **sirf NPB (non-prohibited bore)** hathyar ke liye jari hota hai. Prohibited bore hathyar aam shehri ko nahi milta. Aur license kisi aik shakhs ke naam par hota hai — ye kisi doosre ko becha, tohfe me diya ya transfer nahi kiya ja sakta.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kya Kya Tayyar Rakhein

Apply karne se pehle ye kagzaat tayyar rakh lein. Koi kagaz kam ho to application wahin ruk jati hai, is liye pehle se checklist mukammal kar lein:

- **CNIC ki copy** — valid aur saaf copy. Original bhi verification ke waqt dikhana par sakta hai.
- **Do (2) haal ki passport-size tasveerein** — aam tor par neele background wali tasveerein mangi jati hain.
- **Punjab domicile ki copy** — aap ke Punjab se taluq ka saboot.
- **Medical fitness certificate** — kisi registered medical practitioner se jari shuda certificate ke aap zehni aur jismani tor par hathyar rakhne ke qabil hain.
- **Character certificate** — police se jari shuda kirdaar ka certificate. Is ke bare me tafseel [police character certificate ki guide](/guides/police-character-certificate) me parhein.
- **NTN certificate ki copy** — agar aap income tax dete hain to apna National Tax Number zaroor lagayein. Filer hona eligibility ki shart hai, is liye ye kagaz ahem hai.
- **Peshawar ya karobar ka saboot** — agar aap karobari hain to karobar ka saboot, aur agar mulazim hain to mulazimat ya department ka certificate.
- **Police ya department ki sifarish** — purani checklist ke mutabiq police ya mutaliqa department ki recommendation bhi mangi jati thi. Portal par maujooda form me jo sifarish darkaar ho, woh wahin likhi hoti hai.

![CNIC, domicile certificate, passport-size tasveerein aur application form — Punjab arms license ke liye zaroori kagzaat](/images/punjab-arms-license-banane-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

Sab kagzaat ki **saaf scan copies (JPG ya PNG)** bana lein, kyun ke online form me yehi upload honge. Tasveer dhundli ho ya CNIC number parh na raha ho to application wapas bhej di jati hai. Aik mashwara: form bharne se pehle har kagaz ka naam aur number apne paas likh lein taa ke form bharte waqt ghalti na ho.

## Fees: Kitne Paise Lagenge

Fees Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke Schedule One ke mutabiq muqarrar hoti hain. Punjab cabinet ne July 2023 me fees me izafa manzoor kiya tha, aur November 2023 me jari hone wale gazette me nayi fee structure darj hai. Aam shakhs ke liye ahem fees ye hain:

| Kaam | Fee |
|---|---|
| **Naya personal arms license (NPB)** | **Rs 50,000** |
| **License renewal** | **Rs 5,000** |
| **NADRA processing fee** | **Rs 2,000** |
| **Online registration fee** | **Rs 1,200** (2023 me approve hui thi) |
| **Duplicate license** | **Rs 5,000** |

Kuch ahem wazahatein:

- **Rs 50,000 naye personal license ki fee hai** — ye pehle Rs 10,000–20,000 thi, 2023 me barha kar Rs 50,000 kar di gayi. Ye raqam Dawn, The Express Tribune aur doosri khabron me Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke hawale se aik jaisi chhapi hai.
- **Renewal ki fee Rs 5,000 hai**, jo pehle Rs 1,000 thi.
- **NADRA processing fee Rs 2,000** alag se li jati hai — ye card aur data processing ki fee hai.
- **All Pakistan license ki fee par mukhtalif khabrein hain** — kuch me Rs 100,000 aur kuch me Rs 15,000 likha hai — is liye is ki raqam yahan nahi likh rahe. Agar aap ko All Pakistan license chahiye to aakhri raqam official portal par hi confirm karein.
- **Aakhri aur final raqam apply karte waqt official portal pal.nadra.gov.pk par hi confirm hoti hai.** Fees waqt ke sath badal sakti hain, is liye portal par dikhai gayi raqam hi hatmi hai. Kisi agent ya shakhs ko fee ke naam par naqad raqam na dein — payment sirf portal ke official tareeqe se karein.

## Online Apply Karne Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Naya digital system April 2026 se chal raha hai. Is ke steps ye hain:

**Step 1 — Pak ID app tayyar karein:** Apne mobile par NADRA ki official **Pak ID app** install kar lein aur apna account bana lein. Isi app se aap ki biometric verification hogi, is liye app pehle se tayyar rakhna behtar hai.

**Step 2 — Portal par jayein:** Apne computer ya mobile ke browser me official portal **pal.nadra.gov.pk** kholein. Yehi Punjab arms license ki official website hai — kisi aur website ya agent ke link se apply na karein.

**Step 3 — Account banayein:** Portal par apna CNIC number, mobile number aur email se account banayein. Mobile par aane wala OTP darj karke account verify karein.

**Step 4 — Naye license ka form bharein:** "New License" ya "Apply for Arms License" ka option chunein. Form me apni zaati maloomat (naam, walid ka naam, pata, CNIC number), domicile ki tafseel, pesha aur license ki wajah (hifazat ya sports) darj karein.

**Step 5 — Kagzaat upload karein:** Upar wale section me bataye gaye tamam kagzaat — CNIC, tasveerein, domicile, medical fitness certificate, character certificate, NTN — saaf scan kar ke upload karein.

**Step 6 — Fee jama karwayein:** Portal aap ko fee ki tafseel aur payment ka tareeqa batayega. Fee portal ke official payment channel se jama karwayein aur raseed sambhal kar rakhein.

**Step 7 — Pak ID app se biometric verification:** Application jama karne ke baad aap ko Pak ID app par biometric verification karni hogi — apne phone par ungliyon ke nishan (fingerprint) scan karke apni pehchan ki tasdeeq karein. Yehi woh marhala hai jo pehle NADRA office ja kar hota tha, ab ghar baithe ho jata hai.

**Step 8 — Police verification ka intezar karein:** Aap ki darkhwast mutaliqa police aur Home Department ko bheji jati hai. Police aap ke record, pate aur wajah ki verification karti hai. Is marhale me police aap se rabta bhi kar sakti hai.

**Step 9 — Manzoori aur smart card:** Verification aur Home Department ki manzoori ke baad aap ka license **secure smart card** ki shakal me jari hota hai. License ki status aap portal par apne tracking number ya CNIC se check kar sakte hain.

![Pak ID app par fingerprint biometric verification — Punjab arms license ki online pehchan ki tasdeeq](/images/punjab-arms-license-banane-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Agar portal kisi marhale par koi kagaz dobara mange ya koi maloomat ghalat ho to portal par notification aata hai — apna mobile number aur email theek rakhein taa ke koi message miss na ho.

## Renewal, Duplicate Aur Transfer Ka Tareeqa

**Renewal:** Punjab Home Department ke mutabiq arms license mustaqil nahi hota — ye aam tor par **3 se 5 saal** ke liye valid hota hai. Muddat khatam hone se **pehle hi renewal** ke liye apply kar dein. Muddat khatam shuda license par hathyar rakhna Pakistani qanoon ke tehat aik sangeen jurm hai. Renewal bhi usi portal pal.nadra.gov.pk se hoti hai — apne maujooda license ki copy, CNIC aur tajdeed shuda medical ya character certificate (agar manga jaye) ke sath apply karein aur Rs 5,000 renewal fee jama karwayein.

**Duplicate license:** Agar license gum ho jaye ya kharab ho jaye to duplicate license ke liye apply kiya ja sakta hai. Is ke liye bhi portal par darkhwast dein — gumshudgi ki soorat me police report bhi darkaar ho sakti hai. Duplicate ki fee bhi Rs 5,000 batayi jati hai, hatmi raqam portal par confirm karein.

**Transfer (intiqaal):** License **non-transferable** hai — aap apna license ya hathyar kisi doosre shakhs ko bech ya tohfe me nahi de sakte. Agar hathyar bechna ho to ye kaam sirf licensing authority ke zariye hota hai, jo khareedne wale ke naam par naya license jari karti hai. Kisi bhi qisam ki private sale qanoon ke khilaf hai.

## License Milne Me Kitna Waqt Lagta Hai

Is ka koi aik hatmi waqt official tor par nahi bataya gaya, kyun ke waqt is baat par hai ke aap ke kagzaat kitne mukammal hain aur police verification kitni jaldi hoti hai. Aam tor par ye marhale hote hain:

1. **Online form aur biometric** — aik hi din me mukammal ho jate hain, agar tamam kagzaat tayyar hon.
2. **Police verification** — ye sab se lamba marhala hota hai. Police aap ke pata, record aur wajah ki jaanch karti hai, is me hafton lag sakte hain.
3. **Home Department ki manzoori** — verification ke baad file Home Department ko jati hai, jahan hatmi faisla hota hai.
4. **Smart card ki jari** — manzoori ke baad license smart card ki shakal me jari hota hai.

Kul mila kar **chand hafton se chand mahine** lag sakte hain. Agar aap ke kagzaat mukammal hon aur record saaf ho to kaam jaldi hota hai. Apni darkhwast ki status portal par waqtan fawaqtan check karte rahein. Koi agent "jaldi license" ka wada karke paise mange to hargiz na dein — is system me sifarish ka koi rasta nahi hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Application Reject Karwa Deti Hain

- **Ghalat ya purana tareeqa apnana:** November 2025 ke baad manual tareeqa band hai. DC office ja kar kagaz ki file jama karwana ab kaam nahi karta — sirf pal.nadra.gov.pk se apply karein.
- **CNIC ya domicile ki ghalti:** Muddat khatam CNIC ya ghalat pata application reject karwa deta hai. Pehle CNIC aur domicile durust karwayein.
- **Filer na hona:** ATL me naam na hona eligibility ki khilafi hai. Apply karne se pehle filer ban jayein aur ATL status check kar lein.
- **Wajah ka saboot na dena:** Sirf "hifazat ke liye" likhna kafi nahi — Home Department ke mutabiq khatre ka saboot ya sports ki wajah wazeh honi chahiye.
- **Dhundli ya ghalat scan copies:** Tasveer ya CNIC ki scan saaf na ho to application wapas bhej di jati hai. Har kagaz ki saaf copy upload karein.
- **Purani age wali maloomat par bharosa:** Purane checklists par 25 saal aur sirf khaas peshon ki shart likhi thi. Maujooda Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke mutabiq 20 saal aur filer hona shart hai — hamesha portal ki maujooda shartein dekhein.
- **Muddat khatam license par hathyar rakhna:** License ki muddat guzar jaye to foran renewal karwayein. Expired license par hathyar rakhna sangeen jurm hai.
- **Public me hathyar le jana:** License ka matlab ye nahi ke aap hathyar har jagah le ja sakte hain. Public jagah, ijtima ya taleemi idare me hathyar le jana sakhti se mana hai — hathyar sirf apne ghar/karobar ya shooting range jate waqt le jaya ja sakta hai.
- **Hathyar aur goliyan aik jagah rakhna:** Home Department ke mutabiq hathyar aur ammunition alag alag, locked steel almari ya safe me rakhne hain. Chabiyan apne paas rakhein — khaas tor par bachon ki pohanch se door.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab arms license ke liye online apply kahan se karein?**
Official portal pal.nadra.gov.pk se apply karein. Account banayein, form bharein, kagzaat upload karein aur Pak ID app se biometric verification karein. Manual tareeqa November 2025 se band hai, is liye sirf isi portal se apply karein.

**Punjab arms license ki fee kitni hai?**
Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke mutabiq naye personal (NPB) license ki fee Rs 50,000 hai, renewal Rs 5,000 hai aur NADRA processing fee Rs 2,000 hai. Aakhri aur final raqam apply karte waqt official portal par confirm hoti hai, kyun ke fees waqt ke sath badal sakti hain.

**Punjab arms license ke liye umar ki had kya hai?**
Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke mutabiq darkhwast guzar ki umar 20 saal se zyada honi chahiye, aur us ke paas valid CNIC aur Punjab ka domicile hona lazmi hai. Purane checklists par 25 saal likha milta hai, lekin maujooda qanoon 20 saal kehta hai.

**Kya arms license ke liye filer hona lazmi hai?**
Ji haan. Punjab Arms Rules 2023 ke mutabiq darkhwast guzar ka FBR ki Active Taxpayer List (ATL) me hona lazmi hai. Filer nahi hain to pehle filer banein, phir apply karein — warna application reject ho sakti hai.

**Punjab arms license kitne arse ke liye valid hota hai?**
License aam tor par 3 se 5 saal ke liye valid hota hai. Muddat khatam hone se pehle renewal lazmi hai — muddat khatam license par hathyar rakhna Pakistani qanoon ke tehat sangeen jurm hai. Renewal bhi pal.nadra.gov.pk portal se hoti hai.
