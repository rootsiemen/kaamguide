## Birth Certificate Kyun Pehla Document Hai

Bache ki pedaish ke baad sab se pehla sarkari document **birth certificate (paidaish certificate)** hai — ye **Union Council** jari karti hai. Is ke baghair:

- **B-Form (CRC)** nahi ban sakta — NADRA UC ka birth certificate mangta hai
- **School admission** me pareshani hoti hai
- **Passport** aur mustaqbil me **CNIC** ke liye record adhoora rehta hai

NADRA ke naye Birth and Death Registration System ke tehat ab **Union Council ka indraj sab se pehla aur lazmi qadam** hai — seedha NADRA jaane se kaam nahi hoga. Achhi khabar: process sasta aur aam tor par tez hai.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fees Union Councils ke aam practice se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. Fee UC aur soobe ke hisab se Rs 100-500 tak farq kar sakti hai — apni Union Council se exact fee zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Fee Aur Waqt (Aam Practice)

| Service | Fee (taqreeban) | Waqt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Birth certificate — Normal | **Rs 100 - Rs 300** | 1-7 din |
| Birth certificate — Urgent | **Rs 500 - Rs 700** | 1-2 din |

**Ahem:** Fee har Union Council me thodi alag hoti hai — kuch me Rs 100, kuch me Rs 300. Apni UC office se pehle confirm kar lein. Raseed lazmi lein — baghair raseed ke certificate par aitbaar na karein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

- Bache ki pedaish ka **hospital birth slip** (hospital se milti hai — isi par date aur waqt likha hota hai)
- Walid ka **asal CNIC** (aur copy)
- Walida ka **asal CNIC** (aur copy)
- **Nikah-nama** ki copy (kuch UCs mangte hain — tasdeeq ke liye)
- Taza **passport-size tasveer** (bache ki — agar UC mange)

**Sab se ahem cheez:** Bache ka **naam aur tareekh-e-pedaish** pehle se tay kar lein. Yehi naam aur date **B-Form, school, passport, CNIC** — har jagah jayegi. Ghalti baad me theek karwana lamba kaam hai.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa

### Step 1: Hospital Se Birth Slip Lein

Bache ki pedaish hospital me hui hai to **discharge ke waqt birth slip** zaroor lein — is par bache ki date, waqt aur walidain ke naam hote hain. Agar ghar me pedaish hui hai to **dai (midwife) ki tasdeeq** ya mohallay ke kisi zimmedar ki gawahi kaam aa sakti hai (UC se confirm kar lein).

### Step 2: Apni Union Council Jayen

Bache ki pedaish jis ilaaqe me hui hai, usi ilaaqe ki **Union Council** me indraj hota hai:

1. UC office se **birth registration form** lein.
2. Form bharein: bache ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish, ja-e-pedaish, walidain ke naam aur CNIC numbers.
3. Kagzaat (hospital slip + dono walidain ke CNIC) attach karein.
4. **Fee ada karein** aur raseed lein.

### Step 3: Computerized Certificate Hasil Karein

- Normal case me **1-7 din** me computerized birth certificate tayyar ho jata hai.
- UC ke secretary se **stamp aur dastakhat** wala certificate lein — baghair stamp ke certificate bekar hai.
- Certificate par **naam ki spelling, date aur walidain ke naam** ghor se check karein — ghalti ho to foran wahi theek karwayen.

### Online Apply (Jahan Mojood Hai)

Punjab me **e-Khidmat Markaz** aur **Dastak app** se birth certificate ki khidmat bhi milti hai — facilitator ghar se documents le jata hai. Apne shehar me availability e-Khidmat portal ya Dastak app se check kar lein.

## Der Se Indraj (Late Registration)

Agar bache ki umar **saal** guzar gayi aur abhi tak indraj nahi hua:

1. Phir bhi UC me indraj ho sakta hai — **late fee** lag sakti hai.
2. Hospital slip purani ho to bhi chalti hai; na ho to **affidavit (halaf-nama)** dena parega.
3. Bari umar ke bachon ke liye UC **school ka record** ya mohallay ki tasdeeq mang sakta hai.

Der na karein — jitna waqt guzarta hai, process utna hi mushkil hota jata hai.

## Birth Certificate Me Correction

Agar certificate banne ke baad ghalti nazar aye (naam ki spelling, date):

1. **Foran wapas UC jayen** — hospital slip le kar.
2. Correction ke liye application dein — choti ghalti foran theek ho jati hai.
3. Agar certificate purana hai to **affidavit** mangi ja sakti hai.
4. **NADRA record me bhi update** karwana parega agar B-Form ban chuka hai — pehle UC, phir NADRA (B-Form correction).

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Naam baad me sochna:** Form bharte waqt naam tay na ho to ghalat ya aarzi naam likh diya jata hai — pehle ghar me faisla kar lein.
2. **Date ka farq:** Hospital slip aur form par date aik honi chahiye.
3. **Raseed na lena:** Fee ki raseed na ho to baad me saboot nahi rehta.
4. **Der karna:** B-Form aur school ke waqt bhag-daud se bachne ke liye pehle mahine me karwa lein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya birth certificate online ban sakta hai?**
Punjab me e-Khidmat/Dastak se khidmat milti hai kuch ilaaqon me — lekin bunyadi indraj UC me hi hota hai.

**Ghar me pedaish hui, hospital slip nahi — kya hoga?**
UC office se rabta karein — dai ki tasdeeq ya affidavit se indraj ho sakta hai.

**Kya birth certificate ke liye dono walidain ka jana zaroori hai?**
Nahi — aik walid CNIC ke saath ja kar bhi indraj karwa sakta hai.

**Certificate gum ho gaya to?**
UC se **duplicate** nikalwa lein — purana record un ke register me hota hai, mamooli fee lagti hai.

**Kya birth certificate se B-Form ban jayega?**
Ji haan — B-Form ke liye UC ka birth certificate hi bunyadi document hai.

## Birth Certificate Aur B-Form Ka Rishta (Poori Tasveer)

Bohat se walidain confuse hote hain ke birth certificate alag hai aur B-Form alag — **dono ka rishta samajh lein:**

1. **Union Council** bache ki pedaish ka **pehla indraj** karti hai aur **birth certificate** deti hai.
2. Is birth certificate ki bunyad par **NADRA B-Form (CRC)** jari karta hai — ye qomi database me indraj hai.
3. Phir B-Form ki bunyad par **passport**, phir 18 saal par **CNIC**.

Matlab ye aik **silsila (chain)** hai — pehli kari (UC certificate) kamzor hui to tamam aage wale documents me masla aayega. Is liye birth certificate par **naam ki spelling aur date** bilkul durust honi chahiye.

**Practical mashwara:** Birth certificate milte hi us ki **3-4 photocopies** karwa kar alag-alag jagah rakhein — aur aik scan **mobile/email me** bhi bacha lein. Certificate gumne par duplicate me waqt lagta hai, lekin scan se kaam chal jata hai jahan photocopy chahiye.

## Death Certificate Ka Bhi Mukhtasir Zikr

NADRA ke naye system me **death registration** bhi Union Council se hoti hai — kisi aziz ke inteqal ke baad:

1. Hospital ka **death slip** lein.
2. UC me indraj karwayen aur **death certificate** lein.
3. Phir NADRA se computerized death certificate — ye **wirasat, bank account aur pension** ke muamlat me lazmi hai.

Fee aam tor par **Rs 100-400** hoti hai (UC ke hisab se) — apni UC se confirm kar lein.

## Tajurbakar Walidain Ke Ahem Mashware

1. **Naam pehle, certificate baad me:** Bache ka naam pedaish se pehle hi soch lein — hospital slip par ghalat ya aarzi naam bad me theek karwana lamba kaam hai.
2. **Date par koi "adjustment" na karein:** Kuch log school ki umar ke liye date badal dete hain — ye jhoot baad me **board, passport aur CNIC** tak peechha karta hai. Asal date hi darj karwayen.
3. **Dono spellings check karein:** Urdu ke saath English spelling bhi confirm kar lein — yahi spelling passport par jayegi.
4. **UC ka round pehle mahine:** Pedaish ke pehle mahine me birth certificate aur phir dousre mahine me B-Form — ye tartib rakhi to school ka waqt aaram se guzrega.
5. **Purane waqiyat ke liye:** Agar bache ki umar zyada ho gayi hai aur indraj nahi hua to **der na karein** — affidavit aur school record se kaam ho jata hai, bas waqt lagta hai. Apni UC ke secretary se pehle mil kar poora tareeqa maloom kar lein to zaya chakkaron se bach jayenge.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya certificate UC ke ilawa kahi se mil sakta hai?**
Nahi — pedaish ka indraj **UC ki zimmedari** hai. e-Khidmat/Dastak jahan mojood hai wahan wahi UC ka kaam ghar bethaye karwa deta hai.

**Bache ka naam Urdu me likha jayega ya English me?**
Dono — computerized certificate **Urdu aur English** dono me banta hai. English spelling wahi hogi jo aap form me likhenge.

**Aik se zyada bachon ka indraj aik saath ho sakta hai?**
Ji haan — tamam bachon ke documents le jayen, UC sab ka indraj aik saath kar deti hai. Har bache ka alag certificate banta hai.

## Bache Ke Tamam Documents Ka Folder Banayen

Aik bache ke zindagi bhar ye documents jama hote hain — birth certificate, B-Form, school certificates, passport, CNIC. **Aik folder har bache ka** bana lein aur us me:

- Har document ki **asal copy** (jahan asal chahiye)
- Har document ki **2 photocopies**
- **Scan files** (mobile me bhi)

Jab koi kaam aayega to aap ko pata hoga sab kahan hai. Walidain ka sab se bara dushman **document ka gumna** hai, process nahi — process to is guide me likha hi hua hai.

## File Me Rakhein

Birth certificate ke saath **hospital slip ki copy** bhi file me rakhein — kabhi UC dobara verification mange to ye kaam ati hai.
