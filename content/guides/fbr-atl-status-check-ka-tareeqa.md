## ATL Kya Hai Aur Filer Status Kyun Check Karen

ATL matlab **Active Taxpayer List** — ye Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) ki wo sarkaari fehrist hai jis me un tamam afraad, companies aur associations ke naam hote hain jinhon ne apna income tax return waqt par file kiya ho. Aam zuban me jab koi kehta hai ke "main filer hoon" to is ka matlab yehi hota hai ke us ka naam is waqt ATL me mojood hai.

Ye check is liye zaroori hai ke Pakistan me filer aur non-filer ke darmiyan har bari transaction par farq parta hai. Bank se bari raqam nikalwane, gaari ki registration ya transfer, property ki khareed-o-farokht aur munafe (profit) par filer ko kam withholding tax lagta hai, jab ke non-filer ko zyada dena parta hai. Is liye koi bara kaam karne se pehle apna status confirm kar lena aqalmandi hai — taake baad me koi hairani na ho.

Achi khabar ye hai ke ATL status check karna **bilkul muft** hai aur is ke liye kisi agent ya wakeel ki zaroorat nahi. Aap apne mobile se ek SMS bhej kar, ya FBR ke IRIS portal par ja kar, ek minute me apna status maloom kar sakte hain. Is guide me teenon tareeqe step-by-step diye gaye hain.

Agar aap ne abhi tak return file hi nahi kiya aur filer banna chahte hain to pehle [FBR filer banne ka mukammal tareeqa](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) parh lein — us ke baad is guide se apna status verify kar lein.

## Check Karne Se Pehle Ye Cheezein Tayyar Rakhen

Check shuru karne se pehle sirf do cheezein chahiye hoti hain, is liye ye kaam do minute se zyada nahi leta:

- **Apna 13 hindson wala CNIC number** — baghair dash ke, masalan 3520212345671 ki shakal me. Agar aap company ya association (AOP) ka status check kar rahe hain to us ka **7 hindson wala NTN** chahiye hoga.
- **Wo mobile number jo aap ke naam par registered ho** — SMS wale tareeqe ke liye yehi number istemal karen, taake jawab foran mil jaye.

Ek ahem ehtiyat: apna CNIC number sirf FBR ke sarkaari portals (fbr.gov.pk, iris.fbr.gov.pk, e.fbr.gov.pk) ya us ke official SMS shortcode par hi dein. Kisi ghair-mehfooz website, WhatsApp group ya agent ko apna CNIC number bhej kar status check karwane ki koshish na karen — ye aap ki zaati maloomat ke liye khatarnak ho sakta hai.

## Tareeqa 1: SMS Se ATL Status Check (Sab Se Tez)

Agar aap ke paas internet nahi hai ya foran jawab chahiye to SMS sab se tez tareeqa hai. Is ke liye kisi app ya login ki zaroorat nahi:

1. Apne mobile ka message app kholen.
2. Naya message likhen: **ATL** likhen, phir ek space dein, phir apna 13 hindson wala CNIC number baghair dash ke likhen — masalan `ATL 3520212345671`.
3. Ye message **9966** par bhej dein.
4. Chand lamhon me FBR ki taraf se jawab aa jaye ga jis me bataya jaye ga ke aap **Active** hain, **Inactive** hain ya aap ka record **Not Found** hai, aur ye bhi ke ye status kis tax year ke liye hai.

Company ya AOP ka status check karne ke liye yehi tareeqa hai, bas CNIC ki jagah 7 hindson wala NTN likhen — masalan `ATL 1234567` — aur wohi 9966 par bhej dein.

SMS wale tareeqe ki ek hadd ye hai ke jawab mukhtasar hota hai — sirf status aur tax year. Tafseel dekhni ho, masalan aap ka poora naam aur registration ki tafseel, to agla online tareeqa behtar hai.

## Tareeqa 2: IRIS Portal Par Online Check (Baghair Login)

FBR ka **IRIS portal** (iris.fbr.gov.pk) par ATL check ke liye login karna zaroori nahi — verification wala hissa baghair account ke khula hua hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

1. Browser me **iris.fbr.gov.pk** kholen.
2. Home page par **Online Verifications** ya upar menu me **Verifications** par click karen.
3. List me se **Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax)** muntakhib karen.
4. **Parameter Type** wali dropdown se apni mutabiq option chunen — afraad ke liye **CNIC**, business ke liye **NTN**, aur ghair-mulki shakhs ke liye **Passport No.**
5. **Registration No.** wale khane me apna number baghair dash ke likhen.
6. Screen par nazar aane wala **captcha code** darj karen aur **Verify** par click karen.

Natija foran screen par aa jaye ga: aap ka naam, registration number, status (active ya inactive) aur wo tax year jis ke liye ye status hai. Agar aap ke paas IRIS account hai to login kar ke apni dashboard par filing history bhi dekh sakte hain, lekin sirf status check ke liye login ki koi zaroorat nahi.

FBR ki e-portal **e.fbr.gov.pk** par bhi Online Verification ke zariye yehi ATL lookup mojood hai. Dono portals FBR ke official hain — jo aap ko aasan lage, wohi istemal karen.

![Laptop par FBR IRIS portal se online ATL verification ki illustration](/images/fbr-atl-status-check-online.jpg)

## Tareeqa 3: Tax Asaan App Se Check

Agar aap mobile app pasand karte hain to FBR ki official **Tax Asaan** app (Google Play Store aur Apple App Store dono par mojood hai) se bhi yehi check ho jata hai:

1. Tax Asaan app install karen — publisher ka naam **FBR** hona chahiye, kisi milti-julti app se hoshiyar rahen.
2. App me **Online Verification** wala section kholen.
3. Wahan CNIC ya NTN muntakhib kar ke apna number baghair dash ke likhen aur verify karen.

App ka faida ye hai ke aap ko browser me portal kholne ki zaroorat nahi parti aur dobara check karna ho to number mehfooz rehta hai. Natija wohi hota hai jo IRIS portal par milta hai — status aur tax year.

## Fees Aur Time Kitna Lagta Hai

| Tareeqa | Fees | Waqt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SMS 9966 par | Check muft; aap ke mobile package ke mutabiq aam SMS charges lag sakte hain | Chand lamhe |
| IRIS portal (online) | Bilkul muft | 1–2 minute |
| Tax Asaan app | Bilkul muft | 1–2 minute |

ATL ki fehrist har saal **1 March** ko jari hoti hai aur agle saal February ke aakhir tak qaim rehti hai. Is ke darmiyan FBR is fehrist ko **haftawar update** karta hai — aam tor par peer (Monday) ko — is liye agar aap ne return abhi haal hi me file kiya hai to ho sakta hai ke aap ka naam agle haftawar update tak list me na aaye. Aise me ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi; kuch din baad dobara check karen.

## Natija Samajhna: Active, Inactive Ya Not Found Ka Matlab

Check ke baad teen me se ek natija mile ga, aur har ek ka agla qadam alag hai:

- **Active:** Mubarak ho — aap is waqt filer hain aur filer wale kam tax rates aap par lagu honge. Bas ye yaad rakhen ke har saal return file karna zaroori hai, warna agle saal ki list me naam nahi aaye ga.
- **Inactive:** Is ka matlab hai ke aap ne mutaliqa tax year ka return file nahi kiya, ya return to file kiya lekin koi surcharge ya wajbaat baqi hain. Hal ye hai ke IRIS par login kar ke mutaliqa saal ka return file karen; dair se list me aane par surcharge lag sakta hai jo PSID ke zariye ada hota hai — maujooda raqam IRIS portal par confirm kar lein.
- **Not Found:** Is ka matlab hai ke diye gaye number par FBR ke record me koi registration mojood nahi — shayad number ghalat likha gaya ho, ya aap ne kabhi NTN registration hi nahi karwayi. Pehle number dobara ghaur se check karen; agar number durust hai to IRIS par unregistered shakhs ke tor par registration karwa kar return file karen. Tafseel [FBR filer banne ka tareeqa](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) me mojood hai.

Ek aur baat: natije me apna naam aur tax year zaroor milayen. Agar naam me spelling ka farq nazar aaye ya tax year purana dikhaye to IRIS profile me apni maloomat durust karwayen — ghalat record aage ja kar bank ya property ke kaam me rukawat ban sakta hai.

Ye bhi zehen me rakhen ke income tax wali ATL aur sales tax wali ATL **do alag fehristein** hain. Aam shakhs ko income tax wali list se matlab hota hai. Sales tax wali list sirf registered businesses aur suppliers ko check karne ke kaam aati hai — agar aap kisi supplier ka sales tax status dekhna chahte hain to wo FBR ki sales tax ATL page par alag se check hota hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Bachen

- **CNIC me dash likhna:** SMS aur portal dono me number baghair dash ke likhna hota hai. Dash lagane se aksar "not found" ka jawab aa jata hai.
- **Haal hi me file kar ke foran check karna:** Return file karne ke foran baad status tabdeel nahi hota — haftawar update ka intezar karen, phir dobara check karen.
- **Ghalat tax year dekhna:** Natije me tax year zaroor parhen. Pichle saal active hona is saal active hone ki zamanat nahi.
- **Ghair-sarkaari sites par CNIC dalna:** Jo sites "filer check" ke naam par CNIC mangti hain lekin FBR ki official nahi, un se bachen — aap ka CNIC ghalat haathon me ja sakta hai.
- **SMS ka purana shortcode istemal karna:** Sirf **9966** FBR ka official shortcode hai. Kisi aur number par "ATL" likh kar bhejne se jawab nahi aaye ga.
- **Status check ko filer banna samajhna:** Status check sirf maloomat hai — filer banne ke liye return file karna lazmi hai. Agar aap inactive hain to sirf check karne se kuch nahi badle ga.

Is ke ilawa, apne CNIC ki hifazat ke liye [CNIC status tracking ka tareeqa](/guides/cnic-status-tracking-ka-tareeqa) bhi kaam aa sakta hai, aur agar aap gaari ke maamle me filer faide dekhna chahte hain to [gaari token tax online jama karwana](/guides/gaari-token-tax-online-jama-karwana) aur [gaari transfer karne ka tareeqa](/guides/gaari-transfer-karne-ka-tareeqa) guides parh lein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**FBR ATL status check karne ka sab se tez tareeqa kaunsa hai?**
SMS sab se tez hai — `ATL` space apna 13 hindson wala CNIC number baghair dash ke likh kar 9966 par bhej dein. Chand lamhon me jawab aa jata hai ke aap active hain ya nahi, aur ye status kis tax year ke liye hai.

**Kya ATL status online check karne ke liye IRIS login zaroori hai?**
Nahi. IRIS portal par Online Verifications ke zariye ATL check baghair login ke hota hai — sirf parameter type (CNIC ya NTN), number aur captcha darkar hota hai. Login sirf tab chahiye jab aap return file karna ya apni filing history dekhna chahen.

**Maine return file kar diya hai lekin status abhi inactive dikh raha hai — kyun?**
FBR ATL ko haftawar update karta hai, is liye nayi filing foran list me nahi aati. Aam tor par agle haftawar update (peer ko) tak status tabdeel ho jata hai. Agar ek hafte baad bhi inactive dikhe to IRIS par check karen ke return poori tarah submit hua tha ya koi surcharge baqi hai.

**ATL status check karne ki koi fees hai?**
Nahi, online aur app se check bilkul muft hai. SMS par sirf aap ke mobile package ke mutabiq aam SMS charges lag sakte hain, is ke ilawa FBR status check ke liye koi fees nahi leta. Koi agent is kaam ke paise mange to us se bachen.

**Agar mera naam ATL me nahi hai to main dobara filer kaise ban sakta hoon?**
IRIS portal par login kar ke (ya naye shakhs ke tor par register ho kar) mutaliqa tax year ka income tax return file karen. Return process hone ke baad agle haftawar update me aap ka naam ATL me aa jaye ga. Mukammal step-by-step tareeqa [FBR filer banne ka tareeqa](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) guide me diya gaya hai.
