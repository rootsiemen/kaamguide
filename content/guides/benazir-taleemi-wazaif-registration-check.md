## Pehli Baat: Benazir Taleemi Wazaif Kya Hai

**Benazir Taleemi Wazaif** Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) ka aik conditional cash transfer programme hai, jo un bachon ko har quarter wazifa deta hai jin ki maa **BISP Kafaalat** ki active beneficiary hai. Is ka maqsad aik hi hai — gareeb gharane ke bache school jayein, school me tikay rahein aur taleem poori karein. Wazifa is liye "conditional" kehlata hai, kyun ke raqam sirf us waqt milti hai jab bacha school ya college me dakhil ho aur us ki hazri poori ho. Sirf naam likhwane se paisa nahi milta.

Ye programme pehle **Waseela-e-Taleem** ke naam se jana jata tha. BISP ki official page ke mutabiq ye November 2012 me sirf 5 zilon me pilot ke tor par shuru hua tha, 2015 me 32 zilon, 2018 me 50 zilon aur January 2020 me 100 zilon tak pahuncha, aur July 2020 se ye Pakistan ke tamam zilon me chal raha hai. Shuru se ab tak is programme me **1 crore 20 lakh se zyada bache** (12.01 million) enroll ho chuke hain aur **Rs 63.34 arab** se zyada raqam taqseem ki ja chuki hai.

Aik baat pehle din se samajh lein: ye wazifa alag se koi nayi scheme nahi hai, ye **Kafaalat ka hissa** hai. Agar aap ki walida ya aap khud BISP Kafaalat ki beneficiary nahi hain, to bache ka Taleemi Wazaif nahi ban sakta — pehle Kafaalat me shamil hona zaroori hai. Apni Kafaalat ki status [BISP 8171 par check karne ka tareeqa](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) hamari alag guide me tafseel se likha hai.

## Kaun Eligible Hai: Umar Aur Shartein

BISP ki official page par Taleemi Wazaif ki ehliyaat bohat wazeh likhi hai. Sirf do bunyadi shartein hain — bacha kisi active Kafaalat beneficiary ka ho, aur us ki umar us ke taleemi level ke mutabiq ho:

| Taleemi Level | Bache Ki Umar |
|---|---|
| Primary (Class 1–5) | 4 se 12 saal |
| Secondary (Class 6–10) | 8 se 18 saal |
| Higher Secondary (Class 11–12) | 13 se 22 saal |

Is ke ilawa do co-responsibilities hain jin par wazifa barqarar rehta hai: bacha kisi school ya college me **dakhil** ho, aur har quarter me us ki hazri kam az kam **70%** ho. Pehle quarter ki payment enrollment ke foran baad jaari hoti hai, lekin doosre quarter se aage hazri ki shart lagoo ho jati hai. Agar bacha teen quarter lagatar 70% hazri poori na kar sake, to use programme se suspend kar diya jata hai — is liye hazri par nazar rakhna sab se ahem hai.

Ye bhi yaad rakhein ke wazifa bache ke naam par nahi, us ki **walida (Kafaalat beneficiary)** ke naam par jaari hota hai. Isi liye registration ke waqt maa ka mojood hona aur us ka asli CNIC sath lana lazmi hai. Agar walida ka [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) expired hai ya gum ho gaya hai, to pehle us ka masla hal karein — warna enrollment aage nahi barhega.

## Wazifa Kitna Milta Hai: Har Level Ki Raqam

BISP ki official page par darj table ke mutabiq har quarter (teen mahine) me milne wala wazifa taleemi level aur bache ki jins par munhasir hai. Larkiyon ko har level par larkon se **Rs 500 zyada** milta hai, taake larkiyon ki taleem ki khaas hosla afzai ho:

| Taleemi Level | Larka (har quarter) | Larki (har quarter) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Rs 1,500 | Rs 2,000 |
| Secondary | Rs 2,500 | Rs 3,000 |
| Higher Secondary | Rs 3,500 | Rs 4,000 |

Is ke ilawa aik khaas inaam bhi hai: jo larki primary taleem (Class 5) mukammal karti hai, use **aik dafa Rs 3,000 ka graduation bonus** bhi milta hai. Ye bonus aam wazife ke ilawa hai aur is ka maqsad ye hai ke larkiyan primary ke baad school na chhorein.

Aik zaroori wazahat: 2026 me kuch khabron me wazife me Rs 500 izafe ki ittila mili hai, lekin BISP ki official page par is waqt upar wali hi table darj hai. Is liye guide me sirf official table likha gaya hai — apne bache ki asal raqam **8171 portal ya qareebi BISP office** se confirm kar lein, kyun ke final figure wahi batayega. Kisi agent ya dukaan wale ke bataye hue "naye rate" par bharosa na karein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Enrollment ke liye BISP field office ya enrollment camp jate waqt ye teen cheezein lazmi sath le jayein. In me se aik bhi kami ho to registration usi din mukammal nahi hogi:

1. **Walida ka asli CNIC** — wohi CNIC jis par Kafaalat milti hai. Photocopy nahi, asli card chahiye.
2. **Bache ka B-Form / CRC** — NADRA se jari kardah Child Registration Certificate. B-Form ki tasdeeq NADRA ke database se lazmi ki jati hai, is liye B-Form pehle se bana hua hona chahiye. Agar abhi tak nahi bana, to [B-Form banane ka tareeqa](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh lein — us ke liye bache ka [birth certificate](/guides/birth-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) chahiye hota hai.
3. **School/College admission slip** — jis par bache ki class aur school ya college ka naam darj ho, aur jo us ke ustad (teacher) se verify shuda ho. Ye slip school se hi milti hai, is liye jane se pehle school se le lein.

Bache ko bhi sath le jana zaroori hai, kyun ke enrollment ke waqt us ki mojoodgi aur tasdeeq hoti hai. Apne sath aik purana mobile number bhi rakhein jo walida ke naam par registered ho — is par 8171 se paighamat ate hain.

## Registration Ka Tareeqa: Step-by-Step

Taleemi Wazaif ki registration **online nahi hoti** — ye kaam BISP ke enrollment camp ya Tehsil office ja kar hi hota hai. Poora process ye hai:

![Tehsil office me Taleemi Wazaif ke liye registration karwati maan aur bacha](/images/benazir-taleemi-wazaif-registration-check-2.jpg)

**Step 1 — Pehle Kafaalat ki status confirm karein.** Apna CNIC 8171 par SMS karein ya 8171 portal par check karein ke walida active Kafaalat beneficiary hain ya nahi. Agar status active nahi hai, to pehle us ka masla hal karein.

**Step 2 — Kagzaat mukammal karein.** Upar wali list ke teeno kagzaat tayyar rakhein — walida ka CNIC, bache ka B-Form aur school se verify shuda admission slip.

**Step 3 — Qareebi enrollment camp ya BISP Tehsil office jayein.** BISP waqtan fawaqtan mukhtalif ilaqon me enrollment camps lagata hai; agar aap ke ilaqe me camp na ho, to apne zila ke BISP Tehsil office chale jayein. Pata na ho to BISP helpline **0800-26477** par call karke poch lein.

**Step 4 — Form bharwayein aur tasdeeq karwayein.** Office me bache ki enrollment ki darkhwast jama hogi. Amala bache ka B-Form NADRA ke database se verify karega aur admission slip ki tasdeeq karega.

**Step 5 — Tasdeeqi SMS ka intezar karein.** Enrollment mukammal hone ke baad pehle quarter ki payment jaari ho jati hai aur is ki ittila 8171 se SMS par milti hai. Payment milne me aam tor par chand hafte lagte hain, is liye foran na milne par pareshan na hon.

Yaad rakhein: **registration bilkul muft hai.** Koi bhi shakhs — agent, dukaan wala ya koi "madadgar" — agar registration ke naam par paisa mange, to wo fraud hai. Foran BISP helpline 0800-26477 par shikayat darj karwayein.

## Wazifa Online Check Karne Ka Tareeqa (8171)

Registration ke baad wazifa ki status ghar baithe do tareeqon se check ki ja sakti hai:

![8171 par mobile se Benazir Taleemi Wazaif check karna](/images/benazir-taleemi-wazaif-registration-check-3.jpg)

**Tareeqa 1 — SMS se check karein.** Apne mobile se walida ka 13-hindson wala CNIC number (baghair dash ke) likh kar **8171** par SMS karein. Jawabi SMS me Kafaalat aur Taleemi Wazaif ki status aa jayegi. Ye SMS tamam networks se muft hai.

**Tareeqa 2 — 8171 portal se check karein.** Browser me **8171.bisp.gov.pk** kholein, walida ka CNIC number darj karein, screen par nazar anay wala captcha hal karein aur "Check" par click karein. Portal par gharane ki ehliyaat aur har enrolled bache ki Taleemi Wazaif status nazar aati hai. Portal par [CNIC status tracking](/guides/cnic-status-tracking-ka-tareeqa) ka mukammal tareeqa bhi hamari guide me mojood hai.

Kuch khabron ke mutabiq 2026 me BISP ne **Waseela-e-Taleem mobile app** bhi mutaarif karwayi hai, jis se ghar baithe bache ki registration ki sahulat milti hai. App istemal karne se pehle is ki tasdeeq official BISP page ya helpline 0800-26477 se zaroor kar lein, aur app sirf official Play Store link se download karein — kisi aur link se app download karke apna CNIC ya OTP kabhi na dein.

## Payment Kab Aur Kaise Milti Hai

Taleemi Wazaif ki payment **har quarter** (teen mahine baad) jaari hoti hai. Pehli payment enrollment ke foran baad milti hai; us ke baad har quarter ki payment us waqt jaari hoti hai jab bache ki 70% hazri confirm ho jaye. School har quarter ki hazri BISP ko bhejta hai, is liye agar bacha bemar ho ya kisi wajah se chhuttiyan zyada hon, to school ko waqt par ittila dein aur hazri ka record durust rakhein.

Payment aam tor par wahi tareeqa se milti hai jis se Kafaalat ki raqam milti hai — BISP ke muqarar kardah bank agents, campsite ya wallet ke zariye. Payment lene ke liye walida ko apna asli CNIC sath le jana hota hai aur biometric tasdeeq hoti hai. Kisi ko apna CNIC ya PIN de kar payment nikalwane ki koshish na karein — ye sab se bara fraud ka rasta hai.

Agar kisi quarter ki payment ruk jaye to sab se pehle ye check karein: kya us quarter me bache ki hazri 70% se kam to nahi rahi? Kya school ne hazri ka record waqt par bheja? Agar dono theek hon to qareebi BISP Tehsil office ja kar case management (shikayat) darj karwayein — BISP ki official page ke mutabiq field offices hi shikayaat ka fori hal karne ke zimmedar hain.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Wazifa Rukwa Deti Hain

1. **B-Form na banana ya ghalat B-Form:** Bache ka B-Form NADRA se verify hona lazmi hai. Ghalat ya purana B-Form enrollment hi rukwa deta hai.
2. **School admission slip par teacher ki tasdeeq na hona:** Baghair tasdeeq wali slip qabool nahi hoti. School jate waqt slip par ustad ke dastakhat aur mohar lazmi karwayein.
3. **70% hazri poori na karna:** Ye sab se aam wajah hai. Doosre quarter se aage hazri kam hui to us quarter ki payment ruk jati hai, aur teen quarter lagatar aisa ho to bacha programme se nikal diya jata hai.
4. **Kafaalat ki status par tawajju na dena:** Agar walida ki Kafaalat kisi wajah se ruk jaye ya re-survey me naam nikal jaye, to bache ka wazifa bhi ruk jata hai. Apni Kafaalat ki status waqtan fawaqtan 8171 par check karte rahein.
5. **Agent ko paisa dena:** Registration muft hai. Jo paisa mange, wo fraud hai — us se na sirf paisa zaya hota hai, balke aap ka CNIC aur data bhi ghalat hathon me ja sakta hai.
6. **Ghalat mobile number dena:** 8171 ke tamam paighamat usi number par ate hain jo registration ke waqt diya gaya ho. Number badal jaye to foran BISP office me update karwayein.
7. **Nakli website ya app se check karna:** Sirf official portal **8171.bisp.gov.pk** aur SMS number **8171** istemal karein. Koi bhi aur website ya app jo "Taleemi Wazaif check" ka dawa kare, us par apna CNIC kabhi na darj karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Benazir Taleemi Wazaif ke liye kaun eligible hai?**

Sirf un bachon ko wazifa milta hai jin ki maa BISP Kafaalat ki active beneficiary ho. Umar ki shart ye hai: Primary ke liye 4 se 12 saal, Secondary ke liye 8 se 18 saal aur Higher Secondary ke liye 13 se 22 saal. Bache ka school ya college me dakhil hona aur har quarter 70% hazri poori karna bhi lazmi hai.

**Taleemi Wazaif ki registration online ho sakti hai?**

Nahi, registration online nahi hoti. Walida ko bache ke sath BISP ke enrollment camp ya Tehsil office jana hota hai, sath me apna CNIC, bache ka B-Form aur school se verify shuda admission slip le kar. Kuch khabron ke mutabiq 2026 me Waseela-e-Taleem app aayi hai, lekin us ki tasdeeq official BISP page ya helpline 0800-26477 se karna behtar hai.

**Benazir Taleemi Wazaif kitna milta hai?**

BISP ki official page ke mutabiq har quarter Primary me larke ko Rs 1,500 aur larki ko Rs 2,000, Secondary me larke ko Rs 2,500 aur larki ko Rs 3,000, aur Higher Secondary me larke ko Rs 3,500 aur larki ko Rs 4,000 milta hai. Primary mukammal karne wali larki ko aik dafa Rs 3,000 ka graduation bonus bhi milta hai. Apne bache ki asal raqam 8171 portal par confirm karein.

**Wazifa check karne ka tareeqa kya hai?**

Walida ka 13-hindson wala CNIC 8171 par SMS karein, ya browser me 8171.bisp.gov.pk khol kar CNIC darj karke captcha hal karein. Dono tareeqon se Kafaalat aur har enrolled bache ki Taleemi Wazaif status maloom ho jati hai. Ye check muft hai.

**Agar bache ki hazri 70% se kam ho jaye to kya hoga?**

Us quarter ki payment ruk jayegi. Agar teen quarter lagatar hazri 70% se kam rahe, to BISP bache ko programme se suspend kar deta hai. Is liye bache ki school hazri par har quarter nazar rakhein aur chhuttiyon ki ittila waqt par school ko dein.
