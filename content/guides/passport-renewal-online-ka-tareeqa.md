## Passport Renewal Kya Hai Aur Kaun Online Kar Sakta Hai

Passport ki muddat khatam hone par naya passport lena renewal kehlata hai. Renewal ka matlab ye nahi ke purana passport hi barha diya jaye — aap ko aik naya passport booklet jari hota hai, jis par naya number aur nai muddat hoti hai. Purana passport bhi aap ke paas rehta hai, kyun ke us par lage hue purane visa aksar agle safar me kaam aate hain, lekin safar ke liye sirf naya, valid passport istemal hota hai.

Achi khabar ye hai ke 2026 me aksar Pakistanis apna Machine Readable Passport (MRP) ghar baithe online renew kar sakte hain. Directorate General of Immigration and Passports (DGIP) ka online portal onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk isi kaam ke liye bana hai. Online renewal un logon ke liye hai jin ke paas pehle se aik MRP maujood hai aur jin ki maloomat me koi bari tabdeeli nahi hui. Agar aap ka passport expire ho chuka hai ya us ki muddat khatam hone me aik saal se kam waqt reh gaya hai to aap renewal ke liye apply kar sakte hain.

Online renewal har case me mumkin nahi. Agar aap pehli dafa passport banwa rahe hain to ye guide aap ke liye nahi — pehli dafa passport ke liye passport office jana parta hai, jis ka tareeqa hamari [passport banane wali guide](/guides/pakistan-passport-banane-ka-tareeqa) me tafseel se diya gaya hai. Isi tarah agar aap ka passport gum ho gaya hai ya chori ho gaya hai to us ka tareeqa alag hai, jo [khoya hua passport dobara banwane wali guide](/guides/khoya-hua-passport-dobara-banwana) me maujood hai. Aur agar aap ka naam, walid ka naam ya tareekh-e-paidaish tabdeel hui hai to sirf renewal nahi, balke record ki tabdeeli ka case ban jata hai, jis ke liye mazeed saboot darkar hote hain.

E-passport ke bare me aik zaroori farq samajh lein: online portal bunyadi tor par MRP ki renewal ke liye hai. E-passport (chip wala passport) ke liye aksar passport office me hazir ho kar biometric aur tasveer ki tasdeeq karwani parti hai, chahe aap ne fee online hi kyun na jama karwai ho. Is liye apply karne se pehle ye faisla kar lein ke aap ko MRP chahiye ya e-passport, kyun ke dono ki fees aur tareeqa me farq hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Renewal Se Pehle Ye Cheezein Tayyar Rakhein

Renewal me pehli dafa wale passport jitne kagzaat nahi lagte, kyun ke aap ka record DGIP ke paas pehle se maujood hota hai. Phir bhi ye cheezein pehle se tayyar rakhne se application beech me nahi rukti:

- **Purana passport:** Chahe expire ho chuka ho, us ki saaf scan copy ya tasveer. Purana passport hi aap ki pehchan aur record dhoondne ka sab se bara zariya hai.
- **Valid CNIC ya NICOP:** Passport renewal ke liye valid CNIC lazmi hai. Agar aap ka CNIC expire ho gaya hai to pehle us ki renewal karwayein — [CNIC renewal ka tareeqa](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) is me madad karega. Expired CNIC par passport ki application aage nahi barhti, aur ye overseas Pakistanis ke liye sab se aam rukawat hai.
- **Nai tasveer:** ICAO standard ke mutabiq saaf, samne se li hui tasveer — safed ya halke background ke sath, chehra poori tarah nazar aaye, aenak ka reflection na ho. Online portal par tasveer ki file size ki had bhi hoti hai, is liye tasveer ko portal ki hidayat ke mutabiq compress kar ke upload karein.
- **Fingerprints ki tasdeeq:** Online renewal me fingerprints ki tasdeeq NADRA ki Pak ID app ke zariye mobile se hoti hai. Agar app fingerprints theek se na le sake to passport office par biometrics dene ko kaha ja sakta hai.
- **Mobile number aur email:** Portal par account banane aur OTP tasdeeq ke liye aik active mobile number aur email address lazmi hai. Tracking aur updates bhi isi number aur email par aate hain.
- **Payment ka zariya:** Online fee ke liye debit ya credit card. DGIP ki fee Passport Fee Asaan app ke zariye bhi ada ki ja sakti hai, jis se 17-digit PSID challan banta hai.

Agar renewal ke sath address tabdeel karna hai to naya address darj karein aur us ka saboot tayyar rakhein. Sirf renewal (bina tabdeeli) ke liye purane record par hi naya passport jari ho jata hai.

## Passport Renewal Fees 2026

Renewal ki fee naye passport jitni hi hoti hai — renewal par koi alag discount nahi milta. Fee teen cheezon par munhasir hai: booklet ke pages (36, 72 ya 100), muddat (5 saal ya 10 saal) aur service ki raftaar (Normal, Urgent ya Fast Track). Neeche Machine Readable Passport (MRP) ki 2026 ki shaya shuda fee schedule di gayi hai. Hatmi rakam hamesha DGIP portal ya Passport Fee Asaan app ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai — wahi rakam ada karein, kyun ke fees government notification se tabdeel ho sakti hain:

| Passport (MRP) | Normal | Urgent | Fast Track |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 36 pages, 5 saal | Rs. 4,500 | Rs. 7,500 | Rs. 12,500 |
| 36 pages, 10 saal | Rs. 6,700 | Rs. 11,200 | Rs. 16,200 |
| 72 pages, 5 saal | Rs. 8,200 | Rs. 13,500 | Rs. 18,500 |
| 72 pages, 10 saal | Rs. 12,400 | Rs. 20,200 | Rs. 25,200 |
| 100 pages, 5 saal | Rs. 9,000 | Rs. 18,000 | Rs. 23,000 |
| 100 pages, 10 saal | Rs. 13,500 | Rs. 27,000 | Rs. 32,000 |

Aam safar karne wale aksar logon ke liye 36 pages, 10 saal wala passport sab se munasib rehta hai, kyun ke is me 5 saal wale se thori zyada fee par dugni muddat mil jati hai. E-passport ki fee MRP se taqreeban dugni hoti hai — masalan 36 pages, 5 saal wale e-passport ki normal fee taqreeban Rs. 9,000 aur urgent taqreeban Rs. 15,000 batayi jati hai. Is ke ilawa service charges aur taxes bhi lag sakte hain, is liye payment screen par dikhai gayi kul rakam ko hatmi samjhein.

Fee jama karwane ke baad PSID ya payment receipt ko mehfooz rakhna bohat zaroori hai. Yehi number aap ki application ki pehchan hai aur [passport status tracking](/guides/passport-status-tracking-online) ke waqt bhi isi ki zaroorat parti hai. Fee ki raseed ke baghair application ka status maloom karna mushkil ho jata hai.

## Online Tareeqa: Step-by-Step Renewal

Online renewal ka tareeqa seedha hai, bas har step ko tawajjo se poora karein:

1. **Portal par account banayein:** onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk kholein aur apne CNIC number, mobile number aur email se account banayein. OTP se tasdeeq ke baad login karein.
2. **Renewal ki application muntakhib karein:** Dashboard me passport renewal ka option chunein aur purane passport ki maloomat darj karein. Maloomat bilkul CNIC aur purane passport ke mutabiq likhein — aik harf ka farq bhi application rok sakta hai.
3. **Kagzaat upload karein:** Purane passport, CNIC aur nai tasveer ki saaf scan copies upload karein. Tasveer portal ki size aur format ki hidayat ke mutabiq ho, warna system use qabool nahi karega.
4. **Fingerprints ki tasdeeq karein:** NADRA Pak ID app ke zariye mobile se fingerprints ki tasdeeq mukammal karein. Is step ke baghair application aage nahi barhti.
5. **Fee ada karein:** Apni pasand ki category (pages, muddat, raftaar) muntakhib karein aur debit/credit card se fee ada karein. Passport Fee Asaan app se PSID bana kar bhi fee jama karwai ja sakti hai.
6. **Application submit karein aur tracking number mehfooz karein:** Submit karne ke baad milne wala tracking number aur receipt download kar ke rakh lein. Isi number se aap application ka status check kar sakenge.
7. **Delivery ka intezar karein:** Normal category me passport tayyar hone par aap ko SMS ya email par ittila mil jati hai. Overseas Pakistanis ke liye courier delivery ki sahulat bhi maujood hai.

## Passport Office Se Renewal (RPO Aur Executive Office)

Agar aap online renewal ke ahal nahi hain, ya aap ko e-passport chahiye, to passport office se renewal karwana hoga. Is ke liye qareebi Regional Passport Office (RPO) jayein, ya jaldi kaam ke liye Executive Passport Office muntakhib karein. Office me aap ki tasveer aur fingerprints dobara liye jate hain, is liye asal CNIC aur purana passport sath le kar jayein.

Office ka tareeqa ye hai: pehle fee jama karwayein (online QR ke zariye ya Passport Fee Asaan app se PSID bana kar), phir counter par application form jama karein, biometrics aur tasveer ki tasdeeq karwayein aur akhir me receipt lein. Executive Passport Offices me Fast Track service milti hai, jo Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi aur dosre bare shehron me maujood hain. Fast Track ki fee zyada hai, lekin passport taqreeban 2 working days me tayyar ho jata hai.

Office jane se pehle ye check kar lein ke aap ka CNIC valid hai aur aap ke paas fee ki raseed maujood hai. Adhoori file ke sath office jane se aik extra chakar lag jata hai, jo aasani se bacha ja sakta hai.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai

Renewal ka waqt service ki category par munhasir hai. DGIP ki shaya shuda muddat ke mutabiq:

| Service | Taqreeban Waqt |
| --- | --- |
| Normal | 21 working days tak |
| Urgent | Taqreeban 5 working days |
| Fast Track (Executive Office) | Taqreeban 2 working days |

Ye muddat working days me hai — chhuttiyan aur hafte ke akhir is me shamil nahi hote. Overseas applications me courier ka waqt is ke ilawa lag sakta hai, is liye bahar rehne wale logon ko safar se kam az kam aik mahina pehle apply karna chahiye. Agar application me koi kami nikal aaye to waqt aur bhi barh sakta hai, is liye pehle hi din se file mukammal rakhna behtar hai.

## Overseas Pakistanis Ke Liye Renewal

Bahar rehne wale Pakistanis ke liye online renewal sab se zyada faidemand hai, kyun ke is me consulate ya embassy jane ki zaroorat nahi parti. DGIP ke online portal par apply kar ke naya passport courier ke zariye ghar par wasool kiya ja sakta hai, jahan ye sahulat maujood hai. Fee card se rupees me ada hoti hai, aur overseas fee schedule ke mutabiq 36 pages, 5 saal wale passport ki normal fee taqreeban 50 se 65 dollar ke barabar banti hai, jabke urgent taqreeban 80 se 100 dollar tak ho sakti hai — hatmi rakam portal par confirm karein.

Overseas renewal me sab se aam rukawat expired NICOP ya CNIC hai. Apply karne se pehle apne NICOP/CNIC ki muddat zaroor check karein. Agar wo expire ho gaya hai to pehle NADRA se us ki renewal karwayein, warna passport ki application wahin ruk jayegi. Is ke ilawa tasveer aur fingerprints ki tasdeeq ke liye Pak ID app ka theek se kaam karna bhi zaroori hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Application Roka Deti Hain

- **Expired CNIC par apply karna:** Ye sab se aam ghalti hai. Passport se pehle CNIC ki muddat check karein.
- **Ghalat ya dhundli tasveer upload karna:** Tasveer standard ke mutabiq na ho to system application aage nahi barhata. Safed background, seedha chehra aur achi roshni lazmi hai.
- **Maloomat me farq:** CNIC, purane passport aur form me naam ya tareekh-e-paidaish ka farq application rok deta hai. Form submit karne se pehle har field dobara parhein.
- **Fee ki raseed gum karna:** PSID ya payment receipt ke baghair status check karna aur shikayat darj karwana mushkil ho jata hai.
- **Aakhri waqt par apply karna:** Normal service me 21 working days lag sakte hain. Safar se chand din pehle Normal apply karna ghalti hai — aisi surat me Urgent ya Fast Track muntakhib karein.
- **Ghalat portal ya agent par bharosa:** Sirf DGIP ke official portal onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk aur Passport Fee Asaan app istemal karein. Kisi agent ko apna CNIC, password ya OTP kabhi na dein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya Pakistan me passport renewal poori tarah online ho sakta hai?**
Ji haan, Machine Readable Passport (MRP) ki renewal aksar cases me DGIP ke online portal se poori tarah online ho sakti hai, bas fingerprints ki tasdeeq Pak ID app se karni hoti hai. E-passport ke liye office me biometric tasdeeq ki zaroorat par sakti hai.

**Passport renewal ki fee kitni hai?**
36 pages, 5 saal wale MRP ki normal fee Rs. 4,500, urgent Rs. 7,500 aur fast track Rs. 12,500 hai. 10 saal ya zyada pages wale passport ki fee zyada hoti hai, aur e-passport ki fee taqreeban dugni hai. Hatmi rakam portal ya Passport Fee Asaan app ki payment screen par confirm karein.

**Passport renewal me kitna waqt lagta hai?**
Normal service me 21 working days tak, Urgent me taqreeban 5 working days aur Fast Track (Executive Office) me taqreeban 2 working days lagte hain. Overseas delivery me courier ka waqt is ke ilawa lag sakta hai.

**Kya expired passport renew ho sakta hai?**
Ji haan, expired passport ki renewal mumkin hai. Is ke liye purana passport aur valid CNIC darkar hota hai, aur fee naye passport jitni hi ada karni hoti hai.

**Online renewal ke liye kaun se kagzaat chahiye?**
Purana passport, valid CNIC ya NICOP, ICAO standard ki nai tasveer, Pak ID app se fingerprints ki tasdeeq aur fee ada karne ke liye debit/credit card darkar hota hai.

*Note: Is guide me di gayi fees aur tareeqa Directorate General of Immigration and Passports (DGIP) ki official website dgip.gov.pk aur us ke online portal onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk ki maloomat ki bunyad par likha gaya hai, jo 2 November 2026 ko check ki gayi. Fees government notification se tabdeel ho sakti hain, is liye apply karne se pehle hatmi rakam DGIP portal ya Passport Fee Asaan app par zaroor confirm karein.*
