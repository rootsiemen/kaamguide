## Nikah Nama Aur NADRA Marriage Certificate Me Farq

Aksar log samajhte hain ke Nikah Nama hi kaafi hai — **nahi, dono alag hain**:

| | Nikah Nama | NADRA Marriage Certificate (MRC) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zuban** | Sirf Urdu | Urdu + English |
| **Shakal** | Hath se likha hua | Computerized, QR code wala |
| **Jari karta hai** | Nikah Registrar | Union Council (NADRA ke system se) |
| **Qabooliyat** | Pakistan me | **Duniya bhar me** — visa/immigration ke liye lazmi |

**Seedhi baat:** Visa, immigration, passport me spouse ka naam, bank aur jaidad ke muamlat ke liye **computerized marriage certificate chahiye** — haath wala Nikah Nama bahir qabool nahi hota.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fees Punjab Local Government ke published rates aur UCs ki aam practice se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. Fee UC aur soobe ke hisab se Rs 300-2,000 tak ho sakti hai — apni Union Council se exact fee zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Step 1: Nikah Ka Union Council Me Indraj (30 Din Ke Andar)

**Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961** ke tehat har Nikah ka **30 din ke andar** mutaliqa Union Council me indraj lazmi hai:

1. Nikah ke baad **Nikah Registrar** (molvi sahab) Nikah Nama ki aik copy **Union Council** ko bhejta hai — lekin is par bharosa na karein, **khud follow-up karein**.
2. Apni UC jayen aur confirm karein ke Nikah ka indraj ho gaya hai.
3. Agar indraj nahi hua to **asal Nikah Nama** le kar UC me khud indraj karwayen — dono miyan-biwi ke CNIC aur gawahon ki maloomat saath le jayen.
4. Der se indraj par **late fee** lag sakti hai — is liye 30 din ka waqt zaya na karein.

## Step 2: Computerized Marriage Certificate (MRC) Hasil Karna

UC me indraj ke baad **computerized certificate** ke liye apply karein:

1. Apni **Union Council / Municipal Committee** ke office jayen.
2. **MRC ka form** lein aur bharein — dono miyan-biwi ke naam, CNIC numbers, Nikah ki tareekh.
3. Kagzaat attach karein (neeche list).
4. **Fee ada karein** — Punjab me official fee **Rs 300** hai (normal, 3 working days); kuch UCs/cities me Rs 500-2,000 tak ho sakti hai.
5. **3-7 working days** me computerized certificate mil jata hai.

**Ahem wazahat:** NADRA khud awaam ko marriage certificate nahi deta — certificate **Union Council jari karti hai NADRA ke mehfooz system aur paper par**. Isi liye fee aur waqt har UC me thoda alag ho sakta hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

- **Asal Nikah Nama** (Nikah Registrar se attested)
- Dono miyan-biwi ke **asal CNIC** (aur copies)
- Dono ke **passport-size photographs**
- UC ka **marriage registration form** (office se milta hai)
- Agar aik spouse **overseas** hai to us ka **NICOP/passport** aur **power of attorney** (neeche tafseel)

## Step 3: CNIC Par Marital Status Update

Certificate milne ke baad apne **CNIC par "married" ka status** update karwayen:

1. NADRA center jayen — **CNIC modification** ke liye.
2. **Computerized marriage certificate** aur spouse ka CNIC le jayen.
3. Fee **Rs 400** (normal CNIC modification) — Smart CNIC ho to Rs 750.
4. Naya card **15 din** (normal) me mil jayega.

**Kyun zaroori hai:** Passport renewal, bank account aur visa me CNIC ka status aur certificate ka **match hona** lazmi hai — farq hua to application atak jati hai.

## Overseas Pakistanis Ke Liye

Bahir rehne walon ke liye rasta khula hai:

1. **Pakistani embassy** se rabta karein — wahan se Nikah registration ki rehnumai milti hai.
2. Agar aik spouse Pakistan me hai to **notarized power of attorney** bhej dein — wo aap ki taraf se UC me apply kar sakta hai.
3. Certificate tayyar hone par **courier** se mangwa lein.
4. Overseas fees zyada hoti hain (Rs 5,000-8,000 tak) — embassy se confirm kar lein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Indraj hi na karwana:** Bohat se log samajhte hain Nikah Registrar ne kar diya hoga — **khud UC ja kar confirm karein**.
2. **Naam ki spelling ka farq:** Nikah Nama, CNIC aur certificate par naam **harf-ba-harf** milna chahiye — farq hua to visa reject ho sakta hai.
3. **Court marriage ka indraj bhoolna:** Court marriage bhi qanooni hai lekin **UC me indraj phir bhi lazmi** hai.
4. **CNIC update na karna:** Certificate banwa kar CNIC purana chhor dena — dono ka match hona zaroori hai.
5. **Agent ko hazaron dena:** UC ki fee chand sau hai — agent ka is me koi kirdaar nahi.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Nikah Nama gum ho gaya, kya karun?**
Us UC se **duplicate Nikah Nama** nikalwayen jahan Nikah register hua tha — dono miyan-biwi CNIC ke saath jayen.

**Kya MRC ke baghair visa lag sakta hai?**
Family/spouse visa ke liye **nahi** — embassy computerized certificate mangti hai.

**Shadi ko saal guzar gaye, indraj nahi hua — ab?**
Ab bhi ho sakta hai — **late fee** lagegi (Rs 1,000-4,000 tak, UC ke hisab se). Nikah Nama aur CNIC le kar UC jayen.

**Kya dono ka jana zaroori hai?**
Indraj ke liye aik bhi ja sakta hai, lekin **power of attorney** ke saath. Behtar hai dono jayen.

**Certificate par ghalti ho to?**
Foran wapas UC jayen — Nikah Nama se mila kar correction karwayen. Der ki to har document me ghalti phel jayegi.

## Marriage Certificate Ki Verification (Visa Ke Liye Ahem)

Jis mulk ka visa lag raha hai, us ki embassy aksar certificate ki **verification** karti hai:

1. Computerized certificate par **UC ka stamp aur secretary ke dastakhat** lazmi hon — baghair in ke certificate adhura samjha jata hai.
2. Kuch mulk **MOFA attestation** bhi mangte hain — HEC ki tarah degree par nahi, ye Foreign Office se hoti hai aur certificate ke peeche stamp lagta hai.
3. Certificate **do zuban** (Urdu + English) me hona visa ke liye lazmi hai — yehi MRC ki sab se bari khaasiyat hai.

**Spelling ki tasdeeq:** Certificate ghar la kar **dono ke passport aur CNIC** se har lafz mila lein — ghalti ho to **usi hafte** UC se correction karwayen, visa file jama honay ke baad waqt nahi milta.

## Bivi/Bache Ke Documents Ka Agla Qadam

Marriage certificate banne ke baad aam tor par ye kaam bhi karne hote hain:

1. **CNIC par marital status update** + biwi ke liye **naya CNIC** (walid ke naam ki jagah shohar ka naam).
2. **Passport** par spouse ka naam darj karwana (agar bahir jana hai).
3. **FRC (Family Registration Certificate)** NADRA se — bachon ki paidaish ke baad family ka mukammal record ban jata hai (Rs 1,000 aik category, Rs 2,000 tamam categories — executive only).

Ye teeno kaam waqt par kar lein to mustaqbil ke har sarkari kaam me asani rehti hai.

## Akhri Mashware: Kaam Pehle Din Se Theek Karo

Marriage registration ko aksar log "baad me kar loon ga" par taal dete hain — aur phir yahi adhura kaam visa, passport aur bank ke tamam kamon me sab se pehle rukawat banta hai. Tajurba yehi kehta hai:

1. **Nikah ke pehle hafte** me UC indraj confirm kar lein — jab Nikah Registrar aur kagzaat taza hotay hain, kaam ghanton me hota hai. Saalon baad wahi kaam hafto leta hai aur late fee alag.
2. **Teen cheezen aik saath rakhein:** Nikah Nama (asal), MRC aur dono ke CNIC — in ka aik set (asal) aur aik set (copies) alag-alag rakhein.
3. **Har document par naam aik jaisa:** Agar kisi aik par bhi spelling alag hai to visa se pehle wahi theek karwayen — embassy ki nazar me alag spelling ka matlab **alag shaks** hota hai.
4. **Digital backup:** MRC aur Nikah Nama ka scan email/cloud me rakhein — asal ghum bhi jaye to duplicate nikalwane me scan bohat kaam aata hai.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya talaaq ke baad certificate ka koi update hota hai?**
Talaaq ki soorat me UC me **talaaq ka indraj** karwaya jata hai aur NADRA record me status update hota hai — is ke liye talaq-nama aur CNIC ke saath UC jayen.

**Dusri shadi (doosri biwi) ka indraj kaise hota hai?**
Doosri shadi ka indraj bhi **usi tareeqe** se hota hai — alag Nikah Nama, alag MRC. Pehli shadi ke certificate se koi lena dena nahi.

**Kya certificate ke baghair bivi ka passport ban sakta hai?**
Nahi — spouse ka naam passport par darj karwane ke liye **MRC lazmi** hai.

## Waqt Par Karne Ka Sunehra Usool

Marriage registration me **der karna mehenga** hai — har guzarta mahine late fee aur mushkil barhata hai. Bas **do kaam** yad rakhein:

1. **Nikah ke 30 din ke andar UC me indraj** — is aik kaam se sab kuch shuru hota hai.
2. **MRC milte hi CNIC update** — donon ka match hona har agle kaam (passport, visa, bank) me asani deta hai.

Aur aik ghar-elu mashwara: tamam documents ki **aik file (folder)** bana kar rakhein — Nikah Nama, MRC, CNICs, photographs. Har kaam par yehi folder kaam ayega, aur emergency me dhondhna nahi parega.

## Aik Sunehri Qaaida

Apne tamam **documents ki aik tasveer mobile me** aur **aik email draft me** rakho — waqt aane par saal ke chakkar maheenon ke chakkar se behtar hain.
