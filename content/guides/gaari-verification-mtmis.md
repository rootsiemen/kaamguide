## Gaari Verify Karna Kyun Lazmi Hai

Pakistan me second-hand gaari ya bike khareedte waqt sab se bara dhoka **kagzaat ka hota hai** — chori ki gaari, duplicate number plate, ya bechne wala asal malik hi nahi. In mushkilon se bachne ka aik hi asaan tareeqa hai: **khareedne se pehle gaari online verify kar lein** — ye bilkul **muft** hai aur 2 minute lagte hain.

Excise & Taxation Department ka **MTMIS** (Motor Transport Management Information System) aap ko ye maloomat deta hai:

- Gaari ka **registered malik** (naam)
- **Make/model, engine number, chassis number**
- **Token tax** ada hai ya baqaya hai — aur kitna
- Registration ki tareekh aur status

Agar ye maloomat bechne wale ke dawe se na milein — **sauda wahi rok dein**.

> **Note:** Is guide ke tareeqe aur SMS codes Excise departments ke official channels se liye gaye hain aur **October 2026 me check kiye gaye the**. Codes aur portals kabhi tabdeel ho sakte hain — apne soobe ki Excise website se confirm kar lein.

## Tareeqa 1: MTMIS Punjab Portal (Online, Muft)

Punjab me registered gaari/bike ke liye:

1. **mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk** kholein — **yehi official website hai**. Isi naam se milti-julti bohat si nakli sites hain jo data mangti ya paise leti hain — un se bachein.
2. **Online Vehicle Verification** ka option chunein.
3. Gaari ka **registration number** darj karein (number plate wala — masalan LHR-1234).
4. **Search** par click karein — record screen par aa jayega.
5. Record ko gaari ke **asal documents (registration book / smart card)** se mila lein: malik ka naam, engine number, chassis number.

**Verification bilkul muft hai** — koi site ya agent is ke paise mange to samajh jayen dhoka hai.

## Tareeqa 2: SMS Se Verification (Internet Ke Baghair)

Mobile se gaari ka registration number likh kar apne soobe ke code par bhej dein:

| Sooba | SMS Code |
| --- | --- |
| **Punjab** | **8785** |
| **Sindh** | **8147** |
| **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** | **9818** |
| **Islamabad (ICT)** | **8521** |

- Number **bina dash ke** likhein (masalan LHR1234) — agar jawab na aye to 4-hinson wali shakal try karein (LHR0123).
- Jawaab me malik ka naam aur gaari ki bunyadi tafseel aa jati hai.
- SMS par aap ke network ka **aam SMS charge** lagega, is ke ilawa koi fee nahi.

## Tareeqa 3: Excise Office / e-Khidmat Se Tasdeeq

Online record par shak ho ya mukammal tasalli chahiye ho to:

1. Apne zila ke **Excise & Taxation office** ya **e-Khidmat Markaz** jayen.
2. Apna CNIC, gaari ki **registration book/smart card** aur number le jayen.
3. Wahan se official verification ho jati hai — Police Khidmat Markaz par **vehicle verification** ki khidmat bhi hai jis me police record (chori/FIR) bhi check hota hai — motorcycle ke liye Rs 500 aur degar gaariyon ke liye Rs 2,000 fee hai (PKM ke published rates ke mutabiq; office se confirm kar lein).

## Second-Hand Gaari Khareedne Ki Mukammal Checklist

Sirf online verification kaafi nahi — ye poori checklist follow karein:

1. **Malik match:** MTMIS par malik ka naam bechne wale ke CNIC se milna chahiye. Na mile to bechne wale se **transfer letter/authority** mangein — baghair kagaz ke "main hi malik hun" par aitbaar na karein.
2. **Engine/Chassis number:** Gaari par asal engraved engine aur chassis number registration book se mila lein — **aik digit ka farq bhi sauda rokne ki wajah hai**.
3. **Token tax:** MTMIS par token tax ka status dekhein — baqaya token tax khareedne ke baad **aap ke sar** par jayega. Bechne wale se pehle ada karwayen ya qeemat me adjust kar lein.
4. **Registration book/smart card asal hai?** Nakli book aam hai — Excise office se tasdeeq karwa lein.
5. **Bank lease:** Gaari bank se leased ho to **NOC** ke baghair transfer mumkin nahi — pehle lease clear karwayen.
6. **Police record:** Bari rakam ki gaari ho to PKM verification (Rs 2,000) karwa leina behtar hai — chori ki gaari khareedna aap ko qanooni mushkil me dal sakta hai.
7. **Transfer foran apne naam:** Sauda hote hi **usi hafte transfer** karwa lein — khuli transfer par gaari aap ke naam nahi hoti, jurm/masla bechne wale ke record se judta rahega.

## "No Record Found" Aye To Kya Karein

- Pehle **spelling aur number ka format** dobara check karein.
- Confirm karein ke aap **sahi soobe** ka portal/code istemal kar rahe hain — Punjab ki gaari Sindh ke code par nahi milegi.
- SMS wala tareeqa bhi try karein.
- Agar dono par kuch na mile — **gaari na khareedin** jab tak Excise office se tasdeeq na ho jaye. Asal record ka na milna bara khatra hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya gaari verification muft hai?**
Ji haan — online portal aur SMS (aam SMS charge ke ilawa) bilkul muft hai.

**Kya CNIC se gaari check ho sakti hai?**
Online portal par aam tor par **registration number** chahiye hota hai. CNIC se tafseeli check Excise office se hota hai.

**Kya MTMIS chori ki gaari bata deta hai?**
MTMIS Excise record deta hai — **police record ke liye** Police Khidmat Markaz ki vehicle verification karwayen.

**Number plate purani ho (computerized nahi) to?**
Purani series wali gaariyan bhi MTMIS me hoti hain — phir bhi record na mile to Excise office jayen.

**Kya me apni gaari ka token tax online ada kar sakta hun?**
Ji haan — Punjab me **ePay Punjab** app ya bank se token tax online ada ho sakta hai; MTMIS par status bhi nazar aata hai.

**Doosre soobe ki gaari Punjab me khareed sakte hain?**
Khareed sakte hain lekin transfer ke liye **NOC aur mutaliqa soobe ka process** lagega — pehle Excise office se poora tareeqa maloom kar lein.

## Gaari Khareedne Se Pehle Ke In "Signal" Jin Par Foran Ruk Jayen

Verification ke doran ye alamat nazar ayen to **sauda usi laamhe rok dein** — lakhon ki bachat ho jayegi:

1. **Malik ka naam verification me alag, bechne wala alag** aur koi wajah/kagaz nahi.
2. **Engine ya chassis number ghissa hua / dobara laga hua** — ye sab se khatarnaak alamat hai.
3. **Registration book "gum gayi hai"** aur sirf letter par sauda — pehle duplicate book nikalwayen, phir baat karein.
4. **Token tax kai saal ka baqaya** lekin bechne wala "aap de dena" kahe — raqam bohat ho sakti hai, pehle MTMIS par hisab kar lein.
5. **Qeemat market se bohat kam** — sasti gaari aksar kagazon ki musiibat ke saath aati hai.

**Aik usool yaad rakhein:** Jo sauda jaldi me karwaya jaye, wahi aksar dhoka hota hai. Verification par 30 minute lagana lakhon bachata hai.

## Apni Gaari Ka Token Tax Status Check Karna

Apni gaari ke token tax ka baqaya bhi MTMIS par nazar aata hai:

1. Portal par apni gaari ka number darj karein.
2. Record me **"Tax Paid Upto"** dekhein — kis saal tak ada hai.
3. Baqaya ho to **ePay Punjab app** ya bank se online ada kar dein — late fee barhne se pehle.

Challan aur zabti se bachne ka yehi asaan tareeqa hai — saal me aik dafa check kar lein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Mazeed Sawal

**Gaari ki verification se kya pata nahi chalta?**
MTMIS se accident history, gaari ki asal halat aur price kuch maloom nahi hota — ye sirf **Excise kagazat** ki tasdeeq hai. Gaari ki mechanical halat ke liye apna mistri lazmi le jayein.

**Kya bechne wala online record dikhane se inkar kare to?**
Ye khud aik **bara red flag** hai — jis ke kagaz saaf hon, wo verification se nahi darta. Aisi gaari chod dein.

**Smart card gum ho gaya to verification hoga?**
Ji haan — verification sirf **registration number** se hoti hai, card ki zaroorat nahi. Card ke liye Excise se duplicate ki alag application hoti hai.

**Number plate par number aur record ka number alag ho to?**
Aisi gaari **bilkul na khareedin** — ye duplicate plate ya ghair-qanooni tabdeeli ki alamat ho sakti hai. Pehle Excise office se written tasdeeq, phir sochye ga.

**Verification ki screenshot rakhni chahiye?**
Bohat behtar hai — sauda karne se pehle verification ka **screenshot tareekh ke saath** bacha lein. Baad me dispute hua to ye aap ke "due diligence" ka saboot hai.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya MTMIS record me malik ka phone number milta hai?**
Nahi — MTMIS sirf naam aur gaari ki tafseel dikhata hai, phone number ya address **privacy** ki wajah se public nahi hota.

**Verification kitni dafa kar sakte hain?**
Jitni dafa chahen — koi limit nahi, aur ye hamesha muft hai.

**Record update hone me kitna waqt lagta hai?**
Transfer ke baad MTMIS record aam tor par **kuch din** me update ho jata hai. Na ho to Excise office se rabta karein.

## Aik Aadat Jo Lakhon Bachati Hai

Second-hand gaari len se pehle **hamesha ye 3 kaam karein** — chahe bechne wala kitna hi purana jaanne wala kyun na ho:

1. **MTMIS verification** — 2 minute, muft.
2. **Engine/chassis milana** — 5 minute, aankhon se.
3. **Token tax status** — 1 minute, MTMIS par hi.

Kul **8 minute** ka kaam lakhon ka nuqsan rok sakta hai. Aur yaad rakhein — verification karne par bechne wala naraaz ho to samajh jayein ke sauda us se **zyada** ahem hai jitna wo jata raha hai.
