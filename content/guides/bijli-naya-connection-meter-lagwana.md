## Naya Bijli Connection Kya Hai Aur Kab Chahiye Hota Hai

Naya bijli connection ka matlab hai ke kisi makan, dukaan, factory ya khet ke liye pehli dafa bijli ka meter lagwaya jaye aur us jaga ko bijli ki supply se jora jaye. Jab ap naya ghar banate hain, nayi dukaan kholte hain, purani jaga par pehli dafa meter lagwana hota hai ya kisi plot par tameer ke baad bijli chahiye hoti hai, to naya connection lena parta hai. Ye kaam apke ilaqe ki bijli taqseem karne wali company (DISCO) karti hai — jaise Lahore me LESCO, Islamabad/Rawalpindi me IESCO, Faisalabad me FESCO, Multan me MEPCO, Gujranwala me GEPCO, Hyderabad me HESCO, Sukkur me SEPCO, Peshawar me PESCO aur Quetta me QESCO. Karachi ka nizam alag hai, wahan K-Electric hai.

Aik baat pehle se wazeh kar lein: naya connection lena, purane connection ka naam tabdeel karwana aur load barhana teen alag kaam hain. Agar apke naam par pehle se meter laga hua hai aur sirf naam tabdeel karwana hai to wo alag darkhwast hoti hai. Is guide me hum sirf naye connection ka tareeqa cover karenge — jahan pehle koi meter na ho. Jab connection lag jaye to uska bill check karne ka tareeqa [bijli ka bill online check](/guides/bijli-ka-bill-online-check) wali guide me diya gaya hai, aur gas ke connection ya bill ke liye [gas ka bill online check](/guides/gas-ka-bill-online-check) wali guide dekhein.

## Kaunsa Connection Lena Hai: Pehle Ye Faisla Karein

Apply karne se pehle ye tay karein ke apko kis qisam ka connection chahiye, kyunke form me yehi chuna hota hai aur ghalat qisam chunne par darkhwast wapas ho sakti hai:

- **Domestic (ghar ka) connection:** Rehaishi makanon ke liye. Aam ghar ke liye single phase meter kaafi hota hai.
- **Commercial (tijarti) connection:** Dukaan, office, workshop ya kisi karobar ki jaga ke liye.
- **Industrial connection:** Factory ya bari machinery wali jaga ke liye, jis me zyada load hota hai.
- **Agriculture / Tubewell connection:** Kheti ya tubewell ke liye.
- **Temporary connection:** Tameer ke doran ya kisi arzi kaam ke liye, jo mehdood muddat ke liye hota hai.

Is ke saath load bhi tay karna hota hai — aam ghar ke liye 5 kilowatt tak ka load kaafi hota hai, jabke bara ghar ya dukaan ke liye zyada load chahiye hota hai. Load ka andaza apke istemal hone wale alat se lagaya jata hai — fridge, AC, motor, pankhe aur battiyan mila kar. Agar load kam likhwayenge to meter bar bar trip karega, aur zyada likhwayenge to demand notice ki rakam barh jayegi. Is liye load ka andaza durust lagayen, ya electrician se mashwara kar lein.

Single phase aur three phase ka farq bhi samajh lein: aam ghar me single phase meter lagta hai, jabke bari imarat, factory ya zyada load wali jaga par three phase meter lagta hai. Three phase ki demand notice ki rakam single phase se zyada hoti hai, kyunke is me zyada saman aur kaam lagta hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Apply Karne Se Pehle Tayyar Rakhein

Naye connection ke liye darkhwast ke saath ye kagzaat lagte hain. Kagzaat na-mukammal hone par darkhwast wapas ho jati hai, is liye apply karne se pehle sab kuch tayyar rakhein:

| Kagaz | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| CNIC ki copy | Darkhwast guzar ka shanakhti card (front aur back) |
| Milkiyat ka saboot | Registry, sale deed, allotment letter ya fard — jo sabit kare ke jaga apki hai |
| Kirayedar hone ki surat me NOC | Malik makan ka no-objection certificate, agar jaga kiraye par hai |
| Parosi ka bijli ka bill | Paros ke meter ka bill ya reference number — is se apki jaga ki location verify hoti hai |
| Wiring test report | Licensed electrician ya wiring contractor ki report ke wiring mehfooz hai |
| Gawah ka CNIC | Baaz cases me gawah ka CNIC bhi manga jata hai |
| Site plan | Jaga ka naqsha jis par meter lagane ki jaga nishan ho |
| Undertaking | Ye tehreeri bayan ke di gayi maloomat durust hain aur company ke qawaneen ki pabandi ki jayegi |

Milkiyat ke saboot ke tor par fard ka istemal hota hai — fard nikalwane ka tareeqa [fard-e-malkiat aur property transfer](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) wali guide me tafseel se diya gaya hai. Agar apki jaga ka fard nahi bana hua to pehle wo banwa lein, warna connection ki darkhwast atak sakti hai. Kirayedar ke liye malik ka NOC lazmi hai; baghair NOC ke kirayedar ke naam connection nahi lagta.

## Fees: Demand Notice Hi Hatmi Raqam Hai

Naye connection ki fee ke bare me sab se ahem baat ye hai ke iski koi aik fixed rakam nahi hoti. Internet par mukhtalif websites mukhtalif andaze batati hain — koi saat hazar kehta hai, koi lakh se zyada — lekin ye sab andaze hain. Asal tareeqa ye hai: apki darkhwast ke baad company ka amla apki jaga ka survey karta hai, load aur doori ka andaza lagata hai, aur phir apko aik **demand notice** jari karta hai jis me hatmi rakam likhi hoti hai. Wahi rakam apko bank me jama karwani hoti hai. Demand notice milne se pehle kisi bhi rakam par yaqeen na karein aur kisi agent ko advance paisay na dein.

Fee kin cheezon par munhasir hoti hai:

- **Connection ki qisam:** Domestic sab se sasta, phir commercial, phir industrial.
- **Load:** Jitna zyada load, utni zyada rakam.
- **Single phase ya three phase:** Three phase ki rakam zyada hoti hai.
- **Meter aur saman ki qeemat:** Meter, cable aur installation ka kharcha demand notice me shamil hota hai.
- **Jaga ki doori:** Agar khambe ya transformer door hai to kharcha barh sakta hai.

Demand notice ki rakam bank me jama karwane ke baad uski raseed sambhal kar rakhein aur uski copy mutaliqa office me jama karwayen. Raseed ke baghair meter nahi lagta. Agar demand notice ki rakam apko ghalat lage to mutaliqa sub-division office se uski tafseel pooch sakte hain — har cheez ki tafseel demand notice par darj hoti hai.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa: ENC Portal Se Online Apply

Pakistan me naye connection ke liye ab aik markazi online portal hai — **ENC (Electricity New Connection)** — jis ka pata enc.com.pk hai. Isi portal se tamam DISCOs ke liye darkhwast di jati hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

1. **ENC portal kholein.** enc.com.pk par jayen. Agar ap IESCO ya FESCO ke ilaqe me hain to portal apko unke alag address par bhej dega (iesco.enc.com.pk ya fesco.enc.com.pk) — yehi official tareeqa hai, ghabrayen nahi.
2. **"Apply" par click karein.** Left side ke menu se Apply chunein. Pehle apni company chunein (jaise LESCO), phir apna sub-division chunein, phir connection ki category chunein (domestic ke liye 15 kW tak ki category aam hai).
3. **Parosi ka reference number darj karein.** Form me parosi ke meter ka reference number manga jata hai — ye apke parosi ke bijli ke bill par likha hota hai. Is se apki jaga ki location verify hoti hai.
4. **Zaati maloomat bharein.** Apna naam, walid ka naam, CNIC number, mobile number, email aur pata darj karein. Ye bhi batayen ke ap malik hain ya kirayedar.
5. **Jaga ki tafseel bharein.** Jahan meter lagwana hai us jaga ka pata, pehle se lage hue meters ki tadad (agar koi ho) aur matlooba load darj karein.
6. **Kagzaat upload karein.** CNIC, milkiyat ka saboot, parosi ka bill, wiring report aur baqi kagzaat ki scanned copies upload karein.
7. **Submit karein aur application download karein.** Submit karne ke baad apko aik tracking ID milegi. Application ka print ya PDF sambhal kar rakhein — isi tracking ID se ap apni darkhwast ka status check kar sakenge.

Online apply karne ke baad bhi apko mutaliqa office me kagzaat ki asal copies dikhani ya jama karwani par sakti hain — portal par di gayi hidayat ke mutabiq amal karein. Agar online apply me koi mushkil aye to apne ilaqe ke sub-division office se rabta karein; wahan ka amla form bharne me madad karne ka paband hai.

## Office Ja Kar Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

Agar ap online apply nahi karna chahte to yehi kaam office ja kar bhi ho sakta hai:

1. Apne ilaqe ke sub-division office ya customer service center jayen. Application aur power supply contract ka form wahan muft milta hai, aur LESCO ki website se bhi download kiya ja sakta hai.
2. Form bharein aur tamam kagzaat saath lagayen. Office ka amla form bharne me madad karne ka paband hai — agar koi shak ho to poochein, andaze se na bharein.
3. Form jama karwane par apko acknowledgement receipt milni chahiye. Ye receipt sambhal kar rakhein, isi se ap apni darkhwast ka hawala de sakenge.
4. Is ke baad company ka amla apki jaga ka survey karega, phir demand notice jari hoga. Demand notice ki rakam bank me jama karwayen aur raseed ki copy office me jama karwayen.
5. Raseed ki tasdeeq ke baad meter lagane ki team apki jaga par ayegi aur meter nasb kar degi. Meter lagte waqt wiring ki jaanch bhi ki jati hai, is liye wiring pehle se mukammal aur mehfooz honi chahiye.

## Tracking Aur Time Kitna Lagta Hai

Darkhwast ka status ENC portal par apni tracking ID ya CNIC number dal kar check kiya ja sakta hai. Aam taur par darkhwast in marahil se guzarti hai: Submitted, phir survey mukammal, phir demand notice jari, phir demand notice ki payment, aur aakhir me meter nasb. Har marhale par status portal par update hota hai.

Time kitna lagega, ye connection ki qisam aur load par munhasir hai. Chote domestic connection aam taur par kuch hafton me lag jate hain, jabke bare ya industrial connection me survey, saman aur kaam zyada hone ki wajah se zyada waqt lag sakta hai. Agar apki darkhwast kisi marhale par zyada arsa ruk jaye to apni tracking ID ke saath sub-division office se rabta karein, ya LESCO ke ilaqe me helpline 111-000-118 par call karein.

Aik aur zaroori baat: demand notice ki muddat ka khayal rakhein. Agar demand notice ki rakam waqt par jama na karwayi jaye to darkhwast khatam ho sakti hai aur dobara apply karna par sakta hai. Is liye demand notice milte hi uski rakam jama karwayen aur raseed ki copy foran office me jama karwayen.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

- **Ghalat DISCO ya sub-division chunna:** Apni jaga ke mutabiq company aur sub-division chunein. Ghalat sub-division me darkhwast dena waqt ka zaya hai.
- **Na-mukammal kagzaat:** Milkiyat ka saboot ya wiring report na hone par darkhwast wapas ho jati hai. Apply karne se pehle checklist mukammal karein.
- **Load ka ghalat andaza:** Kam load likhwane par meter trip karega aur baad me load barhane ki alag darkhwast deni hogi. Load ka andaza durust lagayen.
- **Agent ko paisay dena:** Koi agent apka connection "jaldi" nahi lagwa sakta. Jo demand notice se pehle paisay mange, wo fraud hai. Sirf demand notice ki rakam bank me jama karein.
- **Parosi ka ghalat reference number:** Form me parosi ka reference number durust likhein, warna location ki verification atak jati hai.
- **Demand notice ki raseed na jama karwana:** Bank me paisay jama karwana kaafi nahi — raseed ki copy office me jama karwana lazmi hai, warna meter nahi lagta.
- **Wiring adhoori chorna:** Meter lagane wali team wiring check karti hai. Agar wiring ghair mehfooz hui to meter nahi lagega aur dobara visit ka intizar karna hoga.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Naya bijli connection lene ke liye kahan apply karein?**

Pakistan me naye connection ke liye markazi online portal ENC hai (enc.com.pk), jahan apni DISCO (jaise LESCO, IESCO, FESCO) chun kar apply kiya jata hai. IESCO aur FESCO ke apne alag ENC address hain. Is ke ilawa apne ilaqe ke sub-division office ja kar bhi apply kiya ja sakta hai.

**Naye connection ki fee kitni hai?**

Iski koi aik fixed rakam nahi hai. Apki darkhwast aur jaga ke survey ke baad company aik demand notice jari karti hai jis me hatmi rakam likhi hoti hai — wahi rakam ada karni hoti hai. Demand notice se pehle internet par batayi gayi koi bhi rakam sirf andaza hai, us par bharosa na karein.

**Kirayedar apne naam par connection le sakta hai?**

Ji haan, lekin is ke liye malik makan ka NOC lazmi hai. Baghair NOC ke kirayedar ke naam connection nahi lagta. NOC ke saath kiraye ka agreement bhi lagana behtar hota hai.

**Darkhwast ka status kaise check karein?**

ENC portal par apni tracking ID ya CNIC number dal kar status check kiya ja sakta hai. LESCO ke ilaqe me helpline 111-000-118 par bhi maloomat li ja sakti hai.

**Meter lagne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**

Ye connection ki qisam aur load par munhasir hai. Chota domestic connection aam taur par kuch hafton me lag jata hai, jabke bara ya industrial connection me zyada waqt lag sakta hai. Demand notice ki rakam waqt par jama karwana sab se ahem hai, warna darkhwast khatam ho sakti hai.

*Note: Is guide ka tareeqa aur portal ki maloomat ENC ke official portal (enc.com.pk) se li gayi hai, jo 25 October 2026 ko live check kiya gaya tha, aur LESCO ki official website (lesco.gov.pk) se. Connection ki hatmi fee hamesha apke demand notice par darj hoti hai — apply karne se pehle apne ilaqe ke sub-division office ya helpline 111-000-118 (LESCO) se tasdeeq kar lein.*
