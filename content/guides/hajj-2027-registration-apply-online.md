## Pehli Baat: Hajj 2027 Registration Kya Hai Aur Nayi 4 Saal Ki Policy Kya Badal Gayi Hai

**Hajj 2027 registration** Pakistan me Hajj par jane ka pehla aur sab se ahem qadam hai. Pehle har saal alag se darkhwastein mangi jati thin, form bank me jama hota tha aur phir qurandazi (balloting) ka intezar hota tha. Ab ye nizam badal gaya hai. Wafaqi cabinet ne **7 July 2026 ko 4 saal ki Hajj Policy 2027–2030** manzoor ki hai, jis ke tehet registration ka nizam mukammal tor par digital kar diya gaya hai. Aap aik dafa register karte hain aur phir **2027, 2028, 2029 ya 2030 — kisi bhi aik saal me Hajj** ada kar sakte hain.

Is naye nizam ki sab se bari khaasiyat ye hai ke **registration muft hai aur is ki koi muqarrara aakhri tareekh nahi hai**. Registration **22 June 2026 se khuli hui hai** aur ye **pehle aao, pehle pao (first come, first served)** ki bunyad par quota bharne tak chalegi. Is ka matlab ye hai ke jo jitni jaldi register karega, us ki jagah utni hi mehfooz hogi — aakhri tareekh ka intezar karna yahan nuqsan deh ho sakta hai, kyun ke quota bharte hi registration band ho jayegi.

Is guide me aap ko official portal **hajj.mora.gov.pk** aur **Pak Hajj App** se registration ka step-by-step tareeqa, eligibility, zaroori kagzaat, fee aur status check ka mukammal tareeqa milega. Aik baat pehle se saaf kar dein: ye guide **sarkari Hajj scheme** ke liye hai, jo Ministry of Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony chalati hai. Private Hajj scheme alag hoti hai, jo licensed Hajj Group Organizers (HGOs) ke zariye hoti hai — us ka tareeqa is guide me nahi hai.

Internet par Hajj ke bare me parhte waqt sab se bari ghalti saal ki confusion hai, is liye is baat ko saaf samajh lein. **Hajj 2026 guzar chuka hai** — woh May/June 2026 me ada ho gaya. Aaj internet par bohat si websites par ab bhi "Hajj 2026 application" ki purani tareekhein nazar aati hain, jin me November–December 2026 ki dates aur qurandazi ki tareekh likhi hoti hai. **Ye maloomat purane cycle ki hain** — in par bharosa karke apni registration na rokein.

Maujooda registration **Hajj 2027 aur is ke baad ke saalon** ke liye hai, nayi 4 saal ki policy ke tehet. Jo bhi tareekh ya fee aap kisi website par parhein, us ki tasdeeq sirf official portal **hajj.mora.gov.pk** ya Ministry ki website **mora.gov.pk** se karein. Kisi agent, WhatsApp group ya ghair-sarkari website ki batayi hui tareekh par yaqeen na karein — aur kisi shakhs ko registration ke naam par paise bilkul na dein, kyun ke registration muft hai.

## Kaun Register Kar Sakta Hai: Eligibility Ki Shartein

Hajj 2027 ke liye registration har Pakistani shehri kar sakta hai jo ye bunyadi shartein poori karta ho:

- **Pakistani shehri** hon aur aap ke paas **valid CNIC ya NICOP** ho. CNIC ki muddat khatam ho to pehle usay renew karwayein — [CNIC banane aur renew karne ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) is guide me dekhein.
- **Machine-readable passport** ho jo safar ki muddat ke baad bhi **kam az kam 8 mahine tak valid** ho. Passport nahi bana to pehle [passport banane ka tareeqa](/guides/pakistan-passport-banane-ka-tareeqa) parhein, aur purana passport renew karwana ho to [passport renewal online](/guides/passport-renewal-online-ka-tareeqa) ki guide dekhein.
- **Active mobile number** ho jo aap ke apne naam par registered ho — is par SMS ke zariye registration aur payment ki ittila aayegi.
- **Email address** ho jo aap khud istemal karte hon — portal par account isi se banta hai aur tamam khutoot isi par aate hain.
- **Sehat ke lihaz se safar ke qabil** hon. Hajj aik mushkil safar hai — lambi paidal chal, garmi aur bheer. Agar koi sangeen bimari hai to doctor se mashwara karke hi aage barhein.
- **Pehle se Hajj ada kar chuke afrad bhi register kar sakte hain**, lekin policy ke tehet pehli dafa jane walon ko tarjeeh di jati hai. Har saal ki policy me is bare me tafseel aa sakti hai, is liye portal par maujooda shartein zaroor check kar lein.

Aik aur aham baat: registration karte waqt aap ko **apne saal ka intekhab** karna hota hai — ke aap 2027 me jana chahte hain ya baad ke kisi saal me. Saal ka intekhab soch-samajh kar karein, kyun ke is se aap ki baari aur package ki tayyari ka schedule taya hota hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kya Kya Tayyar Rakhein

Online registration shuru karne se pehle ye tamam kagzaat aur maloomat tayyar rakh lein. Koi kagaz kam ho to form adhoora reh jata hai, is liye pehle se checklist mukammal kar lein:

- **CNIC ya NICOP** — valid aur saaf copy. Form me CNIC number bilkul waisa hi likhna hai jaisa card par hai.
- **Machine-readable passport** — passport number, jari hone aur khatam hone ki tareekh tayyar rakhein. Passport ki muddat safar ke baad 8 mahine se kam na ho.
- **Haal ki passport-size tasveer** — saaf background wali tasveer, jo portal par upload ke qabil ho.
- **Mobile number aur email address** — dono active hon, kyun ke in par OTP aur confirmation aayegi.
- **Next of Kin ki tafseel** — kisi qareebi rishtedar ka naam, rishta aur mobile number, jo emergency me rabte ke liye diya jata hai.
- **Bank account aur IBAN ki tafseel** — dues ki adayegi aur zaroorat parne par refund ke liye bank ki maloomat mangi jati hai.
- **Group ki maloomat** — agar aap family ya group ke sath jana chahte hain to group ke tamam afrad ki tafseel aik sath deni hoti hai, taa ke sab ki baari aik hi sath aaye.

![Pakistani passport, CNIC card aur safar ke kagzaat — Hajj 2027 registration ke liye zaroori documents](/images/hajj-2027-registration-apply-online-2.jpg)

Sab kagzaat ki **saaf scan copies ya tasveerein (JPG ya PNG)** bana lein, kyun ke online form me yehi upload hongi. Tasveer dhundli ho ya CNIC number parh na raha ho to application ruk sakti hai. Aik mashwara: form bharne se pehle har kagaz ka number aur tareekh apne paas aik kagaz par likh lein taa ke form bharte waqt ghalti na ho.

## Registration Ki Fee Aur Hajj Package Ki Keemat: Kitne Paise Lagenge

Fee ke mamle me do cheezein alag-alag samajhna bohat zaroori hai — **registration** aur **Hajj package ki keemat**. Dono me farq hai:

| Kaam | Fee / Keemat |
|---|---|
| **Hajj 2027 registration (portal ya app par)** | **Muft — koi fee nahi** |
| **Hajj 2027 ka mukammal package** | **Abhi elan nahi hua** — Ministry package ki keemat baad me elan karegi |
| **Dues ki adayegi (jab package elan ho)** | **17-hindsi PSID** ke zariye — mobile banking (1BILL), Easypaisa/JazzCash ya debit/credit card se |

Registration muft hai — is ke liye aap se koi paise nahi liye jate. Form jama karne par system aap ko aik **17 hindson ka Payment System Identifier (PSID)** deta hai, jo aap ki pehchan ka number hota hai. Jab Hajj package ki keemat elan hoti hai aur dues jama karwane ka waqt aata hai, to yehi PSID istemal hota hai. Dues mobile banking apps, Easypaisa ya JazzCash, ya kisi bhi Visa/Mastercard debit ya credit card se jama karwaye ja sakte hain — bank jane ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

Hajj 2027 ke mukammal package ki keemat abhi tak elan nahi hui, is liye is guide me koi andaze ki raqam nahi likhi ja rahi. Guzre saalon ki keemat se andaza lagana ghalat hoga, kyun ke keemat har saal badalti hai. Package elan hote hi ye maloomat official portal aur Ministry ki website par aa jayegi. **Kisi bhi shakhs ya website ki batayi hui package keemat par paise dene se pehle official elan ka intezar karein.**

## Online Registration Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Official portal **hajj.mora.gov.pk** par registration ka tareeqa ye hai:

1. **Official portal kholein.** Apne mobile ya computer par **hajj.mora.gov.pk** kholein. Ye Ministry of Religious Affairs and Interfaith Harmony ka official Hajj Management System hai, jo National Information Technology Board (NITB) ne banaya hai. Kisi doosri website ya link se registration na karein.
2. **Account banayein (Sign Up).** Portal par "Sign Up" par click karein aur apna **email, mobile number ya CNIC** se account banayein. Jo email aur mobile number dein, woh active hon — in par verification code (OTP) aayega.
3. **OTP se tasdeeq karein.** Mobile ya email par aane wala code darj karke apna account verify karein. Code na aaye to spam folder check karein ya dobara bhejne ka button istemal karein.
4. **Login karke form kholein.** Account banne ke baad login karein aur Hajj registration ka form kholein.
5. **Apni tafseel darj karein.** Apna naam (CNIC ke mutabiq), walid ka naam, tareekh-e-paidaish, CNIC number, passport ki tafseel, address aur rabta number bilkul durust likhein. System aap ki maloomat ko **NADRA ke record se milata hai** — koi farq ho to form ruk jayega, is liye spelling aur number teen dafa check kar lein.
6. **Hajj ka saal chunein.** Bataein ke aap 2027, 2028, 2029 ya 2030 me se kis saal Hajj par jana chahte hain.
7. **Next of Kin aur bank ki tafseel dein.** Emergency rabte ke liye qareebi rishtedar ki tafseel aur apne bank account/IBAN ki maloomat darj karein.
8. **Group banayein (agar family ke sath jana ho).** Agar aap group me jana chahte hain to group ke tamam afrad ko aik hi group me shamil karein taa ke sab ki baari aik sath aaye.
9. **Form jama kar dein (Submit).** Sab kuch dobara parh kar submit karein. Jama hote hi aap ko **17 hindson ka PSID** milega, jo SMS aur email par bhi bhej diya jayega. Is number ko mehfooz rakh lein — ye aap ki registration ki pehchan hai.
10. **Status check karte rahein.** Portal par "Check Status" ya "Application Inquiry" se apni application ki haalat waqtan fawaqtan check karte rahein.

## Pak Hajj App Se Registration Ka Tareeqa

Jo log computer istemal nahi karte, un ke liye Ministry ki official **Pak Hajj App** bhi maujood hai, jo Google Play Store aur Apple App Store se muft download ki ja sakti hai. App se registration ka tareeqa portal jaisa hi hai:

1. **Pak Hajj App download karein.** Sirf official app download karein — app ka naam aur developer check karein ke woh Ministry of Religious Affairs se mutaliq ho. Kisi ghair-sarkari app par apni maloomat na dein.
2. **Account banayein ya login karein.** Apne CNIC, mobile number ya email se account banayein aur OTP se verify karein.
3. **Registration form bharein.** App me wahi tafseel dein jo portal par mangi jati hai — CNIC, passport, saal ka intekhab, Next of Kin aur bank ki maloomat.
4. **PSID aur status app par hi milega.** Form jama karne par PSID app par nazar aayega aur SMS par bhi aayega. Baad me dues ki adayegi aur status check bhi app se hi ho sakta hai.

App ka faida ye hai ke tamam ittila — dues ki tareekh, status ki tabdeeli, aur Ministry ke elanat — aap ko notification ke zariye foran mil jati hai. Is liye app install karke notifications on rakhna behtar hai.

## Registration Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai: Status Check Aur Agle Marhale

Registration jama karne ke baad kaam khatam nahi hota — agle marhale ye hain:

- **Status check karte rahein.** Portal **hajj.mora.gov.pk** par login karke "Check Status" se ya Pak Hajj App se apni application ki haalat check karein. Aap ki tafseel NADRA aur mutaliqa idaron se verify ki jati hai.
- **Dues ki adayegi ka elan.** Jab Hajj package ki keemat elan hoti hai to dues jama karwane ki tareekh bhi elan hoti hai. Dues **PSID ke zariye** mobile banking, Easypaisa/JazzCash ya card se jama karwaye jate hain. Dues waqt par jama na karwaye to aap ki jagah kisi aur ko di ja sakti hai.
- **Passport verification.** Aap ka passport check kiya jata hai — is liye passport ki muddat aur tafseel pehle se durust honi chahiye. Passport ki darkhwast ki haalat [passport status tracking online](/guides/passport-status-tracking-online) se bhi check ki ja sakti hai. Passport ki muddat kam ho to abhi renew karwa lein, baad me waqt nahi milega.
- **Group aur baari ka elan.** Group ki baari aur safar ki tafseel Ministry ki taraf se elan hoti hai. Is ke baad training, vaccination aur safar ki tayyari ke marhale shuru hote hain.
- **Private scheme alag hai.** Agar aap sarkari scheme ke bajaye private Hajj karna chahte hain to sirf Ministry se licensed Hajj Group Organizer (HGO) se rabta karein. Baghair license ke kisi tour operator ko paise dena khatarnak hai.

Aik aur baat yaad rakhein: **registration karna Hajj ki pakki seat ki zamanat nahi hai**. Ye pehla qadam hai — seat quota, dues ki adayegi aur verification ke baad pakki hoti hai. Is liye dues aur elanat par nazar rakhna lazmi hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan: Jin Se Bachna Lazmi Hai

Hajj registration me ye ghaltiyan sab se zyada hoti hain — in se bachein:

- **Purani tareekhon par yaqeen karna.** "Hajj 2026" wali websites ki dates par bharosa karke registration rokna ya ghalat saal me apply karna. Hamesha official portal ki maujooda maloomat dekhein.
- **Agent ko paise dena.** Registration muft hai. Jo shakhs registration ke naam par fee mange, woh dhoka de raha hai. Form khud bharein ya kisi bharose-mand shakhs ki madad lein. Sarkari schemes ki maloomat hamesha official zariye se lein — jaise [BISP 8171 registration check](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) bhi sirf official tareeqe se hoti hai, kisi agent ke zariye nahi.
- **Ghalat CNIC ya passport number likhna.** System NADRA se maloomat milata hai — aik hindsa bhi ghalat ho to application ruk jati hai. Form jama karne se pehle har number teen dafa check karein.
- **Passport ki muddat na dekhna.** Passport safar ke baad 8 mahine se kam valid ho to masla hota hai. Registration se pehle passport ki muddat check karke, zaroorat ho to renew karwa lein.
- **Dues ki tareekh bhool jana.** Registration ke baad dues ki adayegi ki tareekh par nazar na rakhna. Portal aur Pak Hajj App ki notifications on rakhein aur Ministry ke elanat dekhte rahein.
- **Ghair-sarkari app ya website par maloomat dena.** Sirf hajj.mora.gov.pk aur official Pak Hajj App istemal karein. Kisi aur link par CNIC ya passport ki tafseel kabhi na dein.
- **Group alag-alag bharna.** Family ke afrad alag-alag apply kar dein to un ki baari alag aa sakti hai. Family ko aik hi group me register karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Hajj 2027 ki registration ki fee kitni hai?**
Registration bilkul muft hai — portal ya Pak Hajj App par koi fee nahi li jati. Hajj package ki keemat alag cheez hai, jo Ministry baad me elan karegi. Registration ke naam par koi paise mange to woh dhoka hai.

**Hajj 2027 ki registration ki aakhri tareekh kya hai?**
Is ki koi muqarrara aakhri tareekh nahi hai. Registration 22 June 2026 se khuli hui hai aur quota bharne tak chalegi — pehle aao, pehle pao ki bunyad par. Quota bharte hi registration band ho jayegi, is liye jald register karna behtar hai.

**Aik dafa register karke kis saal Hajj par ja sakte hain?**
Nayi 4 saal ki Hajj Policy 2027–2030 ke tehet aik dafa register karke aap 2027, 2028, 2029 ya 2030 me se kisi bhi aik saal me Hajj ada kar sakte hain. Form bharte waqt apne pasandeeda saal ka intekhab karna hota hai.

**Hajj 2027 ke package ki keemat kitni hai?**
Package ki keemat abhi elan nahi hui. Ministry package elan karte waqt tamam tafseel — keemat, dues ki tareekh aur shartein — official portal aur website par jari karegi. Kisi bhi website ya shakhs ki batayi hui keemat par paise dene se pehle official elan ka intezar karein.

**Registration ke baad status kaise check karein?**
Portal hajj.mora.gov.pk par login karke "Check Status" ya "Application Inquiry" se, ya Pak Hajj App se apni application ki haalat check kar sakte hain. PSID number apne paas mehfooz rakhein — status check karte waqt ye kaam aata hai.
