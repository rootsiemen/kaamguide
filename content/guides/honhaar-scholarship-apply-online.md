## Pehli Baat: Honhaar Scholarship Kya Hai Aur Kis Ko Milti Hai

**Honhaar Scholarship** Punjab hukumat ki sab se bari undergraduate scholarship hai, jo Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz ki taraf se shuru ki gayi thi aur Punjab Higher Education Commission (PHEC) ke zariye chalayi ja rahi hai. Is ka maqsad aik hi hai: jo zaheen talib-e-ilm fees ki wajah se parhai chhorne par majboor ho jaye, usay degree poori karne ka mauqa mile — bina kisi qarz ya bojh ke.

Is scholarship ki sab se bari khaasiyat ye hai ke ye **aap ki poori tuition fee** cover karti hai. Matlab agar aap select ho jayein to aap ki degree ki tuition fee hukumat ada karegi, aur ye sahoolat aap ki poori degree (aam tor par 4 se 5 saal) ke liye hoti hai. Ye koi aik dafa ka inaam nahi — ye aap ki parhai ke safar ka sathi hai.

2026 ke round me **50,000 nayi scholarships** di ja rahi hain. Ye scholarship Punjab ke **50 public sector universities**, **13 medical aur dental colleges** aur **359 graduate colleges** ke talaba ke liye hai, aur is me **70 muntakhab (selected) disciplines** shamil hain. Selection sirf merit par hoti hai — na koi sifarish chalti hai, na koi entry test hota hai. Aap ke matric aur intermediate ke numbers hi aap ki merit tay karte hain.

Is guide me aap ko ehliyaat, kagzaat, online apply ka tareeqa, merit formula aur last date — sab kuch aik jagah milega. Aakhri tareekh **19 November 2026** hai, is liye waqt zaya kiye baghair apply karein.

Ye scholarship larkon aur larkiyon — donon ke liye hai, aur is me koi gender quota nahi. Punjab ke domicile holders is ke bunyadi mustahiq hain; kuch seats par dosre ilaqon ke talaba bhi aate hain, lekin pehli tarjeeh Punjab domicile ki hai.

Aik baat saaf kar dein: kuch websites mahana wazifa (stipend) ki raqam bhi likhti hain, lekin PHEC ke portal aur universities ki announcements me is scholarship ki bunyadi sahoolat **tuition fee ki 100% coverage** batayi gayi hai. Is liye is guide me sirf wohi likha gaya hai jo official zariye se confirm hota hai — mahana raqam ke bare me portal ya helpline se tasdeeq kar lein.

## Ehliyaat: Scholarship Ke Liye Kya Shartein Hain

Apply karne se pehle apni ehliyaat in sharton se mila lein. In me se aik bhi shart poori na ho to application reject ho sakti hai:

**1. Punjab domicile:** Aap ke paas **Punjab ka domicile** hona lazmi hai. Domicile aap ki rehaish aur subai shanakht ka saboot hai — agar aap ka domicile abhi tak nahi bana to pehle woh banwayein, phir apply karein. [Domicile certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein.

**2. Admission — selected discipline aur selected idare me:** Aap ka admission kisi **muntakhab (selected) discipline** me, kisi **public sector university, public medical/dental college ya HED college** me hona chahiye. 2026 ke round me ye students apply kar sakte hain:
- **Fresh students** — jin hon ne 2026 me selected degree ka **pehla semester ya pehla saal** shuru kiya ho
- **Continuing students** — jo **dosre semester ya dosre saal** me hon, selected public universities, medical/dental colleges ya HED colleges me

Pehle phases me 3rd se 8th semester ke BS students aur 2nd se 5th year ke MBBS/BDS students bhi shamil kiye gaye thay. Aap kis category me aate hain, ye portal par apna record daal kar zaroor check kar lein, kyun ke har round ki categories thori mukhtalif ho sakti hain.

**3. Ghar ki aamdani (income limit):** Aap ke ghar ki mahana aamdani is had se kam honi chahiye:
- **Public sector HEIs ke liye:** Rs **350,000** mahana se kam
- **Federal / private sector HEIs ke liye:** Rs **500,000** mahana se kam

Aamdani ka saboot **e-stamp paper par affidavit** ki surat me dena hota hai. [Punjab me e-stamp paper online banane ka tareeqa](/guides/punjab-e-stamp-paper-online-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein.

**4. Minimum marks (FSc / Intermediate):** Aap ke intermediate ke numbers is se kam nahi hone chahiye:
- **Medical / dental colleges (MBBS/BDS):** kam az kam **80%** marks
- **Science / engineering subjects:** kam az kam **70%** marks
- **Arts / social sciences:** kam az kam **65%** marks

Continuing students ke liye GPA/CGPA ya annual result ki shart bhi hai — sciences me 70% aur arts me 65%, jab ke MBBS/BDS students ke liye 60%.

**5. Merit formula:** Selection is formula se hoti hai — **Matric ke numbers 30%** aur **Intermediate ke numbers 70%**. Koi entry test nahi hota, aur koi interview bhi nahi. Aap ki merit list aap ke inhi numbers se banti hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Apply Karne Se Pehle Ye Sab Tayyar Rakhein

Form bharne se pehle ye kagzaat scan kar ke ya un ki saaf tasaveer bana kar tayyar rakhein. Aadhe kagzaat ke sath form bharna ghaltiyon ka sabab banta hai:

- **Apna CNIC ya B-Form** — CNIC number aur B-Form number donon kaam aate hain; [CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur [B-Form banane ka tareeqa](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein
- **Punjab domicile certificate** — domicile lazmi hai, is ke baghair application aage nahi barhegi
- **Matric aur Intermediate ki result cards / sanad** — merit inhi se banti hai, is liye numbers durust hon
- **University/college ka admission proof** — admission letter ya fee challan, jis se pata chale ke aap selected discipline me enrolled hain
- **Income affidavit (e-stamp paper par)** — ghar ki mahana aamdani ka halafnama; [income certificate se mutaliqa maloomat](/guides/income-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein
- **Recent passport-size tasveer** — saaf, haliya tasveer upload karein
- **Mobile number aur email address** — dono active hon, kyun ke verification code aur merit list ki khabar inhi par aayegi

**Kagzaat ki tayyari ka mashwara:** Tamam documents ki aik folder bana lein — asal kagzaat bhi aur un ki scanned copies bhi. Merit list me naam aane ke baad **asal kagzaat ke sath verification** ke liye bulaya jata hai, aur us waqt koi kagaz kam ho to selection khatam ho sakti hai. Is liye aaj hi checklist se aik aik cheez mila lein.

## Step-by-Step: Honhaar Scholarship Ke Liye Online Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

Poora process **online** hai — koi hard copy jama nahi karwani, kisi office ke chakkar nahi lagane. Ye steps follow karein:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne browser me official portal **honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk** kholein. Address khud type karein — kisi message ya social media post ke link par click kar ke portal par na jayein, kyun ke nakli websites aap ka data chura sakti hain.

**Step 2 — Account banayein (Register):** Portal par **Register** par click karein. Apna **CNIC ya B-Form number, mobile number aur email address** darj kar ke account banayein. Mobile ya email par aane wala verification code darj kar ke account activate karein.

**Step 3 — Apni tafseel bharein:** Form me apni zaati maloomat (naam, walid ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish, pata), taleemi maloomat (matric aur intermediate ke numbers, board, saal) aur ghar ki aamdani ki tafseel darj karein. Har field me wohi likhein jo aap ke asal kagzaat par hai — aik harf ka farq bhi verification me masla bana sakta hai.

**Step 4 — University aur discipline select karein:** Apni university/college aur discipline ki list me se sahi option chunein. Sirf wohi disciplines nazar aayengi jo is scholarship ke liye muntakhab hain — agar aap ki discipline list me nahi hai to aap is round ke liye eligible nahi hain.

**Step 5 — Kagzaat upload karein:** Upar di gayi list ke tamam kagzaat upload karein — CNIC/B-Form, domicile, result cards, admission proof, income affidavit aur tasveer. Har file saaf aur parhne ke qabil honi chahiye; dhundli tasveer wali file reject ho sakti hai.

**Step 6 — Form submit karein aur number sambhalein:** Sab kuch aik dafa phir parh kar **Submit** karein. Submit ke baad aap ko **application number / tracking ID** milega — isay screenshot le kar ya likh kar sambhal kar rakhein. Isi number se aap apni application ka status aur merit list check karein ge.

**Step 7 — Last date se pehle submit karein:** Application **19 November 2026** se pehle jama karwani hai. Aakhri din ka intezar na karein — aakhri dinon me portal par load zyada hota hai aur website slow ho sakti hai.

## Merit List Aur Selection Kaise Hoti Hai

Form submit karne ke baad selection ka process is tarah chalta hai:

1. **Shortlisting:** PHEC aap ki application ki jaanch karta hai — domicile, discipline, marks aur income, sab kuch check hota hai. Jo applications sharton par poori utarti hain, woh merit list ke liye shortlist hoti hain.
2. **Merit list:** Merit list **30% matric + 70% intermediate** ke formula se banti hai. List aane ki tareekh ka elaan portal par kiya jata hai — is liye portal aur apna email/SMS check karte rahein.
3. **Documents verification:** Shortlist hone ke baad aap ko **asal kagzaat** ke sath verification ke liye bulaya jata hai. Is waqt income affidavit (e-stamp paper par) lazmi dikhana hota hai — agar affidavit missing ho to selection khatam ho sakti hai.
4. **Final selection:** Verification mukammal hone ke baad final list jari hoti hai aur selected students ki tuition fee un ke idare ko ada kar di jati hai.

Merit list kab aayegi, is ki hatmi tareekh portal par elaan hoti hai — is liye kisi bhi ghair-sarkari website ya WhatsApp group ki "list aa gayi" wali khabar par yaqeen na karein, sirf official portal dekhein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Application Rok Deti Hain

- **Aakhri din par apply karna:** 19 November ka intezar na karein — aakhri din portal slow ho sakta hai ya form adhoora reh sakta hai. Aaj hi apply karein.
- **Ghalat income affidavit:** Aamdani ki had se zyada likh dena ya affidavit ke baghair apply karna — dono surat me application reject ho sakti hai. Affidavit e-stamp paper par hona chahiye.
- **Non-selected discipline ya university:** Agar aap ki discipline 70 muntakhab disciplines me nahi hai, to application qabool nahi hogi. Pehle list check karein, phir form bharein.
- **CNIC/B-Form ya numbers me ghalti:** Naam, CNIC number ya matric/inter ke numbers me ghalti verification me pakri jati hai. Form submit karne se pehle har field ko kagzaat se mila lein.
- **Kisi agent ya "fee" wale ko paise dena:** Honhaar Scholarship ki application **bilkul muft** hai. Jo shakhs scholarship dilane ke badle fee, bank transfer ya card details mange, woh PHEC ka numainda nahi — woh fraud hai. Sirf official portal istemal karein.
- **Nakli portal par form bharna:** Sirf **honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk** istemal karein. Milti-julti naam wali kisi aur website par apna CNIC ya documents upload na karein.
- **Application number na sambhalna:** Submit ke baad milne wala application number status check karne ke liye chahiye hota hai. Isay zaya kar diya to baad me pareshani hogi.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Honhaar Scholarship ki last date kya hai?**

2026 ke round ki last date **19 November 2026** hai. Pehle ye 9 November thi, phir PHEC ne ise barha kar 19 November kar diya (portal homepage par 10 November 2026 ko nayi tareekh dikhayi gayi). Is tareekh ke baad applications qabool nahi hongi, is liye is se pehle online apply zaroor kar lein.

**Scholarship me kitni raqam milti hai?**

Ye scholarship aap ki **tuition fee 100%** cover karti hai, aur ye sahoolat poori degree (aam tor par 4–5 saal) ke liye hoti hai. 2026 ke round me **50,000** scholarships di ja rahi hain. Mahana wazife ki koi hatmi raqam official portal par confirm nahi hoti, is liye sirf tuition fee coverage ko hatmi samjhein.

**Honhaar Scholarship ke liye minimum marks kitne chahiye?**

FSc/Intermediate me medical/dental colleges ke liye kam az kam **80%**, science/engineering ke liye **70%** aur arts/social sciences ke liye **65%** marks chahiye. Merit **30% matric + 70% intermediate** ke formula se banti hai. Continuing students ke liye GPA/CGPA ya annual result ki shart bhi hai (sciences 70%, arts 65%, MBBS/BDS 60%).

**Apply karne ki koi fee hai?**

Nahi — Honhaar Scholarship ki application **bilkul muft** hai. PHEC ki taraf se koi fee nahi li jati. Jo shakhs scholarship dilane ke naam par paise mange, woh fraud hai — sirf official portal honhaarscholarship.punjabhec.gov.pk istemal karein aur aisi kisi bhi demand ki ittila helpline par dein.

**Private university ke students apply kar sakte hain?**

Is scholarship ka markaz **public sector universities, public medical/dental colleges aur HED colleges** hain. Income ki had public HEIs ke liye Rs 350,000 mahana aur federal/private HEIs ke liye Rs 500,000 mahana batayi gayi hai — lekin aap ka idara aur discipline dono selected lists me hone chahiye. Apne idare aur discipline ka naam portal par zaroor check karein, phir apply karein.
