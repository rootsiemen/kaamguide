## Police Character Certificate Kab Chahiye Hota Hai

Police character certificate (police clearance certificate) aap ke **saaf record** ka sarkari saboot hai — ye batata hai ke aap ke khilaf koi criminal record nahi. Ye lazmi ya mashroot hota hai:

- **UAE/Gulf visa** ke liye (khaas tor par Pakistani nationals ke liye laazmi)
- **Immigration aur foreign jobs** ke liye
- Kuch **sarkari aur private nokriyon** ke liye
- **Adoption, visa extension aur permanent residency** applications ke liye

Punjab me ye kaam ab bohat asaan hai — **Police Khidmat Markaz (PKM)** se online ya counter par ho jata hai, aur certificate me **QR code** hota hai jis se duniya me kahi bhi verify ho sakta hai.

**Foreign job ya study ke liye ja rahe hain to** character certificate ke sath degree ki tasdeeq bhi mangi jati hai — [HEC attestation process guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) me step-by-step tareeqa maujood hai.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fees Police Khidmat Markaz ke published rates se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. Doosre soobon ke rates mukhtalif hain — apply se pehle pkm.punjab.gov.pk ya apne soobe ke police portal par maujooda fee zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Punjab Me Fee Aur Waqt (Official)

| Channel | Fee | Waqt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PKM counter (kisi bhi zila ka center) | **Rs 500** | **3 working days** |
| Online (PKM portal/app) | Rs 500 + service charges | 3 working days |

Certificate courier se aap ke pate par bhej diya jata hai ya digitally (QR code ke saath) download ho jata hai.

## Sab Se Bari Sahulat: Kisi Bhi Zila Se Apply

Pehle certificate usi zila se banta tha jahan aap **rehe the ya reh chuke the** — logon ko safar karna parta tha. **Ab Punjab ka koi bhi PKM kisi bhi zila ka certificate bana sakta hai:**

**Masalan:** Aap ab Multan me rehte hain lekin pehle Mianwali me rehte the — aap **Multan ke PKM** se hi dono ilaaqon ka certificate apply kar sakte hain. Mianwali jaane ki zaroorat nahi.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

- **Asal CNIC** (ya B-Form) aur copy
- **Asal passport** aur copy (visa ke maqsad ke liye to lazmi)
- **Affidavit** (halaf-nama) — kuch soorat me manga jata hai
- **Authority letter** — agar applicant **bahir mulk** hai aur rishtedaar apply kar raha hai (embassy se attested ya do izzatdar logon se attested; authority letter me rehne ki muddat bhi likhi ho)
- **Photograph** (kuch cases me)

## Tareeqa 1: Online Apply (PKM Portal)

1. **pkm.punjab.gov.pk** par jayen ya **Punjab Police Pakistan** app download karein.
2. Apne **CNIC aur mobile number** se account banayen — SMS par OTP aayega, verify karein.
3. Services me se **"Character Certificate"** chunein.
4. Form bharein — **naam ki spelling CNIC ke harf-ba-harf mutabiq** likhein.
5. Documents scan karke upload karein: **CNIC (front/back), passport (agar visa ke liye hai), tasveer**.
6. Fee **PSID** ke zariye ada karein — bank, ATM, Easypaisa ya JazzCash se.
7. **12-hinson ka tracking number** sambhal kar rakhein — portal/app par status yahi se milega.
8. **3 working days** me certificate tayyar — **download** kar lein ya courier se mangwa lein.

## Tareeqa 2: PKM Counter Par Ja Kar

1. Qareebi **Police Khidmat Markaz** jayen (Punjab ke har zila me hain, Monday se Saturday khule hote hain).
2. Counter par **Character Certificate** ki service kahein.
3. Kagzaat jama karwayen aur **Rs 500** fee ada karein.
4. Raseed aur tracking number lein.
5. **3 working days** me certificate courier se aap ke pate par aa jayega ya portal se download ho jayega.

## Maryam Ki Dastak: Ghar Baithe Khidmat

Punjab ki **Dastak app** se facilitator **ghar aa kar** documents le jata hai aur certificate ghar deliver hota hai — buzurgon aur masroof logon ke liye behtareen option hai. Payment PSID, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya COD se hoti hai.

## Overseas Pakistanis Ke Liye

Bahir rehne walon ke liye teen raste hain:

1. **PKM Global Portal / Consulate:** Punjab, Islamabad, Balochistan waghera ke rehne wale apne **qareebi Pakistani consulate** se PKM ke global portal par apply kar sakte hain — aam tor par **muft ya mamooli** kharcha hota hai; processing lagbhag **2 hafte**.
2. **Authority letter wala tareeqa:** Aap ka koi **khooni rishtedaar** (walid, bhai, behen) Pakistan me aap ki taraf se apply kar sakta hai — authority letter **embassy se stamped** ho ya **do izzatdar logon se attested** ho.
3. Certificate **MOFA attestation** ke baad embassy me jama hota hai (jin mulkon ko chahiye).

## Application Ka Status Check Karna

- **pkm.punjab.gov.pk/public/app/track** par jayen.
- **CNIC, passport number aur district** darj karein.
- Status nazar aa jayega: *Under Review*, *Approved* ya *Rejected* — SMS updates bhi milty hain.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Naam ki spelling:** CNIC se alag spelling likhne par certificate visa me reject ho sakta hai — harf-ba-harf milayein.
2. **Purana address miss karna:** Jahan pehle rahe hain, wahan ka certificate na liya ho to application incomplete samjhi jati hai.
3. **Authority letter ghair-attested:** Bahir se bheja letter attested na ho to counter wapas bhej deta hai.
4. **Waqt ki ghalat andaza:** Visa ki deadline se pehle aakhri dinon me bhagna — kam az kam **2 hafte pehle** apply karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Character certificate kitne din valid hota hai?**
Aam tor par **6 mahine** — is ke baad dobara nikalwana parta hai.

**Kya doosre soobe ke liye bhi yahi tareeqa hai?**
Nahi — Sindh, KPK aur Balochistan me abhi district police office ja kar apply karna parta hai; fees mukhtalif hain (Rs 150-1,000 tak). Islamabad ICT ka system Punjab jaisa hai, fee Rs 1,000 hai.

**Kya koi meri taraf se apply kar sakta hai (main Pakistan me hun)?**
Aam tor par **khud jana parta hai** (biometric ke liye). Bahir rehne walon ke liye authority letter wala tareeqa hai.

**Agar mere khilaf koi case/FIR ho to?**
Certificate par record nazar aayega — is soorat me pehle **wakil se mashwara** karein, certificate wohi sach likhega jo record me hai.

**Certificate urgent chahiye to?**
Punjab me 3 working days hi standard time hai — is se tez official option nahi; agenton ke jhoote wado par paisa zaya na karein.

## Doosre Soobon Ka Mukhtasir Jaiza

Pakistan ke degar hisso me process mukhtalif hai:

| Ilaaqa | Tareeqa | Fee (taqreeban) | Waqt |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Islamabad (ICT)** | PKM system jaisa online | Rs 1,000 | 72 ghante |
| **KPK** | District police office | Rs 1,000 | 5-7 working days |
| **Balochistan** | District police office | Rs 150 | 3 working days |
| **Sindh** | District police office / SSP office | Zila ke hisab se | 5-7 working days |

Fees aur tareeqe soobe ke hisab se badalte rehte hain — apne zila ke police office se pehle confirm kar lein. **FIA ka police clearance certificate** alag hota hai (Rs 3,000-5,000, 7-15 din) — ye un cases me chahiye jahan idarah specifically FIA ki clearance mange.

## Visa Ke Liye Certificate Ka Istemal (Ahem Jhalkian)

- **UAE visa:** Character certificate **lazmi** — certificate ke baad aksar **MOFA attestation** bhi chahiye hoti hai.
- **Saudi / Qatar / Kuwait:** Employer ya embassy ki requirement ke mutabiq certificate + attestation lagti hai.
- **Europe / Canada immigration:** Police clearance **har us mulk se** chahiye jahan aap 6 mahine se zyada rahe hon — sirf Pakistan wala kaafi nahi, lekin Pakistan wala **lazmi** hai.

**Practical mashwara:** Certificate ke baad us ki **2-3 photocopies aur aik scan** bacha lein — attestation ke waqt asal ke saath copies mangi jati hain.

## Certificate Milne Ke Baad Ye Bhoolna Nahi

1. **Certificate ki expiry:** Visa ke liye zyadatar mumalik **6 mahine ke andar** ka certificate mangte hain — visa ki date tay kiye baghair boht pehle na nikalwayen, warna dobara nikalwana parega.
2. **MOFA attestation:** Gulf mumalik ke liye aam tor par certificate par **Foreign Office (MOFA)** ki attestation lazmi hoti hai — ye Islamabad aur camp offices (Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta) se hoti hai, fee mamooli hoti hai.
3. **Copies sambhalen:** Asal certificate ki **scan copy** email me rakhein — attestation aur visa file me copies baar baar lagti hain.
4. **Address history mukammal:** Agar aap ne kai shehron me rehaish rakhi hai, embassy **har period** ka saboot mang sakti hai — apne purane pate aur muddat ki list pehle se bana lein.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya koi meri taraf se certificate le sakta hai?**
Counter par **aap khud** jayen to behtar hai. Bahir mulk hain to authority letter wala tareeqa hai — Pakistan me koi aur aap ka kaam **raseed ke baghair** nahi le sakta.

**Certificate gum ho gaya to?**
PKM portal/app se tracking number ke saath **duplicate** ke liye apply karein — naya QR certificate download ho jayega.

**Certificate me naam ki spelling ghalat ho to?**
Foran **PKM helpdesk ya qareebi center** se correction karwayen — visa file jama karne se pehle sab durust hona chahiye.

## Certificate Ki Hifazat

1. **Asal ko mehfooz jagah** rakhein — attestation aur visa me baar-baar chahiye hota hai.
2. **Scan hamesha saath** — email me aik copy, mobile me aik copy.
3. **Photocopies pehle se** bana lein — har office 2-3 mangta hai.
4. **Lamenaat (lamination)** karwa lein — certificate phat-ta ya kharab nahi hota.

## Yaad Dehani

Visa ho ya nokri — **certificate sab se pehla kagaz** manga jata hai. Usey aise sambhalen jaise passport ko: aik upar scan, aik file, aik dost ke ghar mehfooz copy.
