## Seedha Jawab: Sealed Envelope Kya Hota Hai

Agar aap **Canada ya USA immigration**, ya bahir ki university me admission ke liye apni Pakistani taleem ki **ECA (Educational Credential Assessment)** karwa rahe hain, to **WES (World Education Services)**, **IQAS**, **ICES**, **ICAS** ya **CES** jaisay idaray aap se degree aur transcript ki **HEC-attested copies aik band, sealed envelope** me mangte hain. Is envelope par HEC ki mohar (seal) aur dastakhat hote hain, aur ye is baat ka saboot hota hai ke andar mojood copies asal attested documents ki hi hain aur rastay me badli nahi gayeen. **Envelope aap khud nahi khol sakte** — khula ya phata hua envelope ECA idarah seedha reject kar deta hai.

Sab se ahem baat pehle samajh lein: **sealed envelope attestation ke baad ka marhala hai.** Pehle aap ki asal degree aur transcript HEC se attest hoti hai — poora process online **Degree Attestation System (DAS)** ke zariye **eservices.hec.gov.pk** se hota hai, fee **Rs 3,000 per document** hai — phir un documents ki **attested photocopies** HEC ke official envelope me seal ki jati hain. Asal documents hamesha aap ke paas rehte hain; envelope me sirf attested copies jati hain. Mukammal attestation process ke liye hamari [HEC Degree Attestation guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) parhein.

> **Note:** Is guide me di gayi fee HEC ke online DAS ke rates ke mutabiq hai aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thi**. Application ke waqt portal jo kul amount calculate karke dikhaye, **wohi final** samjhein — payment se pehle portal ka displayed amount lazmi confirm karein.

## Quick English Summary — HEC Sealed Envelope for WES

If you searched for **"HEC sealed envelope for WES"** or **"HEC Pakistan WES ECA documents"**, here is the short version:

1. First get your **original degree and transcript attested by HEC** through the online Degree Attestation System (DAS) at **eservices.hec.gov.pk** — the fee is **Rs 3,000 per document**, paid via 1-Link (1-Bill).
2. Photocopies are attested **only after the originals** have been attested. Request the **sealed envelope option** in your application.
3. HEC places the **attested photocopies** (never your originals) in an official envelope, **seals and signs across the flap**, and hands it to you or your authorized person/courier representative. HEC itself does not dispatch the envelope to WES.
4. **Never open the envelope.** Courier it to your ECA organization (WES Canada/USA, IQAS, ICES, ICAS, CES) using the exact address and your **WES reference number** shown in your WES account.
5. The most common rejections are: an **opened or damaged envelope**, documents sealed **without prior HEC attestation**, a **name mismatch** between your documents and your WES account, and a **wrong or incomplete ECA address**.

## Kis Ko Sealed Envelope Chahiye Hota Hai

- **WES Canada** — Express Entry aur Canadian immigration ke liye ECA; Pakistani degrees ke liye HEC-attested copies sealed envelope me mangta hai
- **WES USA** — America me nokri ya admission ke liye credential evaluation
- **IQAS (Alberta, Canada)**, **ICES (British Columbia)**, **ICAS** aur **CES (University of Toronto)** — Canada ke doosre manzoor-shuda ECA idaray
- Kuch **foreign universities** jo admission ke waqt transcripts seedha issuing authority ke sealed envelope me mangti hain

**Aik confusion door kar lein:** WES ke liye aap ki **Matric aur Inter** ki copies board (BISE) se attest hokar **alag envelope** me jati hain — wo kaam [IBCC attestation](/guides/ibcc-attestation-ka-tareeqa) aur mutaliqa board se hota hai. HEC wala envelope sirf **university degree aur transcript** ke liye hai. Dono envelopes alag alag hote hain, inhein aik me mix na karein.

## Step-by-Step: Sealed Envelope Ka Process

### Step 1: WES/ECA Account Pehle Banayen

1. Apne ECA idare (masalan **WES**) ki website par account bana kar application shuru karein.
2. WES aap ko aik **Reference Number** deta hai — ye number har envelope aur har courier par likhna lazmi hai. Baghair reference number ke bheja gaya envelope WES aap ke account se match nahi kar pata aur process ruk jata hai.
3. WES ki **Document Requirements** wali screen ghor se parhein — wahan likha hota hai ke Pakistan se exactly kaun se documents, kis surat me chahiye hain.

### Step 2: Asal Degree Aur Transcript HEC Se Attest Karwayen

1. **eservices.hec.gov.pk** par account banayen, academic profile (Matric se aakhri degree tak) mukammal karein.
2. **Degree Attestation (DAS)** me degree aur transcript select karein, saaf scans upload karein.
3. Fee 1-Link (1-Bill) se pay karein aur portal par **"Verify Payment"** lazmi karein.
4. HEC aap ke documents **university ko electronic verification** ke liye bhejta hai — ye sab se lamba marhala hai; kul process aam tor par **10-15 working days** leta hai.

### Step 3: Attested Copies Aur Sealed Envelope Ki Request

1. Asal documents attest ho jane ke baad un ki **photocopies ki attestation** ki application karein — copies tab hi attest hoti hain jab asal pehle attest ho chuka ho.
2. Application ke waqt **sealed envelope ka option** select karein / request me wazeh likhein ke copies **WES/ECA ke liye sealed envelope** me chahiye hain.
3. HEC ki **R&I (Receipt & Issue) Section** sirf **HEC-attested photocopies** ko official envelope me rakhti hai — baghair attestation wali copies seal nahi hotin.

### Step 4: Envelope Seal Hona Aur Wasool Karna

1. HEC envelope ko **seal karke flap par dastakhat/mohar** lagata hai — yahi sign-across-the-seal is envelope ki pehchan hai.
2. Envelope **aap ko, aap ke authorized person ko, ya courier company ke representative ko** hand over hota hai. HEC khud WES ko dispatch nahi karta — dispatch ki zimmedari aap ki hai.
3. Envelope lete waqt check karein: seal salim hai, flap par sign/mohar wazeh hai, envelope phata hua ya dobara band kiya hua to nahi lag raha.

### Step 5: Courier Se ECA Idare Ko Bhejein

1. Envelope par **ECA idare ka exact address** likhein jo us ki official website par diya ho — purani listings ya agenton ke diye hue address par na jayen.
2. Apna **WES Reference Number** envelope par aur courier form par lazmi likhein.
3. Koi **reliable international courier** (DHL, FedEx, UPS waghera) use karein aur **tracking number** sambhal kar rakhein.
4. WES account me "Track your package" se delivery confirm karein; documents receive hone ke baad WES un ki review karke ECA report banata hai.

## HEC Envelope Me Kya Seal Karta Hai — Aur Kya Nahi

- **Seal hota hai:** HEC-attested **photocopies** of degree aur transcript (jin copies par HEC ki attestation lagi ho)
- **Seal nahi hota:** aap ke **asal documents** — woh hamesha aap ke paas rehte hain
- **Alag envelope:** Matric/Inter ki copies board/IBCC wala envelope — WES ke liye wo alag se jata hai
- Envelope par HEC ki seal ke ilawa **aap apna kuch na likhein** siwaye courier ke address label ke — envelope ki front/flap par extra likhai se bachein

## Aam Ghaltiyan Aur Rejections

1. **Envelope khol dena:** Sab se aam aur sab se mehngi ghalti. Envelope ghalti se khul jaye to wohi copies dobara attest karwa kar **naya sealed envelope** banana parta hai — khula envelope koi ECA idarah qabool nahi karta.
2. **Attestation se pehle sealing ki koshish:** HEC sirf attested copies seal karta hai. Pehle [HEC attestation](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) mukammal karein, phir envelope.
3. **Naam ka mismatch:** WES account, CNIC aur degree par naam ki spelling **harf-ba-harf aik** honi chahiye. Naam ka farq ho to pehle documents me tas-heeh karwayen — WES name mismatch par documents wapas bhej deta hai.
4. **Reference number na likhna:** Envelope par WES reference number na ho to WES documents ko aap ki file se jor nahi pata — hafte zaya hote hain.
5. **Galat ya purana address:** ECA idaron ke address badalte rehte hain; bhejne se pehle official website se **usi din** address confirm karein.
6. **Deadlines ka hisab na rakhna:** University verification + attestation + courier — kul mila kar **4-6 hafte** ka buffer rakhein. Aakhri dinon me shuru karna Express Entry ki deadline miss karwa sakta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya HEC khud envelope WES ko bhejta hai?**
Nahi. HEC ka R&I Section attested copies ko seal karke envelope aap ko, aap ke authorized person ko ya courier representative ko deta hai. Us ke baad courier se WES/ECA idare ko bhejna aap ki zimmedari hai.

**Envelope ghalti se khul jaye to kya hoga?**
Khula ya damaged envelope WES qabool nahi karta. Aap ko copies ki dobara attestation karwa kar naya sealed envelope banana hoga — is liye envelope ko wasool karte hi mehfooz jagah rakhein aur foran courier kar dein.

**Asal degree bhi envelope me jati hai?**
Nahi. Envelope me sirf HEC-attested **photocopies** jati hain. Asal degree aur transcript hamesha aap ke paas rehte hain.

**Sealed envelope ke liye pehle kya lazmi hai?**
Degree aur transcript ki **HEC attestation** — jo ab online DAS se hoti hai, Rs 3,000 per document. Attestation ka mukammal tareeqa [HEC Degree Attestation guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) me hai. Attested degree ki baad me tasdeeq ka tareeqa [HEC verification guide](/guides/hec-degree-verification-online) me dekhein.

**How do I get an HEC sealed envelope for WES Canada?**
First get your original degree and transcript attested by HEC through the online Degree Attestation System at eservices.hec.gov.pk (Rs 3,000 per document). Then apply for attested photocopies and request the sealed envelope option — HEC's R&I Section seals the attested copies in an official envelope and hands it to you. Write your WES reference number on it and courier it to the WES Canada address shown in your WES account. Never open the envelope yourself; an opened envelope is rejected.

**What happens if my HEC sealed envelope is opened or damaged?**
WES and other ECA organizations reject opened, resealed or damaged envelopes because they can no longer trust that the documents inside are unchanged. You will need fresh attested copies sealed in a new envelope from HEC. If the envelope arrives damaged, contact the ECA organization before anything else and keep the courier tracking and photos as evidence.

**Matric aur Inter ki copies bhi isi envelope me jayengi?**
Nahi. Matric/Inter ki attested copies board aur IBCC ke alag envelope me jati hain. HEC wala envelope sirf university degree aur transcript ke liye hai — tafseel ke liye [IBCC Attestation guide](/guides/ibcc-attestation-ka-tareeqa) parhein.

**Kitna waqt lagta hai poore process me?**
Attestation (university verification samet) aam tor par 10-15 working days, phir copies ki attestation aur sealing, aur Canada/USA courier ke 3-7 din. Kul mila kar **4-6 hafte pehle** shuru karna mehfooz hai.
