## Pehle Ye Samajh Lein: Passport Gum Hone Par Kya Karna Hai

Passport ka kho jana koi choti baat nahi, lekin ghabrane ki bhi zaroorat nahi. Pakistan me khoya hua passport dobara banwane ka kaam Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGIP) karta hai, aur is ka poora process sarkari qawaid ke mutabiq tay hai. Sab se pehli aur sab se zaroori baat ye hai ke jaise hi aap ko maloom ho ke passport gum ho gaya hai, us ki khabar sab se pehle police ko dena hai. Thane me FIR ya gumm-shuda ki report darj karwana usi waqt ka kaam hai jab passport gum ho — der karne par bhi issue hota hai, kyunke koi aur shakhs aap ke passport ka ghalat istemal kar sakta hai.

Dosri zaroori baat: khoye hue passport ka number aap ko kahin likh ke rakhna chahiye tha, lekin agar number yaad nahi ya us ki copy mojood nahi, to Regional Passport Office me tehriki darkhwast de kar apne purane passport ka record hasil kiya ja sakta hai. Teesri baat, jis din se aap ne gumm-shudgi ki report darj ki hai usi din se aap ka khoya hua passport system me "lost" ki haisiyat se mark ho jata hai, taake koi us ka ghalat istemal na kar sake.

Ek aur cheez jo October 2026 me badli hai, usay bhi samajh lijiye. DGIP ne 23 October 2026 se naye applications ke liye e-passport lazmi kar diya hai. Is ka matlab ye hai ke ab jo bhi naya ya khoye hue passport ka reissue hoga, wo e-passport hoga — purani machine-readable passport (MRP) ki nayi kitabche ab jari nahi hongi. Lekin ghabrayein nahi: jinke paas pehle se MRP mojood hai, wo expiry tak chalti rahegi. Fees ka hisab bhi ab e-passport ke naye schedule ke mutabiq lagega, jiska tafseel neeche fee wali section me diya gaya hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: FIR, Affidavit Aur Digar Documents

Khoye hue passport ke liye apply karte waqt aap ke paas ye kagzaat lazmi hone chahiyein:

1. **Police ki FIR ya gumm-shudgi ki report** — ye sab se aham kagaz hai. Report me aap ka naam aur khoye hue passport ka number lazmi likha ho. Agar passport ka number maloom nahi, to pehle Regional Passport Office se purane passport ka number maloom kar lijiye. FIR ki asli copy aur us ki photocopy dono le kar jayein. (Visa ya mulazmat ke liye alag se [police character certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/police-character-certificate) bhi dekhein.)

2. **Khoye hue passport ki copy** — agar aap ke paas purane passport ki photocopy ya us ke pehle do pages ki scan mojood hai, to zaroor le jayein. Copy se verification ka kaam bohot asaan ho jata hai. Agar copy nahi hai, to bhi application ho jayegi, lekin thori der lag sakti hai.

3. **"Report my Loss" ka print** — DGIP ke online portal par passport ki gumm-shudgi ki report darj karne ka option mojood hai. Wahan report darj karne ke baad us ka print le kar office me jayein. Ye step aap ghar baithe bhi kar sakte hain.

4. **Valid CNIC ya NICOP (asl + photocopy)** — NADRA ka asal Computerized National Identity Card ya NICOP, dono taraf ki photocopy ke sath. Agar aap mulk se bahar hain to NICOP chalega. 18 saal se kam umr ke bachon ke liye Child Registration Certificate (B-Form) ya NICOP aur waliden ke CNIC chahiyein honge. Waliden ki shanakht ke liye [FRC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi mufeed reh sakta hai.

5. **Fee ki adaigi ka saboot** — bank ya online tareeqe se fee jama karne ke baad milne wali raseed ya PSID challan ki copy. Fee baghair application aage nahi barhegi.

6. **Doosri nationality ka saboot** — agar aap dual national hain to foreign passport ki copy bhi le jayein.

Aik sawal aksar pucha jata hai ke kya stamp paper par halafnama (affidavit) bhi chahiye? DGIP ke official daftar me stamp paper par halafname ka koi lazmi zikar nahi hai. Zaroori cheez police ki FIR/report aur "Report my Loss" ka record hai. Lekin kuch offices case ki noiyat dekh kar izafi kagaz mang lete hain, is liye apply karne se pehle apne sheher ke Regional Passport Office ki helpline se confirm kar lena behtar hai. Agar aap [CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ya [passport banane ka mukammal tareeqa](/guides/pakistan-passport-banane-ka-tareeqa) pehle se jante hain, to ye process aap ke liye aur bhi asaan ho jayega.

## 2026 Ki Official Fees: Normal, Urgent Aur Lost-Passport Fine

October 2026 se naya passport sirf e-passport ke roop me jari hoga. DGIP ke published schedule ke mutabiq aam (non-lost) e-passport ki fees ye hain:

| E-Passport Category | Normal Fee (Rs) | Urgent Fee (Rs) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 36 pages, 5 saal | 9,000 | 15,000 |
| 36 pages, 10 saal | 13,500 | 22,500 |
| 72 pages, 5 saal | 16,500 | 27,000 |
| 72 pages, 10 saal | 24,750 | 40,500 |

Ab khoye hue passport ki soorat me DGIP ka usool ye hai:

| Khoye hue passport ki dafa | Fee ka usool |
| --- | --- |
| Pehli dafa kho jaye | Normal fee se DOUBLE |
| Dosri dafa kho jaye | Normal fee se QUADRUPLE (chaar guna) |
| Teesri dafa kho jaye | Case DGIP Islamabad ko bheja jayega, wahan se manzoori par issue hoga |

Misal ke taur par, 36 pages aur 5 saal wale e-passport par pehli dafa kho jane ki fee normal fee se double hogi. Lekin dhyan rahe: e-passport ka system 23 October 2026 se abhi-abhi lazmi hua hai, is liye lost-passport ki fine ka exact aakhri raqam application ke waqt official portal confirm karta hai. Fee jama karne se pehle DGIP ki website dgip.gov.pk par mojood fee schedule zaroor dekh lein, ya helpline 051-111-344-777 par call kar ke confirm kar lein. Fee ek dafa jama ho jaye to wo wapas nahi hoti, is liye raqam pakki kar ke hi payment karein.

Fee ki adaigi aaj kal bank branch, online banking, ya PSID challan ke zariye ki jati hai. Aap ko ek 17-digit ka PSID milega, jis se fee track hoti hai. Raseed sambhal kar rakhein — application ke waqt isi ki copy jama hogi.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa: Passport Office Ja Kar Apply Karna

Khoya hua passport dobara banwane ka poora kaam aam taur par Regional Passport Office (RPO) me hota hai. Ye rahe steps:

**Step 1: Police report darj karayein.** Sab se pehle apne ilaqe ke thane me FIR ya gumm-shudgi ki report darj karayein. Report me apna poora naam aur purane passport ka number likhwayein. FIR ki attested copy lein.

**Step 2: DGIP portal par "Report my Loss" darj karein.** Ghar baithe DGIP ke online portal par apne passport ki gumm-shudgi ki report darj karein aur us ka print nikal lein. Ye official record ka hissa hai.

**Step 3: Fee jama karein.** Apne liye munasib category (36 ya 72 pages, 5 ya 10 saal, normal ya urgent) chunte hue DGIP schedule ke mutabiq fee jama karein. Pehli dafa kho jane ki soorat me normal fee se double lagayegi — exact raqam portal confirm karega.

**Step 4: Kagzaat ka set tayyar karein.** FIR ki copy, loss report ka print, CNIC/NICOP ki asli aur photocopy, khoye hue passport ki copy (agar mojood ho), aur fee ki raseed — sab ek file me laga lein.

**Step 5: Regional Passport Office jayein.** Apne sheher ke RPO me token le kar application counter par jayein. Counter par aap ke kagzaat check honge aur form fill karaya jayega.

**Step 6: Biometric aur tasveer.** Office me aap ke fingerprints liye jayenge aur tasveer li jayegi. Ye step lazmi hai — lost passport ka kaam baghair biometric ke aage nahi barhta.

**Step 7: Token sambhal kar rakhein.** Application ke baad aap ko ek 11-digit ka token number milega. Is token se aap apni application ka status track kar sakte hain. Token slip gum na hone dein.

**Step 8: Passport hasil karein.** Processing mukammal hone par naya e-passport aap ke diye hue address par bheja jayega ya office se collect karna hoga — ye option application ke waqt chuna jata hai. DGIP ne TCS ke zariye ghar par delivery ki sahulat bhi di hai.

## Online Tareeqa: Ghar Bethe Lost Passport Ke Liye Apply

Aik aham baat wazeh kar dein: DGIP ka online portal (onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk) bunyadi taur par renewal ke liye bana hai. Khoya hua passport — jahan purani kitabche physical taur par mojood nahi — ke liye poori application online nahi hoti, kyunke biometric capture aur tasveer ke liye aap ko khud office jana padta hai.

Lekin online aap ye do kaam zaroor kar sakte hain:

1. **"Report my Loss" darj karna** — DGIP ke portal par apne passport ki gumm-shudgi ki report online darj ki ja sakti hai. Yehi report print kar ke office me jama hoti hai.

2. **Fee ka PSID generate karna** — online fee jama karne ke liye 17-digit ka PSID challan portal se generate hota hai, jisay aap online banking, mobile wallet ya bank branch me ada kar sakte hain.

Is ke baad documents ka set bana kar Regional Passport Office jana lazmi hai. Mulazmeen ke liye NOC, dual nationals ke liye foreign passport ki copy — ye sab original documents office me hi verify hote hain. Online sirf ibtidai tayyari hoti hai, final application office me hoti hai. Yehi wajah hai ke "lost passport ka poora kaam ghar baithe" ka koi official tareeqa mojood nahi — jo agent aap se online ke naam par paisa mange, us se bach ke rahein.

## Kitna Waqt Lagega: Normal, Urgent, Fast Track

DGIP ke official processing times ye hain:

- **Normal:** lagbhag 14 working days (30 April 2026 se normal delivery ka waqt 21 se ghat kar 14 working days kar diya gaya hai).
- **Urgent:** lagbhag 5 working days.
- **Fast track:** aam taur par 2 working days, lekin DGIP ne wazeh kiya hai ke fast-track delivery e-passport ke liye available nahi hai. Is liye ab lost passport ke reissue par fast track ka option mojood nahi hoga — normal ya urgent me se chunna hoga.

Ye times working days me hain — chuttian (weekend aur sarkari holidays) is me shamil nahi. Application me agar koi kami reh jaye ya verification pending ho jaye to waqt aur bhi lag sakta hai. Khoye hue passport ki soorat me purane passport ka record verify hota hai, is liye renewal se thora zyada waqt lagna normal baat hai.

Status track karne ke liye apna 11-digit token number sambhal kar rakhein aur SMS ya portal ke zariye application ka haal maloom karte rahein. Agar waqt se zyada der ho jaye to RPO ki helpline par apne token number ke sath rabta karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **FIR darj karne me der karna** — passport gum hone ke foran baad police report na karwana sab se bari ghalti hai. Khoye hue passport ka ghalat istemal ho sakta hai, aur report me der ki wazahat bhi mangi ja sakti hai.

2. **Purane passport ka number ya copy na rakhna** — copy ya number mojood na ho to verification me hafte lag sakte hain. Hamesha apne passport ki scan ya copy alag jagah mehfooz rakhein.

3. **Ghalat fee jama karna** — lost passport par fine double lagti hai. Normal fee jama kar ke office jane par application reject ho jayegi aur fee wapas nahi milegi. Fee schedule portal se confirm kar ke hi payment karein.

4. **CNIC ki expiry check na karna** — expired ya naqabil-e-qabool CNIC par application aage nahi barhegi. Pehle apna CNIC valid karwayein. Is ke liye [CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) wali guide dekh lein.

5. **Token slip gum kar dena** — token number baghair aap application track nahi kar sakte. Slip ki tasveer phone me bhi mehfooz rakhein.

6. **Agenton ko paisa dena** — DGIP ka process seedha hai, kisi agent ki zaroorat nahi. Jo shakhs "kaam jaldi karwa dunga" keh kar paisa mange, wo dhoka de raha hai.

7. **Teesri dafa kho jane ko halka lena** — teesri dafa passport kho jane par case seedha DGIP Islamabad jata hai aur naya passport manzoori ke baghair nahi milta. Passport ko hamesha mehfooz jagah par rakhein.

8. **Urgent category chunte waqt fee ka hisab na karna** — urgent fee normal se zyada hoti hai aur lost-passport fine us par bhi apply hoti hai. Budget tay kar ke hi category chunein, aur final raqam portal par confirm karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Khoya hua passport dobara banwane me kul kitna kharcha aata hai?**

Kharcha aap ki chuni hui category (36 ya 72 pages, 5 ya 10 saal, normal ya urgent) aur kho jane ki dafa par munhasir hai. Pehli dafa kho jane par normal fee se double lagti hai. Misal ke taur par 36 pages, 5 saal wale e-passport ki normal fee 9,000 rupay hai, to pehli dafa kho jane par usay double hisab se ada karna hoga. Aakhri raqam application ke waqt official portal confirm karta hai, is liye payment se pehle dgip.gov.pk par fee schedule dekh lein ya helpline 051-111-344-777 par confirm kar lein.

**Kya khoya hua passport baghair FIR ke dobara ban sakta hai?**

Nahi. Police ki FIR ya gumm-shudgi ki report khoye hue passport ke liye lazmi kagaz hai. DGIP ke official usoolon ke mutabiq report me aap ka naam aur khoye hue passport ka number likha hona chahiye. Baghair police report ke application aage nahi barhegi, is liye sab se pehle yehi kaam karein.

**Kya khoye hue passport ke liye online apply ho sakta hai?**

Poori application online nahi hoti. DGIP ke portal par sirf gumm-shudgi ki report ("Report my Loss") darj ki ja sakti hai aur fee ka PSID challan generate kiya ja sakta hai. Biometric capture aur tasveer ke liye aap ko khud Regional Passport Office jana padta hai, is liye final application hamesha office me hi hoti hai.

**Agar purane passport ki copy ya number mojood na ho to kya hoga?**

Application phir bhi ho jayegi, lekin verification me zyada waqt lagega. Aap Regional Passport Office me tehriki darkhwast de kar apne purane passport ka record aur number hasil kar sakte hain — is ke liye aap ke CNIC/NICOP ki copy chahiye hogi. Isi liye mashwara diya jata hai ke hamesha apne passport ki scan ya copy alag se mehfooz rakhein.

**Teesri dafa passport kho jaye to kya hota hai?**

Teesri dafa passport kho jane par Regional Passport Office aap ka case Directorate General of Immigration & Passports, Islamabad ko bhejta hai. Naya passport DGIP ki manzoori ke baghair jari nahi hota. Ye qawaid passport ke ghalat istemal ko rokne ke liye hain. Is soorat me case ki tafseel aur wajohat DGIP ke samne pesh hoti hain aur wahi faisla karta hai.

Ye guide Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (dgip.gov.pk) ki official maloomat ki bunyad par 23 October 2026 ko tayyar ki gayi hai. Fee ya process me tabdeeli ke liye helpline 051-111-344-777 ya dgip.gov.pk check karein.
