## Pehli Baat: Income Certificate Hota Kya Hai Aur Kab Manga Jata Hai

Income certificate aik aisa dastavez hai jo sabit karta hai ke aap ya aap ke khandan ki **mahana ya salana aamdani kitni hai**. Ye certificate un logon ke liye sab se zyada zaroori hai jo **scholarship, zakat, bait-ul-maal ki imdad, fee concession ya kisi welfare scheme** ke liye apply karte hain — kyunke ye schemes sirf mustahiq (zarurat-mand) logon ke liye hoti hain, aur istehqaq ka faisla aamdani dekh kar hota hai.

Misal ke tor par: **PEEF scholarship** ke liye walidain ki mahana aamdani ki hadd muqarrar hai, **Punjab Zakat** ke taleemi wazaif ke liye Local Zakat Committee aamdani check karti hai, aur **Punjab Bait-ul-Maal** ki taleemi imdad ke liye walid/guardian ka income certificate lazmi hai. Is ke ilawa kabhi kabhi visa ya mulazmat ke liye bhi aamdani ka saboot manga jata hai.

> **Ahem wazahat:** Punjab ke e-Khidmat Markaz ki official services list me **koi standalone "Income Certificate" counter mojood nahi** — is liye is guide me wohi do official tareeqe diye gaye hain jo asal me istemal hote hain: mulazim ke liye employer ka certificate, aur ghair-mulazim ke liye stamp paper par affidavit ki attestation. **Fee aur processing time ki koi official figure publish nahi hai**, is liye is guide me koi number invent nahi kiya gaya — sirf verified process diya gaya hai. Maloomat **23 October 2026** ko official sources (PEEF, Punjab Zakat, e-Khidmat) se check ki gayi thin.

## Do Raste: Pehle Ye Faisla Karein Ke Aap Kaunsi Category Me Hain

Income certificate banwane ka tareeqa aap ke **peshe** par munhasir hai — ghalat raste par chalne se waqt zaya hoga:

1. **Mulazim (salaried) shakhs:** Agar aap ya aap ke walid/guardian kisi idare me mulazim hain to sab se asaan aur mazboot tareeqa ye hai ke **apne employer (office/company) se income certificate** lein — jo HR head ya mutaliqa officer ke dastakhat aur idare ki **mohar** se verify ho. PEEF ki official requirement bhi yehi hai: "Income Certificate verified by the Employer".
2. **Self-employed, daily wager ya ghair-mulazim:** Agar koi fixed tankhwah nahi (dukan, mazdoori, kheti-baari waghera) to **stamp paper par income affidavit** banwana hoga — jis me aap halafan apni mahana aamdani zahir karte hain — aur phir usay **Tehsildar, Assistant Commissioner ya Union Council Chairman** se attest karwayein. PEEF khud is affidavit ko qabool karta hai aur us ka official "Income Affidavit" form bhi mojood hai.

Dono suraton me maqsad aik hai: **aamdani ka qabil-e-aitbar saboot** tayyar karna jo mutaliqa idara (PEEF, Zakat Committee, Bait-ul-Maal) qabool kare.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Pehle Se Tayyar Rakhein

Kaam par nikalne se pehle ye kagzaat jama kar lein (PEEF ke official scholarship form ke mutabiq):

- **Applicant ka CNIC** (ya 18 saal se kam umar ho to B-Form) — [B-Form banane ka tareeqa](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa)
- **Walid/Guardian ka CNIC** (jin ki aamdani zahir karni hai)
- **Khandan ke har kamane wale fard ki tafseel:** naam, pesha aur mahana aamdani (PEEF form me is ke liye alag khane hain)
- **Mulazim hon to:** salary slip ya employer ka income certificate (dastakhat + mohar ke saath)
- **Self-employed hon to:** stamp paper (wakeel ya stamp farosh se), jis par income affidavit likhwana hai
- Walid ke zinda/guzre hone aur peshe ki maloomat (form me poochi jati hai)

**Tip:** Affidavit likhwate waqt aamdani ka **sachcha andaza** likhein — jhooti aamdani zahir karna qanooni jurm bhi hai aur pakre jane par scholarship ya imdad khatam bhi ho sakti hai.

## Step-by-Step: Income Affidavit Banwane Aur Attest Karwane Ka Tareeqa

Ye tareeqa self-employed ya ghair-mulazim afrad ke liye hai:

1. **Stamp paper khareedein** — kisi stamp farosh ya katib se. Aam tor par chhote kaam ke liye munasib value ka stamp paper kaafi hota hai (qeemat waqt ke saath badalti rehti hai, is liye stamp farosh se maujooda rate pooch lein).
2. **Affidavit likhwayein** — kisi wakeel ya tajurbekar katib se apne naam, walid ka naam, CNIC number, pata, pesha aur **mahana aamdani** ke saath halaf-nama likhwayein ke mazkoora maloomat durust hain. PEEF ka official Income Affidavit form (peef.org.pk par mojood) bhi rehnumai ke liye dekh sakte hain.
3. **Dastakhat karein** — affidavit par apne dastakhat karein (aur agar gawah darkar hon to un ke bhi).
4. **Attestation ke liye le jayein** — affidavit aur CNIC ki copy ke saath apne ilaqe ke **Tehsildar, Naib Tehsildar, Assistant Commissioner ya Union Council Chairman** ke paas jayein. Ye afsaran tasdeeq ke baad affidavit par **mohar aur dastakhat** kar denge — ab ye aap ka attested income certificate hai.
5. **Copy sambhal kar rakhein** — asal affidavit mutaliqa idare (PEEF/Zakat Committee) ko dein aur apne paas **photocopy** zaroor rakhein; aksar dobara mangi jati hai.

## Mulazim Afraad: Employer Se Income Certificate Lene Ka Tareeqa

Agar aap ya aap ke walid/guardian mulazim hain to affidavit ki zaroorat nahi — ye tareeqa zyada asaan aur zyada qabil-e-aitbar samjha jata hai:

1. Apne idare ke **HR department ya admin officer** ko darkhwast dein ke aap ko scholarship/zakat ke liye income certificate chahiye.
2. Certificate me ye baatein lazmi hon: mulazim ka naam, ohda, **mahana tankhwah (gross pay)**, aur idare ka naam.
3. Certificate par **HR head ya majaz officer ke dastakhat** aur idare ki **official mohar** lazmi hai — baghair mohar ke certificate aksar qabool nahi hota.
4. Saath me **akhri 2-3 mah ki salary slips** bhi rakh lein — kuch idare ye bhi mangte hain.

PEEF ki official shart ke mutabiq employer se verified income certificate ya Union Council Chairman/Tehsildar se verified affidavit — dono qabool hain.

## Ye Certificate Kahan Kaam Aata Hai (Verified Istemal)

- **PEEF scholarships:** Master's level ke liye walidain ki declared mahana aamdani **Rs 60,000 ya us se kam** hona lazmi hai (official PEEF shart). Govt mulazmin ke BPS 1–4 ke bachon ko is shart se chhoot hai agar tankhwah hi aamdani ka wahid zariya ho.
- **Punjab Zakat taleemi wazaif:** Intermediate ke liye **Rs 10,000/saal**, BS/Graduate aur Post Graduate ke liye **Rs 15,000/saal**, Engineering/Medical/Computer Science ke liye **Rs 20,000/saal** — istehqaq Local Zakat Committee tay karti hai (official: zakat.punjab.gov.pk).
- **Punjab Zakat marriage grant:** Mustahiq larkion ki shadi ke liye **Rs 50,000** (official: zakat.punjab.gov.pk).
- **Punjab Bait-ul-Maal taleemi imdad:** Need-based scholarship ke liye walid/guardian ka income certificate lazmi hai.
- **Fee concession:** Kai taleemi idare ghareeb talaba ki fee maaf ya kam karne ke liye income certificate mangte hain.

## Kitna Waqt Lagta Hai Aur Fee Kya Hai — Imandari Se

Is bare me koi official fee schedule ya timeline **publish nahi hui**, is liye koi number invent karne ke bajaye seedhi baat:

- **Fee:** Koi sarkari fee muqarrar nahi — kharcha sirf **stamp paper ki qeemat** aur (agar wakeel se likhwayein to) us ki ujrat ka hai. Tehsil office ya UC Chairman ki attestation par koi official fee darj nahi hai.
- **Waqt:** Tehsildar/AC office ya UC Chairman se attestation aam tor par **usi din ya ek-do din** me ho jati hai — lekin ye mashahide ki baat hai, koi official timeline nahi. Rash ke dinon (scholarship deadlines) me pehle jayein.
- **Validity:** Kisi official source par income certificate ki muddat (masalan 6 mah ya 1 saal) publish nahi mili — jis idare ko dena hai, **usi se pooch lein** ke unhe kitna purana certificate qabool hai.

Tasdeeq ke liye: e-Khidmat Markaz toll-free **0800-09100** (24/7) ya short code **1317**; Dastak doorstep service helpline **1202**.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **e-Khidmat Markaz ja kar waqt zaya karna** — official service list me income certificate ka counter nahi hai; seedha Tehsildar/AC office ya UC Chairman ke paas jayein.
2. **Jhooti aamdani likhna** — kam aamdani zahir kar ke zyada imdad lena fraud hai; verification me pakre jane par faida khatam aur qanooni karwai ho sakti hai.
3. **Baghair mohar ke certificate jama karwana** — employer ka certificate baghair official mohar ke aksar reject ho jata hai.
4. **Asal affidavit de kar copy na rakhna** — dobara zaroorat parne par phir se poora process karna parta hai.
5. **Akhri tareekh ka intezar karna** — scholarship ya zakat ki deadline se 2-3 hafte pehle affidavit tayyar kar lein; rash me attestation me din lag sakte hain.
6. **Ghalat authority se attestation** — aise shakhs se mohar lagwana jo majaz na ho; sirf Tehsildar/Naib Tehsildar/AC ya UC Chairman ki attestation qabil-e-qabool hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya income certificate online ban sakta hai?**

Nahi — is waqt income certificate ka koi official online portal ya e-Khidmat counter mojood nahi hai. Mulazim afrad apne employer se certificate lein, aur self-employed afrad stamp paper par affidavit banwa kar Tehsildar/AC ya UC Chairman se attest karwayein.

**Income affidavit aur income certificate me kya farq hai?**

Income affidavit aap ka apna halaf-nama hota hai (stamp paper par) jis me aap apni aamdani zahir karte hain — phir sarkari afsar usay attest karta hai. Income certificate aam tor par employer jari karta hai jo aap ki tankhwah ki tasdeeq karta hai. Dono hi PEEF aur Zakat ke liye qabool hain.

**Kya be-rozgar shakhs bhi income certificate banwa sakta hai?**

Ji haan — affidavit me aap apni asal surat-e-hal likhte hain, chahe aamdani zero ho. Zakat aur Bait-ul-Maal jaise idare isi bunyad par istehqaq tay karte hain. Jhoot likhne se bachein.

**PEEF scholarship ke liye walidain ki aamdani ki hadd kya hai?**

Master's level PEEF scholarship ke liye walidain ki declared mahana aamdani Rs 60,000 ya us se kam hona lazmi hai (official PEEF shart). Har scheme ki apni hadd hoti hai — apply karne se pehle us scheme ki official site zaroor check karein.

**Kya ek affidavit har jagah chal jayega?**

Aksar haan — attested affidavit PEEF, Zakat aur Bait-ul-Maal sab jagah qabool hota hai. Lekin kuch idare apna makhsoos form bhi dete hain (masalan PEEF ka Income Affidavit form), to jis scheme me apply kar rahe hain us ki requirement pehle parh lein.

**How to get an income certificate in Pakistan?**

There is no online portal for income certificates in Pakistan. Salaried persons should get an income certificate from their employer, verified with the HR head's signature and the organization's official stamp. Self-employed or unemployed persons should write an income affidavit on stamp paper declaring their monthly income and get it attested by the Tehsildar, Assistant Commissioner or Union Council Chairman. The attested affidavit is accepted for PEEF scholarships (parents' income ceiling Rs 60,000/month for Master's level), Punjab Zakat education stipends (Rs 10,000–20,000/year) and Bait-ul-Maal assistance.
