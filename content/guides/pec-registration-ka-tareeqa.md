## Pehli Baat: PEC Registration Kya Hai Aur Kyun Lazmi Hai

**PEC** ka matlab hai **Pakistan Engineering Council** — ye wo sarkari idara hai jo Pakistan me engineering ke peshe ko regulate karta hai. PEC Act ke tehet Pakistan me **engineering ka kaam sirf wohi shakhs kar sakta hai jo PEC me registered ho**. Baghair registration ke aap apne naam ke saath "Engineer" ka laqab istemal nahi kar sakte, sarkari nokriyon me engineer ki post ke liye apply nahi kar sakte, aur kisi PEC-registered firm me engineer ki haisiyat se kaam nahi kar sakte.

PEC registration ke **do darje** hain:

- **Registered Engineer (RE)** — ye pehla darja hai jo har engineering graduate ko milta hai. Degree mukammal hote hi isi ke liye apply kiya jata hai.
- **Professional Engineer (PE)** — ye aala darja hai jo tajurbe, CPD points aur Engineering Practice Examination (EPE) ya interview ke baad milta hai. PE ka darja aap ko apni firm kholne aur bare projects par sign karne ka haq deta hai.

Seedha lafzon me: **RE aap ki pehchan hai, PE aap ki taraqqi hai**. Is guide me dono ka tareeqa diya gaya hai, lekin tafseel RE par hai kyunke naye graduates ko sab se pehle wohi chahiye hota hai.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Eligibility

PEC me Registered Engineer banne ke liye ye shartein hain:

- **Engineering degree** — aap ne kisi **PEC se accredited program** se engineering ki degree (BE/BS Engineering) hasil ki ho. Sirf HEC se recognized university hona kafi nahi — program ka PEC-accredited hona bhi lazmi hai. Apne program ki accreditation PEC ki website par check kar lein.
- **Degree mukammal ho** — final transcript aur degree ya provisional certificate mojood ho. Result awaiting students registration nahi karwa sakte.
- **Pakistani ya bahir ki degree** — Pakistan se degree wale seedha apply karte hain. Bahir mulk se degree wale graduates ko pehle HEC se equivalence aur PEC ki verification se guzarna hota hai — un ke liye fee bhi wohi Rs 1,000 hai, lekin documents ki list me HEC equivalence shamil hoti hai.

Agar aap ki university ya program PEC-accredited nahi hai to pehle apni university se rabta karein — aksar universities accreditation ke amal me hoti hain aur is ka hal university ki taraf se hi nikalta hai.

## Official Fee: PEC Ki Published Fee Table

Ye fee **PEC ki official website (pec.org.pk)** par published fee table se li gayi hai — koi andaza nahi:

| Kaam | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| New Registration as RE (Pakistani HEI se graduates) | Rs 1,000 |
| New Registration as RE (International HEI se Pakistani graduates) | Rs 1,000 |
| Professional Engineer (PE) ka title (EPE aur assessment/interview ke baad) | Rs 5,500 |
| Correction / Change (naam, date of birth waghera) | Rs 1,000 |
| Duplicate Registration Certificate | Rs 1,000 |
| Renewal of Registration (saalana) | Rs 1,000 (arrears ke saath) |
| Duplicate Renewal Card | Rs 500 |
| Endorsement of Higher Qualification (PGD, Master, PhD) | Rs 1,000 |
| Foreign nationals ke liye Temporary License (new/renewal) | $100 |
| International Professional Engineer — Int PE (Pak) | $200 (renewal $100) |

**Arrears ka usool:** agar renewal late ho to har saal ke **Rs 500 arrears** lagte hain. Is liye renewal waqt par karana sasta parta hai — 3 saal late renewal par fee ke saath Rs 1,500 arrears bhi dene honge. Fee PEC ke designated banks me **Online Portal System** ke zariye jama hoti hai aur **acknowledgment receipt ko application ke saath upload karna lazmi** hai — baghair receipt ke application mukammal nahi hoti. Bank charges alag se lag sakte hain.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kya Tayyar Rakhein

Online application bharne se pehle ye kagzaat scan kar ke tayyar rakhein:

- **CNIC ki copy** — dono taraf se saaf scan.
- **Engineering degree aur transcript** — degree ya provisional certificate aur final transcript ki copies.
- **Passport-size tasveer** — haal ki, saaf background wali tasveer jo PEC card par chapegi.
- **Fee deposit ki acknowledgment receipt** — designated bank me jama ki hui fee ki raseed; isay portal par upload karna lazmi hai.
- **HEC equivalence (sirf bahir ki degree walon ke liye)** — bahir mulk se degree lene walon ke liye HEC equivalence certificate.
- **Higher qualification ki asnaad (agar endorsement chahiye ho)** — Master ya PhD ki degree, agar usay record me shamil karwana ho.

Tamam documents ke naam aur CNIC par naam aik jaisa hona chahiye. Naam ki spelling me farq ho to pehle university ya NADRA se durust karayen, warna PEC record me ghalti aa jayegi aur baad me correction ke liye Rs 1,000 fee deni hogi.

## Step-by-Step: Online Registration Ka Tareeqa

PEC ki registration **portal.pec.org.pk** par online hoti hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

1. **portal.pec.org.pk** par jayen — yehi PEC ka official registration portal hai.
2. **"New Registration" ka option chunein** — agar aap pehli dafa register ho rahe hain to New Registration par click karein. Pehle se registered engineers renewal ke liye "Renewal of Registration" chunein.
3. **Account banayen aur profile bharein** — zaati maloomat, CNIC number, rabta number aur email darj karein. Email wohi dein jo aap khud istemal karte hon — PEC ki tamam ittila isi par aayegi.
4. **Taleemi tafseel darj karein** — university ka naam, degree ka naam, passing year aur program ki tafseel. Program ka naam university ke record ke mutabiq likhein.
5. **Documents upload karein** — CNIC, degree/transcript, tasveer aur deegar kagzaat ki saaf scans upload karein.
6. **Fee voucher generate karein aur fee jama karayen** — Rs 1,000 ki fee designated bank me Online Portal System se jama karayen aur acknowledgment receipt ki copy upload karein.
7. **Application submit karein** — tamam tafseel aik dafa phir parh kar submit dabayen. Submission ke baad application number ya tracking ID mehfooz rakhein.
8. **Verification ka intezar karein** — PEC aap ki degree ki verification aap ki university se karta hai. Verification mukammal hone par aap ka **PEC registration number aur card** jaari hota hai.
9. **Card hasil karein** — registration mukammal hone par PEC card aap ke diye gaye address par bhej diya jata hai ya portal se status check kar ke hasil kiya ja sakta hai.

Verification me waqt lag sakta hai — university ki taraf se jawab aane par munhasir hai. Is liye application jama karne ke baad portal par status waqtan fawaqtan check karte rahein aur agar PEC ki taraf se koi kami ki ittila aaye to foran poora karein.

## PEC Number Aur Card: Is Ka Kya Faida Hai

Registration mukammal hone par aap ko **PEC registration number** aur **PEC card** milta hai. Is ke faide ye hain:

- **Sarkari nokriyan** — WAPDA, NESPAK, NHA, Railways aur deegar sarkari idaron me engineer ki posts ke liye PEC registration lazmi shart hai.
- **Private sector** — bari construction aur consultancy firms sirf PEC-registered engineers ko hire karti hain.
- **Apni firm** — PE banne ke baad aap apni consultancy ya contractor firm PEC me register karwa sakte hain.
- **Bahir mulk** — bahir mulk nokri ya immigration ke liye PEC registration aap ki professional pehchan ka saboot hai.
- **Tenders aur projects** — sarkari tenders me engineer ki haisiyat se sign karne ke liye PEC number darkar hota hai.

Apna PEC number CV, LinkedIn profile aur job applications me lazmi likhein — employers pehli nazar me isi se aap ki registration verify karte hain.

## Renewal: Har Saal Card Renew Karana

PEC registration **saalana renew** karani hoti hai. Renewal ka tareeqa ye hai:

1. **portal.pec.org.pk** par login karein aur "Renewal of Registration" chunein.
2. **Rs 1,000 fee** designated bank se jama karayen (arrears hon to Rs 500 per saal izafi).
3. **Receipt upload karein** aur application submit karein.
4. **Naya renewal card** hasil karein.

Renewal waqt par karana ahem hai kyunke **expired registration wale engineer ki haisiyat se kaam nahi kar sakte** — aur agar kisi tender ya nokri ke waqt registration expired nikli to mauqa haath se ja sakta hai. Renewal ki tareekh apne calendar me note kar lein aur aik mah pehle hi renewal karwa lein.

Agar card gum ho jaye to duplicate renewal card Rs 500 me aur duplicate registration certificate Rs 1,000 me ban jata hai.

## Professional Engineer (PE) Kaise Banein: Agla Qadam

RE banne ke baad agla hadaf **Professional Engineer** ka title hai. Is ke liye PEC ke do raste hain:

**Rasta 1: EPE ke zariye (aam rasta)**
- RE registration ke baad **kam az kam 5 saal ka mutaliqa tajurba** hona chahiye.
- **17 CPD points** (Continuing Professional Development) jama karne hote hain — ye points trainings, workshops, seminars aur courses se milte hain. CPD ki tafseel PEC ke CPD portal **cpd.pec.org.pk** par mojood hai.
- **Engineering Practice Examination (EPE)** dena hota hai — ye computer-based exam Pakistan ke 16 shehron me hota hai aur is me aap ki discipline se mutaliq sawal hote hain.
- EPE pass karne ke baad assessment/interview hota hai, phir **Rs 5,500 fee** par PE ka title milta hai.

**Rasta 2: Interview ke zariye**
- Jin engineers ke paas **10 saal ya zyada tajurba** hai, wo interview ke zariye PE ke liye apply kar sakte hain — is me Enrollment Committee interview leti hai.

CPD points ka record shuru se rakhein — har training ka certificate mehfooz rakhein aur CPD portal par waqt par darj karein. Jo engineers CPD ko aakhri waqt tak chor dete hain, un ka PE ka kaam saalon late ho jata hai.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai

- **Online application bharna:** 30-45 minute, agar documents pehle se tayyar hon.
- **University verification:** is me sab se zyada waqt lagta hai — university ke jawab par munhasir, aam tor par kuch haftay.
- **Card ki delivery:** verification mukammal hone ke baad card jaari hota hai aur address par bhej diya jata hai.

Agar application me koi kami ho to PEC portal par ittila deta hai — kami poori karne me der mat karein warna application late hoti jayegi.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

- **Ghair-accredited program se apply karna** — pehle apne program ki PEC accreditation check karein.
- **Fee receipt upload na karna** — baghair receipt ke application mukammal nahi hoti.
- **Naam ki spelling me farq** — CNIC, degree aur form par naam aik jaisa hona chahiye.
- **Renewal bhool jana** — expired registration par kaam karna PEC rules ki khilaf-warzi hai aur arrears bhi lagte hain.
- **CPD points aakhri waqt par jama karna** — PE ke liye 17 points pehle se tayyar rakhein.
- **Agent ko paise dena** — PEC ki registration sirf official portal se hoti hai; jo shakhs "jaldi registration" ke naam par zyada paise mange, us se bachein.
- **Ghalat email/mobile number** — PEC ki tamam ittila isi par aati hai; purana number mat dein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**PEC registration ki fee kitni hai?**
PEC ki official fee table ke mutabiq naye Registered Engineer (RE) ki fee Rs 1,000 hai — chahe degree Pakistan se ho ya bahir mulk se. Professional Engineer (PE) ke title ki fee Rs 5,500 hai, renewal Rs 1,000 saalana hai (arrears Rs 500 per saal), aur duplicate certificate Rs 1,000 ka banta hai.

**PEC registration ke liye kaunsi degree chahiye?**
PEC se accredited program se engineering ki degree (BE/BS Engineering) darkar hai. Sirf HEC-recognized university hona kafi nahi — program ka PEC-accredited hona bhi lazmi hai. Apne program ki accreditation PEC ki website par check karein.

**PEC card kitne din me milta hai?**
Online application ke baad PEC aap ki degree ki verification aap ki university se karta hai, is liye waqt university ke jawab par munhasir hai — aam tor par kuch haftay lagte hain. Status portal.pec.org.pk par check kiya ja sakta hai. Documents mukammal hon to kaam jaldi hota hai.

**Renewal na karaya to kya hota hai?**
Registration expire ho jati hai aur expired registration par engineer ki haisiyat se kaam nahi kiya ja sakta. Late renewal par har saal Rs 500 arrears lagte hain, is liye renewal waqt par karana behtar hai.

**Professional Engineer (PE) kaise banein?**
RE banne ke baad 5 saal tajurba, 17 CPD points aur EPE exam pass kar ke PE ka title milta hai. 10 saal ya zyada tajurbe wale engineers interview ke zariye bhi apply kar sakte hain. CPD ki tafseel cpd.pec.org.pk par mojood hai aur PE ki fee Rs 5,500 hai.
