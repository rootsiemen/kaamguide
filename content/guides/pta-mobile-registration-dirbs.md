## DIRBS Kya Hai Aur Mobile Block Kyun Hota Hai

Bahir se laya gaya mobile Pakistan me **PTA se register** kiye baghair Pakistani SIM par kuch arsa chalta hai — phir **band ho jata hai** (call, SMS, mobile data sab ruk jata hai, sirf WiFi chalta hai). Ye system **DIRBS** (Device Identification, Registration and Blocking System) kehlata hai — PTA is ke zariye har IMEI ka record rakhta hai.

**Qanoon seedha hai:** Imported mobile = PTA registration + tax lazmi. "CPFTA / patch approval" wale ghair-qanooni tareeqe kuch arse baad band ho jate hain aur paise dob jate hain — **sirf official tareeqa** istemal karein.

> **Note:** Is guide ka tareeqa PTA ke DIRBS system (dirbs.pta.gov.pk) se liya gaya hai aur **October 2026 me check kiya gaya tha**. Mobile tax FBR ki valuation par hota hai aur har model ke liye alag hota hai — apne phone ki **exact tax sirf DIRBS portal** par maloom hogi, kisi website ke calculator par bharosa na karein.

## Pehle: Apne Phone Ka Status Check Karein

Registration se pehle ye maloom kar lein ke phone pehle se compliant hai ya nahi:

1. **IMEI maloom karein:** Phone par **\*#06#** dial karein — 15 hinsay ka IMEI screen par aa jayega. (Dual SIM phones ke **do IMEI** hote hain — dono note karein.)
2. **SMS check:** IMEI number likh kar **8484** par SMS bhej dein — status ka jawab mil jayega.
3. **Portal check:** **dirbs.pta.gov.pk** par IMEI dal kar detailed status dekhein.

Status ye ho sakta hai: **Compliant** (registered hai, tension nahi), **Non-compliant** (registration baki hai), **Blocked** (muhлат khatam ho chuki).

## 60 Din Ka Grace Period (Sab Se Ahem Baat)

- Jaise hi imported phone pehli dafa **Pakistani network** se judta hai, **60 din** ki mohlat shuru ho jati hai.
- Is muddat me phone aaram se chalta hai, lekin **registration is ke andar andar lazmi** hai.
- **60 din guzar jayen to IMEI block ho jata hai** — extension nahi milti, koi sifarish kaam nahi aati.
- **Mashwara:** Pakistan pohanchte hi pehle hafte me registration karwa lein — aakhri dinon ka intezar na karein.

## Temporary Registration (Short Visit Ke Liye)

Agar aap **mehman ya short visit** par aaye hain (overseas Pakistani, tourist):

1. Phone me **Pakistani SIM** dalen.
2. **\*8484#** dial karein aur temporary registration ka option chunein — ya DIRBS portal se karein.
3. Temporary registration **muft** hai aur **aik visit par 120 din** tak valid rehti hai.
4. 120 din ke baad bhi Pakistan me rehna hai to **poori tax wali registration** karwani hogi.

## Permanent Registration Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa (DIRBS Portal)

1. **dirbs.pta.gov.pk/drs** kholein aur **"Sign Up"** par click karein.
2. Maloomat darj karein: **CNIC/NICOP number, passport number** (passport registration ke liye), Pakistani mobile number aur email.
3. Email verify karke **login** karein.
4. **"Register Your Device"** par click karein aur phone ka **IMEI darj karein** (dual SIM ke dono IMEI).
5. System aap ke phone model ki **tax ki raqam** dikhayega — ghor se parh lein.
6. **PSID (Payment Slip ID)** generate hoga — **bank branch, ATM, JazzCash ya Easypaisa** se ada karein.
7. Payment ke baad aam tor par **24-48 ghante** me device register ho jata hai — status 8484 par dobara check kar lein.

**USSD tareeqa:** Portal ke bajaye **\*8484#** dial karke bhi on-screen steps follow karke registration shuru ki ja sakti hai.

## CNIC Par Registration Ya Passport Par? (Tax Ka Farq)

Registration ki do qisam hain — **tax me wazay farq** hota hai:

| Tareeqa | Kiske Liye | Tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Passport registration** | Jo shaks haal hi me bahir se aya ho (apne passport par) | **Kam tax** |
| **CNIC registration** | Pakistan me rehne wala koi bhi shaks | **Zyada tax** |

**Hisab samajh lein:** Aik mid-range phone (masalan $200 qeemat) ki tax passport par taqreeban **Rs 10 hazar** ke qareeb aur CNIC par **us se zyada** ho sakti hai; flagship phones (iPhone Pro Max waghera) ki tax **lakhon me** ja sakti hai — aur exact raqam har model ki FBR valuation aur dollar rate par munhasir hai. **Final raqam DIRBS portal hi batayega** — registration shuru karke raqam dekh lein, pasand na aye to PSID ada na karein, koi majboori nahi.

**Bari baat:** Ab pehle phone par bhi **customs duty** lagta hai — purani "aik phone muft" wali riayat khatam ho chuki hai. Traveler allowance ke rules DIRBS par check kar lein.

## Dosra Mobile Khareedte Waqt (Second-Hand Buyers Ke Liye)

Pakistan me **used imported phone** khareedte hue ye lazmi karein:

1. Dukandaar se phone ka **IMEI \*#06# se nikaal kar** khud check karein.
2. **8484 par SMS** karke status apni aankhon se dekhein — "PTA approved" ka sirf dawa kaafi nahi.
3. **Dual SIM phones ke dono IMEI** check karein — aksar sirf aik register hota hai, doosra baad me block ho jata hai.
4. Status **non-compliant** ho to qeemat me tax ki raqam minus karein ya na khareedin — tax **aap ko** deni hogi.

Khareedne ke baad ye bhi confirm kar lein ke aap ke naam par kul kitni SIMs registered hain — [apne naam par kitni SIMs hain check karne ka tareeqa](/guides/apne-naam-par-kitni-sim-hain-check-karne-ka-tareeqa) wali guide se ajnabi SIM foran pakri jati hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Aur Dhoke

1. **"Sasta patch approval":** Server se ki gayi nakli approval kuch arse baad mar jati hai — paisa gaya, phone bhi block.
2. **Sirf aik IMEI register:** Dual SIM ka doosra slot baad me block hota hai.
3. **60 din bhool jana:** Grace period khamoshi se guzar jata hai — SIM dalte hi reminder laga lein.
4. **Agent ko ghair-mamooli fee:** Official tax sirf **PSID se bank/app** me jati hai — agent ko naqd (cash) kabhi na dein.
5. **Kisi aur ke CNIC par registration:** Mustaqbil me phone ke muamlat me wahi shaks darkaar hoga — apne naam par karwayen.

## Registration Ke Liye CNIC/NICOP Ka Durust Hona

DIRBS registration aap ke **CNIC ya NICOP number** se judi hoti hai, is liye ye record durust hona lazmi hai. Agar CNIC **expire** ho chuka hai, naam ki spelling ghalat hai, ya aap ka mobile number CNIC par registered hi nahi, to application verification ke marhale par atak sakti hai aur PSID generate karne me error aa sakta hai.

Registration se pehle apna CNIC hath me le kar us ki maloomat aik dafa ghor se parh lein. CNIC banwana ya renew karwana ho to [NADRA CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) wali guide me poora process step-by-step diya hai — pehle wo kaam nipta lein, phir registration ki taraf jayen. Overseas Pakistanis ke liye yehi kaam NICOP se hota hai, aur jis mobile number se registration kar rahe hain wo bhi aap ke apne CNIC par registered hona chahiye, warna OTP aur status messages nahi ayenge.

## Dual SIM Phone: Dono IMEI Register Karna Kyun Lazmi Hai

Dual SIM phones ke **do IMEI number** hote hain — har SIM slot ka alag — aur DIRBS me har IMEI ki registration alag gini jati hai. Aksar log sirf pehle IMEI ki tax ada karke samajhte hain ke phone register ho gaya, lekin doosra slot kuch arse baad block ho jata hai aur doosri SIM achanak band ho jati hai.

Is masle se bachne ke liye:

1. **\*#06#** dial karke **dono IMEI** note karein.
2. **8484 par dono IMEI alag-alag SMS** karke dono ka status check karein.
3. DIRBS portal par registration ke waqt **dono IMEI darj** karein — system har IMEI ki tax alag dikhayega.
4. Payment ke baad dono ka status **compliant** dobara confirm karein.

Apne CNIC par kul kitni SIMs chal rahi hain, ye jan'ne ke liye [apne naam par kitni SIMs check karne ki guide](/guides/apne-naam-par-kitni-sim-hain-check-karne-ka-tareeqa) bhi parh lein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**PTA helpline kya hai?**
**0800-55055** — registration ya status me masla ho to call karein.

**Kya blocked phone WiFi par chalega?**
Ji haan — WiFi chalta rahega, lekin **Pakistani SIM ki call/SMS/data band** ho jayenge.

**Registration ke baad kitni dair me approve hota hai?**
Payment ke baad aam tor par **24-48 ghante** — details ghalat hon to zyada lag sakta hai.

**iPhone ki tax sab se zyada kyun hoti hai?**
Tax phone ki **qeemat (FBR valuation)** par lagta hai — mehenga phone, zyada tax. Exact raqam DIRBS batata hai.

**Kya main kisi aur ke passport par register kar sakta hun?**
Passport registration **usi shaks** ke liye hai jis ne safar kiya ho aur phone us ke saath aya ho — apne naam ka istemal karna qanooni masail paida kar sakta hai.

**Phone gift me mila, receipt nahi — registration hogi?**
Ji, registration IMEI par hoti hai — lekin **tax ki raqam** DIRBS FBR valuation se lagayega, receipt ki zaroorat nahi.

**CNIC expire ho to registration ho jayegi?**
Mushkil ho sakti hai — verification ke marhale par application ruk sakti hai. Pehle [CNIC renew karwayen](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa), phir registration karein.

**Sirf aik SIM slot band ho jaye to kya matlab hai?**
Matlab hai ke us slot ka IMEI register nahi hua — *#06# se dono IMEI check karke ghair-registered IMEI ki registration DIRBS se karwayen.

## Box-Pack (Naya) Imported Phone Khareedte Waqt

Agar aap Pakistan me **dukaan se imported (non-local) phone** khareed rahe hain:

1. Dukaandaar se poochein phone **PTA approved hai ya non-approved** — non-approved sasta hota hai lekin tax aap ke sar par hogi.
2. **IMEI box aur phone dono par** mila lein — \*#06# se nikaal kar box ke number se compare karein.
3. Tax ka hisab **pehle** laga lein — phone ki qeemat + tax = asal qeemat. Andaze ke liye [PTA tax calculator](https://frinjo.me/pta-tax-calculator) par phone ki qeemat daal kar tax ki raqam khareedne se pehle dekh lein (final raqam DIRBS portal hi confirm karega). Kabhi-kabhi **local version hi sasta par jata hai**.
4. Raseed par **IMEI number lazmi** likhwa lein — baad ke kisi bhi masle me ye saboot hai.

## Registration Ke Baad Ki Tasdeeq (Last Check)

Payment aur approval ke baad aik dafa zaroor confirm karein:

1. **8484 par SMS** karke apna IMEI dobara check karein — **"compliant"** ana chahiye.
2. Phone **restart** karke SIM ke signals aur data check kar lein.
3. **PSID ki raseed** mobile me save rakhein — kisi takneeki ghalti par ye aap ka saboot hai.
4. Dual SIM phone ho to **dono slots me SIM daal kar** check karein — kabhi aik slot reh jata hai.

**Masla aye to:** PTA helpline **0800-55055** par call karein ya apne mobile operator ki franchise par jayen — wo DIRBS registration me rehnumai karte hain.

## Agar DIRBS Portal Kaam Na Kare Ya Error Aye

1. **Portal busy ho to:** Shaam ki peak hours me portal sust hota hai — subah ya late night try karein.
2. **IMEI not found:** Box ka IMEI aur \*#06# wala mila lein — typing ghalti aam hai.
3. **PSID generate na ho:** Dubara login karke koshish karein; browser ka cache clear karein.
4. **Payment kat gayi lekin status update na ho:** 48 ghante tak intezar karein (bank-PTA sync me waqt lagta hai). Raseed sambhal kar rakhein, phir **PTA helpline 0800-55055** par PSID ke saath rabta karein.
5. **Agent portal "fail" kahe:** Agent ki baat par paisa na dein — kabhi-kabhi PTA system khud maintenance par hota hai, kuch ghanton baad theek ho jata hai.

## Akhri FAQs

**Registration ke waqt galti se ghalat IMEI darj ho gaya to?**
DIRBS account se application **cancel** karke dobara sahi IMEI se apply karein — agar payment ho gayi hai to PTA helpline **0800-55055** par PSID ke saath rabta karein.

## Purchase Ke Din: Phone Ke Saath Kya Lein

1. **IMEI ki raseed** — dukaan ki stamp wali.
2. **Warranty card** — official warranty ho to bharosa zyada.
3. **Seller ka CNIC copy** — private khareed ho to (fraud ke case me kaam aata hai).

Aur ghar aane ke baad sab se pehla kaam: **8484 par dobara check** — aik haftay baad phir check kar lein taake yakeen ho jaye registration mustaqil hai.
