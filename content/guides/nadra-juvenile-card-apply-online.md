## Pehli Baat: Juvenile Card Kya Hai

**Juvenile Card (JV Card)** NADRA ka woh plastic identity card hai jo **18 saal se kam umar** ke har Pakistani shehri ke liye banta hai. Ye aap ke bachay ki shanakht ka wohi card hai jo baron ke liye CNIC hota hai — farq sirf itna hai ke ye 18 saal se pehle ke liye hai aur is par bachay ki tasveer, naam, walidain ka naam aur NADRA ka record darj hota hai.

NADRA ki Registration Policy ke mutabiq Juvenile Card wohi haisiyat rakhta hai jo **Child Registration Certificate (CRC)** — aam zuban me **B-Form** — ki hai. NADRA is ko "certificate in the form of a card, carrying same value as of CRC" kehta hai. Matlab: kagaz wala B-Form aur plastic wala Juvenile Card, dono aik hi record ki do shaklein hain — aik kagaz par, dosra card par jo batwe (wallet) me rehta hai aur phatta nahi.

**Aik zaroori wazahat:** Is guide ki maloomat NADRA ki official Juvenile Card page aur NADRA ki fee structure page (**nadra.gov.pk/feeStructure**) se li gayi hain. Fee ke bare me aik ahem baat neeche alag section me saaf likhi hai — mukhtasir ye ke NADRA ki fee page par Juvenile Card ki koi alag line nahi hai, is liye is guide me koi fee andaze se nahi likhi gayi. Apply karne se pehle hatmi raqam PakID app ya NADRA centre par zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Juvenile Card, B-Form Aur CNIC Me Farq

Teeno documents aik hi khandan ke hain, lekin teeno ka kaam alag hai. Ghalat document ke liye apply karna waqt zaya karna hai — is liye pehle farq samajh lein:

| Document | Kin ke liye | Shakal | Kab tak valid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Juvenile Card** | 18 saal se kam umar ke bachay | Plastic card, tasveer ke sath | **10 saal ki umar** par aur phir **18 saal** par expire hota hai |
| **B-Form (CRC)** | 18 saal se kam umar ke bachay | Kagaz ka certificate | Bacha 18 saal ka ho jaye to is ki jagah CNIC banta hai |
| **CNIC / Smart NIC** | 18 saal ya us se zyada umar | Plastic card, chip ke sath | 10 saal ke liye valid |

**Aap ko Juvenile Card kab chahiye aur B-Form kab?** Agar aap ko bachay ki shanakht ke liye aik mazboot, roz istemal hone wala card chahiye — masalan school admission, bank account, ya safar ke liye — to Juvenile Card behtar hai, kyun ke ye kagaz ki nisbat zyada chalta hai aur is par tasveer bhi hoti hai. Agar sirf aik dafa ka kaam hai — masalan passport banwana ya kisi form me bachay ka record darj karwana — to B-Form bhi kafi hai. B-Form banane ki mukammal guide hamari [B-Form guide](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) me hai.

**Aik aur farq yaad rakhein:** Juvenile Card ke liye **bachay ka khud hazir hona lazmi hai** — har umar me. B-Form ke liye 3 saal se kam umar ke bachay ki hazri sirf tab chahiye hoti hai jab CRC passport ke liye ban raha ho. Is liye Juvenile Card ke liye bacha sath le kar jana parta hai, is ki tayyari pehle se kar lein.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Umar Aur Ehliyaat

NADRA (NIC) Rules, 2002 ke Rule 18(1) ke mutabiq **har Pakistani shehri jo 18 saal ka nahi hua**, woh CRC ya Juvenile Card ke liye eligible hai. Is me koi minimum umar nahi hai — naya paida hone wala bacha bhi register ho sakta hai. NADRA ki policy ke mutabiq walidain ya sarparast (guardian) par ye zimma hai ke woh **bachay ki paidaish ke aik mahine ke andar** us ka indraj karwayein.

**Ehliyaat ki shartein seedhi hain:**

- **Bacha Pakistani shehri ho** — Pakistan me paida hone wale bachay ke liye Union Council ka birth certificate, aur bahir paida hone wale bachay ke liye tafseeli birth certificate ya S-1 Form chahiye hota hai.
- **Bacha 18 saal se kam ho** — 18 saal poore hote hi Juvenile Card ki bajaye [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) banta hai.
- **Bacha khud hazir ho** — NADRA kehta hai ke Juvenile Card ke liye bacha "primary applicant" ki haisiyat se hazir hona chahiye. Is ka matlab hai ke bacha sath le kar jana lazmi hai, chahe woh chhota hi kyun na ho.
- **Walidain ya sarparast ki shanakht** — Application walid ya walida ya qanooni sarparast shuru karte hain, aur un ka apna CNIC ya NICOP bhi dikhana parta hai.

**Card kab expire hota hai:** Juvenile Card ki validity NADRA ki fee page par is tarah likhi hai — **10 saal ki umar poori hone par aur 18 saal ki umar poori hone par**. Matlab agar aap ne 5 saal ke bachay ka card banwaya to woh 10 saal ki umar par expire ho ga, aur phir naya card 18 saal tak chalega. Is ke baad bacha adult CNIC ke liye apply karta hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Juvenile Card ke liye kagzaat ki list chhoti hai — NADRA sirf aik hi asal kagaz mangta hai, lekin woh lazmi hai:

| Kagaz | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| **Computerized birth certificate** | Bachay ka computerized birth registration certificate — Pakistan me paidaish ho to Union Council, Municipal Committee ya Cantonment Board se; bahir paidaish ho to tafseeli birth certificate ya **S-1 Form** |
| **Walidain ka CNIC / NICOP** | Walid ya walida ya sarparast ka valid CNIC ya NICOP — centre par dikhana parta hai |
| **B-Form / CRC (agar bana hua ho)** | Agar bachay ka B-Form pehle se bana hua hai to us ko bhi application ke sath lagayein — lekin ye lazmi nahi; NADRA kehta hai ke Juvenile Card ke liye CRC pehle se hona zaroori nahi |
| **Bacha khud** | Bachay ki hazri lazmi hai — tasveer aur biometrics ke liye |

**Birth certificate sab se pehle banwayein:** Agar aap ke bachay ka computerized birth certificate abhi nahi bana to pehle woh banwayein — Juvenile Card ki application is ke baghair shuru nahi hoti. Birth certificate banane ka tareeqa hamari [birth certificate guide](/guides/birth-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) me hai. Union Council se birth certificate lene me aam taur par chand din lagte hain, is liye NADRA jaane se pehle ye kaam mukammal kar lein.

**Biometrics umar ke hisab se:** NADRA Juvenile Card aur CRC ke liye umar ke hisab se alag biometric rules rakhta hai — chhote bachon ki sirf tasveer li jati hai, jab ke bare bachon ki ungliyon ke nishan aur dastakhat bhi liye jate hain. Centre par operator aap ko bata de ga ke aap ke bachay ki umar ke liye kya chahiye — aap ko pehle se kuch tayyar karne ki zaroorat nahi.

## Fees Aur Waqt: Jo NADRA Kehta Hai

Is section ko dhiyan se parhein, kyun ke is me aik ahem sachai hai jo aksar guides chhupa deti hain.

**NADRA ki fee page par Juvenile Card ki koi alag fee line nahi hai.** Hum ne NADRA ki fee structure page (nadra.gov.pk/feeStructure) check ki — us par bachon ke liye sirf **CRC (Child Registration Certificate)** ki fee likhi hai: **Rs 50** normal (7 din) aur **Rs 500** executive (1 din); urgent ki category CRC ke liye nahi hai. Juvenile Card ki koi line us page par mojood nahi hai.

**To phir Rs 750 wali fee kahan se aati hai?** Bohat si websites Juvenile Card ki fee **Rs 750 normal, Rs 1,500 urgent aur Rs 2,500 executive** likhti hain — ye darasal **adult Smart NIC** ki fee hai, Juvenile Card ki nahi. Kuch articles is ko Juvenile Card ki fee keh kar likhte hain, lekin woh kisi NADRA notice ka hawala nahi dete. Punjab ki e-Khidmat page par bhi aik "Smart" category me yehi raqmein likhi hain — lekin NADRA ki apni page is ki tasdeeq nahi karti.

**Is liye hamara mashwara ye hai:**

| Cheez | NADRA ki fee page par kya likha hai |
| --- | --- |
| CRC / B-Form | **Rs 50** normal (7 din), **Rs 500** executive (1 din) |
| Juvenile Card | **Koi alag line nahi** — fee PakID app ya centre par confirm karein |
| Adult Smart NIC (muqable ke liye) | Rs 750 / Rs 1,500 / Rs 2,500 (31 / 23 / 9 din) |

**Fee dene se pehle ye karein:** PakID app me "New Juvenile Card" ka form bharne par app aap ko hatmi fee dikhaye ga — wahi raqam dein. Agar centre par ja rahe hain to counter par fee pooch kar hi jama karwayein. Kisi bhi website par likhi fee par bharosa kar ke paisa na dein — aur kisi agent ko "Juvenile Card ki fee" ke naam par zyada paisa bilkul na dein. NADRA ki policy ke mutabiq **processing ka waqt fee jama karne ke baad** shuru hota hai.

## PakID App Se Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

Juvenile Card ke liye application **PakID app** se ghar baithe shuru ki ja sakti hai — lekin bachay ki tasveer aur biometrics ke liye centre jana phir bhi par sakta hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

**Step 1: PakID app kholein.** Apne mobile par official PakID app kholein aur apne CNIC number aur mobile number se login karein. App sirf Google Play ya App Store se download karein — kisi aur link se milne wali app par apna CNIC aur bachay ki maloomat kabhi na dein.

**Step 2: "New Juvenile Card" chunein.** App me Juvenile Card ka option chunein. Application walidain ya sarparast ke account se shuru hoti hai — bacha khud account nahi banata.

**Step 3: Bachay ki maloomat bharein.** Bachay ka poora naam (bilkul waisa jaisa birth certificate par hai), tareekh-e-pedaish, walidain ke naam aur deegar maloomat darj karein. Naam ki spelling birth certificate se mila kar likhein — ghalat spelling baad me theek karwana mushkil hota hai.

**Step 4: Birth certificate upload karein.** Bachay ke computerized birth certificate ki saaf tasveer upload karein. Tasveer dhundli ya kati hui na ho, warna application wapas aa sakti hai.

**Step 5: Fee dein.** App aap ko hatmi fee dikhaye ga — wohi fee app se ya bataye hue tareeqe se jama karein. Fee ke baad hi aap ki application ki processing shuru hoti hai.

**Step 6: Centre par tasveer aur biometrics.** Agar app kahe to bachay ko le kar qareebi NADRA Registration Centre par jayein — wahan bachay ki tasveer aur (umar ke mutabiq) ungliyon ke nishan liye jayein ge. Is visit ke liye birth certificate ki asal copy aur apna CNIC sath le jayein.

**Step 7: Card ka status app me check karein.** Application ka status PakID app me hi dikhta rehta hai. Card tayyar hone par aap ko SMS se khabar mile gi, aur card aap ke diye hue pate par bhej diya jata hai ya centre se wasool karna hota hai — app par jo bataya jaye, us par amal karein.

## NADRA Centre Par Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Agar aap app istemal nahi karna chahte to poora kaam centre par bhi ho sakta hai — aik hi visit me:

**Step 1: Kagzaat aur bacha sath le jayein.** Bachay ka computerized birth certificate (asal), apna CNIC, aur agar B-Form bana hua ho to woh bhi. **Bacha lazmi sath ho** — is ke baghair application shuru nahi hoti.

**Step 2: Token lein.** Qareebi NADRA Registration Centre par ja kar token lein aur batayein ke Juvenile Card banwana hai. Centre ka pata aur timing NADRA ki website ya helpline **1777** se maloom kar sakte hain — ye helpline NADRA ke mutabiq 24 ghante khuli rehti hai.

**Step 3: Counter par maloomat darj karwayein.** Aap ki bari aane par walidain ke kagzaat check kiye jayein ge, bachay ki maloomat system me darj ki jayein gi, bachay ki tasveer li jaye gi aur umar ke mutabiq biometrics liye jayein ge.

**Step 4: Print check karein.** Operator aap ko aik print de ga — us par bachay ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish aur walidain ke naam ghor se parh lein. Koi ghalti ho to usi waqt theek karwayein, kyun ke card chhapne ke baad tabdeeli ke liye alag modification karwani pare gi.

**Step 5: Tasdeeq aur fee jama karwayein.** Application ki tasdeeq walidain ya khoon ke rishtedar ki biometric se hoti hai. Phir counter par fee jama karein aur **receipt aur Tracking ID sambhal kar rakhein** — isi se aap card ka status check karein ge.

**Step 6: Card wasool karein.** Card tayyar hone par SMS aaye ga. Card aap ke diye hue pate par bhej diya jata hai ya usi centre se milta hai — receipt par likhi hidayat par amal karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

**1. Birth certificate ke baghair NADRA chale jana:** Juvenile Card ki application computerized birth certificate ke baghair shuru nahi hoti. Pehle Union Council se birth certificate banwayein, phir NADRA jayein — warna aik chakkar zaya ho ga.

**2. Bacha sath na le jana:** Ye sab se aam ghalti hai. Juvenile Card ke liye bachay ki hazri har haal me lazmi hai — tasveer aur biometrics ke liye. Bacha sath na ho to application nahi banti, chahe tamam kagzaat mojood hon.

**3. Website wali fee ko hatmi samajhna:** Bohat si websites Rs 750 / Rs 1,500 / Rs 2,500 ko Juvenile Card ki fee likhti hain, lekin NADRA ki fee page par is ki koi line nahi hai. Fee hamesha PakID app ya centre ke counter par confirm karein — andaze se likhi fee par bharosa na karein.

**4. Naam ki spelling birth certificate se mukhtalif likhna:** Form par naam bilkul waisa likhein jaisa birth certificate par hai. Baad me naam theek karwana aik alag aur sakht kaam hai — is liye pehle hi din spelling check kar lein.

**5. Juvenile Card aur B-Form ko aik samajh kar ghalat apply karna:** Agar aap ko sirf aik kagaz chahiye to B-Form kafi hai; agar plastic card chahiye to Juvenile Card ke liye apply karein. Dono ke liye alag application hoti hai — aik ki fee aur form se dosra nahi banta.

**6. Card ki expiry bhool jana:** Juvenile Card 10 saal aur phir 18 saal ki umar par expire hota hai. Agar aap ka bacha 10 saal ka hone wala hai to card renew karwayein, warna school ya safar ke kaam me card reject ho sakta hai. 18 saal poore hone par adult [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ke liye apply karein.

**7. Kisi agent ko paisa dena:** Juvenile Card ke liye koi agent nahi chahiye. Jo shakhs "jaldi card" ya "bina bachay ke card" banane ke naam par paisa mange, woh dhoka de raha hai — bacha hazir hue baghair card nahi banta. Sirf official fee dein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**NADRA Juvenile Card kya hai aur ye kin bachon ke liye hai?**
Juvenile Card NADRA ka plastic identity card hai jo 18 saal se kam umar ke har Pakistani shehri ke liye banta hai. NADRA ke mutabiq is ki haisiyat wohi hai jo B-Form (CRC) ki hai — farq sirf itna hai ke ye card ki shakal me hota hai, is par bachay ki tasveer hoti hai aur ye batwe me rehta hai. Is ke liye bachay ka khud hazir hona lazmi hai.

**Juvenile Card banane ki fee kitni hai?**
NADRA ki official fee structure page par Juvenile Card ki koi alag fee line nahi hai — is liye koi hatmi raqam is guide me nahi likhi gayi. Bohat si websites Rs 750 / Rs 1,500 / Rs 2,500 likhti hain, lekin ye adult Smart NIC ki fee hai, Juvenile Card ki tasdeeq-shuda fee nahi. Hatmi fee PakID app me form bharte waqt dikhayi jati hai ya NADRA centre ke counter par maloom hoti hai. Bachon ke CRC (B-Form) ki fee Rs 50 normal (7 din) aur Rs 500 executive (1 din) hai.

**Juvenile Card ke liye kin kagzaat ki zaroorat hoti hai?**
Sirf aik asal kagaz lazmi hai — bachay ka computerized birth registration certificate (Union Council, Municipal Committee ya Cantonment Board se; bahir paidaish ho to tafseeli birth certificate ya S-1 Form). Is ke sath walidain ya sarparast ka CNIC aur bacha khud hazir hona chahiye. Agar B-Form pehle se bana hua ho to us ko bhi sath laga lein, lekin ye lazmi nahi.

**Juvenile Card kitne din me banta hai?**
NADRA ki fee page par Juvenile Card ke liye koi alag waqt nahi likha — waqt aap ki chuni hui category aur fee par munhasir hai, jo PakID app ya centre par batayi jati hai. NADRA ki policy ke mutabiq processing ka waqt fee jama karne ke baad shuru hota hai. Card tayyar hone par SMS se khabar milti hai aur status PakID app me check hota hai.

**Juvenile Card kab expire hota hai?**
Juvenile Card 10 saal ki umar poori hone par aur phir 18 saal ki umar poori hone par expire hota hai. 10 saal par naya Juvenile Card banwana parta hai, aur 18 saal poore hone par bachay ko adult CNIC ke liye apply karna hota hai. Is liye card ki expiry tareekh yaad rakhein aur waqt par renew karwayein.
