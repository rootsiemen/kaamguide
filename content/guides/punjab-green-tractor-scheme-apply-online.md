## Pehli Baat: Green Tractor Scheme Kya Hai

**Punjab Green Tractor Scheme** Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo programme hai jis ke tehet Punjab ke kisano ko naya tractor khareedne par bari subsidy di jati hai, taake chhote aur darmiyane kisan bhi jadeed machinery se kheti kar sakein. Ye programme **Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif** ne shuru kiya aur is ko **Agriculture Department Punjab** chalata hai. Intekhab kisi sifarish ya agent se nahi hota — muntakhib kisano ka faisla **computerized qurra andazi (digital balloting)** se hota hai, aur zameen ki tasdeeq **Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA)** ke computerized record aur CNIC ki tasdeeq **NADRA** ke zariye hoti hai.

Scheme marhalon (phases) me chalti hai aur har marhale ke apne rules hote hain — tractor ki taaqat (horsepower), subsidy ki raqam, zameen ki shart aur darkhwaston ki aakhri tareekh har phase me mukhtalif ho sakti hai. Is liye purani khabron par bharosa karne se pehle hamesha **mojooda marhale ki taaza status** check karna lazmi hai.

**Sab se ahem taaza khabar:** Phase 5 (Hi-Power) ki darkhwastein **30 October 2026** ko band ho gayin aur Agriculture Department ne saaf keh diya tha ke **mazeed extension nahi hogi**. Khabron ke mutabiq Phase 5 ke liye taqreeban **3,05,000 darkhwastein** aayi thin, jin me se taqreeban **1,88,000 verify** ho chuki hain. **21 November 2026 tak Phase 5 ki qurra andazi ki koi sarkari tareekh elaan nahi hui** — elaan sirf official portal par aaye ga. Agar aap ne Phase 5 ke liye apply kiya tha to ab aap ko qurra andazi ka intezar karna hai, aur agar aap apply nahi kar sake to is guide me agle marhale ki tayyari ka poora tareeqa diya gaya hai.

**Aik ahem note:** official portal **gts.punjab.gov.pk** 21 November 2026 ko check karne par load nahi hua (timeout) — portal waqti tor par dastiyab nahi tha. Is guide ke tamam numbers aur steps portal ki shaya shuda shartein aur Agriculture Department ke elanat se liye gaye hain, jaisa ke neeche source note me likha hai. Qurra andazi ki tareekh aur result ke liye portal hi hatmi zariya hai.

## Subsidy Kitni Hai Aur Kaunse Tractor Milte Hain

Phase 5 is waqt ka sab se taaza marhala hai, lekin Phase 4 ke muqable me is ki shartein sakht hain — bara tractor, zyada subsidy aur zyada zameen ki shart. Dono marhalon ka farq is table me saaf hai:

| Cheez | Phase 4 | Phase 5 (Hi-Power) |
|---|---|---|
| **Tractor ki taaqat** | 50 se 65 HP | **75 se 125 HP** |
| **Subsidy fi tractor** | Rs 7,50,000 (reported) | **Rs 10,00,000 (Rs 10 lakh)** |
| **Zameen ki shart** | Kam az kam 5 acre (reported) | **Kam az kam 7 acre, verified** |
| **Tractors ki tadaad** | 10,000 (reported) | **9,500 (reported)** |
| **Status** | Qurra andazi ho chuki, provisional result portal par live | **Darkhwastein 30 Oct 2026 ko band, qurra andazi ka intezar** |
| **Local ya imported** | Local | **Local + imported dono** |

Phase 5 me muntakhib kisan ko **75 se 125 horsepower** wale tractor par **Rs 10 lakh** ki subsidy milti hai — baqi raqam kisan ko khud deni hoti hai. Tractor **local ya imported** dono ho sakta hai, lekin sirf wo brand aur model jo portal ki manzoor shuda list me ho.

**Manzoor shuda local brands:** Millat, Al-Ghazi, Pak Tractors, Buraq, Orient aur Universal Agri. **Imported brands:** Millat, YTO, Zoomlion, Agri Master aur Lovol. In me Massey Ferguson (Millat Tractors) aur New Holland (Al-Ghazi Tractors) jaise mashhoor models shamil hain. Portal par form bharte waqt pehle brand, phir model aur phir horsepower select karna hota hai — aur aik dafa select karne ke baad **company aur model badla nahi ja sakta**, is liye ye faisla form bharne se pehle hi kar lein.

Aik ghalat figure se bachein: kuch jagah Phase 5 ke liye **Rs 7,50,000** ki subsidy likhi milti hai — ye Phase 4 ki reported raqam hai. Phase 5 ki portal shartein **Rs 10 lakh** kehti hain, jo Rs 1,000,000 banti hai.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Phase 5 ke liye darkhwast guzar ko ye shartein poori karni hoti hain:

**1. Punjab ka rehaishi hona:** Darkhwast guzar ko Punjab me rehna lazmi hai. Scheme sirf Punjab ke kisano ke liye hai.

**2. Valid CNIC:** Darkhwast guzar ke paas NADRA ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) hona lazmi hai. Agar CNIC abhi tak nahi bana ya expire ho gaya hai to pehle CNIC banwa lein ya renew karwa lein — baghair valid CNIC ke portal par registration nahi hoti.

**3. Apne naam par zameen — kam az kam 7 acre:** Phase 5 ke liye darkhwast guzar ke naam par kam az kam **7 acre zaraai zameen** ki verified malkiyat honi chahiye. Zameen ki tasdeeq revenue record ya field visit se hoti hai. Ahem baat ye hai ke **zameen aap ke apne naam par honi chahiye** — agar walidain ya kisi rishtedar ki zameen hai aur aap ke naam transfer nahi hui to aap eligible nahi honge. Khandan me kisi ki wafat ke baad hissa transfer na hua ho to pehle [fard-e-malkiat](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) ke zariye apne naam par zameen karwa lein, phir apply karein.

**4. Mobile number apne naam par:** Form me wo mobile number dena hota hai jo aap ke apne naam par registered ho, kyun ke is par OTP aur baad me qurra andazi ki ittila SMS se aati hai.

**5. Pehle marhalon ke winner apply nahi kar sakte:** Jo kisan **Phase 1 se Phase 4** tak kisi bhi marhale me tractor jeet chuka hai, ya **Wheat Incentive Program** ka faida utha chuka hai, wo Phase 5 ke liye eligible nahi hai.

**6. Kissan Card defaulter nahi hona chahiye:** Phase 5 ki shartein Kissan Card defaulters ko saaf tor par bahar rakhti hain. Agar aap Kissan Card istemal karte hain to apply karne se pehle apni repayments clear kar lein — aik miss hui qist bhi aap ko bahar kar sakti hai. Kissan Card ke bare me tafseel [Punjab Kisan Card guide](/guides/punjab-kisan-card-apply-online) me parhein.

**7. Umar aur digar shartein:** Portal ki shartein CNIC, zameen aur mobile par markooz hain. Koi alag umar ki had Phase 5 ki shartein me shaya nahi hui — jo shart portal par likhi hai, wahi hatmi hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Tayyari

Online form bharne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakhein taake form adhoora na reh jaye:

- **CNIC** — apna 13-digit CNIC number aur us ki saaf copy (front aur back).
- **Apne naam par registered mobile number** — OTP isi par aaye ga, is liye phone apne paas rakhein.
- **Zameen ka fard (malkiyat ka saboot)** — taaza fard jis se pata chale ke kam az kam 7 acre zameen aap ke naam par hai. Agar fard purana hai to naya nikalwa lein.
- **Kissan Card ki tafseel** — agar aap ke paas Kissan Card hai to us ki maloomat aur repayment status tayyar rakhein.
- **Tractor ka faisla** — kaunsi company, kaunsa model aur kitne horsepower ka tractor lena hai, ye pehle se tay kar lein, kyun ke submit karne ke baad model badla nahi ja sakta.
- **Email address (agar manga jaye)** — active email tayyar rakhein.

![CNIC aur zameen ke fard ke sath darkhwast ki tayyari — Green Tractor Scheme](/images/punjab-green-tractor-scheme-apply-online-2.jpg)

Aik ahem tayyari zameen se mutaliq hai: agar aap ki zameen ke record me koi ghalti hai — naam ki spelling, raqba kam likha ho ya hissa transfer na hua ho — to use **apply karne se pehle** durust karwa lein. Verification ke waqt PLRA record hi dekha jata hai, aur record ki ghalti ki wajah se achi darkhwast bhi reject ho sakti hai.

## Online Apply Karne Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

**Ahem:** Phase 5 ki darkhwast window **30 October 2026 ko band ho chuki hai** — is waqt naya apply nahi ho sakta. Neeche diye gaye steps portal ke asal tareeqe hain; in ko agle marhale (Phase 6 ya nayi window) ke liye yaad rakhein, aur jab nayi window khule to isi tareeqe se apply karein:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne phone ya computer par **gts.punjab.gov.pk** kholein. Sirf isi official portal par apply karein — kisi aur website, SMS ya WhatsApp number par apply nahi hota.

**Step 2 — CNIC aur mobile number likhein:** Form me apna **13-digit CNIC number** (baghair dash ke) aur **apne naam par registered mobile number** likhein. Is number par OTP aaye ga.

**Step 3 — Tractor ki category chunein:** **"Local" ya "Imported"** me se aik category select karein.

**Step 4 — Brand, model aur horsepower chunein:** Pehle company (make), phir model aur phir horsepower select karein. Lists aik ke baad aik khulti hain. Apna faisla soch samajh kar karein — submit ke baad model tabdeel nahi ho sakta.

**Step 5 — "New Application" select karein:** Nayi darkhwast ke liye **"New Application"** chunein. "Update Application" sirf us soorat me istemal karein jab aap pehle se ki hui darkhwast me tabdeeli karna chahte hon.

**Step 6 — Shartein parh kar tick karein:** Terms and conditions parh kar tick karein, **captcha** type karein aur **Submit** dabayein.

**Step 7 — Reference number sambhal kar rakhein:** Submit karne ke baad milne wala reference ya tracking number note kar lein — isi se aap apni darkhwast aur baad me result check kar sakein ge.

**Apply karne ki fee:** Online form par **koi fee nahi** dikhai gayi — registration muft hai. Winner ko sirf apne hisse ki raqam aur registration waghera ki fees allotment letter ke baad deni hoti hai.

**Kaghazi form (non-digital mauza):** Jin gaonon ke zameen ke record abhi digital nahi hue, un ke liye Agriculture Department **kaghazi form** bhi deta hai — department ki Green Tractor page par "Download Application Form" se milta hai. Is me apni tafseel, zameen ka raqba, zila, tehsil, gaon aur tractor ki pasand likhni hoti hai, CNIC aur fard ki copy lagani hoti hai, aur kisi waris ka naam aur signature ya thumb impression bhi dena hota hai. Form kahan jama karwana hai, ye form par nahi likha — apne zila ke **Deputy Director Agriculture (Extension)** ke daftar se maloom karein ya **Agriculture Helpline 0800-17000** par rabta karein (rozana subah 8 se raat 8 baje tak).

## Qurra Andazi Aur Result Check Karne Ka Tareeqa

Phase 5 ki qurra andazi ki tareekh abhi elaan nahi hui. Jab elaan aaye ga to wo sirf **official portal gts.punjab.gov.pk** par aaye ga — kisi news channel, Facebook page ya agent ki batayi hui tareekh par bharosa na karein.

**Result check karne ka tareeqa:**

1. Official portal **gts.punjab.gov.pk** kholein aur **ballot result** ka link chunein.
2. Apna **CNIC number** aur **captcha** likh kar submit karein.
3. Result me aap ka naam aur status nazar aaye ga.

**Phase 4 ka result** is waqt portal par **provisional** tor par live hai. Provisional ka matlab hai ke jeetne walon ki zameen ki dobara tasdeeq **Punjab Land Records Authority** se ki jati hai aur winners ki screening **Schedule-IV** (Anti-Terrorism Act 1997 ki Fourth Schedule — security ki buniyad par nazar rakhe jane walon ki list) ke khilaf bhi hoti hai. Tasdeeq ke baad hi hatmi (final) list banti hai.

![Qurra andazi ka intezar karte kisan — Green Tractor Scheme balloting](/images/punjab-green-tractor-scheme-apply-online-3.jpg)

Agar aap ka naam qurra andazi me nikal aaye to aap ko **allotment letter** apne zila ke **Deputy Director Agriculture (Extension)** ke daftar se lena hota hai. Letter milne ke baad tay shuda waqt ke andar apne hisse ki raqam aur fees jama karwani hoti hai — der karne par allotment khatam bhi ho sakti hai.

## Jeetne Ke Baad Kya Karna Hai

Qurra andazi me naam aana sirf pehla marhala hai — is ke baad ye steps hain:

**1. Allotment letter lein:** Apne zila ke Deputy Director Agriculture (Extension) ke daftar se allotment letter hasil karein. Is me aap ke tractor, subsidy aur adaigi ki tafseel likhi hogi.

**2. Apna hissa waqt par ada karein:** Tractor ki kul qeemat me se subsidy (Rs 10 lakh) nikal kar baqi raqam aap ko deni hoti hai. Is ke sath **registration, card, number plate aur transaction fees** bhi aap ke zimme hain, aur ye sab allotment letter me likhe hue waqt ke andar jama karwana hota hai. Agar aap ko qarz ki zaroorat hai to is ka bandobast **qurra andazi se pehle** kar lein, baad me nahi — der hui to allotment ja sakti hai.

**3. Tractor 3 saal tak apne naam par rakhein:** Jeeta hua tractor kam az kam **3 saal** tak aap ke naam par rehna lazmi hai. Is se pehle bechna **jurm** hai aur kaghazi form ki shartein ke mutabiq jaldi bechne par **subsidy wapas karni parre gi**. Hukumat subsidy baghair notice ke wapas bhi le sakti hai aur jaanch me koi bey-zabtgi mile to darkhwast reject bhi kar sakti hai.

**4. Tasdeeq me taawun karein:** Jeetne ke baad aap ki zameen aur kagzaat ki dobara tasdeeq hoti hai. Kaghaz mukammal aur durust rakhein taake is marhale me koi rukawat na aaye.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Aur Fraud Se Bachao

Green Tractor jaisi bari scheme me fraud aur ghaltiyan aam hain. In se bachein:

**1. Band window me apply karne ki koshish:** Phase 5 ki aakhri tareekh 30 October 2026 guzar chuki hai. Jo shakhs ya website ab bhi "abhi apply kar dein" keh kar paise mange, wo **jhoot bol raha hai**. Nayi window ka elaan sirf official portal par aaye ga.

**2. Agent ko paise dena:** Qurra andazi **computerized** hoti hai — koi agent, sifarish ya rishwat aap ka naam nahi nikalwa sakti. Jo shakhs Phase 5 me naam dalwane ke paise mange, wo **fraud** hai. Apply muft hai aur sirf portal par hota hai.

**3. CNIC kisi ajnabi ko bhejna:** Portal ya department ki page Phase 5 ke liye **koi SMS code nahi** deti. Agar koi SMS ya WhatsApp par aap se CNIC ki copy ya bank ki tafseel mange to kabhi na dein. Result sirf portal ya official SMS se check karein.

**4. Ghalat zameen ki shart par apply karna:** Phase 5 me kam az kam **7 acre** chahiye — 5 acre wala rule Phase 4 ka tha. 5 acre par Phase 5 ke liye apply karna waqt zaya karna hai.

**5. Model select karne me jaldi:** Form submit karne ke baad company aur model **badla nahi ja sakta**. Pehle market me qeemat aur apni zaroorat dekh lein, phir select karein.

**6. Kissan Card ki qist miss karna:** Kissan Card defaulter Phase 5 se bahar ho jata hai. Apni repayments waqt par clear rakhein, warna darkhwast khud ba khud reject ho jaye gi.

**7. Fake websites:** Sirf **gts.punjab.gov.pk** aur Agriculture Department ki official website par bharosa karein. Milti-julti naam wali kisi aur site par form na bharein aur na fee jama karwayein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Green Tractor Scheme Phase 5 ki aakhri tareekh kya thi?**
Phase 5 ki darkhwastein 30 October 2026 ko band ho gayin aur Agriculture Department ne saaf keh diya tha ke mazeed extension nahi hogi. Is waqt koi darkhwast window khuli nahi hai — nayi window ka elaan sirf official portal gts.punjab.gov.pk par aaye ga.

**Phase 5 me kitni subsidy milti hai?**
Phase 5 (Hi-Power) me 75 se 125 horsepower wale tractor par Rs 10 lakh (Rs 1,000,000) subsidy milti hai. Baqi raqam kisan ko khud deni hoti hai. Rs 7,50,000 ki raqam Phase 4 ki thi, Phase 5 ki nahi.

**Phase 5 ke liye kam az kam kitni zameen chahiye?**
Kam az kam 7 acre zaraai zameen jo aap ke apne naam par ho aur PLRA/revenue record se verified ho. 5 acre ki shart Phase 4 ke liye thi. Zameen kisi aur ke naam par ho to aap eligible nahi honge.

**Green Tractor Scheme ka result kaise check karein?**
Official portal gts.punjab.gov.pk par ballot result ka link kholein, apna CNIC number aur captcha likh kar submit karein. Phase 4 ka provisional result is waqt portal par live hai; Phase 5 ki qurra andazi ki tareekh abhi elaan nahi hui.

**Kya abhi bhi Green Tractor ke liye apply ho sakta hai?**
Nahi. Phase 5 ki window 30 October 2026 ko band ho chuki hai aur koi extension nahi hui. Jo bhi ab apply karwane ke paise mange, wo fraud hai. Agle marhale ka intezar karein aur tab tak apne kagzaat — CNIC, fard aur Kissan Card ki repayments — durust kar lein.
