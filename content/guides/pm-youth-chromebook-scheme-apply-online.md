## Pehli Baat: PM Youth Chromebook Scheme Kya Hai

**PM Youth Chromebook Scheme** hukumat-e-Pakistan ki aik nayi scheme hai jis ke tehet mulk bhar ke sarkari schools ke honhaar talaba ko **muft Chromebook** diye ja rahe hain. Is scheme ka iftitah **25 October 2026** ko Islamabad me Wazir-e-Azam Shehbaz Sharif ne kiya tha. Ye scheme **Prime Minister's Youth Programme** ke tehet chal rahi hai aur is ka official portal **Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC)** chala raha hai — portal ka address hai **pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk**.

Scheme ka hadaf agle **do saalon me 500,000 Chromebooks** taqseem karna hai. Har muntakhab student ko sirf Chromebook hi nahi milta — launch ki khabron ke mutabiq har device ke sath aik **bag aur aik MiFi internet device** bhi diya jata hai, taa ke student ghar baithe online classes aur digital library bhi istemal kar sake. Ye scheme khaas tor par un talaba ke liye hai jo sarkari schools me parhte hain aur parhai me aage hain — is ka maqsad digital taleem ko har ghar tak pahunchana hai.

Aik farq saaf samajh lein: ye **PM Laptop Scheme** nahi hai. Laptop scheme university students ke liye thi aur us ki registration is waqt band hai. Chromebook scheme school students — Grade 9 aur kuch ilaqon me Grade 10 — ke liye hai, aur is ka portal bhi alag hai (pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk). Dono ko aik samajh kar ghalat portal par apply na karein.

Sab se ahem baat: is scheme me apply karna **bilkul muft** hai. Na form ki koi fee hai, na Chromebook ki koi qeemat. Jo shakhs Chromebook dilane ke badle aap se paise mange, ya WhatsApp par koi "registration link" bheje, woh fraud hai — us se bachein aur apni maloomat sirf official portal par hi dein.

## Kon Eligible Hai: Grade 9 Aur Grade 10 Ki Shartein

Official portal par ehliyaat bilkul saaf likhi hai. Apna ilaqa aur class check kar lein — in me se aik shart bhi poori na ho to application aage nahi barhegi:

| Ilaqa / Board | Kon eligible hai |
|---|---|
| **Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan** | Grade 9 (SSC-I) ke top-performing students, sarkari schools se, Annual Examinations 2026 ki bunyad par |
| **Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Islamabad (ICT), Gilgit-Baltistan** | Grade 9 (SSC-I) **aur** Grade 10 (SSC-II) ke top-performing students, sarkari schools se, Annual Examinations 2026 ki bunyad par |

**Sirf sarkari (government) schools ke students eligible hain.** Private school ke students is scheme me shamil nahi hain — official portal par ye shart saaf lafzon me likhi hai. Kuch khabron me religious seminaries (madrassa) ke students ka bhi zikr aaya hai, lekin official portal par sirf government schools likha hai, is liye portal ki shart hi hatmi samjhein — agar aap madrassa ke student hain to apne board ya IBCC se tasdeeq kar lein ke aap ka record list me hai ya nahi.

Selection **merit** par hoti hai — aap ka education board apne top-performing students ki list IBCC ko bhejta hai, aur portal aap ka record usi list se milata hai. Is ka matlab hai ke apply karne se pehle aap ka naam aap ke board ki list me hona chahiye; sirf form bhar dena kaafi nahi. **Koi aik qomi cut-off nahi hai** — kuch khabron me 50% marks ki shart ka zikr hai, lekin official portal par koi aik qomi cut-off nahi diya gaya; faisla aap ke board ki merit list karti hai, aur har board ki apni merit hoti hai. Apne board ki merit list ya notice board se apni position zaroor check karein.

## Scheme Me Kya Kuch Milta Hai: Chromebook, Bag Aur MiFi

Muntakhab honay par har student ko ye cheezein milti hain:

| Cheez | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| **Chromebook** | Aik muft Chromebook — online parhai, assignments aur digital library ke liye |
| **Bag** | Chromebook ke sath carrying bag |
| **MiFi internet device** | Ghar par internet ke liye portable MiFi device |
| **Kul hadaf** | Do saalon me **500,000** Chromebooks |

Soba-wise taqseem ki ye table khabron me shaya hui hai — official portal par soba-wise quota nahi diya gaya, is liye isay hatmi na samjhein, lekin is se andaza hota hai ke kis ilaqe ko kitne devices milne hain:

| Ilaqa | Chromebooks (khabron ke mutabiq) |
|---|---|
| Punjab | 181,090 |
| Islamabad | 100,000 |
| Sindh | 85,925 |
| Khyber Pakhtunkhwa | 51,170 |
| Balochistan | 31,815 |
| **Kul** | **500,000** |

Chromebook milne ke baad is ka istemal parhai ke liye karein — ye device aap ko online classes, e-libraries aur assignments me madad dega. Device ki taqseem aap ke board aur university ki tarah nahi hoti — merit list ke baad boards ke zariye taqseem ki khabar aap tak pahunchegi, is liye apne board ki announcements par nazar rakhein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Apply Se Pehle Ye 4 Cheezein Tayyar Rakhein

Apply shuru karne se pehle ye chaar kagzaat tayyar kar lein. Official portal ke mutabiq har file **300 KB se kam** honi chahiye aur saaf parhni chahiye — dhundli ya aadhi kati hui scan par application ruk sakti hai:

| Kagaz | Format | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **Recent colour photograph** — saamne se, plain background wali | JPG ya PNG | 300 KB tak |
| **Mark sheet** — SSC-I (9th) ya SSC-II (10th) ki mark sheet | JPG, PNG ya PDF | 300 KB tak |
| **B-Form ya CNIC ki attested copy** | JPG, PNG ya PDF | 300 KB tak |
| **Walid ya guardian ke CNIC ki attested copy** | JPG, PNG ya PDF | 300 KB tak |

![Chromebook scheme ke liye zaroori kagzaat: tasveer, mark sheet, B-Form aur walid ka CNIC](/images/pm-youth-chromebook-scheme-apply-online-2.jpg)

**Tasveer** nayi honi chahiye — saamne se li hui, plain background ke sath. Purani ya group photo na lagayein. **Mark sheet** wohi ho jo aap ke Annual Examinations 2026 ke result ki hai — agar mark sheet gum ho gayi ho to [duplicate matric/inter certificate nikalwane ka tareeqa](/guides/duplicate-matric-inter-certificate) pehle dekh lein. **B-Form** ki attested copy ke liye B-Form ka hona lazmi hai — agar abhi tak B-Form nahi bana to [B-Form banane ka tareeqa](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh lein; aur agar aap ki umar 18 saal ho gayi hai to [CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi kaam aayega. Attestation kisi gazetted officer ya apne school ke principal se karwa lein — baghair attestation ke copy qabool nahi hoti.

Files ko scan karne ke baad har file ka size check karein: 300 KB se bari file upload nahi hogi. Mobile se photo lein to image ko compress kar lein ya scan app se saaf scan bana lein — lekin itna compress na karein ke likhai parhna mushkil ho jaye.

## Step-by-Step: Online Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

Poora process online hai aur official portal par hota hai. Neeche ke steps ko tartib se follow karein — aik step chhoot jaye to form submit nahi hoga:

![Student ghar par laptop par Chromebook scheme ka online form bhar raha hai](/images/pm-youth-chromebook-scheme-apply-online-3.jpg)

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne browser me khud type karein: **pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk**. Kisi WhatsApp forward, Facebook post ya YouTube description wale link par click na karein — sirf yehi official portal hai. Portal khulne par "Find your record" ya application ka section dikhega.

**Step 2 — Apna board select karein:** Us education board ko chunein jis ke tehet aap ne SSC ka imtihan diya tha — misal BISE Lahore, BISE Karachi, BISE Peshawar, ya apna mutaliqa board. Ghalat board select karne par aap ka record nahi milega.

**Step 3 — Number aur date of birth darj karein:** Apna **CNIC ya B-Form number** aur **date of birth** likhein. Number baghair dash ke, sirf digits me likhein. Agar CNIC/B-Form se record na mile to **roll number ya registration number** se bhi try kar sakte hain — portal ye option bhi deta hai.

**Step 4 — Apni details check karein:** Portal aap ka record aap ke board ki bheji hui list se milayega. Screen par aap ka naam, school aur marks aa jayenge. Inhe ghaur se parhein — agar naam ki spelling ya marks me ghalti ho to form submit na karein, pehle apne board se record durust karwayein.

**Step 5 — Kagzaat upload karein:** Upar bataye gaye chaaron kagzaat upload karein — tasveer, mark sheet, attested B-Form/CNIC aur walid/guardian ke CNIC ki attested copy. Har file 300 KB se kam honi chahiye.

**Step 6 — Review karein aur submit karein:** Submit karne se pehle review screen par har cheez aik dafa phir parh lein — naam, number, board aur documents. **Yaad rakhein: aik student sirf aik dafa apply kar sakta hai, aur submit karne ke baad form ko khud edit nahi kar sakte.** Is liye jaldi na karein; ghalti se bachne ke liye walid ya teacher se bhi aik dafa check karwa lein.

**Step 7 — Reference number aur slip save karein:** Submit karne ke baad aap ko aik **reference number** milega aur aik slip download karne ka option aayega. Slip ko download kar ke save kar lein aur reference number ko bhi likh lein — ye aage status check karne aur Chromebook milne tak kaam aayega.

## Record Na Mile To Kya Karein Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Agar portal par aap ka record nahi mil raha to ghabrayein nahi — ye aam masla hai aur is ke kuch wazeh hal hain:

- **Roll number ya registration number try karein:** Official portal khud kehta hai ke agar CNIC ya B-Form se record na mile to roll number ya registration number se dhoondein. Aksar record in numbers se mil jata hai.
- **Board se rabta karein:** Agar kisi bhi number se record na mile to masla aap ke board ki list me hai — ho sakta hai aap ka naam list me shamil na ho ya record me koi farq ho. Apne board ke office ya school ke zariye rabta karein; IBCC record tab hi dikhata hai jab board ne list bheji ho.
- **Aakhri tareekh ka khayal rakhein:** Is scheme ki **koi aik qomi aakhri tareekh nahi** hai — har board apni tareekh deta hai. Kuch boards ki tareekhein guzar bhi chuki hain (khabron ke mutabiq Lahore aur AJK boards ki 19 October 2026 wali tareekh guzar gayi). Is liye apne board ki notice aur portal par maujooda halat check karte rahein; agar aap ke board ki tareekh guzar gayi ho to agle marhale ya nayi announcement ka intezar karein.
- **Status check karna:** Submit karne ke baad apna reference number sambhal kar rakhein. Portal par dobara ja kar apne record se status dekh sakte hain, aur merit list ya taqseem ki khabar aap ke board ki notice se milegi — apne school se bhi rabte me rahein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Application Radd Karwa Deti Hain

- **Ghalat ya jhooti maloomat dena:** Official portal saaf kehta hai ke ghalat maloomat par Chromebook ki allocation cancel ho jati hai aur mazeed karwai bhi ho sakti hai. Marks, naam ya school ke bare me kuch bhi ghalat na likhein.
- **Dobara apply karna:** Aik student sirf aik application de sakta hai. Dobara form bharne se pehli application bhi khatre me par sakti hai.
- **Submit ke baad edit ki koshish:** Form submit hone ke baad aap khud usay badal nahi sakte. Is liye submit se pehle review screen ko achhi tarah parh lein.
- **300 KB se bari files upload karna:** Bari file upload nahi hogi aur form atak jayega. Har file ko pehle compress kar ke size check karein.
- **Baghair attestation ke kagzaat lagana:** B-Form aur walid ke CNIC ki attested copy chahiye hoti hai — baghair attestation ke copy qabool nahi hoti.
- **Fake portal par data dalna:** Sirf pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk official hai. "PM Chromebook registration" ke naam par bani hui kisi aur website par apna CNIC, B-Form ya tasveer kabhi na dein — woh aap ka data chori kar sakti hai.
- **Kisi agent ko paise dena:** Ye scheme muft hai. Jo shakhs Chromebook dilane ke naam par paise mange, woh aap ko loot raha hai — us ki shikayat apne board ya school ko karein.
- **Private school se apply karna:** Portal ke mutabiq sirf government schools ke students eligible hain. Private school ka student apply karega to us ka record list me nahi milega aur waqt zaya hoga.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**PM Youth Chromebook Scheme me kon apply kar sakta hai?**

Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa aur Balochistan ke sarkari schools ke Grade 9 (SSC-I) ke top-performing students, aur Azad Jammu and Kashmir, Islamabad aur Gilgit-Baltistan ke Grade 9 aur Grade 10 ke top-performing students, Annual Examinations 2026 ki bunyad par. Sirf government schools ke students eligible hain; private school ke students shamil nahi. Selection aap ke board ki merit list se hoti hai.

**Apply karne ki koi fee hai?**

Nahi, bilkul muft hai. Form bharna, documents upload karna aur Chromebook milna — is me koi fee nahi hai. Jo shakhs Chromebook dilane ke badle paise mange, woh fraud hai. Sirf official portal pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk istemal karein.

**Chromebook ke sath aur kya milta hai?**

Launch ki khabron ke mutabiq har muntakhab student ko Chromebook ke sath aik bag aur aik MiFi internet device bhi milta hai, taa ke ghar par online parhai ho sake. Scheme ka kul hadaf do saalon me 500,000 Chromebooks taqseem karna hai.

**Apply karne ki aakhri tareekh kya hai?**

Is ki koi aik qomi aakhri tareekh nahi hai — har education board apni tareekh deta hai, aur kuch boards ki tareekhein guzar bhi chuki hain. Apne board ki notice aur official portal pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk par maujooda halat check karte rahein. Portal par apna record check karein — agar record milta hai to foran apply karein.

**Mera record portal par nahi mil raha to kya karoon?**

Pehle roll number ya registration number se try karein — aksar record in se mil jata hai. Agar phir bhi na mile to apne education board se rabta karein, kyun ke portal sirf wohi record dikhata hai jo board ne IBCC ko bheja ho. Ho sakta hai aap ka naam board ki merit list me shamil na ho; is surat me board hi bata sakta hai ke aap agle marhale me shamil ho sakte hain ya nahi.
