## Pehli Baat: Punjab Kisan Card Kya Hai

**Punjab Kisan Card** Hukumat-e-Punjab ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet chhote aur darmiyane kashtkaron ko **bila sood qarz** milta hai taa ke wo fasal ke liye beej, khaad aur doosri zarai cheezein waqt par khareed sakein. Ye card **Punjab Agriculture Department** aur **Bank of Punjab** ke zariye chalaya jata hai, aur is ka maqsad ye hai ke kisan ko fasal bone se pehle paisay ki kami ki wajah se na to mehengay qarze lene parein aur na hi fasal der se bone par majboor hona pare.

Card ki khaas baat ye hai ke is par milne wala qarz **mukammal tor par bila sood** hai — aap jitni raqam istemaal karte hain, utni hi wapas karte hain, us par koi markup nahi lagta. Official poster ke mutabiq is scheme ke liye kul **350 arab rupay** ka fund rakha gaya hai aur **10 lakh kashtkaron** tak ko is se faida pohanchane ka hadaf hai. Card se aap sirf manzoor-shuda **registered dealers** se zarai inputs khareed sakte hain — is tarah raqam seedha fasal par lagti hai, kahin aur kharch nahi hoti.

Is guide me aap ko Phase II (2026-2027) ki taaza maloomat milegi — kitna qarz milta hai, kaun apply kar sakta hai, 8070 par SMS se apply kaise karte hain aur card milne ke baad khareedari aur wapsi ka kya tareeqa hai. Aik baat pehle se wazeh kar dein: is scheme me apply karne ki **koi fee nahi** hai aur koi agent is kaam ke paise nahi le sakta — jo shakhs card dilane ke badle paise maange, wo dhoka de raha hai.

## Kisan Card Se Kitna Qarz Milta Hai

Official poster (Phase II, 2026-2027) ke mutabiq Kisan Card par qarz ki hadood ye hain. Raqam aap ki zameen ke raqbe par munhasir hai — jitni zameen, utna qarz:

| Cheez | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| **Fi acre qarz** | **30,000 rupay** |
| **Zyada se zyada qarz** | **3,60,000 rupay** |
| **Markup / sood** | **0% — mukammal bila sood** |
| **Cash nikalne ki sahulat** | Kul had ka **30%** tak |
| **Kul fund** | **350 arab rupay** |
| **Hadaf** | **10 lakh kashtkar** |
| **Zameen ki had** | **1 se 25 acre** |

Misaal ke taur par, agar aap ke paas **5 acre** zameen hai to aap ko **1,50,000 rupay** tak ka qarz mil sakta hai (5 × 30,000). Agar zameen **12 acre** hai to had **3,60,000 rupay** ho jayegi — is se zyada zameen par bhi qarz 3,60,000 se zyada nahi hoga, kyun ke ye card ki zyada se zyada had hai. Isi tarah **1 acre** wale kisan ko **30,000 rupay** milte hain.

Card ki raqam do hisson me istemaal hoti hai: **70% raqam** se aap registered dealers se beej, khaad aur pesticide khareedte hain, aur **30% raqam** aap cash nikal kar fasal ke doosre kharchon — masalan mazdoori ya pani — par laga sakte hain. Ye 30% cash wali sahulat is liye di gayi hai ke kisan ko har chhote kharch ke liye card se khareedari na karni pare.

Aik ahem baat: pehle marhalon me qarz ki had aur zameen ki had mukhtalif thi. **Maujooda Phase II (2026-2027) ki hadood wohi hain jo upar table me official poster se li gayi hain.** Agar koi purani khabar ya video aap ko mukhtalif raqam bataye to us par yaqeen karne se pehle **Agriculture Helpline 0800-17000** par taaza had confirm kar lein.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Official poster ke mutabiq Kisan Card ke liye buniyadi shart **zameen ki malkiat** hai: darkhwast guzar ke paas **1 se 25 acre** tak zarai zameen honi chahiye. Is ke ilawa darkhwast ke waqt aam tor par ye shartein bhi check ki jati hain — in ki tasdeeq aap ko apply karne se pehle helpline se kar leni chahiye:

**1. Punjab ka rehaishi hona:** Ye scheme sirf **Punjab** ke kashtkaron ke liye hai. Aap ka mustaqil pata Punjab me hona chahiye aur aap ki zameen bhi Punjab me waqa honi chahiye.

**2. Valid CNIC:** Darkhwast guzar ke paas NADRA ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) hona lazmi hai. Agar CNIC expired hai to pehle [CNIC renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) karwa lein, warna darkhwast aage nahi barhegi.

**3. Zameen ka record (PLRA):** Aap ki zameen ka record **Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA)** ke computerised system me maujood hona chahiye. Is ki tasdeeq ke liye aap ko apni zameen ki [fard-e-malkiat](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) ki zaroorat par sakti hai. Jin ki zameen ka record abhi computerised nahi hua, un ki darkhwast verification me ruk sakti hai — is liye pehle apna record check karwa lein.

**4. Apne naam par SIM:** Jis mobile number se aap apply karte hain, wo SIM **aap ke apne CNIC** par registered honi chahiye. Kisi aur ke naam wali SIM se bheja gaya SMS ya form verify nahi hota.

**5. Bank of Punjab account:** Qarz Bank of Punjab ke zariye diya jata hai, is liye darkhwast guzar ka Bank of Punjab me account hona ya apply ke doran khulwana zaroori hota hai. Card bhi isi bank se jari hota hai.

**6. Koi default nahi:** Agar aap ka kisi bank ya idare me pehle se koi zarai ya doosra qarz default hai to darkhwast mushkil ho sakti hai. Bank aap ki credit history zaroor check karta hai.

**7. Thekedar (tenant) kashtkar:** Jin kashtkaron ke paas apni zameen nahi, lekin wo theke par zameen kasht karte hain, un ke liye alag sharait ho sakti hain. Theke ka muahida ya malik ki tasdeeq darkar ho sakti hai — exact shart ke liye helpline **0800-17000** par rabta karein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Tayyari

Apply karne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein taa ke darkhwast aadhi na reh jaye:

- **Valid CNIC** — number aur us ki copy. CNIC number baghair dash ke likhna hota hai (masalan 3520212345678).
- **Apne naam par registered mobile number** — isi number par tasdeeqi SMS aur card ki maloomat aayengi.
- **Zameen ka record** — fard-e-malkiat ya PLRA record ki tafseel; zameen kitne acre hai aur kis mauza/zila me hai, ye maloom hona chahiye.
- **Bank of Punjab account** — agar account nahi hai to branch se khulwane ka bandobast kar lein; account number aur branch ka naam darkar ho sakta hai.
- **Bijli ya doosra pata ka saboot** — pata ki tasdeeq ke liye kabhi kabhi bijli ka bill ya domicile ki copy bhi maangi jati hai. [Domicile certificate](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) na ho to pehle banwa lein.
- **Fasal ki tafseel** — aap kis fasal ke liye qarz le rahe hain (masalan gandum, kapas, chawal), ye batana hota hai kyun ke qarz fasal ke season se jura hota hai.

Ye kagzaat asal me darkhwast ke sath jama nahi karwane parte — zyada tar tasdeeq **online system** se hoti hai: aap ka CNIC NADRA se, SIM ka record telecom database se aur zameen PLRA se check ki jati hai. Is liye sab se ahem cheez ye hai ke in tamam record me aap ki maloomat **aik jaisi aur durust** hon. Agar CNIC par naam ki spelling aur zameen ke record me naam mukhtalif hai to verification ruk jayegi.

## Fees Aur Charges: Kitna Kharcha Aayega

Kisan Card ke liye apply karne ya card hasil karne par **koi fee nahi** li jati. Na registration ki fee hai, na card ki fee, aur na hi qarz par koi markup hai:

| Cheez | Fee |
|---|---|
| SMS se registration (8070) | **Muft** (sirf aam SMS charges) |
| Online apply (official portal) | **Muft — koi fee nahi** |
| Card jari karna | **Muft** |
| Qarz par markup / sood | **0%** |
| Agent ya middleman ki fee | **Koi nahi — agent se bachein** |

Qarz milne ke baad aap ko sirf **asal raqam wapas** karni hoti hai — us par koi sood ya izafi charge nahi lagta. Wapsi ka waqt aur tareeqa aap ko card milte waqt bataya jata hai; aam tor par istemaal shuda raqam **Bank of Punjab ki qareebi branch** me jama karwani hoti hai.

Agar koi shakhs aap se Kisan Card banwane, registration karwane ya qarz dilane ke badle paise maange to samajh lein ke wo dhoka de raha hai. Official tareeqa sirf SMS, official portal aur Bank of Punjab hai — aur wo sab muft hai. Aisi kisi shikayat ke liye Agriculture Helpline **0800-17000** par rabta karein.

## Step-by-Step: Kisan Card Ke Liye Apply Ka Tareeqa

Kisan Card ke liye apply ke do tareeqe hain — SMS aur online portal. Dono ka maqsad aik hi hai: aap ki maloomat ki tasdeeq kar ke aap ko card jari karna.

**Step 1 — SMS se registration (sab se aasan tareeqa):** Apne mobile se aik SMS likhein: **PKC** likh kar aik space dein, phir apna **13 huroof wala CNIC number baghair dash** ke likhein (masalan: `PKC 3520212345678`). Ye SMS **8070** par bhej dein. SMS **apne naam par registered SIM** se bhejna lazmi hai — doosri SIM se bheja gaya SMS qabool nahi hota.

**Step 2 — Tasdeeqi SMS ka intezar karein:** SMS bhejne ke baad system aap ka record check karta hai — CNIC NADRA se, zameen PLRA se aur SIM aap ke naam se. Agar aap eligible hain to aap ko aik tasdeeqi SMS aayega jis me aage ka tareeqa bataya jayega.

**Step 3 — Online portal par registration (doosra tareeqa):** Kisan Card ka official portal **kisan.punjab.gov.pk** hai. Portal par ja kar apna CNIC number darj karein aur form me apni maloomat bharein. **Note:** 12 November 2026 ko check karte waqt ye portal khul nahi raha tha (DNS kharabi), is liye us waqt SMS wala tareeqa ya helpline hi qaabil-e-aitemad raasta hai. Portal dobara chalne par is se bhi apply ho sakta hai — apply karne se pehle URL me **.gov.pk** zaroor check kar lein, taa ke kisi jaali website par apni maloomat na dein.

**Step 4 — Bank of Punjab se rabta:** Tasdeeq ke baad Bank of Punjab aap se rabta karega. Bank aap ke kagzaat aur zameen ke record ki aakhri jaanch karega aur zaroorat parne par aap ko qareebi branch bula sakta hai. Is marhale par apna asal CNIC aur zameen ka record sath le jayein.

**Step 5 — Card hasil karein:** Manzoori ke baad aap ko **Kisan Card** jari kar diya jayega. Card milte hi aap registered dealers se beej aur khaad khareed sakte hain aur 30% had tak cash nikal sakte hain.

**Step 6 — Card ki hifazat:** Card aur us ka PIN kisi ko na dein. Card gum ho jaye to foran Bank of Punjab ya helpline 0800-17000 par ittila dein taa ke card block kar diya jaye.

## Apply Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai: Card Milna, Khareedari Aur Wapsi

Darkhwast jama karane ke baad ye marahil hote hain:

1. **Record ki tasdeeq (kuch din):** System aap ke CNIC, SIM aur zameen ke record ko mutaliqa idaron se check karta hai. Agar record me koi farq ho to aap ko SMS se ittila mil jayegi aur darkhwast wahin ruk jayegi — is liye Step 1 se pehle apna record durust karwana behtar hai.

2. **Bank ki jaanch (1 se 3 haftay):** Tasdeeq ke baad darkhwast Bank of Punjab ko bhej di jati hai. Bank aap ke kagzaat aur credit history check karta hai. Is doran bank aap ko call ya SMS kar ke mazeed maloomat maang sakta hai — bank ki call ka foran jawab dein.

3. **Card jari hona:** Manzoori ke baad card jari kar diya jata hai. Card aap ko bank ki branch se ya mutaliqa daftar se milta hai — exact tareeqa aap ko SMS se bataya jata hai.

4. **Khareedari:** Card se khareedari **sirf registered dealers** se hoti hai. Agriculture Department ki website par har zila ke registered dealers ki fehrist maujood hai — khareedari se pehle apne zila ka dealer check kar lein. Dealer ke paas card swipe kar ke ya card ke zariye adayegi kar ke beej, khaad ya pesticide lein.

5. **Wapsi:** Istemaal shuda raqam **Bank of Punjab ki qareebi branch** me jama karwani hoti hai. Official poster ke mutabiq jama ki gayi raqam ke baad aap ki qarz ki had **48 ghanton ke andar** dobara bahal ho jati hai, taa ke aap agle season ke liye dobara qarz le sakein. Wapsi ki aakhri tareekh aur tareeqa card milte waqt bank se zaroor confirm kar lein — waqt par wapsi na karne par agli dafa qarz milne me mushkil ho sakti hai.

Kul mila kar darkhwast se card milne tak **2 se 4 haftay** lag sakte hain — ye waqt aap ke record ki durustgi aur bank ki jaanch par munhasir hai. Agar aik mahine tak koi rabta na ho to helpline **0800-17000** (rozana subah 8 se sham 8 bajay tak) par apni darkhwast ka status poochein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Rukwa Deti Hain

- **Doosre ki SIM se SMS bhejna:** SMS hamesha **apne naam wali SIM** se bhejein. Biwi, bhai ya dost ki SIM se bheja gaya SMS verify nahi hota aur darkhwast reject ho jati hai.
- **CNIC number ghalat likhna:** SMS me CNIC number **baghair dash** aur poora 13 huroof likhna hai. Aik huroof ki ghalti bhi darkhwast ruka deti hai — bhejne se pehle number do dafa parh lein.
- **Zameen ka record computerised na hona:** Agar aap ki zameen PLRA ke system me nahi hai to verification wahin ruk jayegi. Apply se pehle apni [fard-e-malkiat](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) ya record check karwa lein.
- **Jaali portal ya agent par yaqeen karna:** Sirf **kisan.punjab.gov.pk** aur **8070** asal hain. Kisi WhatsApp number, Facebook page ya agent ko apna CNIC aur paisa kabhi na dein.
- **Expired CNIC par apply karna:** Expired CNIC par darkhwast aage nahi barhti. Pehle [CNIC renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) karwa lein.
- **Registered dealer ke ilawa khareedari:** Card sirf manzoor-shuda dealers par chalta hai. Ghalat dukan par card istemaal karne ki koshish par transaction fail ho jayegi — pehle apne zila ke registered dealers ki fehrist dekh lein.
- **Wapsi me der karna:** Waqt par raqam wapas na karne se agle season ka qarz ruk sakta hai aur bank ki jaanch me bhi asar parta hai. Wapsi ki tareekh yaad rakhein aur waqt par Bank of Punjab me jama karwayein.
- **Ek se zyada dafa apply karna:** Aik CNIC par aik hi card banta hai. Baar baar SMS bhejne se darkhwast tez nahi hoti — aik dafa apply kar ke tasdeeqi SMS ka intezar karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab Kisan Card par kitna qarz milta hai?**
Official poster (Phase II, 2026-2027) ke mutabiq fi acre **30,000 rupay** aur zyada se zyada **3,60,000 rupay** qarz milta hai. Raqam aap ki zameen ke raqbe par munhasir hai — masalan 5 acre par 1,50,000 rupay aur 12 acre ya is se zyada par 3,60,000 rupay.

**Kya Kisan Card par koi sood lagta hai?**
Nahi. Kisan Card par milne wala qarz **mukammal tor par bila sood** hai — aap jitni raqam istemaal karte hain, utni hi wapas karte hain. Is ke ilawa apply karne ya card banwane ki bhi koi fee nahi hai.

**Kisan Card ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?**
Punjab ka wo rehaishi kisan jis ke paas **1 se 25 acre** tak zarai zameen ho, valid CNIC ho aur zameen ka record PLRA me computerised ho. SIM apne naam par honi chahiye aur Bank of Punjab me account hona zaroori hai.

**8070 par SMS kaise bhejte hain?**
Apne naam wali SIM se SMS likhein: **PKC** phir aik space, phir apna 13 huroof wala CNIC number baghair dash ke (masalan `PKC 3520212345678`), aur ye SMS **8070** par bhej dein. Tasdeeq ke baad aap ko aage ka tareeqa SMS se bataya jayega.

**Card se cash bhi nikal sakte hain?**
Ji haan — kul qarz ki had ka **30% tak cash** nikal sakte hain. Baqi 70% raqam se aap registered dealers se beej, khaad aur pesticide khareedte hain. Cash ki had se zyada raqam nahi nikali ja sakti.
