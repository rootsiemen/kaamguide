## Expired CNIC Renew Karna Kyun Zaroori Hai

CNIC Pakistan me shehri ki pehchan ka sab se bunyadi dastavez hai. Jab is ki muddat khatam ho jati hai to bank account, mobile SIM, passport ki application, jaidad ki khareed-o-farokht aur aksar sarkari kaam ruk sakte hain. Expired card ke sath aap ki pehchan ki tasdeeq nahi hoti, is liye bank ya kisi idare ke samne purana card dikhane par kaam aage nahi barhta.

Achi khabar ye hai ke expired CNIC ko dobara banwana mushkil nahi raha. NADRA ke Pak ID portal aur mobile app ke zariye aksar log ghar baithe renewal kar sakte hain — centre jane ki zaroorat sirf un cases me parti hai jin me biometrics ya record ki mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho. Renewal ka matlab ye hai ke aap ka CNIC number wohi rehta hai, sirf card ki muddat aur (agar Smart CNIC ho to) chip wali nai copy jari hoti hai.

Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jin ka CNIC expire ho chuka hai ya jald expire hone wala hai. Is me renewal ki dono surten — standard CNIC aur Smart CNIC — ki fees, kagzaat, online tareeqa, centre ka tareeqa aur status check karne ka tareeqa darj hai.

Renewal kab karwana chahiye, is ka seedha jawab ye hai: jitni jaldi ho sake. Expiry se pehle bhi renewal ki application di ja sakti hai, aur expire hone ke baad bhi — expired card ki renewal par koi alag jurmana ya penalty ki shart darkar nahi hoti, bas aam renewal fee ada karni hoti hai. Card expire hone ka intezar karne ka koi faida nahi, kyun ke us doran aap ke bank aur SIM se mutaliq kaam atak sakte hain.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Renewal Se Pehle Ye Cheezein Tayyar Rakhein

Renewal ki application me pehli dafa wale [CNIC banane](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) jitne kagzaat nahi lagte, kyun ke aap ka record NADRA ke paas pehle se maujood hota hai. Phir bhi ye cheezein pehle se tayyar rakhne se application beech me nahi rukti:

- **Purana CNIC:** Chahe expire ho chuka ho, us ki front aur back ki saaf scan copies ya tasveer. Purana card hi aap ki pehchan aur record dhoondne ka sab se bara zariya hai.
- **Nai tasveer:** ICAO standard ke mutabiq saaf, samne se li hui tasveer — safed ya halke background ke sath, chehra poori tarah nazar aaye, aenak ka reflection na ho. Pak ID app me live tasveer li jati hai, is liye achi roshni me tasveer lena zaroori hai.
- **Biometrics:** Pak ID app ke zariye mobile camera se fingerprints capture hote hain. Agar app fingerprints theek se na le sake to NADRA centre par biometrics dene ko kaha ja sakta hai.
- **Maujoooda address:** Agar aap ka rehaishi ya postal address tabdeel ho gaya hai to nai address ka saboot tayyar rakhein — masalan utility bill ya kiraya-nama. Sirf renewal (bina tabdeeli) ke liye purane address par hi card bhej diya jata hai.
- **Mobile number aur email:** Pak ID account banane aur OTP tasdeeq ke liye aik active mobile number aur email address lazmi hai. Tracking ID aur updates bhi isi number/email par aate hain.
- **Payment ka zariya:** Online fee ke liye debit/credit card, ya app me maujood JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR jaisi payment options.

Agar renewal ke sath naam, walid ka naam ya tareekh-e-paidaish me tabdeeli bhi karni hai to ye sirf renewal nahi, modification ka case ban jata hai — us ke liye tabdeeli ka saboot (masalan nikah-nama ya court order) bhi darkar hoga aur NADRA mazeed tasdeeq kar sakta hai. Aisi surat me sirf renewal select kar ke tabdeeli ki umeed mat rakhein; modification ka option alag se muntakhib karein.

## CNIC Renewal Fees 2026

NADRA ki shaya shuda fee schedule ke mutabiq renewal ki fee card ki qism (standard ya Smart) aur service ki raftaar (Normal, Urgent ya Executive) par munhasir hai. Neeche di gayi fees October 2026 me shaya shuda NADRA fee schedule se li gayi hain — hatmi rakam hamesha Pak ID portal/app ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai, usi ko ada karein:

| Card Ki Qism | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard CNIC renewal | Rs. 400 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| Smart CNIC (chip wala) renewal | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |

Fee ke sath delivery ki muddat bhi farq hoti hai:

| Card Ki Qism | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard CNIC | taqreeban 15 din | taqreeban 12 din | taqreeban 6 din |
| Smart CNIC | taqreeban 31 din | taqreeban 23 din | taqreeban 9 din |

Ye muddat fee ada karne ke baad shuru hoti hai aur is me courier ka waqt shamil nahi ho sakta — door daraz ilaqon me delivery me chand din mazeed lag sakte hain. Agar aap ko card jald chahiye — masalan visa interview ya safar ki wajah se — to Urgent ya Executive category behtar hai; warna Normal category sab se sasti hai.

Aik aham wazahat: pehli dafa standard CNIC Normal category me muft banta hai, lekin renewal muft nahi hai — renewal ki fee upar wali table ke mutabiq ada karni hoti hai. Social media par "renewal muft ho gaya" wali posts par yaqeen na karein; NADRA ki fee schedule me renewal ki fee maujood hai.

Overseas Pakistanion ke liye note: bahir mulk me rehne walon ka card [NICOP](/guides/nicop-card-banane-ka-tareeqa) hota hai, jis ki fee dollar me aur zone ke hisab se mukhtalif hai. Agar aap Pakistan ke andar rehte hain to upar wali rupee fees aap par lagu hoti hain; bahir mulk se renewal ke liye NICOP ki guide dekhein.

## Online Tareeqa: Pak ID Portal/App Se Step-by-Step Apply

Online renewal ka rasta NADRA ka official Pak ID system hai — website id.nadra.gov.pk ya Pak ID mobile app (Android aur iOS dono par dastiyab, publisher NADRA hona chahiye). Steps ye hain:

![Pak ID app par CNIC renewal ke liye mobile se tasveer aur fingerprint verification karte hue aik shakhs](/images/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

1. **Account banayein:** Pak ID app install karein ya id.nadra.gov.pk kholein. Apna mobile number aur email de kar account banayein aur OTP se tasdeeq karein.
2. **Renewal select karein:** Services me se "Identity Card" phir renewal ka option muntakhib karein — standard CNIC ya Smart CNIC, jo card aap chahte hain.
3. **Purana record check karein:** Apna CNIC number darj karein. System aap ka maujooda record dikhayega — naam, walid ka naam, address aur purane card ki tafseel ghaur se check karein. Agar koi ghalti nazar aaye to aage barhne se pehle us ki darustgi ka tareeqa samajh lein.
4. **Tasveer aur fingerprints dein:** App ki rehnumai ke mutabiq live tasveer lein aur mobile camera se fingerprints capture karein. Tasveer dhundli ho ya fingerprints na liye ja sakein to application aage nahi barhegi — achi roshni aur saaf camera se ye step dobara karein.
5. **Category muntakhib karein:** Normal, Urgent ya Executive me se aik chunein. Screen par us category ki fee aur andazan delivery waqt dikhai dega.
6. **Fee ada karein:** Debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR se payment karein aur receipt mehfooz rakhein. Payment ke baad aap ko aik tracking ID milegi — ye ID sambhal kar rakhein, isi se status check hoga.
7. **Application submit karein:** Tamam maloomat ka aakhri dafa jaiza le kar submit karein. Submit hone ke baad SMS/email se tasdeeq aayegi.

Online application ke doran koi step adhura chhor kar band na karein — adhuri application baad me dhoondna mushkil ho sakta hai. Agar app kisi step par atak jaye to app dobara khol kar apni application ki maujooda halat check karein, doosri application shuru karne se pehle pehli wali ka status dekh lein.

## NADRA Centre Par Renew Karne Ka Tareeqa

Agar aap online apply nahi kar sakte — masalan app fingerprints na le sake, record me koi masla ho, ya aap ko centre jana zyada aasan lage — to qareebi NADRA Registration Centre (NRC) par bhi renewal ho jata hai:

1. Qareebi NADRA centre par apna purana CNIC (chahe expire ho) aur aik tasveer ke sath jayein.
2. Counter par renewal ka token lein aur form me apni maloomat darj karein.
3. Biometric verification (fingerprints aur tasveer) centre par hi ho jayegi.
4. Counter par fee ada karein aur receipt ke sath tracking ID wasool karein.
5. Card tayyar hone par ya to centre se wasool karein ya courier ke zariye apne address par mangwayein — ye option form bharte waqt muntakhib hota hai.

Centre par jane se pehle NADRA helpline se apne centre ke auqaat confirm kar lein, aur rush se bachne ke liye subah jaldi jana behtar hai. Centre par bhi wohi fee schedule lagu hota hai jo upar table me diya gaya hai.

## Card Kab Tak Milega Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Card ki delivery ki muddat aap ki muntakhib ki hui category par munhasir hai — standard card ke liye Normal me taqreeban 15 din aur Smart card ke liye Normal me taqreeban 31 din, jaisa ke fees wali table me darj hai. Is me courier ka waqt alag se lag sakta hai.

Status check karne ke do aasan tareeqe hain:

- **Pak ID dashboard:** Pak ID app ya portal me login kar ke apni application ka status dekhein — processing, ready ya delivered ki halat wahan nazar aati hai.
- **SMS se tracking:** Apni tracking ID 8400 par SMS karein, jawab me application ki maujooda halat maloom ho jayegi.

![Courier ke zariye naye CNIC ki delivery ghar ke darwaze par wasool karte hue aik shakhs](/images/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Agar card matlooba muddat ke baad bhi na mile to pehle tracking ID se status check karein, phir NADRA helpline 051-111-786-100 ya mobile se 1777 par rabta karein aur apni tracking ID batayein. Card milne par foran us par naam, number aur muddat check karein — koi ghalti ho to foran NADRA ko report karein, taake darustgi ki application waqt par ho sake.

Naya card milne ke baad purana expired card sambhal kar rakhein ya mehfooz tareeqe se zaya karein — us par aap ka CNIC number aur zaati maloomat hoti hain, is liye use kisi ke sath share na karein. Bank account ya SIM se mutaliq kaam ke liye naya card milte hi apna record update karwa lein, taake dobara koi rukawat na aaye.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Application Ruk Jati Hai

- **Dhundli tasveer ya ghalat fingerprints:** App me tasveer andhere me lena ya fingerprints adhoore dena application rokne ki sab se aam wajah hai. Achi roshni me, saaf camera se ye steps karein.
- **Purane card se mukhtalif maloomat darj karna:** Renewal me naam ya tareekh-e-paidaish purane record se mukhtalif likhna verification me masla paida karta hai. Tabdeeli chahiye to modification ka rasta ikhtiyar karein, renewal me nahi.
- **Ghalat card qism chunna:** Standard aur Smart CNIC ki fee aur muddat mukhtalif hai. Jo card chahiye, wohi qism muntakhib karein — warna fee ya delivery waqt ki shikayat hogi.
- **Tracking ID zaya kar dena:** Payment ke baad mili tracking ID ke baghair status check karna mushkil ho jata hai. Is ka screenshot ya note foran mehfooz karein.
- **Doosri application shuru kar dena:** Pehli application ka status dekhe baghair dobara apply karna do applications ka masla bana sakta hai. Pehle maujooda application ka status check karein.
- **Jhooti "muft renewal" posts par yaqeen:** Renewal ki fee NADRA ki schedule me maujood hai. Koi agent ya post muft renewal ka dawa kare to us se bachein aur sirf official Pak ID portal/app istemal karein.
- **Delivery address ghalat likhna:** Courier aap ke diye hue address par card bhejta hai. Address me ghalti ho to card wapas ja sakta hai — is liye address do dafa check karein.

Agar application reject ho jaye to ghabrayein nahi — reject hone ki wajah Pak ID dashboard ya SMS me batayi jati hai. Wajah durust kar ke dobara apply karein, aur agar wajah samajh na aaye to helpline par tracking ID ke sath rabta karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Expired CNIC ko online renew kar sakte hain?**

Ji haan, expired card ki renewal Pak ID portal (id.nadra.gov.pk) ya Pak ID mobile app se online ho jati hai, bashart ye ke aap ki bunyadi maloomat (naam, tareekh-e-paidaish waghera) me koi tabdeeli na ho. Tasveer aur fingerprints app ke zariye diye jate hain; kuch cases me NADRA centre par biometrics ke liye bulaya ja sakta hai.

**CNIC renewal ki fee kitni hai?**

Standard CNIC renewal ki fee Normal me Rs. 400, Urgent me Rs. 1,150 aur Executive me Rs. 2,150 hai. Smart CNIC (chip wala) ki fee Normal me Rs. 750, Urgent me Rs. 1,500 aur Executive me Rs. 2,500 hai. Hatmi rakam Pak ID ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai — wahi ada karein.

**Renewal me kitna waqt lagta hai?**

Standard card Normal me taqreeban 15 din, Urgent me 12 din aur Executive me 6 din me tayyar hota hai. Smart card ke liye ye muddat Normal me taqreeban 31 din, Urgent me 23 din aur Executive me 9 din hai. Courier ka waqt is se alag ho sakta hai.

**CNIC renewal ka status kaise check karein?**

Do tareeqe hain: Pak ID app ya portal me login kar ke dashboard par status dekhein, ya apni tracking ID 8400 par SMS karein. Dono surton me application ki maujooda halat — processing, ready ya delivered — maloom ho jayegi.

**Kya renewal ke liye NADRA centre jana lazmi hai?**

Nahi, aksar renewals ghar baithe Pak ID app/portal se ho jati hain. Centre sirf un cases me jana parta hai jin me app se biometrics mukammal na hon, record me koi masla ho, ya NADRA mazeed tasdeeq maange. Agar aap ko online tareeqa mushkil lage to centre par bhi renewal ki sahulat maujood hai.
