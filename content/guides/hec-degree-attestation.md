## HEC Attestation Kyun Lazmi Hai

Pakistan ki kisi university ki degree ho — jab tak **HEC (Higher Education Commission)** us ki attestation na kar de, degree **bahir ke mulk me qabool nahi hoti** aur kayi sarkari muamlat me bhi rukawat aati hai. HEC attestation lazmi hai:

- **Bahir nokri ya higher education** (visa process ka pehla qadam)
- **MOFA aur embassy attestation** — HEC ke baghair ye nahi hote
- Kuch **sarkari nokriyon aur equivalence** muamlat ke liye

Sab se bari tabdeeli: HEC ne **fully online, paperless Degree Attestation System (DAS)** launch kar diya hai — ab Islamabad ya regional office ki lambi qataaron me khare hone ki zaroorat nahi. Tamam kaam **eservices.hec.gov.pk** se hota hai.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fee HEC ke naye online system ke rates se li gayi hai aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thi**. Portal par fee system **khud-ba-khud calculate** karta hai — payment se pehle portal ke displayed amount ko hi final samjhein.

## HEC Attestation Process in Pakistan — Quick English Summary

If you searched for the **HEC attestation process** or **HEC Pakistan attestation**, here is the short version:

1. Create an account on **eservices.hec.gov.pk** and complete your academic profile (Matric to your last degree).
2. Upload clear scans of the degree and transcript you want attested, and select only those documents — the fee is calculated automatically.
3. Pay the fee online via 1-Link (1-Bill): **Rs 3,000 per document** — a degree plus transcript costs Rs 6,000 in total.
4. HEC sends your documents to your university for **electronic verification** — this is usually the slowest step.
5. After verification and scrutiny, download your **e-attestation certificate** from the portal. No office visit is needed.

The full step-by-step process in Roman Urdu is given in the sections below.

**Fee change note:** Purane system me attestation **Rs 1,000 per document** thi. Naye fully-online DAS ke tehat fee **Rs 3,000 per document** kar di gayi hai — ye HEC ki taraf se revised rate hai. Payment **1-Link (1-Bill)** ke zariye online hoti hai.

**Hisab ki misal:** Bachelor degree + transcript = 2 documents = **Rs 6,000**.

## Kaun Se Documents Attest Hote Hain

- **Degree** (asal)
- **Transcript / DMC / Result Card**
- **Provisional Certificate** (sirf university ke examination office se jari shuda)
- **HEC Equivalence Letter**

**Photocopies tab hi attest hongi jab asal document pehle attest ho chuka ho** — is liye pehle asal attest karwayen.

## Step-by-Step Online Process

### Step 1: Portal Par Account Banayen

1. **eservices.hec.gov.pk** kholein — **"Sign Up"** par click karein.
2. Apni maloomat bharein: naam, **father name, date of birth, CNIC, marital status, gender, address**.
3. **Ahem:** Tamam maloomat **CNIC aur taleemi documents ke harf-ba-harf mutabiq** hon — aik harf ka farq bhi application rok sakta hai.

### Step 2: Academic Profile Mukammal Karein

1. Login ke baad **"Degree Attestation"** chunein.
2. **Matric (SSC) se le kar aakhri degree tak** tamam taleemi maloomat darj karein: degree title, institution/campus, session, duration, registration/roll number.
3. Sirf **un hi documents ko select karein jin ki attestation chahiye** aur jo aap ke paas mojood hain — fee isi hisab se banti hai.

### Step 3: Documents Upload Karein

Degrees aur transcripts ke **saaf scans** upload karein. Blurry ya kata hua scan scrutiny me reject ho jata hai — din ke rosni me saaf tasveer/scan lein.

### Step 4: University Verification (Sab Se Lamba Marhala)

1. HEC aap ke documents **aap ki university ko electronic verification** ke liye bhejta hai.
2. University confirm karti hai ke degree asal hai aur record se milti hai.
3. Total processing **10-15 working days** aam tor par lagte hain — is ka zyada hissa university verification ka hota hai.
4. **Mashwara:** Apne mulk jana/visa ki deadline se **4-6 hafte pehle** apply karein.

### Step 5: Fee Ada Karein (1-Link)

1. System **kul fee gin kar** consumer number generate karega.
2. **"Proceed for Payment"** ke baad **1-Link (1-Bill)** se kisi bhi bank app, mobile banking ya counter se ada karein.
3. Payment ke baad portal par **"Verify Payment"** par click lazmi karein — baghair is ke application aage nahi barhti.

### Step 6: E-Attestation Certificate Download Karein

1. HEC final review ke baad **SMS aur email** se notification aayega.
2. Apne portal account se **e-Attestation Certificate download** kar lein.
3. Ye **verifiable digital certificate** hai — is ke baad MOFA/embassy attestation ke liye istemal ho sakta hai.
4. **WES/ICAS waghera** ke liye sealed envelope chahiye ho to application ke waqt hi wo option select karein.

## Purana Walk-in / Courier Tareeqa

Jin logon ne purane system me apply kiya tha ya jin ke liye walk-in zaroori hai:

- **Walk-in:** HEC Islamabad head office aur regional centers (Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta) — **urgent cases** ke liye appointment milta tha.
- **Courier:** Authorized courier services (TCS waghera) normal applications ke liye documents le jaati aur wapas laati thin.

Ab main raasta **online DAS** hi hai — nayi application online hi karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Waqt Zaya Karti Hain

1. **Naam ka farq:** CNIC, matric sanad aur degree par naam alag ho to pehle ye masla hal karein — HEC match na ho to aage nahi barhta.
2. **Galat document select karna:** Jo document paas nahi, wo select kar lene se fee barh jati hai aur application atakti hai.
3. **Payment verify na karna:** 1-Link se pays de kar "Verify Payment" na dabana sab se aam bhool hai.
4. **Non-HEC-recognized university:** Degree jis university ki hai wo HEC se recognized hona lazmi hai — pehle hec.gov.pk se check kar lein.
5. **Deadline ki ghalti:** University verification me waqt lagta hai — aakhri dinon me apply karna visa miss karwa sakta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya ab HEC office jana hi nahi parta?**
Naye online system me tamam process ghar bethe ho sakta hai — documents upload, university verification, payment aur certificate download sab online.

**Matric/Inter ki sanad bhi attest karwani hogi?**
HEC sirf **university degrees** attest karta hai. Matric/Inter ki attestation **IBCC** se hoti hai — dono alag idare hain.

**Fee waqai Rs 3,000 ho gayi hai?**
Ji — naye online DAS ke tehat Rs 3,000 per document hai (pehle Rs 1,000 thi). Portal fee khud calculate karke dikhata hai, wahi final hai.

**Agar university jawab na de to?**
Application dashboard par status nazar aata hai — university verification ruki ho to apni university ke **examination/controller office** se rabta karein, wahi se tezi aati hai.

**Kya koi aur meri taraf se apply kar sakta hai?**
Online system me aap **khud apne account** se apply karte hain — CNIC ki maloomat aap ki hoti hain. Kisi agent ko apna portal password na dein.

**Attested document ki verification baad me kaise hoti hai?**
E-attestation certificate **QR/portal par verifiable** hai — employer ya embassy online tasdeeq kar sakte hain.

**What is the HEC attestation process in Pakistan?**
The HEC attestation process in Pakistan is fully online: create an account on eservices.hec.gov.pk, complete your academic profile, upload your degree and transcript, pay Rs 3,000 per document via 1-Link (1-Bill), wait for your university's electronic verification, and download the e-attestation certificate from the portal — no office visit is needed.

**How long does HEC Pakistan attestation take?**
Most applications complete in about 2 to 4 weeks. University verification takes the longest — if your university responds quickly, the whole HEC Pakistan attestation finishes sooner. Start at least 6-8 weeks before any visa or admission deadline.

**What documents are needed for HEC degree attestation?**
For HEC degree attestation you need the original degree and transcript/DMC you want attested, your CNIC, and clear scanned copies of all of them for upload on eservices.hec.gov.pk. Your name and details must match exactly across your CNIC, Matric certificate and degree — a spelling mismatch is the most common reason an HEC degree attestation application is returned.

## HEC Ke Baad Ka Silsila: MOFA Aur Embassy Attestation

Bahir mulk ke liye aksar sirf HEC kaafi nahi hoti — poora silsila ye hai:

1. **HEC attestation** (ye guide) — sab se pehla qadam.
2. **MOFA (Foreign Office) attestation** — Islamabad aur camp offices se; HEC ke baghair degree MOFA attest nahi karti.
3. **Embassy attestation** — jis mulk jana hai, us ki embassy. Kuch mulk **Apostille** qabool karte hain.

**Waqt ka hisab:** Har marhale me din lagte hain — visa ki deadline se kam az kam **6-8 hafte pehle** silsila shuru kar dein. Agenton ke "2 din me sab kuch" wale jhoote wado se bachein — embassy attestation ki fees aur tareeqe mutaliqa embassy ki website se khud confirm karein.

## Degree Equivalence (Bahir Ki Degree Pakistan Me)

Agar aap ki degree **Pakistan se bahir** ki hai aur yahan nokri/admission ke liye chahiye to HEC ka **Equivalence** process alag hai:

1. eservices.hec.gov.pk par **Equivalence** ki alag application hoti hai.
2. Bahir ki university ka HEC-recognized hona aur us mulk ke system se milna zaroori hai.
3. Kuch degrees (medical MBBS waghera) ke liye mutaliqa council (PMDC waghera) se alag tasdeeq bhi chahiye hoti hai.

## Agar Application Reject Ya Wapas Aa Jaye

Wapis aane ki **teen aam wajuhaat** aur un ka hal:

1. **Naam ka mismatch:** CNIC, matric aur degree par farq. Hal: pehle bunyadi documents me tas-heeh karwayen (NADRA / board), phir attestation. Kuch surat me HEC **supporting affidavit** bhi qabool karti hai — portal ki scrutiny note ghor se parhein.
2. **University ka jawab na ana:** University verification ruki hai to **university ke Controller of Examinations** ke daftar se rabta karein — aksar file wahi atki hoti hai.
3. **Scan ghair-wazeh:** Dobara **saaf scan** upload karein — document seedha, chaaron konay nazar aane chahiyen.

**Ahem:** Scrutiny ke objections portal par **likhit** aate hain — agenton ki zabani bataoon par paisa na dein, portal ka note khud parhein aur usi ke mutabiq kagaz pura karein.

## Overseas Pakistanis Ke Liye

Bahir rehne wale bhi **eservices portal se apply** kar sakte hain — Pakistani embassy/consulate ke zariye bhi degree attestation hoti hai, lekin tareeqa aur fees har mulk ke consulate ke hisab se mukhtalif hain. Embassy ki website se pehle confirm kar lein. Pakistan me koi rishtedaar ho to usey **apna portal password na dein** — application apne account se khud track karein.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya duplicate degree ki attestation ho sakti hai?**
Agar asal degree gum ho gayi hai to pehle **university se duplicate degree** lein — phir us ki attestation hogi. Gumshudgi ki FIR/report bhi sath lagegi.

**Transcript (DMC) aur degree dono attest karwani chahiyen?**
Zyada tar cases me **donon** mangi jati hain — degree bhi, transcript bhi. Job ke ad me wazeh na ho to donon karwa lein, baad me waqt bachta hai.

## Agla Qadam: WES/ECA Ke Liye Sealed Envelope

Agar aap **Canada/USA immigration (WES, IQAS, CES)** ke liye ja rahe hain to attestation ke saath **sealed envelope** bhi lazmi hota hai — university aur HEC ki sealed copies wohi idarah qabool karta hai.

- Application ke waqt **sealed envelope ka option** select karein.
- Envelope **khud na kholain** — khula envelope ECA idarah reject kar deta hai.
- Courier se seedha mutaliqa idare ko bhejne ka intizam karein.
