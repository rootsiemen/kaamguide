## Pehli Baat: EOBI Pension Kya Hai Aur Kis Ko Milti Hai

**EOBI pension** Pakistan ke private sector me kaam karne wale mulazmeen ke liye hukumat ki taraf se mahana pension hai. Is ka matlab hai ke jis shakhs ne apni jawani me kisi factory, company, dukaan ya kisi bhi registered idare me naukri ki aur us ki tankhwah se har mahine chhota sa hissa EOBI fund me jama hota raha, retirement ke baad usay har mahine pension milti hai — zindagi bhar, aur us ke inteqal ke baad us ke khandan ko bhi.

Ye scheme **Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI)** chalati hai, jo EOBI Act 1976 ke tehet bana hua aik wafaqi idara hai. Qanoon ke mutabiq har woh idara jis me **5 ya us se zyada mulazmeen** kaam karte hain, usay apne mulazmeen ko EOBI me register karwana lazmi hai. Employer idare ko register karwata hai, phir har mulazim ko aik **registration card (PI-03)** milta hai — ye card aap ki pension ki buniyad hai, isay sambhal kar rakhein.

Tankhwah se contribution is tarah kata hai: mulazim ki taraf se **1%** aur employer ki taraf se **5%**. Ye raqam aap ki tankhwah se kaat kar EOBI fund me jama hoti rehti hai, aur retirement par wohi fund aap ko mahana pension deta hai. Aap ko is ke liye alag se koi bachat ya investment nahi karni — sirf ye yaqeeni banana hai ke aap registered hain aur aap ki contribution ka record durust hai.

Sab se ahem baat: **pension aap ka haq hai, kisi ki mehrbani nahi.** Is ke liye kisi agent, wakeel ya middleman ki zaroorat nahi, aur EOBI registration ya pension form ki **koi fee nahi** hoti. Jo shakhs pension dilane ke badle paise mange, us se hoshiyar rahein — woh fraud hai.

## EOBI Pension Ki Iqsam: Buzurg, Bewa, Mazoor Aur Grant

EOBI sirf aik pension nahi deta — mukhtalif halaat ke liye mukhtalif benefits hain. Apni surat-e-haal ke mutabiq sahi qisam pehchan lein, kyun ke har qisam ka form aur kagzaat thore mukhtalif hote hain:

| Pension / Benefit | Kis ko milti hai |
|---|---|
| **Old-Age Pension** | Retirement ki umar (mard 60, aurat 55) par, kam az kam 15 saal contribution ke sath — mahana pension, zindagi bhar |
| **Early Pension** | Jo shakhs umar se 5 saal pehle retire ho jaye (mard 55, aurat 50) — kam raqam par mahana pension |
| **Survivor Pension** | Insured mulazim ke inteqal ke baad us ki bewa/bewah aur bachon ko — mahana pension |
| **Invalidity Pension** | Jo mulazim mustaqil tor par mazoor ho jaye aur kaam na kar sake — mahana pension |
| **Old-Age Grant** | Jin ki contribution 2 se 15 saal ke darmiyan ho (pension ke liye kam) — aik dafa lump sum raqam |

Old-age pension sab se aam hai aur is guide ka markaz bhi wohi hai, lekin neeche survivor aur invalidity ke kagzaat bhi diye gaye hain taa ke kisi ko alag guide na dhoondni pare.

## Ehliyaat: Pension Ke Liye Kya Shartein Hain

Pension claim karne se pehle ye teen shartein check kar lein — in me se aik bhi poori na ho to claim ruk sakta hai:

**1. Registered mulazim hona:** Aap ka naam EOBI me registered hona chahiye aur aap ke paas PI-03 registration card hona chahiye. Agar aap ne kisi 5 ya zyada mulazmeen wale idare me kaam kiya hai to qanoon ke mutabiq aap ka employer aap ko register karwane ka paband tha. Agar employer ne register nahi karwaya, to aap khud bhi registration karwa sakte hain — EOBI ke **Form PE-02** par rozgar ka saboot (appointment letter, salary slips, service book ya settlement letter) laga kar qareebi regional office me darkhwast dein.

**2. Umar ki shart:** Old-age pension ke liye mard ki umar **60 saal** aur aurat ki umar **55 saal** honi chahiye. Jo 5 saal pehle claim karna chahe, woh early pension le sakta hai (mard 55, aurat 50), lekin us ki raqam kam hoti hai. Umar ka saboot aam tor par aap ke [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur service record se liya jata hai — 2026 se CNIC-based age verification ko tarjeeh di ja rahi hai, is liye CNIC par darj tareekh-e-pedaish aur service record ki tareekh aik jaisi honi chahiye.

**3. Contribution ki muddat:** Old-age pension ke liye kam az kam **15 saal** ki contribution (insurable employment) lazmi hai. Invalidity pension ke liye kul 5 saal contribution chahiye, jin me se 3 saal aakhri 5 saalon me hon. Agar contribution 2 saal se zyada aur 15 saal se kam hai to mahana pension ki bajaye old-age grant (aik dafa lump sum) milta hai.

**Agar idara band ho gaya ho:** Agar aap ka purana idara band ho chuka hai aur employer ke certificates nahi mil rahe, to EOBI employer ke kagzaat ki jagah aap ke apne rozgar ke saboot qabool karta hai — appointment letter, resignation ya dismissal letter, full and final settlement letter, service book, rozgar ke bare me affidavit, ya social security card. Koi aik saboot kafi nahi hota, jitne zyada hon utna behtar hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Claim Se Pehle Ye Sab Tayyar Rakhein

Claim form jama karwane se pehle tamam kagzaat ki aik file bana lein. EOBI ke claim form ki apni checklist ke mutabiq ye kagzaat chahiye hote hain — attestation **grade 17 ya us se upar ke officer** se karwani hoti hai:

- **Apna CNIC** — 3 attested photocopies (donon taraf se); [CNIC banane ya renewal ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein
- **Sharik-e-hayat ka CNIC** — attested copy (agar sharik ka inteqal ho gaya ho to death certificate ya affidavit)
- **4 recent tasaveer** — attested (teen peeche se, aik saamne se)
- **Service certificate** — aakhri employer se jari shuda
- **Age certificate** — aakhri employer se jari shuda
- **Original PI-03 registration card** — ye original chahiye hota hai, photocopy nahi
- **12 mahine ki salary statement** — rozgar khatam hone se pehle ke aakhri 12 mahine ki (1983 se pehle ki service ke liye aakhri 6 mahine ki)
- **No-claim certificate** — employer ki taraf se ke aap ke liye pehle koi pension, grant, survivor ya invalidity claim jama nahi hua

![Pension claim ke kagzaat: CNIC card, claim form aur pen aik desk par rakhe hue](/images/eobi-pension-claim-karne-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

**Survivor pension (bewa/bewah) ke liye izafi kagzaat:** Mulazim ka [death certificate](/guides/death-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa), Nikah Nama, NADRA ka [Family Registration Certificate (FRC)](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa), aur 18 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke B-Form. Survivor pension ka form alag hota hai — regional office se "Survivor Pension Form" lein, aam old-age form se kaam nahi chalega.

## Pension Kitni Milti Hai: Raqam, Formula Aur Fees

| Cheez | Raqam / Tafseel |
|---|---|
| **Kam az kam mahana pension** | **Rs 11,500** (1 January 2025 se; pehle Rs 10,000 thi) |
| **Formula-based pension me izafa** | **15%** (1 January 2025 se) |
| **Registration ya form ki fee** | **Koi fee nahi** — EOBI ke mutabiq form aur registration muft hain |
| **Pension card jari hone ki muddat** | Kagzaat mukammal hon to **30 din** ke andar |
| **Der se claim par arrears** | Zyada se zyada **6 mahine** ke arrears milte hain |
| **Payment ka tareeqa** | Har mahine aap ke muntakhab bank account / branch me |

Kam az kam pension Rs 11,500 mahana hai — ye raqam wafaqi cabinet ne 1 January 2025 se barha kar Rs 10,000 se Rs 11,500 ki thi, aur usi ke sath formula ke tehet banti hui pensionon me 15% izafa bhi kiya gaya tha. Aap ki asal pension is se zyada bhi ho sakti hai, kyun ke woh aap ki tankhwah aur kul service ke saalon se EOBI ke formula ke mutabiq banti hai. Is liye do mulazmeen ki pension aik jaisi nahi hoti — jis ki tankhwah aur service zyada, us ki pension zyada.

![Mahana pension bank account me jama hone ki alamat: calendar aur bank card](/images/eobi-pension-claim-karne-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Aik baat zehen me rakhein: August 2026 me EOBI ne bataya tha ke aane wale izafe ka faisla fund ki maliyati halat aur actuarial review par munhasir hai, jo qanoon ke tehet lazmi hai. Is liye Rs 11,500 se upar ki koi nayi raqam tab tak hatmi na samjhein jab tak EOBI ki website ya helpline se us ki tasdeeq na ho jaye. Pension har mahine aap ke bank account me aati hai — is liye form me wohi bank account likhein jo aap ke naam par active ho.

## Step-by-Step: EOBI Pension Claim Karne Ka Tareeqa

**Step 1 — Claim form hasil karein:** EOBI ki official website **eobi.gov.pk** se Old-Age Pension claim form download karein, ya apne qareebi EOBI regional office se muft hasil karein. Form muft hai — koi is ke paise mange to na dein. (Note: Form PE-02 registration ka form hai, pension claim ka nahi — claim ke liye alag old-age pension claim form hota hai.)

**Step 2 — Employer ke kagzaat lein:** Apne aakhri employer se service certificate, age certificate, aakhri 12 mahine ki salary statement aur no-claim certificate lein. Ye chaaron kagzaat form ka lazmi hissa hain. Agar employer taawun na kare ya idara band ho gaya ho to upar bataye gaye apne rozgar ke saboot jama karein.

**Step 3 — Kagzaat attach karein aur attest karwayein:** Oopar di gayi list ke tamam kagzaat form ke sath lagayein. CNIC ki copies, tasaveer aur tamam certificates grade 17 ya us se upar ke officer se attest karwayein. Original PI-03 card bhi sath rakhein — woh jama karwana hota hai.

**Step 4 — Form mukammal bhar dein:** Form me apna naam, walid/shohar ka naam, purana aur naya CNIC number, sharik-e-hayat ka naam, mukammal pata, EOBI registration number, service record ke mutabiq tareekh-e-pedaish aur umar, EOBI scheme me shamil hone ki tareekh, rozgar khatam hone ki tareekh, tamam idaron ke naam jahan kaam kiya, darkhwast ki tareekh aur mobile number darj karein. Form ke aakhir me employer ka certificate bhi bharwana hota hai — is ke baghair form namukammal samjha jata hai.

**Step 5 — Regional office me jama karwayein:** Mukammal form apne qareebi EOBI regional office me jama karwayein aur **rasheed (receipt) lazmi lein**. Rasheed par aap ka claim form number darj hota hai — ye number status check karne ke liye chahiye hoga, isay sambhal kar rakhein.

**Step 6 — Verification aur pension card:** EOBI aap ke kagzaat aur contribution record ki verification karta hai. Kagzaat mukammal hon to EOBI **30 din ke andar pension card** jari kar deta hai. Card milne ke baad aap ki mahana pension aap ke muntakhab bank branch/account me aana shuru ho jati hai.

**Step 7 — Waqt par apply karein:** Ehl honay ke **12 mahine ke andar** darkhwast dein. Der se claim ki surat me bhi darkhwast achi wajah se qabool ho sakti hai, lekin arrears zyada se zyada **6 mahine** ke milte hain — us se pehle ke mahine zaya ho jate hain. Is liye retirement ki tareekh aate hi foran apply karna sab se behtar hai.

## Pension Status Online Kaise Check Karein

Claim jama karwane ke baad aap ko har dafa office jaane ki zaroorat nahi — status is tarah check karein:

1. EOBI ki official website **eobi.gov.pk** kholein (website **http** par khulti hai, https par nahi — address ghalat likhne par nakli websites khul sakti hain, is liye eobi.gov.pk bilkul durust likhein).
2. Website par **"Pension Claim Status"** ka section kholein.
3. Apna **claim form number** (jo rasheed par hai) ya **CNIC number** darj karein.
4. Aap ke claim ki maujooda halat screen par aa jayegi — ke verification ho rahi hai, card jari ho gaya hai, ya koi kagaz kam hai.

Agar online status samajh na aaye ya website na khule to EOBI ki muft helpline **08000-3624 (08000-EOBI)** par subah 8 se sham 4 baje tak, peer se juma, rabta karein. Helpline par apna CNIC number aur claim form number tayyar rakhein. 2026 me EOBI ne pensioners ke liye NADRA ke face-verification system se bhi jorna shuru kiya hai — agar aap ko face verification ka message aaye to woh EOBI ki janib se hi hoga, kisi ghair-mutaliqa link par apni tasveer ya CNIC upload na karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Claim Rok Deti Hain

- **Der se apply karna:** 12 mahine guzarne ke baad arrears sirf 6 mahine ke milte hain — baqi mahine hamesha ke liye zaya ho jate hain.
- **PI-03 card gum hona:** Original registration card ke baghair claim atak jata hai. Card gum ho to pehle us ka duplicate nikalwayein, phir claim dein.
- **CNIC aur service record me umar ka farq:** Agar CNIC par tareekh-e-pedaish aur service record ki tareekh aik jaisi na ho to verification ruk jati hai. Pehle NADRA record durust karwayein — [birth certificate](/guides/birth-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) se mutaliqa maloomat yahan dekhein.
- **Employer ke kagzaat adhoore chhorna:** Service certificate, age certificate ya salary statement me se aik bhi kagaz kam ho to file wapas ho jati hai. Jama karwane se pehle checklist se aik aik cheez mila lein.
- **Attestation na karwana:** Baghair attestation ke copies qabool nahi hotin — grade 17 ya upar ke officer se attest karwana lazmi hai.
- **Agent ko paise dena:** EOBI ka koi form, registration ya claim muft hai. Jo shakhs pension dilane ke naam par paise mange, woh aap ko loot raha hai — seedha helpline 08000-3624 par shikayat karein.
- **Ghair-sarkari websites par CNIC dalna:** Sirf eobi.gov.pk istemal karein. "EOBI pension check" ke naam par bani hui kai websites sirf aap ka data chori karti hain.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**EOBI pension kitni milti hai?**

Kam az kam pension Rs 11,500 mahana hai, jo 1 January 2025 se laagu hai (is se pehle Rs 10,000 thi). Aap ki asal pension is se zyada bhi ho sakti hai, kyun ke woh aap ki tankhwah aur kul service ke saalon se EOBI ke formula ke mutabiq banti hai. Formula-based pensionon me 1 January 2025 se 15% izafa bhi kiya gaya tha. Hatmi raqam ki tasdeeq helpline 08000-3624 ya regional office se kar lein.

**15 saal se kam service ho to kya milega?**

Agar aap ki contribution 2 saal se zyada aur 15 saal se kam hai to aap ko mahana pension ki bajaye **old-age grant** milta hai — ye aik dafa milne wali lump sum raqam hoti hai. Agar contribution 2 saal se bhi kam hai to na pension milti hai na grant. Invalidity (mustaqil mazoor hone) ki surat me shart mukhtalif hai: kul 5 saal contribution, jin me se 3 saal aakhri 5 saalon me hon.

**Claim form kahan se milega aur koi fee hai?**

Old-Age Pension claim form EOBI ki official website eobi.gov.pk se muft download hota hai, ya qareebi EOBI regional office se muft milta hai. EOBI ke mutabiq registration ya kisi bhi pension form ki **koi fee nahi** hoti. Form PE-02 se ulajhna na — woh insured person ki registration ka form hai, pension claim ka nahi.

**Pension kitne din me shuru hoti hai?**

Kagzaat mukammal hon to EOBI 30 din ke andar pension card jari kar deta hai, aur card milne ke baad mahana pension aap ke muntakhab bank account me aana shuru ho jati hai. Agar 30 din guzar jayein aur koi khabar na aaye to apne claim form number ke sath helpline 08000-3624 par rabta karein ya regional office ja kar status poochein.

**Bewa ko pension kaise milegi?**

Insured mulazim ke inteqal ke baad us ki bewa (ya bewah) ko survivor pension milti hai. Is ke liye Survivor Pension Form ke sath mulazim ka death certificate, Nikah Nama, NADRA ka Family Registration Certificate (FRC) aur 18 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke B-Form jama karwane hote hain. Pension us waqt tak milti rehti hai jab tak bewa dobara shadi na kar le ya bachay baligh na ho jayein — tafseel regional office se confirm kar lein.
