## Apne Naam Par SIMs Check Karna Kyun Zaroori Hai

Pakistan me har mobile SIM kisi na kisi ke **CNIC** par registered hoti hai — aur qanoon ki nazar me us SIM se jo kuch bhi hota hai, us ki zimmedari usi shakhs par aati hai jis ke naam par SIM hai. Is liye waqtan fawaqtan ye check karna bohat zaroori hai ke aap ke CNIC par **kul kitni SIMs** registered hain aur kya un me koi aisi SIM to nahi jo aap ne kabhi li hi nahi.

Ye masla aam hai: kisi dukaan par SIM lete waqt aap ke CNIC ki copy se **extra SIM** nikal li jati hai, ya purani SIM jo aap ne band karwa di thi, woh ab bhi aap ke naam par chal rahi hoti hai. June 2026 me PTA ne aam awam ko khaas tor par ye hidayat di thi ke agar aap ke naam par registered kisi SIM se **ghair-qanooni kaam** hota hai to registered malik ko jawabdeh thehraya ja sakta hai — chahe SIM asal me koi aur istemal kar raha ho. Is liye apne naam ki SIMs par nazar rakhna sirf ehtiyat nahi, **apni hifazat** hai.

Achi khabar ye hai ke check karna **muft ya taqreeban muft** hai aur sirf **30 second** lagte hain. PTA ne is ke liye do official tareeqe diye hain: **668 par SMS** aur **cnic.sims.pk portal**. Dono tareeqe is guide me step-by-step hain — aur sath ye bhi ke koi ajnabi SIM nazar aaye to use **band kaise karwayein**.

> **Note:** Is guide ka tareeqa PTA ki official SIM Information System (cnic.sims.pk) aur PTA ke awami elanat se liya gaya hai aur **7 November 2026 ko check kiya gaya tha**. Portal par koi fee ya SIM ki had darj nahi — jo charges aur had is guide me likhi hai, woh PTA ke purane elanat aur 2026 ki mutabar reports se li gayi hai; hatmi raqam ya had ke liye portal ya apne operator se tasdeeq kar lein.

## PTA SIM Information System Kya Hai

PTA ka **SIM Information System (SVMS)** woh official nizaam hai jis se har shehri apne CNIC par registered SIMs ki tadad maloom kar sakta hai. Ye nizaam PTA aur NADRA ke mushtarka record par chalta hai — is liye is me dikhai dene wali tadad **hatmi aur qanooni** hoti hai, kisi teesri website ke andaze par mabni nahi.

Is system ki do khaas baatein yaad rakhein:

- **Ye sirf tadad batata hai, number nahi.** Aap ko ye pata chalega ke Jazz ki 2, Zong ki 1, Telenor ki 0 SIMs hain — lekin poore mobile number screen par nahi aate. Ye aap ki **privacy** ke liye hai.
- **Sirf apna CNIC check kar sakte hain.** Kisi doosre shakhs ke number se us ka naam ya CNIC maloom karne ka koi official tareeqa nahi hai. Jo websites "kisi bhi number ka malik batane" ka dawa karti hain, woh ghair-qanooni databases bechti hain — un se **door rahein**.

Check karne ke **teen official tareeqe** hain, jo agle sections me hain. Sab se asan 668 SMS hai, sab se mukammal cnic.sims.pk portal hai.

## Tareeqa 1: 668 Par SMS Bhej Kar Check Karein

Ye sab se purana aur sab se asan tareeqa hai — is ke liye **internet ki zaroorat nahi**, kisi bhi sada mobile se bhi kaam ho jata hai. PTA ne ye service **October 2009** me shuru ki thi aur aaj bhi chal rahi hai.

**Step-by-step tareeqa:**

1. Apne mobile ka **Messages** app kholein.
2. Message me apna **13 hinsay ka CNIC number baghair dash ke** likhein — masalan `3520212345671`. Koi aur lafz na likhein, sirf number.
3. Ye SMS **668** par bhej dein. 668 ko **call na karein** — ye sirf SMS short code hai.
4. Chand second me jawab aayega jis me **har operator ke hisab se** SIMs ki tadad hogi — masalan Jazz: 2, Zong: 1, Telenor: 0, Ufone: 0, SCO: 0.

**Kuch zaroori baatein:**

- SMS bhejne se pehle apni SIM me **thora balance** rakhein — is SMS par aam tor par **Rs 2 + tax** lagta hai (ye raqam PTA ne service ke aghaz par batayi thi aur 2026 ki reports bhi yahi kehti hain; portal par koi charge darj nahi, is liye hatmi raqam aap ka operator batayega).
- CNIC **baghair dash aur baghair space** ke likhna lazmi hai — dash ke sath likha number ghalat samjha ja sakta hai.
- Agar jawab na aaye to dobara bhej dein ya **cnic.sims.pk** wala tareeqa azmayein — kabhi kabhi network ki takheer hoti hai.

## Tareeqa 2: cnic.sims.pk Portal Se Online Check Karein

Agar aap ke paas internet hai to **cnic.sims.pk** sab se behtar tareeqa hai — ye **bilkul muft** hai, screen par wazeh natija dikhata hai aur **Pakistan se bahir** se bhi kaam karta hai (jo log bahir mulk me hain, un ke liye yehi tareeqa hai).

**Step-by-step tareeqa:**

1. Apne browser me **cnic.sims.pk** kholein — address khud type karein, kisi message ya ad me mile link par click na karein.
2. Screen par **"SIM Information System"** likha hoga aur ye hidayat: "To find out the total number of SIMs registered against your CNIC, enter your CNIC and click Submit."
3. **CNIC Number** wale khane me apna 13 hinsay ka CNIC baghair dash ke likhein (misaal ke tor par `6110123456789`).
4. **"I'm not a robot"** wala reCAPTCHA tick karein.
5. **Submit** par click karein — screen par har operator ke hisab se aap ke naam par registered SIMs ki tadad aa jayegi.

**Portal ke baare me chand baatein:**

- Portal par **Foreign ID** ka alag tab bhi hai — ghair-mulki shehri apni Foreign ID se check kar sakte hain.
- Portal par **koi fee nahi** likhi aur **SIM ki had bhi nahi** likhi — sirf check ka kaam hota hai.
- November 2026 me portal check karte waqt reCAPTCHA par ye likha nazar aaya: "This site is exceeding reCAPTCHA Enterprise free quota" — is se check par koi farq nahi parta, Submit phir bhi kaam karta hai.
- Apna CNIC sirf **official portal** par dalein. Milti-julti naam wali websites (jaise simdata wali sites) par CNIC dalna khatarnak ho sakta hai — hamesha address bar me **cnic.sims.pk** dekh kar tasdeeq karein.

## Tareeqa 3: MNP 667 — Ek SIM Ka Malik Maloom Karein

Upar wale dono tareeqe **kul tadad** batate hain. Agar aap ye jan'na chahte hain ke **aap ke haath me jo SIM hai, woh kis ke naam par hai**, to us ke liye alag tareeqa hai:

1. Us SIM se **MNP** likh kar **667** par SMS bhej dein.
2. Jawab me us SIM ke registered malik ka **naam aur CNIC ka kuch hissa** (masked) aa jayega.

Ye tareeqa khaas tor par tab kaam aata hai jab aap ne **second-hand mobile** liya ho, ya koi purani SIM mili ho aur ye maloom karna ho ke woh kis ke naam par hai.

Is ke ilawa ek aur mufeed code hai: kisi number ka **network maloom karne** ke liye `N 03xxxxxxxxx` likh kar **76367** par bhej dein (masalan `N 03001234567`) — jawab me bataya jayega ke ye number kis network ka hai. Is par bhi aam tor par **Rs 2 + tax** lagta hai.

## Fees Aur Ek CNIC Par SIMs Ki Had

Neeche wali table me teeno tareeqon ki fees aur waqt ek jaga par hai:

| Tareeqa | Fee | Waqt | Kya milega |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 668 par SMS | Taqreeban Rs 2 + tax | Chand second | Har operator ke hisab se SIMs ki tadad |
| cnic.sims.pk portal | Muft | Chand second | Wahi tadad, screen par, bahir mulk se bhi |
| MNP 667 par SMS | Mamuli SMS charges | Chand second | Us SIM ke malik ka naam + masked CNIC |
| N 76367 par SMS | Taqreeban Rs 2 + tax | Chand second | Kisi number ka network |

**Ek CNIC par SIMs ki had:** PTA ke nizaam ke mutabiq ek CNIC par zyada se zyada **5 voice SIMs + 3 data-only SIMs** (kul 8) register ho sakti hain. Lekin **September 2026** me PTA chairman ne National Assembly ki committee ke samne **kul 5 SIMs** ki had ka zikr kiya tha — is liye ye mumkin hai ke had me tabdeeli ho ya ho rahi ho. Portal par check karte waqt wahan dikhai dene wali had hi hatmi hogi; agar aap ki tadad had ke qareeb hai to nayi SIM lene se pehle **portal par tasdeeq** lazmi kar lein.

**Ahem:** SIM check karwana **muft ya taqreeban muft** hai — agar koi dukaan ya website aap se is kaam ke **Rs 500 ya Rs 1,000** mange to woh dhoka hai. Khud check karein, kisi ko paise na dein.

## Ghair-Zaroori Ya Ajnabi SIM Band Karwane Ka Tareeqa

Agar check karne par koi aisi SIM nazar aaye jo aap ne **kabhi li hi nahi**, ya jo aap istemal nahi karte, to use **foran band karwayein** — der karna khatarnak ho sakta hai, kyun ke us SIM se hone wale har kaam ki zimmedari aap par aayegi.

**Band karwane ka tareeqa:**

1. **Operator ki franchise ya service centre** par jayein — wohi operator jis ki SIM hai (Jazz, Zong, Telenor ya Ufone).
2. Apna **asal CNIC** sath le jayein — baghair CNIC ke SIM band nahi hogi.
3. Bataein ke ye SIM aap ke naam par hai lekin aap ki nahi — woh tasdeeq ke baad use **block** kar denge.
4. Chand din baad **dobara 668 par SMS** bhej kar tasdeeq kar lein ke tadad kam ho gayi hai.

**Agar franchise madad na kare ya ghalat istemal ki shikayat karni ho:**

- PTA ke **Complaint Management System** par shikayat darj karwayein: **complaint.pta.gov.pk**.
- PTA ki helpline **0800-55055** par bhi rabta kar sakte hain (ye number PTA ne SIM se mutaliq shikayat ke liye diya hai).
- Agar aap ko lagta hai ke aap ke CNIC se **fraud** hua hai to qareebi police station ya FIA Cybercrime Wing ko bhi ittila dein — aur apne [police character certificate](/guides/police-character-certificate) ki guide bhi dekh lein agar aap ko kisi kaam ke liye character certificate chahiye.

**Nayi SIM lete waqt ehtiyat:** SIM hamesha **authorised franchise ya service centre** se lein, sadak kinare lage stall se nahi. SIM lete waqt **biometric tasdeeq** lazmi hoti hai — agar koi dukaan baghair biometric ke SIM de raha ho to woh ghair-qanooni hai, wahan se SIM na lein. Aur SIM lene ke **chand din baad** 668 par check kar lein ke aap ke naam par sirf wahi SIMs hain jo aap ne li hain.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Aur Dhoke

**1. "Kisi bhi number ka malik batayein" wali websites:** Koi official tareeqa kisi ajnabi ke number se us ka naam ya CNIC nahi batata. Jo website ye dawa kare, woh ya to **paise loot-ti** hai ya aap ka CNIC **chori** karti hai. Sirf cnic.sims.pk istemal karein.

**2. Apna CNIC kisi ko SMS ya WhatsApp par bhejna:** 668 ke ilawa **kisi number par apna CNIC na bhejein**. Koi shakhs ya page aap se CNIC mange to woh fraud ho sakta hai. Aap ka CNIC aap ki pehchan hai — is se SIM, bank account aur bohat kuch khul sakta hai.

**3. Dash ke sath CNIC likhna:** 668 ya portal par CNIC hamesha **baghair dash** ke likhein. Dash ke sath likhne par nizaam number ko pehchan nahi pata aur ghalat jawab aa sakta hai.

**4. SIM ki tadad dekh kar mutma'in ho jana:** Sirf tadad dekhna kafi nahi — agar tadad **aap ki yaad se zyada** hai to foran franchise jayein. "Shayad purani SIM hogi" keh kar chorna khatarnaak hai.

**5. Second-hand mobile baghair check ke khareedna:** Mobile khareedne se pehle us ki SIM par **MNP 667** par bhej kar malik check karein, aur mobile ki registration ke liye hamari [PTA mobile registration (DIRBS) guide](/guides/pta-mobile-registration-dirbs) bhi parh lein — bahir se aaya mobile baghair registration ke band ho jata hai.

**6. Apna CNIC dobara banwana ho to:** Agar aap ka CNIC gum ho gaya ho to pehle [gumshuda CNIC dobara banane ka tareeqa](/guides/gumshuda-cnic-dobara-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh lein — gumshuda CNIC par SIM nikalna fraud ka sab se aam tareeqa hai. Naya CNIC banwane ke liye [NADRA CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur status jan'ne ke liye [CNIC status tracking](/guides/cnic-status-tracking-ka-tareeqa) guides kaam ayengi.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Apne naam par kitni SIMs hain, ye kaise check karein?**

Apna 13 hinsay ka CNIC number baghair dash ke **668** par SMS karein — jawab me har operator ke hisab se SIMs ki tadad aa jayegi. Ya **cnic.sims.pk** khol kar wahan CNIC dal kar Submit karein — ye tareeqa muft hai aur bahir mulk se bhi kaam karta hai.

**668 par SMS bhejne ki fee kitni hai?**

Aam tor par **Rs 2 + tax** lagta hai — ye raqam PTA ne service ke aghaz par batayi thi aur 2026 ki reports bhi yahi kehti hain. Portal (cnic.sims.pk) par koi fee nahi likhi aur wahan check **bilkul muft** hai. Hatmi charge aap ka mobile operator batayega.

**Ek CNIC par zyada se zyada kitni SIMs ho sakti hain?**

PTA ke nizaam ke mutabiq ek CNIC par **5 voice SIMs + 3 data-only SIMs** (kul 8) register ho sakti hain. September 2026 me PTA chairman ne National Assembly committee ke samne kul 5 SIMs ki had ka zikr kiya tha, is liye had me tabdeeli mumkin hai — portal par dikhai dene wali had hi hatmi hogi.

**Kya me kisi doosre shakhs ke number ka malik maloom kar sakta hoon?**

Nahi — koi official tareeqa kisi ajnabi ke number se us ka naam ya CNIC nahi batata. Sirf **apne haath me mojood SIM** ka malik **MNP 667** par bhej kar maloom kar sakte hain. "Kisi bhi number ka malik batane" wali websites ghair-qanooni hain, un par apna CNIC kabhi na dalein.

**Mere naam par ajnabi SIM nazar aaye to kya karoon?**

Foran us operator ki **franchise ya service centre** par asal CNIC le kar jayein aur SIM block karwayein. Phir chand din baad 668 par dobara check kar ke tasdeeq karein. Shikayat ke liye PTA ke Complaint Management System **complaint.pta.gov.pk** ya helpline **0800-55055** par rabta karein.
