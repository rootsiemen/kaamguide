## Duplicate Certificate Kya Hai Aur Kab Zaroorat Parti Hai

Matric ya inter ki sanad yani certificate wo official kagaz hota hai jo board apko result ke baad deta hai, jis par apke number, grade aur passing year likha hota hai. Agar ye sanad gum ho jaye, chori ho jaye, jal jaye ya itni kharab ho jaye ke parhi na jaye, to apko usi board se uski dosri copy nikalwani parti hai — isi ko duplicate certificate kehte hain.

Duplicate certificate par aam taur par "Duplicate" ya "Second Copy" ki mohar lagi hoti hai, lekin iski qanooni hesiyat asal sanad jesi hi hoti hai. Naukri ke liye apply karte waqt, university me admission lete waqt, visa ke kagzaat jama karwate waqt ya bahar mulk ke liye documents attest karwate waqt ye duplicate sanad bilkul qabool hoti hai. Iske baad [IBCC attestation ka tareeqa](/guides/ibcc-attestation-ka-tareeqa) apnane se ye bahar mulk ke liye bhi tayyar ho jati hai.

Aik baat zehen me rakhein: result card (DMC) aur certificate do alag kagzaat hain. Result card me har mazmoon ke alag number likhe hote hain, jabke sanad yani certificate me kul number, grade aur division hoti hai. Dono ke gum hone par dono ki duplicate alag alag nikalwani parti hai aur dono ki fee bhi alag hoti hai. Is guide me hum dono ka tareeqa cover karenge.

## Kaunsa Board: Apne Board Ka Pata Lagana

Pakistan me matric aur inter ke imtehan alag alag boards lete hain, aur duplicate sanad hamesha usi board se milti hai jis board ka apne imtehan diya tha. Apna board maloom karne ka sab se asaan tareeqa apna purana result card ya sanad dekhna hai — us par board ka naam wazeh likha hota hai. Agar purana kagaz bilkul nahi hai to apne school ya college se poochein, unke record me apka board darj hota hai.

Mulk ke aham boards ye hain:

- **Punjab ke 9 boards:** BISE Lahore, BISE Gujranwala, BISE Rawalpindi, BISE Multan, BISE Faisalabad, BISE Bahawalpur, BISE Sargodha, BISE Sahiwal aur BISE Dera Ghazi Khan. Lahore board ke ilaqe me Lahore, Kasur, Sheikhupura aur Nankana Sahib ke azla atay hain.
- **Karachi ke 2 alag boards:** Matric ke liye Board of Secondary Education Karachi (BSEK) aur inter ke liye Board of Intermediate Education Karachi (BIEK). Ye do alag idaray hain — matric ki duplicate BSEK se aur inter ki BIEK se milegi, is me ghalti na karein.
- **Sindh ke digar boards:** Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana aur Mirpurkhas.
- **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa:** BISE Peshawar, aur digar boards jese Bannu waghera.
- **Balochistan:** BISE Quetta.
- **Azad Kashmir:** BISE Mirpur (AJK).
- **Hazara:** BISE Abbottabad.
- **Federal:** FBISE Islamabad — ye wo board hai jiska imtehan Islamabad aur cantt/garrison ilaqon ke bohat se schools lete hain.

Ghalat board me darkhwast dena waqt aur paisay dono ka zaya hai, is liye apply karne se pehle apna board pukhta kar lein. Board ki official website par apna roll number aur passing year dal kar bhi apna record verify kiya ja sakta hai. Board sanad ke baad university ki degree ki tasdeeq ke liye [HEC degree verification online](/guides/hec-degree-verification-online) ka amal apnaya jata hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Affidavit, Newspaper Ad Aur Digar Documents

Duplicate sanad ke liye sirf form bharna kafi nahi hota — board ko ye yaqeen chahiye hota hai ke ap asal me wahi shakhs hain aur sanad waqai gum hui hai, isi liye kuch lazmi kagzaat mange jate hain. Boards ke official forms ke mutabiq ye kagzaat tayyar rakhein:

| Kagaz | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| Duplicate certificate ka form | Apne board ki website se download karein ya board office ke counter se lein |
| Tasdeeq shuda tasweer | Haal ki passport size tasweer, form par chipka kar mutaliqa idaray ke sarbarah se tasdeeq karwayein |
| Affidavit (halafnama) | Stamp paper par halafnama ke asal sanad gum/kharab ho gayi hai, Notary Public ya Oath Commissioner se tasdeeq shuda |
| Purani sanad/result card ki copy | Agar maujood ho to zaroor lagayein — record milna bohat asaan ho jata hai |
| CNIC ki copy | Apni shanakht ke saboot ke tor par (agar CNIC abhi nahi bana to pehle [CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) parhein) |
| Fee ka bank challan | Board ki hidayat ke mutabiq bank me fee jama karwa kar asal raseed lagayein |

Sab se aham kagaz **affidavit** hai. Ye aik stamp paper par likha halafnama hota hai jis me ap qasam utha kar kehte hain ke apki asal sanad gum ho gayi hai ya tabah ho gayi hai, aur agar wo kabhi mil gayi to ap usay board ko wapas kar denge. Misal ke tor par BISE Rawalpindi ka official form wazeh kehta hai ke Notary Public ya Oath Commissioner se tasdeeq shuda affidavit lagana lazmi hai. Affidavit kisi bhi stamp farosh se stamp paper le kar aur Oath Commissioner ke daftar se tasdeeq karwa kar banwaya ja sakta hai — ye aam taur par chand so rupay me ban jata hai.

Akhbar me gumshudgi ka ishthar dene ke bare me aksar pocha jata hai. Wazeh kar dein: boards ke duplicate certificate forms (Lahore, Rawalpindi aur Federal board) me akhbar ka ishthar officially nahi manga gaya — asal qanooni kagaz affidavit hai. Akhbar ka ishthar aur FIR aam taur par university ki duplicate degree ke liye manga jata hai, board ki matric/inter sanad ke liye nahi. Phir bhi, apne board ka form ghor se parh lein — agar us me ishthar ka zikr ho to usi par amal karein.

Kuch boards form aur tasweer ki tasdeeq mutaliqa school ya college ke sarbarah se karwane ka kehte hain. Misal ke tor par Rawalpindi board ke official form ke mutabiq regular talib ilm apne hi idaray ke sarbarah se, aur private talib ilm BISE Rawalpindi ke ilaqe ke kisi bhi tasleem shuda idaray ke sarbarah se form tasdeeq karwaye ga.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa: Board Office Ja Kar Apply Karna

Ziyada tar boards me duplicate sanad ke liye board office jana parta hai. Boards ke official forms ki bunyad par mukammal tareeqa ye hai:

1. **Form hasil karein:** Apne board ki official website se duplicate certificate ka form download kar lein, ya board office ke facilitation counter se le lein. Form par wazeh likha hota hai ke wo matric ke liye hai ya inter ke liye.
2. **Form pur karein:** Apna naam, walid ka naam, roll number, imtehan ka saal (annual ya supplementary), aur school/college ka naam bilkul purane record ke mutabiq likhein. Roll number aur saal ghalat likhne se apka record nahi milta.
3. **Tasweer lagayein aur tasdeeq karwayein:** Haal ki passport size tasweer form par gond se chipkayein aur apne board ki hidayat ke mutabiq mutaliqa school/college ke sarbarah se form aur tasweer par mohar aur dastakhat karwayein.
4. **Affidavit banwayein:** Stamp paper par halafnama likhwa kar Notary Public ya Oath Commissioner se tasdeeq karwayein ke apki asal sanad gum ya tabah ho chuki hai.
5. **Kagzaat jama karein:** Form ke sath tasdeeq shuda affidavit, purani sanad ya result card ki copy (agar ho), CNIC ki copy aur tasweer lagayein.
6. **Fee jama karwayein:** Board ki hidayat ke mutabiq bank (misal ke tor par Rawalpindi board ke liye kisi bhi HBL branch) me fee jama karwayein aur challan ki asal raseed form ke sath lagayein.
7. **Board office me jama karwayein:** Mukammal file mutaliqa branch ya one-window counter par jama karwayein aur diary number ya raseed zaroor lein — yehi apke case ka saboot hai.
8. **Wusool ka tareeqa maloom karein:** Poochein ke sanad kab tayyar hogi aur kya apko khud ana parega ya dak se bheji jaye gi. FBISE ke official form ke mutabiq sanad sirf qareebi rishtedar (walid, walda, bhai ya behan) hi wasool kar sakta hai, is liye CNIC sath le kar jayein.

## Online Tareeqa: Agar Board Online Apply Deta Hai

Kuch boards ne duplicate sanad ke liye online apply ki sahulat di hai, jis se board office ke chakkar kam ho jate hain. Sab se pukhta misaal BISE Lahore hai, jiska official e-services portal mojood hai. Wahan "Duplicate Certificate / Result Card" ki service muntakhib kar ke apna roll number aur imtehan ka saal dalne par apka record khud nikal ata hai, phir form aur challan print kar ke fee jama karwai jati hai aur case ko online track bhi kiya ja sakta hai.

Punjab ke digar boards ke bhi apne online portals aur e-services apps mojood hain jahan duplicate sanad jesi services di jati hain. Apne board ki official website khol kar "Online Services" ya "E-Services" ka section dekhein — agar wahan duplicate certificate ki service mojood hai to wahin se apply karein. Aik baat ka khayal rakhein: fee hamesha usi challan ke mutabiq jama karwayein jo portal generate kare, aur payment ke baad milne wali tracking ID ya raseed sambhal kar rakhein taake apne case ka status check kar sakein.

Jo boards online apply nahi dete, wahan yehi kaam board office ja kar karna parta hai — upar step-by-step tareeqa usi ke liye hai.

## Kitna Waqt Lagega Aur Certificate Kaise Milega

Duplicate sanad kitne din me milegi, ye har board ka apna mamool hai aur official taur par har board ne waqt ka elan nahi kiya. Jo muddatein official kagzaat se verify hui hain, wo ye hain:

- **FBISE Islamabad:** Urgent fee (Rs 4,000) par certificate usi din jari ho jata hai — ye board ke official form par likha hai.
- **BISE Lahore:** Board ne duplicate ke liye koi official muddat ka elan nahi kiya, is liye apply karte waqt counter se ya portal se milne wali expected date hi hatmi samjhein.
- **BISE Rawalpindi:** Official form me muddat darj nahi, file jama karwate waqt raseed par di gayi date note kar lein.
- **Digar boards:** Aam taur par aik se teen haftay lag jate hain, lekin ye sirf tajurbay ki bunyad par andaza hai — apne board ke counter se poochi hui date par hi bharosa karein.

Sanad wasool karne ke liye aam taur par apko khud board office jana parta hai aur apna asal CNIC dikhana parta hai. Kuch boards dak ya courier se bhi bhejte hain, lekin iski tasdeeq apply karte waqt hi kar lein. Yaad rakhein ke FBISE jese boards me sanad sirf ap ya apka qareebi rishtedar wasool kar sakta hai — kisi dost ya agent ke hath bhejna qabil-e-qabool nahi hota.

Agar muqarrar waqt guzar jaye aur sanad na mile to apni raseed ya diary number le kar board office ke mutaliqa counter par rabta karein. Record purana ho (misal ke tor par 15-20 saal purana) to talash me thora zyada waqt lag sakta hai, is liye purane record walay waqt se pehle apply karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Ghalat board me apply kar dena:** Karachi me matric ki duplicate BIEK se nahi, BSEK se milti hai. Pehle apna board pukhta karein.
2. **Roll number ya passing year ghalat likhna:** Record computer me roll number aur saal se nikalta hai — aik hindsa ghalat hua to file hi nahi khulti.
3. **Affidavit lagana bhool jana:** Ye sab se aham kagaz hai. Iske baghair file namukammal samjhi jati hai aur wapas ho jati hai.
4. **Fee ka andaza laga kar jama karwa dena:** Purani fee tables par bharosa na karein. Hamesha board ke portal ya counter se maujooda fee maloom kar ke usi ke mutabiq challan banwayein.
5. **Challan ki asal raseed gum kar dena:** Bank ki mohar lagi raseed hi apki payment ka saboot hai. Uski tasweer bhi bana kar mobile me rakhein.
6. **Kisi agent ya dalal ko paisay de dena:** Duplicate sanad ka kaam seedha board se hota hai, kisi sifarish ya agent ki zaroorat nahi. Agent aksar zyada paisay le kar wahi kaam karta hai jo ap khud kar sakte hain.
7. **Naam ya walid ke naam me farq:** Form par naam bilkul wesa likhein jesa purane record me tha. Agar naam me tabdeeli karwani hai to wo alag correction case hota hai, duplicate ke sath nahi hota. Tareekh-e-pedaish ke saboot ke tor par aksar [birth certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/birth-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi kaam ata hai, aur sarkari mulazmat ke liye [domicile certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) alag se darkar hota hai.
8. **Asal sanad milne par board ko wapas na karna:** Affidavit me apne wada kiya hota hai ke asal mil gayi to board ko de denge. Do asnaad rakhna qanooni masla ban sakta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya duplicate sanad asal sanad jitni hi qabil-e-qabool hoti hai?**
Ji han, bilkul. Duplicate sanad par "Duplicate" ki mohar zaroor hoti hai lekin iski qanooni hesiyat asal jesi hi hai. Naukri, admission, visa aur IBCC attestation — har jaga ye qabool hoti hai.

**Kya me kisi aur ke liye duplicate sanad nikalwa sakta hon?**
Aam taur par nahi. Boards ka usool hai ke darkhwast guzar khud aaye ya uska qareebi rishtedar (walid, walda, bhai, behan) apna CNIC dikha kar wasool kare. Misal ke tor par FBISE ke official form me ye shart wazeh likhi hai.

**Agar mera roll number yaad nahi to kya hoga?**
Koi pareshani ki baat nahi. Apne school ya college ke record se roll number aur passing year maloom kiya ja sakta hai. Board ki website par naam se record talash karne ki sahulat bhi aksar hoti hai. Purane result card ya sanad ki koi bhi copy ho to kaam bohat asaan ho jata hai.

**Kya duplicate sanad par IBCC se attestation ho sakti hai?**
Ji han, ho sakti hai. Pehle board se duplicate sanad nikalwayein, phir IBCC ke standard tareeqay se attestation karwayein. Board ki tasdeeq ke baad IBCC duplicate sanad ko attest kar deta hai.

**Meri sanad bohat purani hai, kya uski duplicate bhi mil jaye gi?**
Ji han, board ka record mehfooz rehta hai aur purani sanad ki duplicate bhi nikalwai ja sakti hai. Bas itna zehen me rakhein ke jitna purana record hoga, talash me utna hi zyada waqt lag sakta hai, is liye jald apply karein.

Ye guide BISE Lahore (biselahore.com), BISE Rawalpindi (biserawalpindi.edu.pk) aur Federal Board Islamabad (FBISE) ke official forms aur fee schedules ki bunyad par 23 October 2026 ko tayyar ki gayi hai. BISE Peshawar, BISE Multan aur Karachi boards ki taaza fee official source se verify na ho saki, is liye apply karne se pehle apne board ki website ya helpline se maujooda fee zaroor confirm karein.
