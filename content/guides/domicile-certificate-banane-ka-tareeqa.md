## Domicile Kya Hai Aur Kab Chahiye Hota Hai

Domicile certificate aap ke **mustaqil sukkunat (permanent residence)** ka sarkari saboot hai — ye batata hai ke aap kis **zila (district)** aur soobe ke mustaqil rehne wale hain. Ye chahiye hota hai jab:

- **University admission** me apne zila/soobe ke **quota** par apply karna ho
- **Sarkari nokri** ke liye zilai ya soobai quota claim karna ho
- Kuch scholarship schemes aur sarkari muamlat me

Domicile usi **zile se banta hai jahan aap (ya aap ke walid) mustaqil rehte hain** — jahan marzi ho wahan se nahi ban sakta. Aik shaks ka aam tor par aik hi domicile hota hai, is liye **zila soch samajh kar chunein** — khaas tor par talib-ilmon ke liye ye faisla admission quota par seedha asar dalta hai.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fee aur process **Punjab ke e-Khidmat system** (ekhidmat.punjab.gov.pk) se li gayi hai aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thi**. Dosre soobon me process aur fee mukhtalif ho sakti hai — apne soobe ke official portal ya DC office se zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Punjab Me Fee Aur Waqt (Official)

| Service | Fee | Waqt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Naya domicile | **Rs 200** (Faisalabad me Rs 300) | 16 working days |
| Duplicate domicile (gumshuda) | Rs 200 (32-A challan) | 16 working days |
| Correction / modification | Rs 200 (32-A challan) | 16 working days |

**Kharcha samajh lein:** Certificate ki asal service fee Rs 200 hai, lekin **affidavit (halaf-nama)** par bhi Rs 50-300 lag jate hain — kul kharcha taqreeban **Rs 300-500** samjhein. Agenton ko hazaron dene ki zaroorat nahi.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

- Apna **CNIC** (asal aur copy) — na ho to B-Form
- **Walid ka CNIC** (domicile aksar walid ke record se banta hai)
- **Rehne ka saboot (proof of residence):** in me se aik — bijli/gas ka bill, jaidad ke kagzaat, kiraya-nama, resident certificate, taleemi sanad ya voter list ka indraj
- **Affidavit** (halaf-nama) — mustaqil rehaish ki tasdeeq ke liye
- Taza **passport-size tasveer**
- **Form P / P1** (application form — e-Khidmat par digital bhar jata hai)

**Tip:** Residence proof wo chunein jis par **aap ka ya aap ke walid ka naam aur zila ka pata** sab se saaf nazar aye — file objections zyada tar isi par lagte hain.

## Tareeqa 1: e-Khidmat Markaz Se Apply (Sab Se Aasan)

1. Apne qareebi **e-Khidmat Markaz** jayen (Punjab ke har zila me hain).
2. Counter par **Domicile Certificate** ki service kahein — staff aap ki file **Domicile Management System (DMS)** me bana dega.
3. Kagzaat scan hongay, **Form P** bhar jayega aur aap ki tasveer li jayegi.
4. **PSID** milega — fee **ePay Punjab, JazzCash, Easypaisa, mobile banking** ya qareebi National Bank se ada karein.
5. **Tracking ID** lazmi lein — status SMS aur portal se milta rahega.
6. **16 working days** ke andar certificate tayyar hoga — Assistant Commissioner ke office se jari hota hai, e-Khidmat se wasool karein.

**Achhi baat:** e-Khidmat staff aap ki rehnumai muft karta hai — is service ke liye **koi izafi fee nahi** hai, sirf sarkari Rs 200 hai.

## Tareeqa 2: Maryam Ki Dastak App (Ghar Bethe)

Punjab ki **Dastak app** se facilitator **aap ke ghar** aa kar documents le jata hai:

1. **Dastak Citizen App** download karke CNIC se register karein.
2. **Board of Revenue** services me **Domicile Certificate** chunein.
3. Zila/tehsil aur appointment ka waqt chunein.
4. Facilitator tay waqt par ghar aayega — asal kagzaat scan karke raseed dega.
5. Fee app ke payment options se ada karein aur status app me track karein.
6. Tayyar certificate **ghar deliver** ho jata hai.

## Tareeqa 3: Tehsildar / DC Office (Purana Tareeqa)

Jin ilaaqon me e-Khidmat nahi, wahan:

1. Apne zila ke **Assistant Commissioner / Tehsildar office** jayen.
2. Form P lein aur bharein, kagzaat aur affidavit attach karein.
3. Fee **32-A challan** se National Bank me jama karwayen.
4. File jama karke di gayi tareekh par certificate le lein.

## Duplicate Domicile (Gum Ho Jaye To)

1. Certificate gumne ki **FIR** qareebi thane me katwayen.
2. FIR ki copy, CNIC, affidavit aur tasveer ke saath e-Khidmat jayen.
3. **Rs 200** ka 32-A challan National Bank me jama karwayen.
4. **16 working days** me duplicate mil jayega.

**Correction ke liye:** FIR ke bajaye **V form (correction form)** lagega — baaki process wahi hai aur fee bhi Rs 200 hi hai.

## PRC Kya Hai Aur Domicile Se Farq

Aksar log **PRC (Permanent Residence Certificate)** aur domicile ko aik samajhte hain — dono alag hain:

- **Domicile:** Soobe/zila ki mustaqil sukkunat ka certificate — quota aur nokriyon ke liye.
- **PRC:** Kuch soobon (khaas kar Sindh) me milta hai — ye residence ka saboot hai aur bank account waghera ke liye istemal hota hai.

Jahan apply kar rahe hain, pehle confirm kar lein ke **chahiye kaunsa hai** — ghalat document par waqt aur fee dono zaya honge.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Ghalat zila chunnna:** Domicile walid ke permanent zila se banta hai — "jahan admission chahiye wahan ka bana lo" wali soch rejection deti hai.
2. **Dono soobon se bana lena:** Aik se zyada domicile rakhna ghair-qanooni hai — pakra jaye to dono cancel ho sakte hain.
3. **Affidavit me ghalti:** Naam ki spelling CNIC ke mutabiq rakhein.
4. **Agent ko paise:** Fee sirf Rs 200 hai — agent ka is me koi kirdaar nahi.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya larki shadi ke baad shohar ke zila ka domicile bana sakti hai?**
Ji haan — shadi-shuda khawateen shohar ke permanent zila se domicile banwa sakti hain; nikah-nama aur shohar ka CNIC lagega.

**Kya NADRA domicile banata hai?**
Nahi — domicile **zila intezamia (Assistant Commissioner)** jari karta hai, NADRA ka is se taluq nahi.

**Domicile ki expiry hoti hai?**
Aam tor par domicile zindagi bhar valid hota hai — jab tak aap mustaqil sukkunat na badal lein.

**Talib-ilm ka domicile kis zila se bane ga?**
Walid ke permanent zila se — yahi zila admission quota tay karega, is liye pehle walid se confirm karein.

**Kitne din me mil jata hai?**
Punjab me official waqt **16 working days** hai, bashart kagzaat par koi objection na lage.

## Application Ka Status Aur Objection Handle Karna

File jama karne ke baad aap ko **tracking ID** milti hai — is se status maloom hota hai:

1. **e-Khidmat portal** par tracking ID dalen ya **SMS** ka intezar karein.
2. Agar status **"objection"** par ruka hai to ghabrayen nahi — aam tor par koi document adhoora ya ghair-wazeh hota hai.
3. SMS/portal par **wajah likhi hoti hai** — wahi document durust karke dobara jama karwayen.
4. **Aam objections:** residence proof par naam ka farq, affidavit me ghalti, tasveer purani, walid ka CNIC expire.

**Mashwara:** File jama karne se pehle e-Khidmat staff se **checklist par tick** lagwa lein — aik dafa me file mukammal ho to 16 din me certificate pakka milta hai.

## Domicile Aur Taleemi Quota: Talib-ilmon Ke Liye Khaas

Domicile ka sab se bara faida **talib-ilmon** ko hota hai:

- **Medical/Engineering universities** me har soobe/zila ka **quota** hota hai — domicile hi tay karta hai ke aap kis quota par apply kar sakte hain.
- **MPT (medical)** ya **ECAT** jaisi entry tests me domicile certificate admission form ke saath lagana parta hai.
- **Wazifa (scholarship)** schemes me bhi domicile manga jata hai.

**Is liye:** 12th class ke **result se pehle** domicile banwa lein — admission ka season shuru hote hi e-Khidmat par bheer barh jati hai aur kaam slow ho jata hai. Pehle se tayyar certificate admission me sakoon deta hai.

## Doosre Soobon Me Domicile (Mukhtasir Guide)

- **Sindh:** Domicile aur PRC dono hote hain — DC office / mukhtarkar daftar se milte hain; kuch marhale online bhi hain lekin office jana parta hai.
- **KPK:** Citizen Facilitation Centers se apply hota hai — documents online jama aur tracking ki sahulat hai.
- **Balochistan:** DC office se — process zyada tar daftar-adharit hai.
- **Islamabad:** Citizen Facilitation Center (ICT administration) — official website se maujooda tareeqa confirm karein.

**Usool sab ka aik hai:** Certificate **usi zila** ka jahan sthayi sukkunat hai, documents taqreeban wohi (CNIC, residence proof, affidavit, tasveer), aur fee nominal. Apne soobe ke portal se latest maloomat lena na bhoolein — process har saal digitize ho raha hai.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya aik se zyada domicile ho sakta hai?**
Nahi — aik se zyada domicile **ghair-qanooni** hai aur pakre jane par dono cancel ho sakte hain.

**Bivi shohar ke zila se domicile banwaye to walid ka CNIC chalega?**
Is soorat me **shohar ka CNIC** aur **nikah-nama** lagega — walid ka CNIC tab kaam ata hai jab aap walid ke zila se banwa rahe hon.

**Domicile par zila ka naam ghalat ho to?**
**Modification** ke liye apply karein — V form, affidavit aur saboot ke saath. Fee Rs 200 hai, 16 working days me naya milega.

## File Jama Karne Se Pehle Aakhri Check

- tamam copies **saaf aur wazeh** (photo-copy dhundh-li na ho)
- Affidavit par **naam CNIC ke mutabiq** likha hai
- Residence proof ka pata **zila ke andar** hai
- Tracking ID mil gayi hai

Ye chaar cheezen theek hon to aap ki file **objection-mukt** rahegi.

## Certificate Ke Baad

Milne par **3 fotokapiyan** aur mobile me scan kar lein — admission/nokri ke form har jagah domicile mangte hain.
