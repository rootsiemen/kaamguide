## Pehli Baat: CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme Kya Hai

**CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme** — sarkari naam ke tor par **Chief Minister Punjab E-Bike Scheme** — Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo programme hai jis ke tehet Punjab ke students ko **subsidy wali electric bikes** di jati hain. Is ka maqsad ye hai ke jo student rozana college ya university aane jaane par petrol aur kiraye ka bojh uthata hai, wo sasti aur mahol-dost sawari par safar kar sake. Ye scheme **Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif** ke "Green Initiative" ka hissa hai aur is ki darkhwastein sirf **online** li jati hain — kisi daftar jaane ya kisi agent ko paisay dene ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Scheme ke doosre marhale (Phase 2) me taqseem ki jaane wali bikes aam petrol bikes nahi hain — ye **electric bikes (e-bikes)** hain, jin ka petrol ka kharcha bilkul nahi hota aur jinhe ghar par charge kiya ja sakta hai. Hukumat ne is marhale ke liye **100,000 electric bikes** ka elaan kiya tha. Larkiyon ke liye khaas tor par **gulabi (pink) rang ki bikes** bhi rakhi gayi thin.

**Sab se ahem taaza khabar — pehle ye samajh lein:** Phase 2 ki darkhwastein **band ho chuki hain**. Darkhwastein **5 September 2026** ko khuli thin aur aakhri tareekh **4 October 2026** thi. **26 November 2026 tak naye marhale (Phase 3) ka koi sarkari elaan nahi hua** — na official portal par, na Punjab Hukumat ki taraf se, na kisi bare akhbar me. Is waqt internet par jo websites "2026 me registration khuli hai — abhi apply karein" likh rahi hain, wo **sarkari websites nahi hain** aur un par bharosa nahi karna chahiye. Is guide me aap ko Phase 2 ki asal shartein, qeemat aur qiston ki tafseel, e-balloting ka tareeqa aur agle marhale ki tayyari ka poora tareeqa diya gaya hai.

**Aik ahem note:** official portal **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** 26 November 2026 ko check karne par load nahi hua — text fetch aur live browser dono par "connection reset" ki ghalti aayi. Is liye is guide ke numbers aur shartein Punjab Hukumat ke elaan aur September 2026 ki mutabar akhbari reports (ProPakistani, TechJuice, Daily Pakistan) se li gayi hain, aur Punjab Portal ke scheme page (punjab.gov.pk/cm-ebikes-scheme) se bhi tasdeeq ki gayi hai. Portal dobara khulne ya naye marhale ke elaan par wahi page hatmi zariya ho ga.

## Phase 2 Ki Taaza Haalat: Registration Band, 100,000 Bikes

Phase 2 ka safar ab kis marhale par hai, ye samajhna zaroori hai taake aap ko pata ho ke aap kahan khare hain:

**Registration band hai:** Phase 2 ke liye darkhwastein 4 October 2026 ko band ho gayin. Is marhale me Punjab bhar ke **33,000 se zyada** sarkari aur niji taleemi idaron ke students apply kar sakte the — school, college aur university, sab shaamil the.

**100,000 electric bikes:** Is marhale ka elaan 100,000 electric bikes ke sath hua tha. Ye Punjab ki tareekh ki sab se bari student bike scheme hai. Is se pehle ke marhale (Phase 1) me sirf **19,000 bikes** thin — jin me 10,000 electric aur 10,000 petrol bikes shaamil thin — aur Punjab Portal ke scheme page ke mutabiq is ke liye kul **Rs 2.6 billion ki subsidy** rakhi gayi thi, jis se Punjab ke **800,000 enrolled students** ko mauqa milna tha.

**Phase 1 se Phase 2 tak kya badla:** Pehle marhale me e-bike sirf **5 zilon** — Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Multan aur Bahawalpur — ke students ke liye thi. Phase 2 me daira barha kar poore Punjab ke students tak kar diya gaya. Pehle marhale me sirf degree college ya university ke regular students eligible the, jab ke Phase 2 me school, college aur university — sarkari aur niji, dono — ke students shaamil kar liye gaye.

**Aap ne Phase 2 me apply kiya tha?** Agar haan, to apna status official portal par zaroor check karein — ho sakta hai aap e-balloting me muntakhib hue hon aur bike ki taqseem ka intezar kar rahe hon. Agar aap eligible the lekin is marhale me muntakhib nahi hue, to neeche di gayi tayyari agle marhale ke liye kaam aaye gi.

| Tareekh / Marhala | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| Phase 1 (2024–25) | 19,000 bikes (10,000 electric + 10,000 petrol); e-bike sirf 5 zilon ke liye |
| 5 September 2026 | Phase 2 ki darkhwastein khul gayin — 100,000 electric bikes |
| 4 October 2026 | Phase 2 ki aakhri tareekh — darkhwastein band |
| 26 November 2026 | Phase 3 ka koi sarkari elaan nahi; portal check par load nahi hua |

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Ehliyaat

Phase 2 ki shartein neeche di gayi hain. In me se **aik bhi shart poori na ho to darkhwast khud-bakhud reject ho jati hai**, is liye apply karne se pehle har nukta check kar lein:

- **Punjab ka student hona lazmi hai:** Aap Punjab ke kisi school, college ya university me enrolled hon — sarkari ya niji, dono chalte hain. Phase 2 me 33,000 se zyada idare shaamil the.
- **Umar kam az kam 16 saal:** Phase 2 ki shartein ke mutabiq darkhwast ke waqt aap ki umar **16 saal ya is se zyada** honi chahiye. (Pehle marhale me Punjab Portal par hadd 18 saal darj thi — naye marhale me ye hadd kam kar di gayi thi.)
- **Driving licence ya learner's permit lazmi hai:** Aap ke paas **valid driving licence, learner's permit ya juvenile driving permit** hona chahiye. Agar aap ke paas permit nahi hai to pehle learner permit banwayein — hamari [driving licence banane ki guide](/guides/driving-license-banane-ka-tareeqa) me is ka poora tareeqa diya gaya hai.
- **Guarantor lazmi hai:** Aap ko apne **walid ya sarparast (parent/legal guardian)** ko guarantor banana hota hai, aur guarantor ki aamdani ka saboot bhi dena hota hai.
- **Domicile Punjab ka:** E-balloting domicile zila ki bunyad par hoti hai, is liye aap ka domicile Punjab ka hona chahiye.

**Kaun eligible nahi hai:** Jo student Punjab se bahar ke idare me parh raha ho, jis ke paas licence ya learner permit na ho, ya jo guarantor na de sake, wo is scheme ke liye eligible nahi hai. Isi tarah jo student pehle hi kisi sarkari scheme se bike le chuka ho, us ki darkhwast bhi qubool nahi hoti.

Agar aap abhi student nahi hain lekin agle saal admission lene wale hain, to abhi se apna learner permit banwa lein aur CNIC tayyar rakhein — ye dono cheezein agle marhale me sab se pehle kaam aayen gi. Apna [CNIC banane ya renewal ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi waqt par mukammal kar lein taake aakhri waqt par koi rukawat na aaye.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Darkhwast bharte waqt ye kagzaat aur maloomat haath me rakhein. Koi aik kagaz ghalat ya ghair-wazeh ho to darkhwast verification me atak sakti hai:

- **CNIC ya B-Form number:** 13 hindson ka number, bilkul waisa hi jaisa CNIC par darj hai. Bina CNIC/B-Form ke account hi nahi banta.
- **Taleem ka saboot:** Student ID card ya admission ka saboot — jis idare me parh rahe hain us ka naam, programme aur maujooda semester/class.
- **Learner's permit ya driving licence:** Permit ya licence ki tafseel — number aur us par darj suba (province) bilkul durust likhna hai.
- **Domicile ka saboot:** Domicile zila ki maloomat, kyun ke e-balloting domicile ki bunyad par hoti hai.
- **Guarantor ki tafseel:** Walid ya sarparast ka naam, CNIC aur aamdani ka saboot.
- **Rabta ki maloomat:** Apna active mobile number aur email address — verification aur balloting ki khabar isi par aati hai.
- **Passport-size tasveer:** Aik saaf, haaliya tasveer tayyar rakhein.

**Zaroori mashwara:** Form bharne se pehle apne CNIC, permit aur student ID ki saaf tasveerein ya scan apne mobile me save kar lein. Form bharte waqt naam ki spelling CNIC se harf-ba-harf milni chahiye — aik harf ka farq bhi verification me masla bana sakta hai.

## Qeemat, Subsidy Aur Qistain

Is scheme ki sab se bari khaasiyat is ki qeemat hai. Bike ki asal qeemat ka aik bara hissa Punjab Hukumat ada karti hai aur baqi raqam aap **bina markup (sood) ke, 3 saal ki aasan qiston** me ada karte hain:

| Mad | Raqam |
| --- | --- |
| Electric bike ki kul qeemat | Rs 199,000 |
| Hukumat ki subsidy | Rs 90,000 |
| Student ko ada karni wali raqam | Rs 109,000 |
| Qiston ki muddat | 36 mahine (3 saal) |
| Mahana qist | Rs 3,028 |
| Down payment | Koi nahi (zero) |
| Markup / sood | Zero — bilkul muft financing |

**Hukumat kya kya bardasht karti hai:** Student ke hisse ke ilawa **insurance, markup aur registration ka kharcha bhi Punjab Hukumat bardasht karti hai**. Matlab aap ko sirf Rs 109,000 ki qistein ada karni hain — is ke ilawa bike lene par koi chhupa kharcha nahi hai.

**Misaal se samjhein:** Agar aap ki bike ki kul qeemat Rs 199,000 hai to Hukumat Rs 90,000 subsidy ke tor par ada karti hai aur aap ke zimme Rs 109,000 rehte hain. Ye raqam 36 mahine me taqseem hoti hai — har mahine sirf **Rs 3,028**. Is hisaab se rozana ka kharcha taqreeban Rs 100 banta hai, jo aik student ke liye rozana ke bus ya rickshaw ke kiraye se bhi kam hai.

**Fee ka ahem note:** Registration aur darkhwast ki **koi fee nahi** thi. Agar koi shakhs ya website aap se "registration fee", "processing fee" ya "booking" ke naam par paisay mange to samajh jayein ke wo **fraud** hai — foran us se door rahein aur sirf official portal par bharosa karein.

Isi tarah ki sarkari sahulat wali aik aur scheme [CM Punjab Laptop Scheme](/guides/cm-punjab-laptop-scheme-apply-online) hai, jis me students ko muft laptop diye jate hain, aur [PM Youth Chromebook Scheme](/guides/pm-youth-chromebook-scheme-apply-online) bhi students ke liye muft device deti hai — in ki guides bhi isi site par maujood hain.

## Online Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

Jab agla marhala khule ga to darkhwast ka tareeqa yahi ho ga jo Phase 2 me tha. Poora amal **sirf online** hota hai — kisi daftar jaane ki zaroorat nahi:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne mobile ya computer par **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** kholein. Kisi aur website ya Google ad par click na karein — hamesha yehi address khud type kar ke kholein.

**Step 2 — Account banayein:** "Apply Now" par click karein aur apna account banayein. Is ke liye apna **poora naam aur gender bilkul waisa hi likhein jaisa CNIC par hai**, phir active email address aur 13 hindson ka CNIC number darj karein. Jis sube ka aap ka driving licence hai wo suba select karein. Agar aap ke paas licence nahi hai to portal par diya gaya link use karein.

**Step 3 — Mazboot password lagayein:** Account ke liye **kam az kam 12 harf ka password** chahiye jis me bara harf (uppercase), chhota harf (lowercase), number aur khaas nishan (special character) — chaaron shaamil hon. Aik kamzor password par account nahi banta, is liye ye shart pehle se zehen me rakhein.

**Step 4 — Paanch lazmi hisse mukammal karein:** Account banne ke baad aap dashboard par aa jayein ge. Wahan darkhwast ke **paanch lazmi hisse** bharne hain, jin me shuruaat in teen se hoti hai:

- **Personal Details** — apni zaati maloomat, rabta number aur pata
- **Parent / Guarantor Details** — walid ya sarparast ki maloomat aur aamdani ka saboot
- **Educational Institution Detail** — idare ka naam, programme aur maujooda semester

Baaki hisse portal par dikhaye gaye hidayat ke mutabiq mukammal karein. Har hissa bharne ke baad **Save** zaroor karein — beech me browser band karne se bhara hua hissa zaya ho sakta hai.

**Step 5 — Maloomat dobara check karein aur submit karein:** Submit karne se pehle apna naam, CNIC, idare ka naam aur permit number aik dafa phir parh lein. Ghalti ki surat me verification me darkhwast reject ho sakti hai. Sab durust ho to form submit karein aur **confirmation slip ya tracking ID** download kar ke mehfooz kar lein — yehi aap ki darkhwast ka saboot hai.

**Step 6 — Status check karte rahein:** Darkhwast jama karne ke baad apne account me login kar ke waqtan fawaqtan status check karte rahein. Balloting aur taqseem ki khabar email aur SMS par bhi aati hai, is liye form me diya gaya number aur email active rakhein.

## E-Balloting Aur Bike Milne Ka Amal

Darkhwast jama karne ke baad bike foran nahi milti — is ka aik shaffaf amal hai:

**Verification:** Pehle aap ki maloomat ki tasdeeq hoti hai — aap ka idara aap ke student hone ki tasdeeq karta hai aur aap ke kagzaat check kiye jate hain. Scheme ki maloomat ke mutabiq tasdeeq, balloting aur taqseem ka kaam **Bank of Punjab** ke zariye hota hai.

**E-balloting:** Agar darkhwastein bikes ki tadad se zyada hon to **e-balloting** hoti hai — matlab computer ke zariye qura-andazi. Balloting **domicile zila ki bunyad par** hoti hai, aur Phase 1 me larkon aur larkiyon ka kota bhi muqarrar tha: divisional satah par 50% mard aur 50% khawateen, jab ke baqi Punjab me 70% mard aur 30% khawateen. E-bike ki category me koi pabandi wala kota nahi tha.

**Taqseem:** Muntakhib hone walon ko taqseem ki taqreeb ya muqarrar tareeqe se bike di jati hai. Aap ko apni qist ki adayegi ka schedule bhi isi waqt bataya jata hai. Bike milne ke baad mahana qist waqt par ada karna aap ki zimmedari hai.

**Agar naam na aaye to kya karein:** Balloting me naam na aane ka matlab ye nahi ke aap na-ehl hain — sirf ye hai ke is marhale me aap ki bari nahi aayi. Aap ki maloomat agle marhale ke liye kaam aa sakti hain, is liye apna account aur kagzaat tayyar rakhein aur agle elaan ka intezar karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

Ye ghaltiyan har marhale me hazaaron darkhwastein zaya karti hain — in se bachein:

- **Ghalat ya na-mukammal maloomat likhna:** Naam, CNIC ya idare ke naam me ghalti verification me darkhwast reject karwa deti hai. Har field CNIC aur kagzaat se mila kar likhein.
- **Learner permit ke baghair apply karna:** Licence ya learner permit ke baghair darkhwast aage hi nahi barhti. Pehle permit banwayein, phir apply karein.
- **Kisi agent ko paisay dena:** Ye scheme **muft** hai — koi fee nahi hai. Jo "guarantee" ya "jaldi approval" ke naam par paisay mange, wo dhoka hai.
- **Ghalat website par form bharna:** Sirf **bikes.punjab.gov.pk** official portal hai. Is ke ilawa koi bhi website, WhatsApp number ya Facebook page sarkari nahi hai — in par apna CNIC aur maloomat kabhi na dein.
- **Aakhri tareekh ka intezar karna:** Aakhri dinon me portal par bojh barh jata hai aur aksar khulne me masla hota hai. Agle marhale ke elaan par pehle hafte me hi darkhwast mukammal kar lein.
- **Guarantor ki maloomat tayyar na rakhna:** Guarantor ki aamdani ka saboot darkhwast ke waqt hi chahiye hota hai. Walid ya sarparast se ye kagaz pehle se le lein.
- **Confirmation slip mehfooz na karna:** Submit karne ke baad slip ya tracking ID download na karna aap ko baad me status check karne se mehroom kar deta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CM Punjab E-Bike Scheme me apply karne ki aakhri tareekh kya thi?**

Phase 2 ki darkhwastein 5 September 2026 ko khuli thin aur aakhri tareekh 4 October 2026 thi. Is ke baad registration band ho gayi hai. 26 November 2026 tak Phase 3 ka koi sarkari elaan nahi hua — agle marhale ki tareekh ka elaan sirf official portal bikes.punjab.gov.pk par ho ga.

**E-bike ki qeemat kitni hai aur student ko kitna dena hota hai?**

Electric bike ki kul qeemat Rs 199,000 hai. Punjab Hukumat Rs 90,000 subsidy deti hai, is liye student ko sirf Rs 109,000 ada karne hote hain — wo bhi 36 mahine ki qiston me, har mahine Rs 3,028. Is par koi markup ya sood nahi hai aur koi down payment nahi hai. Insurance, markup aur registration ka kharcha bhi Hukumat bardasht karti hai.

**Kya private college ya university ke students bhi apply kar sakte hain?**

Ji haan — Phase 2 me Punjab ke sarkari aur niji dono tarah ke schools, colleges aur universities ke students eligible the, aur kul 33,000 se zyada idare is me shaamil the. Shart sirf ye hai ke aap enrolled student hon aur aap ke paas valid licence ya learner's permit ho.

**Learner permit ke baghair apply ho sakta hai?**

Nahi — valid driving licence, learner's permit ya juvenile driving permit hona lazmi shart hai. Agar aap ke paas permit nahi hai to pehle learner permit banwayein, phir agle marhale me apply karein. Hamari driving licence wali guide me learner permit ka tareeqa tafseel se diya gaya hai.

**Bike kaise mile gi — pehle aao pehle pao ya qura-andazi?**

Agar darkhwastein bikes se zyada hon to e-balloting (qura-andazi) hoti hai, jo domicile zila ki bunyad par ki jati hai. Is liye jaldi apply karna zaroori hai lekin selection merit aur balloting se hoti hai, sirf jaldi karne se nahi. Muntakhib hone walon ko taqseem ki taqreeb ke zariye bike di jati hai.

Official portal bikes.punjab.gov.pk 26 November 2026 ko check karne par load nahi hua. Qeemat, subsidy aur tareekhein tabdeel ho sakti hain — darkhwast dene se pehle official portal par taaza maloomat zaroor check karein.*
