## B-Form Kya Hai Aur Kyun Zaroori Hai

B-Form ka official naam **Child Registration Certificate (CRC)** hai — ye aap ke bache ki Pakistan me **pehli sarkari pehchan** hai. NADRA har bache ka record isi certificate ke zariye apne database me darj karta hai. Is ke baghair:

- Bache ka **school admission** nahi hota (aksar schools B-Form mangte hain)
- Bache ka **passport** nahi ban sakta
- **18 saal par CNIC** banwane me pareshani hoti hai
- Wirasat aur qanooni muamlat me bache ka record sabit nahi hota

NADRA walidain se guzarish karta hai ke bache ki pedaish ke **3 se 6 mahine ke andar** B-Form banwa lein. Jitni jaldi banwayenge, utna hi behtar — baad me school ya passport ke waqt bhagna nahi parega. Achhi khabar ye hai ke fee bohat mamooli hai — sirf **Rs 50** normal processing par.

> **Note:** Is guide me di gayi fees NADRA ke published fee schedule se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. NADRA fees tabdeel karta rehta hai, is liye payment se pehle Pak ID app ya nadra.gov.pk par maujooda fee zaroor confirm kar lein.

## 2026 Ki Official Fee

| Service | Fee | Processing Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B-Form (CRC) — Normal | **Rs 50** | 7 din |
| B-Form (CRC) — Executive | **Rs 500** | 1 din |

Ye fees online (Pak ID app) aur NADRA center dono par aik jaisi hain. Overseas Pakistanis ke liye fees dollar me hoti hain jo Pak ID portal par checkout ke waqt nazar aati hain.

## Pehla Qadam: Union Council Me Pedaish Ka Indraj

NADRA ke naye Birth and Death Registration System ke tehat **pehle bache ki pedaish apni Union Council me register karwana zaroori hai** — is ke baghair NADRA B-Form jari nahi karta. Tareeqa:

1. Bache ki pedaish ke baad hospital se **birth slip** lein.
2. Apni Union Council ke office jayen — walidain ke CNIC aur hospital ki slip le kar jayen.
3. Wahan bache ka indraj karwayen aur **computerized birth certificate** hasil karein.
4. Ab isi birth certificate ke saath NADRA se B-Form ke liye apply karein.

Union Council ki fee aam tor par **Rs 100 se Rs 300** hoti hai (ilaaqe ke hisab se farq ho sakta hai) — apni UC se confirm kar lein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

- Walid ka **asal CNIC**
- Walida ka **asal CNIC** (ya NICOP, agar overseas hain)
- Bache ka **Union Council birth certificate**
- Bache ki **haaliya tasveer** (1 saal se bare bache ke liye lazmi hai — chote bachon ki tasveer NADRA center par bhi li ja sakti hai)
- Agar pehle B-Form bana hua hai aur us me **correction** karwani hai to purana B-Form bhi le jayen

**Ahem:** Bache ka naam aur tareekh-e-pedaish **birth certificate se harf-ba-harf milna chahiye**. Naam ya date me farq hua to mustaqbil me passport, school aur CNIC — har jagah masla hoga.

## Tareeqa 1: Pak ID App Se Online Apply (1 Saal Se Chote Bache Ke Liye)

**1 saal se kam umar** ke bachon ke liye poora kaam Pak ID app se ghar bethe ho sakta hai:

1. **Pak ID app** download karein (Android/iOS) aur apne CNIC se account banayen.
2. **Issuance of Identity Document** me jayen aur **Child Registration Certificate (CRC)** chunein.
3. Apne (walid/ walida) ka 13-hinson ka CNIC number darj karein — aap ki maloomat khud-b-khud aa jayegi.
4. Bache ki maloomat darj karein: naam, tareekh-e-pedaish, ja-e-pedaish, walidain ke naam.
5. Darkar documents upload karein: **bache ki tasveer, walid ke digital dastakhat, birth certificate ki tasveer**.
6. **Aik walid ki fingerprint verification** app ke camera se karein.
7. Fee ada karein — **debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR** se.
8. Submit karein — **tracking ID** mil jayegi. Approval ke baad digital copy app me aa jati hai, ya printed form center se le sakte hain.

## Tareeqa 2: NADRA Center Ja Kar (1 Saal Se Bare Bache Ke Liye)

1 saal se bare bache ke liye **NADRA Registration Center jana zaroori hai** kyunke bache ki tasveer aur biometric wahan li jati hai:

1. Qareebi NRC/Mega Center jayen aur **CRC ke liye token** lein.
2. Upar diye gaye tamam kagzaat jama karwayen.
3. Bache ki **tasveer** li jayegi (bare bachon ke fingerprints bhi).
4. Form par tamam maloomat **ghor se check karein** — naam ki spelling aur date of birth sab se ahem hain.
5. Fee ada karein aur raseed + tracking ID lein.

## B-Form Ka Status Kaise Check Karein

- **Pak ID app:** "Track Your Application" me tracking ID dalen.
- **SMS:** Tracking ID **8400** par bhej dein.
- **NADRA helpline:** 1777 par call karein.

## B-Form Me Correction Ya Update Karwana

Agar B-Form me naam, date of birth ya walidain ki maloomat ghalat darj ho gayi hai to:

1. Dono walidain ke asal CNIC le kar NADRA center jayen.
2. **Union Council ka birth certificate** (durust maloomat wala) le jayen — ye sab se ahem saboot hai.
3. Agar school me dakhil hai to **school certificate** bhi kaam aa sakta hai.
4. Bari correction ke liye **affidavit** (halaf-nama) mangi ja sakti hai.
5. Correction ki fee normal CRC fee schedule ke mutabiq hoti hai — center se confirm kar lein.

**NADRA khud walidain se kehta hai ke bachon ke B-Form foran update karwayen** — ghalat record 18 saal par CNIC banwane me rukawat banta hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Walidain Karte Hain

1. **Der se apply karna:** School admission ke waqt bhag-daud hoti hai — pedaish ke chand mahine me banwa lein.
2. **Naam ka farq:** Birth certificate aur B-Form par naam aik jaisa hona chahiye — "Muhammad" vs "Mohammad" jaisa farq bhi baad me masla banta hai.
3. **UC registration bhoolna:** Seedha NADRA chale jate hain, wahan se wapas UC bheja jata hai — pehle UC, phir NADRA.
4. **Purani tasveer:** Bare bache ki haaliya tasveer le jayen, warna dobara ana parega.
5. **Sirf aik walid ka CNIC:** Dono walidain ke CNIC zaroori hain — aik na ho to kaam rukk jata hai.

## B-Form Aur School Admission: Waqt Par Tayyari

Pakistan ke aksar private aur sarkari schools admission ke waqt B-Form mangte hain — kuch schools to form ke saath hi B-Form ki copy lagane ko kehte hain. Agar admission ka season (aam tor par March-April) qareeb hai aur B-Form nahi bana, to:

1. **Foran executive processing** par apply karein (Rs 500, 1 din) — normal ka 7 din ka wait admission se pehle khatam ho sakta hai.
2. School se **waqti mohlat** mang lein — aksar schools tracking ID ya raseed dekh kar aik-do hafte ki mohlat de dete hain.
3. Admission form par bache ka **naam B-Form wali spelling se** likhein — baad me board ke exams me naam ka farq bara masla banta hai.

Board (Matric/Inter) ki registration me to B-Form/CRC number lazmi hai — wahan ghalti ki gunjaish bilkul nahi.

## Overseas Pakistanis Ke Liye Tafseel Se

Bahir rehne wale Pakistani walidain ke liye process thoda mukhtalif hai:

1. **Pak ID portal** (id.nadra.gov.pk) par account banayen — wahan "Child Registration Certificate" chunein.
2. Bache ki maloomat aur documents **scan karke upload** karein — walidain me se aik ka NICOP lazmi hai.
3. Fee **dollar me** ada hogi — exact amount checkout par nazar aayegi (card se payment).
4. Processing me **2 se 6 hafte** lag sakte hain (courier ka waqt shamil).
5. Digital copy Pak ID inbox me aa jati hai; printed form **courier** se bheja jata hai.

Agar bache ki pedaish bahir hui hai to **foreign birth certificate** ke saath walidain ke NICOP ki copies lagana na bhoolein — baghair is ke application reject ho sakti hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal (Jari)

**Kya B-Form ghar bethe ban sakta hai?**
1 saal se chote bache ke liye ji haan — Pak ID app se. Bare bachon ke liye center jana parta hai.

**B-Form ki fee kitni hai?**
Rs 50 normal (7 din), Rs 500 executive (1 din).

**Kya B-Form ke baghair bache ka passport ban sakta hai?**
Nahi — bache ke passport ke liye B-Form lazmi hai.

**Bache ki umar 18 saal ho gayi, ab kya karein?**
Ab B-Form nahi, **CNIC** banega — pehli dafa ka CNIC normal processing par muft hai.

**Judwaan bachon ke liye alag-alag B-Form banta hai?**
Ji haan — har bache ka alag CRC banta hai, har aik ki fee alag hogi.

**Overseas Pakistani bache ka B-Form kaise banwayen?**
Pak ID portal (id.nadra.gov.pk) se online apply karein — fees dollar me hongi aur document courier se bhejey jayenge.

**Kya B-Form ki bhi expiry hoti hai?**
B-Form ki koi expiry nahi hoti, lekin bache ki maloomat (naam, date of birth) durust hona zaroori hai. 18 saal ki umar me CNIC banwate waqt B-Form ka record istemal hota hai, is liye us se pehle correction karwa lein.

## B-Form Ke Baad School Admission Checklist

School admission ke liye sirf B-Form kaafi nahi — ye bhi sambhal lein:

- Walidain ke **CNIC ki copies**
- Bache ki **tasaveer** (school ke form ke hisab se)
- **Pichli class ki report** (bari classes ke liye)
- **Polio/vaccination card**

B-Form agar taza bana hai to us ki **3 photocopies** admission se pehle karwa lein.
