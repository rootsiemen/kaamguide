## Pehli Baat: Free Solar Panel Scheme Kya Hai Aur Kis Ko Milta Hai

**CM Punjab Free Solar Panel Scheme** — jise **Roshan Gharana** ke naam se bhi jana jata hai — Punjab hukumat ki aik bari scheme hai, jo Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Shareef ki taraf se shuru ki gayi thi. Is ka maqsad aik hi hai: jin gharon ka bijli ka bill kam aata hai, un ka bill aur kam ho jaye — aur woh bhi bina koi kharcha kiye.

Is scheme ke tehat Punjab hukumat **100,000 solar systems muft** de rahi hai. Ye systems un gharon ko milenge jin ki bijli ki khapat **0 se 200 units mahana** ke darmiyan hai. Selection **transparent balloting** (qurandazi) se hoti hai — matlab na koi sifarish chalti hai, na koi fee li jati hai. Agar aap ki khapat is had me hai aur aap Punjab ke domestic (gharlu) bijli sarfeen hain, to aap is scheme ke mustahiq ho sakte hain.

Scheme December 2024 me shuru hui thi aur is ke baad is ke naye phases aate rahe hain. 2026 me bhi scheme chal rahi hai — applications phases me li jati hain, is liye apply karne se pehle maujooda phase ki status official portal par zaroor check karein. Is guide me aap ko ehliyaat, zaroori cheezein, portal aur SMS dono tareeqon se apply karne ka step-by-step tareeqa, balloting aur installation ka process — sab kuch aik jagah milega.

## Scheme Ki Tafseel: Aap Ko Kya Milega

Sab se pehle ye samajh lein ke is scheme me aap ko milega kya:

| Cheez | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| **Muft solar systems** | **100,000** tak (official portal ke mutabiq) |
| **Kis ko milega** | Punjab ke domestic bijli sarfeen, jin ki khapat **0–200 units** mahana ho |
| **System me kya hota hai** | Solar panel, inverter aur battery — ghar ka chhota solar system |
| **Apply ki fee** | **Koi fee nahi** — registration bilkul muft hai |
| **Selection ka tareeqa** | **Transparent balloting** (qurandazi) |
| **Apply ke tareeqe** | Official portal **cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk** ya **SMS 8800** par |
| **Ehliyaat kis bill par** | **June 2024** ke bijli ke bill par (official portal ke mutabiq) |
| **Sanctioned load** | **2 kW tak** |

Solar system lagne ke baad aap ka bijli ka bill kafi kam ho sakta hai, kyun ke din ke waqt aap ki bijli ka aik hissa suraj se banega. Is ke sath sath load shedding ke waqt bhi aap ko aik reliable bijli ka zariya mil jata hai, aur ye mahol ke liye bhi behtar hai — isi liye hukumat is scheme ko "Greener Punjab" ke hisse ke tor par chala rahi hai.

Aik baat saaf kar dein: kuch websites system ki wattage (kitne watt ka panel milega) bhi likhti hain, lekin official portal par is ki koi hatmi tafseel nahi di gayi. Is liye is guide me sirf wohi likha gaya hai jo official portal se confirm hota hai — system ka size aur tafseel selection ke baad verification ke waqt portal ya DISCO ki taraf se batayi jayegi.

## Ehliyaat: Apply Karne Ki Shartein Kya Hain

Apply karne se pehle apni ehliyaat in sharton se mila lein. In me se aik bhi shart poori na ho to application aage nahi barhegi:

**1. Domestic (gharlu) connection hona lazmi hai:** Sirf gharlu bijli ke sarfeen is scheme ke liye eligible hain. Commercial ya industrial connection wale is scheme ke liye apply nahi kar sakte.

**2. Mahana khapat 0–200 units ke darmiyan ho:** Aap ki bijli ki khapat in categories me se kisi aik me aani chahiye:
- **Up to 50 units (Life Line)**
- **51–100 units (Life Line)**
- **0–100 units (Protected)**
- **0–100 units (Un-protected)**
- **101–200 units (Protected)**
- **101–200 units (Unprotected)**

**3. June 2024 ka bill bunyad hai:** Official portal ke mutabiq aap ki ehliyaat **June 2024 ke bijli ke bill** par tay hoti hai. Matlab us mahine aap ki khapat 200 units ya is se kam honi chahiye. Agar koi naya phase kisi aur mahine ka bill mangta hai to ye portal par saaf likha hoga — is liye apply karne se pehle portal par maujooda shart zaroor parh lein. [Bijli ka bill online check karne ka tareeqa](/guides/bijli-ka-bill-online-check) yahan dekhein.

**4. Sanctioned load 2 kW tak ho:** Aap ke connection ka sanctioned load **2 kW ya is se kam** hona chahiye. Ye maloomat aap ke bijli ke bill par likhi hoti hai.

**5. Punjab ka rehaishi hon:** Ye Punjab hukumat ki scheme hai, is liye is ka faida Punjab ke DISCOs (LESCO, MEPCO, FESCO, GEPCO aur IESCO) ke ilaqon ke sarfeen ko milta hai.

**Kin logon ko ye scheme NAHI milti:** Official portal ke mutabiq ye sarfeen eligible nahi hain:
- Bijli chori me mulawwis sarfeen
- Jin ka meter kharab (defective) ho
- Jin ke aik hi rehaish par multiple (aik se zyada) meter lage hon
- Jin hon ne pichhle 12 mahinon me **3 se zyada dafa bill ki adayegi me takheer** ki ho

## Zaroori Cheezein: Apply Karne Se Pehle Ye Tayyar Rakhein

Form bharne se pehle ye cheezein apne paas rakh lein — in ke baghair registration mukammal nahi hogi:

- **Apna CNIC number** — 13 hindson ka CNIC number; [CNIC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein
- **Bijli ke bill ka reference number** — 14 hindson ka reference number aap ke bijli ke bill par upar ki taraf likha hota hai; isi se system aap ki khapat aur load khud check karta hai
- **Active mobile number** — is par verification aur balloting ki khabar SMS se aayegi
- **June 2024 ka bijli ka bill** — ehliyaat isi bill se check hoti hai, is liye bill sambhal kar rakhein

![Bijli ka bill aur CNIC haath me liye shakhs mobile par solar scheme ki eligibility check kar raha hai](/images/cm-punjab-free-solar-panel-scheme-apply-online-2.jpg)

**Tayyari ka mashwara:** Bill ka reference number ghalat likhna sab se aam ghalti hai. Bill uthayein, reference number ko aik aik hinsa mila kar likhein, aur phir CNIC number bhi isi tarah check karein. Dono numbers durust hon to system aap ka record foran nikal leta hai aur aap ko form bharne me aik minute bhi nahi lagta.

## Step-by-Step: Official Portal Se Online Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

Poora process **online** hai — kisi office jane ya kisi agent ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi. Ye steps follow karein:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne browser me official portal **cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk** kholein. Address khud type karein — kisi message, WhatsApp forward ya social media post ke link par click kar ke portal par na jayein, kyun ke nakli websites aap ka data chura sakti hain.

**Step 2 — Register par click karein:** Portal ki homepage par **Register** ka option milega. Us par click karein.

**Step 3 — CNIC aur reference number darj karein:** Form me apna **13 hindson ka CNIC number** aur bijli ke bill ka **14 hindson ka reference number** likhein. System aap ka data DISCO ke record se khud mila kar aap ki ehliyaat check karega.

**Step 4 — Apni tafseel bharein:** Agar aap eligible nikle to form me apni zaati maloomat (naam, pata, mobile number) darj karein. Har field me wohi likhein jo aap ke bill aur CNIC par hai — aik harf ka farq bhi verification me masla bana sakta hai.

**Step 5 — Form submit karein aur Tracking ID sambhalein:** Sab kuch aik dafa phir parh kar **Submit** karein. Submit ke baad aap ko **Tracking ID** milega — isay screenshot le kar ya likh kar sambhal kar rakhein. Isi ID se aap apni application ka status aur balloting ka natija check karein ge.

**Step 6 — Balloting ka intezar karein:** Applications jama hone ke baad **transparent balloting** hoti hai. Balloting ke natije ka elaan phases me, zila-wah-zila kiya jata hai — is liye portal aur apna mobile check karte rahein.

## SMS Se Apply: 8800 Par Message Bhejein

Agar aap ke paas internet nahi hai ya portal khul nahi raha to aap **SMS** se bhi apply kar sakte hain — ye tareeqa bhi official hai:

**Step 1 — SMS likhein:** Apne mobile se aik SMS likhein, jis me pehle apne bijli ke bill ka **reference number**, phir aik **space**, aur phir apna **CNIC number** likhein. Misal ke tor par: `ReferenceNumber CNICNumber`

**Step 2 — 8800 par bhejein:** Ye SMS **8800** par bhej dein.

**Step 3 — Confirmation ka intezar karein:** Agar aap eligible hain to aap ko SMS se confirmation aur aap ki application ka reference milega. Isay sambhal kar rakhein.

SMS se apply karna bhi **bilkul muft** hai — sirf aap ke mobile package ke mutabiq SMS ka aam charge lag sakta hai. Jo shakhs registration ke naam par koi fee mange, woh hukumat ka numainda nahi — woh fraud hai.

## Balloting, Verification Aur Installation Kaise Hoti Hai

Form submit karne ke baad aap ki application is process se guzarti hai — official portal ke mutabiq ye 6 stages hain:

1. **Registration on web portal:** Aap portal ya SMS se register karte hain.
2. **Add details:** Aap apni tafseel aur documents ki maloomat darj karte hain.
3. **Check eligibility:** System aap ki khapat, load aur record DISCO se check karta hai.
4. **Balloting of registered consumers:** Eligible sarfeen me se **transparent balloting** se selection hoti hai.
5. **Physical verification of qualified consumers:** Selected sarfeen ki **physical verification** hoti hai — team aap ke ghar aa kar meter, connection aur chhat ki jagah check karti hai.
6. **Successful installation:** Verification mukammal hone ke baad aap ke ghar par **solar system install** kar diya jata hai.

![Chhat par solar panel install karte hue technicians — balloting aur verification ke baad installation ka marhala](/images/cm-punjab-free-solar-panel-scheme-apply-online-3.jpg)

**Kitna waqt lagta hai:** Is ka koi aik hatmi waqt nahi bataya gaya, kyun ke process phases me chalta hai — balloting zila-wah-zila hoti hai aur installation bhi batches me hoti hai. Aam tor par registration se installation tak **kuch hafte se kuch mahine** lag sakte hain. Aap ki bari kab aayegi, ye aap ke zila aur phase par munhasir hai — is liye Tracking ID se portal par status check karte rahein aur kisi bhi "foran installation" ke dawe par yaqeen na karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Application Rok Deti Hain

- **Ghalat reference number likhna:** Bill ka reference number aik hinsa bhi ghalat ho to system aap ka record nahi nikal sakta. Bill se mila kar likhein.
- **June 2024 ke bill ki shart nazar andaz karna:** Aaj ka bill kam aana kafi nahi — ehliyaat June 2024 ke bill par tay hoti hai. Pehle apna purana bill check karein, phir apply karein.
- **200 units se zyada khapat par apply karna:** Agar June 2024 me aap ki khapat 201 units bhi thi to aap eligible nahi hain — chahe baaqi mahinon me khapat 100 se bhi kam rahi ho.
- **Nakli portal ya agent ko paise dena:** Scheme ki registration **bilkul muft** hai. Jo website ya shakhs apply karane ke badle fee, bank transfer ya card details mange, woh fraud hai. Sirf **cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk** ya **8800 SMS** istemal karein.
- **Aik se zyada dafa apply karna:** Aik connection par aik hi application kafi hai. Dobara apply karne se aap ki application par shak ho sakta hai ya record kharab ho sakta hai.
- **Tracking ID zaya kar dena:** Submit ke baad milne wala Tracking ID status check karne ke liye chahiye hota hai. Isay screenshot le kar sambhal kar rakhein.
- **DISCO office ke chakkar lagana:** Registration sirf portal ya SMS se hoti hai — DISCO office jane se application tez nahi hoti. Verification team khud aap se rabta karegi.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CM Punjab Free Solar Panel Scheme ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?**

Punjab ke domestic (gharlu) bijli sarfeen jin ki mahana khapat **0–200 units** ho aur jin ka sanctioned load **2 kW tak** ho, woh apply kar sakte hain. Ehliyaat **June 2024 ke bill** par tay hoti hai. Bijli chori, kharab meter, aik rehaish par multiple meter, ya 12 mahinon me 3 se zyada late payments wale sarfeen eligible nahi hain.

**Free solar panel ke liye apply kaise karein?**

Do tareeqe hain: official portal **cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk** par ja kar apna CNIC aur bill ka reference number darj karein, ya apne bill ka reference number aur CNIC aik SMS me likh kar **8800** par bhej dein. Dono tareeqe muft hain — kisi ko koi fee na dein.

**Scheme me kitne solar systems diye ja rahe hain?**

Official portal ke mutabiq hukumat **100,000 tak solar systems muft** de rahi hai. Selection transparent balloting se hoti hai, aur systems phases me, zila-wah-zila diye jate hain.

**Solar system milne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**

Is ka koi aik hatmi waqt nahi hai — registration, eligibility check, balloting, physical verification aur phir installation, ye sab phases me hota hai. Registration se installation tak kuch hafte se kuch mahine lag sakte hain. Apna status Tracking ID se portal par check karte rahein.

**Apply karne ki koi fee hai?**

Nahi — is scheme ki registration **bilkul muft** hai. Na portal par koi fee hai, na SMS par. Jo shakhs solar panel dilane ke naam par paise, bank details ya card ki maloomat mange, woh fraud hai — sirf official portal cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk ya 8800 SMS istemal karein aur aisi kisi bhi demand ki ittila apne DISCO ya helpline par dein.
