## Renewal Kya Hai Aur Kab Karwana Lazmi Hai

Driving license aik muddat ke liye banta hai — Punjab me aap 1 se 5 saal tak ki validity le sakte hain. Jab ye muddat khatam ho jaye to license **expire** ho jata hai, aur expired license par gaari ya bike chalana qanoonan ghalat hai. Aise me pakre jane par [e-challan](/guides/punjab-e-challan-check-aur-pay-karne-ka-tareeqa) aur bhari jurmana ho sakta hai, aur kisi hadse ki soorat me insurance claim bhi mushkil ho sakta hai.

Renewal ka matlab hai purane license ki muddat barhana — is ke liye aap ko dobara learner license lene ya dobara driving test dene ki zaroorat **nahi** hoti. Aam tor par sirf fee ada karni hoti hai, kagzaat verify hote hain aur naya card courier se ghar aa jata hai. Agar license bohat arse (aik saal se zyada) se expired ho ya umar ya sehat ki wajah se medical darkar ho to center mazeed jaanch maang sakta hai, lekin ye mustasna (exception) hai, usool nahi.

Behtareen waqt ye hai ke **expiry se aik mahine pehle** renewal ke liye apply kar dein. Is se aik to late jurmana bachta hai, doosra naye card ke aane tak aap kabhi baghair valid license ke nahi rehte. Expiry ki tareekh aap ke card par likhi hoti hai — aaj hi check kar lein, kyunke bohat se logon ko pata hi nahi chalta ke un ka license kab ka expire ho chuka hai.

Ye guide sirf **Punjab** ke driving license ki renewal ke liye hai. Agar aap ka license kisi doosre soobe se bana hai to renewal usi soobe ke nizam se hoga, lekin Punjab me rehne walon ke liye agla license Punjab se banwana zyada aasan rehta hai. Naye license ka poora tareeqa (learner se permanent tak) hamari [driving license banane ki guide](/guides/driving-license-banane-ka-tareeqa) me mojood hai.

## Renewal Ke Liye Zaroori Kagzaat

Online ya center, dono routes par ye cheezein tayyar rakhein:

- **Asal CNIC** (aur us ki aik copy) — CNIC par darj pata aur license record ka pata aik jaisa hona chahiye
- **Purana (expired ya expiring) driving license** — card ya us ka number
- **Haaliya passport-size tasveer** (online apply ke liye digital photo)
- **Mobile number** jo aap ke naam par registered ho — SMS aur tracking isi par aayegi
- **Medical certificate** — sirf 50 saal se zyada umar walon ke liye, ya HTV/PSV category ke liye (sarkari doctor se)
- **Fee ki payment raseed (PSID)** — online apply me ye system khud generate karta hai

Agar aap ka pata tabdeel ho gaya hai to pehle apne record me pata durust karwayen. Pate ki tabdeeli ke liye [CNIC banane aur update karne ki guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) madadgar rahegi, kyunke license par ghalat pate par courier ghalat jagah ja sakta hai.

## Renewal Fees 2026 (DLIMS Fee Table)

Neeche aik saal ki renewal ki fees hain. Har category me **Rs 480 courier charge** aik dafa shamil hai — card aap ke ghar courier se aata hai, isi liye ye charge har row me aik jaisa hai. Combination categories (masalan bike + car) me dono fees jama nahi hoti — sirf **zyada fee wali category** ki fee lagti hai.

| Category | Renewal Fee (1 saal) | Courier | Kul (1 saal) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motorcycle | Rs 450 | Rs 480 | **Rs 930** |
| Rickshaw | Rs 400 | Rs 480 | **Rs 880** |
| Tractor (zarai) | Rs 980 | Rs 480 | **Rs 1,430** |
| Car / Jeep | Rs 1,350 | Rs 480 | **Rs 1,830** |
| Tractor (commercial) | Rs 1,400 | Rs 480 | **Rs 1,880** |
| LTV / HTV / PSV | Rs 1,850 | Rs 480 | **Rs 2,330** |
| Invalid Carriage | Muft | Rs 480 | **Rs 480** |

### 5 Saal Ki Validity Lena Behtar Hai?

Punjab me license 1 se 5 saal tak ki validity par renew ho sakta hai. Fee usi hisab se multiply hoti hai, courier sirf aik dafa lagta hai. Masalan 5 saal ka car license = Rs 1,350 × 5 + Rs 480 = **Rs 7,230**. Agar budget ijazat de to 5 saal ka renew karwa lein — har saal renewal ki pareshani khatam aur mustaqbil me fee barhne ka khatra bhi nahi. Motorcycle ke liye 5 saal ka kul Rs 450 × 5 + Rs 480 = **Rs 2,730** banta hai.

**Ahem note:** Fees waqtan-fo-waqtan revise hoti hain. Upar di gayi fees Punjab Traffic Police ke DLIMS fee schedule ke mutabiq hain — lekin **hatmi rakam wohi hogi jo aap ke PSID challan par likhi ho**. Payment se pehle PSID par rakam zaroor mila lein.

## Late Renewal Ka Jurmana (Der Se Renew Karwane Ki Qeemat)

Ye woh hissa hai jo logon ko sab se zyada mehnga parta hai. Jurmana expiry ki tareekh se shumaar hota hai — apply karne ki tareekh se nahi. Matlab agar aap ne der se apply kiya to der ka waqfa kam nahi hoga.

| Der kitni hui | Jurmana (renewal fee ka) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 se 3 mahine late | Fee ka **aadha (50%)** |
| 3 mahine se 1 saal late | Fee ke **barabar (100%)** |
| 1 saal se zyada late | Fee ka **2.5 guna (250%)** |

Aam categories ke liye jurmane ki raqam:

| Category | 1–3 mahine | 3 mahine–1 saal | 1 saal se zyada |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motorcycle | Rs 225 | Rs 450 | Rs 1,125 |
| Car / Jeep | Rs 675 | Rs 1,350 | Rs 3,375 |
| LTV / HTV / PSV | Rs 925 | Rs 1,850 | Rs 4,625 |

Misaal: car ka license 1 saal se zyada late renew karwayen to kul kharcha Rs 1,350 (fee) + Rs 3,375 (jurmana) + Rs 480 (courier) = **Rs 5,205** ho jayega, jabke waqt par renewal sirf **Rs 1,830** me ho jati. Is liye sab se sasta tareeqa ye hai ke expiry se pehle hi renew karwa lein, aur agar der ho bhi gayi ho to 3 mahine ki had se pehle kaam poora kar lein.

## Online Renewal Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Punjab me renewal ab DLIMS portal (dlims.punjab.gov.pk) aur Dastak service ke zariye online ho jati hai. Dono ka usool aik hai: pehchan, PSID, payment, phir courier.

1. **DLIMS portal ya Dastak app kholein** — apne CNIC aur mobile number se login karein ya naya account banayen.
2. **Service chunein** — services me se **License Renewal** muntakhib karein aur apni category (bike, car, LTV waghera) batayen.
3. **License number darj karein** — purana license number aur CNIC number likhein; system aap ka record khud utha lega.
4. **Maloomat ki tasdeeq karein** — naam, pata aur category screen par ghor se parhein; ghalti ho to aage barhne se pehle durust karwayen.
5. **Photo aur kagzaat upload karein** — haaliya tasveer aur zaroorat parne par medical certificate upload karein.
6. **PSID generate karein** — system aap ki fee + jurmana (agar late hai) + courier mila kar aik Payment Slip ID (PSID) banayega.
7. **Fee ada karein** — PSID ke zariye **ePay Punjab, JazzCash, Easypaisa, mobile banking, ATM ya bank branch** se payment karein.
8. **Raseed sambhaal kar rakhein** — payment confirm hone par tracking number mil jayega; SMS bhi aa jayega.

![Mobile se PSID ke zariye driving license renewal fee ki online payment karte hue aik shaks](/images/punjab-driving-license-renewal-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

**Doorstep option:** Maryam Ki Dastak ke zariye ghar par bhi renewal ki sahulat mil sakti hai, lekin is par aik alag service charge lag sakta hai. Agar aap ko center jane me dikkat hai to ye option pooch lein, warna online tareeqa sab se sasta hai.

## Licensing Center Se Renewal (Walk-in Tareeqa)

Agar aap online apply nahi karna chahte to qareebi **Traffic Police Licensing Center** ja kar bhi renewal ho jati hai:

1. Center par token lein aur renewal counter par jayen.
2. Asal CNIC, purana license aur tasveeren dikhayen.
3. Biometric tasdeeq (fingerprints aur photo) hogi.
4. Counter se PSID banwayen aur wahin ya qareebi bank/mobile wallet se fee ada karein.
5. Raseed le kar ghar jayen — naya card courier se aayega.

Center jane ka faida ye hai ke koi masla (masalan record me ghalti) wahin hal ho jata hai. Nuqsan ye ke waqt zyada lagta hai aur rush bhi ho sakta hai. Jo log online apply me ulajh jate hain, un ke liye center ka rasta behtar hai. Kisi **agent ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi** — renewal me na test hota hai na sifarish chalti hai, agent sirf aap ka waqt aur paisa zaya karega.

## Naya License Kab Milega Aur E-License Kaise Hasil Karein

Payment ke baad naya card banne aur courier se pohanchne me aam tor par **kuch hafte** lag sakte hain — is doran aap baghair license ke nahi rahenge, kyunke **e-license** foran kaam aata hai:

1. **dlims.punjab.gov.pk/elicense** par jayen.
2. License type chunein, **CNIC aur date of birth** darj karein.
3. E-license generate karke **PDF download** kar lein aur phone me save rakhein.

Traffic police ne khud tasdeeq ki hai ke e-license qanooni tor par valid hai jab tak printed card na aa jaye. Card aane tak purana (expired) card bhi saath rakhein taake record milan me asani ho. Agar card 4 hafte se zyada late ho to DLIMS tracking se status check karein ya licensing center se rabta karein.

![Courier ke zariye ghar par naya driving license card deliver hote hue](/images/punjab-driving-license-renewal-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

**Valid license ki aik aur zaroorat:** Agar aap bahar ke mulk me gaari chalana chahte hain to [international driving permit](/guides/international-driving-permit-pakistan) sirf valid Pakistani license par hi banta hai — expired license par IDP nahi milega, is liye pehle renewal karwayen.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Expiry bhool jana:** Card par expiry date kabhi check hi nahi karte — aaj hi check karein aur phone me reminder lagayen.
2. **Der se apply karna:** Jurmana expiry se shumaar hota hai, apply ki tareekh se nahi — der jitni barhegi, jurmana utna barhega.
3. **Agenton ko paise dena:** Renewal me koi test ya sifarish nahi hoti — agent kuch nahi karwa sakta.
4. **Ghalat pata:** Courier ghalat pate par chala jaye to card wapas ho jata hai aur dobara delivery me hafte lag jate hain — apply se pehle pata durust karwayen.
5. **PSID ki rakam na milana:** Payment se pehle PSID par fee, jurmana aur courier alag-alag dekh lein — ghalat payment wapas lena mushkil kaam hai.
6. **E-license na rakhna:** Card ke intezar me phone me e-license na ho to checking par pareshani ho sakti hai.
7. **Expired license par gaari chalana:** Ye sirf challan nahi, hadse ki soorat me qanooni aur insurance dono masle paida karta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab me driving license renewal ki fee kitni hai?**
Aik saal ke liye motorcycle Rs 930, rickshaw Rs 880, car/jeep Rs 1,830 aur LTV/HTV/PSV Rs 2,330 — in me Rs 480 courier charge shamil hai. 5 saal ki validity par fee 5 guna ho jati hai, courier sirf aik dafa lagta hai.

**Late renewal par kitna jurmana lagta hai?**
1 se 3 mahine late par fee ka aadha, 3 mahine se 1 saal late par fee ke barabar, aur 1 saal se zyada late par fee ka 2.5 guna jurmana lagta hai. Jurmana expiry ki tareekh se shumaar hota hai.

**Kya renewal ke liye dobara driving test dena parta hai?**
Aam tor par nahi — renewal me sirf fee aur record ki tasdeeq hoti hai. Sirf khaas sooraton (bohat arse se expired license ya medical wajah) me center mazeed jaanch maang sakta hai.

**Kya license online renew ho sakta hai?**
Ji haan — DLIMS portal ya Dastak service se online apply hota hai. PSID banwayen, ePay Punjab, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya bank se fee ada karein aur naya card courier se ghar aa jayega.

**Naya card aane tak gaari kaise chalaun?**
DLIMS se e-license download kar lein (CNIC aur date of birth se) — ye qanooni tor par valid hai jab tak printed card post se na aa jaye. Phone me save rakhna kaafi hai.

Mazeed tasdeeq ke liye dlims.punjab.gov.pk ya qareebi Traffic Police Licensing Center se rabta karein. Ye maloomat 27 October 2026 ko check ki gayi thin.
