## Pehli Baat: Punjab Rashan Card Kya Hai

**Punjab Rashan Card** (jise CM Punjab Ration Card bhi kaha jata hai) Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo programme hai jis ke tehet kam aamdani wale gharanon ko har mahine **Rs 3,000 ki grocery madad** di jati hai. Ye card koi cash payment nahi hai — is par milne wali raqam sirf rozmarra ki khane-peene ki cheezon par kharch ho sakti hai, jaise aata, chawal, cheeni, ghee aur daalein. Programme **29 April 2025** ko Chief Minister Punjab **Maryam Nawaz Sharif** ne launch kiya tha aur is ko **Punjab Social Protection Authority (PSPA)** chalati hai.

Is programme ki bunyad **PSER — Punjab Socio-Economic Registry** hai. PSER Punjab bhar ke gharanon ka aik bara database hai jis me ghar ki aamdani, afrad ki tadad, taleem, rozgar aur rehaish jaisi maloomat darj hoti hain. Aap Rashan Card ke liye seedha apply nahi karte — pehle aap ka gharana PSER survey me darj hota hai, phir is data ki bunyad par tay hota hai ke aap mustahiq hain ya nahi. Isi liye is guide me sab se zyada tawajjo PSER registration par di gayi hai, kyun ke wahi asal apply ka rasta hai.

**Phase 1** me is programme ka hadaf **12.5 lakh (1.25 million) mustahiq families** tak pohanchna tha, aur agle marhalon me mazeed families shamil karne ka elan waqtan fawaqtan hota rehta hai. Is ka matlab ye hai ke agar aap ki family abhi select nahi hui to bhi PSER me registration karwana faida-mand hai — agle phase ki selection bhi isi data se hoti hai.

## Rashan Card Se Kya Kuch Milta Hai

Rashan Card par milne wali sahulat seedhi aur saaf hai. Neeche ki table me har cheez ka khulasa diya gaya hai:

| Cheez | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| **Mahana madad** | **Rs 3,000** har mahine grocery ke liye |
| **Kis cheez par kharch ho sakti hai** | Aata, chawal, cheeni, ghee, daalein aur rozmarra ki grocery |
| **Cash nikalna** | **Nahi** — card se naqad raqam nahi nikalti |
| **Kahan istemal hota hai** | Registered utility stores aur karyana shops par |
| **Registration fee** | **Muft — Rs 0** |
| **Registration ka rasta** | PSER portal ya Union Council / e-Khidmat Markaz |
| **Chalane wala idara** | Punjab Social Protection Authority (PSPA) |
| **Official portal** | **pser.punjab.gov.pk** |

Aik baat pehle se saaf kar dein: internet par kuch guides Rs 3,000 ke bajaye Rs 10,000 mahana ka zikr karti hain. Ye Rs 10,000 wali raqam Ramzan ke khaas package ke hawale se report hui hai, mahana bunyadi madad nahi. Mahana grocery madad **Rs 3,000** hi hai — Ramzan ya kisi khaas mauqe par izafi package ka elan alag se hota hai, aur us ki tasdeeq official elan ya PSER portal se hi karein.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai — Ehliyaat Ki Shartein

Rashan Card har Punjab rehne wale ko nahi milta. Selection PSER data ki bunyad par hoti hai aur aam tor par ye shartein dekhi jati hain:

- **Punjab ka mustaqil rehaishi hona lazmi hai.** Doosre soobe ke rehaishi, chahe wo Punjab me rehte hon, is programme ke liye eligible nahi hain.
- **PSER survey mukammal hona lazmi hai.** Aap ke gharane ka record Punjab Socio-Economic Registry me darj hona chahiye. Bina PSER record ke application aage nahi barhti.
- **Mahana gharana aamdani Rs 50,000 se kam** honi chahiye. Ye woh hadd hai jo is programme ke hawale se aam tor par report hoti hai.
- **Sarkari mulazim eligible nahi.** Agar gharane ka koi fard sarkari mulazmat me hai to aam tor par family is programme ke liye eligible nahi hoti.
- **BISP se faida uthane wale gharanon ko Rashan Card nahi milta.** Jo family pehle se Benazir Income Support Programme se madad le rahi hai, wo is card ke liye apply nahi kar sakti. Agar aap BISP ki status check karna chahte hain to hamari [BISP 8171 registration check guide](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) dekhein.
- **Aik family, aik application.** Har gharane se sirf aik fard apply kar sakta hai — aik se zyada applications se faida nahi hota, ulta verification me masla ho sakta hai.
- **Valid CNIC** lazmi hai — apply karne wale ke paas apna computerized CNIC hona chahiye.

**PMT score ke baare me aik zaroori wazahat:** Internet par kuch guides PMT (Proxy Means Test) score ki hadd "35 se kam" batati hain aur kuch "32 se kam". Ye dono figures aapas me mutabiq nahi hain, is liye in me se kisi aik ko hatmi sach ke tor par is guide me nahi likha ja raha. Asal me aap ka PMT score aap ke PSER survey ke data se banta hai, aur aap ki ehliyaat ka hatmi faisla official portal aur PSPA ka data karta hai — kisi guide ki likhi hui hadd nahi. Is liye score ki fikar chhor kar PSER survey mukammal karwane par tawajjo dein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Maloomat

PSER registration ke waqt ye cheezein tayyar rakhein to form bharna asaan ho jayega:

- **Apna CNIC** (aur ghar ke barhe afrad ke CNIC numbers bhi)
- **Apne naam par registered mobile number** — is par verification aur status ki maloomat aati hai
- **Ghar ke tamam afrad ki maloomat** — naam, umar, taleem aur rozgar ki tafseel
- **Mahana aamdani ki maloomat** — ghar ke tamam kamane wale afrad ki aamdani
- **Rehaish ka pata** — maujooda pata aur agar ho to domicile se mutaliq maloomat
- **Bachon ki taleem ki maloomat** — agar bache school ya college me parhte hain

Koi bhi kagaz jama nahi karwana hota — PSER survey me maloomat darj hoti hain, aur baad me agar kisi cheez ki tasdeeq darkar ho to mutaliqa idara khud rabta karta hai. Kisi bhi shakhs ko apne CNIC ki copy ya paisay dene se pehle yaqeen kar lein ke wo asal sarkari numainda hai.

## PSER Survey Aur Registration Ka Tareeqa

Rashan Card ke liye apply karne ka asal matlab hai **PSER me apna gharana register karwana**. Is ke do tareeqe hain — online aur offline. Dono muft hain.

### Tareeqa 1 — Online: PSER Portal Par

1. **Official portal kholein:** Apne mobile ya computer par **pser.punjab.gov.pk** likh kar kholein. Pata khud type karein — WhatsApp ya Facebook par aane wale links par click na karein, kyun ke jaali websites asal jaisi dikh kar aap ka CNIC aur maloomat chura sakti hain.
2. **Register par click karein:** Portal par "Register" ka button milega. Apna CNIC number aur mobile number darj karein.
3. **Account banayein:** Mobile par aane wale verification code se apna account confirm karein.
4. **Household survey form bharein:** Form me ghar ke afrad, aamdani, taleem, rozgar aur rehaish ki maloomat darj karein. Har jawab sahi likhein — ghalat maloomat se verification ruk jati hai.
5. **Form submit karein:** Submit karne ke baad aap ko aik reference ya confirmation milegi — is ko mehfooz rakh lein.
6. **Selection ka intezar karein:** PSER registration ka matlab ye nahi ke card foran mil jayega. Aap ka data verify hota hai, PMT score banta hai aur phir marhala-waar (phase-wise) selection hoti hai. Jo gharanay muntakhib hote hain, un ko card jaari kiya jata hai.

### Tareeqa 2 — Offline: Union Council Ya e-Khidmat Markaz

Agar aap ke paas internet nahi ya online form bharna mushkil lagta hai to ye tareeqa behtar hai:

1. Apna **asal CNIC** le kar apne qareebi **Union Council office** ya **e-Khidmat Markaz** jayein.
2. Wahan maujood staff aap ki PSER registration muft kar dega — koi fee nahi li jati.
3. Staff aap se wohi maloomat poochega jo online form me hoti hain, is liye upar wali kagzaat ki list tayyar rakh kar jayein.
4. Registration ke baad aap ko bataya jayega ke aap ka record darj ho gaya hai.

**PSER survey ghar par bhi hota hai:** PSER ka asal tareeqa door-to-door survey hai — trained teams tablet par maloomat darj karte hue gharon par jati hain. Agar aap ke ilaqe me survey team aaye to un se bhi registration karwa sakte hain. Bas yaqeen kar lein ke team ke paas official pehchan hai aur wo koi paisay nahi maang rahi.

## Card Milne Ke Baad Istemal Ka Tareeqa

Jab aap select ho jayein aur card mil jaye to is ka istemal aise karein:

1. **Registered dukaan par jayein:** Card sirf un utility stores aur karyana shops par chalta hai jo programme me registered hain. Kisi bhi aam dukaan par ye card kaam nahi karega.
2. **Grocery khareedein:** Aata, chawal, cheeni, ghee, daalein aur rozmarra ki zaroorat ki cheezein lein.
3. **Card se payment karein:** Dukaandar card se Rs 3,000 tak ki payment kaat lega. Is par cash nahi milta aur na hi ye raqam bank account me transfer hoti hai.
4. **Mahana raqam waqt par istemal karein:** Mahana Rs 3,000 usi mahine me istemal kar lena behtar hai — bacha hua balance agle mahine transfer hone ki koi tasdeeq-shuda guarantee nahi hai, is liye mahine ke aakhir se pehle grocery le lena aqalmandi hai.
5. **Card sambhal kar rakhein:** Card gum ho jaye ya kharab ho jaye to foran PSER portal ya e-Khidmat Markaz se rabta karein — naya card jaari karwane ka tareeqa wahi se maloom hoga.

## Status Check Kaise Karein

Apni registration ya selection ki status maloom karne ke liye sab se mehfooz tareeqe ye hain:

- **PSER portal par check karein:** pser.punjab.gov.pk par apne account se login kar ke apne record ki status dekhein.
- **e-Khidmat Markaz ya Union Council jayein:** Apna CNIC le kar jayein, staff aap ko bata dega ke aap ka survey mukammal hai ya nahi aur aap select hue hain ya nahi.
- **Helpline par rabta karein:** PSER ki helpline **0800-02345** (toll-free) par call kar ke maloomat le sakte hain — call karne se pehle number PSER portal par tasdeeq kar lein.

**8123 SMS ke baare me ehtiyat:** Kuch guides kehti hain ke apna CNIC number 8123 par SMS kar ke ehliyaat check ki ja sakti hai. Ye tareeqa official portal par tasdeeq-shuda nahi hai aur is bare me mukhtalif guides mukhtalif baatein kehti hain. Is liye 8123 par koi paisa ya maloomat bhejne se pehle PSER portal ya helpline se is ki tasdeeq zaroor kar lein — mehfooz rasta portal aur e-Khidmat Markaz hi hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

- **Jaali website par apply karna:** Sab se bari ghalti ye hai ke log Google par "Rashan Card apply" search kar ke pehli milne wali site par apna CNIC daal dete hain. Sirf **pser.punjab.gov.pk** official portal hai. Koi bhi doosri site, WhatsApp number ya agent official nahi hai.
- **Kisi agent ko paisay dena:** Registration **bilkul muft** hai. Jo shakhs Rashan Card banwane ke paisay maange, wo fraud kar raha hai — chahe wo khud ko kisi idare ka numainda hi kyun na bataye.
- **Ghalat aamdani likhna:** Kuch log sochte hain ke kam aamdani likhne se card jaldi milega. Ghalat maloomat verification me pakri jati hai aur application reject ho sakti hai — aur mustahiq logon ka haq bhi mara jata hai.
- **PSER survey ko nazar-andaz karna:** Bohat se log seedha "Rashan Card form" dhoondte rehte hain jab ke asal kaam PSER survey hai. Jab tak aap ka gharana PSER me darj nahi, card ka sawal hi paida nahi hota.
- **Aik se zyada applications dena:** Aik family se aik se zyada apply karne se selection tez nahi hoti — is se record me garbar hoti hai aur verification atak sakti hai.
- **BISP aur Rashan Card dono ki umeed rakhna:** Jo family BISP se faida utha rahi hai, wo Rashan Card ke liye eligible nahi hai. Dono me se aik ka intekhab karna hota hai. BISP ke taleemi wazaif ke bare me jan'ne ke liye hamari [Benazir Taleemi Wazaif guide](/guides/benazir-taleemi-wazaif-registration-check) dekhein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab Rashan Card par har mahine kitni raqam milti hai?**

Rashan Card par har mahine **Rs 3,000** ki grocery madad milti hai. Ye raqam naqad nahi milti — sirf registered utility stores aur karyana shops par khane-peene ki cheezein khareedne ke liye istemal hoti hai. Ramzan jaisay khaas mauqon par izafi package ka elan alag se hota hai.

**Rashan Card ke liye apply kahan karna hai?**

Seedha Rashan Card ka koi alag form nahi hai — apply **PSER portal pser.punjab.gov.pk** par registration ke zariye hota hai, ya apne qareebi Union Council office ya e-Khidmat Markaz par ja kar. Registration muft hai aur is ke baad selection marhala-waar hoti hai.

**Kya Rashan Card ke liye koi fee hai?**

Nahi, registration **bilkul muft** hai. Agar koi shakhs card banwane ke paisay maange to wo fraud hai — foran us ki shikayat karein aur apni maloomat usay na dein.

**Rashan Card se cash nikal sakte hain?**

Nahi. Rashan Card se naqad raqam nahi nikalti aur na hi ye raqam bank account me transfer hoti hai. Ye sirf grocery khareedne ke liye hai aur sirf registered dukaanon par chalta hai.

**Agar meri family BISP se madad le rahi hai to kya Rashan Card mil sakta hai?**

Nahi. Jo gharanay pehle se BISP se faida utha rahe hain, wo Rashan Card ke liye eligible nahi hain. Aap ko dono me se aik programme ka faida mil sakta hai. Mazeed sahulat cards ke bare me jan'ne ke liye hamari [Sehat Sahulat Card guide](/guides/sehat-sahulat-card-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi dekhein.
