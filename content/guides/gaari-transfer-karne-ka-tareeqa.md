## Gaari Transfer Kyun Zaroori Hai — "Khuli Transfer" Ka Khatra

Pakistan me bohat se log gaari khareed kar **"transfer letter par"** chala lete hain aur asal transfer nahi karwate. Ye bohat khatarnak aadat hai:

- Gaari qanooni tor par **purane malik ke naam** rehti hai — accident ya jurm ho to pehla shak aap par nahi, kagzaat par jayega — aur aap ke paas malkiat ka **koi saboot nahi**.
- Purana malik kal ko **intahqal** kar jaye to warison se transfer aik qanooni larai ban jata hai.
- Aap gaari **aagay nahi bech sakte** jab tak aap ke naam na ho.

**Qanoon seedha hai:** Gaari khareedi hai to **foran apne naam transfer karwayen** — is guide me Punjab ka process, fees aur kagzaat mukammal diye gaye hain.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fees Punjab Excise & Taxation ke published schedule se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. Fees aur tax rates har saal budget me tabdeel ho sakte hain — Excise office ya excise.punjab.gov.pk par maujooda rates zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Is Ke Ilawa: Withholding Tax (Filer vs Non-Filer)

Transfer fee ke ilawa gaari ki **qeemat par withholding tax (WHT)** lagta hai — aur yahan **filer hona lakhon bacha sakta hai**:

- Filer ke liye rate kam, non-filer ke liye **teen guna tak zyada** hota hai (engine capacity ke slabs ke hisab se).
- WHT gaari ki **umar ke saath har saal 10% kam** hota jata hai — purani gaari par tax bhi kam lagta hai.
- **Masalan:** 1300cc ki nayi gaari ki registration par filer ka rate 1.5% aur non-filer ka 4.5% lag sakta hai (rates budget ke hisab se badalte hain — asal raqam Excise office ka system gin kar deta hai).

**Mashwara:** Transfer se pehle [FBR filer](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) ban jayen — WHT me bachat akeli transfer fee se kahin zyada hogi.

## CVT (Capital Value Tax)

1300cc se zyada ki gaariyon par transfer/registration ke mauqe par **1% Capital Value Tax** bhi lag sakta hai — lekin sirf **pehle 5 saal** ke andar (registration wale saal se hisab hota hai, har saal value 10% kam hoti hai). 5 saal purani ho jaye to CVT zero ho jata hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

**Kharidar (aap) ki taraf se:**

- Asal **CNIC** aur copy
- Transfer ke din **biometric verification** (Excise office me hoti hai)

**Bechne wale ki taraf se:**

- Asal **registration book / smart card**
- Asal **CNIC** aur copy
- **Transfer letter / TO form** par dastakhat (Excise office se milta hai)
- Bechne wale ki bhi **biometric verification** hoti hai — wo bhi hazir hona parega (kuch soorat me authority letter chalta hai, office se confirm karein)
- **Token tax** ki tamam adaigiyan clear hon — baqaya ho to pehle ada karna hoga

**Bank lease wali gaari:** Pehle bank se **NOC aur lease clearance** — baghair is ke transfer mumkin nahi.

## Step-by-Step Process

1. **Sauda tay karein aur kagzaat check karein:** [MTMIS par gaari verify](/guides/gaari-verification-mtmis) karein, engine/chassis number mila lein, token tax status dekh lein.
2. **Apne zila ka Excise & Taxation office jayen:** Kharidar aur bechne wala **dono** jayen — asal kagzaat saath hon.
3. **Transfer ki file banwayen:** Counter se transfer form (TO Form) lein, bharein aur kagzaat attach karein.
4. **Biometric verification:** Dono taraf ki biometric tasdeeq hogi — ye **fraud rokne ke liye lazmi** hai.
5. **Fee aur tax ki chalan:** System kul raqam gin kar dega (transfer fee + WHT + CVT agar laga) — **bank/ePay** se ada karein.
6. **File jama karein aur raseed lein:** Aap ko token/raseed milegi.
7. **Nayi registration book / smart card:** Mukarrar waqt (aam tor par kuch hafton) me gaari **aap ke naam** ho jayegi — smart card courier se aata hai, record MTMIS par foran update ho jata hai.
8. **Tasdeeq karein:** MTMIS par apna naam check karke confirm kar lein transfer mukammal hai.

## Transfer Me Kitna Kul Kharcha Aata Hai (Misal)

Aik aam misal — **1300cc car, 3 saal purani, filer kharidar**:

- Transfer fee: **Rs 2,000**
- WHT: qeemat aur slab ke hisab se (umar ki wajah se kam hua rate)
- CVT: agar 1300cc se zyada aur 5 saal ke andar ho to 1% tak
- **Kul:** zyada tar aam gaariyon par chand hazar se le kar qeemat ke hisab tak — asal gin-ti Excise system deta hai, is liye pehle office se **estimate** le lena behtareen hai.

## Doosre Soobe Ki Gaari Transfer Karna

- Doosre soobe me registered gaari ke liye pehle us soobe se **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** lena parta hai.
- Phir apne soobe me nayi registration aur transfer — process lamba hai, **2-4 hafte** lag sakte hain.
- Mashwara: Mumkin ho to **usi soobe me transfer** karwa kar gaari layen, ya pehle dono taraf ke Excise offices se likhit process confirm kar lein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **"Baad me transfer karwa loon ga":** Yahi sub se mehngi ghalti hai — sauda wale din hi process shuru karein.
2. **Bechne wale ke baghair jana:** Biometric ke liye bechne wala lazmi hai — pehle din tay kar lein.
3. **Token tax baqaya:** Bechne wale se pehle clear karwayen, warna aap ke hisse ayega.
4. **Engine number na milana:** Transfer ke baad pata chale to qanooni masla aap ka hoga.
5. **Nayi number plates:** Transfer ke baad apne naam ki registration ke mutabiq number plates/meter record update karna na bhoolein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya bechne wala mar gaya ho to transfer ho sakta hai?**
Ho sakta hai lekin process qanooni hai — death certificate, warisana (succession) kagzaat aur warison ki razamandi lagegi. Wakil se mashwara karein.

**Transfer me kitne din lagte hain?**
File jama karne ka kaam aik din ka hai — smart card/book milne me aam tor par 2-4 hafte lagte hain. MTMIS record jaldi update ho jata hai.

**Kya main khud transfer kar sakta hun ya agent lazmi hai?**
Khud kar sakte hain — agent lazmi nahi. Khud karne se hazaron ki bachat hoti hai.

**Bike ki transfer fee kitni hai?**
Sirf **Rs 150** — is ke ilawa WHT bike par aam tor par nahi lagta ya mamooli hota hai (system final raqam batayega).

**Khareed kar saal ho gaya, transfer nahi kiya — ab kya hoga?**
Ab bhi transfer ho jata hai lekin late fee/token baqaya aap ko dena parega. Mazeed der na karein.

## Transfer Se Mutalliq Kharchon Ka Checklist (Jeb Ki List)

Office jane se pehle apna budget bana lein — ye tamam cheezen jama hoti hain:

1. **Transfer fee** (uper wali table) — Rs 150 se Rs 4,000
2. **Withholding tax** — gaari ki qeemat aur engine capacity par (filer/non-filer ka farq)
3. **CVT** — sirf 1300cc+ aur pehle 5 saal ke andar, 1%
4. **Token tax ka baqaya** — agar purana malik ne nahi diya to aap ko dena hoga
5. **Affidavit / file charges** — chhote-mote kagzi kharche

**Tip:** Excise office me file jama karne se pehle **counter se kul estimate** likhit me le lein — taake bank challan aik hi dafa me poora jama ho, dobara office na ana pare.

## Transfer Ke Baad Kya Samhal Kar Rakhein

Transfer mukammal hone ke baad ye jagah bana lein:

- **Transfer ki raseed** — jab tak nayi book/smart card na aye, ye aap ki malkiat ka saboot hai.
- **Purani registration book ki copy** — baad me comparison ke liye.
- **Sale deed / agreement** ki copy — kharidar aur bechne wale dono ke paas honi chahiye.

Aur foran ye bhi karein: **insurance** apne naam par karwayen (agar hai), aur **e-challan** ka record MTMIS par apne naam ke saath check kar lein.

## Ahem: Transfer Ke Baghair Gaari Bechna Ya Mout Ka Case

**Agar bechne wala faut ho gaya ho:** Warisana (succession) ke baad hi transfer hota hai — death certificate, warison ki razamandi aur court se tasdeeq lazmi. Ye lamba qanooni process hai; is soorat me **wakil lazmi** kar lein, khud Excise office ke chakkar kaafi nahi honge.

**Agar aap gaari dobara bechna chahte hain:** Bechne se pehle confirm karein ke registration book **aap ke naam par** ho — "double transfer" (aap khud transfer karwaye baghair aagay bech dena) qanoonan theek nahi aur naye khareeddar ke liye musiibat ban sakta hai.

## Akhri FAQs

**Transfer ke baad number plate badlegi?**
Aam tor par **number wahi rehta hai** — sirf malkiat ka record aap ke naam hota hai. Number change karwana alag process hai.

**Kya Friday ko Excise office khula hota hai?**
Punjab ke Excise offices **Monday se Saturday** aam tor par khule hote hain (Friday ko break ke saath) — jane se pehle phone par timing confirm kar lein.

## Transfer Wali Din: Waqt Ki BACHAT Ke Tips

1. **Subah jaldi jayen** — 9-10 baje ke aas paas bheer kam hoti hai.
2. **Photocopies pehle se** karwa lein — office ke bahir photocopy wala mehenga hota hai.
3. **Biometric ke liye angutha saaf** rakhein — mehnat kash logon ke fingerprints kabhi read nahi hote, aisi soorat me doosri ungli ya hath saaf karke dobara try hota hai, ghabrayen nahi; staff madad karta hai.
