## NICOP Kya Hai Aur Ye Kis Ko Chahiye

NICOP ka poora naam **National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis** hai. Ye wo smart identity card hai jo NADRA un Pakistani shehrion ko jari karta hai jo Pakistan se bahir muqeem hain ya jin ke paas kisi doosre mulk ki nationality bhi hai. Aam CNIC Pakistan ke andar rehne walon ke liye hota hai, jab ke NICOP bahir rehne walon ki pehchan, safar aur sarkari kaamon ke liye bana hai.

NICOP ki sab se bari sahulat ye hai ke is ke zariye **dual national Pakistan me visa ke baghair dakhil ho sakta hai**. Is ke ilawa ye card Pakistan me bank account khulwane, jaidad ki khareed-o-farokht, passport banwane aur doosre sarkari kaamon me pehchan ke tor par kaam aata hai. Jo log bahir mulk me paida hue hain ya bachpan me hi family ke sath bahir chale gaye thay, un ke liye bhi NICOP hi woh dastavez hai jo un ka Pakistani shehri hona sabit karta hai.

Ye card kin logon ke liye hai, ye samajhna sab se pehle zaroori hai, kyun ke ghalat card ki application waqt aur fee dono zaya kar deti hai:

- **Overseas Pakistani:** Jo Pakistani shehri rozgar, parhai ya mustaqil rehaish ke liye bahir mulk me reh rahe hain.
- **Dual nationals:** Jo log kisi aise mulk ki nationality bhi rakhte hain jis ke sath Pakistan ka dual nationality ka agreement hai — masalan UK, USA ya Canada.
- **Bahir paida hone wale bache:** Jin ke walidain Pakistani hain aur bacha bahir mulk me paida hua hai, wo bhi NICOP ke ahal hain.
- **Wapas aane wale:** Jo log bahir se Pakistan wapas aa gaye hain, wo bhi apna NICOP renew ya modify karwa sakte hain.

Aik aham farq zehen me rakhein: agar kisi shakhs ne Pakistani nationality **tark (surrender)** kar di hai aur wo kisi aise mulk ka shehri hai jis ke sath dual nationality ka agreement nahi hai, to us ke liye NICOP nahi, **POC (Pakistan Origin Card)** hota hai. POC ki fee aur sharaet NICOP se mukhtalif hain, is liye apply karne se pehle ye tay kar lein ke aap kis category me aate hain. Agar aap ko shak hai ke aap ka purana card CNIC tha ya NICOP, to purane card par likhi category ya Pak ID portal par apna record check kar lein — andaze par application mat bharein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Category Ke Mutabiq Checklist

NICOP ki application me sab se zyada waqt kagzaat ki tayyari par lagta hai. Har category ke kagzaat thore mukhtalif hain, is liye apni category ki list par tik (tick) laga kar hi apply karein. Tamam kagzaat ki **saaf scan copies** tayyar rakhein — dhundli tasveer ya kata hua scan application rokne ki aam wajah hai.

**Naya NICOP (pehli dafa, adult):**

- Valid Pakistani passport (ya foreign passport, agar dual national hain)
- Purana CNIC ya B-Form, agar kabhi bana ho
- Bahir mulk ki rehaish ka saboot: residence permit, iqama, work permit, PR card ya utility bill
- Walid ka CNIC/NICOP number (family tree ki tasdeeq ke liye lazmi)
- Walida ka CNIC/NICOP number, agar dastiyab ho
- ICAO standard ke mutabiq nai tasveer (portal/app ki rehnumai ke mutabiq li jaye)

**Renewal (purana NICOP expire ya expire hone wala):**

- Purana NICOP (chahe expire ho chuka ho) — us ki front aur back ki copy
- Valid Pakistani ya foreign passport
- Maujooda bahir mulk ke address ka saboot
- Agar naam, walid ka naam ya address tabdeel karna hai to mutaliqa saboot (masalan [marriage certificate](/guides/nadra-marriage-certificate) agar shadi ke baad naam tabdeel ho raha hai)

**Modification ya duplicate (gumshuda/kharab card):**

- Modification ke liye tabdeeli ka saboot (nikah-nama, talaq-nama, court order waghera)
- Gumshuda card ke liye purane card ki koi copy, agar maujood ho — kuch missions gumshuda card par police report bhi maang leti hain, is liye apni mission se pehle confirm kar lein
- Valid passport aur rehaish ka saboot (upar wali list ke mutabiq)

**Bahir paida hone wale bache ka pehla NICOP:**

- Bache ka foreign birth certificate (tafsili wala, jis me walidain ke naam hon)
- Pakistani embassy/consulate se jari **S1 form**, agar darkar ho
- Walidain dono ke CNIC/NICOP ya passport
- Bache ka [B-Form](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) ya CRC, agar pehle se bana ho
- Bache ki tasveer aur walidain ki maujoodgi (minor ki application me walidain ka hissa lazmi hota hai)

**Witnesses (gawah) — ye step aksar log bhool jate hain:**

Bahir mulk se apply karne par aap ko **do witnesses** darkar hote hain jo khud valid CNIC ya NICOP rakhte hon. Aham shart ye hai ke witnesses aap ke **walidain, bache, shareek-e-hayat ya behan-bhai nahi** ho sakte — matlab immediate family ke afrad gawah nahi ban sakte. Witnesses ke CNIC/NICOP ki copies aur rabta numbers darkar hote hain. Pakistan ke andar se apply karne par is ki jagah **attester form** hota hai jo majaz officer se tasdeeq shuda hona chahiye. Gawah ka intikhab pehle se kar lein, warna application adhoori reh jayegi.

> Mashwara: apne tamam kagzaat par naam, walid ka naam aur date of birth **aik jaisi spelling** me rakhein. Aksar applications sirf is liye ruk jati hain ke passport par naam kuch aur hai aur purane CNIC par kuch aur. Farq ho to pehle [CNIC banane ya modification](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) wali guide ke mutabiq record durust karwayen, phir NICOP ke liye apply karein.

## NICOP Fees 2026: Zone A, Zone B Aur Pakistan Me

NICOP ki fee do cheezon par munhasir hai: aap **kis zone ke mulk** me reh rahe hain aur aap ko card **kitni jaldi** chahiye. NADRA ne duniya ko do zones me taqseem kiya hua hai. Zone A me aam tor par USA, UK, Canada aur zyada tar European mumalik aate hain, jab ke Zone B me Gulf mumalik (UAE, Saudi Arabia), Africa aur zyada tar Asian mumalik shamil hain. Portal par mulk muntakhib karte hi aap ka zone aur hatmi fee khud zahir ho jati hai, is liye andaze par fee mat bhejein.

| Processing category | Zone A (USA, UK, Canada, Europe) | Zone B (Gulf, Africa, Asia) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal (31 din) | $39 | $20 |
| Urgent (23 din) | $57 | $30 |
| Executive (9 din) | $75 | $40 |

**Pakistan ke andar se apply karne par** fee Pakistani rupees me hoti hai: Normal **Rs. 5,563**, Urgent **Rs. 8,346** aur Executive **Rs. 11,128**. Ye option un logon ke liye mufeed hai jo chhutti par Pakistan aaye hue hain aur wahin se NICOP banwana ya renew karwana chahte hain.

Kuch embassy/consulate counters maqami currency me bhi fee lete hain — masalan Saudi Arabia me Riyal aur UAE me Dirham me adaigi hoti hai — aur kuch missions NADRA fee ke ilawa **alag counter processing fee** bhi leti hain. Is liye appointment se pehle apni mission ki website ya phone se kul rakam confirm kar lein. Card ki delivery courier se hoti hai, is liye delivery charges ya mission ke mutabiq izafi kharcha bhi ho sakta hai.

> Aham note: fees waqtan-fo-waqtan revise hoti hain. **Hatmi rakam wohi hogi jo Pak ID portal ya aap ke PSID/challan par apply ke waqt likhi ho.** Is guide ki fees NADRA fee structure ke mutabiq hain, lekin adaigi se pehle portal par fee zaroor dekh lein.

## Online Tareeqa: Pak ID Portal/App Se Step-by-Step Apply

Aaj kal NICOP ka sab se aasan tareeqa online hai. NADRA ne wazeh kiya hai ke 18 saal ya us se zyada umar ke afrad apna pehla NICOP bhi **Pak ID mobile app** ya **Pak Identity portal (id.nadra.gov.pk)** se apply kar sakte hain, baghair pehle kisi center jane ke. Poora process is tarteeb se karein:

1. **Account banayen:** Pak ID app download karein ya id.nadra.gov.pk par jayen aur apne email address aur mobile number se account banayen. Account ki tasdeeq ke liye OTP aayega.
2. **Category chunein:** "New NICOP" (pehli dafa), "Renewal" (expiry par), "Modification" (maloomat ki tabdeeli) ya "Duplicate" (gumshuda/kharab card) me se apni category muntakhib karein.
3. **Form bharen:** Zaati maloomat, walidain ki maloomat, maujooda address aur bahir mulk ki rehaish ki tafseel darj karein. Naam aur date of birth apne passport ke ain mutabiq likhein.
4. **Tasveer lein:** App/portal ki rehnumai ke mutabiq tasveer capture karein. Tasveer ICAO standard ki honi chahiye — seedha chehra, saaf roshni, safed background aur koi topi ya chashma nahi (mazhabi wajah ke ilawa).
5. **Fingerprints dein:** Aksar cases me app ke zariye camera-based fingerprint scan ho jata hai. Agar online scan na ho sake to fingerprint form download kar ke bharna aur attester se tasdeeq karwana parti hai — app aap ko batayegi ke aap ke case me kya darkar hai.
6. **Witnesses ya attester darj karein:** Bahir mulk se apply karne par do witnesses ki tafseel aur un ki CNIC/NICOP copies dein (immediate family nahi). Pakistan se apply karne par attester form upload karein.
7. **Kagzaat upload karein:** Passport, purana card, rehaish ka saboot aur category ke mutabiq deegar kagzaat ki saaf scans upload karein. Har file ka size aur format portal ki had ke andar rakhein.
8. **Form ka jaiza lein aur declaration par dastakhat karein:** Submit karne se pehle poora form aik dafa parh lein. Ghalti submit ke baad durust karna mushkil aur waqt-talab hota hai.
9. **Fee ada karein:** Debit ya credit card se online fee ada karein. Kuch categories me fee shuru me aur kuch me aakhir me li jati hai — portal ki hidayat par amal karein. Payment ki raseed sambhal kar rakhein.
10. **Tracking ID sambhalen:** Submit hone par aap ko tracking ID milegi. Is se aap Pak ID portal/app par apni application ka status dekh sakte hain — submitted, processing, printed, dispatched aur delivered ke marahil nazar aate hain.

Agar online biometric verification mukammal na ho sake to pareshan na hon — NADRA ke mutabiq aise cases me attester se tasdeeq, ahal family member ke zariye verification ya qareebi embassy/consulate me mukhtasar biometric appointment ka mutabadil tareeqa hota hai. App ya portal ki hidayat par amal karein, kisi agent ko apna account ya password mat dein.

## Embassy/Consulate Ya Pakistan Me Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

Agar aap online apply nahi karna chahte, ya aap ke case me hazri lazmi hai, to doosre raste ye hain:

**Pakistani embassy ya consulate se:**

1. Apni mission ki website se **appointment** book karein — baghair appointment ke aksar missions dakhla nahi deti hain.
2. Tamam asal kagzaat (passport, purana card, rehaish ka saboot) aur un ki photocopies sath le jayen.
3. Mission par tasveer, fingerprints aur dastakhat liye jayenge.
4. Fee mission ke mutabiq ada karein (card se ya maqami currency me, mission ki hidayat ke mutabiq) aur raseed lein.
5. Card banne ke baad aap ke diye hue bahir mulk ke address par courier se bhej diya jayega.

**Pakistan me NADRA center se:**

Pakistan me maujoodgi ke doran kisi bhi NADRA Registration Centre (NRC) se NICOP ke liye apply ho sakta hai. Tareeqa wohi hai jo [CNIC banane](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ka hai: token lein, tasveer aur biometrics dein, form ki tasdeeq karein aur fee ada karein. Farq sirf ye hai ke fee upar wali table ke mutabiq rupees me hogi aur category NICOP muntakhib karni hai. Jo log mustaqil tor par bahir reh rahe hain, un ke liye online ya mission ka rasta zyada aasan hai, kyun ke Pakistan aana sirf card ke liye mehnga par sakta hai.

## Card Kab Tak Milega Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Processing ka waqt us category par munhasir hai jo aap ne chuni hai: **Normal taqreeban 31 din, Urgent taqreeban 23 din aur Executive taqreeban 9 din**. Ye muddat aam tor par biometric verification mukammal hone ke baad shuru hoti hai. Is ke baad courier delivery ka waqt alag se lagta hai — bahir mulk ke address par delivery me kuch din izafi lag sakte hain, khaas tor par door-daraz ilaqon me.

Status check karne ke tareeqe:

- **Pak ID portal/app:** Apne account me "Existing Applications" kholein aur apni tracking ID par click karein — tafseeli status nazar aayega.
- **NADRA helpline:** Tracking ID ke sath **051-111-786-100** par rabta karein (mobile se **1777** bhi istemal ho sakta hai).
- **Mission se rabta:** Agar aap ne embassy/consulate se apply kiya tha to mission bhi status ki maloomat de sakti hai.

Agar status lambi muddat tak aik hi marhale par ruka rahe to pehle ye check karein ke NADRA ne aap se koi mazeed kagaz ya tasdeeq to nahi maangi — aisi darkhwast aam tor par portal/app par ya email/SMS ke zariye aati hai. Jawab na dene par application "on hold" ho sakti hai. Phir bhi masla hal na ho to helpline par tracking ID ke sath rabta karein, dobara application mat bhar dein — duplicate application se mamla mazeed ulajh sakta hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Application Ruk Jati Hai

- **Naam ki spelling ka farq:** Passport, purane card aur family record me naam mukhtalif ho to verification ruk jati hai. Pehle record aik jaisa karwayen.
- **Ghalat tasveer:** Mobile se andheri jagah li hui tasveer, selfie angle ya rangdar background wali tasveer reject ho jati hai. App ki rehnumai ke mutabiq safed background aur seedhi tasveer dein.
- **Immediate family ko witness banana:** Walidain, behan-bhai ya shareek-e-hayat gawah nahi ban sakte. Kisi dost ya rishtedar ko witness banayen jo khud valid CNIC/NICOP rakhta ho.
- **Adhoora attester/fingerprint form:** Form par dastakhat ya mohar na ho to application wapas ho jati hai. Upload karne se pehle har field check karein.
- **Purana address likhna:** Delivery usi address par hogi jo form me darj hai. Agar aap makan tabdeel kar chuke hain to naya address aur us ka saboot dein.
- **Ghalat zone ki fee:** Kisi doosre mulk ki fee dekh kar adaigi mat karein — portal par apne mulk ke mutabiq zahir hone wali rakam hi hatmi hai.
- **Agenton ke hathon account dena:** Koi agent aap ka NICOP "jaldi" banwane ka wada kare to ehtiyat karein. NADRA ki koi fast-track agent service nahi hai — sirf official categories (Normal/Urgent/Executive) hain.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**NICOP aur CNIC me kya farq hai?**
CNIC Pakistan ke andar rehne wale shehrion ka card hai, jab ke NICOP bahir mulk me muqeem Pakistani shehrion aur dual nationals ke liye hai. Dono NADRA ke smart card hain aur dono se pehchan sabit hoti hai, lekin NICOP dual nationals ko Pakistan me visa-free dakhle ki sahulat bhi deta hai. Agar aap mustaqil tor par bahir reh rahe hain to aap ki category NICOP hai, CNIC nahi.

**Kya NICOP se Pakistan visa ke baghair ja sakte hain?**
Ji haan — dual national ke liye NICOP hi woh dastavez hai jis se wo Pakistan me visa ke baghair dakhil ho sakta hai. Lekin is ke liye card valid hona chahiye aur safar ke waqt sath hona chahiye. Expire shuda card par ye sahulat nahi milti, is liye expiry se pehle renewal karwa lein.

**Bahir mulk me paida hone wale bache ka NICOP kaise banta hai?**
Bache ke liye walidain ko foreign birth certificate, apne CNIC/NICOP ya passport aur zaroorat parne par embassy se jari S1 form ke sath Pak ID app ya mission se apply karna hota hai. Minor ki application me walidain ka hissa lazmi hai. Bache ka [B-Form](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) pehle se bana ho to wo bhi madadgar hota hai.

**Agar meri application reject ya on hold ho jaye to kya karun?**
Pehle Pak ID portal par rejection ya hold ki wajah parhein — aam tor par koi kagaz adhoora hota hai ya naam ka farq hota hai. Wajah durust kar ke dobara darkar kagaz jama karwayen ya helpline (051-111-786-100) par tracking ID ke sath rabta karein. Baghair wajah jane dobara application bharne se mamla ulajhta hai, is liye pehle wajah maloom karein.

**Kya Pakistan me rehte hue bhi NICOP ban sakta hai?**
Ji haan — Pakistan me maujoodgi ke doran kisi NADRA center se NICOP ke liye apply ho sakta hai aur fee rupees me hoti hai (Normal Rs. 5,563, Urgent Rs. 8,346, Executive Rs. 11,128). Ye un logon ke liye mufeed hai jo chhutti par Pakistan aaye hon. Lekin agar aap mustaqil tor par bahir reh rahe hain to online ya mission ka rasta zyada munasib hai.

Mazeed tasdeeq ke liye NADRA helpline 051-111-786-100 (mobile se 1777) ya qareebi Pakistani embassy/consulate se rabta karein.
