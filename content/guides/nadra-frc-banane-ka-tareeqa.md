## FRC Kya Hai Aur Kis Kaam Aata Hai

**Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** NADRA ki taraf se jari hone wala aik official document hai jo ye sabit karta hai ke **falaan shaks falaan khandan ka hissa hai** — matlab aap ke walidain, behan-bhai, biwi aur bachay kaun hain, ye sab aik hi certificate par darj hota hai. CNIC sirf aap ki apni pehchan hota hai, jabke FRC poore khandan ka record hota hai.

FRC ki sab se zyada zaroorat **visa aur immigration** ke waqton par parti hai. Jab aik poora khandan ya us ka koi hissa bahir mulk safar karta hai, to embassy ye dekhna chahti hai ke in sab ka aapas me kya rishta hai — FRC yahi rishta sabit karta hai. Is ke ilawa **passport (bachon ke liye), bank account, jaidad ke muamlat, school admission aur overseas Pakistani community ke kaam** me bhi FRC manga jata hai.

**FRC ki teen qisam hoti hain** — apply karne se pehle ye samajhna lazmi hai:

| Qisam | Kis ke liye | Kya darj hota hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **FRC by Birth** | Walidain-behan bhaiyon ka record (alpha family) | Walidain aur behan-bhai |
| **FRC by Marriage** | Shadi-shuda joray (beta family) | Biwi/shohar aur bachay |
| **FRC by Adoption** | God liye hue (adopted) bachay | Qanooni sarparast (guardian) aur bachay |
| **FRC by All** | Dono khandan aik saath | Alpha + beta family (tamam afraad) |

Aksar log FRC aur **B-Form (CRC)** ko aik hi cheez samajhte hain — **nahi, dono alag hain**: B-Form 18 saal se kam umar ke bache ki apni paidaish ka record hai, jabke FRC poore khandan ke rishton ka record hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

NADRA ki official FRC requirements bohat seedhi hain — **sirf aik cheez lazmi** hai:

- **Applicant ka 13-digit ID number** — CNIC, NICOP, POC ya bache ka CRC (B-Form) / Juvenile Card

Aur **eligibility ki shart** ye hai ke **khandan ke tamam afraad NADRA me registered hon aur un ka valid 13-digit ID number ho**. Kuch khaas suraton me ghair-registered (wafat pa jane wale ya zinda) afraad ko bhi applicant ki di hui maloomat par shamil kiya ja sakta hai.

**Amali tor par ye maloomat saath rakhein** taake form bharte waqt rukna na pare:

- Khandan ke tamam afraad ke **CNIC/NICOP numbers** (walidain, behan-bhai, biwi ya bachon ke)
- 18 saal se kam umar ke bachon ka **B-Form (CRC) number** — har fard ka 13-digit number lazmi hai
- **Mobile number** jo NADRA ke record me darj ho (verification ke liye)
- **Email address** — digital delivery ke liye
- **Adoption ke liye:** adalat (court) ka adoption decree aur guardian certificate

**Sab se ahem baat:** Agar khandan ka koi fard NADRA ke record me **registered hi nahi** to FRC me us ka naam nahi ayega. Pehle us ka CNIC/B-Form banwayen ya record update karwayen — yehi FRC me sab se aam rukawat hai.

## Fee Kitni Hai (Official — 2026)

FRC ki fee NADRA ki official **Fee Structure** me darj hai — aur wahan **sirf Executive column bhara hua hai** (Normal aur Urgent FRC ke liye list hi nahi):

| Category | Official timeline | Fee (PKR) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FRC (aik qisam: by Birth / by Marriage / by Adoption) | **01 din** | **Rs 1,000** |
| FRC (Both — tamam categories aik saath) | **01 din** | **Rs 2,000** |

**Seedhi baat:** Official table ke mutabiq FRC **1 din me** jari hota hai aur aik category ki fee **Rs 1,000** hai, jabke dono khandan (alpha + beta) aik saath **Rs 2,000** me. Overseas Pakistanis ke liye fee USD me hoti hai jo consulate ke hisab se taqreeban **$20–25** banti hai.

> **Note:** Ye fee NADRA ki official Fee Structure (page last updated 17-09-2026) se **20 October 2026** ko check ki gayi hai. NADRA fees revise karta rehta hai — apply karne se pehle NADRA ki website ya helpline **1777** (24/7) par fee confirm kar lein.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa (NADRA Center Se)

NADRA ki official FRC page par apply ke ye channels darj hain: **NADRA Registration Centre, Mega Centre, Mobile Registration Van, Home Delivery Service, e-Sahulat franchise, Overseas Counter** — aur digital ke liye **PakID Mobile App**. Qareebi center jane ka tareeqa:

1. **Center jayen** — Apna asal CNIC/NICOP aur khandan ke afraad ke 13-digit ID numbers likh kar le jayen.
2. **FRC ke liye token/form lein** — Batayen ke kis qisam ka FRC chahiye (by Birth, by Marriage, by Adoption ya by All).
3. **Family record ki tasdeeq** — Staff NADRA ke database me khandan ka record nikalega. Har fard ka naam aur ID number dekh kar **confirm karein** — agar koi naam missing hai ya spelling ghalat hai to pehle usay theek karwayen (neeche "Aam Ghaltiyan" me tafseel).
4. **Fee ada karein** — Rs 1,000 (ya Rs 2,000 by All ke liye) counter par ada karein aur **receipt lazmi lein**.
5. **Certificate wasool karein** — Official timeline **1 din** hai. Record durust hone ki soorat me FRC jald mil jata hai; uljhe hue case me tracking slip milti hai jis se status maloom hota hai.

**Kharcha bachat ka mashwara:** Agar sirf aik category ka FRC chahiye (masalan sirf by Marriage visa ke liye) to Rs 1,000 wala hi lein — dono categories aik saath lene par Rs 2,000 dena parta hai.

## Online Tareeqa (PakID Mobile App)

NADRA ki official FRC page par digital apply ka channel **PakID Mobile App** darj hai — overseas Pakistanis ke liye yehi sab se asan rasta hai:

1. **PakID Mobile App** apne phone par install karein (NADRA ka official app).
2. App me apne **CNIC/NICOP** se account banayen ya login karein.
3. Services me se **Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** select karein aur **New Application** shuru karein.
4. FRC ki **qisam chunein** — by Birth, by Marriage, by Adoption ya by All.
5. Apna **13-digit ID number** darj karein aur khandan ke afraad ki maloomat (un ke 13-digit ID numbers) add karein.
6. **Fee online ada karein** — Rs 1,000 (ya Rs 2,000 by All ke liye) app ke zariye.
7. Application submit hone ke baad **tracking ID** milti hai. Record durust hone par FRC digital soorat me mil jata hai — official timeline **1 din** hai.

**Online apply me 2 baton ka khayal rakhein:** (1) Tamam afraad ke **13-digit numbers bilkul durust** hon — ghalat number par application atak jati hai. (2) Jo mobile number dein wo **NADRA ke record me darj** ho, kyunke verification usi par hoti hai.

Center jana mushkil ho to **Home Delivery Service** aur **e-Sahulat franchise** bhi official channels hain — apne shehar ke qareebi point ka pata NADRA helpline **1777** se lein.

## Waqt Kitna Lagta Hai

NADRA ki official Fee Structure me FRC ka timeline **01 din** darj hai:

- **Record mukammal aur durust ho:** FRC **1 din me** jari ho jata hai — chahe center se apply karein ya PakID app se.
- **Record me kami ho** (koi fard registered nahi, spelling ghalat, shadi darj nahi): Pehle record theek karwana parta hai — is me chand din lag sakte hain, us ke baad FRC 1 din me jari hota hai.
- **Adoption ya uljhe hue cases:** Court documents ki tasdeeq me mazeed waqt lag sakta hai.

**Seedhi baat:** Jin logon ka NADRA record pehle se theek hai un ke liye FRC aik **1-din ka kaam** hai. Der sirf tab hoti hai jab record me ghaltiyan hon — is liye apply karne se pehle apna family record check karwa lein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan (In Se Bachein)

1. **Khandan ka koi fard NADRA record me registered nahi** — Official eligibility ke mutabiq tamam afraad ka valid 13-digit ID hona lazmi hai. Bina is ke naam FRC me nahi ayega — pehle us ka CNIC/B-Form banwayen.
2. **Biwi ka naam record me nahi** — Shadi ke baad record update karwayen; bina is ke FRC by Marriage me biwi ka naam nahi ayega.
3. **Naamon ki spelling alag-alag** — CNIC par "Muhammad", B-Form par "Mohammad" to FRC me masla ayega. Pehle sab documents par spelling aik jaisi karwayen.
4. **Bachon ke B-Form ka number nahi** — Har fard ka 13-digit number lazmi hai, chahay bacha ho. Bachon ke B-Form numbers likh kar le jayen.
5. **Ghalat qisam select karna** — Visa ke liye aksar **by Marriage** chahiye hota hai, log by Birth le aate hain. Embassy ki requirement pehle confirm kar lein.
6. **Purana mobile number** — NADRA ke record me purana number ho to verification me masla hota hai. Pehle number update karwayen.
7. **Fee ki receipt na lena** — Counter par fee de kar receipt lazmi lein, yehi aap ka saboot hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**FRC aur B-Form me kya farq hai?**
B-Form (CRC) 18 saal se kam umar ke bache ki apni paidaish aur pehchan ka record hai, jabke FRC poore khandan ke rishton (walidain, behan-bhai, biwi-bachay) ka certificate hai. Visa aur immigration ke liye aksar FRC manga jata hai, B-Form nahi.

**Kya FRC ki koi expiry hoti hai?**
Nahi, FRC ki koi fixed expiry date nahi hoti. Lekin agar khandan ki maloomat badal jaye (paidaish, shadi, talaaq, wafat) to **naya FRC** banwana chahiye, kyunke purana certificate ghalat maloomat dikhayega.

**Kya overseas Pakistani FRC ke liye apply kar sakta hai?**
Ji haan — Pak Identity portal (id.nadra.gov.pk) se NICOP par online apply karein. Fee USD me hoti hai (taqreeban $20–25) aur certificate PDF me email par mil jata hai.

**FRC by Marriage ke liye kya Nikah Nama lazmi hai?**
NADRA ki official requirements me sirf 13-digit ID number manga gaya hai — lekin shart ye hai ke shadi NADRA ke record me darj ho. Agar shadi record me nahi hai to pehle record update karwayen, phir FRC apply karein.

**Kya FRC visa ke liye qabool hota hai?**
Ji haan, FRC duniya bhar ki aksar embassies aur immigration authorities ke paas khandan ke rishte ka valid proof hai — aur NADRA ke certificate par online ya Pak-ID App se tasdeeq (verification) bhi hoti hai. Kuch mumalik ke liye is par **attestation ya apostille** bhi karwana parta hai — apni embassy ki requirement zaroor check kar lein.

Fee aur waqt waqton-waqton par badal sakta hai — apply karne se pehle NADRA helpline **1777** (24/7) ya csd@nadra.gov.pk se confirm kar lein.
