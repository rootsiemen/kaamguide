## Gumshuda CNIC Dobara Banwana Kya Hai

CNIC gum ho jana aam baat hai — jaib se nikal jata hai, purse chori ho jata hai, ya card itna kharab ho jata hai ke chip scan hona band ho jati hai. Aisi surat me ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi, kyun ke NADRA ke paas aap ka mukammal record pehle se maujood hota hai. Dobara card banane ki service ko NADRA "Reprint" kehti hai, aur is ka matlab ye hai ke aap ko wohi CNIC number, wohi naam aur wohi maloomat wala aik naya card milta hai — koi naya record nahi banta, koi naya number jari nahi hota.

Reprint ko renewal aur modification se alag samajhna zaroori hai. Renewal us waqt hoti hai jab card ki muddat khatam ho jaye, aur modification us waqt jab naam, address, azdawaji haisiyat ya tasveer me tabdeeli karni ho. Agar aap ka card sirf gum hua hai ya toot gaya hai aur tamam maloomat wohi hain jo pehle thin, to aap ko sirf Reprint chahiye — is me na kisi naye kagaz ki zaroorat hoti hai aur na hi lambi tasdeeq ki. Isi wajah se reprint, renewal ya modification se zyada seedha kaam hai.

Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jin ka CNIC gum, chori ya kharab ho gaya hai. Is me card gum hone par fori hifazati qadam, Pak ID app se online reprint ka step-by-step tareeqa, NADRA centre ka tareeqa, 2026 ki fees, delivery ka waqt, status check karne ka tareeqa aur woh ghaltiyan darj hain jin se application atak jati hai.

## Card Gum Hone Par Fori Kya Karein

Card milne ka intezar karne se pehle chand fori qadam utha lena behtar hai, kyun ke gumshuda card ka ghalat istemal bhi ho sakta hai. Sab se pehle apna 13 hindson wala CNIC number kisi mehfooz jagah par likh lein — agar purane card ki tasveer ya photocopy maujood hai to wohi kaafi hai, kyun ke reprint ki application me number hi sab se bara hawala hota hai. Agar number yaad nahi aur koi copy bhi nahi hai to pareshan na hon: NADRA centre par aap ka record naam, walid ka naam aur tareekh-e-paidaish se bhi dhoonda ja sakta hai.

Dosra qadam ye hai ke apne bank aur mobile company ko ittila dein agar aap ko lagta hai ke card kisi ghalat shakhs ke haath lag gaya hai. Bank account ya SIM ka kaam CNIC se hota hai, is liye waqt par ittila dena aap ko nuqsan se bacha sakta hai. Teesra, agar card chori hua hai to qareebi thane me report darj karwana aik hifazati qadam hai — ye NADRA reprint ke liye lazmi shart nahi hai, lekin chori ki surat me report ka hona aap ke haq me hota hai agar gumshuda card baad me kisi ghalat kaam me istemal ho.

Aakhri fori qadam ye hai ke reprint ki application me dair na karein. Card ke baghair bank, SIM, passport aur aksar sarkari kaam ruk jate hain, aur jitni jaldi application jama hogi utni jaldi naya card milega. Reprint ki application ghar baithe Pak ID app se bhi di ja sakti hai, is liye centre jane ka intezar karne ki zaroorat nahi.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Reprint Ke Liye Kya Chahiye

Reprint ki sab se bari sahulat ye hai ke is ke liye koi naya dastavez darkar nahi hota, kyun ke aap ka record NADRA ke database me pehle se maujood hai. Phir bhi ye cheezein pehle se tayyar rakhne se kaam aasan ho jata hai:

- **CNIC number:** 13 hindson wala number. Purane card ki tasveer ya photocopy ho to behtareen, warna naam aur walid ka naam se record dhoonda ja sakta hai.
- **Nai tasveer:** Pak ID app me live tasveer li jati hai — saaf roshni me, samne se, chehra poori tarah nazar aaye. Centre par bhi tasveer wahin li jati hai.
- **Biometrics:** Pak ID app mobile camera se fingerprints capture karti hai. Agar app fingerprints theek se na le sake to NADRA centre par biometrics dene ko kaha ja sakta hai.
- **Mobile number aur email:** Pak ID account aur OTP tasdeeq ke liye aik active mobile number aur email address lazmi hai. Tracking ID aur updates bhi isi par aate hain.
- **Payment ka zariya:** Online fee ke liye debit/credit card, ya app me maujood JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR jaisi payment options.
- **Delivery address:** Card us address par bheja jata hai jo aap application me muntakhib karein — maujooda ya mustaqil address me se aik chunna hota hai.

Aik baat wazeh rakhna zaroori hai: agar reprint ke sath address, naam ya tasveer me tabdeeli bhi karni hai to ye sirf reprint nahi rahega, modification ka case ban jayega aur us ke liye tabdeeli ka saboot bhi darkar hoga. Sirf gumshuda card dobara banwana hai to "Reprint" ka option hi muntakhib karein, warna application ghalat category me chali jayegi aur waqt zaya hoga.

## Gumshuda CNIC Dobara Banane Ki Fees 2026

NADRA ki shaya shuda fee schedule ke mutabiq reprint (duplicate) ki fee card ki qism — standard CNIC ya Smart CNIC (chip wala) — aur service ki raftaar par munhasir hai. Neeche di gayi fees October 2026 me shaya shuda NADRA fee schedule se li gayi hain. Hatmi rakam hamesha Pak ID app/portal ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai, usi ko ada karein:

| Card Ki Qism | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard CNIC reprint | Rs. 400 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| Smart CNIC (chip wala) reprint | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |

Fee ke sath delivery ki muddat bhi farq hoti hai, jo agle section me tafseel se di gayi hai. Aam tor par log Normal category hi chunte hain jab jaldi na ho, kyun ke is ki fee sab se kam hoti hai. Agar card fori chahiye — masalan visa ya naukri ki application ke liye — to Urgent ya Executive behtar hai, lekin fee dugni se bhi zyada ho jati hai, is liye zaroorat ke mutabiq faisla karein.

Overseas Pakistanis ke liye NICOP ki reprint ki fee dollar me aur zone ke mutabiq hoti hai, is liye bahar mulk se apply karne wale apni zone ki hatmi fee Pak ID app ki payment screen par hi dekhein. Pakistan ke andar rehne walon ke liye upar di gayi rupee wali fees lagu hoti hain. Fees waqtan fawaqtan tabdeel ho sakti hain, is liye payment karne se pehle screen par likhi rakam ko aik dafa phir dekh lein.

## Online Tareeqa: Pak ID App Se Reprint Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

NADRA ne khud elan kiya hai ke gumshuda CNIC ya NICOP ki reprint ghar baithe Pak ID mobile app se ki ja sakti hai. Online tareeqa sab se aasan hai aur is me centre jane ki zaroorat nahi parti, bas ye steps follow karein:

![Pak ID app par gumshuda CNIC ki reprint ke liye mobile se tasveer aur fingerprint verification karte hue aik shakhs](/images/gumshuda-cnic-dobara-banane-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

1. **Pak ID app download karein:** Google Play Store ya Apple App Store se official "Pak Identity" app install karein. Jaali apps se bachein — sirf NADRA ki official app istemal karein.
2. **Account banayein ya login karein:** Apne email aur active mobile number se account banayein, OTP se tasdeeq karein. Agar account pehle se hai to seedha login karein.
3. **Applicant muntakhib karein:** "Myself" muntakhib karein agar apna card banwana hai. Kisi khooni rishtedar ke liye apply kar rahe hain to woh shakhs aap ke sath maujood hona chahiye, kyun ke us ki facial verification darkar hogi.
4. **"Reprint" ka option chunein:** CNIC ki services me se "Reprint" muntakhib karein. Yehi woh option hai jo gumshuda ya kharab card ke liye hai — Renewal ya Modification ghalti se bhi na chunein.
5. **Live tasveer aur fingerprints dein:** App ki hidayat par samne se tasveer lein aur mobile camera se fingerprints capture karein. Achi roshni aur saaf camera se ye marhala aik hi dafa me mukammal ho jata hai.
6. **Delivery address chunein:** "Card Delivery" me maujooda ya mustaqil address muntakhib karein. Wahi address likhein jahan courier aasani se pahuncha sake.
7. **Processing category chunein:** Normal, Urgent ya Executive me se aik muntakhib karein. Fee screen par dikhai degi — tasdeeq kar ke aage barhein.
8. **Application jama karein aur fee ada karein:** Application approve hone ke baad tracking ID ke sath "Pay Now" ka option aayega. Debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR se fee ada karein aur receipt mehfooz rakhein.

Fee ada hone ke baad aap ka naya card tayyari ke marahil me chala jata hai aur usi tracking ID se is ki paigiri ki ja sakti hai. Agar kisi marhale par app fingerprints na le sake ya mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho to NADRA centre jane ki hidayat mil sakti hai — ye koi kharabi nahi, aik aam marhala hai.

## NADRA Centre Se Reprint Ka Tareeqa

Agar app se apply karna mumkin na ho, ya aap centre jana zyada pasand karte hon, to qareebi NADRA Registration Centre (NRC) se bhi reprint ho jata hai. Centre ka tareeqa ye hai:

1. **Qareebi centre dhoondein:** NADRA ki Rahbar app ya website se apne qareebi Registration Centre ka pata aur timing maloom karein.
2. **Token lein:** Centre par "Reprint" ke liye token lein aur apna CNIC number batayein. Purane card ki tasveer ya photocopy sath ho to record dhoondna aur aasan ho jata hai.
3. **Record ki tasdeeq karein:** Counter par aap ka maujooda record dikhaya jayega — naam, walid ka naam aur address ghor se check karein ke sab kuch durust hai. Koi tabdeeli darkar ho to usi waqt batayein, warna reprint purani maloomat par hi hoga.
4. **Biometrics aur tasveer:** Centre par fingerprints aur nai tasveer li jayegi. Ye marhala chand minute ka hota hai.
5. **Fee ada karein aur receipt lein:** Counter par fee ada karein aur receipt par likhi mutawaqqa delivery date note kar lein. Receipt par tracking number bhi hota hai, use sambhal kar rakhein.

Centre ka faida ye hai ke agar app me koi masla aa raha ho to woh wahin hal ho jata hai, aur staff ghalat category chunne jaisi ghaltiyon se bacha leta hai. Nuqsan sirf ye hai ke centre jane, qatar me lagne aur wapas aane ka waqt lagta hai, jab ke online tareeqa ghar baithe mukammal ho jata hai.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Reprint ki delivery ka waqt card ki qism aur chuni hui category par munhasir hai. NADRA ki shaya shuda schedule ke mutabiq andazan muddat ye hai:

| Card Ki Qism | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard CNIC | taqreeban 15 din | taqreeban 12 din | taqreeban 6 din |
| Smart CNIC | taqreeban 31 din | taqreeban 23 din | taqreeban 9 din |

Ye muddat fee ki adayegi ke baad shuru hoti hai, application jama karne ke din se nahi. Is liye fee waqt par ada karna zaroori hai — warna application ruki rehti hai aur card ki tayyari shuru nahi hoti. Delivery courier ke zariye muntakhib shuda address par hoti hai, is liye address durust likhna aur delivery ke din kisi ka ghar par maujood hona zaroori hai.

![Courier ke zariye naya CNIC ghar ke darwaze par wasool karte hue aik shakhs](/images/gumshuda-cnic-dobara-banane-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Status check karne ke do aasan tareeqe hain. Pehla, Pak ID app ya portal (id.nadra.gov.pk) par apni tracking ID se application ka status dekhein. Dosra, apni tracking ID SMS ke zariye **8400** par bhej kar status maloom karein — ye wohi number hai jo NADRA ki digar services ke liye bhi istemal hota hai. Agar card muddat guzarne ke baad bhi na mile to NADRA helpline **051-111-786-100** ya **1777** par tracking ID ke sath rabta karein.

Agar aap ka card expire bhi ho chuka hai to [CNIC renewal ka tareeqa](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) alag guide me tafseel se diya gaya hai, aur pehli dafa card banwane ke liye [NADRA CNIC banane ki guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) dekhein. Overseas Pakistanis apne [NICOP card](/guides/nicop-card-banane-ka-tareeqa) ki reprint bhi isi Pak ID app se kar sakte hain. Visa ya immigration ke liye family ka saboot chahiye ho to [NADRA FRC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) kaam aayega, aur bachon ke [B-Form](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) se mutaliq maloomat bhi site par maujood hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Bachein

Reprint ki application aksar chhoti ghaltiyon ki wajah se atak jati hai. In se bachna aasan hai agar pehle se maloom hon:

- **Ghalat category chunna:** Gumshuda card ke liye "Reprint" chunein, Renewal ya Modification nahi. Ghalat category me application ya to reject ho jati hai ya ghalat card ban jata hai.
- **Fee ada karne me dair:** Application approve hone ke baad fee waqt par ada na ki to card ki tayyari shuru nahi hoti. "Pay Now" aate hi fee ada karein aur receipt mehfooz rakhein.
- **Ghalat delivery address:** Card usi address par jata hai jo application me likha ho. Purana address likh dena sab se aam wajah hai jis se card kisi aur ke paas pahunch jata hai ya wapas ho jata hai.
- **Kharab tasveer ya fingerprints:** Andheri jagah ya dhundli tasveer se verification fail ho sakti hai. Saaf roshni me, samne se tasveer lein aur ungliyan saaf rakh kar fingerprints dein.
- **Jaali app download karna:** Sirf official Pak Identity app istemal karein. Milti-julti naam wali apps se fee ya maloomat ka nuqsan ho sakta hai.
- **Purana card milne par dono rakhna:** Agar reprint ke baad purana card mil jaye to use istemal na karein. Aik shakhs ka aik hi valid card hota hai — purana card NADRA ko wapas karna ya zaya karna behtar hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Gumshuda CNIC dobara banane ki fee kitni hai?**
Standard CNIC ki reprint Normal me Rs. 400, Urgent me Rs. 1,150 aur Executive me Rs. 2,150 hai. Smart CNIC (chip wala) ki reprint Normal me Rs. 750, Urgent me Rs. 1,500 aur Executive me Rs. 2,500 hai. Hatmi rakam Pak ID app ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai, usi ko ada karein.

**Kya gumshuda CNIC ke liye FIR lazmi hai?**
Nahi, NADRA ki reprint ke liye FIR lazmi shart nahi hai, kyun ke card aap ke maujooda record se dobara print hota hai aur koi naya dastavez darkar nahi hota. Albatta agar card chori hua hai to police report karwana aik hifazati qadam hai jo baad me ghalat istemal ki surat me aap ke haq me hota hai.

**Kya naya card milne par CNIC number tabdeel ho jata hai?**
Nahi, reprint me CNIC number wohi rehta hai jo pehle tha. Sirf naya card print hota hai, aap ka record aur number tabdeel nahi hote. Number tabdeel sirf tab hota hai jab NADRA kisi khaas case me purana record mansookh kar ke naya jari kare, jo reprint ka mamla nahi hai.

**Gumshuda CNIC kitne din me milta hai?**
Standard CNIC Normal me taqreeban 15 din, Urgent me 12 din aur Executive me 6 din me milta hai. Smart CNIC Normal me taqreeban 31 din, Urgent me 23 din aur Executive me 9 din leta hai. Ye muddat fee ki adayegi ke baad shuru hoti hai aur delivery courier se hoti hai.

**Kya gumshuda CNIC ki reprint online ho sakti hai ya centre jana lazmi hai?**
Online ho sakti hai — NADRA ki official Pak Identity app se ghar baithe "Reprint" ki application di ja sakti hai, jis me live tasveer, fingerprints, address aur fee sab app se mukammal hote hain. Centre jana sirf us surat me zaroori hota hai jab app se biometrics na li ja sakein ya mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho.

Hatmi rakam aur delivery ki date ke liye Pak ID app/portal ki payment screen aur official website nadra.gov.pk ya helpline 051-111-786-100 / 1777 se tasdeeq karein.*
