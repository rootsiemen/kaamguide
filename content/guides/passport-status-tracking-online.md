## Pehli Baat: Passport Kahan Atka Hai, Ye Jan'na Aap Ka Haq Hai

Passport ke liye apply karne ke baad sab se bechain karne wala waqt wohi hota hai jab aap ko pata nahi hota ke aap ka passport **bana bhi ya nahi, print hua ya nahi, aur ghar kab aayega**. Khush-qismati se Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGIP) ne status maloom karne ke official tareeqe diye hain — sab se mashhoor **SMS short code 9988** hai, jis se aap apne mobile se hi passport ki surat-e-hal maloom kar sakte hain.

Is guide me hum wohi tareeqe batayenge jo **official tor par tasdeeq-shuda** hain: SMS 9988, helpline, email aur online apply walon ke liye portal. Aur aik ahem imandari bhi: internet par kai websites "token number dal kar online status dekhein" ka dawa karti hain — **DGIP ki official site par aisa koi public tracking form mojood nahi**, is liye un websites par apna CNIC ya token number kabhi na likhein.

> **Note:** Is guide ki maloomat DGIP ki official website (dgip.gov.pk), official 9988 SMS service notice aur DGIP ke published process ki bunyad par **23 October 2026** ko check ki gayi thin. Tareeqa ya numbers tabdeel ho sakte hain — tasdeeq ke liye helpline **051-111-344-777** istemal karein.

## Tracking ID / Token Number Kahan Se Milega

Status check karne se pehle aap ko apna **token number** chahiye — ye 11 digits ka number hai jo aap ki application ki pehchan hai:

- Passport office me data entry ke waqt jo **receipt slip (token)** mili thi, us par ye number **print** hota hai — wohi parchi sambhal kar rakhein.
- Yehi number aap ko **SMS aur email** ke zariye bhi bheja jata hai (jo mobile number aur email aap ne data entry ke waqt diya tha).
- **Online apply** (onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk) karne walon ko **Online Tracking ID** un ke account aur email me milti hai.
- Passport **wusool karte waqt asal token** saath le jana lazmi hai — DGIP passport sirf applicant ko ya us ke khoon ke rishte-dar ko deta hai.

**Agar token gum ho gaya ho** to pareshan na hon — apne CNIC ke saath usi Regional Passport Office jayein jahan apply kiya tha; record se token dobara nikala ja sakta hai. Passport banwane ka poora tareeqa hamari [passport guide](/guides/pakistan-passport-banane-ka-tareeqa) me mojood hai.

## Tareeqa 1: SMS 9988 Se Status Maloom Karna (Sab Se Asaan)

**9988 DGIP ka official SMS short code hai** — is ki tasdeeq DGIP ki apni website par mojood official notice ("hiring of SMS service through 9988 short code") se hoti hai. DGIP is system ko **"SMS based Passport Tracking Intimation System"** kehta hai, aur is ke zariye applicants ko passport ke marahil (printing, shipment/delivery) ki ittila bhi bheji jati hai — us mobile number par jo aap ne data entry ke waqt diya tha.

**Status maloom karne ke liye:**

1. Apne mobile ke SMS me apna **11-digit token number** likhein.
2. Isay **9988** par bhej dein (Pakistan ke andar se).
3. Jawabi SMS me aap ki application ka **maujooda status** aajayega.

**Do ahem baatein:**

- SMS bhejne par **aam SMS charges** lagte hain — koi alag sarkari fee nahi hai.
- DGIP ki official site par **exact SMS format publish nahi** hai (sirf token number likhna hai ya kuch aur bhi) — upar bataya gaya format mustanad guides me darj hai, lekin agar jawab na aaye to helpline par tasdeeq kar lein.

**Overseas Pakistanis ke liye:** Bahar mulk se apply karne walon ko DGIP ki **Email Rabta** service ke zariye un ke diye gaye email par status bheja jata hai.

## Tareeqa 2: Online Apply Walon Ke Liye Portal, Email Aur Helpline

Agar aap ne **onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk** se online apply kiya tha:

- Apne **online account me login** kar ke application ka status dekhein — online applications ka status account ke andar show hota hai.
- **Masla ho to email karein:** online application me koi problem ho to **mrponline@dgip.gov.pk** par rabta karein; delivery ka masla ho to **onlinedelivery@dgip.gov.pk** par email karein — email me apna naam, mobile number, **Online Tracking ID**, CNIC aur pata lazmi likhein.
- **Application Tracking email:** DGIP ke contact page par tracking ke liye **opts@dgip.gov.pk** darj hai — is par naam, mobile, CNIC, token number aur pata bhej kar status maloom kiya ja sakta hai.
- **Helpline:** **051-111-344-777** (DGIP ke official scam-warning advisory me yehi number darj hai). Auqat: Monday se Friday, subah 8:30 se sham 4:30 tak. Bahar mulk se: +92-51-111-344-777.

## Status Messages Ka Matlab Samjhein

DGIP ke official process me ye marahil aate hain — SMS ya helpline par yehi alfaz sunne ko milenge:

| Status | Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| **Go ahead** | Assistant Director ne interview ke baad case approve kar diya — ab aap office se ja sakte hain |
| **Deferred** | Mazeed kagzaat ya security clearance darkar hai — clearance aate hi DGIP aap ko **phone par** ittila dega |
| **Fee paid** | Fee ki adayegi confirm ho gayi — ab passport **printing** ke liye bheja jayega |
| **Printed** | Passport production facility me **print ho chuka** hai |
| **Dispatched** | Passport production se nikal kar aap ke passport office / mission / ghar ke pate par **bhej diya gaya** hai |

**Note:** "Under Process" ya "Ready for Collection" jaise alfaz bhi SMS me aate hain, lekin DGIP ne status messages ki **koi mukammal official fehrist publish nahi ki** — upar wali table official process stages ki hai.

## Kitna Waqt Lagega: Normal, Urgent Aur Fast Track

DGIP ke official waqt (production fee confirm hone ke baad shuru hote hain):

| Category | Official waqt | Kahan available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Normal** | **21 working days** | Tamam passport offices |
| **Urgent** | **5 working days** | Tamam passport offices |
| **Fast Track** | **2 working days** | Sirf 47 designated cities; e-Passport par nahi |

**Yaad rakhein:** Door-daraz ilaqon aur bahar mulk ke missions tak courier ka waqt is me **shamil nahi** hai. Aur token slip par jo **collection date** likhi hai, passport usi tareekh ko ya us ke baad wusool karein — pehle jane ka faida nahi.

## Passport Waqt Par Na Aaye To Kya Karein

1. **Pehle collection date check karein** — token slip par di gayi tareekh se pehle passport ki umeed na rakhein.
2. **9988 par SMS** kar ke taza status lein — shayad status "Deferred" ho, jis ka matlab hai mazeed kagzaat ya clearance darkar hai (DGIP clearance par phone karta hai — unknown numbers se aane wali calls miss na karein).
3. **Helpline 051-111-344-777** par call karein (Mon–Fri, 8:30am–4:30pm) — apna token number aur CNIC tayyar rakhein.
4. **Email karein:** opts@dgip.gov.pk par naam, mobile, CNIC, token number aur pata likh kar bhejein.
5. **Regional Passport Office jayein** — jahan apply kiya tha, wahan token aur asal CNIC ke saath khud jayein; counter par record check ho jayega.
6. **Shikayat (complaint):** Pakistan Citizen's Portal par **"Immigration & Passport"** category me shikayat darj karwayein. Overseas applicants **complaints.passport@interior.gov.pk** par email kar sakte hain.

**Khoya hua passport dobara banwana ho** to us ka alag process hai — hamari [khoya hua passport guide](/guides/khoya-hua-passport-dobara-banwana) parh lein. Aur agar CNIC me koi masla ho to [NADRA CNIC guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) dekhein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Farzi tracking websites par CNIC/token likhna** — DGIP ka koi public "token dal kar status dekhein" web form nahi hai. Jo website aap se CNIC aur token mange, woh DGIP ki nahi — apni maloomat aisi jagah kabhi na dein.
2. **Token slip gum kar dena** — ye parchi passport wusool karne tak sambhal kar rakhein; is ke baghair delivery me dikkat hoti hai.
3. **Ghalat mobile number dena** — data entry ke waqt jo number diya tha, status SMS usi par aate hain. Number band ho to foran office ko ittila dein.
4. **Collection date se pehle office ke chakkar** — token par likhi tareekh se pehle jane se waqt zaya hota hai, passport pehle nahi milta.
5. **"Deferred" status ko nazar-andaz karna** — is ka matlab hai aap ki taraf se koi kaam baqi hai; DGIP ki call ka intezar karein ya khud rabta karein, warna file atki rahegi.
6. **Agenton ko paise dena** — status check karna muft hai (9988 par sirf aam SMS charges). Koi agent "jaldi nikalwane" ke paise mange to DGIP helpline par report karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya me website par token number dal kar passport ka status dekh sakta hun?**

Nahi — DGIP ki official website par aisa koi public tracking form mojood nahi hai. Jo websites ye dawa karti hain, woh DGIP ki nahi hain; un par apna CNIC ya token number kabhi na likhein. Official tareeqe: SMS 9988, helpline 051-111-344-777, ya online apply walon ke liye un ka portal account.

**9988 par SMS bhejne ka sahi format kya hai?**

Mustanad guides ke mutabiq apna 11-digit token number likh kar 9988 par bhej dein. DGIP ne apni site par exact format publish nahi kiya, is liye agar jawab na aaye to helpline par tasdeeq kar lein. SMS par sirf aam charges lagte hain.

**Passport "Deferred" show ho raha hai — is ka kya matlab hai?**

Is ka matlab hai ke mazeed kagzaat ya security clearance darkar hai. DGIP ka kehna hai ke clearance report aate hi applicant ko phone par ittila di jati hai. Aap khud bhi helpline ya apne Regional Passport Office se rabta kar sakte hain.

**Normal passport kitne din me milta hai?**

Official waqt: Normal 21 working days, Urgent 5 working days, Fast Track 2 working days (sirf 47 designated cities me, e-Passport par nahi). Ye waqt fee confirm hone ke baad shuru hota hai; door-daraz ilaqon tak courier ka waqt alag hai.

**Mera token gum ho gaya hai — ab status kaise check karun?**

Apne asal CNIC ke saath usi Regional Passport Office jayein jahan apply kiya tha — record se aap ka token number dobara nikala ja sakta hai. Us ke baad 9988 par SMS ya helpline se status maloom karein.

**How to track passport status in Pakistan?**

Send your 11-digit token number via SMS to **9988** (DGIP's official SMS short code) from inside Pakistan to receive your application status. Online applicants can check status in their account on onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk or email mrponline@dgip.gov.pk. DGIP has no public web form for tracking — never enter your CNIC or token on third-party websites. Official delivery times: Normal 21 working days, Urgent 5 working days, Fast Track 2 working days. For help, call the DGIP helpline 051-111-344-777 (Mon–Fri, 8:30am–4:30pm).
