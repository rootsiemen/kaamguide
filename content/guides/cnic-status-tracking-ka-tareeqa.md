## CNIC Application Ka Status Check Kyun Zaroori Hai

CNIC ke liye apply karne ke baad sab se bara sawal yehi hota hai ke card kahan tak pahuncha — kya application abhi processing me hai, card print ho gaya hai, ya dispatch ho chuka hai. NADRA har application ko aik unique tracking ID deta hai, aur isi tracking ID ki madad se aap ghar baithe apni application ka status maloom kar sakte hain, bina centre ke chakkar lagaye.

Status check karna is liye bhi zaroori hai ke agar application me koi masla ho — masalan documents ki kami, biometric verification ka masla, ya address ki ghalti — to wo waqt par pata chal jaye. Aksar log card ka intezar hafton karte rehte hain jab ke asal me un ki application kisi aik stage par ruki hoti hai aur sirf aik chhoti si tasdeeq darkar hoti hai. Tracking se ye bhi pata chalta hai ke card dispatch ho gaya hai ya collection ke liye tayyar hai, taake aap waqt par centre ya courier se card wasool kar saken.

Ye guide CNIC ki har qism ki application par lagu hoti hai — naya CNIC, [renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa), [gumshuda card ka dobara ijra](/guides/gumshuda-cnic-dobara-banane-ka-tareeqa), naam ya address ki tabdeeli, [NICOP](/guides/nicop-card-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur [B-Form](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) — kyun ke in sab ke liye tracking ka tareeqa aik jaisa hai: tracking ID, SMS 8400, Pak-ID app/portal, ya helpline.

## Status Check Karne Se Pehle Ye Cheezein Tayyar Rakhen

Status check karne ke liye aap ko sirf aik cheez lazmi chahiye: **tracking ID**. Ye 10 hindson ka number hota hai jo NADRA Registration Centre par application jama karate waqt di gayi receipt (token slip) par darj hota hai, aur aksar application ke baad aap ke mobile par SMS ke zariye bhi bhej diya jata hai. Is receipt ko card milne tak mehfooz rakhen — is ke baghair status check karna mushkil ho jata hai.

Is ke ilawa, tareeqe ke mutabiq ye cheezein kaam aati hain: SMS tareeqe ke liye Pakistani mobile number aur thora sa SMS balance; Pak-ID app ya portal ke liye internet, aap ka CNIC number aur Pak-ID account ka PIN; aur helpline par call ke liye tracking ID aur apna CNIC number. Apna tracking ID kisi ghair mutaliqa website ya shakhs ke sath share na karen — sirf NADRA ke official channels par hi dalein. Koi bhi ghair sarkari website jo tracking ID ya CNIC number mang kar status batane ka dawa kare, us se bachen; NADRA ka status sirf us ke apne channels se maloom hota hai.

## Tareeqa 1: SMS Se Status Check (8400)

Sab se tez aur sab se aasan tareeqa SMS hai — is ke liye na internet chahiye, na app, na login. Apne mobile ke messaging app me jayen, sirf apni **10 hindson ki tracking ID** likhen (koi lafz, space ya dash nahi), aur use **8400** par bhej den. Chand lamhon me NADRA ki taraf se jawab aa jata hai jis me aap ki application ki maujooda stage likhi hoti hai, masalan "in process", "printed" ya "dispatched".

![Tracking ID 8400 par SMS bhej kar CNIC application ka status maloom karte hue aik shakhs](/images/cnic-status-tracking-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

SMS tareeqe ke bare me chand zaroori batein zehen me rakhen. Pehli, SMS sirf **Pakistani mobile number** se kaam karta hai — agar aap mulk se bahar hain aur aap ke paas Pakistani SIM nahi, to ye tareeqa kaam nahi karega; us surat me Pak-ID portal ya helpline istemal karen. Dosri, SMS bhejne par aap ke mobile network ka aam SMS charge lag sakta hai, jab ke status dekhna apne aap me NADRA ki taraf se muft hai. Teesri, agar jawab na aaye to pehle ye check karen ke tracking ID receipt par darj number se bilkul mil rahi hai ya nahi — aik hindse ki ghalti bhi jawab rok sakti hai — aur dobara SMS karen.

Aik aam confusion ye hai ke log CNIC number 8400 par bhej dete hain. Application ka status maloom karne ke liye **tracking ID** bhejni hoti hai, CNIC number nahi. 8300 aik alag service hai jo voter se mutaliq maloomat ke liye hai, is liye CNIC application track karne ke liye hamesha 8400 istemal karen.

## Tareeqa 2: Pak-ID App Aur Portal Se Online Tracking

Agar aap ko sirf aik jumle ka jawab nahi, balkay poori tafseel chahiye — ke application kab jama hui, kis stage par kitne din lagi, aur card kab dispatch hua — to Pak-ID app ya portal behtareen hai. Pak Identity NADRA ki official app hai jo Google Play Store aur Apple App Store par mojood hai; is ka web portal **id.nadra.gov.pk** par hai. Sirf official store listing se hi app download karen, kisi link ya file se nahi.

App ya portal par apne CNIC number aur registered mobile number se login karen, aur zaroorat parne par biometric tasdeeq mukammal karen. Phir "Existing Applications" ya "Tracking" wale hisse me jayen, apni tracking ID aur PIN code dalein, aur aap ki application ki poori timeline samne aa jaye gi. Agar application aap ke account se linked hai to wo khud hi nazar aa sakti hai. Ye tareeqa mulk se bahar rehne wale Pakistaniyon ke liye bhi kaam karta hai, jo NICOP ya POC ki application track karna chahte hain — is ke liye Pakistani SIM ki zaroorat nahi, sirf internet aur Pak-ID account chahiye.

Online tracking ka faida ye hai ke is me aap ko har stage ki tareekh aur waqt bhi nazar aata hai, jis se andaza hota hai ke application kis raftaar se aage barh rahi hai. Agar koi stage ghair mamooli tor par lambi ho jaye, to yehi woh waqt hai jab helpline par rabta karna chahiye.

## Tareeqa 3: Helpline Aur NADRA Centre Se Maloomat

Agar SMS aur online tracking dono se jawab na mile, ya status samajh na aaye, to NADRA ki helpline par rabta karen. Pakistan ke andar mobile se **1777** (toll-free) par call karen, aur mulk se bahar ya landline se **+92 51 111 786 100** par. Call karne se pehle apni tracking ID aur CNIC number tayyar rakhen taake numainda foran aap ki application nikal sake. Helpline din raat khuli rehti hai.

Agar masla helpline se hal na ho — masalan biometric dobara dena ho, ya koi document jama karana ho — to usi NADRA Registration Centre par jayen jahan application jama karai thi, apni original receipt aur CNIC ke sath. Centre par staff aap ki application ki stage bata kar ye bhi bata de ga ke aage kya darkar hai. Yaad rakhen, koi agent ya darmiyani shakhs aap ki application tez nahi kar sakta; NADRA ne khud ye wazeh kiya hai ke wo WhatsApp ya social media par koi service nahi deta aur kisi agent ko majaz nahi karta.

## Status Messages Ka Matlab Samjhen

Tracking ke jawab me aap ko mukhtalif stages nazar aati hain. In ka matlab samajhna is liye zaroori hai ke aap ko pata chale ke intezar karna hai ya koi qadam uthana hai:

| Status | Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| Application Submitted / In Process | Application mil gayi hai aur jaanch ke marhale me hai |
| Under Verification | Aap ka record aur documents verify ho rahe hain |
| Card Printed | Card chap gaya hai, dispatch ki tayyari hai |
| Card Dispatched | Card courier ya centre ki taraf bhej diya gaya hai |
| Ready for Collection / Delivered | Card centre par wasool ke liye tayyar hai, ya aap tak pahunch gaya hai |

Agar status lambi muddat tak "In Process" ya "Under Verification" par ruka rahe to ghabrayen nahi — verification me waqt lag sakta hai, khaas tor par jab record me koi tabdeeli ho. Lekin agar normal delivery time guzar jaye aur status aage na barhe, to helpline par rabta karen aur tracking ID ke sath shikayat darj karayen.

## Card Milne Par Kya Karen Aur Aam Ghaltiyan

Jab status "Ready for Collection" ya "Delivered" ho jaye to card wasool karte waqt apni original receipt aur purana CNIC (agar ho) sath le jayen. Card milte hi is par naam, walid ka naam, date of birth aur address ghor se check karen — agar koi ghalti ho to usi waqt centre par batayen, taake [CNIC me naam ya address ki tabdeeli](/guides/cnic-me-naam-address-tabdeeli-ka-tareeqa) ka amal waqt par shuru ho sake.

![NADRA centre par naya CNIC wasool karte hue aik shakhs](/images/cnic-status-tracking-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Aam ghaltiyon se bachen: tracking ID ki jagah CNIC number SMS karna; receipt gum kar dena; ghair sarkari websites par apna CNIC ya tracking ID dalna; aur kisi agent ko paisay de kar "tez" service lena. Ye sab na sirf waqt zaya karte hain, balkay aap ki zaati maloomat bhi khatre me dal sakte hain. Tracking ke liye sirf teen official raste istemal karen — SMS 8400, Pak-ID app/portal, aur NADRA helpline — aur card ki delivery ke baad apni receipt mehfooz rakhen.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CNIC ka status check karne ke liye kya chahiye?**

Sirf aap ki 10 hindson ki tracking ID chahiye, jo NADRA centre par application jama karate waqt di gayi receipt par darj hoti hai aur aksar SMS ke zariye bhi bhej di jati hai. Is ke baghair status check karna mushkil hota hai, is liye receipt ko card milne tak sambhal kar rakhen.

**8400 par kya bhejna hota hai — CNIC number ya tracking ID?**

Application ka status maloom karne ke liye apni tracking ID bhejni hoti hai, CNIC number nahi. Message me sirf tracking ID likhen, koi lafz ya space nahi, aur use 8400 par bhej den. Chand lamhon me jawab aa jata hai.

**Kya CNIC ka status check karna muft hai?**

Pak-ID app aur portal se status dekhna muft hai, aur helpline par call bhi Pakistan ke andar mobile se 1777 par toll-free hai. SMS tareeqe par aap ke mobile network ka aam SMS charge lag sakta hai, lekin NADRA ki taraf se status batane ki koi fee nahi hai.

**Mulk se bahar rehte hue CNIC ka status kaise check karen?**

SMS 8400 sirf Pakistani mobile number se kaam karta hai, is liye bahar rehne wale log Pak-ID portal (id.nadra.gov.pk) ya Pak-ID app se tracking karen, ya helpline +92 51 111 786 100 par call karen. NICOP aur POC ki applications bhi isi tareeqe se track hoti hain.

**Status lambi muddat tak "In Process" par ruka ho to kya karen?**

Pehle tracking ID dobara check karen aur Pak-ID portal par poori timeline dekhen. Agar normal delivery time guzar gaya ho aur status aage na barh raha ho, to helpline 1777 (ya bahar se +92 51 111 786 100) par tracking ID ke sath rabta karen, ya usi NADRA centre par jayen jahan application jama karai thi.
