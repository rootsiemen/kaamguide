## Pehli Baat: PM Youth Loan Scheme Kya Hai

**PM Youth Business & Agriculture Loan Scheme (PMYB&ALS)** hukumat-e-Pakistan ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet naujawanon ko apna karobar shuru karne ya maujooda karobar barhane ke liye aasan sharait par loan milta hai. Ye scheme **Prime Minister's Youth Programme (PMYP)** ke tehet chalti hai aur is ka maqsad ye hai ke naujawan naukri dhoondne ki bajaye khud karobar karein aur doosron ko bhi rozgar dein. Loan **15 commercial, Islamic aur SME banks** ke zariye diya jata hai, lekin apply sirf aik hi jagah se hota hai — official portal **pmybals.pmyp.gov.pk**.

Is scheme ko aksar log **Kamyab Jawan Program** ke naam se bhi jante hain, kyun ke ye usi program ki nayi aur behtar shakal hai. Farq sirf itna hai ke ab loan ki raqam, markup aur tiers wazeh tor par tay hain aur poora process online hai — kisi daftar ke chakkar, kisi sifarish ya kisi agent ki zaroorat nahi. Agar aap ke paas aik acha karobari idea hai, valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) hai aur aap ki umar 21 se 45 saal ke darmiyan hai, to aap is scheme ke liye apply kar sakte hain.

Sab se ahem baat pehle samajh lein: ye loan **muft raqam nahi** — ye aik bank loan hai jo wapas karna hota hai. Farq ye hai ke chhoti raqam par markup **0%** hai aur bari raqam par bhi markup aam bank loan se bohat kam hai. Jo shakhs karobar ka irada rakhta hai aur waqt par qistein ada kar sakta hai, us ke liye ye scheme aik behtareen mauqa hai. Jo sirf paisa lena chahta hai aur wapas karne ka koi plan nahi rakhta, us ki darkhwast bank ki jaanch me hi ruk jayegi.

## Teen Tiers: Kitna Loan, Kitna Markup

Official portal ke mutabiq loan teen tiers me taqseem hai. Tier ka faisla is baat par hota hai ke aap ko kitni raqam chahiye aur aap ka karobar kis noiyat ka hai:

| Tier | Loan ki raqam | Markup (aap ke liye) | Muddat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | 5 lakh rupay tak | **0% — bila sood** | 3 saal tak |
| **Tier 2** | 5 lakh se zyada, 15 lakh tak | **5% saalana** | 8 saal tak (1 saal grace ke sath) |
| **Tier 3** | 15 lakh se zyada, 75 lakh tak | **7% saalana** | 8 saal tak (1 saal grace ke sath) |

Tier 1 sirf **microfinance banks aur idaron** ke zariye milta hai, jab ke Tier 2 aur Tier 3 scheduled banks dete hain. Working capital (rozmarra ke karobari kharchay) ke liye Tier 2 aur Tier 3 ki muddat 5 saal tak hoti hai. Grace period ka matlab ye hai ke Tier 2 aur Tier 3 me pehle saal tak aap ko asal raqam ki qist nahi deni parti — karobar khara karne ka waqt milta hai.

Tier chunte waqt hoshiyari se kaam lein. Agar aap ko sirf 3 ya 4 lakh chahiye to Tier 1 hi behtareen hai, kyun ke us par **koi markup nahi**. Zyada raqam maang kar Tier 2 ya Tier 3 me jana sirf is liye nuqsan-deh hai ke aap ko 5% ya 7% markup dena parega aur bank ki jaanch bhi sakht ho jayegi. Hamesha utni hi raqam maangein jitni aap ke business plan me wazeh tor par zaroori ho.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Official portal ke mutabiq PM Youth Loan ke liye ye shartein hain:

**1. Shehriat:** Darkhwast guzar **Pakistani shehri** ho aur us ke paas NADRA ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ho. **Non-resident Pakistanis (jo mulk se bahar rehte hain) apply nahi kar sakte** — ye scheme sirf Pakistan me rehne walon ke liye hai.

**2. Umar:** Apply karte waqt umar **21 saal se 45 saal** ke darmiyan honi chahiye. **IT aur e-commerce** ke karobar ke liye umar ki kam se kam had **18 saal** hai, lekin us ke liye kam se kam Matric taleem zaroori hai.

**3. Taleem:** Aam karobar ke liye taleem ki koi lazmi shart nahi hai, lekin taleem bank ke faisle me behtar asar dalti hai. Jin karobaron ke liye koi degree, certificate ya license lazmi hota hai (masalan medical ya technical kaam), un ke liye wo qualification honi chahiye.

**4. Sarkari mulazim nahi:** **Government employees is scheme ke liye eligible nahi hain.** Agar aap sarkari mulazim hain to aap apply nahi kar sakte.

**5. Karobar:** Aap ka karobar naya ho ya pehle se chal raha ho — dono surat me apply ho sakta hai. Karobar Pakistan ke kisi bhi hisse me ho sakta hai, bashmole Azad Kashmir aur Gilgit-Baltistan. Ziraat (agriculture) ke liye bhi loan milta hai — us ke liye zameen ka saboot ya theke ka muahida chahiye.

**6. Khawateen ke liye quota:** Scheme me **khawateen entrepreneurs ke liye 25% quota** rakha gaya hai, taa ke zyada se zyada khawateen apna karobar shuru kar sakein. Khawateen ko apply karte waqt koi alag form nahi bharna parta — quota bank ki satah par laagu hota hai.

**7. Koi default nahi:** Agar aap ka kisi bank ya idare me pehle se koi loan default hai to darkhwast mushkil ho jayegi. Bank aap ki credit history zaroor check karta hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Online form bharne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein taa ke form aadha chhora hua na rahe:

- **Valid CNIC** aur us ki **issue date** — form me CNIC number aur issue date dono likhni hoti hai. Agar CNIC expired hai to pehle [CNIC renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) karwa lein.
- **Apne naam par registered mobile number** — OTP verification isi number par aati hai.
- **Business plan ya feasibility study** — har tier ke liye aik buniyadi karobari mansuba chahiye hota hai: aap kya bechna ya banana chahte hain, kharcha kitna hai, aamdani ka andaza kya hai. SMEDA is me muft madad karta hai.
- **Do references** — do aise afrad ke naam aur numbers jo aap ke **khoon ke rishtedar na hon** aur aap ki shanakht ki tasdeeq kar sakein.
- **Bank ka intikhab** — form me aap ko apni pasand ka partner bank chunna hota hai (HBL, NBP, Bank of Punjab, UBL, Meezan Bank, Allied Bank waghera).
- **Ziraat ke liye:** Zameen ki malkiat ka saboot (fard ya registry) ya zameen ke theke ka muahida.
- **Income ka saboot:** Agar aap ka karobar pehle se chal raha hai to us ki aamdani ka record aur [FBR filer](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) hona faida-mand hota hai. Naye karobar ke liye ye lazmi nahi.

Bank darkhwast ki jaanch ke doran mazeed kagzaat bhi maang sakta hai — masalan bank statement, karobar ki jagah ka saboot ya [income certificate](/guides/income-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa). Is liye form jama karane ke baad bank ki call ya SMS par foran jawab dein.

## Fees Aur Charges: Kitna Kharcha Aayega

Is hisse me aik achi khabar hai: **official portal par online apply karne ki koi fee nahi li jati.** Na form ki fee hai, na registration ki. Agar koi shakhs ya agent aap se apply karne ke paise maange to samajh lein ke wo dhoka de raha hai — official tareeqa sirf portal hai aur wo muft hai.

| Cheez | Fee |
|---|---|
| Online apply (pmybals.pmyp.gov.pk) | **Muft — koi fee nahi** |
| Tier 1 markup | **0%** |
| Tier 2 markup | **5% saalana** |
| Tier 3 markup | **7% saalana** |
| Agent ya middleman ki fee | **Koi nahi — agent se bachein** |

Loan milne ke baad aap ko sirf bank ki tay shuda qistein ada karni hoti hain — markup ke sath. Is ke ilawa bank naye karobar ke liye aap se apni taraf se kuch **equity (apni hissa-dari)** bhi maang sakta hai, jis ki sharah tier aur bank ke mutabiq mukhtalif hoti hai; exact sharah apply karte waqt portal aur bank se confirm kar lein. Koi bhi raqam dene se pehle bank ki tehreeri sharait parh lein — zabani wada kafi nahi hota.

## Step-by-Step: Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

Poora apply **sirf online** hota hai — kaghaz ka form nahi hai aur bank ki branch me ja kar form nahi bhara jata. Tareeqa ye hai:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne browser me **pmybals.pmyp.gov.pk** likhein. URL ke aakhir me **.gov.pk** zaroor check kar lein — WhatsApp aur social media par is se milte-julte jaali portal ghoom rahe hain. Sirf asal portal par apni maloomat dein.

**Step 2 — Apply Now par click karein:** Homepage par **Apply Now** ya **Apply for Loan** ka button milega. Us par click kar ke loan application form kholein.

**Step 3 — CNIC se registration karein:** Apna **CNIC number, CNIC ki issue date** aur apne naam par registered **mobile number** likhein. Aap ke mobile par aik **OTP code** aayega — wo code likh kar apni shanakht verify karein.

**Step 4 — Tier aur bank chunein:** Apni zaroorat ke mutabiq **Tier 1, 2 ya 3** chunein aur pasand ka **partner bank** select karein. Tier 1 ke liye microfinance bank ki list aayegi.

**Step 5 — Zaati aur karobari maloomat bharein:** Apna naam, pata, taleem, karobar ki noiyat (business ya agriculture), karobar ki jagah aur **business plan** ki tafseel likhein. Jitna wazeh aur haqeeqat-pasand plan hoga, utni zyada approval ke chances honge.

**Step 6 — References aur documents upload karein:** Do references ke naam aur numbers dein aur manga gaya documents (CNIC ki copy, business plan waghera) upload karein.

**Step 7 — Form jama kar dein:** Sab kuch dobara parh kar **Submit** karein. Jama karane par aap ko aik **tracking number ya reference number** milega — use mehfooz rakh lein, isi se aap apni darkhwast ka status check kar sakte hain.

**Step 8 — Bank ki jaanch aur branch visit:** Online form ke baad aap ke chune hue bank ki branch aap se rabta karegi. Bank aap ke kagzaat aur business plan ki jaanch karega aur zaroorat parne par aap ko branch bula sakta hai. Sirf online form bharna loan ki guarantee nahi — **bank ki manzoori ke baad hi loan milta hai.**

## Apply Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai: Time Kitna Lagta Hai

Darkhwast jama karane ke baad ye marahil hote hain:

1. **Online verification (kuch din):** Portal aap ki CNIC aur mobile ki tasdeeq karta hai aur darkhwast mutaliqa bank ko bhej deta hai.
2. **Bank ki jaanch (2 se 6 haftay):** Bank aap ke kagzaat, credit history aur business plan ko check karta hai. Is doran bank aap ko call ya SMS kar ke mazeed maloomat ya kagzaat maang sakta hai.
3. **Manzuri aur disbursement:** Manzuri ke baad bank aap ko qist ka schedule aur sharait batata hai, phir raqam aap ke account me transfer hoti hai. Tier 1 ki raqam aam tor par jaldi milti hai kyun ke us ki jaanch halki hoti hai.

Kul mila kar darkhwast se raqam milne tak **aik se do mahine** lag sakte hain — ye waqt bank aur aap ke kagzaat ki mukammal hone par munhasir hai. Agar bank mazeed kagzaat maange aur aap der se dein to waqt barh jata hai. Apni darkhwast ka status portal par apne tracking number se waqtan fawaqtan check karte rahein. Agar aik mahine tak bank ki taraf se koi rabta na ho to apne chune hue bank ki branch se khud rabta karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Rukwa Deti Hain

- **Ghalat tier chunna:** Zaroorat se zyada raqam maangna ya ghalat tier select karna rejection ki aam wajah hai. Business plan me jo raqam likhi ho, usi ke mutabiq tier chunein.
- **Khalis business plan na hona:** "Kuch bhi kar loon ga" likhna kaafi nahi. Bank ko wazeh plan chahiye — kya bechna hai, kis ko bechna hai, kharcha aur aamdani kitni hogi. **SMEDA** is kaam me muft rehnumai karta hai — us ki helpline **111-111-456** hai aur smeda.org.pk par templates bhi milte hain.
- **Jaali portal par apply karna:** Sirf **pmybals.pmyp.gov.pk** asal hai. Kisi bhi doosri website, WhatsApp number ya agent ko apna CNIC aur OTP kabhi na dein.
- **Expired CNIC par apply karna:** Expired CNIC par darkhwast aage nahi barhti. Pehle [CNIC renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) karwa lein.
- **Agent ko fee dena:** Koi bhi shakhs jo loan dilane ke badle fee maange, wo dhoka de raha hai. Portal muft hai aur bank bhi kisi agent ke zariye darkhwast nahi leta.
- **Ghalat mobile number ya references:** OTP aap ke apne naam wali SIM par aata hai aur references aap ke khoon ke rishtedar nahi hone chahiye. Ghalat number likhne par verification wahin ruk jati hai.
- **Bank ki call nazar-andaz karna:** Bank ki jaanch ke doran aap ko call aaye to foran jawab dein aur manga gaya kagaz waqt par dein — der karne se darkhwast reject bhi ho sakti hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**PM Youth Loan Scheme ke liye apply karne ki aakhri tareekh kya hai?**
Official portal par darkhwastein saal bhar li jati hain — koi aik aakhri tareekh muqarar nahi hai. Jab bhi aap tayyar hon, pmybals.pmyp.gov.pk par apply kar sakte hain. Kisi bhi "aakhri tareekh" wali khabar par yaqeen karne se pehle portal khud check kar lein.

**Kya PM Youth Loan par waqai koi markup nahi hai?**
Tier 1 (5 lakh rupay tak) par markup **0%** hai — ye bilkul bila sood hai. Tier 2 par 5% aur Tier 3 par 7% saalana markup hai. Markup ki ye sharahain official portal pmybals.pmyp.gov.pk par darj hain.

**Kya sarkari mulazim ya mulk se bahar rehne wala Pakistani apply kar sakta hai?**
Nahi. Official portal ke mutabiq **sarkari mulazim eligible nahi** aur **non-resident Pakistanis bhi apply nahi kar sakte** — ye scheme sirf Pakistan me rehne wale ghair-sarkari shehrion ke liye hai.

**Loan milne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**
Aam tor par darkhwast se raqam milne tak **aik se do mahine** lagte hain — is me online verification, bank ki jaanch aur manzoori shamil hai. Waqt aap ke kagzaat mukammal hone aur bank ke process par munhasir hai.

**Kya loan ke liye koi zamanat ya property girvi rakhni parti hai?**
Tier 1 aur Tier 2 me aam tor par sirf **personal guarantee** kaafi hoti hai. Tier 3 me bank apni credit policy ke mutabiq zamanat ya collateral maang sakta hai. Exact shart aap ke chune hue bank par munhasir hai aur manzoori se pehle bank aap ko tehreeri tor par batata hai.
