## Seedha Jawab: USAT Kya Hai Aur Kis Ke Liye Lazmi Hai

**USAT (Undergraduate Studies Admission Test)** Higher Education Commission ke idarah **ETC (Education Testing Council)** ka standardized entry test hai jo Pakistan me **undergraduate (BS/BBA/LLB waghera) programs me admission** ke liye liya jata hai. Jin universities ne apne BS admissions ke liye USAT score lazmi kiya hai (jaise Quaid-i-Azam University ne Fall 2026 admissions ke liye dedicated USAT liya), wahan admission **USAT score ke baghair nahi** mil sakta.

Eligibility seedhi hai: **HSSC (Intermediate) mukammal kar chuke** ya **result awaiting** students dono apply kar sakte hain. Test **saal me quarterly** (HEC ki official FAQ ke mutabiq) munaqid hota hai, registration sirf **etc.hec.gov.pk** par hoti hai, aur koi aur website ya agent USAT registration nahi kar sakta.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fee aur pattern ki maloomat HEC ki official FAQ (hec.gov.pk) aur haaliya ETC cycles ki published updates se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. ETC har cycle me fee aur dates apne announcement me deta hai — registration ke waqt portal par generate hone wala amount hi final hota hai.

## 6 Categories: Apni Category Sahi Chunein

USAT **6 categories** me hota hai — apne Intermediate ke group ya intended BS program ke mutabiq category chunein. **Submit ke baad category change nahi hoti**, is liye faisla dhyaan se karein:

| Category | Kis Ke Liye |
| --- | --- |
| **USAT-M** | Pre-Medical — BS Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, allied health |
| **USAT-E** | Pre-Engineering — Engineering, physical sciences, technology |
| **USAT-CS** | Computer Science — BS CS, Software Engineering, Data Science, AI |
| **USAT-GS** | General Science — Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, interdisciplinary sciences |
| **USAT-COM** | Commerce — BBA, BS Accounting & Finance |
| **USAT-A** | Arts & Humanities — Social Sciences, Law, Fine Arts, Languages, Mass Communication |

Har category ka paper alag hota hai (test me color-coded booklets istemal hote hain), is liye **ghalat category ka paper aap ke admission ke kaam ka nahi** — apni university ke required category zaroor confirm karein.

## Paper Pattern: 100 Marks, Passing 50

Haaliya ETC cycles ke mutabiq USAT ka paper **kul 100 marks** ka hota hai:

- **75 marks: MCQs** — English, Quantitative Reasoning, Analytical Reasoning aur General Knowledge/Academic Aptitude par mabni
- **25 marks: Essay Writing** — English ya Urdu me essay (lafzon ki had announcement me di jati hai)
- **Qualifying cut-off: 50 marks** (100 me se)
- **Negative marking nahi** — andaza lagana mehfooz hai, koi sawal khali na chhorein
- **Validity: 1 saal** (result declaration ki date se) — is arse me score istemal ho sakta hai

Tayyari ka focus **English aur essay** par rakhein — ye dono hisse sab se zyada marks dete hain. HEC apni website par **sample paper** bhi deta hai — tayyari usi se shuru karein.

### Tayyari Ka Practical Plan

1. **Sample paper pehle hal karein:** HEC ka official sample paper paper ka asal andaz, waqt ki pabandi aur sawalon ki noiyat batata hai — isay timed conditions me hal karein.
2. **English rozana:** Vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms), grammar aur comprehension par roz 45 minute dein — MCQs ka sab se bara hissa English ka hai.
3. **Essay ki practice:** Haftay me 2 essays likhein aur lafzon ki had ka khayal rakhein — safai aur structure par marks milte hain.
4. **Quantitative aur analytical:** Basic mathematics aur logical reasoning ke sawal roz hal karein — formula sheet banayen aur revise karte rahein.
5. **Mock test:** Aakhri 2 hafton me pooray paper ke mock tests dein taake waqt ka intezam pakka ho jaye.

## Registration Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa (etc.hec.gov.pk)

1. **Portal kholein:** **etc.hec.gov.pk** par jayen aur ETC Candidate Portal me account banayen (email + password). Purane users apne maujooda credentials se log in karein.
2. **Profile mukammal karein:** "My Profile" section me personal details **bilkul CNIC/B-Form ke mutabiq** darj karein — naam, CNIC number aur contact details double-check karein.
3. **Test select karein:** Left sidebar me **"Undergraduate Studies Admission Test"** ka link kholein aur apni **category (USAT-M/E/CS/GS/COM/A)** chunein.
4. **Form bharein:** Personal, academic aur contact details darj karein; **haaliya passport-size tasveer** (clear front face) upload karein.
5. **Apna CNIC/B-Form istemal karein:** Walidain ya behan-bhai ke CNIC par application **disqualify** ho jati hai.
6. **Test city chunein:** Apne sheher ka centre dhyaan se select karein — **submit ke baad city change nahi hoti** (bohat kam candidates hone par ETC qareebi centre muntaqil kar sakta hai).
7. **Fee pay karein:** "My Application" me **"Online Payment"** dabane se **consumer number** generate hoga — isay **1Link/1Bill** ke zariye bank branch, ATM, internet/mobile banking ya wallet (**Easypaisa, JazzCash, UPaisa**) se pay karein.
8. **Payment verify karein:** Wapas dashboard par **"Verify Online Payment"** lazmi click karein — is ke baghair application submit nahi hogi.
9. **SUBMIT dabayen:** Application ka status **"Submitted"** hona chahiye. **SAVE ya INCOMPLETE** par chhori hui applications reject ho jati hain.

**Fee:** Haaliya cycles me USAT fee **Rs 1,200** hai (portal par generate hone wala amount final hai). Fee **non-refundable aur non-transferable** hai — test miss ho jaye ya application reject ho, fee wapas nahi milti.

**Roll number slip:** Test se taqreeban **aik hafta pehle** ETC portal par apne account se download karein. Test day par **printed slip + asal CNIC** lazmi le jayen (18 se kam umar wale **asal B-Form + tasveer wala SSC/HSSC certificate** le jayen).

## Test Day Ke Rules

- **Mobile phone, calculator, recording devices, earphones** — sab **sakht mana** hain test centre ke andar.
- Question booklet ki **tasveer lena ya share karna** jurm hai — ETC rules ke tehet saza ho sakti hai.
- Waqt par pohnchein; slip par di gayi reporting time se pehle centre me dakhil hon.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Students Karte Hain

1. **Application SAVE par chhor dena:** Submit dabaye baghair registration mukammal nahi hoti — sab se aam ghalti.
2. **Fee pay karke verify na karna:** 1Bill se pay karke **"Verify Online Payment"** na dabayen to application atki rehti hai.
3. **Ghalat category chunna:** Submit ke baad category change nahi hoti — university ki required category pehle confirm karein ([HEC recognized universities check](/guides/hec-recognized-universities-check) se university ki status bhi dekh lein).
4. **Doosre ke CNIC par apply karna:** Apne CNIC/B-Form par hi registration hogi.
5. **Centre ghalat chunna:** Submit ke baad centre change nahi hota.
6. **Deadline ka intezar karna:** Closing date ke aakhri ghanton me portal par rush hota hai — 2-3 din pehle mukammal kar lein.
7. **Sample paper dekhe baghair jana:** HEC ka official sample paper paper ka asal andaz batata hai — tayyari usi se shuru karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**USAT ki fee kitni hai aur kaise pay hoti hai?**
Haaliya ETC cycles me USAT fee **Rs 1,200** hai. Portal par application ke baad generate hone wale **consumer number** se fee **1Link/1Bill** ke zariye bank, ATM, mobile banking ya wallet (Easypaisa, JazzCash, UPaisa) se pay hoti hai. Pay karne ke baad dashboard par **"Verify Online Payment"** lazmi click karein. Fee non-refundable aur non-transferable hai; portal par generate hone wala amount hi final hai.

**USAT me passing marks kitne hain aur result kitne arse valid hai?**
Kul 100 marks me se **50 marks** qualifying cut-off hai. USAT ka score **1 saal** tak valid rehta hai (result declaration ki date se).

**Kya result awaiting students USAT de sakte hain?**
Ji. Jin students ka HSSC/Intermediate ka result aanay wala hai, woh current rules ke mutabiq USAT ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Admission ke waqt university ki result wali shart pura karna hogi.

**USAT saal me kitni dafa hota hai?**
HEC ki official FAQ ke mutabiq ETC ke tests **quarterly** (saal me 4 dafa) hote hain. Har cycle ki registration deadline aur test date ETC apne announcement aur test calendar me deta hai — current dates ke liye **etc.hec.gov.pk** check karein.

**USAT aur HAT me kya farq hai?**
**USAT** undergraduate (BS) admission ke liye hai (12 saal taleem ke baad), jabke **HAT** MS/MPhil aur PhD admission aur HEC scholarships ke liye hai (16 saal taleem ke baad). Dono ETC leta hai lekin categories aur maqsad alag hain.

**Roll number slip kab milegi aur test day par kya le jana hai?**
Slip test se taqreeban **aik hafta pehle** ETC portal se download hoti hai. Saath **printed slip + asal CNIC** lazmi hai (under-18: asal B-Form + tasveer wala SSC/HSSC certificate). Mobile aur calculator sakht mana hain.
