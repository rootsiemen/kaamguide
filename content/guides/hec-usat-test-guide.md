## Seedha Jawab: USAT Kya Hai Aur Kis Ke Liye Lazmi Hai

**USAT (Undergraduate Studies Admission Test)** Higher Education Commission ke idarah **ETC (Education Testing Council)** ka standardized entry test hai jo Pakistan me **undergraduate (BS/BBA/LLB waghera) programs me admission** ke liye liya jata hai. Jin universities ne apne BS admissions ke liye USAT score lazmi kiya hai (jaise Quaid-i-Azam University ne Fall 2026 admissions ke liye dedicated USAT liya), wahan admission **USAT score ke baghair nahi** mil sakta. HEC ke official USAT ad ke mutabiq score **HEC undergraduate scholarships** ke liye bhi istemal hota hai.

Eligibility seedhi hai (official ad ke mutabiq): **HSSC ya 12-year equivalent pass kar chuke** ya **exam de kar result awaiting** students dono apply kar sakte hain. Test **saal me quarterly** munaqid hota hai, registration sirf **etc.hec.gov.pk** par hoti hai, aur koi aur website ya agent USAT registration nahi kar sakta. Test fee **non-refundable aur non-transferable** hai.

> **Note:** Is guide ki tamam maloomat HEC/ETC ki official pages se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin** — official USAT ad, ETC FAQ aur HEC ka content-weightages document. **Ahem:** HEC/ETC apni public pages par **fee ki rakam aur passing marks publish nahi karta** — fee ki exact amount sirf ETC portal me application ke waqt generate hone wale 1Link/1Bill consumer-number invoice par nazar aati hai, aur passing/merit cut-off universities apne admission rules me tay karti hain. Neeche sirf official sources se verified cheezen di gayi hain.

ETC portal ke mutabiq filhal **koi active ETC test nahi** — haaliya USAT cycle ka portal **2 October 2026 ko band** hua aur test **Sunday, 25 October 2026** ko schedule hai (roll number slips jari ho rahi hain). Is ka matlab: agar aap ne is cycle me apply nahi kiya to ab **agle cycle** ka intezar karein — naye cycle ki registration dates ETC apne **announcement page** par deta hai. Har cycle me apply karne ke liye portal khulne ka intezar karein, purani dates par bharosa na karein.

## 6 Categories: Apni Category Sahi Chunein

Official USAT ad ke mutabiq USAT **6 categories** me hota hai — apne Intermediate ke group ya intended BS program ke mutabiq category chunein. **Submit ke baad category change nahi hoti**, is liye faisla dhyaan se karein:

| Category | Kis Ke Liye |
| --- | --- |
| **USAT-M** | Pre-Medical |
| **USAT-E** | Pre-Engineering |
| **USAT-CS** | Computer Science |
| **USAT-GS** | General Science |
| **USAT-COM** | Commerce |
| **USAT-A** | Arts & Humanities |

Har category ka paper alag hota hai (test me color-coded booklets istemal hote hain), is liye **ghalat category ka paper aap ke admission ke kaam ka nahi** — apni university ki required category zaroor confirm karein.

## Official Paper Pattern: 100 Marks, 140 Minutes

HEC ke official content-weightages document ke mutabiq paper is tarah hai:

**Part I — 75 MCQs (100 minutes):**
- **Verbal Reasoning: 20** — Analogy 5, Synonym/Antonym 4, Sentence Completion 6, Comprehension 5
- **Quantitative Reasoning: 25** — Arithmetic 6, Algebra & Functions 4, Geometry 3, Equations 3, Statistics 3, Scenario-Based/Mental Mathematics 6
- **Subject-specific: 30** — aap ki category ke stream subjects me 10+10+10 (masalan USAT-E me Physics/Chemistry/Mathematics)

**Part II — Essay (40 minutes, 25 marks):**
- **Argument-Based Essay: 15 marks** — 3 topics me se 1 chun kar likhein (English ya Urdu)
- **Narrative-Based Essay: 10 marks** — 3 topics me se 1 chun kar likhein (English ya Urdu)

**Kul: 100 marks (75 MCQs + 25 essay), kul waqt 140 minutes.** Negative marking ka zikr official documents me nahi — koi sawal khali na chhorein.

**Passing marks ke baare me wazahat:** HEC/ETC ki public documents me koi official passing threshold publish nahi hui. Admission ka faisla universities apne merit/cut-off rules par karti hain — apni target university ka required score us ke admission office ya website se confirm karein.

### Tayyari Ka Practical Plan

1. **HEC ka official sample paper pehle hal karein:** Paper ka asal andaz, waqt ki pabandi aur sawalon ki noiyat batata hai — timed conditions me hal karein.
2. **English rozana:** Vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms), sentence completion aur comprehension par roz 45 minute dein — MCQs ka sab se bara hissa English/Verbal ka hai.
3. **Essay ki practice:** Haftay me 2 essays likhein — argument aur narrative dono styles ki mashq karein, English ya Urdu jis me mazboot hon.
4. **Quantitative roz:** Arithmetic, algebra aur mental mathematics ke sawal roz hal karein — formula sheet banayen aur revise karte rahein.
5. **Subject-specific 30 MCQs:** Apne Intermediate ke subjects (Physics/Chemistry/Maths ya apne stream ke) ke concepts revise karein — ye 30 marks aksar faisla-kun hote hain.
6. **Mock test:** Aakhri 2 hafton me pooray 140-minute ke mock tests dein taake waqt ka intezam pakka ho jaye.

## Registration Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa (etc.hec.gov.pk)

Official ETC FAQ ke mutabiq:

1. **Profile banayen:** **etc.hec.gov.pk** (webpage section) par apne **apne valid CNIC** se profile banayen — 18 se kam umar wale NADRA Juvenile Card/B-Form number istemal karein. Naam **Matric certificate ke mutabiq** likhein. Profile banne ke baad **lock** ho jati hai.
2. **Test select karein:** Log in karne ke baad dashboard par latest available test ka link nazar aayega — apne academic stream se match karti **USAT category** chunein.
3. **Form bharein aur SUBMIT karein:** Personal, academic aur contact details darj karein. **SAVE ya INCOMPLETE** par chhori hui applications consider nahi hoti — **SUBMIT** lazmi hai.
4. **Consumer number generate karein:** "My Application" section me **"Online Payment"** button dabayen — aik unique consumer number banega.
5. **Fee pay karein:** Is consumer number se fee **1Link/1Bill invoice** ke zariye pay karein — participating banks, ATM, internet/mobile banking sab chalte hain. Fee non-refundable aur non-transferable hai.
6. **Payment verify karein:** Wapas dashboard par **"Verify Online Payment"** click karein — status **"Submitted"** me badal jayega. Is step ke baghair application mukammal nahi hoti.
7. **Roll number slip:** Test se taqreeban **aik hafta pehle** ETC portal par apne account se download karein.

**Test day par:** Printed slip + **asal CNIC** lazmi (under-18: asal B-Form/Juvenile Card). **Mobile phone, calculator, recording devices** sakht mana hain; question booklet ki tasveer lena ya share karna jurm hai.

**Madad chahiye?** ETC helpline: **0334-111-9432** / **051-111-119-432**, ya **onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk**.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Students Karte Hain

1. **Application SAVE par chhor dena:** Submit dabaye baghair registration mukammal nahi hoti — sab se aam ghalti.
2. **Fee pay karke verify na karna:** 1Bill se pay karke **"Verify Online Payment"** na dabayen to application atki rehti hai.
3. **Ghalat category chunna:** Submit ke baad category change nahi hoti — university ki required category pehle confirm karein ([HEC recognized universities check](/guides/hec-recognized-universities-check) se university ki status bhi dekh lein).
4. **Doosre ke CNIC par apply karna:** Apne CNIC/B-Form par hi registration hogi — profile lock hone ke baad tabdeeli nahi hoti.
5. **Centre ghalat chunna:** Submit ke baad test city change nahi hoti (bohat kam candidates hone par ETC qareebi centre muntaqil kar sakta hai).
6. **Deadline ka intezar karna:** Closing date ke aakhri ghanton me portal par rush hota hai — 2-3 din pehle mukammal kar lein. Consumer numbers registration deadline par **expire** ho jate hain.
7. **Sample paper dekhe baghair jana:** HEC ka official sample paper paper ka asal andaz batata hai — tayyari usi se shuru karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**USAT ki fee kitni hai aur kaise pay hoti hai?**
HEC/ETC apni public pages par fee ki rakam publish nahi karta — **exact amount sirf ETC portal me application ke waqt** generate hone wale 1Link/1Bill consumer-number invoice par nazar aata hai. Payment ka tareeqa official hai: "My Application" me "Online Payment" se consumer number banayen, use **1Link/1Bill** se bank, ATM ya mobile/internet banking se pay karein, phir dashboard par **"Verify Online Payment"** lazmi click karein. Fee non-refundable aur non-transferable hai.

**USAT me passing marks kitne hain?**
HEC/ETC ki official documents me koi public passing threshold nahi di gayi. Admission ka faisla har university apne merit/cut-off par karti hai — apni target university ka required score us ke admission office ya website se confirm karein. Paper kul 100 marks ka hota hai (75 MCQs + 25 essay).

**USAT ka result kitne arse valid hai?**
Official USAT ad ke mutabiq USAT score **1 saal** tak valid hai (HEC undergraduate scholarships ke liye bhi isi arse me istemal hota hai).

**Kya result awaiting students USAT de sakte hain?**
Ji. Official ad ke mutabiq HSSC/12-year equivalent **pass ya exam de kar result awaiting** dono apply kar sakte hain. Admission ke waqt university ki result wali shart pura karna hogi.

**USAT saal me kitni dafa hota hai?**
HEC ki official FAQ ke mutabiq ETC ke tests **quarterly** hote hain. Haaliya cycle ka portal 2 October 2026 ko band hua aur test 25 October 2026 ko hai — agle cycle ki dates ke liye **etc.hec.gov.pk** aur HEC ka announcement page check karein.

**USAT aur HAT me kya farq hai?**
**USAT** undergraduate (BS) admission ke liye hai (12 saal taleem ke baad), jabke **HAT** MS/MPhil aur PhD admission aur HEC scholarships ke liye hai (16 saal taleem ke baad). Dono ETC leta hai lekin categories aur maqsad alag hain.
