## Pehli Baat: Kaunsa Passport Lena Hai, Ye Faisla Pehle Kar Lein

Pakistan me passport banwane se pehle aksar log seedha office chale jate hain aur wahan pohnch kar pata chalta hai ke unhon ne galat category ya galat validity ka form bhar diya hai. Waqt zaya hone se bachne ke liye **teen faislay ghar beth kar kar lein**:

1. **Kaunsa type:** Aaj kal Pakistan me do qisam ke passport jari ho rahe hain — **Machine Readable Passport (MRP)** aur **e-passport** (jis me electronic chip hoti hai). E-passport zyada mehfooz hai lekin fee lagbhag dugni hai. Jo log saal me aik-do dafa travel karte hain, un ke liye MRP kaafi hai; jo aksar safar karte hain ya lambay arsay ke liye soch rahe hain, un ke liye e-passport behtar hai.
2. **Kitne saal ki validity:** **15 saal ya us se zyada umar** ke afrad (15 se 18 saal ke naujawan bhi isi me shamil hain) **5 saal ya 10 saal** ki validity chun sakte hain. 10 saal wala option aik dafa me zyada fee leta hai lekin har 5 saal baad renewal ki pareshani se bacha leta hai. **Pabandi sirf 15 saal se kam umar ke bachon par hai — un ke liye sirf 5 saal ki validity milti hai**, ye DGIP ka qanoon hai.
3. **Kitne pages:** **36 pages** aam musafiron ke liye kaafi hain. Jo log mulazmat, karobar ya parhai ke liye aksar bahir jate hain, un ke liye **72 pages** behtar hai. **100 pages** wala passport bohat zyada safar karne walon ke liye hai.

Ye teenon faislay fee par asar dalte hain, is liye fee table parhne se pehle in ka faisla kar lein.

**Agar bahir parhai ya nokri ke liye ja rahe hain:** Passport ke sath aksar degree ki attestation bhi lazmi hoti hai — [HEC Degree Attestation guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) me poora online process aur fee di hui hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kaunsi Category Me Kya Chahiye

Kagzaat na-mukammal hona sab se aam wajah hai jis ki bina par logon ka aik din zaya ho jata hai. Apni category ke mutabiq ye kagzaat tayyar kar lein:

**Naya passport (pehli dafa, adult):**

- Asal CNIC aur us ki aik photocopy
- Walid ya walida ka CNIC (sirf pehli dafa apply karne walon ke liye tasdeeq ke liye manga jata hai)
- Nikah-nama (agar shadi-shuda hain aur spouse ka naam darj karwana hai)
- Fee ki raseed (online ya bank se ada ki hui)

**Passport renewal (purana passport expire ya expire hone wala):**

- Asal CNIC aur photocopy
- Purana passport (chahe expire ho chuka ho)
- Agar naam, walid ka naam ya koi aur maloomat tabdeel karni hai to mutaliqa saboot (nikah-nama, court order waghera)

**Bache ka passport (15 saal se kam):**

- Bache ka **B-Form** (NADRA se bana hua)
- Walid ya walida me se aik ka CNIC (jo bache ke saath office aye ga)
- Agar aik walid ghair-mulki hai to mutaliqa additional documents

**15 se 18 saal ke naujawan ka passport:**

- Is umar me CNIC nahi bana hota, is liye bache ka **B-Form (CRC)** le kar jayein
- Walid ya walida ka CNIC (jo saath office aye ga)
- Validity 5 saal ya 10 saal — dono options dastiyab hain (upar wali tafseel dekhein)

**Khoya ya chori hua passport (duplicate):**

- **Police FIR** ki copy — ye lazmi hai, is ke baghair duplicate passport jari nahi hota
- Asal CNIC
- Pehle loss par fee **dugni** ada karni parti hai

**Sarkari mulazim hon to:** apne department se **NOC (No Objection Certificate)** bhi darkar hota hai.

> Mashwara: tamam kagzaat ki photocopy ghar se karwa kar le jayein. Office ke bahir wali photocopy shops par lambi line hoti hai aur qeemat bhi zyada hoti hai.

## Passport Fees 2026: Mukammal Table

Fees ka inhisaar teen cheezon par hai: passport ki qisam (MRP ya e-passport), pages ki tadad, aur processing speed (normal ya urgent). Ye fees Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGIP) ke published fee chart ke mutabiq hain — **21 October 2026 ko dobara verify ki gayi hain**. Fees waqt ke saath badal sakti hain, is liye adaigi se pehle [dgip.gov.pk](https://dgip.gov.pk/) par zaroor tasdeeq kar lein.

**Machine Readable Passport (MRP) fees:**

| Pages | Validity | Normal | Urgent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 36 | 5 saal | Rs 4,500 | Rs 7,500 |
| 36 | 10 saal | Rs 6,700 | Rs 11,200 |
| 72 | 5 saal | Rs 8,200 | Rs 13,500 |
| 72 | 10 saal | Rs 12,400 | Rs 20,200 |
| 100 | 5 saal | Rs 9,000 | Rs 18,000 |
| 100 | 10 saal | Rs 13,500 | Rs 27,000 |

**E-passport fees (chip wala):**

| Pages | Validity | Normal | Urgent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 36 | 5 saal | Rs 9,000 | Rs 15,000 |
| 36 | 10 saal | Rs 13,500 | Rs 22,500 |
| 72 | 5 saal | Rs 16,500 | Rs 27,000 |
| 72 | 10 saal | Rs 24,750 | Rs 40,500 |

**Fee se mutaliq ahem baten:**

- Pehli dafa passport **gum** hone par duplicate ki fee **dugni** hoti hai (masalan 36 pages, 5 saal normal ka Rs 9,000).
- Fee ab **online ada** ki ja sakti hai — DGIP ki Passport Fee Asaan app ya bank ke zariye. Agent ko extra paise dene ki zaroorat nahi.
- DGIP ki **no-refund policy** hai: aik dafa application submit ho jaye to fee wapas nahi milti. Is liye pages, validity aur speed ka faisla adaigi se pehle pakka kar lein.
- E-passport par **fast track** ki sahulat dastiyab nahi hai.

## Office Ja Kar Apply Karne Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

DGIP ki official website ke mutabiq Regional Passport Office (RPO) me application ke **paanch marhalay** hain. In ko samajh kar jayein to kaam asaan ho jata hai:

1. **Fee verification, tasveer aur token:** Sab se pehle apni fee ki raseed verify karwayein, office me aap ki digital tasveer li jati hai aur aap ko aik **token number** milta hai. Ye token sambhal kar rakhein — is se hi aap ki application track hoti hai.
2. **Biometric data:** Aap ke **fingerprints** (ungliyon ke nishan) liye jate hain. Hath saaf hon aur mehndi ya rang na laga ho to behtar hai.
3. **Data entry:** Aap ki tamam maloomat computer me darj ki jati hai — naam, walid ka naam, pata, aur mobile number ya email. **Mobile number ghalat na likhwayein**, kyunke isi par aap ko passport ki status updates SMS ke zariye milti hain (9988 se).
4. **Record verification:** Office apne record se aap ki maloomat ki tasdeeq karta hai.
5. **Interview aur faisla:** Akhir me Assistant Director se mukhtasir interview hota hai aur application ka faisla hota hai.

Interview se ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi — ye sirf ye tasdeeq karne ke liye hota hai ke maloomat durust hain. Seedhe jawab dein aur kagzaat saath rakhein.

## Ghar Bethe Renewal: Online Tareeqa

Agar aap ka passport **renew** hona hai (naya nahi banana), to aap ko office jane ki zaroorat nahi — poora kaam online ho sakta hai:

1. DGIP ka online passport portal kholein aur account banayein.
2. Renewal ki category chunein aur form me apni maloomat bharein — maujooda aur mustaqil pata dono.
3. Tasveer aur kagzaat upload karein. Fingerprints ke liye **NADRA ki Pak-ID mobile app** istemal hoti hai — hath se fingerprint dene ki zaroorat nahi.
4. Apne qareebi passport office aur delivery ka option chunein.
5. Credit ya debit card se fee ada karein aur raseed mehfooz kar lein.

**Online renewal kin ke liye nahi hai:** pehli dafa adult passport, passport ki cancellation, aur teesri dafa gum hua passport — in ke liye office jana lazmi hai.

> Hoshiyar rahein: sirf DGIP ki official website se apply karein. Internet par kai jaali websites hain jo extra fee le kar "passport service" ke naam par dhoka deti hain. Hamesha website ka address ghor se check karein.

## Kitna Waqt Lagega: Normal, Urgent Aur Fast Track

- **Normal:** taqreeban **21 working days**. Ye sab se sasta option hai — agar aap ko jaldi nahi to yehi chunein.
- **Urgent:** taqreeban **5 working days** me print ho jata hai, lekin delivery me kuch din aur lag sakte hain. Fee normal se taqreeban 65% zyada hai.
- **Fast Track:** sirf **2 working days** — lekin ye sahulat sirf makhsoos **47 shehron** me dastiyab hai aur fee sab se zyada hai. Note karein ke e-passport par fast track nahi milta.

**Amali mashwara:** agar aap ki flight ko aik mahina ya zyada waqt hai to normal hi kaafi hai aur hazaaron rupay bachtay hain. Sirf waqai emergency ho (masalan achanak medical ya family emergency) to urgent ya fast track chunein. Aur haan — application normal se urgent me **tabdeel** bhi ki ja sakti hai, farq ki fee ada karke.

## Kahan Apply Karein: Offices Aur Helpline

Pakistan bhar me **Regional Passport Offices (RPOs)** hain — aap apne qareebi office me apply kar sakte hain. Is ke ilawa DGIP ne kuch sahulaten bhi di hain:

- **24/7 service:** Lahore aur Karachi ke **NADRA Mega Centers** me passport counters 24 ghante khulay hain, aur **RPO Islamabad** me bhi round-the-clock service hai. Din me waqt na milne walon ke liye ye behtreen option hai.
- **Executive Passport Offices:** bare shehron me Executive Passport Offices hain jahan bheer kam hoti hai (kuch additional service charges lag sakte hain).
- **Helpline:** 051-111-344-777 (national) — application ki status ya shikayat ke liye.
- **SMS updates:** data entry ke waqt apna sahi mobile number dein, aap ko 9988 se status updates milti rahengi.
- **Online shikayat:** overseas Pakistanis apni shikayat complaints.passport@interior.gov.pk par bhej sakte hain.

## 7 Ghaltiyan Jo Log Aksar Karte Hain

1. **Ghalat category ka intekhab:** fee ada karne ke baad category tabdeel karna mushkil aur mehnga parta hai. Pehle faisla karein, phir paise dein.
2. **Na-mukammal kagzaat:** sab se aam ghalti. Ghar se checklist bana kar jayein — is guide ki list upar di gayi hai.
3. **Ghalat mobile number:** aik digit ghalat ho to status SMS nahi milte aur aap andhere me rehte hain.
4. **Agenton par bharosa:** office ke bahir khare agent "kaam jaldi karwane" ke naam par hazaaron le lete hain. Poora process aap khud kar sakte hain — is guide me sab likha hai.
5. **Purani tasveer ya ghalat format (online):** online apply karte waqt portal par di gayi tasveer ki requirements parhein, warna form wapas bhej diya jata hai.
6. **Aakhri waqt me apply karna:** normal me 21 working days lagte hain — flight se aik hafta pehle apply karna aur phir urgent ki zyada fee deni par jati hai — ye ghalti aap ko mehngi par sakti hai. Pehle se plan karein.
7. **Fee ki raseed gum karna:** adaigi ki raseed ya reference number mehfooz rakhein — yehi aap ka saboot hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya me baghair CNIC ke passport banwa sakta hun?**
Nahi. Adult ke liye valid CNIC lazmi hai. Bache ke liye B-Form chahiye hota hai.

**Passport renew karne ki fee naye passport jitni hai?**
Ji haan — renewal ki fee naye passport wali hi hoti hai. Sirf gumshuda passport ki duplicate fee dugni hoti hai.

**Kya me apne shehar se bahir kisi aur office se apply kar sakta hun?**
Ji, aap Pakistan ke kisi bhi Regional Passport Office se apply kar sakte hain. Online renewal me to office ka intekhab aap khud karte hain.

**Bache ka passport kitne saal ke liye banta hai?**
15 saal se kam umar ke bachon ke liye sirf 5 saal ki validity ka passport jari hota hai.

**Passport me ghalat naam ya tareekh-e-pedaish darj ho jaye to?**
Renewal ya modification ke waqt durust maloomat ke saboot (CNIC, B-Form, court order) ke saath correction karwayi ja sakti hai.

**Kya fee bank me jama karwani lazmi hai?**
Nahi — ab fee online bhi ada ki ja sakti hai (Passport Fee Asaan app ya bank channels ke zariye). Lekin adaigi hamesha official tareeqe se karein.

**Urgent passport kitne din me mil jata hai?**
Official tor par 5 working days me print hota hai, lekin delivery same shehar ya door ke shehar ke hisab se kuch din aur le sakti hai. Total 7 se 10 din ka andaza rakhein.

**Overseas Pakistani passport kaise renew karein?**
DGIP ke online portal se mukammal online renewal ho sakti hai — fingerprints Pak-ID app se, fee card se, aur passport ghar deliver ho jata hai. Sirf pehli dafa adult passport ya teesri dafa gumshuda passport ke liye consulate jana parta hai.

**How to apply for a passport in Pakistan?**

You can apply at your nearest passport office or start online at onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk. Pehle apni category ke mutabiq fee ada karein, phir asal CNIC (ya 18 saal se kam ke liye B-Form) aur baqi kagzaat ke saath office jayein — mukammal step-by-step tareeqa, fees aur documents ki list upar is guide me di gayi hai.
