## Pehli Baat: Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Kya Hai

**Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG)** Punjab hukumat ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet un logon ko **15 lakh rupay tak ka bila sood (interest-free) loan** milta hai jin ke paas apna plot hai lekin ghar banane ke paise nahi. Ye Chief Minister Punjab ka flagship program hai, jisay **Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA)** chalati hai aur **Housing, Urban Development and Public Health Engineering Department (HUD&PHED)** is ki nigraani karta hai. Program ka hadaf hai ke **saade chaar saal me 100,000 ghar** Punjab bhar me banaye jayein, taa ke kiraye par rehne wale ya kachchi bastiyon me rehne wale khandaan apne ghar ke malik ban sakein.

Is program ke **teen tareeqe (execution models)** hain: sarkaari zameen par ghar banana, private housing schemes me sasti rehaish, aur — jis par ye guide hai — apne plot par ghar banane ke liye **bila sood loan**. Loan wale hisse ke liye **70,000 units** rakhe gaye hain. Is ka matlab saaf hai: ye scheme plot khareedne ke liye nahi hai. Agar aap ke paas plot nahi hai to ye loan aap ke liye nahi — plot wale logon ke liye hai, aur plot bhi aap ke apne naam par hona chahiye.

Sab se ahem baat pehle samajh lein: ye loan **naqd cash ki surat me aik sath nahi** milta. Raqam **qiston (tranches) me** milti hai, taameer ke marhalon ke mutabiq — bunyadein, deewarein, chhat — taa ke paisa asal taameer hi par lage. Aur is par **koi sood nahi**: aap jitna lete hain, utna hi wapas karte hain. Wazeer-e-Aala ki taraf se saaf hidayat hai ke qist **14,000 rupay se zyada na ho**, taa ke aik aam khandaan bhi aasani se ada kar sake.

## Loan Ki Teen Categories: 5, 10 Aur 15 Lakh

Portal par loan ki **teen categories** dikhayi jati hain. Apply karte waqt aap ko apni zaroorat ke mutabiq aik category chunni hoti hai. Teeno par sharait aik jaisi hain — **0% markup, 7 saal ki muddat** — farq sirf raqam aur maqsad ka hai:

| Category | Loan ki raqam | Kis kaam ke liye |
|---|---|---|
| **Category 1** | **Rs 5 lakh** | Washroom / buniyadi sahulat ki tameer |
| **Category 2** | **Rs 10 lakh** | Mojooda ghar par upper portion / mazeed manzil banana |
| **Category 3** | **Rs 15 lakh** | Ghar ki marammat ya tausee, ya apne plot par naya ghar banana |

Aksar log **Category 3** ke liye apply karte hain, kyun ke isi me naye ghar ki poori tameer hoti hai. Lekin agar aap ka ghar pehle se bana hua hai aur sirf aik washroom ya aik kamra barhana hai to chhoti category chunna behtar hai — jitna kam loan, utni kam qist aur utni jaldi chhutkara. Category chunte waqt ye bhi yaad rakhein ke loan ki raqam aap ki zaroorat aur portal par darj sharaait ke mutabiq tay hoti hai; form bharte waqt jo category aap chunein, usi ke mutabiq aap ki darkhwast aage barhegi.

Ye bhi note karein ke is scheme ke ilawa Punjab me **Apni Zameen Apna Ghar** naam ki aik alag scheme bhi hai jis me **muft plot** milta hai — us ka portal alag hai (azag.punjab.gov.pk). Dono ko mila kar ghalat fehmi na karein: Apni Chhat Apna Ghar **loan** deta hai, plot nahi; plot aap ka pehle se hona chahiye.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Punjab Portal par darj official sharaait ke mutabiq Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan ke liye ye **6 shartein** hain. Apply karne se pehle in sab ko aik aik kar ke check kar lein:

**1. Khandaan ka sarbarah:** Darkhwast guzar NADRA record ke mutabiq apne **nucleus (chhote) khandaan ka sarbarah** ho. Ye shart is liye hai taa ke aik hi khandaan se kayi darkhwastein na aayein.

**2. Punjab ka rehne wala:** Darkhwast guzar apne [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ke mutabiq **Punjab ka mustaqil rehne wala** ho. Dosre subon ke rehne walon ke liye ye Punjab wali scheme nahi hai.

**3. Apne naam par plot:** Darkhwast guzar online darkhwast jama karane ki tareekh par **plot ka wahid malik aur qabiz** ho — **shehri ilaqon me 5 marla ya is se kam**, aur **dehati ilaqon me 10 marla tak**. Mushtarka (joint family) zameen tab tak nahi chalegi jab tak wo aap ke naam par muntaqil na ho jaye. Plot ke baghair darkhwast aage nahi barhti.

**4. Saaf record:** Darkhwast guzar ka **koi criminal record na ho** — riyasat mukhalif, samaj mukhalif ya sangin jurm me saza yafta na ho.

**5. Loan defaulter na ho:** Darkhwast guzar kisi bhi bank ya mali idare ka **loan defaulter na ho**. Ye check aap ke credit record se hota hai.

**6. NSER / PMT score:** Darkhwast guzar ka khandaan **National Socio-Economic Registry (NSER)** me registered ho aur us ka **PMT score 60 ya kam** ho. Ye score aap ki mali haalat batata hai — scheme kam aamdani wale khandaanon ke liye hai.

In ke ilawa aik ahem baat: darkhwast guzar ke naam par **pehle se koi rehne wala ghar ya residential property registered na ho**. Agar aap ke naam par pehle se ghar hai to aap is scheme ke mustahiq nahi. Agar aap ka [CNIC expired](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) hai to pehle usay renew karwa lein, kyun ke expired CNIC par verification wahi ruk jayegi.

## Loan Ki Sharait Aur Fees

| Cheez | Detail |
|---|---|
| Loan ki hadd | **Rs 15 lakh tak** (bila sood loan model) |
| Markup (sood) | **0%** — mukammal bila sood, jitna lein utna wapas |
| Kul muddat | **7 saal** (official program page ke mutabiq) |
| Mahana qist | **Taqreeban Rs 14,000** (Wazeer-e-Aala ki hidayat: qist 14,000 se zyada na ho; hatmi schedule approval ke waqt portal par milta hai) |
| Grace period | **3 mahine** — pehli qist chauthe mahine se deni hoti hai |
| Darkhwast ki fee | **Muft** — koi processing fee nahi, koi agent ko paisa nahi dena |
| Raqam milne ka tareeqa | **Qiston (tranches) me**, taameer ke marhalon ke mutabiq |
| Portal | **acag.punjab.gov.pk** |
| Helpline | **0800-09100** (toll-free) |

Do baatein khaas tor par yaad rakhein. Pehli: **darkhwast bilkul muft hai**. Jo shakhs aap se "registration fee", "file charges" ya "approval ke paise" mange, wo dhoka de raha hai — official channels sirf portal, helpline aur aap ke zila ke DC/AC office ya PHATA desk hain. Dosri: qist ki raqam loan ki raqam aur muddat par munhasir hoti hai; upar di gayi 14,000 ki raqam 15 lakh ke loan ke liye elan kardah taqreebi raqam hai, hatmi qist ka schedule aap ko approval ke waqt portal par mil jayega.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Online form bharne se pehle ye kagzaat tayyar rakh lein, taa ke form adhoora na reh jaye:

![Apni Chhat Apna Ghar ke kagzaat: CNIC, plot ki malkiyat ke papers aur ghar ka plan desk par](/images/apni-chhat-apna-ghar-loan-apply-online-2.jpg)

**1. Valid CNIC:** Apna aur khandaan ke sarbarah ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) — yehi aap ki pehchan aur Punjab ki rehaish ka saboot hai. Expired CNIC pehle renew karwa lein.

**2. Plot ki malkiyat ka saboot:** Zameen ki malkiyat ka kagaz — **Fard-e-Malkiat** ya allotment letter — jis se saaf ho ke plot aap ke naam par hai aur aap hi us ke qabiz hain. [Fard-e-Malkiat nikalwane ka tareeqa](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) alag guide me parh lein. Plot par koi muqadma, stay order ya rehan (mortgage) nahi hona chahiye.

**3. Apne naam par registered mobile number:** Mobile number **aap ke apne CNIC par registered** ho — isi par SMS confirmation aur status ki khabar aati hai. Kisi rishtedar ke naam wali SIM se apply karenge to verification wahi ruk jayegi.

**4. NSER / PMT record:** Aap ke khandaan ka NSER me record aur PMT score — ye portal khud check karta hai, lekin agar aap ka record NSER me nahi hai to pehle [BISP 8171](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) ke zariye apni registration aur PMT score check kar lein.

**5. Income ka saboot (agar manga jaye):** Portal form me aamdani ki maloomat bhi mangi ja sakti hai. Agar aap ke paas [income certificate](/guides/income-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) hai to wo bhi tayyar rakhein — manga gaya to foran upload kar sakein.

Form me jo kagzaat mange jayein, un ki **saaf tasaveer ya scan** upload karein. Dhundli ya aadhi tasveer ki wajah se verification ruk jati hai aur darkhwast wapas bhej di jati hai.

## Step-by-Step: Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

Poora tareeqa online hai aur ghar baithe hota hai. Ye steps follow karein:

**Step 1 — Portal kholein:** Apne mobile ya computer par **acag.punjab.gov.pk** kholein. Sirf isi official portal par apply karein — Google par milne wali dosri websites, WhatsApp forwards ya "agent" wale links se hargiz apply na karein.

**Step 2 — Citizen Registration Form kholein:** Portal par **Citizen Registration Form** par click karein. Yehi form aap ki darkhwast hai.

**Step 3 — Apni maloomat bharein:** Apna naam, CNIC number, walid ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish aur apne naam par registered mobile number darj karein. Maloomat **CNIC ke mutabiq bilkul durust** likhein — aik harf ki ghalti bhi verification me rukawat ban sakti hai.

**Step 4 — Mazeed maloomat bharein:** Khandaan ki maloomat, rehaish ka pata aur aamdani se mutaliq sawalon ke jawab dein. Jo sawal pooche jayein, un ke sachay jawab likhein.

**Step 5 — Zameen ki maloomat bharein:** Apne plot ki tafseel darj karein — zila, tehsil, mauza, plot ka size (marla) aur malkiyat ka saboot (Fard-e-Malkiat waghera). Yahi wo hissa hai jahan plot ki shart check hoti hai.

**Step 6 — Loan category chunein:** Apni zaroorat ke mutabiq category chunein — 5 lakh (washroom), 10 lakh (upper portion) ya 15 lakh (naya ghar / marammat).

**Step 7 — Password banayein aur form jama karayein:** Aik mazboot password banayein, form aik dafa phir parh kar jama karayein. Jama karane ke baad aap ko **SMS ke zariye confirmation** milegi.

**Step 8 — Status check karte rahein:** Apni darkhwast ka status portal par apne account se ya SMS se check karte rahein. Koi kagaz ya maloomat mazeed mangi jaye to foran jama karayein.

Agar online apply karna mushkil lage to aap apne zila ke **Deputy Commissioner (DC) ya Assistant Commissioner (AC) office** ya **PHATA facilitation desk** par ja kar bhi madad le sakte hain. Helpline **0800-09100** par call kar ke bhi rehnumai mil jati hai.

## Loan Milne Ke Baad: Qistein Aur Taameer

Darkhwast manzoor hone ke baad kaam yun chalta hai:

![Online apply ke baad laptop par ghar ke loan ki manzoori aur confirmation](/images/apni-chhat-apna-ghar-loan-apply-online-3.jpg)

**1. Selection:** Eligible darkhwaston ki selection **transparent tareeqe** se hoti hai — har zila ka quota tay hai, is liye jald apply karna faida deta hai. Selection ki khabar aap ko SMS aur portal dashboard par milegi.

**2. Tranche-wise disbursement:** Loan aik sath nahi milta. Raqam **taameer ke marhalon ke mutabiq qiston me** milti hai — pehle bunyadon ke liye, phir deewaron aur chhat ke liye. Har marhala mukammal hone par agla hissa milta hai. Yehi wajah hai ke raqam ka istemal sirf ghar ki tameer par hota hai.

**3. Grace period:** Pehle **3 mahine grace period** hai — is dauran qist nahi deni. Pehli qist **chauthe mahine** se shuru hoti hai.

**4. Mahana qist:** Qist taqreeban **Rs 14,000 mahana** hoti hai (15 lakh ke loan par), jo aik chhote ghar ke kiraye ke barabar hai — farq sirf ye hai ke qist khatam hone par ghar aap ka apna hota hai. Hatmi qist ka schedule aap ko approval ke waqt mil jayega.

**5. Qist ki adaigi:** Qist waqt par ada karein. Qist late hone par aap ka record kharab hota hai aur agle marhale ki raqam ruk sakti hai. Qist ki adaigi ka tareeqa (bank ya mobile) approval ke waqt bataya jata hai.

**6. Taameer ki nigrani:** PHATA taameer ke mayaar ki nigrani karta hai. Ghar ka naqsha aur tameer PHATA ki hidayat ke mutabiq honi chahiye, taa ke agle marhale ki raqam waqt par mile.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Rukwa Deti Hain

Ye ghaltiyan roz hoti hain aur darkhwastein inhi ki wajah se rukti hain — in se bachein:

**1. Plot kisi aur ke naam par hai:** Sab se bari ghalti. Plot aap ke naam par nahi to darkhwast aage nahi barhegi. Pehle plot apne naam par muntaqil karayein, phir apply karein.

**2. Ghalat portal ya agent ko paisa dena:** Sirf **acag.punjab.gov.pk** official hai. Jo website ya shakhs aap se fee mange, wo fake hai. Apply karna muft hai.

**3. CNIC ya mobile number ki ghalti:** CNIC number ya mobile number ghalat likhna — verification SMS nahi aayega aur darkhwast atak jayegi. Form jama karane se pehle har harf check karein.

**4. Dhundli kagzaat upload karna:** Fard-e-Malkiat ya CNIC ki dhundli tasveer upload karne se verification reject ho jati hai. Saaf, poori aur roshan tasveer dein.

**5. PMT score ki ghaflat:** Agar aap ka khandaan NSER me registered nahi ya PMT score 60 se zyada hai to darkhwast manzoor nahi hogi. Apply karne se pehle apna PMT record check kar lein.

**6. Aik se zyada darkhwastein:** Aik shakhs ya aik khandaan ki taraf se aik hi darkhwast di jati hai. Dobara form bharna aap ki pehli darkhwast ko bhi kharab kar sakta hai.

**7. Ghalat category chunna:** Sirf washroom banana hai aur 15 lakh ki category chun li — aisi ghalti se darkhwast ki jaanch me waqt zaya hota hai. Apni asal zaroorat wali category chunein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan kitna milta hai?**

Is scheme me 15 lakh rupay tak ka bila sood loan milta hai. Loan ki teen categories hain — 5 lakh (washroom), 10 lakh (upper portion) aur 15 lakh (naya ghar ya marammat). Markup 0% hai, muddat 7 saal hai aur mahana qist taqreeban 14,000 rupay hoti hai.

**Kya loan ke liye plot hona lazmi hai?**

Ji haan, bilkul lazmi hai. Darkhwast guzar ko plot ka wahid malik aur qabiz hona chahiye — shehri ilaqon me 5 marla ya is se kam, aur dehati ilaqon me 10 marla tak. Plot ke baghair ye loan nahi milta, kyun ke ye scheme plot khareedne ke liye nahi, us par ghar banane ke liye hai.

**PMT score 60 se zyada ho to kya apply ho sakta hai?**

Punjab Portal par darj sharaait ke mutabiq khandaan ka NSER me registered hona aur PMT score 60 ya kam hona lazmi hai. Apply karne se pehle apna PMT score check kar lein — portal ki form me bhi ye shart darj hoti hai aur wahi hatmi faisla karta hai.

**Apply karne ki fee kitni hai?**

Koi fee nahi — darkhwast bilkul muft hai. Na registration ki fee hai, na processing fee. Jo shakhs aap se fee mange wo agent ya dhoka hai. Sirf official portal acag.punjab.gov.pk par apply karein ya helpline 0800-09100 par rabta karein.

**Loan milne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**

Is ka koi fixed waqt nahi hai. Darkhwast ke baad verification, selection aur phir tranche-wise disbursement hoti hai, aur waqt aap ke zila ke quota aur verification ki raftaar par munhasir hai. Aap apni darkhwast ka status portal par apne account se ya SMS se check kar sakte hain.
