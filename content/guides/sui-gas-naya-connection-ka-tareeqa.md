## Naya Sui Gas Connection Kya Hai Aur 2026 Me Kya Badla Hai

**Sui gas ka naya connection** wo gharailu (domestic) gas connection hai jo Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited (SNGPL) ya Sui Southern Gas Company (SSGC) aap ke ghar tak pipeline bichha kar aur meter laga kar deti hai. Is ke baghair ghar me chulha, geezer aur heater chalana mumkin nahi hota, is liye naya ghar banane ya naye ilaqe me shift hone par ye sab se pehle darkar hone wali sahulaton me se aik hai.

2026 me sab se bari tabdeeli ye hai ke **naye domestic connections ab sirf RLNG tariff par mil rahe hain**. RLNG ka matlab hai Re-Gasified Liquefied Natural Gas — yani daraamad shuda gas jo dobara gas ki shakal me badal kar pipeline me di jati hai. Hukoomat ne 2025 me naye connections par lagi pabandi me narmi ki aur RLNG ki bunyad par naye connections dobara khol diye. Is ka seedha matlab ye hai ke aap ko ab purani system gas (sasti, mulki gas) wala connection nahi milega — naya connection RLNG rate par hoga, jo aam tor par system gas se mehnga hota hai. Apply karne se pehle ye farq samajhna zaroori hai taake baad me bill dekh kar hairani na ho.

Aik aur ahem baat: jo log pabandi se pehle system gas ke liye apply kar chuke the aur un ki application process nahi hui thi, un ki purani position khatam ho chuki hai. Unhe bhi RLNG ki nayi application deni hogi — chahe normal merit par ya fast-track par. Jo log pabandi se pehle urgent fee ya demand notice ada kar chuke the, unhe tarjeeh di jati hai, lekin unhe bhi RLNG ka undertaking aur contract sign karna hota hai aur security ka farq (differential) ada karna hota hai.

## SNGPL Ya SSGC: Aap Ke Sheher Ki Company Kaun Si Hai

Pakistan me gas ki taqseem do bari companies karti hain, aur ghalat company ke portal par apply karna waqt zaya karne ke mutradif hai:

**SNGPL (Sui Northern Gas Pipelines Limited):** Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Islamabad, Gilgit-Baltistan aur Azad Jammu-o-Kashmir ke shehron me gas deti hai. Is ki official website **sngpl.com.pk** hai aur mobile app ka naam **ConnectOn** hai. Helpline **1199** hai.

**SSGC (Sui Southern Gas Company):** Sindh aur Balochistan me gas deti hai. Is ki official website **ssgc.com.pk** hai.

Is guide me tafseel SNGPL ke official elan ki bunyad par di gayi hai, kyun ke SNGPL ne apni website par RLNG connections ki mukammal fee aur sharaait shaya ki hui hain. Agar aap Karachi, Hyderabad ya Quetta me rehte hain to SSGC ke portal aur daftar se apne ilaqe ki fee aur tareeqa confirm kar lein — bunyadi kagzaat aur steps aksar aik jaise hote hain, lekin fee aur form mukhtalif ho sakta hai.

Ye bhi zehen me rakhein ke connection sirf wahan lag sakta hai jahan aap ki gali ya mohalle me gas pipeline mojood ho. Agar aap ke ghar ke samne pipeline nahi hai to pehle network extension ki darkhwast deni hoti hai, jo aik alag aur lamba amal hai. Apply karne se pehle parosi ka gas bill dekh lein — us se ye bhi pata chal jata hai ke ilaqe me gas mojood hai aur aap ke connection ke liye konsa meter ya line reference istemal hoga.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Apply Se Pehle Ye Sab Tayyar Rakhein

Application rukne ki sab se bari wajah adhoore kagzaat hote hain. Neeche di gayi list SNGPL ke tareeqa-e-kar aur aam darkhwaston ki bunyad par banayi gayi hai — form bharne se pehle in sab ki saaf scans ya tasveeren mobile me rakh lein:

- **Applicant ka CNIC (dono taraf ki copy)** — shanakht aur malkiyat ki tasdeeq ke liye lazmi hai. Agar aap ka [CNIC banwana ya renewal](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) baqi hai to pehle woh mukammal kar lein.
- **Malkiyat ka saboot** — registry, allotment letter ya [fard-e-malkiyat](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa). Jo kagaz aap ke paas ho, wahi dein — lekin us par aap ka naam aur wohi pata hona chahiye jis par connection chahiye.
- **Parosi ka gas bill ki copy** — is se company ko aap ki gali ki pipeline aur qareebi meter ka pata chalta hai. Ye kagaz log aksar bhool jate hain aur phir application atak jati hai.
- **Mukammal tameer ka certificate (completion certificate)** — naye makaan ki surat me mutaliqa authority se jari shuda certificate aur manzoor shuda naqsha (building plan) darkar ho sakta hai.
- **Bijli ka bill (usi pate ka)** — pate ki tasdeeq ke liye. Agar aap ne abhi [bijli ka naya connection](/guides/bijli-naya-connection-meter-lagwana) lagwaya hai to us ka bill bhi kaam aa sakta hai.
- **Kirayedar hon to malik ka NOC aur kiraya nama** — kiraye ke ghar par connection malik ki ijazat ke baghair nahi lagta.
- **Housing society ka NOC (agar laagu ho)** — society ke ilaqe me society ki ijazat darkar hoti hai.
- **Do passport size tasveeren** — form aur record ke liye.
- **Rs. 100 ke stamp paper par RLNG undertaking** — ye sirf un logon ke liye hai jo pabandi se pehle urgent fee ya demand notice ada kar chuke the aur ab RLNG connection lena chahte hain. Undertaking notary public se tasdeeq shuda hona chahiye aur is ke sath RLNG contract par dastakhat karne hote hain.

Aik mashwara: tamam kagzaat ki original aur photocopy dono alag file me rakh lein, aur online apply karte waqt har scan ka naam saaf rakhein (masalan "CNIC-front", "Registry") taake ghalat file upload na ho.

## Fees Aur Charges: Kitna Kharcha Aayega

Neeche di gayi fee SNGPL ki official website par shaya shuda RLNG connection charges hain. Yehi is guide ki bunyad hai — is ke ilawa koi rakam kisi ghair-sarkari website se nahi li gayi:

| Charge | Rakam | Tafseel |
|---|---|---|
| Application jama karwane ki fee | Muft (Rs. 0) | Nayi RLNG application jama karwane par koi fee nahi hai |
| Security deposit (refundable) | Rs. 20,000 | Wapsi ke qaabil — connection khatam karne par sharaait ke mutabiq wapas milta hai |
| Service line cost (10 marla tak ka ghar) | Rs. 1,500 | Ghar tak service line bichhane ki laagat |
| Service line cost (10 marla se bara ghar) | Rs. 3,000 | Bare plot ya ghar ke liye service line ki laagat |
| Fast-track (urgent) fee | Rs. 25,000 | Sirf fast-track application ke liye, non-refundable; normal application par ye fee nahi hai |
| RLNG undertaking ke liye stamp paper | Kam az kam Rs. 100 | Sirf purane applicants ke liye jo RLNG par shift ho rahe hain; notary charges alag ho sakte hain |

Is hisab se aik normal application ka bunyadi kharcha **Rs. 21,500 (10 marla tak)** ya **Rs. 23,000 (10 marla se bara)** banta hai, jab ke fast-track application par is me Rs. 25,000 urgent fee shamil ho jati hai. Yaad rakhein ke **RLNG ka monthly bill system gas se mukhtalif aur aam tor par zyada hota hai**, is liye sirf connection ki fee nahi, balkay mustaqbil ke bill ka andaza bhi pehle se laga lein. Demand notice milne par us par likhi hui final rakam hi ada karein — kisi agent ko advance me extra rakam na dein.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa: Apply Se Meter Lagane Tak

### Step 1: Gas ki dastiyabi confirm karein

Pehle ye confirm karein ke aap ki gali me SNGPL ki pipeline mojood aur faal hai. Parosi ka gas bill is ki sab se aasan nishani hai. Pipeline na hone ki surat me pehle network extension ki darkhwast deni hogi.

### Step 2: Sahi category chunein — normal ya fast-track

Normal merit par application muft jama hoti hai aur bari (queue) ke mutabiq lagti hai. Fast-track par Rs. 25,000 urgent fee ada karni hoti hai aur application tarjeehi bunyad par process hoti hai. Jaldi chahiye to fast-track chunein, warna normal hi kafi hai — dono ka connection aik jaisa RLNG connection hota hai, farq sirf raftaar ka hai.

### Step 3: Online application ya daftar se form jama karwayen

SNGPL ki website (sngpl.com.pk) par **New Application** ka option kholein ya **ConnectOn** mobile app istemal karein. Form me apna CNIC number, mukammal pata, parosi ka bill number, plot ka size (marla) aur connection ki category darj karein. Tamam kagzaat upload karein aur form submit karne se pehle har field dobara parh lein — CNIC number ya pate me aik ghalti bhi application ko hafton rok sakti hai.

### Step 4: Application number sambhal kar rakhein

Submit hone ke baad aap ko application ya tracking number milega. Is ka screenshot lein aur diary me bhi likh lein. Isi number se aap apni application ki paishraft maloom kar sakenge.

### Step 5: Site visit aur demand notice

Company ka numainda aap ke ghar ka visit karega — pipeline ki doori, meter ki jagah aur tameer ki haalat check karega. Visit ke waqt ghar par mojood rahein aur meter lagane ki munasib jagah (hawa-dar, aasan rasai wali) pehle se tay kar lein. Visit ke baad aap ko **demand notice** jari hoga jis me security aur service line ki rakam darj hogi.

### Step 6: Demand notice ki rakam ada karein

Demand notice par bataye gaye tareeqe se (bank ya batayi gayi payment channel se) rakam waqt par ada karein. Raseed sambhal kar rakhein — ye aap ka saboot hai aur meter lagane ke waqt bhi kaam aa sakti hai.

### Step 7: Meter installation aur connection chalu

Payment ki tasdeeq ke baad company service line bichhayegi aur meter lagayegi. Meter lagne ke baad numainda gas chalu karne se pehle leakage check karega. Pehli dafa chulha jalane se pehle us ki hidayat zaroor sun lein aur ghar ke tamam afraad ko gas safety ke bunyadi usool bata dein.

## Purani Application Walon Ke Liye Kya Hukm Hai

Agar aap ne pabandi se pehle system gas ke liye apply kiya tha to ye section aap ke liye sab se ahem hai. SNGPL ke elan ke mutabiq:

- **Purani merit list ki position khatam ho chuki hai.** System gas ke liye jama shuda purani applications par naya RLNG connection khud-ba-khud nahi milega — nayi RLNG application deni hogi, normal ya fast-track merit par.
- **Urgent fee ya demand notice ada karne walon ko tarjeeh milegi.** Agar aap ne pabandi se pehle urgent fee ya demand notice ada kiya tha to aap tarjeehi bunyad par RLNG connection le sakte hain.
- **RLNG undertaking aur contract lazmi hai.** Aise applicants ko kam az kam Rs. 100 ke stamp paper par notarized undertaking dena hogi aur RLNG contract par dastakhat karne honge.
- **Security ka farq ada karna hoga.** Purani security aur nayi Rs. 20,000 security ke darmiyan farq (differential) ada karna hoga — final rakam aap ke case ke mutabiq demand notice me aayegi.
- **Purana record zaroor sath rakhein.** Purani application ka number, urgent fee ya demand notice ki raseed sambhal kar rakhein — tarjeeh ka dawa isi saboot par hoga.

Agar aap ko yaqeen nahi ke aap ka case kis category me aata hai to SNGPL helpline **1199** par apna purana application number bata kar pooch lein — daftar ke chakkar lagane se pehle phone par tasdeeq karna zyada sasta aur tez hai.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai

Naye gas connection ka waqt aik hi jawab se bayan nahi hota — ye aap ki category, ilaqe aur pipeline ki dastiyabi par munhasir hai. Normal merit par application ki bari aane me kayi mahine lag sakte hain, kyun ke applications ki tadaad bohat zyada hai aur har ilaqe me team ki capacity mehdood hoti hai. Fast-track application tarjeehi bunyad par process hoti hai, is liye us ka waqt aam tor par kam hota hai, lekin ye bhi guarantee nahi ke meter aik hi hafte me lag jayega.

Amal ke marahil ka andaza is tarah lagayen: online application aur tasdeeq kuch din me ho jati hai, site visit ilaqe ki team ki masroofiyat par munhasir hai, demand notice ke baad payment foran ho sakti hai, aur meter installation payment ki tasdeeq ke baad schedule hoti hai. Agar aap ki gali me pipeline mojood hai aur kagzaat mukammal hain to kaam sab se tez hota hai; pipeline na hone ya kagaz adhoora hone ki surat me mahine bhi lag sakte hain. Is liye apply karne ke baad har hafte apna application number se status check karte rahein aur demand notice milte hi payment me der na karein — der se payment bari se bahar bhi kar sakti hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Application Roka Deti Hain

- **Ghalat company ko apply karna:** Punjab ya Islamabad ke rehne wale SSGC ke portal par aur Sindh ke rehne wale SNGPL par apply kar dein to application aage nahi barhegi. Pehle apni company confirm karein.
- **Parosi ka bill na dena:** Ye kagaz chhota lagta hai lekin pipeline ki nishandahi isi se hoti hai. Baghair is ke application aksar wapas aa jati hai.
- **Pate ka farq:** CNIC, registry aur form par pata aik jaisa hona chahiye. Gali number ya makaan number ka farq bhi verification rok deta hai.
- **RLNG tariff ko nazarandaz karna:** Log purani sasti gas ke hisab se budget banate hain aur RLNG bill dekh kar pareshan hote hain. Apply se pehle RLNG rate aur monthly andaza samajh lein.
- **Purani application par bharosa rakhna:** Pabandi se pehle ki application khud-ba-khud RLNG me tabdeel nahi hoti. Nayi application dena lazmi hai — warna saal guzar jayenge aur connection nahi aayega.
- **Agents ko advance rakam dena:** Connection ke naam par "jaldi lagwa dene" ka wada kar ke advance mangne walon se bachein. Asli fee sirf demand notice ke zariye company ko ada hoti hai, kisi shakhs ko nahi.
- **Demand notice ki payment me der:** Notice milne ke baad der karne se aap ki bari nikal sakti hai. Raseed milte hi payment karein aur raseed mehfooz rakhein.
- **Meter ki jagah tay na karna:** Visit ke waqt meter ki jagah par ittefaq na ho to kaam ruk jata hai. Pehle se aik hawa-dar, khushk aur aasan rasai wali jagah muntakhib kar lein.

Connection lagne ke baad [gas ka bill online check karne ka tareeqa](/guides/gas-ka-bill-online-check) bhi seekh lein taake har mahine bill waqt par dekh saken aur koi ghalti foran pakar saken.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Sui gas ka naya connection 2026 me kitne ka lagta hai?**

SNGPL ke official charges ke mutabiq normal RLNG connection ka bunyadi kharcha Rs. 21,500 (10 marla tak) ya Rs. 23,000 (10 marla se bara) hai — is me Rs. 20,000 refundable security aur Rs. 1,500 ya Rs. 3,000 service line cost shamil hai. Application jama karwana muft hai. Fast-track par is me Rs. 25,000 urgent fee alag se shamil ho jati hai. Monthly RLNG bill is ke ilawa hota hai.

**Kya 2026 me naya sui gas connection system gas par mil sakta hai?**

Nahi. Hukoomat ne pabandi me sirf RLNG ki bunyad par narmi ki hai — naye domestic connections ab sirf RLNG tariff par diye ja rahe hain. Purani system gas wali scheme par naya connection nahi mil raha, chahe normal ho ya fast-track.

**SNGPL ka naya connection online kaise apply karein?**

SNGPL ki official website sngpl.com.pk par "New Application" ka option kholein ya ConnectOn mobile app istemal karein. Form me CNIC, pata, parosi ka bill number aur plot ka size darj karein, zaroori kagzaat upload karein aur application number mehfooz kar lein. Aap qareebi Customer Service Centre ja kar bhi apply kar sakte hain.

**Naya connection lagne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**

Koi fixed waqt nahi hai — normal merit par bari ke mutabiq kayi mahine lag sakte hain, jab ke fast-track application tarjeehi bunyad par tez process hoti hai. Pipeline ki mojoodgi, kagzaat ki mukammalgi aur demand notice ki fori payment waqt ko sab se zyada mutasir karte hain.

**Purani application walon ko naya connection kaise milega?**

Purani system-gas application par khud-ba-khud connection nahi milega — nayi RLNG application deni hogi. Jin logon ne pabandi se pehle urgent fee ya demand notice ada kiya tha, unhe tarjeeh milegi, lekin unhe Rs. 100 ke notarized stamp paper par RLNG undertaking, RLNG contract par dastakhat aur security ka farq ada karna hoga. Tasdeeq ke liye SNGPL helpline 1199 par rabta karein.
