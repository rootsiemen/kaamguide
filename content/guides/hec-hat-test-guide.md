## Pehli Baat: HAT Kya Hai Aur Kis Ko Dena Lazmi Hai

**HAT** ka matlab hai **Higher Education Aptitude Test**. Ye test **Higher Education Commission (HEC)** ka **Education Testing Council (ETC)** leta hai. Is ka maqsad ye check karna hai ke aik student me MS, MPhil ya PhD jaisi aala taleem ke liye zaroori zehni salahiyat — English samajhna, mantiqi soch aur hisaabi masail hal karna — mojood hai ya nahi.

HAT sirf aik "entry test" nahi hai, is ke **do bare istemal** hain. Pehla, Pakistan ki public aur private universities me **MS/MPhil aur PhD programs me admission** ke liye aksar universities HAT score mangti hain — HEC ne universities ko hidayat di hai ke MS/MPhil admission ke liye HEC/ETC ka HAT qabool karein. Doosra, **HEC scholarships** — mulki aur ghair-mulki scholarships (masalan Commonwealth, Chinese Government aur Hungarian scholarships) — ke liye HAT score merit ka aik ahem hissa hota hai. Is liye jo student agle 2 saal me MS/MPhil, PhD ya scholarship ke liye apply karna chahta hai, us ke liye aik acha HAT score aik lambi muddat tak kaam aata hai.

Ahem farq samajh lein: **USAT** undergraduate (BS) admission ke liye hai jo 12 saal taleem ke baad diya jata hai, jabke **HAT** graduate admission ke liye hai jo 16 saal taleem ke baad diya jata hai. Dono ETC leta hai, dono ki registration aik hi portal par hoti hai, lekin categories, paper aur maqsad bilkul alag hain.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Eligibility Ki Shartein

HAT dene ke liye bunyadi shart **16 saal taleem** hai. Is ka matlab hai ke aap ne 4 saala Bachelor (BS/BE/BBA waghera) ya us ke barabar taleem mukammal ki ho. Tafseel ye hai:

- **16 saal taleem mukammal** karne wale students apply kar sakte hain — chahe wo MS/MPhil admission lena chahte hon, PhD admission lena chahte hon, ya mustaqbil me HEC scholarship ke liye apply karna chahte hon.
- **Final semester ke students** bhi apply kar sakte hain — jin ki degree takmeel ke qareeb hai aur wo agle admission cycle ke liye score pehle se lena chahte hain.
- **Pehle se HAT de chuke students** apna score behtar karne ke liye dobara bhi de sakte hain — naya score purane ki jagah le lega.
- **Scholarship ke khwahishmand** students, jin hon ne abhi kisi university me apply nahi kiya, wo bhi HAT de sakte hain taake jab scholarship ka ilan aaye to un ke paas valid score mojood ho.

Koi umar ki had ya kisi khaas university se degree ki shart nahi hai — shart sirf taleem ki muddat aur us ka HEC se tasleem-shuda hona hai. Apni degree ki HEC recognition admission se pehle lazmi check karein.

## HAT Ki 5 Categories: Apni Category Kaise Chunein

HAT aik hi test nahi hai — ye **5 categories** me hota hai aur aap ko apne subject ke mutabiq category chunni hoti hai. Ghalat category chunna sab se bara nuqsan hai kyunke **submission ke baad category tabdeel nahi hoti**. Is liye form bharne se pehle apni university ya department se confirm kar lein ke wo kaunsi category mangta hai.

| Category | Kis Field Ke Liye | Verbal | Analytical | Quantitative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HAT-1 | Engineering & Technology, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Physics | 30% | 30% | 40% |
| HAT-2 | Management Sciences, Business Education | 30% | 40% | 30% |
| HAT-3 | Arts & Humanities, Social Sciences, Psychology (Clinical & Applied), Law | 40% | 35% | 25% |
| HAT-4 | Agriculture & Veterinary Sciences, Biological & Medical Sciences, Physical Sciences, Education, Media & Mass Communication | 40% | 30% | 30% |
| HAT-General | Religious Studies (Madrasa graduates) | 40% | 30% | 30% |

Misal ke tor par: aik software engineering graduate jo MS Computer Science karna chahta hai, wo **HAT-1** dega; aik MBA ka khwahishmand **HAT-2** dega; aik psychology graduate **HAT-3** dega. Agar aap ko apni category ka yaqeen na ho to apni target university ke admission office se poochein — ye aik minute ka kaam aap ko ghalat paper se bacha sakta hai.

## Paper Pattern: 100 MCQs, Teen Hisse

HAT ka paper **100 multiple-choice questions (MCQs)** par mushtamil hota hai aur is ke teen hisse hote hain:

1. **English / Verbal Reasoning** — vocabulary, sentence completion aur reading comprehension. Ye hissa aap ki English samajhne aur alfaz ke istemal ki salahiyat napta hai.
2. **Analytical Reasoning** — mantiqi soch, arguments ka tajziya aur masail ko tarteeb se hal karna. Is hisse me aap ko diye gaye bayan se durust natija nikalna hota hai.
3. **Quantitative Reasoning** — bunyadi mathematics: ratio, percentage, algebra ki bunyad, geometry aur data interpretation. Ye university-level maths nahi hai — intermediate level ki maths ki samajh kafi hai.

Paper ka kul waqt **120 minute** hota hai, yani har sawal ke liye austa aik minute se thora zyada. **Negative marking nahi hoti** — ghalat jawab par number nahi katte, is liye koi sawal khaali mat chorein; andaza bhi lagana faida-mand hai. Har category ka wazan (weightage) oopar wali table ke mutabiq mukhtalif hota hai, is liye tayyari me apni category ke sab se bare hisse ko sab se zyada waqt dein — masalan HAT-1 me quantitative 40% hai to maths par zyada mehnat karein, HAT-3 me verbal 40% hai to English par.

Qualifying score ke bare me aam tor par **50 marks** ko qualifying mana jata hai, lekin yaad rakhein ke **admission ka final faisla university ki merit par hota hai** — kisi achi university me 50 marks kafi nahi bhi ho sakte. Is liye hadaf sirf "pass" nahi, balkay jitna zyada score ho sake, utna behtar hai. Maujooda cycle ki qualifying shart hamesha ETC ke ilan me check karein.

## Fee: Kitni Hai Aur Kaise Jama Hoti Hai

HAT ki fee ke bare me sab se ehmiyat wali baat ye hai: **final fee wo hoti hai jo ETC portal par aap ke challan/invoice par likhi ho**. HEC/ETC ke public pages par fee ki aik mustaqil rakam publish nahi hoti — jab aap portal par apply karte hain to system aap ke liye **1Link/1Bill invoice** banata hai aur us par fee ki rakam darj hoti hai. Usi invoice ko bank, ATM, mobile banking app, Easypaisa ya JazzCash se jama karaya jata hai.

Fee ke bare me ye usool zehen me rakhein:

- Fee **non-refundable aur non-transferable** hoti hai — aik dafa jama ho jaye to wapas nahi milti aur kisi doosre test ya shakhs ko transfer nahi hoti.
- Sirf **SUBMITTED** applications qabool hoti hain — jo application SAVE ya INCOMPLETE mode me reh jaye, wo shumaar nahi hoti, chahe fee jama ho chuki ho.
- Fee jama karne ke baad **paid challan ki scan copy portal par upload** karna aur **asal challan test wale din saath le jana** zaroori hota hai — baghair is ke test center me dakhla mushkil ho sakta hai.
- Kisi agent ya website ko fee mat dein — sirf ETC portal se bana hua challan istemal karein. Jo website "HAT fee" ke naam par apna account number de, wo fraud hai.

Agar fee ki rakam ya bank ki tafseel me koi confusion ho to ETC helpline **0334-111-9432** ya **051-111-119-432** par rabta karein, ya **onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk** par query darj karein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Form Bharne Se Pehle Ye Tayyar Rakhein

Online form bharte waqt ye cheezein haath me rakhein taake form adhoora na reh jaye:

- **CNIC** (ya passport, agar Pakistani CNIC nahi hai) — number aur tasveer ki zaroorat parti hai.
- **Aik haal ki passport-size tasveer** — saaf background wali, jo roll number slip par chapegi.
- **Taleemi asnaad ki tafseel** — degree ka naam, university ka naam, passing year aur marks/CGPA.
- **Valid email address aur mobile number** — roll number slip email par aati hai aur SMS bhi isi number par aata hai; wo number dein jo aap khud istemal karte hon.
- **Category ka faisla** — oopar wali table se apni category pehle se tay kar lein.
- **Test center ki tarjeeh** — ETC test Pakistan ke bare shehron me leta hai; apne qareebi center ka intekhaab karein.

Tasveer ya CNIC ki ghalat scan sab se aam wajah hai jis se application rukti hai — tasveer saaf, roshni wali aur haal ki honi chahiye.

## Step-by-Step: ETC Portal Par Registration Ka Tareeqa

Registration sirf online hoti hai — koi paper form nahi hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

1. **etc.hec.gov.pk** par jayen — yehi ETC ka waahid asli portal hai.
2. **Account banayen** — email aur CNIC/B-Form number se sign up karein. Agar pehle USAT ya LAT ke liye account ban chuka hai to wohi account istemal karein.
3. **"My Profile" mukammal karein** — zaati maloomat, taleemi tafseel aur tasveer upload karein. Profile mukammal kiye baghair application nahi khulti.
4. **"Higher Education Aptitude Test" ka link kholein** — menu se HAT ka option chunein.
5. **Apni category chunein** — HAT-1, HAT-2, HAT-3, HAT-4 ya HAT-General, apne field ke mutabiq.
6. **Form bhar kar SAVE karein, phir ghaur se check karein** — naam ki spelling, CNIC number, category aur center aik dafa phir parhein.
7. **Fee challan generate karein aur jama karayen** — 1Link/1Bill invoice se bank/ATM/app se payment karein aur paid challan ki copy upload karein.
8. **SUBMIT dabayen** — sirf SUBMITTED application hi consider hoti hai. Submission ke baad confirmation ka screenshot mehfooz rakhein.
9. **Roll number slip download karein** — test se kuch din pehle slip aap ke ETC dashboard par aur email par aajati hai; isay print kar lein.

Registration ki **aakhri tareekh** har cycle me mukhtalif hoti hai — ETC ke test **quarterly** hote hain, yani saal me 4 dafa. Maujooda cycle ki dates ke liye hamesha **etc.hec.gov.pk** ka announcement page check karein; kisi coaching website ki purani dates par bharosa kar ke aakhri din ka intezar mat karein.

## Test Wale Din: Kya Le Kar Jayen Aur Kya Mana Hai

Test wale din ye teen cheezein lazmi saath le jayen:

- **Print ki hui roll number slip** — mobile par tasveer kafi nahi, paper print chahiye.
- **Asal CNIC ya passport** — photocopy qabool nahi hoti.
- **Paid fee challan ki asal copy** — jo aap ne jama karayi thi.

Ye cheezein **mana** hain: mobile phone, calculator, smartwatch, koi bhi electronic device, aur question booklet ki tasveer lena ya share karna — is par sakht karwai ho sakti hai. Center par waqt se pehle pahunchein, kyunke der se aane walon ko aksar andar nahi jane diya jata. Roll number slip par likha center aur waqt aik din pehle dobara check kar lein.

## Result, Validity Aur Score Ka Istemal

Result aam tor par test ke kuch hafton baad ETC portal par aata hai — aap apne account me login kar ke score dekh sakte hain. **HAT score 2 saal tak valid** rehta hai. Is ka matlab ye hai ke aap aik dafa acha score le lein to agle 2 saal me kai admission cycles aur scholarship announcements me wohi score istemal kar sakte hain — har dafa test dene ki zaroorat nahi.

Score ka istemal is tarah karein:

- **University admission** — jis university me apply kar rahe hain, wahan HAT score jama karayen; har university apni merit list apne usool se banati hai.
- **HEC scholarships** — jab koi HEC scholarship announce ho to E-portal par application ke saath apna valid HAT score darj karein.
- **Score behtar karna** — agar score kam aaye to agle cycle me dobara de sakte hain; naya score purane ki jagah le lega.

Result aane ke baad apni score card ki copy mehfooz rakhein — admission ke waqt universities aksar is ki copy mangti hain.

## Tayyari Ke Tips: 4 Hafton Ka Seedha Plan

HAT ki tayyari ke liye kisi mehngi academy ki zaroorat nahi — aik seedha plan kafi hai:

- **Pehle hafta:** Apni category ka weightage samjhein aur ETC ke sample papers dekh lein. Apne kamzor hisse ki nishandahi karein.
- **Doosra aur teesra hafta:** Har roz aik hissa practice karein — verbal ke liye roz 20 naye alfaz aur aik reading passage; quantitative ke liye ratio, percentage aur basic algebra; analytical ke liye logic puzzles aur statement-based sawal.
- **Chautha hafta:** Poore 100 sawal ke mock tests waqt dekh kar dein — 120 minute me paper mukammal karne ki aadat banayen. Ghalatiyon ka register banayen aur un topics ko dobara parhein.
- **Aakhri 2 din:** Naya topic mat shuru karein — sirf revision aur mock test ke ghalat sawal dobara dekhein. Neend poori karein; thaka hua zehen test me nuqsan deta hai.

Tayyari ke liye HEC ki taraf se sample material aur purane papers ETC portal aur HEC website par milte hain — pehle wahi istemal karein, phir koi aur kitab.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

- **Ghalat category chunna** — form submit hone ke baad category nahi badalti; university se pehle confirm karein.
- **SAVE par chor dena** — application SUBMIT nahi ki to wo shumaar hi nahi hoti.
- **Ghalat email ya mobile number** — roll number slip isi par aati hai; purana ya kisi aur ka number mat dein.
- **Aakhri din par apply karna** — portal par load ya bank ki delay se challan late ho sakta hai; aakhri tareekh se aik hafta pehle kaam mukammal karein.
- **Fee kisi agent ko dena** — sirf ETC portal ka challan istemal karein.
- **Sirf "pass" ka hadaf rakhna** — achi universities aur scholarships me merit zyada hoti hai; jitna zyada score, utna behtar chance.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**HAT kis ke liye lazmi hai?**
HAT MS/MPhil aur PhD programs me admission lene walon aur HEC scholarships ke liye apply karne walon ke liye hota hai. Is ke liye 16 saal taleem ya us ke barabar qualification darkar hai. Har university ki apni shart ho sakti hai, is liye apni target university se confirm karein ke wo HAT mangti hai ya nahi.

**HAT ki fee kitni hai?**
Final fee ETC portal par aap ke challan/invoice par darj hoti hai — 1Link/1Bill ke zariye jama hoti hai aur non-refundable hoti hai. HEC/ETC ke public pages par aik mustaqil rakam publish nahi hoti, is liye portal par bane challan ki rakam hi hatmi hai. Kisi teesri website ya agent ke bataye account par fee mat bhejein.

**HAT ka score kitne arse tak valid rehta hai?**
HAT score 2 saal tak valid rehta hai. Is arse me aap isi score se kai admission cycles aur HEC scholarship announcements me apply kar sakte hain. Agar score behtar karna ho to agle cycle me dobara test de sakte hain.

**HAT me passing marks kitne hain?**
Aam tor par 50 marks ko qualifying mana jata hai, lekin admission ka final faisla university ki merit par hota hai — achi universities me merit is se zyada bhi ho sakti hai. Maujooda cycle ki qualifying shart ETC ke ilan me check karein aur hadaf sirf pass nahi, balkay zyada se zyada score rakhein.

**HAT saal me kitni dafa hota hai aur agla test kab hai?**
ETC ke test quarterly hote hain, yani saal me 4 dafa. Har cycle ki announcement, aakhri tareekh aur test ki tareekh mukhtalif hoti hai, is liye agle test ki dates ke liye hamesha etc.hec.gov.pk ka announcement page check karein — purani dates par bharosa mat karein.
