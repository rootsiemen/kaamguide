## E-Stamp Paper Kya Hai Aur Ye Kis Kaam Aata Hai

E-stamp paper wohi purana stamp paper hai, bas ab ye kagaz par haath se likha hua nahi milta — ye Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) ke electronic system se jari hota hai. Aap online apni deal ki tafseel darj karte hain, system stamp duty compute karta hai, aap Challan Form 32-A ke zariye fee jama karwate hain aur us ke badle me aap ko aik printed e-stamp paper milta hai jis par aik unique ID aur QR code hota hai. Ye paper adalat, sub-registrar daftar, bank aur housing society — har jagah qabool hota hai, kyun ke is ki tasdeeq online database se ho sakti hai.

Purane nizam me stamp paper lene ke liye stamp vendor ke paas jana parta tha, phir bank se payment ki tasdeeq, phir treasury daftar se paper lena — zyada value wale paper ke liye ye amal chaar din tak le sakta tha. E-stamping ke nizam me yehi kaam aadhe ghante se bhi kam me ho jata hai, aur sab se bara faida ye hai ke naqli stamp paper ka khatra khatam ho gaya hai: har e-stamp paper central database me darj hota hai, is liye koi is par haath se tabdeeli nahi kar sakta.

E-stamp paper aam tor par do qisam ke kaamon ke liye istemal hota hai. Non-judicial stamp paper woh hai jo aap roz-marra ke muahidon aur halafnamon ke liye lete hain — maslan rent agreement, sale agreement, affidavit ya bayana. Judicial stamp paper adalati karwai ke liye hota hai. Is guide me hum non-judicial e-stamp paper par baat karenge, kyun ke yehi woh paper hai jo aam shehri ko sab se zyada chahiye hota hai.

## Punjab Me E-Stamp Paper Kin Kaamon Ke Liye Chahiye Hota Hai

Punjab me e-stamp paper ki zaroorat aksar in kaamon me parti hai:

- **Jaidad ki khareed-o-farokht:** Sale deed ya registry ke liye stamp duty e-stamp paper ke zariye ada ki jati hai. Jaidad ki registry ab PLRA ke e-Registration portal se hoti hai, aur us ka pehla qadam bhi e-stamp challan banana hai. Jaidad transfer ki mukammal guide ke liye [fard malkiat aur property transfer ki guide](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) bhi parh lein.
- **Rent agreement aur sale agreement:** Makan ya dukaan kiraye par dene ya jaidad ka soda karne se pehle agreement stamp paper par likha jata hai.
- **Affidavit (halafnama):** [Domicile certificate](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) ke liye halafnama, ya kisi bhi sarkari kaam me bayaan-e-halfi dene ke liye Rs 300 ka e-stamp paper istemal hota hai. March 2026 se Rs 300 ka e-stamp online pay karke saada kagaz par print kiya ja sakta hai.
- **Gaari ki khareed-o-farokht:** Gaari bechte ya khareedte waqt sale agreement aur affidavit ke liye stamp paper chahiye hota hai — [gaari transfer karne ki guide](/guides/gaari-transfer-karne-ka-tareeqa) me is ka zikr maujood hai.
- **Power of attorney aur mukhtarnama:** Kisi ko apni taraf se kaam karne ka ikhtiyar dene ke liye bhi stamp paper istemal hota hai.
- **Partnership deed aur business agreement:** Karobari sharakatdari ya kisi bhi bare muahide ke liye stamp paper ki zaroorat parti hai.

Aik baat yaad rakhein: e-stamp paper sirf Punjab ke nizam se Punjab ke kaamon ke liye jari hota hai. Agar aap ka muahida ya jaidad kisi doosre soobe se mutalliq hai to wahan ka apna nizam hoga.

## E-Stamp Paper Ke Liye Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Maloomat

Challan 32-A banane se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein, taake form bharte waqt rukna na pare:

- **Apna CNIC:** Jis shakhs ke naam par stamp paper jari hona hai, us ka CNIC number lazmi hai. System aap ke CNIC ki tasdeeq NADRA se karta hai. Agar aap ka CNIC abhi tak nahi bana to pehle [NADRA se CNIC banane ki guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh lein.
- **Doosre fareeq ka CNIC:** Muahide ya sale deed me jo doosra fareeq hai — maslan bechne wala, khareedne wala ya makan malik — us ka naam aur CNIC number bhi chahiye hota hai.
- **Jaidad ki tafseel (agar jaidad ka kaam hai):** Zila, tehsil, mauza, khewat/khatooni number, raqba aur jaidad ki qisam (rehayshi ya tijarati). System inhi maloomat aur DC valuation tables ki bunyad par stamp duty compute karta hai.
- **Muahide ki value:** Jis raqam ka soda ya muahida hai, woh raqam darj karni hoti hai. Stamp duty isi value par lagti hai.
- **Mobile number:** Challan aur verification ke liye aik active mobile number rakhein.
- **Muahide ki qisam:** Aap ko batana hota hai ke stamp paper kis kaam ke liye hai — sale deed, rent agreement, affidavit, gift deed ya koi aur. Har qisam ki duty alag ho sakti hai, is liye sahi qisam chunna lazmi hai.

Agar family se mutalliq koi kagaz darkar ho, maslan wirasat ya family tree ka saboot, to [Family Registration Certificate (FRC)](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) kaam aata hai.

## Punjab E-Stamp Paper Ki Fees 2026

E-stamp paper ki koi aik fixed fee nahi hoti — stamp duty muahide ki qisam aur value par munhasir hoti hai, aur final raqam wohi hoti hai jo aap ke Challan 32-A par likhi ho. Neeche woh sharhein di gayi hain jo 2026 me report hui hain:

| Kaam / Document | Stamp Duty / Fee |
|---|---|
| Sale deed — shehri ilaqa | Property value ka 2% |
| Sale deed — deegar ilaqe | Property value ka 1% |
| Registry par izafi stamp duty | Rs 500 (Rs 500,000 tak ki value) ya Rs 1,000 (us se zyada) |
| Affidavit / halafnama | Rs 300 fixed e-stamp |
| Rent agreement, sale agreement, general muahida | Value ke mutabiq — system challan par batata hai |
| Registration fee aur mutation fee | System challan par compute karta hai |

Is table ke bare me do zaroori wazahatein hain. Pehli: shehri ilaqon me 2% aur deegar ilaqon me 1% ki sharah Stamp (Amendment) Act 2026 se hai, jo Punjab Gazette me 6 May 2026 ko shaya hua — is tarmeem se dehi ilaqon ki sharah 3% se kam hokar 1% hui. Dosri: PITB ki e-Stamping page par abhi bhi purani 3% wali sharah likhi hui hai, aur kuch sources Social Infrastructure Cess aur PIDB fee mila kar sale deed par kul 5% se 7% tak ki raqam bhi batate hain. Is liye kisi bhi aik sharah ko hatmi na samjhein — **aap ke Challan 32-A par likhi hui raqam hi aap ki asal fee hai**, aur wahi ada karni hai. Rates har budget ke sath badal sakte hain.

Rs 300 wala affidavit stamp sab se aam fixed-value e-stamp hai. Domicile ke liye halafnama is ki sab se aam misaal hai, aur March 2026 se is ki payment online hokar print saada kagaz par nikala ja sakta hai.

## Online Tareeqa: Challan 32-A Generate Karke E-Stamp Paper Banana

E-stamp paper banane ka asal tareeqa ye hai:

![Laptop par online challan form bharte hue shakhs, sath bank ki imarat aur payment receipt](/images/punjab-e-stamp-paper-online-banane-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

1. **E-stamping portal kholein:** Punjab ka official e-stamping portal **es.punjab.gov.pk** hai. Is par "Generate Challan 32-A" ka option chunein.
2. **Zila aur tehsil chunein:** Jis zila aur tehsil se aap ka kaam mutalliq hai, woh chunein.
3. **Muahide ki qisam chunein:** Sale deed, rent agreement, affidavit ya jo bhi qisam ho, woh chunein.
4. **Fareeqain ki tafseel darj karein:** Khareedne wale aur bechne wale — ya muahide ke dono fareeqain — ke naam aur CNIC number likhein.
5. **Jaidad ya muahide ki tafseel likhein:** Jaidad ka kaam hai to zila, tehsil, mauza, raqba aur qisam darj karein; aam muahida hai to muahide ki value likhein.
6. **Challan generate karein:** System aap ki di hui maloomat aur DC valuation tables ki bunyad par stamp duty compute karke Challan Form 32-A bana dega. Is par aik PSID number hoga.
7. **Challan par dastakhat karein:** Challan ko print karke us par dastakhat karein — ye aap ki taraf se tasdeeq hai ke di hui maloomat durust hain.

Aik ahem baat: portal par darj ki gayi value aur DC rate me se jo zyada ho, duty usi par lagti hai. Is liye value kam likh kar duty bachane ki koshish na karein — system DC table se khud check karta hai, aur ghalat value par challan reject bhi ho sakta hai.

## Payment, Printing Aur Kitna Waqt Lagta Hai

Challan banne ke baad payment ka tareeqa ye hai:

- **Bank se payment:** Challan lekar National Bank, Bank of Punjab ya kisi bhi designated scheduled bank ki branch par jayein aur raqam jama karwayein. Sub-registrar daftar ke counter par bhi payment ho sakti hai.
- **Banking app se payment:** Challan par likhe hue PSID number se apni banking app ke zariye bhi payment ki ja sakti hai — is ke liye bank account hona zaroori nahi hota.
- **Printed stamp paper hasil karein:** Payment ke baad aap ko e-stamp paper legal-size kagaz par print karke diya jata hai. Rs 300 wale affidavit stamp ki surat me aap khud bhi is ko saada kagaz par print kar sakte hain.
- **Waqt:** PITB ke mutabiq e-stamp paper ka poora amal taqreeban 30 minute me mukammal ho jata hai, jab ke purane nizam me zyada value wale paper ke liye chaar din tak lag jate thay. Agar aap ka kaam jaidad ki registry se mutalliq hai to us ke baad PLRA ke e-Registration portal (eregistration.punjab-zameen.gov.pk) par registry ki darkhwast jama hoti hai, jis me sub-registrar ke samne aik dafa hazri bhi lazmi hai.

**Ahem note:** Official portal es.punjab.gov.pk 6 November 2026 ko check kiya gaya to woh load nahi hua — browser par "site can't be reached" ki khata aayi. Agar aap ko bhi portal na khule to pareshan na hon: qareebi designated bank branch ya e-Khidmat Markaz se ruju karein, wahan se bhi Challan 32-A ban sakta hai. Portal ki kharabi ki surat me kisi agent ko extra paisay dekar kaam karwane ki zaroorat nahi hai.

## E-Stamp Paper Ki Tasdeeq (Verification) Kaise Karein

E-stamp paper ki sab se bari khasiyat is ki online tasdeeq hai. Koi bhi shakhs — bank, sub-registrar ya housing society — paper par likhi hui unique ID ya QR code se is ki asliat check kar sakta hai.

![Magnifying glass se official stamp paper aur QR code ki tasdeeq karte hue, sath green checkmark](/images/punjab-e-stamp-paper-online-banane-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Tasdeeq ka tareeqa ye hai:

1. E-stamping portal par verification ka option kholein.
2. Stamp paper par likha hua unique ID ya challan number darj karein.
3. System aap ko batayega ke paper asli hai ya nahi, kis ke naam par jari hua, kis tareekh ko aur kis value ka hai.

Agar aap kisi se jaidad khareed rahe hain to soda karne se pehle us ke stamp paper ki tasdeeq lazmi karwayein. Naqli stamp paper par hone wala muahida adalat me nahi chalta, aur baad me is ka koi hal nahi hota. Sub-registrar daftar aur housing societies ko e-stamping database tak mehdood rasai bhi di gayi hai, taake woh khud bhi paper ki tasdeeq kar sakein.

Agar verification me koi farq nazar aaye — maslan naam, value ya tareekh me ghalti — to foran usi bank ya daftar se ruju karein jahan se paper jari hua tha, aur ghalti durust karwayein baghair muahida aage na barhayein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Bachna Chahiye

- **Ghalat value likhna:** DC rate se kam value likhne se duty kam nahi hoti — system khud DC table se check karta hai, aur challan reject ho sakta hai.
- **Ghalat muahide ki qisam chunna:** Sale deed ke bajaye aam agreement chunne se duty ghalat compute hoti hai aur baad me registry ruk jati hai.
- **CNIC number me ghalti:** Aik hindse ki ghalti bhi NADRA verification fail kar deti hai. Challan submit karne se pehle dono fareeqain ke CNIC dobara check kar lein.
- **Naqli ya purane tareeqe se paper lena:** Ab Punjab me stamp paper sirf e-stamping system se milta hai. Koi vendor aap ko haath se likha hua ya "purana stock" wala paper de to woh qabool na karein — us ki online tasdeeq nahi ho sakegi.
- **Portal na khulne par agent ko paisay dena:** Portal ki kharabi aam baat hai. Is ka hal designated bank branch ya e-Khidmat Markaz hai, agent nahi.
- **Paper ki tasdeeq kiye baghair soda karna:** Kisi bhi bare muahide se pehle stamp paper ki online tasdeeq lazmi karwayein.
- **Challan ki receipt gum karna:** Payment ki receipt aur challan ki copy sambhal kar rakhein — registry ya kisi bhi dispute ki surat me yehi aap ka saboot hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab me e-stamp paper online kaise banwaya jata hai?**

Official portal es.punjab.gov.pk par "Generate Challan 32-A" chunein, zila, tehsil, muahide ki qisam, fareeqain ke naam aur CNIC aur jaidad ya muahide ki tafseel darj karein. System stamp duty compute karke Challan 32-A bana dega. Phir National Bank, Bank of Punjab ya kisi designated bank se ya banking app se PSID ke zariye payment karein aur printed e-stamp paper hasil karein. Poora amal taqreeban 30 minute me mukammal ho jata hai.

**E-stamp paper ki fee kitni hai?**

Koi aik fixed fee nahi hai — fee muahide ki qisam aur value par munhasir hoti hai. 2026 ki tarmeem ke mutabiq sale deed par stamp duty shehri ilaqon me 2% aur deegar ilaqon me 1% hai, jab ke affidavit ke liye Rs 300 ka fixed e-stamp hai. Final raqam wohi hoti hai jo aap ke Challan 32-A par likhi ho, is liye challan par likhi raqam ko hi hatmi samjhein.

**E-stamp paper ki tasdeeq kaise ki jati hai?**

Har e-stamp paper par aik unique ID aur QR code hota hai. E-stamping portal par verification option me ye ID darj karke aap paper ki asliat, jari karne ki tareekh, value aur naam check kar sakte hain. Bank, sub-registrar aur housing society bhi isi tarah tasdeeq karte hain.

**Agar e-stamping portal na khule to kya karein?**

Portal ka na khulna aam masla hai — 6 November 2026 ko bhi portal load nahi hua tha. Is surat me qareebi designated bank branch (National Bank ya Bank of Punjab) ya e-Khidmat Markaz se ruju karein, wahan se bhi Challan 32-A ban sakta hai. Kisi agent ko extra paisay dekar kaam karwane ki zaroorat nahi hai.

**Kya e-stamp paper purane stamp paper ki jagah har jagah qabool hota hai?**

Ji haan. Punjab me e-stamp paper hi official tareeqa hai aur ye adalat, sub-registrar daftar, bank aur housing society — har jagah qabool hota hai, kyun ke is ki tasdeeq online database se ho sakti hai. Haath se likha hua ya ghair-tasdeeq-shuda paper ab qabool nahi kiya jata.
