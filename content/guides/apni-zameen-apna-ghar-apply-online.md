## Pehli Baat: Apni Zameen Apna Ghar Kya Hai

**Apni Zameen Apna Ghar (AZAG)** Wazeer-e-Aala Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet Punjab ke be-ghar aur mustahiq khandaanon ko **muft 3 marla rehaishi plot** diya jata hai. Ye koi qiston wala plot ya subsidy wala plot nahi — ye plot **muft** hai, aur is ka maqsad saaf hai: jis khandaan ke paas apna ghar ya plot nahi, usay apni zameen ka malik banana.

Scheme ko **Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA)** chala rahi hai, aur is ki nigrani **Housing, Urban Development and Public Health Engineering Department (HUD&PHED)** karta hai. Pehle marhale me Punjab ke **19 azlaa** me **2,000 se zyada plots** taqseem kiye ja rahe hain. Plot ki allotment **shaffaf balloting (qurra andazi)** se hoti hai — na sifarish chalti hai, na paisa.

Aik baat shuru me hi saaf kar dein, taa ke koi ghalat fehmi na rahe: **Phase 1 ki darkhwaston ki aakhri tareekh 16 July 2025 guzar chuki hai.** Is waqt nayi darkhwastein jama nahi ho rahin — jo log apply kar chuke hain, un ki **scrutiny (kagzaat ki jaanch)** chal rahi hai. Is guide me aap ko scheme ka mukammal tareeqa, ehliyaat, kagzaat aur balloting ka process milega, taa ke agle marhale ke liye aap pehle se tayyar hon, aur agar aap ne pehle apply kiya tha to aap ko pata ho ke ab kya karna hai.

Ye guide un logon ke liye khaas taur par mufeed hai jin ke paas apna ghar nahi aur jo kiraye par rehte hain. Plot milne ke baad ghar banane ke liye aap [Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan](/guides/apni-chhat-apna-ghar-loan-apply-online) ki guide bhi dekh sakte hain, jis me 15 lakh tak bila sood loan ka tareeqa bataya gaya hai.

## Scheme Me Kya Milta Hai: Muft 3 Marla Plot

Scheme ki bunyadi tafseel ye hai:

| Tafseel | Maloomat |
| --- | --- |
| Plot ka size | 3 marla (rehaishi) |
| Plot ki qeemat | Muft — koi qeemat nahi |
| Pehle marhale ke azlaa | 19 azlaa |
| Pehle marhale ke plots | 2,000 se zyada |
| Chalane wala idara | PHATA |
| Nigrani | Housing, Urban Development and Public Health Engineering Department |
| Intikhab ka tareeqa | Shaffaf digital balloting (qurra andazi) |
| Registration fees | Muft |
| Official portal | azag.punjab.gov.pk |

3 marla plot aik chhota magar mukammal rehaishi plot hota hai — is par aik chhota ghar, do kamre, aik baithak aur sehan aasani se ban sakta hai. Scheme ka asal faida ye hai ke plot **muft** milta hai; ghar ki taameer ke liye baad me [Apni Chhat Apna Ghar](/guides/apni-chhat-apna-ghar-loan-apply-online) jaisi schemes se bila sood loan bhi mil sakta hai.

Pehle marhale me jin **19 azlaa** ko shamil kiya gaya hai, wo ye hain: Attock, Bahawalnagar, Bahawalpur, Bhakkar, Chiniot, Faisalabad, Gujrat, Jhang, Jhelum, Kasur, Khushab, Layyah, Lodhran, Mandi Bahauddin, Okara, Rajanpur, Sahiwal, Sargodha aur Vehari. Darkhwast sirf **apne zila** ki scheme ke liye di ja sakti hai — dosre zila me apply karna qubool nahi hota.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Official portal ke mutabiq darkhwast dene ke liye ye sharaait lazmi hain:

- **Punjab ka mustaqil rehaishi hona:** Darkhwast guzaar ka CNIC Punjab ke address par hona chahiye. Dosre subon ke rehaishi is scheme ke liye ehl nahi hain.
- **Apne zila me darkhwast:** Aap sirf us zila ki scheme ke liye apply kar sakte hain jis zila ke aap rehaishi hain. Zila-based quota hai, is liye ghalat zila chunna darkhwast radd karwa deta hai.
- **Koi plot ya ghar na ho:** Darkhwast guzaar, us ki biwi/shohar aur zer-e-kafalaat bachon ke naam par Pakistan bhar me koi rehaishi plot ya ghar nahi hona chahiye. Ye scheme sirf un ke liye hai jin ke paas kuch nahi.
- **Saaf criminal record:** Darkhwast guzaar ka criminal record saaf hona chahiye. Is ki tasdeeq ke liye police character certificate manga jata hai.
- **Aamdani ki had:** Portal ke mutabiq darkhwast guzaar ki gharana aamdani ki aik muqarrar had hai. Khabron ke mutabiq ye had taqreeban **Rs 50,000 mahana** batayi jati hai aur NSER/PMT score ko bhi tarjeeh di jati hai — lekin hatmi had wahi hogi jo darkhwast ke waqt official portal par likhi ho, is liye aakhri tasdeeq portal se hi karein. Apna PMT score check karne ke liye [BISP 8171 registration check](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) wali guide dekh lein.

Agar aap in sharaait par poora utarte hain to aap agle marhale ke liye ehl hain. Agar aap ke naam par pehle se koi plot ya ghar hai — chahe wo chhota hi kyun na ho — to darkhwast dene se pehle soch lein, kyun ke scrutiny me ye baat saamne aa jati hai aur darkhwast radd ho jati hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Official portal ke notice ke mutabiq scrutiny committee ko ye kagzaat darkhwast ki bandish ki tareekh (16 July 2025) ke baad **30 din ke andar** mutalliqa District Deputy Commissioner Office ya PHATA Field Office me jama karwane hain. Jo log pehle apply kar chuke hain, un ke liye ye kagzaat abhi bhi ahem hain:

![Apni Zameen Apna Ghar ke kagzaat: CNIC, domicile certificate aur stamp paper desk par](/images/apni-zameen-apna-ghar-apply-online-2.jpg)

- **CNIC:** Darkhwast guzaar aur shohar/biwi (agar shadi shuda hon) ka asal CNIC aur us ki copy.
- **Domicile certificate:** Punjab ka domicile jo aap ke zila ki rehaish sabit kare. Domicile banane ka tareeqa [domicile certificate guide](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) me dekh lein.
- **Rs 200 ke e-stamp paper par halafname (affidavits):** Teen halafname chahiye — **Financial Integrity** (aamdani ki durustgi), **Property Ownership** (koi jayedad na hone ka halaf) aur **Correctness of Information** (di gayi maloomat durust hone ka halaf). Ye halafname Rs 200 ke e-stamp paper par banwane hote hain.
- **Police character certificate:** Mutalliqa police station ya E-Khidmat Markaz se hasil kiya gaya character certificate, jo saaf criminal record sabit kare. Is ka tareeqa [police character certificate guide](/guides/police-character-certificate) me mojood hai.
- **Family Registration Certificate (FRC):** NADRA se jari FRC khandaan ki tafseel sabit karta hai — ye bhi tayyar rakhna behtar hai. FRC banane ka tareeqa [NADRA FRC guide](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) me dekh lein.
- **Scrutiny committee ki mazeed talab:** Committee mazeed koi kagaz bhi mang sakti hai — jaise aamdani ka saboot (salary slip ya halafnama) ya rehaish ka saboot.

**Ahem note:** Kagzaat jama na karwane ya waqt par na karwane se ehliyaat mutasir ho sakti hai — portal ke notice me saaf likha hai ke deadline ke andar kagzaat na dene par darkhwast par asar par sakta hai. Is liye jin logon ne Phase 1 me apply kiya tha, wo foran apne DC Office ya PHATA Field Office se rabta karein.

Official portal ke ilawa kisi bhi website ya WhatsApp number par apply na karein. Sirf **azag.punjab.gov.pk** hi asal portal hai.

## Step-by-Step: Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

Jab registration khuli thi to online apply ka tareeqa ye tha — agle marhale me bhi yehi tareeqa mutawaqqa hai, is liye isay samajh kar tayyar rahein:

1. **Official portal kholein:** Apne browser me **azag.punjab.gov.pk** likhein. Kisi bhi dosri website se apply na karein.
2. **Account banayein ya login karein:** "Register" par click karke apna **13 hindson wala CNIC number** aur active **mobile number** darj karein, aur aik mazboot password banayein. Pehle se account hai to CNIC aur password se login karein.
3. **Darkhwast form bharein:** Apni zaati tafseel (naam, walid ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish), maujooda pata, gharana aamdani aur zila ki maloomat durust darj karein. Naam aur tafseel **CNIC ke mutabiq** likhein.
4. **Kagzaat upload karein:** CNIC, domicile aur deegar kagzaat ki saaf scanned copy PDF ya JPEG format me upload karein.
5. **Form check karke submit karein:** Submit karne se pehle har field dobara parh lein — ghalat maloomat scrutiny me pakdi jati hai aur darkhwast radd ho sakti hai.
6. **Confirmation mehfooz rakhein:** Submit karne ke baad milne wali confirmation ya tracking tafseel ka screenshot ya print mehfooz rakh lein.
7. **Status check karte rahein:** Portal ke dashboard se apni darkhwast ki haalat (pending, scrutiny, balloting) check karte rahein.

**Offline madad:** Jin logon ke paas internet nahi, wo apne **Deputy Commissioner (DC) Office** ja kar amle ki madad se darkhwast jama karwa sakte hain. Kisi agent ko paisa de kar apply karwana zaroori nahi aur khatarnaak bhi hai.

## Balloting Aur Allotment Ka Tareeqa

![Apni Zameen Apna Ghar ki balloting aur plot ki allotment ka manzar](/images/apni-zameen-apna-ghar-apply-online-3.jpg)

Darkhwast jama karwane ke baad ye marhale hote hain:

1. **Scrutiny:** PHATA aap ki darkhwast aur kagzaat ki jaanch karta hai — CNIC, domicile, aamdani, jayedad ka record aur criminal record verify kiye jate hain.
2. **Balloting:** Ehl darkhwast guzaaron me se kamyab logon ka intikhab **shaffaf digital balloting** se hota hai. Is me na merit list hoti hai, na sifarish — qurra andazi se faisla hota hai.
3. **Allotment:** Balloting me kamyab hone walon ko plot allot kar diya jata hai. Allotment ke baad taameer ki sharaait bhi hoti hain — khabron ke mutabiq allotment ke baad aik muqarrar muddat me taameer shuru karni hoti hai, is ki hatmi tafseel allotment letter ya portal se milegi.
4. **Ghar ki taameer:** Plot milne ke baad ghar banane ke liye [Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan](/guides/apni-chhat-apna-ghar-loan-apply-online) se 15 lakh tak bila sood loan liya ja sakta hai.

Balloting ki tareekh aur nateeja official portal par ilan kiya jata hai — apni darkhwast ka status portal ke dashboard se check karte rahein, aur kisi bhi SMS ya call par bharosa karne se pehle portal se tasdeeq karein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Rukwa Deti Hain

- **Ghalat zila chunna:** Apne zila ke ilawa kisi dosre zila ki scheme ke liye apply karna — darkhwast foran radd ho jati hai.
- **CNIC se mukhtalif naam ya tafseel:** Form me naam, tareekh-e-pedaish ya pata CNIC se mukhtalif likhna scrutiny me pakda jata hai.
- **Halafname na banana:** Rs 200 ke e-stamp paper par teenon halafname na dena — ye scrutiny ka lazmi hissa hai.
- **Kagzaat waqt par jama na karwana:** 30 din ki deadline guzar jana ehliyaat khatam kar sakta hai.
- **Jhooti maloomat:** Aamdani ya jayedad ke bare me ghalat bayani — verification me pakdi jati hai aur mustaqbil ki schemes se bhi na-ehli ho sakti hai.
- **Agent ko paisa dena:** Registration muft hai; agent ko paisa dena na sirf nuqsan hai, balkay aap ki darkhwast bhi khatre me par sakti hai.
- **Fake portal par apply karna:** Sirf azag.punjab.gov.pk asal hai — milti julti naam wali websites se bachein.
- **Police character certificate na lagana:** Saaf criminal record ka saboot na dena darkhwast radd karwa sakta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Apni Zameen Apna Ghar scheme me plot muft milta hai ya qeemat deni hoti hai?**
Plot bilkul muft milta hai — 3 marla rehaishi plot ke liye koi qeemat nahi. Registration bhi muft hai. Sirf halafname ke liye Rs 200 ka e-stamp paper aur character certificate jaisi chhoti fees apni taraf se deni hoti hain.

**Kya abhi Apni Zameen Apna Ghar ke liye apply kar sakte hain?**
Phase 1 ki darkhwaston ki aakhri tareekh 16 July 2025 guzar chuki hai, is liye is waqt nayi darkhwastein jama nahi ho rahin. Jo log apply kar chuke hain, un ki scrutiny chal rahi hai. Agle marhale ki registration ke liye official portal azag.punjab.gov.pk par nazar rakhein aur kagzaat pehle se tayyar rakh lein.

**Apni Zameen Apna Ghar ke liye kaun ehl hai?**
Punjab ka mustaqil rehaishi (CNIC par Punjab ka address), apne zila me darkhwast dene wala, jis ke naam par ya us ke khandaan ke naam par Pakistan me koi ghar ya plot na ho, aur jis ka criminal record saaf ho. Aamdani ki hatmi had darkhwast ke waqt portal par check karein.

**Darkhwast ke liye kon se kagzaat chahiye?**
CNIC (darkhwast guzaar aur shohar/biwi), domicile certificate, Rs 200 ke e-stamp paper par teen halafname (Financial Integrity, Property Ownership, Correctness of Information), police character certificate aur FRC. Ye kagzaat DC Office ya PHATA Field Office me jama karwane hote hain.

**Plot ka intikhab kaise hota hai — merit par ya qurra andazi se?**
Intikhab shaffaf digital balloting (qurra andazi) se hota hai. Pehle PHATA scrutiny karta hai ke darkhwast guzaar ehl hai ya nahi, phir ehl logon me se balloting se kamyab log chune jate hain. Is me sifarish ya paisa nahi chalta.

**Apni Zameen Apna Ghar aur Apni Chhat Apna Ghar me kya farq hai?**
Apni Zameen Apna Ghar me **muft 3 marla plot** milta hai un ko jin ke paas zameen nahi. Apni Chhat Apna Ghar me un ko **15 lakh tak bila sood loan** milta hai jin ke paas pehle se plot hai aur ghar banana hai. Dono schemes PHATA chalati hai aur aik dosre ki mukammal hain — plot milne ke baad ghar banane ke liye loan liya ja sakta hai.
