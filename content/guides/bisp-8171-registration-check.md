## Pehli Baat: BISP Kya Hai Aur 2026 Me Qist Kitni Milti Hai

**Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP)** Pakistan ka sab se bara social safety net hai — 2008 se chal raha hai aur 2026 tak is me **1 crore se zyada khandan** shamil hain. Is ka maqsad ghareeb aur deserving khandano, khaas tor par **auraton**, ko maali madad dena hai. BISP ki sab se mashhoor scheme **Benazir Kafaalat** hai jis ke tehet registered mustahiq auraton ko **har 3 mah baad (sah-maahi) qist** milti hai.

2026 me Kafaalat ki qist **Rs 14,500** har quarter hai — March 2026 se ye rakam **Rs 13,500 se barha kar Rs 14,500** ki gayi hai (BISP ki published updates aur news reports ke mutabiq). Final amount hamesha aap ke official payment slip aur BISP ke announcements par hota hai — is guide ki rakam **October 2026 me check ki gayi** thi, qist lene se pehle official helpline **0800-26477** par confirm kar lein.

Sab se ahem baat: BISP ki **saari services bilkul muft** hain. Registration free hai, ehliyaat check free hai, aur qist se koi katouti nahi honi chahiye. Jo bhi shakhs registration ya qist ke badle paise mange, wo fraud hai — foran **0800-26477** par report karein.

## Ehliyaat Check Karne Ke 2 Sarkari Tareeqe

BISP ne apni ehliyaat (eligibility) check karne ke sirf do official tareeqe diye hain — dono muft hain aur dono se ek hi jawab milta hai:

### Tareeqa 1: SMS Se (Sab Se Aasaan)

1. Apne mobile phone me SMS wala app kholein.
2. Apna **13 hindsyon wala CNIC number baghair dashes ke** likhein — masalan `3520212345678`.
3. Isay **8171** par bhej dein.
4. Kuch seconds me BISP ka reply aayega jis me aap ki ehliyaat ki surat-haal batayi jayegi.

Ye SMS **bilkul muft** hai — balance ki zaroorat nahi, kisi bhi mobile network se chalta hai. Jo SIM aap ke apne CNIC par registered hai, behtar hai ke usi se bhejein.

### Tareeqa 2: Official Web Portal Se

1. **8171.bisp.gov.pk** par jayen — yahi BISP ka **waahid asli portal** hai, is ke ilawa koi aur website BISP ki nahi hai.
2. Apna 13 hindsyon wala CNIC number darj karein.
3. Tasveer me diya gaya code (captcha) sahi likhein aur "Check" dabayen.
4. Aap ke gharane ki ehliyaat, registration status aur qist ki surat-haal screen par aajayegi.

> **Note:** BISP ke naamo par **darjnon fake websites** bani hui hain jo CNIC number aur personal data mangti hain. Sirf **8171.bisp.gov.pk** use karein — portal sirf CNIC aur captcha mangta hai, **kabhi OTP, bank PIN, password ya koi fee nahi mangta**. Agar koi website aap se paise ya bank details mange, wahan se foran nikal jayen.

## Naya Registration: Sirf Tehsil Office Se (Online Form Nahi Hai)

Ye sab se aam ghalat-fehmi hai: **BISP me naya registration online nahi hota.** Internet par "BISP online registration form" ke naam par jo websites aur apps hain, wo sab ya to fake hain ya purani. BISP ki official policy ke mutabiq:

1. Apne **ilaaqe ke BISP Tehsil Office** jayen (har tehsil me BISP ka dynamic registration desk mojood hai).
2. Wahan **NSER Dynamic Survey** (National Socio-Economic Registry) mukammal karein — ye survey aap ke gharane ki maali surat-haal ka record banata hai.
3. Survey me ghar ke tamam afraad ki maloomat, aamdan, ghar ka kiraaya/milkiyat, aur degar details darj hongi.
4. Survey ke baad aap ke gharane ka **PMT score** (Proxy Means Test) banaya jayega — isi score par ehliyaat ka faisla hoga.
5. Survey mukammal hone par **8171 se confirmation SMS** aayega.

Registration **100% muft** hai — form bharne, survey ya biometric ka koi charge nahi hai. Survey me 15–30 minute lagte hain. Bheed se bachne ke liye subah jaldi jana behtar hai.

**Ehsaas Kafaalat me pehle se registered thay?** To aap ka record ab bhi valid hai — dobara registration ki zaroorat nahi. 2022 me program ka naam Ehsaas se wapas BISP ho gaya tha, lekin purane beneficiaries ka data as it is transfer ho gaya hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kya Le Kar Jayen

Tehsil office jate waqt ye kagzaat **asal** le kar jayen (photocopy bhi saath rakh lein to behtar):

- **Khatoon ka asal CNIC** (valid, expiry na hui ho) — payment khatoon ke naam par hi register hota hai.
- **Bachon ke B-Forms** (Family Registration Certificate bhi chal sakta hai).
- **Ghar ka bijli ya gas ka bill** (haal ka, pata confirm karne ke liye).
- **Mobile phone** jis ki SIM aap ke apne CNIC par registered ho.
- Agar ghar me koi **mazoor (disabled) shakhs** hai to us ka disability certificate — PMT score ki cut-off us surat me zyada hoti hai (neeche dekhein).

## Ehliyaat Ki Shartein: Kaun Mustahiq Hai

BISP ka faisla aap ke gharane ke **PMT score** par hota hai — ye score 0 se 100 tak hota hai (kam score = zyada ghareeb):

| Shart | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| **PMT score** | **32 ya is se kam** (standard) |
| **Mazoor shakhs wala gharana** | PMT score **37 ya is se kam** bhi qabil-e-qabool |
| **Bunyadi beneficiary** | Aurat — qist ghar ki khatoon ke naam par register hoti hai |
| **Valid CNIC** | CNIC ki expiry na hui ho, aur NSER survey mukammal ho |
| **Na-ehliyat** | Ghar ka koi fard sarkari mulazim ho, ya NADRA record me data mismatch ho |

Mard aam tor par **direct beneficiary nahi** ban sakte — payment ghar ki khatoon (biwi, maa, beti) ke naam par aati hai. Agar jawab "not eligible" aaye to aksar wajah ye hoti hai ke gharane ka survey hua hi nahi, ya PMT score cut-off se upar hai — dono surat me **Tehsil Office me dynamic survey** hal hai.

## Qist Kab Aur Kaise Milti Hai

Qist **har 3 mah baad** milti hai. Payment ke channels:

1. **BISP Digital Wallet** — beneficiary ke naam par banai gayi wallet; payment ki ittila SMS se milti hai.
2. **HBL ke biometric ATMs** — CNIC aur biometric (angootha) se raqam nikal sakte hain.
3. **Designated payment campsites** — BISP ke mukarar kardah payment centers/camps.

Qist milne se pehle **8171 se SMS** aata hai. Qist lene ke liye **koi fee ya katouti nahi** — agar koi agent "commission" ya "khidmat ki fee" ke naam par paise kaate, ye ghair-qanooni hai; foran **0800-26477** (Monday–Friday, toll-free) par shikayat karein.

**Benazir Taleemi Wazaif** (bachon ki taleem ka wazifa): Kafaalat wale khandano ke bachon ko school jaane par alag sah-maahi wazifa milta hai — **kam az kam 70% school attendance** lazmi hai. Registration ke liye bachay ka B-Form aur school enrollment ka saboot lekar Tehsil Office jayen. Ladkiyon ka wazifa ladkon se zyada hai, aur oonchi classes ka wazifa zyada hai.

## Qist Ruk Gayi Hai? Aam Wajuhat Aur Hal

Qist rukne ki sab se aam wajuhat ye hain — aur har ek ka hal bhi mojood hai:

1. **CNIC expire ho gaya:** Sab se aam wajah. [NADRA CNIC renewal guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) se card renew karwayen, phir payment auto-restore ho jayegi.
2. **NSER survey purana (3+ saal):** Dynamic survey dobara karwayen Tehsil Office se.
3. **Ghar ka koi fard sarkari mulazim nikal aaya:** NADRA/tax record me data update hone par na-ehliyat ho sakti hai.
4. **NADRA record me mismatch:** Naam, pata ya family record me farq — [FRC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur NADRA office se record durust karwayen.
5. **Biometric fail hona:** HBL ATM par angootha na match ho to NADRA se biometric update karwayen, ya payment campsite ka option use karein.

Shikayat ke liye BISP Complaint Cell aur toll-free helpline **0800-26477** available hai.

## Fraud Se Kaise Bachein: 5 Golden Rules

1. **BISP ke SMS sirf 8171 se aate hain.** Kisi aur number se "BISP" ke naam par aane wala message fraud hai — reply na karein, link na kholein.
2. **Registration aur check hamesha muft hai.** Jo agent fee mange, us ki report 0800-26477 par karein.
3. **OTP, bank PIN ya password kabhi kisi ko na dein** — na SMS par, na call par, na kisi website par.
4. **Sirf official portal 8171.bisp.gov.pk** par apna CNIC darj karein. Naam se milti-julti domains (jaise 8171ehsaas..., bisp8171...) se bachein.
5. **"Aap ki qist double ho gayi hai" ya "Rs 25,000 ka inaam"** jaise messages sab fraud hain — BISP aisi koi offer nahi deta.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**BISP 8171 ki ehliyaat SMS se kaise check karun?**
Apna 13 hindsyon wala CNIC number (baghair dashes ke) **8171** par SMS karein. Kuch seconds me reply aayega jis me aap ki ehliyaat ki surat-haal hogi. Ye service muft hai aur har mobile network par chalti hai. Online check ke liye **8171.bisp.gov.pk** par CNIC aur captcha darj karein.

**Kya BISP me online registration ho sakti hai?**
Nahi. BISP ki official policy ke mutabiq **naya registration sirf BISP Tehsil Office** me hota hai — wahan NSER Dynamic Survey mukammal hota hai aur biometric verification hoti hai. Online sirf **ehliyaat check** hoti hai (8171.bisp.gov.pk). "Online registration form" wali websites se bachein.

**2026 me BISP ki qist kitni hai?**
Benazir Kafaalat ki sah-maahi qist **Rs 14,500** hai — March 2026 se Rs 13,500 se barhai gayi (BISP ki published updates ke mutabiq, October 2026 me check ki gayi). Final amount hamesha official payment slip par hota hai; confirm karne ke liye helpline **0800-26477** par call karein.

**PMT score kitna hona chahiye?**
Aam gharane ke liye PMT score **32 ya is se kam**, aur jis gharane me koi mazoor (disabled) fard ho us ke liye **37 ya is se kam** hona chahiye. Score NSER survey ke data se banta hai.

**Meri qist kyun ruk gayi hai?**
Sab se aam wajuhat: expired CNIC, 3 saal se purana NSER survey, ghar ke kisi fard ka sarkari mulazim hona, ya NADRA record me mismatch. Apne asal kagzaat lekar qareebi **BISP Tehsil Office** jayen aur survey/data update karwayen — masla hal hote hi payment bahal ho jati hai.
