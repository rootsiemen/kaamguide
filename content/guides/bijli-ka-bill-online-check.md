## Bijli Ka Bill Online Kyun Check Karein

Har mahine bill ka intezar karna, phir kahin ghum ho jaye to bank se late fee ke saath ada karna — ye purani mushkilen thin. Ab Pakistan ki **tamam bijli companies** apna bill online deti hain — **bilkul muft**. Aap ko sirf aik cheez chahiye: **reference number**, jo aap ke purane bill par likha hota hai.

Online bill check karne se aap:

- Mahine ka **duplicate bill download** karke print kar sakte hain (ghum gaya ho to)
- Bill ki **akhri tareekh** aur units pehle se maloom kar sakte hain
- Purane bills ka record dekh sakte hain (kuch companies)

Is guide me sab se pehle aap ki company pehchanna, phir har bari company ka **step-by-step tareeqa** bataya gaya hai.

## Step 2: Reference Number Kya Hai Aur Kahan Milta Hai

Reference number aap ke bijli account ki **unique pehchan** hai:

- Zyada tar companies (IESCO, LESCO, FESCO waghera) me ye **14 hinsay (digits)** ka hota hai.
- Purane bill par aam tor par **upar ki taraf, "Reference No"** ke neeche likha hota hai.
- **K-Electric** me ye aap ka **Account Number** hota hai (13 hinsay).
- Reference number **meter ke saath permanent** hota hai — ghar badalne par naya meter ka naya number hota hai.

**Mobile par note kar lein** — har mahine yahi number kaam aayega, purane bill dhoondhne ki zaroorat nahi paregi.

## IESCO Ka Bill Check Karna (Islamabad / Rawalpindi)

1. **iesco.com.pk** kholein aur **"Bill"** ya **"View Bill"** ka option chunein.
2. Apna **14-hinson ka reference number** darj karein.
3. **Submit** par click karein — maujooda bill screen par aa jayega.
4. **Print/Download** karke PDF bacha lein — yehi duplicate bill bank me ada karne ke kaam aayega.

## LESCO Ka Bill Check Karna (Lahore)

1. **lesco.gov.pk** par jayen.
2. Homepage par **"Customer Bill"** ka option milega.
3. **Reference number** darj karein aur submit karein.
4. Bill nazar aayega — download ya print kar lein.

**Tip:** LESCO ki website par bill ke saath **load-shedding schedule** bhi check kar sakte hain — apne feeder ke hisab se timing maloom ho jati hai.

## FESCO, MEPCO, GEPCO Aur Baaki Companies

Tareeqa sab ka taqreeban **aik jaisa** hai:

1. Apni company ki official website kholein (fesco.com.pk, mepco.com.pk, gepco.com.pk, pesco.com.pk waghera).
2. **"Bill Inquiry" / "View Bill"** wala section dhoondein.
3. **Reference number** darj karein — bill aa jayega.

**Ahtiyat:** Google par search karne se aksar **nakli websites** pehle aa jati hain jo aap ka number aur data mangti hain. Hamesha **official website** (company ke naam wali .com.pk ya .gov.pk) istemal karein — bill check karna har jagah muft hai, koi site paise mange to wo nakli hai.

## K-Electric Ka Bill Check Karna (Karachi)

Karachi ke liye tareeqa thoda alag hai:

1. **ke.com.pk** par jayen — **"My Account" / bill** wala section kholein.
2. Apna **13-hinson ka account number** darj karein (purane bill par likha hota hai).
3. Bill screen par aa jayega — download kar lein.
4. Chaahein to KE ka **app (KE Live)** ya WhatsApp service bhi istemal kar sakte hain — wahan bill, complaint aur load-shedding update sab milta hai.

## Bill Ada Karna: Online Tareeqe

Bill dekhne ke baad ada karna bhi ghar bethe ho sakta hai — qatar me khara hone ki zaroorat nahi:

- **Bank apps:** Har bare bank ka app bijli ke bill ki option deta hai — reference number dalen, bill ki raqam khud aa jati hai.
- **JazzCash / Easypaisa:** "Utilities" me ja kar company chunein, reference number dalen — bill ada ho jayega.
- **ATM:** Bill payment ki sahulat har ATM par bhi hai.

**Ahem:** Online ada karne ke baad **transaction ID / screenshot** lazmi bacha lein — kisi ghalti ki soorat me yehi saboot hoga.

## Bill Zyada Aane Ki Aam Wajuhat (Aur Ilaj)

Agar bill achanak bohat zyada aa gaya ho to pehle ye check karein:

1. **Meter reading ghalat:** Bill par likhi reading apne meter ki asal reading se mila lein — farq ho to company ke office complaint karein, bill durust ho jata hai.
2. **Estimated bill:** Kabhi meter reader na aye to andaze par bill bhej diya jata hai — agle mahine adjustment ho jati hai, lekin complaint karna behtar hai.
3. **Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA):** Kuch mahino me FPA ki wajah se bill barh jata hai — ye government ki taraf se lagta hai, bill par saaf likha hota hai.
4. **Purana baqaya (arrears):** Bill me "Arrears" ka khana dekhein — pichli koi raqam baaki ho to wo jama ho kar aati hai.
5. **Zyada units:** Garmi me AC, ya nayi bijli wali cheez — units ka hisab bill par mojood hota hai, units gun lein.

## Complaint Kahan Karein

- **Company ka helpline:** Har bill par company ka phone number likha hota hai.
- **Wafaqi Mohtasib / NEPRA:** Bill durust na ho raha ho to **NEPRA** ko online complaint kar sakte hain (CMS portal: cms.nepra.org.pk) — ye bilkul muft hai aur companies ko jawab dena parta hai.
- **Apne ilaake ka SDO office:** Meter ya connection ka masla ho to wahan jana parega.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya online bill check karna muft hai?**
Ji haan — bilkul muft. Koi website paise mange to wo nakli hai.

**Reference number ghum gaya, kya karun?**
Koi bhi purana bill dhoondein — us par likha hota hai. Na mile to company ke office ja kar CNIC dikha kar reference number maloom kar sakte hain.

**Duplicate bill se bank me ada ho sakta hai?**
Ji haan — online download kiya gaya bill hi duplicate bill hai, bank aur post office me qabool hota hai.

**Kya kirayedaar apne naam ka bill check kar sakta hai?**
Bill malik-e-makan ke naam par hota hai, lekin **reference number se koi bhi** bill check aur ada kar sakta hai.

**Bill me "Units" ghalat lagte hon to?**
Meter ki asal reading se mila lein — farq ho to SDO office ya NEPRA portal par complaint karein.

**Do mahine ka bill aik saath kyun aa gaya?**
Kabhi meter reading late ho to do mahino ka jama bill aata hai — bill par "billing month" ghor se dekhein, shak ho to complaint karein.

## Meter Reader Se Ghalat Reading Ka Masla (Tafseel Se)

Pakistan me meter reading ki ghaltiyan bohat aam hain — aur ye seedha aap ki jeb par asar dalti hain. Asal misaal se samjhein: agar aap ka asal istemal 200 units hai aur reader ne 500 units likh diye, to aap ko zyada units ke mehngi slab par bill ada karna parega.

**Apni hifazat khud karein:**

1. **Har mahine meter ki tasveer lein:** Reader ke aane wali tareekh ke aas-paas apne meter ki photo date ke saath bacha lein — yehi sab se mazboot saboot hai.
2. **Bill par reading mila lein:** "Present Reading" ko meter ki asal reading se compare karein — farq ho to **foran SDO office** jayen.
3. **Self-reading bhejein:** Kuch companies mobile app/SMS se khud reading bhejne ki sahulat deti hain — apne ilaake me pata karein.

**Complaint ka tareeqa:** SDO office me **written complaint** (darakhast) dein apni meter ki tasveer ke saath — zabani complaint ka record nahi hota. Masla hal na ho to **NEPRA CMS portal** (cms.nepra.org.pk) par online complaint karein — ye bilkul muft hai aur DISCO ko jawab dena parta hai.

## Naya Bijli Connection Lene Ka Mukhtasir Tareeqa

Agar naye ghar me bijli ka connection hi nahi:

1. Apni DISCO (IESCO/LESCO waghera) ke office se **new connection ka form** lein ya online download karein.
2. CNIC, ghar ki malkiat/kiraye ke kagzaat ke saath jama karwayen.
3. **Demand notice** milega — security deposit aur installation charges bank me ada karein.
4. DISCO ki team **survey** karke meter lagayegi — ye process ilaaqe ke hisab se hafton se mahino tak le sakta hai.

**Kharcha:** Demand notice ki raqam load (units ki capacity) par munhasir hoti hai — ghar ke liye aam tor par chand hazar hote hain. Office se pehle **estimate** zaroor le lein.

## Agla Qadam: Bill Payment Ka Auto-Reminder

Bill late hona aam tor par bhoolne ki wajah se hota hai, raqam ki kami se nahi. Apne phone par **har mahine ka reminder** laga dein:

1. Bill aane ki tareekh (bill ki copy par likhi hoti hai) se aik din baad ka reminder.
2. **Akhri tareekh se 2 din pehle** ka aik reminder — taake late fee kabhi na lage.
3. Kuch banks ki apps me **auto-pay** ka option bhi hota hai — mukhtasar si limit ke saath on kar dein.

Aik saal ki late fee jama karein to hisab karein: chhote surcharge bhi mahino me hazaron bana dete hain. Reminder muft hai, late fee nahi.
