## IBCC Attestation Kya Hai — Seedha Jawab

**IBCC (Inter Boards Coordination Commission)** Pakistan ka woh idarah hai jo **Matric (SSC) aur Intermediate (HSSC) certificates** ki attestation karta hai. Agar aap ki taleem Matric ya Inter tak hai aur aap ko bahir mulk nokri, visa ya admission ke liye documents attest karwane hain, to pehla stop IBCC hai — HEC nahi. **HEC sirf university degrees attest karta hai; Matric/Inter ki attestation sirf IBCC se hoti hai.** IBCC ki attestation ke baghair MOFA (Foreign Office) bhi aap ke school certificates attest nahi karta.

Zyada tar log yahin ghalti karte hain: degree ho to HEC, lekin Matric/Inter ki sanad le kar HEC chale jate hain aur waqt zaya hota hai. Pehle ye tay kar lein ke document kya hai — usi se idarah tay hota hai.

- **Matric / Inter certificate ya result card** → IBCC
- **University degree / transcript** → [HEC Degree Attestation](/guides/hec-degree-attestation)
- **Bahir ki degree Pakistan me chalani hai** → [HEC Equivalence Certificate](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate)

## Fee (2026 Ke Rates)

| Document | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Har **asal certificate** (Matric ya Inter sanad) | **Rs 1,200** |
| Har **photocopy** ki attestation | **Rs 600** |

Ye rates Pakistan ki Senate me diye gaye official jawab aur IBCC ke published schedule ke mutabiq hain. Result card aur certificate **alag alag documents** ginay jate hain — dono attest karwane hain to fee dono ki lagegi. Payment portal par online ya designated tareeqe se hoti hai; final amount application ke waqt portal hi confirm karta hai.

**Hisab ki misal:** Matric certificate + Inter certificate = 2 documents = **Rs 2,400**. Dono ke result cards bhi hon to kul 4 documents = Rs 4,800.

## Sab Se Pehla Qadam: Board Verification

IBCC attestation se pehle aap ke board (BISE) ki verification lazmi hai:

1. Jis board se Matric/Inter kiya hai (BISE Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi, FBISE waghera), wahan se apne certificate ki **verification** karwayen.
2. Aksar boards ab verification **seedha electronic tareeqe se IBCC ko bhej dete hain** — sealed envelope ka purana silsila khatam hota ja raha hai.
3. Board verification ke baghair IBCC application aage nahi barhti — pehle ye qadam pura karein.

## Zaroori Documents

- **Asal Matric certificate (sanad)** — jo attest karwana hai
- **Asal Intermediate certificate (sanad)** — agar Inter bhi attest karwana hai
- **Result cards** (agar un ki bhi attestation chahiye)
- **CNIC** ki copy
- Board verification ka saboot (electronic ya sealed, jo board de)

**Lamination wali warning:** Agar certificate **laminated** hai to pehle lamination utarwani hogi — IBCC laminated document attest nahi karta. Ghar par khud lamination utarne ki koshish na karein, certificate phat sakta hai; board se duplicate nikalwana par jayega.

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Online Application

1. **attest.ibcc.edu.pk** par jayen aur apna account bana kar application bharein.
2. Apne documents select karein — sirf wahi jo waqai attest karwane hain, kyunke fee per document hai.
3. Fee pay karein aur application submit karein — aap ko aik **case/application number** milega, use sambhal kar rakhein.

### Step 2: Document Jama Karwana — Teen Tareeqe

1. **Walk-in:** Khud IBCC office ja kar documents dein — aam tor par **usi din** attestation ho jati hai (office ke timings aur rush par munhasir hai). Sirf applicant khud ya **blood relative** (walid, walida, bhai, behen — asal CNIC ke sath) documents jama kar sakta hai.
2. **Courier:** Documents courier se bhejein — wapas ane me **7-10 working days** lag sakte hain. Self-addressed envelope shamil karein.
3. **Express/Evening service:** Islamabad aur Lahore me express service mojood hai (zyada fee ke sath) — portal par availability confirm karein.

IBCC ke offices Islamabad (head office), Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, Quetta aur Bahawalpur me hain — kisi bhi office se attestation karwayi ja sakti hai, board koi sa bhi ho.

### Step 3: Tracking Aur Wapsi

Application number se **portal par status track** karein. Attested document par IBCC ki stamp aur signature a jate hain — yehi document phir MOFA attestation ke liye tayyar hai.

## IBCC Ke Baad Kya Hota Hai

Bahir mulk ke liye aam silsila ye hai:

1. **Board verification** (ye guide ka pehla qadam)
2. **IBCC attestation** (ye guide)
3. **MOFA attestation** — Foreign Office IBCC ki stamp ko attest karta hai
4. **Embassy attestation** — jis mulk jana hai

Poora silsila visa deadline se pehle shuru karein — har marhale me din lagte hain. Degree walon ka silsila IBCC ki bajaye [HEC attestation](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) se shuru hota hai, aur attest shuda degree ki online tasdeeq ka tareeqa [HEC degree verification guide](/guides/hec-degree-verification-online) me diya hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Waqt Zaya Karti Hain

1. **HEC chale jana:** Matric/Inter ke liye HEC nahi, IBCC hai — sab se aam ghalti.
2. **Board verification skip karna:** Baghair verification ke application ruk jati hai.
3. **Laminated certificate le jana:** Pehle lamination ka masla hal karein.
4. **Naam ka mismatch:** CNIC aur certificate par naam ki spelling aik jaisi honi chahiye — farq ho to pehle board se tas-heeh karwayen.
5. **Sirf certificate, result card nahi:** Kuch embassies dono mangti hain — pehle confirm karke dono attest karwa lein, dobara ana nahi parega.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**IBCC attestation ki fee kitni hai?**
Rs 1,200 per asal certificate aur Rs 600 per photocopy. Matric aur Inter dono ke certificates attest karwane hon to kul Rs 2,400 baneinge.

**Kya IBCC degree bhi attest karta hai?**
Nahi. IBCC sirf Matric aur Intermediate (school/college level) ke documents attest karta hai. University degree ki attestation HEC se hoti hai.

**IBCC attestation me kitna waqt lagta hai?**
Walk-in par aam tor par usi din ho jati hai. Courier se 7-10 working days lag sakte hain. Board verification ka waqt is ke ilawa hai.

**Kya koi aur meri taraf se documents jama kar sakta hai?**
Sirf khoon ka rishta (walid, walida, bhai, behen) apne asal CNIC ke sath jama kar sakta hai. Agents ke zariye jama karwana IBCC qabool nahi karta.

**What is the IBCC attestation fee in Pakistan?**
The IBCC attestation fee is Rs 1,200 per original Matric or Intermediate certificate and Rs 600 per photocopy. Apply online at attest.ibcc.edu.pk — your board (BISE) must verify the certificate before IBCC attests it.

**Board verification pehle kyun lazmi hai?**
Kyunkay IBCC khud certificate ki asliyat board se confirm karta hai — jab tak board verification na bheje, IBCC stamp nahi lagata. Ye jaali documents rokne ke liye hai.
