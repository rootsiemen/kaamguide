## Pehli Baat: PM Youth Laptop Scheme Kya Hai

**PM Youth Laptop Scheme** Hukumat-e-Pakistan ki sab se bari talba (students) ke liye muft laptop scheme hai. Ye scheme **Prime Minister's Youth Programme (PMYP)** ke tehet **Higher Education Commission (HEC)** chalati hai, aur is ka maqsad ye hai ke mulk ke sarkari (public sector) universities me parhne wale qabil talba ko parhai aur research ke liye aik behtareen laptop muft diya jaye — taake online classes, assignments, research aur freelancing ke liye un ke paas apna aala ho.

2026 ke phase — **Phase 4** — me takreeban **100,000 laptops** taqseem kiye ja rahe hain, jo pehle phases se aik bara izafa hai. Selection sirf **academic merit** par hoti hai: na koi sifarish chalti hai, na pehle aane wale ko pehle milta hai. Aap ki CGPA ya marks hi faisla karte hain ke aap merit list me aate hain ya nahi.

Apply karna **bilkul muft** hai aur sirf online hota hai — official portal **laptop.pmyp.gov.pk** par (yehi portal **laptop.hec.gov.pk** se bhi khulta hai). Koi form kisi office me jama nahi hota aur kisi agent ki zaroorat nahi hai.

**Aik zaroori wazahat:** is guide ke facts 27 November 2026 ko check kiye gaye hain. Is din official portal **laptop.pmyp.gov.pk** hamari taraf se nahi khula (browser me timeout aaya) aur **laptop.hec.gov.pk** bhi load nahi hua — is liye ehliyaat ki hatmi had (CGPA cut-off) aur aakhri tareekh jaise numbers neeche mutadid aazad (independent) guides aur aik university ke official notice se mutabiq kar ke likhe gaye hain. Apply karne se pehle official portal par taaza haalat zaroor check kar lein. Guide ke aakhir me is ka zikr dobara hai.

## Teeno Laptop Schemes Me Farq Samajh Lein

Pakistan me is waqt teen alag laptop schemes ki khabrein chal rahi hain, aur log in ko aapas me mila dete hain. Ghalat scheme par apply karna waqt zaya karna hai — is liye pehle farq samajh lein:

| Scheme | Kaun chalata hai | Kin ke liye | Kitne laptops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **PM Youth Laptop Scheme (Phase 4)** | HEC, PM Youth Programme (federal) | HEC-recognized **public sector** universities ke regular students (PhD, MS/MPhil, 4/5-year bachelor) | Takreeban **100,000** |
| **CM Punjab Laptop Scheme** | Hukumat-e-Punjab | Punjab ke universities/colleges ke talba | **110,000** se zyada |
| **PM Chromebook Scheme** | IBCC ke zariye (reported) | Class 9 ke talba (50% ya zyada marks) | Takreeban **500,000** Chromebooks (reported, abhi mukammal confirm nahi) |

**Ye guide sirf pehli scheme — federal PM Youth Laptop Scheme — ke baare me hai.** Agar aap Punjab ke student hain to aap dono (federal aur CM Punjab) schemes ke liye alag alag apply kar sakte hain, bashart-e-ke aap dono ki shartein poori karte hon. CM Punjab scheme ki tafseel hamari [CM Punjab Laptop Scheme guide](/guides/cm-punjab-laptop-scheme-apply-online) me hai, aur Chromebook scheme ki tafseel [PM Youth Chromebook guide](/guides/pm-youth-chromebook-scheme-apply-online) me hai.

**Aik aur farq yaad rakhein:** PM Youth Laptop Scheme me laptop aap ko **HEC ki merit list** se milta hai, jab ke CM Punjab scheme ki apni alag portal aur apni merit hoti hai. Dono ke form, portal aur tareekhein alag hain — aik ka form bharne se dosre me apply nahi hota.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Ehliyaat

Is scheme ki ehliyaat seedhi hai, lekin sakht hai. Neeche di gayi tamam shartein poori honi chahiye:

**1. Pakistani hona lazmi hai:** Aap Pakistani shehri hon — Azad Jammu-o-Kashmir aur Gilgit-Baltistan ke talba bhi eligible hain.

**2. Public sector university ka regular student hona:** Aap ko **HEC se recognized kisi public sector university ya degree awarding institute (DAI)** ka regular student hona chahiye. Private university ke students is scheme ke liye eligible nahi hain (mutadid guides is par mutafiq hain). Aap ka naam aap ki university ke record me mojood hona chahiye, kyun ke baad me university hi aap ki tasdeeq kare gi.

**3. Degree level:** Sirf ye degree programmes eligible hain:
- **PhD**
- **MS / MPhil** (18-year equivalent)
- **4-year ya 5-year bachelor** (BS/BBA waghera)

Diploma, short course ya 2-year programme ke students is scheme me nahi aate.

**4. Minimum merit (CGPA ya marks):** Aam taur par batayi jane wali had ye hai:
- Semester system: **2.80 CGPA out of 4.00**
- Annual system: **60% marks**

**Ahem note:** Kuch sources me is phase ke liye zyada had — **3.00 CGPA ya 70% marks** — bhi likhi hui hai. Sources me is farq ki wajah se hum aap ko yehi mashwara dete hain ke form bharne se pehle **official portal par is waqt ki hatmi had zaroor check kar lein**. Jo bhi had ho, yaad rakhein ke selection merit par hoti hai — had se zyada marks hon to bhi final merit list aap ke number aur aap ke degree category me muqable par munhasir hoti hai.

**5. Pehle laptop na mila ho:** Agar aap ko is scheme ke kisi pehle phase me laptop mil chuka hai, to aap dobara apply nahi kar sakte. System aap ka record check kar ke dosri application khud hi reject kar deta hai.

**6. Valid CNIC ya B-Form:** 18 saal ya us se zyada umar ke students ke liye valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) number lazmi hai; 18 saal se kam umar ke liye [B-Form](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) number istemal hota hai. CNIC/B-Form ka number wohi likhein jo NADRA ke record me hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Maloomat

Form bharne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein taake aap ka kaam adhoora na reh jaye. Zyada tar maloomat aap ko form me likhni hoti hai — koi bari file upload karne ki zaroorat aam taur par nahi parti, lekin apne kagzaat sath rakhna behtar hai:

| Kagaz / Maloomat | Kis liye chahiye |
| --- | --- |
| Valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ya B-Form number | Shanakht aur pehle phase ka record check |
| University ka naam (HEC list se) | Aap ki university ki tasdeeq |
| Department aur degree programme | Degree level aur category ka faisla |
| Current semester aur enrollment ki haalat | Aap regular student hain ya nahi |
| Academic record (CGPA ya marks, transcript ke mutabiq) | Merit ka hisab |
| Active email address | Merit list aur ahem paighamat |
| Active mobile number (apne naam ki SIM) | OTP aur status ki khabrein |

**CGPA likhne me ehtiyat:** Apni **CGPA** likhein, GPA nahi — university ke notice me is par khaas zor diya gaya hai. Apni transcript par likhi hui CGPA hi likhein; andaze se ya GPA ko CGPA bana kar likhna ghalat hoga aur university ki tasdeeq me pakra jaye ga to application reject ho sakti hai.

**Email aur mobile number:** Woh email aur mobile number dein jo aap ke paas rehta ho aur band na ho. Merit list, verification aur laptop milne ki khabar isi par aaye gi. Kisi dosre ka number ya band email dena aap ka nuqsan hai.

## Online Apply Ka Tareeqa: Step-by-Step

Poora tareeqa online hai aur aam taur par 20–30 minute me mukammal ho jata hai. Steps is tarteeb se karein:

**Step 1: Official portal kholein.** Apne browser me **laptop.pmyp.gov.pk** type karein. Website ka pata kholne se pehle dobara check kar lein — is naam se milti-julti nakli websites bhi hain jo aap ka CNIC aur maloomat chura sakti hain. Sirf isi official portal par bharosa karein. Agar portal is waqt load na ho raha ho to kuch der baad dobara koshish karein — kabhi kabhi portal rash ki wajah se slow ho jata hai.

**Step 2: Account banayein ya login karein.** Portal par **New Registration** ya **Create Account** ka option mile ga. Apna CNIC ya B-Form number aur active mobile number/email de kar account banayein. Agar aap ne pehle kisi phase me register kiya tha to naya account banane ke bajaye **us purane account me login kar ke** apni maloomat — khaas taur par current semester aur CGPA — update karein. Do account banana ghalti hai.

**Step 3: CNIC/B-Form verification.** System aap ka CNIC ya B-Form number check kare ga — is se ye pata chalta hai ke aap ko pehle kisi phase me laptop to nahi mila. Number bilkul sahi likhein, baghair dash ke, sirf 13 hindse.

**Step 4: Personal maloomat dein.** Apna poora naam (bilkul waisa jaisa CNIC par hai), tareekh-e-pedaish, walid ka naam, mustaqil aur mojooda pata, aur rabte ki maloomat darj karein.

**Step 5: University aur programme chunein.** Apni university ko HEC ki list se chunein, phir department, degree level (PhD, MS/MPhil ya bachelor) aur current semester darj karein. University ka naam khud type karne ke bajaye list se chunna behtar hai — is se tasdeeq me ghalti nahi hoti.

**Step 6: Academic record darj karein.** Apni CGPA (semester system) ya marks percentage (annual system) apni transcript ke mutabiq likhein. Aik dafa phir: CGPA likhein, GPA nahi. Tamam numbers dobara check kar lein — ghalat CGPA likhna disqualification ki sab se aam wajah hai.

**Step 7: Form review kar ke submit karein.** Submit karne se pehle poora form aik dafa dobara parh lein. Koi spelling ki ghalti, ghalat number ya reh jane wala hissa ho to usi waqt theek kar lein. Phir **Submit** par click karein.

**Step 8: Tracking number sambhal kar rakhein.** Submit ke baad aap ko aik **tracking number** mile ga — us ko screenshot le kar ya kagaz par likh kar sambhal lein. Isi number se aap apni application aur merit list ka status check karein ge. Ye number kho jaye to status dekhna mushkil ho jata hai.

**Status kaise check karein:** Deadline ke baad HEC **provisional merit lists** tayyar karti hai. Apna status dekhne ke liye dobara usi portal par apne account se login karein aur tracking number se check karein. Agar aap ka naam provisional list me aaye to aap ki university aap ke record ki tasdeeq kare gi, aur us ke baad final list aaye gi.

## Fees, Merit List Aur Waqt: Kitna Kharcha, Kitna Time

**Fees: bilkul muft.** PM Youth Laptop Scheme ke liye apply karne ki **koi fee nahi hai** — na form ki, na registration ki. Laptop bhi muft milta hai. Agar koi shakhs ya website is scheme ke naam par aap se koi fee, "processing charges" ya "guarantee" ke paise mange to woh dhoka de raha hai — foran us se door rahein aur kisi ko apna CNIC, OTP ya bank ki maloomat na dein.

| Cheez | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| Apply karne ki fee | **Muft (Rs 0)** |
| Laptop ki qeemat | **Muft** — scheme ke tehet diya jata hai |
| Online form bharne ka waqt | Kagzaat tayyar hon to **20–30 minute** |
| Deadline ke baad provisional merit list | HEC tayyar karti hai; waqt ka elaan portal par hota hai |
| University verification | Provisional list ke baad, har university apne students ki tasdeeq karti hai |
| Laptop ki taqseem | Final list ke baad, university ke zariye |

**Merit list kaise banti hai:** Deadline ke baad HEC tamam applications ki academic merit tayyar karti hai. Phir provisional merit list portal par lagti hai. Is ke baad har university apne muntakhib students ke record — enrollment, degree, CGPA — ki tasdeeq karti hai. Jo student tasdeeq me ghalat sabit ho, us ka naam list se nikal diya jata hai. Aakhir me final list ke mutabiq laptops taqseem hote hain. Is liye form me sahi maloomat dena hi aap ki asal tayyari hai.

**Aakhri tareekh:** Is waqt ki hatmi aakhri tareekh hamari taraf se portal se verify nahi ho saki (portal 27 November 2026 ko load nahi hua). Kisi bhi website par likhi tareekh par bharosa karne se pehle **laptop.pmyp.gov.pk** par taaza haalat zaroor check kar lein — portal par jo tareekh likhi ho, wahi hatmi hai. Aam mashwara ye hai ke form aakhri din ka wait kiye baghair jald bhar dein, kyun ke aakhri dinon me portal par rash ki wajah se slow ho jata hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

**1. Ghalat scheme par apply karna:** Federal PM Youth Laptop Scheme, CM Punjab Laptop Scheme aur PM Chromebook Scheme ko aik samajhna sab se aam ghalti hai. Pehle upar di gayi table se apni scheme pehchanein, phir us ka form bharein.

**2. Nakli website par form bharna:** "PM laptop scheme" search kar ke pehli milne wali website par apna CNIC daal dena khatarnak hai. Sirf **laptop.pmyp.gov.pk** official hai. WhatsApp ya Facebook par forward hone wale link par bharosa karne se pehle us ka address check karein.

**3. GPA ko CGPA likhna:** University ke notice me saaf kaha gaya hai ke **CGPA** istemal karein, GPA nahi. Ye farq chhota lagta hai lekin tasdeeq me pakra jane par application reject ho sakti hai.

**4. Dosra account banana:** Agar aap pehle se registered hain to purana account update karein. Naya account banane se aap ka record duplicate ho jata hai aur masla hota hai.

**5. Kisi agent ko paisa dena:** Ye scheme muft hai. Jo shakhs "jaldi laptop" ya "guarantee selection" ke naam par paisa mange, woh dhoka de raha hai. Selection sirf merit par hoti hai — koi agent is ko nahi badal sakta.

**6. Tracking number na sambhalna:** Form submit kar ke tracking number bhool jana aam ghalti hai. Is number ke baghair aap apna status check nahi kar sakte — is ko foran save karein.

**7. Ghalat ya purani maloomat dena:** Purani semester ya purani CGPA likhna, ya university ka naam ghalat likhna, tasdeeq me masla paida karta hai. Form bharne se pehle apni transcript aur university record se maloomat mila lein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**PM Youth Laptop Scheme Phase 4 me kitne laptops mil rahe hain?**
2026 ke Phase 4 me takreeban 100,000 laptops taqseem kiye ja rahe hain. Ye scheme HEC, Prime Minister's Youth Programme ke tehet, sirf public sector universities ke regular students ke liye chalati hai. Hatmi tadaad aur tafseel official portal laptop.pmyp.gov.pk par check karein.

**PM Youth Laptop Scheme ke liye CGPA kitni chahiye?**
Aam taur par batayi jane wali had semester system me 2.80 CGPA out of 4.00 hai, ya annual system me 60% marks. Kuch sources me 3.00 CGPA ya 70% marks bhi likhe hain — is liye form bharne se pehle official portal par is waqt ki hatmi had zaroor confirm kar lein. Selection sirf merit par hoti hai, is liye jitni behtar CGPA, utna behtar chance.

**PM Youth Laptop Scheme me apply karne ki fee kitni hai?**
Koi fee nahi hai — apply karna bilkul muft hai aur laptop bhi muft milta hai. Agar koi aap se is scheme ke naam par fee mange to woh fraud hai; apna CNIC, OTP ya bank ki maloomat kisi ko na dein.

**CM Punjab Laptop Scheme aur PM Youth Laptop Scheme me kya farq hai?**
Dono alag schemes hain: PM Youth Laptop Scheme federal hai, HEC chalati hai aur public sector universities ke students ke liye hai (portal laptop.pmyp.gov.pk); CM Punjab Laptop Scheme Punjab hukumat ki hai aur Punjab ke students ke liye hai (portal cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk). Dono ke form aur merit alag hain. CM Punjab scheme ki tafseel hamari [CM Punjab Laptop Scheme guide](/guides/cm-punjab-laptop-scheme-apply-online) me hai.

**Pehle phase me laptop mil chuka ho to dobara apply kar sakte hain?**
Nahi. Agar aap ko is scheme ke kisi pehle phase me laptop mil chuka hai to aap dobara apply nahi kar sakte — system aap ka record check kar ke dosri application reject kar deta hai. Is liye apna CNIC number sahi likhein taake record saaf rahe.
