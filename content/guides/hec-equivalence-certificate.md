## HEC Equivalence Kya Hai — Seedha Jawab

Agar aap ne degree **Pakistan se bahir** kisi university se li hai (UK, China, Malaysia, Gulf, Europe, America — koi bhi mulk), to Pakistan me woh degree usi waqt qabool hoti hai jab **HEC (Higher Education Commission)** us ka **Equivalence Certificate (equivalence letter)** jari kare. Ye letter sirf aik baat batata hai: aap ki bahir wali degree Pakistan ke system me **kis level** ki hai — Bachelor ke barabar hai ya Master ke barabar. **HEC equivalence fee Rs 5,000 per degree hai** aur poora process online hai — eservices.hec.gov.pk se, koi hard copy HEC bhejne ki zaroorat nahi.

Equivalence aur attestation do alag cheezen hain, aur yahin sab se zyada confusion hoti hai:

- **Pakistani degree** → [HEC Degree Attestation](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) (degree ki asliyat ki tasdeeq)
- **Bahir ki degree** → **Equivalence** (ye guide) — degree ka Pakistani level tay hota hai
- **Bahir ki school taleem (O/A Levels waghera)** → IBCC equivalence — school level ka kaam [IBCC](/guides/ibcc-attestation-ka-tareeqa) karta hai, HEC nahi

## Kis Ko Equivalence Lazmi Hai

- **Sarkari nokri** ke liye apply karne walon ko — advertisement me aam tor par "HEC equivalence lazmi" likha hota hai
- **Pakistan me PhD ya aagli taleem** ke admission ke liye
- **Promotion aur service records** ke liye jahan foreign degree darj karwani ho
- Kayi **private idaron aur banks** ko bhi joining par equivalence letter mangna parta hai

## Fee

| Item | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Har **degree/diploma** ki equivalence | **Rs 5,000** (non-refundable) |

Ye fee HEC ki apni official maloomat ke mutabiq hai aur **1-Link (1-Bill)** ke zariye online pay hoti hai. Aik ahem baat: agar aap ki foreign university degree ki tasdeeq ke liye **apni verification fee** mangti hai, woh Rs 5,000 ke **ilawa** hai aur aap ko seedha university ko deni hogi — HEC ki fee me woh shamil nahi.

**Hisab ki misal:** Bahir se Bachelor + Master dono ki equivalence chahiye to 2 applications, kul **Rs 10,000** — aur pehle Bachelor ki equivalence hogi, phir Master ki.

## Zaroori Documents

- **Foreign degree** (asal + saaf scan) jis ki equivalence chahiye
- **Transcript** (degree ke sath wali)
- **CNIC ya NICOP**
- **Passport** — main page aur **visa/entry-exit stamps** jo sabit karein ke aap taleem ke doran us mulk me mojood the
- **Purani taleem:** Matric aur Inter certificates; agar woh bhi bahir ki hain to un ki **IBCC equivalence** pehle chahiye hogi
- Master ki equivalence ke liye: **Bachelor degree pehle se equivalent/attested** honi chahiye

Degree ya transcript English me na ho to **official translation** sath lagani hogi.

## Step-by-Step Online Process

### Step 1: Portal Par Account Aur Profile

1. **eservices.hec.gov.pk** par Sign Up karein — naam, CNIC aur tamam maloomat documents ke harf-ba-harf mutabiq bharein.
2. Apna academic profile mukammal karein — purani taleem se shuru karke foreign degree tak.

### Step 2: Equivalence Application

1. **"Equivalence Services"** me ja kar **"Foreign Degree Recognition/Equivalence"** chunein.
2. Degree ki tafseel bharein, documents ke saaf scans upload karein aur application submit karein.
3. **"Task Assigned"** wale hisse se fee challan/task khulega — **1-Link se Rs 5,000 pay karein aur payment verify karein.** Payment verify kiye baghair application process nahi hoti.

### Step 3: University Verification — Sab Se Ahem Marhala

Equivalence letter tab hi banta hai jab aap ki **foreign university khud HEC ko degree ki asliyat confirm kare**:

1. University HEC ko **seedha email** karti hai (fe-degree-verification@hec.gov.pk) ke ye degree asal hai — ya university ke official verification link/sealed letter ke zariye tasdeeq hoti hai.
2. Apni university ke registrar/examinations office ko khud follow up karein — apna **HEC application ID** un ko dein taake wohi reference tasdeeq me likha jaye.
3. University jawab na de to letter ruk jata hai — ye marhala aap ke apne follow-up se tez hota hai, HEC ke daftar jaane se nahi.

### Step 4: Equivalence Letter Download

Scrutiny aur verification mukammal hone par **system-generated equivalence letter** aap ke dashboard par a jata hai — wahan se download karein. Letter par sirf degree ka **level** likha hota hai; **CGPA ya grades nahi hote** — ye normal hai, HEC sirf level tay karta hai. Letter ki online verification bhi portal se ho jati hai — verification ka tareeqa [HEC degree verification guide](/guides/hec-degree-verification-online) me diya hai.

## Kitna Waqt Lagta Hai

Aam tor par **15-30 working days** — lekin asal raftar university verification par hai. University foran jawab de to letter jaldi ban jata hai; university sust ho to mahina se zyada bhi lag sakta hai. Sarkari nokri ki deadline ho to kam az kam **2 mahine pehle** application de dein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Attestation aur equivalence ka farq na samajhna:** Pakistani degree par equivalence nahi lagti, bahir ki degree ki "attestation" HEC Pakistan me nahi hoti — us ki equivalence hoti hai.
2. **Payment verify na karna:** Fee pay karke "Task Assigned" me verify na karein to application atki rehti hai.
3. **University ko follow up na karna:** Verification university ke jawab par ruki hoti hai — registrar ko email karna aap ka kaam hai.
4. **CGPA ki umeed:** Letter par grades nahi ate — employer ko ye pehle se wazeh kar dein ke HEC sirf level deta hai.
5. **Incomplete degree:** Jo degree mukammal hi nahi hui, us ki equivalence nahi banti. Us surat me sirf "University Recognition Status letter" mil sakta hai (us ki koi fee nahi).

## Aik Misal: Malaysia Se Bachelor Karne Wale Ka Safar

Aik talib ilm ki misal lein jis ne Malaysia ki university se Bachelor degree li aur wapas a kar Pakistan me sarkari nokri ke liye apply karna tha — advertisement me HEC equivalence lazmi thi:

1. **Deadline pehle gin li:** Us ne nokri ki aakhri tareekh se do mahine pehle equivalence ki application di, kyunkay use maloom tha ke ye process university ke jawab par munhasir hai.
2. **Documents pehle tayyar kiye:** Foreign degree, transcript, passport ki woh copies jin par taleem ke doran ki entry-exit stamps wazeh thein, CNIC aur Matric/Inter certificates — sab ke saaf scans pehle se bana liye.
3. **Application aur payment:** eservices.hec.gov.pk par account bana kar profile mukammal ki, Equivalence Services se application bhari, **Rs 5,000 fee 1-Link se pay ki aur "Task Assigned" me payment verify bhi kiya** — yehi qadam aksar log bhool jate hain.
4. **University ko follow up:** Application ID le kar apni Malaysian university ke registrar ko khud email kiya, taake university ki tasdeeq HEC ko waqt par pohche. University ke jawab ke baad hi us ki file aage barhi.
5. **Letter download:** Scrutiny mukammal hone par equivalence letter dashboard par a gaya — us par sirf degree ka level (Bachelor ke barabar) likha tha, CGPA nahi, aur yehi woh maloomat thi jo nokri ke liye darkar thi.

Is misal ki sab se ahem baat waqt ki planning hai: fee aur application us ke apne ikhtiyar me the, lekin university ka jawab nahi — is liye margin rakhna hi aqalmandi hai.

## Submit Karne Se Pehle Ki Aakhri Checklist

Application submit karne se pehle ye table dekh lein — zyada tar rukawatein inhi cheezon se ati hain:

| Cheez | Tasdeeq kar lein |
| --- | --- |
| Degree aur transcript ke scans | Saaf aur parhne ke qabil hon, English na ho to official translation sath ho |
| Passport ki copies | Main page ke sath taleem ke doran ki visa/entry-exit stamps wali pages |
| Matric/Inter certificates | Bahir ki hon to pehle [IBCC equivalence certificate](/guides/ibcc-equivalence-certificate) tayyar ho |
| Bachelor degree (Master ki application ke liye) | Pehle se equivalent ya HEC-attested ho — sequence ulta nahi chalta |
| Profile ki maloomat | Naam aur tafseel documents ke harf-ba-harf mutabiq hon |
| Payment ki verification | Fee pay karne ke baad "Task Assigned" me verify lazmi karein |
| University ka rabta | Registrar/examinations office ka email pehle se nikaal kar rakhein |

Degree lene se pehle ya admission ke waqt hi ye bhi check kar lena chahiye ke university HEC ki recognized list me hai ya nahi — is ka tareeqa [HEC recognized universities check guide](/guides/hec-recognized-universities-check) me hai. Jo degree recognized university ki hi na ho, us ki equivalence ka process shuru hi nahi hona chahiye.

## Har Marhale Ka Waqt — Asal Rukawat Kahan Ati Hai

Upar diya gaya kul andaza **15-30 working days** hai, lekin woh waqt har marhale me barabar taqseem nahi hota:

- **Account, profile aur application:** Ye sab aap ke ikhtiyar me hai — documents tayyar hon to aik hi din me mukammal ho jata hai.
- **Payment verification:** Fee pay karke verify kar dein to ye marhala bhi usi din nikal jata hai; verify na karein to application yahin atki rehti hai.
- **University verification:** **Sab se lamba aur ghair-yaqeeni marhala yehi hai.** University foran jawab de to letter jaldi banta hai, sust jawab aye to kul waqt mahine se bhi aage ja sakta hai.
- **Scrutiny aur letter:** Verification ke baad HEC ki scrutiny aur system-generated letter dashboard par a jana — ye hissa aam tor par tez hota hai.

Natija wazeh hai: jo marahale aap ke ikhtiyar me hain woh pehle din hi nipta dein, aur university wale marhale ke liye follow-up apna kaam samjhein — wahi is process ki asal raftar tay karta hai.

## Equivalence Letter Milne Ke Baad Kya Karein

Letter download karna aakhri qadam nahi — us ke baad ye cheezen kar lein:

1. **Letter ki online verification khud check karein:** Jo letter aap employer ko dein ge, woh use portal se verify karega — pehle khud check karke tasalli kar lein ke record saaf nazar ata hai. Verification ka tareeqa [HEC degree verification guide](/guides/hec-degree-verification-online) me diya hai.
2. **Asal file sambhal kar rakhein:** Letter system-generated hai aur dashboard se dobara download ho sakta hai, phir bhi apni copies (print aur scan) mehfooz jagah rakhein — har application me yehi letter lagega.
3. **Level ka matlab pehle se wazeh karein:** Private employer letter par CGPA na dekh kar sawal kar sakta hai — pehle hi bata dein ke HEC sirf degree ka Pakistani level tay karta hai, grades nahi deta.
4. **Aagli darkhwaston me wohi letter istemal karein:** Sarkari nokri, promotion ya aagli taleem — har jagah yehi letter chalta hai; har dafa nayi equivalence nahi karwani parti.

Yun samjhein ke equivalence letter aap ki foreign degree ka **Pakistani shanakhti card** hai — aik dafa ban jaye to degree har sarkari aur taleemi maqsad ke liye system me darj ho jati hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**HEC equivalence ki fee kitni hai?**
Rs 5,000 per degree/diploma, non-refundable, 1-Link se online pay hoti hai. University ki apni verification fee (agar woh mange) is ke ilawa hai.

**Kya HEC office ja kar documents jama karwane hain?**
Nahi. Poora process paperless hai — application, documents upload, fee aur letter, sab eservices.hec.gov.pk se hota hai.

**Equivalence letter par CGPA kyun nahi likha hota?**
Kyunkay HEC sirf degree ka level (Bachelor/Master ke barabar) tay karta hai — grades ya CGPA us ke dairay me nahi. Ye letter ki khami nahi, us ka maqsad hi yahi hai.

**Master ki equivalence ke liye pehle kya chahiye?**
Bachelor degree ki equivalence (agar woh bhi foreign hai) ya HEC-attested Pakistani Bachelor degree. Sequence ulta nahi chal sakta.

**How much is the HEC equivalence fee for a foreign degree?**
The HEC equivalence fee is Rs 5,000 per degree or diploma, paid online through 1-Link on eservices.hec.gov.pk. The process is fully paperless, but your foreign university must confirm your degree directly to HEC before the equivalence letter is issued.

**O/A Levels ki equivalence kahan se hogi?**
School level ki foreign taleem ki equivalence IBCC deta hai, HEC nahi. Matric/Inter level ke documents ke liye [IBCC attestation guide](/guides/ibcc-attestation-ka-tareeqa) dekhein.

**Kya Bachelor aur Master ki equivalence aik hi application me ho sakti hai?**
Nahi. Har degree ki alag application aur alag fee (Rs 5,000 per degree) hoti hai, aur tartib bhi tay hai — pehle Bachelor ki equivalence, phir Master ki. Dono applications ulta tartib se nahi chal sakti.

**Agar meri university HEC ko jawab hi na de to kya hoga?**
Letter usi marhale par ruka rahega, kyunkay equivalence university ki tasdeeq ke baghair jari nahi hoti. Apni university ke registrar/examinations office ko application ID ke sath khud follow up karein — aksar aik email se ruki hui file chal parti hai.
