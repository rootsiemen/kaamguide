## Pehli Baat: Token Tax Kya Hai Aur Har Saal Kyun Dena Parta Hai

Pakistan me gaari rakhna sirf petrol aur maintenance ka kharcha nahi — har saal **token tax** (Motor Vehicle Tax) bhi dena parta hai. Ye tax soobayi Excise & Taxation Department leti hai aur is ke baghair aap ki gaari ka record "defaulter" ho jata hai. Matlab ye ke gaari bechte waqt transfer nahi hoga, traffic police rok kar challan kar sakti hai, aur record me outstanding dues jama hote rahenge.

Achhi khabar ye hai ke ab token tax jama karwane ke liye Excise office ke chakkar lagane ki zaroorat nahi. Punjab me **e-Pay Punjab** system ke zariye ghar bethe, mobile se, 17-digit PSID (Payment Slip ID) bana kar payment ho jati hai — ATM, mobile banking, bank branch ya JazzCash/EasyPaisa se. Is guide me hum Punjab ke **2026-27 ke official rates**, PSID banane ka poora tareeqa, payment ke baad receipt aur status check, aur doosre soobon ka mukhtasir jaiza cover karenge.

> **Note:** Is guide me di gayi tamam fees Punjab Excise & Taxation Department ke official Motor Vehicle Tax page (excise.punjab.gov.pk) se li gayi hain aur **23 October 2026 ko check ki gayi thin**. Rates Finance Bill ke tehat tabdeel hote rehte hain, is liye payment se pehle official page ya helpline **1035** par tasdeeq zaroor kar lein.

## Punjab Token Tax Rates 2026-27: Mukammal Table

Punjab me 1 July 2026 se naye rates nafiz hain (Punjab Finance Bill 2026). Bari gaarion par ab tax gaari ki **invoice price ka percentage** hai, jabke chhoti gaarion ke liye lifetime ka option hai:

| Engine capacity | Token tax (2026-27) | Kab dena hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1000cc tak | **Rs 20,000 — lifetime (ek martaba)** | Sirf ek dafa |
| 1001cc se 2000cc tak | **Invoice price ka 0.3%** | Har saal |
| 2000cc se upar | **Invoice price ka 0.4%** | Har saal |
| Electric vehicles | **95% maaf** (sirf 5% dena hai) | Har saal |

**Misal:** Agar aap ki 1300cc gaari ki invoice price Rs 40 lakh hai to saal ka token tax = 0.3% × 40,00,000 = **Rs 12,000**. Aur har gaari par is ke ilawa **Rs 200 professional tax** bhi lagta hai (official schedule ke mutabiq).

**10% rebate ka mauqa:** Agar poore saal ka token tax **31 August** se pehle jama karwa dein to 10% chhoot milti hai (official schedule me darj hai). FY 2026-27 ki 31 August 2026 ki deadline guzar chuki hai — agli financial year me is rebate ka faida uthana na bhoolein.

## 1000cc Tak Ki Gaari: Rs 20,000 Lifetime Token Ki Sahulat

Ye sab se ahem baat hai jo aksar log nahi jante: **1000cc tak engine wali gaari ka token tax zindagi bhar ke liye ek martaba Rs 20,000 de kar khatam** ho jata hai. Har saal line me lagne ya online payment ki zaroorat nahi rehti.

Ek shart yaad rakhein (official page par darj hai): agar 1000cc tak ki gaari **registration ke 10 saal ke andar transfer** ho to naya owner dobara Rs 20,000 dega — lekin har guzre hue financial year par **10% rebate** kat kar. Matlab purani gaari khareedte waqt token ka hisab zaroor check kar lein.

## Online Payment Ke Liye Kya Chahiye Hoga

e-Pay Punjab se token tax jama karne ke liye ye cheezen tayyar rakhein:

- **Gaari ka registration number** (masalan LE-21-4567) — yehi sab se ahem cheez hai, isi se dues niklenge
- **Mobile phone** jis par e-Pay Punjab app ho (Google Play / App Store se muft download), ya computer par **epay.punjab.gov.pk**
- Payment ke liye: bank account (mobile/internet banking), ATM card, ya **JazzCash/EasyPaisa** wallet
- Gaari aap ke naam par registered honi chahiye taake record me koi ghalat-fehmi na ho

**Zaroori wazahat:** PSID (Payment Slip ID) **17 digits** ki hoti hai aur ye sirf usi gaari aur usi raqam ke liye banti hai. Kisi aur ki PSID par apni gaari ka tax jama nahi ho sakta.

## e-Pay Punjab Se Token Tax Jama Karne Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

1. **e-Pay Punjab app** kholein (developer: Punjab Information Technology Board — PITB) ya **epay.punjab.gov.pk** par jayein.
2. Department ki list me se **"Excise & Taxation"** select karein, phir **"Token Tax"** ya "Vehicle Token Tax" chunein.
3. Apni gaari ka **registration number** likhein (baghair dash ke bhi chal jata hai). App khud Excise ke record se aap ki gaari ki maloomat aur **wajiba-ada raqam** (token tax + agar koi traffic fine outstanding ho to) dikha dega.
4. Dikhayi gayi details — gaari ka number, owner ka naam, engine capacity aur raqam — **ghour se verify** karein. Ghalat gaari ka tax jama ho jaye to wapas lena mushkil hota hai.
5. **"Generate Challan"** dabayein. System aap ke liye **17-digit PSID** bana dega. Ye PSID aap ko screen par milegi aur email par bhi bheji jati hai — is ka **screenshot ya print** zaroor rakhein.
6. Ab is PSID se payment in me se kisi bhi channel se karein:
   - **ATM:** Kisi bhi 1Link member bank ke ATM par jayein → Bill Payment → **GoPb** select karein → 17-digit PSID likhein → raqam confirm kar ke pay karein.
   - **Mobile/Internet banking:** Apne bank ki app kholein → Bill Payment → **GoPb** → PSID likhein → payment confirm karein.
   - **Bank branch:** PSID ka print le kar kisi bhi 1Link member bank ki branch me counter par jama karwa dein.
   - **JazzCash / EasyPaisa:** e-Pay app ke andar "Pay Now" se wallet select karein — app khud wallet par redirect kar dega.
   - **Debit/Credit card:** e-Pay Punjab app ke andar hi card se payment ka option mojood hai (jahan supported ho).
7. Payment kamyab hote hi **digital receipt** save karein ya screenshot le lein — yehi aap ke token tax jama hone ka saboot hai. Excise office se alag sticker lene ki zaroorat nahi hoti; record online update ho jata hai.

**Ahem:** PSID ki **expiry hoti hai** — zyada din purani PSID par payment na karein. Agar PSID expire ho jaye to pareshan na hon, dobara Generate Challan kar ke nayi PSID bana lein.

## Payment Ke Baad: Status Check Aur Record Verify Karna

Payment ke baad tasalli ke liye apni gaari ka tax status khud check kar lein — ye **bilkul muft** hai:

- **MTMIS Punjab** (Motor Transport Management Information System — Punjab Excise ka official portal): **mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk** par registration number likhein. Gaari ka poora record samne aajayega — owner ka naam, make/model/year, engine aur chassis number, registration date, aur sab se ahem: **token tax kis saal tak paid hai aur koi outstanding dues to nahi**.
- **SMS se check:** Apni gaari ka registration number **8785** par SMS karein — basic vehicle aur tax details jawab me mil jati hain.
- Gaari khareedte waqt bhi isi tareeqe se token tax ka record check karein — agar pichle owner ne token nahi diya to zimmedari aap par aajayegi. Is silsile me hamari [gaari verification guide](/guides/gaari-verification-mtmis) bhi parh lein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Ghalat registration number likhna** — ek digit ki ghalti kisi aur ki gaari ka tax jama karwa degi. PSID banane se pehle number do dafa check karein.
2. **Receipt na sambhalna** — digital receipt ka screenshot na lena. Record me kabhi ghalti ho jaye to receipt hi aap ka saboot hai.
3. **31 August ki rebate zaya karna** — 10% chhoot har saal ka mauqa hai; financial year ke shuru me hi tax jama karwa dein.
4. **Transfer ke waqt token bhool jana** — gaari bechte ya khareedte waqt outstanding token tax ka hisab clear karein, warna [transfer](/guides/gaari-transfer-karne-ka-tareeqa) me masla hoga.
5. **Farzi websites se payment** — token tax sirf e-Pay Punjab ya official channels se dein. Koi agent "discount" ka jhansa de to Excise helpline **1035** par tasdeeq karein.
6. **1000cc wali gaari par har saal tax dena** — agar lifetime token jama hai to dobara dene ki zaroorat nahi; MTMIS par status check kar lein.

## Doosre Soobon Ka Mukhtasir Jaiza

- **Sindh:** Fixed yearly slabs (engine size par, price par nahi) — 1000cc tak Rs 1,500/saal; 1001–1300cc Rs 2,000; 1301–1600cc Rs 4,000; 1601–2000cc Rs 4,500; 2001–2500cc Rs 5,000; 2500cc se upar Rs 7,000. 1000cc tak ki nayi gaari ke liye Rs 20,000 once-for-all ka option bhi hai. Payment **ePay Sindh** portal/app se hoti hai. Official site: excise.gos.pk.
- **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa:** Fixed yearly slabs — 1000cc tak Rs 2,000/saal; 1001–1300cc Rs 3,000; 1301–1500cc Rs 4,000; 1501–2500cc Rs 5,000; 2500cc se upar Rs 8,000. Electric gaarian June 2028 tak exempt hain. Official site: kpexcise.gov.pk.
- **Islamabad:** ICT Excise ke apne rates hain — apni gaari ke liye excise ki official site se tasdeeq karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya token tax har saal dena lazmi hai?**

Ji haan — 1000cc se bari gaari ka token tax har financial year dena parta hai. Sirf 1000cc tak ki gaari ka Rs 20,000 lifetime token ek martaba de kar hamesha ke liye khatam ho jata hai. Tax na dene par record me dues jama hote rehte hain aur gaari transfer nahi hoti.

**PSID kya hai aur ye kitne din valid rehti hai?**

PSID (Payment Slip ID) 17 digits ka unique number hai jo e-Pay Punjab aap ki gaari aur raqam ke liye banata hai. Is ki expiry hoti hai, is liye PSID banane ke baad jald payment kar dein. Expire ho jaye to nayi PSID bana lein — koi extra fee nahi lagti.

**Kya me kisi aur ki gaari ka token tax jama karwa sakta hun?**

Ji haan — sirf gaari ka registration number chahiye hota hai. Lekin number likhte waqt intehai ehtiyat karein; ghalat number par jama raqam wapas lena mushkil hai.

**Token tax ki receipt kahan se milegi?**

Payment ke baad e-Pay app ya bank ki taraf se digital receipt milti hai — is ka screenshot ya print sambhal kar rakhein. Alag se Excise office ja kar sticker ya raseed lene ki zaroorat nahi; MTMIS portal par record khud update ho jata hai.

**Gaari khareed raha hun — token tax paid hai ya nahi, kaise check karun?**

mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk par gaari ka registration number likh kar check karein — wahan token tax kis saal tak paid hai, sab nazar aajayega. Ye check bilkul muft hai. Paison ka len-den se pehle ye step kabhi na chhorein.

**How to pay vehicle token tax online in Punjab, Pakistan?**

Use the e-Pay Punjab app (by PITB) or epay.punjab.gov.pk: select Excise & Taxation → Token Tax, enter your vehicle registration number, verify the dues, generate a 17-digit PSID challan, then pay via any 1Link bank ATM (GoPb), mobile/internet banking, bank branch, JazzCash/EasyPaisa or debit/credit card. For 2026-27, cars up to 1000cc pay a one-time Rs 20,000 lifetime token; 1001–2000cc pay 0.3% of invoice price yearly; above 2000cc pay 0.4% yearly. Verify your payment status free on MTMIS Punjab (mtmis.excise.punjab.gov.pk).
