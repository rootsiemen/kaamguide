## Death Certificate Kyun Har Ghar Ke Liye Ahem Document Hai

Kisi apne ki wafat ke baad gham ke sath-sath kaagzi kam bhi khare rehte hain, aur un me se sab se ahem document **death certificate** hai. Ye Union Council ki taraf se jari hone wala sarkari saboot hai ke falan shakhs ka inteqal ho gaya hai — aur is ke baghair marhoom ke har qisam ke waraasati aur qanoni kam ruke rehte hain.

Bank accounts band karwana hon, succession certificate lena ho, pension ya insurance ka claim karna ho, zameen/jaydaad ki mutation karani ho, ya marhoom ka CNIC NADRA se cancel karwana ho — har jagah pehla sawal yehi poocha jata hai ke **computerized death certificate dikhayen**. Baghair is certificate ke bank marhoom ke paisay heirs ko nahi deta, NADRA succession certificate ki application aage nahi barhti, aur pension kabhi start nahi hoti.

Is liye hamari salah ye hai: inteqal ke baad is kaam ko **mahinoon tak mat latkayen**. Punjab ke naye rules ke mutabiq ab ye certificate **bilkul muft** ban raha hai, aur 7 saal tak ki registration ke liye court decree ki mushkil shart bhi khatam kar di gayi hai. Is guide me hum aap ko batayenge ke kagzaat kaun se chahiye, Union Council me apply kaise karna hai, online option kya hai, aur kitna waqt lagta hai — taake aap aik dafa me ye kaam poora kar lein.

> **Note:** Is guide me di gayi fee aur rule ki maloomat Punjab Birth and Death Registration Rules 2025 (Punjab Gazette me notify) aur Sindh cabinet ke faisle se li gayi hain, aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. Waqfat-o-waqtan rules tabdeel ho sakte hain — payment ya apply se pehle apni Union Council ya NADRA helpline **1777** par maujooda haalat zaroor confirm kar lein.

## 2026 Ki Ahem Tabdeeli: Punjab Aur Sindh Me Fee Ab Muft Hai

Is guide ki sab se khush-aind khabar ye hai: pehle Union Council se death registration aur computerized death certificate par fee lagti thi (aam tor par Rs 100 se Rs 200 ke darmiyan), lekin ab soorat-e-haal badal gayi hai.

**Punjab** me May 2025 se **Punjab Birth and Death Registration Rules 2025** nafiz hain, jin ke tehat:

- Birth aur death ki **registration bilkul muft** hai — koi fee nahi.
- **Computerized certificate bhi muft** jari hota hai.
- Ye sahoolat inteqal ke **7 saal tak** ke andar registration par milti hai.
- 7 saal se kam ki late registration ke liye ab **court decree ki zaroorat nahi** — sirf mutallqa officer ki manzoori kaafi hai. (7 saal se zyada purani death registration par ab bhi court decree lagta hai.)

**Sindh** me bhi cabinet ne death certificate ki tamam fees **khatam** kar di hain — NADRA ki service charges ab soobayi hukumat khud ada karti hai, aur shahrion se Union Council, town committee ya municipal level par kuch nahi liya jata.

**Khyber Pakhtunkhwa aur Balochistan** ke liye: in soobon me fee ka maujooda schedule waqtan-fo-waqtan tabdeel hota rehta hai, is liye yahan hum ne koi number nahi likha. Apne ilaqe ki Union Council ya e-Khidmat center se fee zaroor confirm kar lein — aksar ye Rs 100 se Rs 500 ke darmiyan hoti hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: File Pehle Se Taiyar Kar Lein

Kaghaz na-mukammal hona sab se aam wajah hai jis se logon ke chakkar lagte hain. Ghar se nikalne se pehle ye tamam kagzaat ikatthe kar lein:

1. **Marhoom ka CNIC** (original + photocopy) — agar CNIC gum ho gaya hai to FRC ya koi doosra shanakhti saboot le jayen.
2. **Apply karne wale ka CNIC** (original + photocopy) — apply sirf qareebi rishtedar kar sakta hai (beta/beti, shohar/biwi, bhai/behen, walidain). Rishta ka saboot sath rakhein.
3. **Hospital ki death slip / medical certificate** — agar inteqal hospital me hua hai to hospital ka jari karda death report.
4. **Ghar par inteqal ki soorat me:** do gawahon ke CNIC ki photocopies aur aik **affidavit** (halaf-nama) jis me gawah tasdeeq karein ke inteqal kahan aur kab hua. Kuch Union Councils me gawah ka khud hazir hona bhi manga jata hai.
5. **Burial slip / qabristan ki raseed** — qabristan committee ki taraf se dafn ki tasdeeq wali parchi.
6. **Nikah nama ya FRC** (agar rishta sabit karne ke liye manga jaye) — khas tor par jab apply karne wala shohar/biwi ho.

**Ek zaroori mashwara:** tamam originals ke sath **2-2 photocopies** bana kar le jayen. Union Council ka office aksar photocopy ki sahoolat nahi deta, aur aik photocopy ki kami ki wajah se poora din zaya ho sakta hai.

## Union Council Se Apply Karne Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Computerized death certificate ka data **NADRA ke Civil Registration Management System (CRMS)** me darj hota hai, aur entry ka kaam aap ke ilaqe ki Union Council (ya Municipal Committee) ke secretary ke zariye hota hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

**Step 1 — Mutallqa Union Council ka pata karein.** Apply usi Union Council me hoga jis ke ilaqe me inteqal hua hai ya jahan marhoom rehta tha. Apne ilaqe ki UC ka naam aur secretary ke office ke auqaat pehle maloom kar lein.

**Step 2 — Application form hasil karein aur pur karein.** UC office se death registration ka form lein. Is me marhoom ka naam, walid ka naam, CNIC number, tareekh-e-wafat, wafat ki jagah, aur apply karne wale ka naam, CNIC aur marhoom se rishta darj karna hota hai. Har lafz marhoom ke CNIC ke mutabiq likhein — spelling ki ghalti baad me correction ka jhanjhat ban jati hai.

**Step 3 — Kagzaat jama karayen aur biometric tasdeeq.** Form ke sath upar diye gaye tamam kagzaat jama karayen. Apply karne wale ki **biometric (angoothay ki) tasdeeq** hoti hai — is liye apply karne wale ka khud jana lazmi hai.

**Step 4 — Data entry aur raseed lein.** Secretary aap ka data NADRA ke CRMS me darj karega aur aap ko aik **token/rasseed** dega. Is raseed ko sambhal kar rakhein — certificate lene aur tracking ke liye yehi kaam aayegi.

**Step 5 — Tasdeeq ka intezar karein.** UC ka mutallqa officer record ki tasdeeq karta hai. Punjab ke naye rules me 1 saal tak ki registration UC Secretary ke ikhtiyar me hai, 1 se 7 saal tak ki Assistant Director Local Government se manzoor hoti hai.

**Step 6 — Computerized certificate wasool karein.** Tasdeeq ke baad NADRA ka computerized death certificate print ho kar UC office se mil jata hai. Wasool karte waqt naam, tareekh-e-wafat aur CNIC number **foran check kar lein** — ghalti foran theek karana asaan hai, baad me mushkil.

## Online Tareeqa: e-Khidmat Aur NADRA Portals

Agar aap Punjab me hain to **e-Khidmat Markaz** ke zariye bhi apply kar sakte hain — wahan filing, fee (agar ho) aur tracking aik hi jagah se hoti hai:

1. Apne qareebi e-Khidmat Markaz jayen ya Punjab ke e-Khidmat portal par death certificate ki service dekhein.
2. Form pur karein aur kagzaat ki scanned copies upload karein (ya markaz me jama karayen).
3. Biometric tasdeeq markaz me hi ho jati hai.
4. SMS ke zariye application ka status maloom hota rehta hai.

NADRA ka **Pak ID app / id.nadra.gov.pk** bhi kuch categories ke liye online apply ki sahoolat deta hai, lekin death certificate ki entry ka bunyadi kaam ab bhi Union Council ke CRMS se hota hai — is liye UC ka aik chakkar aam tor par lagana hi parta hai. Online apply karne ke baad bhi biometric aur kagzaat ki asli tasdeeq ke liye UC ya markaz jana par sakta hai, ye baat zehen me rakhein.

**Fraud se khabardar:** Sirf **.gov.pk** websites aur apni Union Council par bharosa karein. Har UC ke bahar "tez certificate" banane wale agent ghoomte hain jo paise le kar ghalat ya jaali certificate de dete hain — aisa certificate barson baad visa ya waraasat ke waqt verification me fail ho jata hai aur aap ki mushkil barha deta hai.

## Kitna Waqt Lagega: Normal Aur Late Registration

Waqt ka doraniya soobe aur case ki noiyat par munhasir hai, lekin aam practice ye hai:

- **Normal registration (waqt par):** Kagzaat mukammal hon to UC me entry usi din ya 2-3 din me ho jati hai, aur computerized certificate **7 se 15 din** me mil jata hai. Kuch ilaqon me ye 31 din tak bhi ja sakta hai.
- **Late registration (1 se 7 saal, Punjab):** Court decree ki shart khatam hone ke baad ye kaam pehle se kaafi asaan hai — Assistant Director Local Government ki manzoori ke baad certificate jari hota hai, aam tor par **2 se 4 haftay** lagte hain.
- **7 saal se purana case:** Death registration ke liye ab bhi **court decree** lazmi hai — pehle adalat se decree lena hogi, phir UC me registration hogi. Is me mahine lag sakte hain.

**Waqt par registration ki aadat banayen:** Punjab me ab regular registration ki muddat 60 din se barha kar **1 saal** kar di gayi hai. Inteqal ke baad jitni jaldi register karayenge, utna hi kam jhanjhat hoga.

## 6 Ghaltiyan Jo Log Aksar Karte Hain

1. **Mahinoon tak registration me deri** — jab tak succession ya bank ka kaam na atke, log certificate banwate hi nahi. Phir ek kaam ke liye doosra atka rehta hai. Inteqal ke foran baad apply karein.
2. **Naam ki spelling CNIC se mukhtalif likhna** — form me jo naam likha jayega, wohi certificate par aayega. Hamesha marhoom ke CNIC ke ain mutabiq likhein.
3. **Photocopies bhool jana** — UC office me photocopy ki sahoolat aam tor par nahi hoti. Ghar se 2-2 copies bana kar jayen.
4. **Ghar par wafat par gawah ka intezam na karna** — hospital ki death slip na ho to do gawahon ke CNIC aur affidavit lazmi hain. Gawah pehle se tay kar lein.
5. **Agenton ko paise dena** — koi bhi agent sarkari fee se zyada lekar "tez" certificate nahi bana sakta. Punjab/Sindh me ye kaam muft hai — agent ko diya gaya paisa seedha zaya hai.
6. **Certificate wasool kar ke check na karna** — naam, tareekh ya CNIC me ghalti foran pakri jaye to usi waqt theek ho jati hai. Ghar ja kar ghalti nikalna matlab dobara chakkar.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Death certificate banane me kitni fee lagti hai?**

Punjab aur Sindh me 2026 me death registration aur computerized death certificate **bilkul muft** hai — Punjab Birth and Death Registration Rules 2025 aur Sindh cabinet ke faisle ke tehat koi fee nahi li jati. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa aur Balochistan me fee ka maujooda schedule apni Union Council se confirm karein.

**Kya death certificate online ban sakta hai?**

Mukammal online nahi — entry NADRA ke CRMS me Union Council ke zariye hoti hai. Punjab me e-Khidmat Markaz filing aur tracking me madad karta hai, aur Pak ID app par bhi kuch sahoolat mojood hai, lekin biometric tasdeeq aur kagzaat ki asli checking ke liye UC ya markaz ka aik chakkar aam tor par lagana parta hai.

**Agar inteqal ko kayi saal guzar gaye hon to kya certificate ban sakta hai?**

Ji haan. Punjab me 7 saal tak purani death registration ab **baghair court decree** ke ho jati hai — sirf Assistant Director Local Government ki manzoori darkar hoti hai. 7 saal se zyada purane case me ab bhi court decree lena lazmi hai.

**Death certificate ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?**

Sirf marhoom ka qareebi rishtedar — beta/beti, shohar/biwi, bhai/behen ya walidain. Apply karne wale ka apna CNIC aur marhoom se rishte ka saboot (FRC, nikah nama waghera) darkar hota hai, aur biometric tasdeeq ke liye us ka khud hazir hona lazmi hai.

**Certificate me naam ya tareekh ghalat likhi gayi ho to kya karein?**

Foran usi Union Council se rabta karein jahan se certificate jari hua hai. Ghalti ki nishandahi par correction ki application di jati hai aur record CRMS me durust kar ke naya certificate jari hota hai. Is liye certificate wasool karte waqt tamam details foran check kar lena behtar hai.
