## Divorce Certificate Kya Hai Aur Ye Kab Zaroori Hota Hai

**Divorce Certificate** (jise **Divorce Registration Certificate** ya **Divorce Effectiveness Certificate** bhi kehte hain) wo sarkari dastavez hai jo ye sabit karta hai ke aik nikah qanooni tor par khatam ho chuka hai. Pakistan me sirf zubani talaq keh dena ya aik talaq nama likh lena kafi nahi hota — jab tak Union Council ki taraf se ye certificate jari na ho, qanoon ki nazar me talaq mukammal tasleem nahi hoti.

Ye certificate kayi kaamon ke liye lazmi hai: dobara nikah karna, NADRA ke record me marital status "Married" se "Divorced" karwana, bachon ki custody ya kharcha (maintenance) ke muqaddamat, jayedad ya warasat ke mamlaat, aur visa ya immigration ke liye marital status sabit karna. Jo shakhs baghair certificate ke dobara shadi kar le, use baad me qanooni mushkilat ka samna ho sakta hai — is liye certificate lena sirf aik formality nahi, balkay apni hifazat hai.

Ahem baat ye hai ke divorce certificate **NADRA ke daftar se nahi**, balkay mutaliqa **Union Council** se jari hota hai. NADRA ka kirdaar is ke baad shuru hota hai: certificate milne ke baad aap apna NADRA record update karwate hain, aur ab Pak-ID app ke zariye computerized certificate bhi hasil kiya ja sakta hai — lekin us ki bhi bunyad Union Council ka record hi hai.

## Talaq, Khula Aur Certificate: Kaunsa Rasta Aap Ke Liye Hai

Divorce certificate tak pohanchne ke do mukhtalif raste hain, aur ye farq samajhna zaroori hai kyun ke kagzaat aur pehla qadam dono me mukhtalif hota hai:

**1. Talaq (shohar ki taraf se):** Shohar talaq dene ke baad Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ke Section 7 ke tehet Union Council ke Chairman ko tehreeri notice bhejta hai, aur isi notice ki aik copy biwi ko registered post ke zariye bhejta hai. Is notice ke baad 90 din ka reconciliation period shuru hota hai, jis me Union Council ki Arbitration Council dono fareeqain me sulah ki koshish karti hai. Agar sulah na ho to muddat poori hone par certificate jari ho jata hai.

**2. Khula (biwi ki taraf se):** Agar biwi khula chahti hai to use pehle Family Court me case file karna hota hai. Adalat ka faisla (decree) aane ke baad us decree ki bunyad par Union Council me divorce register karwaya jata hai aur phir certificate jari hota hai. Sirf khula ki darkhwast dena kafi nahi — adalat ka decree lazmi hai.

Dono suraton me aakhri manzil aik hi hai: Union Council ka computerized Divorce Certificate. Farq sirf shuruaat ka hai — talaq me notice se kaam shuru hota hai, khula me adalat ke faislay se.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: File Banane Se Pehle Ye Sab Jama Kar Lein

Certificate ki darkhwast se pehle ye kagzaat tayyar rakhne se aap ke chakkar bachte hain:

- **Dono fareeqain ke CNIC ki copies** — shohar aur biwi dono ke shanakhti card ki wazeh copies lazmi hain.
- **Nikahnama ya marriage certificate ki copy** — ye sabit karta hai ke nikah darj tha aur kis Union Council me darj tha. Agar aap ne abhi tak marriage certificate nahi banwaya to pehle [marriage certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-marriage-certificate) dekh lein.
- **Talaq nama (divorce deed)** — talaq ki surat me tehreeri talaq nama; khula ki surat me Family Court ka decree.
- **Union Council ko bheje gaye notice ki raseed** — talaq notice ki copy aur registered post ki raseed, jo ye sabit kare ke notice waqai bheja gaya tha.
- **Dono fareeqain ke mojooda pate** — Union Council ko dono ke addresses darkhwast me darj karne hote hain.
- **Manual divorce certificate (sirf Pak-ID apply ke liye)** — agar aap Pak-ID app se computerized certificate lena chahte hain to aap ke paas pehle se Arbitration Council ya Union Council ka jari karda manual divorce certificate hona lazmi hai.

Aik mashwara: tamam kagzaat ki do-do photocopies aur mobile me wazeh tasveeren bhi rakh lein. Union Council aksar aik set apne record ke liye rakh leti hai, aur Pak-ID apply ke waqt scanned copies kaam aati hain.

## Fees: Kitni Lagti Hai Aur Kahan Se Confirm Karein

Divorce certificate ki koi aik mulk-geer fixed fee nahi hai — fee aap ke sube aur Union Council ke qawaneen ke mutabiq mukhtalif hoti hai. Is liye is guide me koi aik rakam likhna ghalat hoga. Neeche di gayi table batati hai ke kis mad me kharcha aa sakta hai aur final rakam kahan se confirm karni hai:

| Kharcha | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| Union Council registration fee | Sube aur Union Council ke mutabiq mukhtalif; mutaliqa UC daftar se confirm karein |
| Pak-ID app processing fee | Application shuru karne se pehle app khud subayi qawaneen ke mutabiq qaabil-e-ada fee dikhata hai — wahi final rakam hoti hai |
| Wakeel ki fee (agar wakeel rakhen) | Lazmi nahi; wakeel rakhne par us ki fee alag se aap ke aur wakeel ke darmiyan tay hoti hai |
| Attestation ya courier (agar zaroorat ho) | Visa ya bahar mulk istemal ke liye attestation ki fee alag hoti hai |

Yaad rakhein: jo shakhs aap se certificate ke naam par bari rakam advance me mange, ya "setting" se jaldi certificate dilane ka wada kare, us se bachein. Sarkari fee hamesha raseed ke sath ada karein aur raseed sambhal kar rakhein.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa: Union Council Se Certificate Tak

### Step 1: Tehreeri notice tayyar karein

Talaq ki surat me shohar aik tehreeri notice likhta hai jis me dono fareeqain ke naam, CNIC numbers, nikah ki tareekh aur talaq ki tafseel darj hoti hai. Ye notice Union Council ke Chairman ko bhejna qanoon ke tehet lazmi hai — sirf biwi ko batana ya talaq nama banana kafi nahi.

### Step 2: Notice Union Council aur biwi ko bhejein

Notice ki aik copy Union Council ke daftar me jama karwayen aur doosri copy biwi ko registered post (Acknowledgment Due) ke zariye bhejein. Post ki raseed sambhal lein — ye aap ka saboot hai ke notice waqai bheja gaya.

### Step 3: 90 din ka reconciliation period

Notice milne ke baad Union Council aik Arbitration Council tashkeel deti hai jo 90 din ke andar dono fareeqain me sulah ki koshish karti hai. Ye muddat qanoon me lazmi hai — is se pehle certificate jari nahi ho sakta. Is doran iddat aur bachon ke kharche jaise mamlaat bhi tay ho sakte hain.

### Step 4: Sulah na hone par certificate ka ijra

Agar 90 din me sulah na ho to Union Council **Divorce Effectiveness Certificate** jari kar deti hai. Yehi wo dastavez hai jo qanooni tor par talaq ko effective qarar deta hai. Certificate lete waqt naam, CNIC numbers aur tareekhen ghor se check karein — ghalti ho to foran usi waqt durust karwayen, baad me masla barhta hai.

### Step 5: Khula ki surat me adalat ka rasta

Khula ke case me pehle Family Court se decree hasil karein, phir us decree ke sath Union Council me registration karwayen. Decree ke baghair Union Council khula ka certificate jari nahi karti.

### Step 6: NADRA record update karwayen

Certificate milne ke baad dono fareeqain ko **alag alag** apna NADRA record update karwana hota hai — sirf aik fareeq ka certificate lena doosre ka record khud-ba-khud nahi badalta. NADRA daftar ya Pak-ID ke zariye marital status "Divorced" karwayen aur CNIC se saabiq shohar/biwi ka naam hatwayen. Is ke baad aap ka [FRC](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur degar family record bhi durust ho jayega.

## Pak-ID App Se Online Tareeqa (Computerized Certificate)

NADRA ne Pak-ID mobile app me Provincial Civil Registration Services ke tehet divorce certificate ki sahulat di hai — is se ghar baithe computerized certificate ke liye apply kiya ja sakta hai. Lekin aik shart zehen me rakhein: **ye sahulat sirf un logon ke liye hai jin ke paas pehle se Union Council ya Arbitration Council ka manual divorce certificate mojood hai.** Agar aap ne abhi tak Union Council ka amal mukammal nahi kiya to pehle upar wala step-by-step tareeqa apnayen.

Pak-ID se apply karne ke steps:

1. Apne mobile par **Pak-ID app** download karein aur apne CNIC se account banayen.
2. Login ke baad **Provincial Civil Registration Services** me apna suba select karein aur **"Divorce"** option chunein.
3. Apni maloomat darj karein, category me "Divorce" confirm karein aur shohar/biwi (saabiq) ka intekhab karein jin ka naam record se hatana hai.
4. Apna aur saabiq shareek-e-hayat ka pata darj karein, tamam details ka jaiza lein aur declarations ko tasleem karein.
5. App par zahir hone wali fee (subayi qawaneen ke mutabiq) ada karein aur application submit karein. Submit hone ke baad divorce mutaliqa Union Council me register ho jati hai.
6. Manzoori ke baad certificate download karne ke liye app ki home screen par **Download → ID Vault → My Documents → CRMS** par jayen.

Ye sahulat is waqt **Punjab ke tamam azla**, Sindh ke muntakhab azla (Karachi East, Karachi Central, Naushahro Feroze, Mirpurkhas, Shaheed Benazirabad, Larkana, Jamshoro, Tando Allahyar aur Tando Muhammad Khan) aur Balochistan me **Quetta** zila me mojood hai. NADRA ke mutabiq isay jald poore mulk me phailaya ja raha hai — agar aap ka zila abhi list me nahi to Union Council ka brah-e-raast rasta apnayen.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai

Divorce certificate ka waqt do hisson me taqseem hota hai. Pehla hissa qanooni muddat hai jo kisi surat kam nahi ho sakti: talaq notice ke baad **90 din** ka reconciliation period lazmi hai, is liye Union Council ke raste certificate kam az kam 3 mahine me milta hai. Khula ke case me is me adalat ki karwai ka waqt alag se shamil hota hai, jo case ki noiyat par munhasir hai.

Doosra hissa Pak-ID ka computerized certificate hai: aik dafa manual certificate mojood ho to app par application aur manzoori ka amal aam tor par kuch din me mukammal ho jata hai, aur certificate ID Vault se foran download ho jata hai. Agar app par application ruk jaye to NADRA ki Pak-ID helpline **1777** par rabta karein — mobile se ye number muft ya mamooli charges par milta hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Baar Baar Karte Hain

- **Zubani talaq ko kafi samajhna:** Sirf keh dena qanooni tor par mukammal talaq nahi. Jab tak Union Council ko notice na bheja jaye aur certificate na mile, record me aap shadi-shuda hi rahenge.
- **Notice bhejna bhool jana:** Bohat se log talaq nama bana kar samajhte hain ke kaam ho gaya, jab ke Section 7 ke tehet Chairman ko notice bhejna lazmi hai — is ke baghair certificate nahi milega.
- **Certificate se pehle dobara shadi:** Jab tak Divorce Effectiveness Certificate haath me na ho, dobara nikah na karein. Baad me ye nikah qanooni challenge ka shikar ho sakta hai.
- **NADRA record update na karna:** Certificate lene ke baad bhi log CNIC aur family record update nahi karwate, jis se saabiq shareek ka naam record me rehta hai aur visa ya sarkari kaamon me rukawat aati hai. Certificate milte hi apna [CNIC record durust karwayen](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa).
- **Pak-ID par baghair manual certificate apply karna:** App ki sahulat sirf un ke liye hai jin ke paas pehle se manual certificate hai — is ke baghair application aage nahi barhegi.
- **Ghalat Union Council jana:** Darkhwast usi Union Council me dein jahan nikah darj tha ya jahan divorce ki karwai hui hai. Ghalat UC me file dene se waqt zaya hota hai.
- **Fake agents aur websites se bachna:** Divorce certificate ke naam par online "fori certificate" bechne wali websites aur agents se door rahein. Asli rasta sirf Union Council aur NADRA ki Pak-ID app hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya divorce certificate NADRA ke daftar se milta hai?**

Nahi, divorce certificate Union Council se jari hota hai, NADRA se nahi. NADRA ka kirdaar certificate milne ke baad shuru hota hai — aap apna marital status update karwate hain aur Pak-ID app ke zariye computerized certificate hasil kar sakte hain, lekin bunyadi record aur ijra Union Council ka hi hota hai.

**Talaq ke baad certificate milne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**

Kam az kam 90 din, kyun ke talaq notice ke baad qanoon ke tehet 90 din ka reconciliation period lazmi hai. Is muddat me Union Council ki Arbitration Council sulah ki koshish karti hai, aur sulah na hone par hi certificate jari hota hai. Khula ke case me adalat ki karwai ka waqt is ke ilawa hota hai.

**Kya Pak-ID app se poora divorce certificate online ban sakta hai?**

Sirf tab jab aap ke paas pehle se Union Council ya Arbitration Council ka manual divorce certificate mojood ho. Pak-ID app us manual certificate ki bunyad par computerized certificate deta hai. Agar manual certificate nahi hai to pehle Union Council ka mukammal amal (notice aur 90 din) poora karna lazmi hai.

**Divorce certificate ki fee kitni hai?**

Koi aik mulk-geer fixed fee nahi hai — fee aap ke sube aur Union Council ke qawaneen ke mutabiq mukhtalif hoti hai. Pak-ID app par application shuru karne se pehle qaabil-e-ada fee dikha di jati hai, wahi final rakam hoti hai. Final rakam ki tasdeeq ke liye mutaliqa Union Council daftar ya Pak-ID helpline 1777 par rabta karein.

**Certificate milne ke baad NADRA record kaise update karwayen?**

Dono saabiq fareeqain ko alag alag apna record update karwana hota hai — aik ka certificate doosre ka record khud nahi badalta. Computerized divorce certificate ke sath NADRA daftar jayen ya Pak-ID ke zariye marital status "Divorced" karwayen taake CNIC aur family record se saabiq shareek ka naam hat jaye.
