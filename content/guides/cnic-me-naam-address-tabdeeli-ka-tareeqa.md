## CNIC Modification Kya Hai Aur Kab Zaroori Hoti Hai

CNIC par likhi maloomat ghalat ho ya zindagi me tabdeeli aa jaye to card khud-ba-khud durust nahi hota — is ke liye NADRA ki "Modification" service istemal hoti hai. Modification ka matlab hai ke aap ka maujooda CNIC record update kiya jaye aur us ke baad aap ko nayi maloomat wala naya card jari kiya jaye. Is me aap ka CNIC number tabdeel nahi hota, sirf woh field badalti hai jis ki tabdeeli darkar ho — masalan naam ki spelling, maujooda ya mustaqil address, azdawaji haisiyat, tasveer ya dastakhat.

Modification ko renewal aur reprint se alag samajhna sab se pehle zaroori hai, kyun ke ghalat category chunna hi sab se aam wajah hai jis se application atak jati hai. Renewal us waqt hoti hai jab card ki muddat khatam ho jaye aur maloomat wohi hon jo pehle thin. Reprint us waqt hota hai jab card gum, chori ya kharab ho jaye aur maloomat me koi tabdeeli na karni ho. Modification us waqt hoti hai jab maloomat me tabdeeli karni ho — chahe card ki muddat baqi ho ya na ho. Agar aap ne ghar badla hai, shadi hui hai, naam ki spelling ghalat chhapi hai ya card par purani tasveer hai to aap ko Modification chahiye, Renewal ya Reprint nahi.

Ye guide un logon ke liye hai jin ke CNIC par naam ya address durust nahi hai. Is me bataya gaya hai ke kis tabdeeli ke liye kaunsa kagaz chahiye, Pak ID app se online modification ka step-by-step tareeqa kya hai, NADRA centre ka tareeqa kya hai, 2026 ki fees kitni hai, naya card kitne din me milta hai aur woh ghaltiyan kaun si hain jin se application reject ho jati hai. Sab se aham baat ye hai ke tabdeeli ka saboot pehle se tayyar rakhein — baghair saboot ke application aage nahi barhti.

## Naam Aur Address Tabdeel Karne Ke Liye Zaroori Kagzaat

Har tabdeeli ka apna saboot hota hai, aur wohi saboot application ki bunyad hai. Neeche har qism ki tabdeeli ke liye darkar kagzaat diye gaye hain — apni tabdeeli ke mutabiq sirf mutaliqa kagaz tayyar karein, tamam kagzaat har shakhs ko nahi chahiye hote:

- **Naam ki spelling ki durusti:** Matric ka certificate, birth certificate ya woh sarkari dastavez jis me naam ki durust spelling likhi ho. Chhoti spelling ki ghalti aksar isi saboot par durust ho jati hai.
- **Poora naam tabdeel karna:** Court ka order ya affidavit aur akhbar me ishtihar (newspaper advertisement) darkar ho sakta hai. Bara naam tabdeel karna aik qanooni amal hai, is liye is me waqt aur mazeed tasdeeq lagti hai.
- **Maujooda address tabdeel karna:** Bijli, gas ya pani ka utility bill (aam tor par 3 mah se purana na ho) ya rent agreement (kiraya nama). Kisi khooni rishtedar ke address par muntakili ki surat me us rishtedar ka CNIC aur us ki tasdeeq bhi darkar hoti hai.
- **Mustaqil address tabdeel karna:** Property ki registry, allotment letter, Fard-e-malkiat ya utility bill. Mustaqil address ki tabdeeli par tasdeeq sakht hoti hai, kyun ke ye record me lambi muddat ke liye rehta hai.
- **Azdawaji haisiyat tabdeel karna:** Shadi ki surat me Nikahnama, talaq ki surat me talaq ka certificate aur bewah hone ki surat me shareek-e-hayat ka death certificate.
- **Tasveer ya dastakhat ki tabdeeli:** Is ke liye kisi alag kagaz ki zaroorat nahi hoti — nai tasveer aur dastakhat application ke waqt hi liye jate hain.
- **Tareekh-e-paidaish ki durusti:** Matric ka certificate ya birth certificate. Tareekh-e-paidaish ki tabdeeli par NADRA sakht tasdeeq karta hai aur har case online nahi hota.

In ke ilawa har application me ye cheezein lazmi hain: aap ka maujooda CNIC (asl ya us ki tasveer), aik active mobile number aur email address (Pak ID account aur OTP ke liye), biometrics (fingerprints ya facial verification) aur fee ki adayegi ka zariya (debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR). Agar aap kisi khooni rishtedar ke liye apply kar rahe hain to woh shakhs tasdeeq ke liye aap ke sath maujood hona chahiye.

![Naam aur address ki tabdeeli ke liye kagzaat — CNIC, utility bill, Nikahnama aur school certificate aik mez par](/images/cnic-me-naam-address-tabdeeli-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

Aik baat pehle se zehen me rakhein: jo kagaz aap pesh karein woh saaf, mukammal aur asal ho. Dhundli tasveer, kata hua bill ya naam se mutabiqat na rakhne wala dastavez application ko fori rok deta hai. Agar kagaz par naam ki spelling CNIC se mukhtalif hai to pehle woh farq samajh lein ke aap kis spelling ko durust qarar de rahe hain — warna aik ghalti durust karte hue dosri darj ho jayegi.

## CNIC Modification Ki Fees 2026

NADRA ki shaya shuda fee schedule ke mutabiq modification ki fee card ki qism — standard CNIC ya Smart CNIC (chip wala) — aur service ki raftaar par munhasir hai. Neeche di gayi fees November 2026 me shaya shuda schedule se li gayi hain. Hatmi rakam hamesha Pak ID app/portal ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai, usi ko ada karein:

| Card Ki Qism | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard CNIC modification | Rs. 400 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| Smart CNIC (chip wala) modification | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |

Fee ke sath delivery ki muddat bhi farq hoti hai, jo agle sections me tafseel se di gayi hai. Aam tor par log Normal category hi chunte hain jab jaldi na ho, kyun ke is ki fee sab se kam hoti hai. Agar naya card fori chahiye — masalan visa, naukri ya bank ke kaam ke liye — to Urgent ya Executive behtar hai, lekin fee dugni se bhi zyada ho jati hai, is liye zaroorat ke mutabiq faisla karein.

Overseas Pakistanis ke liye NICOP ki modification ki fee dollar me aur zone ke mutabiq hoti hai, is liye bahar mulk se apply karne wale apni zone ki hatmi fee Pak ID app ki payment screen par hi dekhein. Pakistan ke andar rehne walon ke liye upar di gayi rupee wali fees lagu hoti hain. Fees waqtan fawaqtan tabdeel ho sakti hain, is liye payment karne se pehle screen par likhi rakam ko aik dafa phir dekh lein. Chhoti ghair-printable field ki tabdeeliyon (masalan kuch makhsoos record updates) par centre par kam fee bhi lag sakti hai — is ka faisla counter par hi hota hai.

## Online Tareeqa: Pak ID App Se Modification Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

NADRA ki official Pak Identity app se ghar baithe modification ki application di ja sakti hai, jab aap ki biometrics pehle se NADRA ke system me maujood hon. Address ki tabdeeli, azdawaji haisiyat ki tabdeeli, naam ki spelling ki durusti aur tasveer ki tabdeeli aksar online ho jati hai. Online tareeqa ye hai:

1. **Pak ID app download karein:** Google Play Store ya Apple App Store se official "Pak Identity" app install karein, ya portal id.nadra.gov.pk kholein. Jaali apps se bachein — sirf NADRA ki official app ya portal istemal karein.
2. **Account banayein ya login karein:** Apne email aur active mobile number se account banayein, OTP se tasdeeq karein. Agar account pehle se hai to seedha login karein.
3. **"Modification" ka option chunein:** CNIC ki services me se "Modification" muntakhib karein — Renewal ya Reprint ghalti se bhi na chunein, warna application ghalat category me chali jayegi.
4. **Tabdeel hone wala field muntakhib karein:** Naam, address, azdawaji haisiyat, tasveer ya dastakhat me se woh field chunein jis me tabdeeli karni hai, aur nai maloomat durust spelling ke sath darj karein.
5. **Saboot ki tasveer upload karein:** Mutaliqa kagaz — masalan utility bill, Nikahnama ya matric certificate — ki saaf tasveer upload karein. Tasveer mukammal ho, kinare kate hue na hon aur likhai saaf nazar aaye.
6. **Live tasveer aur fingerprints dein:** App ki hidayat par samne se tasveer lein aur mobile camera se fingerprints capture karein. Achi roshni aur saaf camera se ye marhala aik hi dafa me mukammal ho jata hai.
7. **Processing category chunein:** Normal, Urgent ya Executive me se aik muntakhib karein. Fee screen par dikhai degi — tasdeeq kar ke aage barhein.
8. **Application jama karein aur fee ada karein:** Application approve hone ke baad tracking ID ke sath "Pay Now" ka option aayega. Debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR se fee ada karein aur receipt mehfooz rakhein.

Fee ada hone ke baad aap ki application tayyari ke marahil me chali jati hai aur usi tracking ID se is ki paigiri ki ja sakti hai. Agar kisi marhale par app fingerprints na le sake, ya tabdeeli aisi ho jis ke liye mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho (masalan poora naam tabdeel karna), to NADRA centre jane ki hidayat mil sakti hai — ye koi kharabi nahi, aik aam marhala hai.

## NADRA Centre Se Modification Ka Tareeqa

Agar app se apply karna mumkin na ho, ya aap centre jana zyada pasand karte hon, to qareebi NADRA Registration Centre (NRC) se bhi modification ho jati hai. Centre ka tareeqa ye hai:

1. **Qareebi centre dhoondein:** NADRA ki Rahbar app ya website se apne qareebi Registration Centre ka pata aur timing maloom karein.
2. **Token lein:** Centre par "Modification" ke liye token lein aur batayein ke kis field me tabdeeli karni hai. Form me nai maloomat durust spelling ke sath likhein.
3. **Saboot jama karayein:** Mutaliqa kagaz counter par dikhayein — masalan address ke liye utility bill ya registry, shadi ke liye Nikahnama, naam ki durusti ke liye matric certificate. Counter par kagaz ki tasdeeq ki jayegi.
4. **Record ki tasdeeq karein:** Counter par aap ka maujooda record dikhaya jayega — naam, walid ka naam aur address ghor se check karein ke sab kuch durust hai aur sirf wohi field badal rahi hai jis ki tabdeeli darkar hai.
5. **Biometrics aur tasveer:** Centre par fingerprints aur nai tasveer li jayegi. Ye marhala chand minute ka hota hai. Agar tasveer tabdeel karni hai to isi waqt nai tasveer li jayegi.
6. **Fee ada karein aur receipt lein:** Counter par fee ada karein aur receipt par likhi mutawaqqa delivery date note kar lein. Receipt par tracking number bhi hota hai, use sambhal kar rakhein.

Centre ka faida ye hai ke agar app me koi masla aa raha ho to woh wahin hal ho jata hai, aur staff ghalat category chunne ya ghalat kagaz lagane jaisi ghaltiyon se bacha leta hai. Nuqsan sirf ye hai ke centre jane, qatar me lagne aur wapas aane ka waqt lagta hai, jab ke online tareeqa ghar baithe mukammal ho jata hai. Jin tabdeeliyon ke liye court order ya akhbar ka ishtihar darkar ho, un ke liye centre ka rasta hi munasib hai.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Modification ke baad naye card ki delivery ka waqt card ki qism aur chuni hui category par munhasir hai. NADRA ki shaya shuda schedule ke mutabiq andazan muddat ye hai:

| Card Ki Qism | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard CNIC | taqreeban 15 din | taqreeban 12 din | taqreeban 6 din |
| Smart CNIC | taqreeban 31 din | taqreeban 23 din | taqreeban 9 din |

Ye muddat fee ki adayegi ke baad shuru hoti hai, application jama karne ke din se nahi. Is liye fee waqt par ada karna zaroori hai — warna application ruki rehti hai aur card ki tayyari shuru nahi hoti. Agar tabdeeli ke liye mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho (masalan court order ya akhbar ke ishtihar ki tasdeeq) to muddat is se zyada bhi ho sakti hai. Delivery courier ke zariye muntakhib shuda address par hoti hai, is liye address durust likhna aur delivery ke din kisi ka ghar par maujood hona zaroori hai.

![Courier ke zariye naya CNIC ghar ke darwaze par wasool karte hue aik shakhs](/images/cnic-me-naam-address-tabdeeli-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

Status check karne ke do aasan tareeqe hain. Pehla, Pak ID app ya portal (id.nadra.gov.pk) par apni tracking ID se application ka status dekhein. Dosra, apni tracking ID SMS ke zariye **8400** par bhej kar status maloom karein — ye wohi number hai jo NADRA ki digar services ke liye bhi istemal hota hai. Agar card muddat guzarne ke baad bhi na mile to NADRA helpline **051-111-786-100** ya **1777** par tracking ID ke sath rabta karein.

Modification ke baad aap ka CNIC number wohi rehta hai jo pehle tha — sirf tabdeel shuda maloomat wala naya card milta hai. Agar aap ka card expire bhi ho chuka hai to [CNIC renewal ka tareeqa](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) alag guide me tafseel se diya gaya hai, aur card gum hone ki surat me [gumshuda CNIC dobara banane ka tareeqa](/guides/gumshuda-cnic-dobara-banane-ka-tareeqa) dekhein. Pehli dafa card banwane ke liye [NADRA CNIC banane ki guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) kaam aayegi, aur shadi ke baad family record ke liye [NADRA FRC banane ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi site par maujood hai. Azdawaji haisiyat ki tabdeeli ke sath [NADRA marriage certificate](/guides/nadra-marriage-certificate) ki maloomat bhi zaroori ho sakti hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Bachein

Modification ki application aksar chhoti ghaltiyon ki wajah se atak jati hai. In se bachna aasan hai agar pehle se maloom hon:

- **Ghalat category chunna:** Naam ya address tabdeel karna hai to "Modification" chunein, Renewal ya Reprint nahi. Ghalat category me application ya to reject ho jati hai ya ghalat card ban jata hai.
- **Baghair saboot apply karna:** Har tabdeeli ka saboot lazmi hai. Utility bill, Nikahnama ya matric certificate ke baghair application aage nahi barhti.
- **Ghalat spelling darj karna:** Nai maloomat likhte waqt spelling do dafa check karein. Aik ghalti durust karte hue dosri ghalti darj ho jana aam masla hai.
- **Purana ya kata hua utility bill lagana:** Address ke saboot ke liye taaza aur mukammal bill dein. 3 mah se zyada purana ya kinaron se kata hua bill tasdeeq me rukawat banta hai.
- **Fee ada karne me dair:** Application approve hone ke baad fee waqt par ada na ki to card ki tayyari shuru nahi hoti. "Pay Now" aate hi fee ada karein aur receipt mehfooz rakhein.
- **Ghalat delivery address:** Card usi address par jata hai jo application me likha ho. Naya address tabdeel kar rahe hain to delivery ke liye wohi address muntakhib karein jahan courier aasani se pahuncha sake.
- **Jaali app download karna:** Sirf official Pak Identity app istemal karein. Milti-julti naam wali apps se fee ya maloomat ka nuqsan ho sakta hai.
- **Purana card istemal karte rehna:** Naya card milne ke baad purana card valid nahi rehta. Aik shakhs ka aik hi valid card hota hai — purana card NADRA ko wapas karna ya zaya karna behtar hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CNIC me naam ya address tabdeel karne ki fee kitni hai?**
Standard CNIC ki modification Normal me Rs. 400, Urgent me Rs. 1,150 aur Executive me Rs. 2,150 hai. Smart CNIC (chip wala) ki modification Normal me Rs. 750, Urgent me Rs. 1,500 aur Executive me Rs. 2,500 hai. Hatmi rakam Pak ID app ki payment screen par dikhai jati hai, usi ko ada karein.

**Kya CNIC me address online tabdeel ho sakta hai ya centre jana lazmi hai?**
Address ki tabdeeli aksar Pak ID app se online ho jati hai, jab aap ki biometrics pehle se NADRA ke system me maujood hon aur utility bill ya rent agreement jaisa saboot upload kar diya jaye. Centre jana sirf us surat me zaroori hota hai jab app se biometrics na li ja sakein ya mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho.

**Naam ki spelling durust karne ke liye kaunsa kagaz chahiye?**
Chhoti spelling ki ghalti ke liye matric ka certificate, birth certificate ya woh sarkari dastavez kaafi hota hai jis me naam ki durust spelling likhi ho. Poora naam tabdeel karne ke liye court ka order ya affidavit aur akhbar me ishtihar darkar ho sakta hai, kyun ke ye aik qanooni amal hai.

**CNIC modification ke baad naya card kitne din me milta hai?**
Standard CNIC Normal me taqreeban 15 din, Urgent me 12 din aur Executive me 6 din me milta hai. Smart CNIC Normal me taqreeban 31 din, Urgent me 23 din aur Executive me 9 din leta hai. Ye muddat fee ki adayegi ke baad shuru hoti hai, aur agar mazeed tasdeeq darkar ho to is se zyada waqt bhi lag sakta hai.

**Kya modification ke baad CNIC number tabdeel ho jata hai?**
Nahi, modification me CNIC number wohi rehta hai jo pehle tha. Sirf tabdeel shuda maloomat wala naya card jari hota hai, aap ka record aur number tabdeel nahi hote. Number tabdeel sirf tab hota hai jab NADRA kisi khaas case me purana record mansookh kar ke naya jari kare, jo modification ka mamla nahi hai.

Hatmi rakam aur delivery ki date ke liye Pak ID app/portal ki payment screen aur official website nadra.gov.pk ya helpline 051-111-786-100 / 1777 se tasdeeq karein.*
