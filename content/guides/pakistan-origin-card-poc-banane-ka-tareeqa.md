## POC Kya Hai Aur Ye Kis Ko Chahiye

Pakistan Origin Card (POC) NADRA ki taraf se un ghair-mulki shehrion ko jari kiya jata hai jin ki asal Pakistani hai. Ye card NADRA Ordinance, 2000 ki Section 11 ke tehat jari hota hai aur is ka sab se bara faida ye hai ke is ke hamil ko **Pakistan me visa ke baghair dakhil hone** ki ijazat milti hai. Is ke ilawa POC Pakistan me jaidad ki khareed-o-farokht, karobar aur banking jaise kaamon me bhi pehchan aur sahulat ke tor par kaam aata hai.

POC un logon ke liye hai jo ab Pakistani shehri nahi rahe lekin un ki jadein Pakistan se juri hain — maslan wo shakhs jo kabhi Pakistani shehri tha aur ab kisi doosre mulk ki nationality le chuka hai, ya wo ghair-mulki shakhs jis ke walidain ya dada-dadi Pakistani thay, ya wo ghair-mulki shakhs jis ki shadi kisi Pakistani shehri se hui hai. Agar aap UK, USA, Canada, Australia ya kisi bhi mulk me rehte hain aur aap ki family ka talluq Pakistan se hai to ye guide aap ke liye hai.

NADRA POC ki teen qisamein batata hai: **Smart POC** — chip wala jadeed card, behtar security aur mazbooti ke sath; **POC with QR-code** — QR code wala naya version jis se card ki tasdeeq tez hoti hai; aur purana **Teslin-based POC** — NADRA ke mutabiq naye Teslin POC ab jari nahi kiye ja rahe, is liye nayi applications Smart POC ki hi hoti hain.

## POC Ke Liye Kaun Eligible Hai

NADRA ki official page ke mutabiq POC in categories ke applicants ko jari kiya jata hai:

- **Ex-Pakistani:** Wo shakhs jo Pakistani shehri nahi raha, lekin zindagi ke kisi marhale par Pakistani shehri tha.
- **Pakistani walidain ya dada-dadi:** Wo ghair-mulki shakhs jis ke walidain ya dada-dadi Pakistani ya ex-Pakistani hon.
- **1951 se pehle ki nationality:** Wo shakhs jis ne Pakistan Citizenship Act 1951 ke nafiz hone se pehle ghair-mulki nationality hasil ki ho.
- **Pakistani shehri ka ghair-mulki shareek-e-hayat:** Wo foreigner jis ki shadi kisi Pakistani shehri ya Pakistani origin ke shakhs se hui ho — lekin is category me wo shakhs eligible **nahi** hai jo India ya Bangladesh ka shehri ho, kisi aise mulk ka shehri ho jise Pakistan tasleem nahi karta, ya kisi dushman mulk ka shehri ho.
- **Dependent bacha:** Pakistani shehri ke ghair-mulki shareek-e-hayat ka dependent bacha bhi POC ke liye eligible hai.

Agar aap in me se kisi bhi category me aate hain to aap POC ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Agar aap ke walidain me se aik Pakistani hai aur aap bahir mulk me paida hue hain to aap ki category aam tor par "parents based" hoti hai.

## POC Aur NICOP Me Farq Kya Hai

Ye woh sawal hai jo sab se zyada poocha jata hai, aur is ka jawab seedha hai: **NICOP un logon ke liye hai jo Pakistani shehri hain aur bahir mulk rehte hain ya dual nationality rakhte hain**, jab ke **POC un logon ke liye hai jo Pakistani shehri nahi hain lekin Pakistani origin rakhte hain**. Agar aap ke paas ab bhi Pakistani nationality hai to aap ko [NICOP card](/guides/nicop-card-banane-ka-tareeqa) chahiye, POC nahi. Agar aap ne Pakistani nationality chhor di hai, ya aap kabhi Pakistani shehri rahe hi nahi lekin aap ke walidain Pakistani thay, to POC aap ka card hai.

Dono cards ke fawaid milte-julte hain — visa-free entry, Pakistan me bank account aur jaidad ke kaam — lekin eligibility bilkul mukhtalif hai. Ghalat card ke liye apply karna sab se aam ghalti hai aur is se application reject ho jati hai ya waqt zaya hota hai. Is liye pehle apni category tay kar lein, phir apply karein. Agar aap ko NICOP aur POC ke farq par mazeed tafseel chahiye to KaamGuide ki [NICOP card banane ki guide](/guides/nicop-card-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh lein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Category Ke Mutabiq Checklist

NADRA ki POC page par kagzaat ki alag se checklist nahi di gayi — page sirf ye kehta hai ke "Fresh / New Registration" ke liye NADRA Registration Policy ke POC section se ruju karein. PakID app apply ke doran aap ki category ke mutabiq exact kagzaat ki list dikhata hai. Phir bhi, eligibility ki sharaet aur aam amal ki roshni me aam tor par ye kagzaat darkar hote hain:

![Foreign passport, family registration certificate aur marriage certificate jaise kagzaat desk par rakhe hue, upar magnifying glass ke sath](/images/pakistan-origin-card-poc-banane-ka-tareeqa-2.jpg)

- **Valid foreign passport** — aap ki maujooda nationality ka passport, jis ki muddat khatam na hui ho.
- **Pakistani origin ka saboot** — apna purana Pakistani passport ya CNIC (agar kabhi Pakistani shehri thay), ya walidain/dada-dadi ka CNIC ya NICOP. [Family Registration Certificate (FRC)](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi family talluq sabit karne me madadgar hota hai.
- **Spouse-based category ke liye** — Pakistani shareek-e-hayat ka CNIC ya NICOP aur NADRA se jari shuda [marriage certificate](/guides/nadra-marriage-certificate).
- **Dependent bache ke liye** — walidain ka POC ya NICOP aur bache ka birth certificate.
- **Tasveer** — PakID app ki specifications ke mutabiq haliya tasveer (aam tor par safed background, seedha rukh).
- **Biometric** — fingerprint aur tasveer ki tasdeeq ke liye NADRA Registration Centre jana par sakta hai, ye app apply ke doran bata dega.

Kagzaat apply karne se pehle scan ya saaf tasveer ki shakal me tayyar rakh lein. Purane ya dhundhle kagzaat upload karne se application atak sakti hai. Agar kisi kagaz par naam ki spelling aap ke passport se mukhtalif hai to pehle us ki durusti karwa lein — [CNIC me naam ya address ki tabdeeli](/guides/cnic-me-naam-address-tabdeeli-ka-tareeqa) ki guide me is ka tareeqa maujood hai, lekin POC ke liye ye masla aam tor par aap ke foreign passport aur origin ke saboot ke darmiyan paida hota hai.

## POC Fees 2026: Normal, Urgent Aur Executive

Neeche di gayi fees NADRA ki official fee structure page se li gayi hain, jo 5 November 2026 ko check ki gayi thin. Fees **US dollar** me hain aur NADRA ke mutabiq fee darkhwast ki tareekh par lagu official conversion rate ke mutabiq PKR me charge ki jati hai. Timeline fee ki adayegi ke baad shuru hoti hai.

| Application Type | Normal (31 din) | Urgent (23 din) | Executive (9 din) |
|---|---|---|---|
| New POC | USD 150 | USD 200 | USD 250 |
| POC Modification | USD 200 | USD 250 | USD 300 |
| POC Duplicate | USD 200 | USD 250 | USD 300 |
| POC Renewal | USD 150 | USD 200 | USD 250 |
| Cancellation (death ki surat me) | — | — | USD 25 (2 din) |
| Cancellation (divorce ki surat me) | — | — | USD 25 (2 din) |
| Cancellation (nationality surrender) | — | — | USD 15 (2 din) |

Is ke ilawa "non-printable fields" (wo maloomat jo card par chhapti nahi, sirf chip/database me hoti hain) ki update ki fee **USD 30** hai aur is me 2 din lagte hain. Delivery charges alag hain: Pakistan ke andar delivery **Rs. 165** hai, jab ke bahir mulk delivery ka kharch mulk ke mutabiq mukhtalif hota hai aur apply ke waqt maloom hota hai.

Fee bharne se pehle ye zaroor dekh lein ke aap ko kitni jaldi card chahiye. Aam tor par Normal (31 din) sab se sasta hai; agar safar qareeb hai to Executive (9 din) behtar hai. Note karein ke cancellation sirf Executive category me hoti hai aur us me 2 din lagte hain.

## Online Tareeqa: PakID App Se Step-by-Step Apply

POC ke liye online apply NADRA ki official **PakID** mobile application se hota hai. PakID NADRA ki identity registration aur tracking ke liye official app hai. Tareeqa ye hai:

![Smartphone par PakID app se identity card ke liye apply karte hue shakhs, fingerprint verification aur courier delivery ke sath](/images/pakistan-origin-card-poc-banane-ka-tareeqa-3.jpg)

1. **PakID app download karein** — Google Play Store ya Apple App Store se NADRA ki official PakID app install karein.
2. **Account banayein ya sign in karein** — apna mobile number aur email se account banayein. Agar pehle se account hai to sign in karein.
3. **"New POC" muntakhib karein** — app me POC ki category chunein (New POC, Renew, Modify ya Reprint — apni zaroorat ke mutabiq).
4. **Tafseelat bharein** — apni shakhsiyat, origin aur rabte ki maloomat darust bharein. Naam ki spelling apne foreign passport ke mutabiq likhein.
5. **Kagzaat upload karein** — app ki hidayat ke mutabiq passport aur origin ke saboot ki saaf tasaveer upload karein.
6. **Fee ada karein** — apni muntakhib ki gayi category (Normal, Urgent ya Executive) ke mutabiq fee credit ya debit card se ada karein. Fee ada hote hi timeline shuru ho jati hai.
7. **Biometric ya NRC visit (agar zaroori ho)** — NADRA ke mutabiq biometric verification aur tasveer ke liye NADRA Registration Centre jana par sakta hai. App aap ko bata dega ke visit zaroori hai ya nahi.
8. **Status track karein** — application ka status PakID app me hi dekha ja sakta hai.

Pakistan se bahir rehne wale applicants ke liye PakID app hi aasan tareeqa hai, kyun ke is se NADRA centre jane ki zaroorat aksar khatam ho jati hai. Lekin agar app biometric ke liye centre bulaye to qareebi Pakistani embassy ya NADRA centre se waqt le kar jayein.

## Card Kab Tak Milega, Validity Aur Delivery

Processing time aap ki muntakhib ki gayi category par munhasir hai: **Normal 31 din, Urgent 23 din aur Executive 9 din**. Ye muddat fee ada karne ke baad se shuru hoti hai, apply karne ke din se nahi. Is liye agar aap ka safar qareeb hai to fee bharne me der na karein.

Smart POC ki **validity** category ke mutabiq hai: ex-Pakistani, parents-based aur FPO family members based Smart POC **7 saal** ke liye valid hota hai, jab ke spouse-based Smart POC **5 saal** ke liye valid hota hai. Card ki muddat khatam hone se pehle renewal ke liye apply karein — renewal ki fee naye card jitni hi hai (Normal USD 150).

Delivery ke liye NADRA card aap ke diye gaye pate par bhejta hai. Pakistan ke andar delivery charges Rs. 165 hain; bahir mulk delivery ka kharch mulk ke mutabiq hota hai. Card milne par us par naam, tasveer aur QR code ki tasdeeq kar lein — agar koi ghalti ho to foran modification ke liye apply karein, kyun ke baad me safar ke waqt masla ho sakta hai. Agar card gum ho jaye to duplicate ke liye apply karein (fee oopar table me di gayi hai); [gumshuda CNIC dobara banane](/guides/gumshuda-cnic-dobara-banane-ka-tareeqa) ki guide me is tarah ke mamlat ka tareeqa samjhaya gaya hai, lekin POC duplicate ke liye PakID app se hi apply hota hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Application Ruk Jati Hai

- **NICOP ki bajaye POC, ya POC ki bajaye NICOP apply karna** — jo log ab bhi Pakistani shehri hain unhe POC nahi milta; pehle apni category tay karein.
- **Spouse-based category me ghalat fehmi** — India ya Bangladesh ke shehri, ghair-tasleem-shuda mulk ke shehri aur dushman mulk ke shehri spouse-based POC ke liye eligible nahi hain.
- **Naam ki spelling ka farq** — passport, origin ke saboot aur application form par naam aik jaisa hona chahiye. Chhoti si spelling ki ghalti bhi verification me rukawat ban sakti hai.
- **Dhundhli ya purani tasveer upload karna** — PakID app ki specifications ke mutabiq haliya aur saaf tasveer use karein.
- **Fee ki category ghalat chunna** — Normal fee bhar kar Executive jaisi jaldi ki umeed na rakhein; timeline fee ke sath bandhi hui hai.
- **Biometric visit ko nazar-andaz karna** — agar app NADRA centre jane ka kahe to waqt par jayein, warna application aage nahi barhegi.
- **Origin ka saboot na dena** — sirf ye kehna kafi nahi ke walidain Pakistani thay; CNIC, NICOP, FRC ya purana passport jaisa saboot lazmi hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Pakistan Origin Card (POC) kya hai?**
POC NADRA ki taraf se un ghair-mulki shehrion ko jari kiya jane wala identity card hai jin ki asal Pakistani hai. Ye NADRA Ordinance, 2000 ki Section 11 ke tehat jari hota hai aur is se hamil ko Pakistan me visa-free entry aur Pakistan me jaidad, karobar aur banking ke kaamon me sahulat milti hai.

**POC ki fee 2026 me kitni hai?**
Naye Smart POC ki fee Normal USD 150 (31 din), Urgent USD 200 (23 din) aur Executive USD 250 (9 din) hai. Renewal ki fee bhi itni hi hai, jab ke modification aur duplicate ki fee USD 200 se USD 300 tak hai. Fee darkhwast ki tareekh ke official rate par PKR me charge hoti hai.

**POC aur NICOP me kya farq hai?**
NICOP un logon ke liye hai jo Pakistani shehri hain aur bahir mulk rehte hain, jab ke POC un logon ke liye hai jo Pakistani shehri nahi hain lekin Pakistani origin rakhte hain — maslan ex-Pakistani, Pakistani walidain ki aulad ya Pakistani shehri ke ghair-mulki shareek-e-hayat. Ghalat card ke liye apply karne se application reject ho sakti hai.

**POC kitne arse ke liye valid hota hai?**
Smart POC ex-Pakistani, parents-based aur FPO family members based categories me 7 saal ke liye valid hota hai, jab ke spouse-based Smart POC 5 saal ke liye valid hota hai. Muddat khatam hone se pehle renewal ke liye apply karein.

**Kya POC ke liye online apply ho sakta hai?**
Ji haan, POC ke liye NADRA ki official PakID mobile app se online apply hota hai — account banayein, "New POC" chunein, tafseelat aur kagzaat upload karein aur fee ada karein. Biometric verification ya tasveer ke liye NADRA Registration Centre jana par sakta hai, jo app apply ke doran bata dega.
