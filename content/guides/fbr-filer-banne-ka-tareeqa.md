## Filer Hona Kyun Zaroori Hai (Paise Ki Zuban Me)

"Filer" matlab aap ka naam FBR ki **Active Taxpayer List (ATL)** me hai. Ye sirf izzat ki baat nahi — **seedha paiso ka hisab** hai. Non-filer ko Pakistan me har bari transaction par **dugna ya teen guna zyada tax** dena parta hai:

- **Gaari ki registration/transfer** par non-filer ke liye withholding tax aksar **3 guna** hota hai — filer ke Rs 10,000 ke muqable non-filer ke Rs 30,000 tak lag sakte hain.
- **Bank transactions** (bare cash nikalwane, transfer) par filer ko kam withholding tax lagta hai.
- **Property ki khareed-o-farokht** par filer ke rates taqreeban **aadhe** hote hain.
- **Sarkari tender, bank loan aur kai visas** ke liye filer hona lazmi ya bohat faidamand hai.

Sab se achhi baat: **Agar aap ki aamdani taxable hadd se kam hai to aap "nil return" file karke bhi filer ban sakte hain** — talab-ilam, ghar-elu khawateen aur kam aamdani walon ke liye rasta khula hai. Aur **NTN registration bilkul muft** hai — kisi agent ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi.

> **Note:** Is guide ki maloomat FBR ke IRIS portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk) ke process par mabni hai aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thi**. Tax rates aur dates har budget me tabdeel ho sakte hain — filing se pehle FBR ki official website fbr.gov.pk par maujooda rates zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Step 1: NTN Registration (IRIS Portal Par, Muft)

NTN (National Tax Number) ke liye koi office nahi jana — poora process online hai:

1. **iris.fbr.gov.pk** kholein.
2. Homepage par **"Registration for Unregistered Person"** par click karein.
3. Apna **13-hinson ka CNIC number** (bina dashes ke) darj karein aur captcha hal karein.
4. FBR aap ke **CNIC par registered mobile number** aur email par **verification PIN/code** bhejega — dono darj karein.
5. **Form 181** bharein: naam, pata, nokri/karobar ki tafseel aur aamdani ka zariya.
6. **Mazboot password** banayen aur jama karwayen.

**Bohat ahem:** Fard (individual) ke liye **NTN aap ka CNIC number hi hota hai** — alag se koi number yaad nahi rakhna. Companiyon aur firms ko alag 7-hinson ka NTN milta hai.

**Registration fail ho to?** Sab se aam wajah hai ke aap ka **mobile number aap ke apne CNIC par registered nahi** hai. Pehle apni SIM apne CNIC par hone ki tasdeeq karein (biometric verification franchise se ho jati hai), phir dobara koshish karein. Bar-bar ghalat submit na karein — CNIC manual review me ja sakta hai.

## Step 2: Tax Return File Karna (Asal Kaam Yahi Hai)

**Sirf NTN lene se aap filer Nahi bante** — filer banne ke liye **saalana income tax return** file karna lazmi hai:

1. IRIS me login karein — username aap ka **CNIC** hai.
2. **"Declaration"** section me jayen aur **"Income Tax Return"** chunein.
3. Maujooda **tax year** select karein.
4. Apni aamdani darj karein: tankhwah, karobar, kiraya, ya jo bhi zariya hai. Employer ya bank ki taraf se pehle se kata hua tax bhi darj karein — wo aap ke khate me adjust hota hai.
5. **Wealth statement** bharein: apni jaidad, gaari, bank balance aur degar assets — total aamdani se **milna chahiye**, system balance hue baghair submit nahi hone deta.
6. Review karke **submit** karein aur **acknowledgement number** lazmi bacha lein — yehi file hone ka saboot hai.

**Nil return:** Agar aamdani tax-free hadd se kam hai (ya hai hi nahi) to bhi return file karein — andar aamdani zero/kam likh dein. **Nil return bhi aap ko ATL me le jati hai.**

## Step 3: ATL Me Naam Check Karna

Return file hone ke **24 se 72 ghante** ke andar aap ka naam ATL me aa jata hai. Check karne ke do tareeqe:

- **SMS:** Apna CNIC number (bina dashes) **9966** par bhej dein — "Active" ka jawab aaye to aap filer hain.
- **Website:** **atl.fbr.gov.pk** par ja kar CNIC darj karein.

## Deadline Aur Late Filing

- Individuals ke liye return ki aakhri tareekh aam tor par **30 September** hoti hai (har saal FBR confirm karta hai).
- Deadline guzarne ke baad bhi return file kar sakte hain, lekin ATL me wapas aane ke liye **surcharge** dena parta hai.
- Mashwara: deadline ka intezar na karein — aakhri dino me IRIS portal bohat sust ho jata hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat (Return Ke Liye Tayyar Rakhein)

- **CNIC**
- **Mobile number** (apne CNIC par registered) aur email
- **Salary slip / employer ka tax certificate** (naukri pesha ke liye)
- **Bank statement** (saal bhar ka mukhtasir record)
- Jaidad/gaari ke kagzaat (wealth statement ke liye)
- Pichle saal ki return (agar pehle file ki thi)

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Agent ko password dena:** IRIS ka password aur OTP **kabhi kisi ko na dein** — aap ke tax record ki hifazat aap ki zimmedari hai.
2. **Sirf NTN par iktefa karna:** NTN ≠ filer. Return lazmi file karein.
3. **Ghalat mobile number:** CNIC par registered number hi kaam aayega.
4. **Wealth statement me farq:** Assets aur aamdani ka hisab na mile to system rok dega — numbers pehle kagaz par bana lein.
5. **Deadline par bharosa karna:** "Extension mil jayegi" soch kar intezar karna mehenga parta hai — extension ki koi guarantee nahi hoti.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya NTN registration ki koi fee hai?**
Nahi — bilkul muft hai. Fee mangne wali website ya agent se bachein.

**Main student hun, aamdani nahi hai — kya main filer ban sakta hun?**
Ji haan — nil return file karke aap ATL me aa sakte hain. Aamdani ki koi minimum hadd nahi.

**Filer banne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**
Registration 10-15 minute, return file karna 1-2 ghante (pehli dafa). ATL me naam 24-72 ghante me aa jata hai.

**Freelancer / online kamane walon ke liye?**
Aap bhi filer ban sakte hain — aamdani ka zariya "freelancing / services" darj karein. Bank se aane wali raqam ka record return se milna chahiye.

**Har saal return file karni hogi?**
Ji haan — filer rehne ke liye har saal ki return lazmi hai. Aik saal miss kiya to naam ATL se nikal jata hai aur dobara surcharge ke saath wapas aata hai.

**Kya main khud file kar sakta hun ya wakil lazmi hai?**
Seedhi salaried ya nil return **khud** file kar sakte hain. Karobari ya paycheeda hisab ho to tax consultant se mashwara behtar hai.

## IRIS 2.0: Naye Portal Ki Khaasiyat

FBR ne apna portal **IRIS 2.0** me upgrade kar diya hai — kuch faide:

- **Pre-filled data:** FBR ke paas jo record hai (bank withholding, gaari ka record, utility connections) wo form me **pehle se bhara** aata hai — aap ko sirf tasdeeq karni hoti hai.
- **Ahem:** Pre-filled figures ko apne documents se **mila kar** hi aage barhein — ghalat data par submit karna aap ki zimmedari hogi.

## Filer Rehne Ke Liye Har Saal Kya Karna Hai

Filer banna aik dafa ka kaam nahi — **har saal** ye karna parta hai:

1. Har saal ki **return waqt par file** karein (aam tor par 30 September ki deadline).
2. **Wealth statement** har saal update karein — nayi jaidad, gaari ya bank balance shamil karein.
3. ATL status **har saal check** karein (9966 par SMS) — kabhi system me naam miss ho jata hai.

**Aik saal miss kiya to:** Naam ATL se nikal jata hai aur wapas aane ke liye **surcharge** dena parta hai — is liye calendar me reminder laga lein.

## Business / Company Ke Liye Mukhtasir Note

Agar aap ka **karobar ya company** hai to:

- Company ka **alag 7-hinson ka NTN** hota hai (CNIC wala nahi).
- Company ki **income tax return** ke ilawa **sales tax** ki alag filing bhi ho sakti hai (agar applicable ho).
- Partnership/firm ke liye **AOP (Association of Persons)** ka NTN banta hai.
- Karobari return **khud file karne ke bajaye** tax consultant se karwana behtar hai — ghalti par notice aa sakta hai.

## FBR Notice Aye To Ghabrana Kaisa

Return file karne ke baad kabhi-kabhi FBR notice aata hai — ye **har filer ke liye aam baat** hai, jurm ki alamat nahi:

1. Notice IRIS account me aur registered email par ata hai — **kabhi ignore na karein**.
2. Aam notices me kisi raqam ya document ki **وضاحت (explanation)** mangi jati hai — bank record ya salary slip bhej kar jawab ho jata hai.
3. Jawab dene ki **date notice par likhi** hoti hai — usi ke andar jawab dein.
4. Paycheeda notice ho to tax consultant ki fees sakoon se sasti parti hai.

**Aik sach ye bhi hai:** Non-filers ko bhi notices ate hain (bank/property data se) — aur un ke paas jawab ke liye return ka record nahi hota. Filer hona is lihaz se bhi hifazat hai.

## Akhri FAQs

**Kya filer banne ke baad tax lazmi dena parega?**
Sirf **taxable aamdani** par tax lagta hai — kam aamdani ho to nil return file karein, koi tax nahi. Filer hona tax dene ke barabar nahi.

**IRIS ka password bhool gaya to?**
Portal par **"Forgot Password"** se CNIC aur registered mobile par OTP aayega — naye password bana lein. Password kisi ke saath share na karein.

## Pehli Return Ke Liye Asaan Trick

Pehli dafa sab se mushkil hissa hota hai. Ye tarika asaan bana dega:

1. **Peeche (pehle) hisab kagaz par banayein** — salary, bank balance, gaari/jaidad.
2. Phir IRIS khol kar har cheez **usi tarteeb** se bharein.
3. Aik ghanta pehli dafa ke liye rakhein — **jaldi karne walo ki ghaltiyan hoti hain**, sakoon se bharein.
