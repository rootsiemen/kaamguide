## Pehli Baat: CM Punjab Laptop Scheme Kya Hai

**CM Punjab Laptop Scheme** — sarkari naam **CM Punjab Laptop Programme** — Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo programme hai jis ke tehet Punjab ke sarkari colleges aur universities ke mehnati students ko **muft laptop** diye jate hain. Is ka maqsad ye hai ke jo student achhe number to la sakta hai lekin laptop khareedne ki istitaat nahi rakhta, wo parhai, research aur online learning me peeche na rahe. Ye programme **Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif** ki taleemi sahulat ka hissa hai aur is ko **Higher Education Department (HED) Punjab** chalata hai.

Scheme ke doosre marhale (Phase 2) me taqseem kiye jane wale laptops aam laptops nahi hain — ye **Core i7 13th Generation processor, SSD storage aur high-speed RAM** wale high-performance laptops hain, jo research, programming aur online classes ke liye banaye gaye hain. Darkhwast dene ka tareeqa sirf **online** tha aur is ki **koi fee nahi** thi — registration bilkul muft thi.

**Sab se ahem taaza khabar — pehle ye samajh lein:** Phase 2 ki darkhwastein **band ho chuki hain**. Higher Education Department ke notification ke mutabiq Phase 2 ki registration ki aakhri tareekh **20 April 2026** thi (pehle 28 February 2026 thi, phir barha kar 20 April ki gayi thi). **25 November 2026 tak naye marhale (Phase 3) ka koi sarkari elaan nahi hua** — na official portal par, na HED ki taraf se, na kisi bare akhbar me. Is waqt internet par jo websites "2026 me registration khuli hai — abhi apply karein" likh rahi hain, wo **sarkari websites nahi hain** aur un par bharosa nahi karna chahiye. Is guide me aap ko Phase 2 ki asal shartein, distribution ki taaza haalat, status check karne ka tareeqa aur agle marhale ki tayyari ka poora tareeqa diya gaya hai.

**Aik ahem note:** official portal **cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk** 25 November 2026 ko check karne par load nahi hua — text fetch aur live browser dono par "connection reset" ki ghalti aayi. Is liye is guide ke numbers aur shartein HED ke notification aur 3 November 2026 ki APP ki report (jo Business Recorder aur ARY News ne shaya ki) se li gayi hain. Portal dobara khulne ya naye marhale ke elaan par yahi page hatmi zariya ho ga.

## Phase 2 Ki Taaza Haalat: Registration Band, Distribution Jari

Phase 2 ka safar ab kis marhale par hai, ye samajhna zaroori hai taake aap ko pata ho ke aap kahan khare hain:

**Registration band hai:** Phase 2 ke liye darkhwastein 20 April 2026 ko band ho gayin. Official portal ke mutabiq Phase 2 ke liye kul **1,63,326 darkhwastein** aayi thin. In me se sirf merit par muntakhib students ko laptop mile ga — pehle aao pehle pao ka koi nizaam nahi hai.

**Laptops ki tadad barha di gayi hai:** Phase 2 ka elaan pehle **40,000 laptops** ke sath hua tha. Punjab Budget 2026-27 me is ki tadad **1,10,000** kar di gayi, aur 3 November 2026 ko Attock ki taqreeb me Chief Minister ne allocation **1,12,000 laptops** tak barha di. Matlab darkhwast dene walon ke liye mauqa pehle se zyada hai.

**Distribution zila ba zila jari hai:** APP ki 3 November 2026 ki report ke mutabiq us waqt tak Punjab bhar me **48,292 laptops** taqseem ho chuke the. Taqseem division-wise taqreebat ke zariye ho rahi hai:

| Taqreeb | Tareekh | Tafseel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sahiwal | 15 October 2026 | Phase 2 ki pehli taqseem ki taqreeb |
| Mandi Bahauddin | 27 October 2026 | Division ki taqreeb |
| Attock | 3 November 2026 | Rawalpindi division ke **6,161 laptops**; isi taqreeb me allocation 1,12,000 ka elaan hua |
| Jhelum | 10 November 2026 | **537 laptops** taqseem hue |

**Aap ne Phase 2 me apply kiya tha?** Agar haan, to apna status official portal par zaroor check karein — ho sakta hai aap muntakhib hue hon aur taqseem ki taqreeb ka intezar kar rahe hon. Agar aap eligible the lekin is marhale me muntakhib nahi hue, to neeche di gayi tayyari agle marhale ke liye kaam aaye gi.

## Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai: Ehliyaat

Official launch ki shartein programme ke hisab se mukhtalif hain — aik hi rule sab par lagu nahi hota. Aap apply kar sakte the agar ye tamam shartein poori hoti hon:

**1. Punjab ka domicile lazmi hai:** Darkhwast guzar ke paas Punjab ke kisi zila ka valid [domicile certificate](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) hona chahiye. Punjab se bahir ke domicile wale students is scheme ke eligible nahi hain.

**2. Regular student hona lazmi hai:** Aap ko kisi sarkari (public sector) university ya college me regular student hona chahiye tha. Portal par shaya fehrist ke mutabiq **48 private universities** ke students bhi eligible the — lekin sirf wo private universities jo portal ki fehrist me shamil thin. Apni university ka naam hamesha portal ki fehrist se milayein, kisi aur ki baat par bharosa na karein.

**3. Semester aur programme ki shart:** BS programme ke students sirf **1st ya 2nd semester** me hone chahiye the. Medical aur Dental colleges ke students **1st professional (academic session 2025 aur is ke baad)** me hone chahiye the. BS Nursing ke students bhi 1st professional me hone chahiye the.

**4. Kam az kam number ki shart:** Intermediate ke number programme ke hisab se ye the:

| Programme | Intermediate me kam az kam number |
| --- | --- |
| BS (public sector universities/colleges) | **65%** |
| Medical aur Dental colleges | **80%** |
| DVM (Doctor of Veterinary Medicine) | **75%** |
| BS Nursing (public sector) | **65%** |

**5. Pehle laptop na mila ho:** Jo student pehle kisi bhi sarkari laptop scheme se laptop le chuka ho, wo dobara eligible nahi hai. Ye shart sakhti se check ki jati hai — portal aap ka record pehle se maujood data se milata hai.

**6. Valid CNIC ya B-Form:** Aap ka apna valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ya B-Form hona lazmi hai. Agar aap ki umar 18 saal se kam hai to B-Form kaam ata hai, lekin naam aur tareekh-e-pedaish CNIC/B-Form ke mutabiq hi darj karni hoti hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Agle marhale ki window khulne se pehle ye kagzaat tayyar rakhein taake aakhri waqt me bhag-dor na karni pare:

**1. CNIC ya B-Form ki saaf scan copy:** Dono taraf ki copy saaf aur parhne layak ho. Dhundli ya kati hui copy darkhwast atak sakti hai.

**2. Punjab domicile certificate:** Domicile valid ho aur us par aap ka naam, zila aur CNIC number saaf darj ho. Agar domicile abhi tak nahi bana to pehle [domicile banane ka tareeqa](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh kar banwa lein — baghair domicile ke apply nahi hota.

**3. Admission ya enrollment ka saboot:** University ya college ka admission letter, enrollment slip ya current student ID card. Is se sabit hota hai ke aap waqai us programme aur semester me regular student hain.

**4. Intermediate ki result card:** Jis par aap ke number saaf nazar aayein — yehi aap ki ehliyaat ka bunyadi saboot hai. Original result card ki scan copy tayyar rakhein.

**5. Current student ID card:** University ya college ka jari karda student ID card, jis par aap ki photo, naam aur roll number ho.

**6. Haal ki passport-size photo:** Saaf background wali taaza photo, jis me chehra saaf nazar aaye.

**7. Halafnama (affidavit):** Portal par darkhwast ke sath **Rs 100 ke e-stamp paper par halafnama** bhi manga jata tha, jis me aap tasdeeq karte hain ke aap tamam shartein poori karte hain. E-stamp banane ka tareeqa [yahan](/guides/punjab-e-stamp-paper-online-banane-ka-tareeqa) parha ja sakta hai.

Kagzaat ki **saaf scan ya photo** tayyar rakhein — portal par har file ki size ki hadd hoti hai (aam tor par 200KB se kam), is liye bari files ko pehle chhota kar lein. Ghalat ya na-mukammal kagaz darkhwast reject karwa sakta hai.

## Laptop Kaisa Milta Hai: Selection Aur Specs

**Selection sirf merit par hoti hai:** Darkhwastein jama hone ke baad pehle aap ki university ya college ka focal person aap ki enrollment, semester aur number ki tasdeeq karta hai. Us ke baad Higher Education Department merit list banata hai. Jis ka number zyada hota hai, us ka naam list me upar hota hai — is me kisi sifarish, agent ya rishwat ka koi kirdar nahi hai.

**Kitne students ko laptop milta hai:** Phase 2 ke liye 1,63,326 darkhwastein aayi thin aur allocation 1,12,000 laptops ki hai. Matlab har darkhwast guzar ko laptop nahi mile ga — merit list me aane walon ko hi mile ga. Jo student is marhale me reh jaye, us ki tayyari agle marhale ke liye kaam aaye gi.

**Laptop ki specs:** Phase 2 ke laptops **Core i7 13th Generation processor, SSD storage aur high-speed RAM** ke sath diye ja rahe hain. Ye aik aam laptop se zyada taqatwar hai aur research, programming, video editing aur online classes ke liye banaya gaya hai. Laptop muntakhib student ko **muft** milta hai — is ki koi qeemat nahi li jati aur na hi koi security ya advance manga jata hai.

**Taqseem ka tareeqa:** Muntakhib students ko zila ya division ki taqreeb me bulaya jata hai aur laptop wahi diya jata hai. Kuch students ko un ki university ke zariye bhi laptop mil sakta hai. Apni taqreeb ki tareekh aur jagah ke liye portal aur apne registered mobile number par ane wale SMS par nazar rakhein.

## Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

Ye tareeqa **Phase 2 ka hai**, jo official portal par shaya hua tha. Is waqt window band hai, lekin jab naya marhala khule ga to tareeqa yahi hoga — tab portal par dobara confirm zaroor kar lein:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne mobile ya computer par **cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk** kholein. Yaad rakhein: sirf isi address par bharosa karein. Milti-julti naam wali kisi aur website par form na bharein — wo fraud ho sakti hai.

**Step 2 — Account banayein:** Portal par "Candidate Signup" ya "Register" par click karein. Apna CNIC number, naam (bilkul CNIC ke mutabiq), email aur apne naam par registered mobile number darj karein. Mobile par ane wala OTP likh kar tasdeeq karein.

**Step 3 — Form bharein:** Form me apni personal tafseel (naam, walid ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish, pata), university ka naam (portal ki fehrist se chunein), programme, semester aur roll number darj karein. Intermediate ke number bilkul result card ke mutabiq likhein.

**Step 4 — Kagzaat upload karein:** CNIC/B-Form, domicile, admission proof, intermediate result card, student ID card aur photo upload karein. Har file saaf aur size ki hadd ke andar ho. Rs 100 ke e-stamp par halafnama bhi upload karna tha.

**Step 5 — Form submit karein aur confirmation sambhal lein:** Submit karne se pehle form aik dafa phir parh lein — aik ghalat number ya naam darkhwast reject karwa sakta hai. Submit karne ke baad confirmation slip ya reference number milta hai. Is ko **sambhal kar rakhein** — isi se aap apni darkhwast ka status check kar sakein ge.

**Step 6 — Tasdeeq ka intezar karein:** Pehle aap ki university tasdeeq karti hai, phir HED merit list banata hai. Is amal me kuch hafte lag sakte hain. Is doran portal aur apne email par nazar rakhein — agar koi kagaz kam ho to portal aap ko batata hai.

**Apply muft tha — koi fee nahi:** Phase 2 me darkhwast dene ki **koi fee nahi** thi. Jo shakhs form bharne, naam dalwane ya merit list me naam nikalwane ke paise mange, wo **fraud** hai.

**Apni application ka status kaise check karein:**

Agar aap ne Phase 2 me apply kiya tha to apna status is tarah check karein:

**1. Official portal par login karein:** **cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk** par apne CNIC number aur password se login karein. Dashboard par aap ki darkhwast ki haalat nazar aaye gi — pending, verified, merit list me muntakhib, ya distribution ke liye tayyar. (Note: portal 25 November 2026 ko load nahi ho raha tha — agar aap ko bhi na khule to kuch waqt baad dobara koshish karein.)

**2. Merit list check karein:** Muntakhib students ki merit list portal par shaya hoti hai. Apna naam, CNIC number ya roll number se list me talash karein.

**3. SMS aur email par nazar rakhein:** Selection aur taqreeb ki khabar aap ke registered mobile number par SMS aur email par bhi aati hai. Apna wohi number active rakhein jo form me diya tha — number band hone par khabar nahi mile gi.

**4. Helpline par rabta karein:** Scheme ki madad ke liye toll-free helpline **0800-12345** bhi di gayi hai. Agar portal na khule ya status samajh na aaye to is number par call kar ke apna CNIC number bata kar maloom kar sakte hain. Zila bhar me madad ke liye district help desks bhi banaye gaye the.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Aur Scam Se Bachao

**1. "30 September 2026 aakhri tareekh hai" — ghalat hai:** Kuch websites likhti hain ke Phase 2 ki aakhri tareekh 30 September 2026 thi. Ye **ghalat** hai — HED ke notification ke mutabiq asal aakhri tareekh **20 April 2026** thi (28 February se barha kar). Jo website aaj bhi 30 September ki tareekh dikha kar "abhi apply karein" ka button dikhaye, us par bharosa na karein.

**2. "60% ya CGPA 2.5 sab ke liye kafi hai" — ghalat hai:** Kuch reports me aik aam rule (60% marks ya CGPA 2.5) likha hai. Asal shartein programme ke hisab se hain — BS ke liye 65%, Medical/Dental ke liye 80% aur DVM ke liye 75%. Apne programme ki shart upar di gayi table se milayein, aam rule par bharosa na karein.

**3. Fake "apply now" websites:** Sirf **cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk** asal portal hai. Is ke ilawa koi bhi website, Facebook page ya WhatsApp number jo "laptop apply" ka dawa kare, fraud hai. In par apna CNIC number, photo ya paise kabhi na dein.

**4. Agent ko paise na dein:** Laptop merit par milta hai — koi agent, daftar ka banda ya "sifarish" aap ka naam list me nahi dalwa sakta. Jo shakhs is ke paise mange, us ki shikayat HED ki helpline par karein.

**5. Ghalat naam ya number:** Form me naam, CNIC number aur number bilkul kagzaat ke mutabiq likhein. Aik harf ki ghalti bhi tasdeeq ke waqt darkhwast rok sakti hai. Submit karne se pehle form dobara parhna na bhoolein.

**6. Private university ki fehrist check kiye baghair apply karna:** Sirf wo 48 private universities eligible thin jo portal ki fehrist me shamil thin. Agar aap ki university fehrist me nahi hai to aap eligible nahi hain — pehle fehrist check karein, phir apply karein.

## Agle Marhale Ki Tayyari: Abhi Kya Karein

Naya marhala kab khule ga, is ka koi sarkari elaan abhi nahi hai. Lekin jo students abhi se tayyar honge, wo window khulte hi sab se pehle apply kar sakein ge:

**1. Apne kagzaat abhi se durust kar lein:** CNIC valid rakhein, domicile banwa lein aur intermediate ki result card sambhal kar rakhein. Agar aap abhi 1st ya 2nd semester me hain to aap agle marhale ke liye behtareen waqt par hain — lekin yaad rakhein ke semester ki shart har marhale me dobara check hoti hai.

**2. Number barhane par tawajjo dein:** Selection merit par hoti hai, is liye jitne achhe number honge, mauqa utna zyada ho ga. BS ke liye 65% sirf kam az kam hadd hai — asal merit list is se zyada number walon ki banti hai.

**3. Sirf sarkari zariye par nazar rakhein:** Naye marhale ka elaan **cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk**, **hed.punjab.gov.pk** aur bare akhbaron par aaye ga. Kisi private website ke "apply now" button par click na karein.

**4. Doosri schemes bhi dekhein:** Agar aap laptop ke muntazir hain to [Honhaar Scholarship](/guides/honhaar-scholarship-apply-online) aur [PM Youth Chromebook Scheme](/guides/pm-youth-chromebook-scheme-apply-online) bhi check karein — ye taleemi madad ki doosri sarkari schemes hain jin se aap ko faida ho sakta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CM Punjab Laptop Scheme Phase 2 ki registration abhi khuli hai ya band?**

Band hai. Phase 2 ki registration Higher Education Department ke notification ke mutabiq 20 April 2026 ko band ho gayi thi. 25 November 2026 tak Phase 3 ya kisi naye marhale ka koi sarkari elaan nahi hua. Jo website abhi bhi "registration khuli hai" likh kar apply ka button dikhaye, wo sarkari nahi hai.

**Laptop ke liye apply karne ki koi fee hai?**

Nahi, registration bilkul muft thi. Na apply karne ki fee hai, na laptop ki koi qeemat li jati hai aur na hi koi security ya advance manga jata hai. Jo shakhs form bharne ya naam dalwane ke paise mange, wo fraud hai — us ki shikayat HED ki helpline 0800-12345 par karein.

**Kaun se students eligible hain — kitne number chahiye?**

Punjab domicile, sarkari university/college (ya portal ki fehrist wali 48 private universities) me regular student, BS me 1st ya 2nd semester, aur Intermediate me kam az kam 65% (BS/Nursing), 80% (Medical/Dental) ya 75% (DVM) number. Pehle kisi sarkari scheme se laptop lene wala dobara eligible nahi hai.

**Phase 2 me kitne laptops taqseem ho rahe hain aur ab tak kitne mile?**

Allocation 40,000 se barh kar 1,12,000 laptops kar di gayi hai (3 November 2026 ka elaan). APP ki report ke mutabiq 3 November 2026 tak Punjab bhar me 48,292 laptops taqseem ho chuke the, aur distribution division-wise taqreebat ke zariye jari hai.

**Apni application ka status kaise check karoon?**

Official portal cmlaptophed.punjab.gov.pk par apne CNIC number aur password se login kar ke dashboard par status dekhein. Merit list bhi portal par shaya hoti hai. Portal na khule to helpline 0800-12345 par call kar ke apna CNIC number bata kar maloom kar sakte hain. Status ki khabar aap ke registered mobile par SMS se bhi aati hai.
