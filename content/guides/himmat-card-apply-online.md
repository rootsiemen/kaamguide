## Pehli Baat: Himmat Card Kya Hai

**Himmat Card** Punjab hukumat ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet soobay ke mazoor afrad (persons with disabilities) ko har teen mahine mali imdad di jati hai. Ye Chief Minister Punjab ka program hai, jisay **Social Welfare aur Bait-ul-Maal Department Punjab** chalata hai aur is ki registration aur verification **DPMIS (Disabled Persons Management Information System)** ke zariye hoti hai. Raqam **Bank of Punjab** ke jari karda khusoosi ATM card ke zariye milti hai, taa ke mustahiq shakhs ko kisi daftar ke chakkar na lagane parein.

Is scheme ka maqsad saaf hai: jo shakhs mazoor honay ki wajah se kaam nahi kar sakta, usay har teen mahine aik muqarrar raqam mile, taa ke wo apni roz-marra ki zarooratein poori kar sake. Ye koi aik dafa ki imdad nahi — manzoori ke baad ye sahulat har quarter (teen mahine) par milti rehti hai, jab tak mustahiq shakhs sharaet par poora utarta rahe.

Aik baat shuru me hi samajh lein, kyun ke is par sab se zyada ghalat maloomat phaili hui hai: **Himmat Card ka koi alag se application form nahi hota.** Aap seedha "Himmat Card ka form bhar dein" nahi kar sakte. Pehle aap ka disability certificate banna, phir aap ka record DPMIS me darj hona, aur phir is record se mustahiq afrad ko shortlist kiya jata hai. Is liye is guide me jo tareeqa bataya gaya hai, wo asal me **DPMIS registration aur disability certificate** ka tareeqa hai — yehi Himmat Card ki asal darkhwast hai.

## Himmat Card Par Kitni Raqam Milti Hai

Maujooda official maloomat ke mutabiq Himmat Card par milne wali imdad ki tafseel ye hai:

| Tafseel | Raqam / Tareeqa |
|---|---|
| Har teen mahine ki imdad (quarterly stipend) | Rs 10,500 |
| Saal bhar ki kul imdad | Rs 42,000 |
| Raqam milne ka tareeqa | Bank of Punjab ka Himmat ATM Card |
| Registration / darkhwast ki fee | Muft — koi fee nahi |
| Scheme ki muddat | Rolling — koi muqarrar aakhri tareekh nahi |

Pehle is scheme ki raqam **Rs 7,500 har teen mahine** thi, jo baad me barha kar **Rs 10,500** kar di gayi. Agar aap ko internet par kisi purani post ya video me 7,500 ki raqam nazar aaye to wo purani maloomat hai — maujooda raqam 10,500 hai. Raqam me tabdeeli waqt ke sath ho sakti hai, is liye card milne se pehle maujooda raqam **DPMIS portal ya helpline 1312** par lazmi confirm kar lein.

Is ke ilawa card holders ko **Metro Bus aur Orange Line par muft safar** ki sahulat bhi di jati hai — is ki maujooda tafseel bhi portal ya helpline se confirm kar lein, kyun ke shehron aur routes ke hisab se is me tabdeeli ho sakti hai.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Himmat Card har mazoor shakhs ko nahi milta — is ke liye chand wazeh sharaet hain. Darkhwast guzar ko in sab par poora utarna hota hai:

**1. Punjab ka rehne wala:** Darkhwast guzar apne [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ke mutabiq Punjab ka mustaqil rehne wala ho. Dosre soobon ke rehne walon ke liye ye Punjab wali scheme nahi hai.

**2. Certified disability:** Shakhs ke paas Social Welfare Department ka jari karda **disability certificate** ho. Sirf ye kehna ke "me mazoor hoon" kafi nahi — certificate zila (district) disability board ki jaanch ke baad milta hai.

**3. Not Fit to Work:** Disability board ne darkhwast guzar ko **"Not Fit to Work"** qarar diya ho, yani mazoor honay ki wajah se wo koi kaam karne ke qabil nahi. Jo shakhs kaam kar sakta hai, wo is scheme ka mustahiq nahi.

**4. PMT score 45 ya kam:** Khandaan ka **Proxy Means Test (PMT) score 45 ya is se kam** ho. Ye score BISP ke qomi database (NSER) me darj hota hai. Agar aap ka PMT score is se zyada hai to aap is scheme ke liye eligible nahi hain. Apna PMT score aur registration [BISP 8171](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) ke zariye check kiya ja sakta hai.

**5. Kisi naukri me na ho:** Darkhwast guzar kisi sarkari ya private naukri me mulazim na ho. Naukri karne wala shakhs is scheme ka mustahiq nahi.

**6. Koi aur imdad na le raha ho:** Darkhwast guzar pehle se **BISP, Zakat, PSPA ya Bait-ul-Maal** ki koi mali imdad na le raha ho. Aik waqt me aik hi scheme ki imdad milti hai.

**7. Valid CNIC:** Darkhwast guzar ke paas valid CNIC ho — behtar hai ke wo NADRA ka disability logo wala CNIC ho (jis par wheelchair ka nishan bana ho). Agar aap ka [CNIC expired](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) hai to pehle usay renew karwa lein, kyun ke expired CNIC par verification wahin ruk jayegi.

Agar in me se koi aik shart bhi poori na ho to darkhwast manzoor nahi hoti. Is liye apply karne se pehle apni ehliyaat khud check kar lein — is se aap ka waqt bachega aur daftar ke chakkar bhi kam honge.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

Registration aur verification ke liye ye kagzaat tayyar rakhein:

**1. Valid CNIC:** Darkhwast guzar ka apna CNIC (13 hindson wala number). Disability logo wala CNIC ho to behtar hai, warna aam valid CNIC bhi chal jata hai.

**2. Disability certificate:** Social Welfare Department ya zila disability board ka jari karda mazoor honay ka certificate. Agar certificate abhi tak nahi bana to pehle zila Social Welfare Office se assessment karwa kar ye certificate hasil karein — is ke baghair aagay koi kaam nahi hoga.

**3. Rehaish ka saboot:** Punjab me rehaish ka saboot — CNIC par darj pata hi is ka saboot hota hai. Agar pata tabdeel hua hai to pehle CNIC par pata update karwa lein.

**4. Mobile number:** Apna active mobile number, jo aap ke naam par registered ho. Isi number par status aur raqam se mutaliq SMS aate hain.

**5. Taleem aur tajurbay ki maloomat:** DPMIS profile me taleem aur kaam ke tajurbay ki maloomat bhi mangi jati hai — ye maloomat tayyar rakhein taa ke form bharte waqt rukna na pare.

**6. Aamdani ka saboot (agar manga jaye):** Khandaan ki aamdani ki maloomat bhi mangi ja sakti hai. Agar aap ke paas [income certificate](/guides/income-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) hai to wo bhi tayyar rakhein — manga gaya to foran de sakein.

## Step-by-Step: Himmat Card Ke Liye Registration Ka Tareeqa

Jaisa upar bataya gaya, Himmat Card ka koi alag form nahi — tareeqa ye hai:

**Step 1 — Disability certificate hasil karein:** Agar aap ke paas Social Welfare Department ka disability certificate nahi hai to sab se pehle apne **zila ke District Social Welfare Office** jayein. Wahan se aap ko zila disability board ke samne assessment ke liye bheja jayega. Board ki jaanch ke baad certificate jari hota hai aur is me "Not Fit to Work" ki sifarish darj hoti hai. Ye certificate Himmat Card ki bunyad hai.

**Step 2 — DPMIS par apna record darj ya update karein:** Certificate milne ke baad official portal **dpmis.punjab.gov.pk** kholein aur apna record register ya update karein. Form me apna 13 hindson wala CNIC number, pata, mobile number, taleem aur tajurba darj karein. Tamam maloomat CNIC aur certificate ke mutabiq bilkul durust likhein — ghalat number ya pata verification me masla paida kar dega.

**Step 3 — Social Welfare Office se record ki tasdeeq karwayein:** Agar aap online form khud nahi bhar sakte to apne zila Social Welfare Office chale jayein. Wahan ka staff aap ka data check kar ke aap ko mauqe par hi DPMIS me register kar dega. Ye rasta un logon ke liye behtar hai jin ke paas internet ya smartphone ki sahulat nahi.

**Step 4 — Shortlist ka intezar karein:** Record darj honay ke baad Social Welfare Department aap ki maloomat ko **NADRA aur BISP database** se milata hai — CNIC ki tasdeeq, PMT score aur pehle se milne wali imdad ki jaanch. Jo afrad tamam sharaet par poora utarte hain, unhein system khud shortlist karta hai.

**Step 5 — Card ki wasooli:** Shortlist honay par aap ko ittila di jati hai aur **Bank of Punjab** ke zariye Himmat ATM Card jari hota hai. Card milne ke baad har teen mahine ki raqam is card par aati hai, jo aap kisi bhi ATM se nikal sakte hain.

Yaad rakhein: kisi agent, dukaan ya WhatsApp number ko paise de kar "card banwa lena" aik dhoka hai. Registration **bilkul muft** hai aur sirf official portal ya Social Welfare Office se hoti hai. Google Play Store par "Himmat Card" ke naam se bohot si jaali apps bhi hain — hukumat ki is kaam ke liye koi official app nahi, sirf official website **dpmis.punjab.gov.pk** par bharosa karein.

## Status Check Karne Ka Tareeqa

Registration ke baad apni darkhwast ya card ka status ghar baithe check kiya ja sakta hai:

**1.** Official portal kholein: **dpmis.punjab.gov.pk/himmatcard-verification**

**2.** Us shakhs ka **13 hindson wala CNIC number** baghair dash (—) ke darj karein jis ke naam par registration hai. Kisi sarparast ya rishtedaar ka CNIC na likhein — sirf usi mazoor shakhs ka CNIC jis ka record hai.

**3.** Submit karne par screen par aap ki darkhwast ya card ka status dikhaya jayega — masalan registration mukammal hai, verification jari hai, ya card jari ho chuka hai.

Agar portal par status na dikhe ya page load na ho to pareshan na hon — pehle apna CNIC number dobara check karein, phir kuch der baad dobara koshish karein. Agar masla barqarar rahe to **helpline 1312** par call kar ke apna CNIC number bata kar status maloom kar lein. Helpline par bhi yehi maloomat di jati hai jo portal par hoti hai.

## Raqam Kab Aur Kaise Milti Hai

Card manzoor honay ke baad raqam **har teen mahine (quarterly)** Bank of Punjab ke Himmat ATM Card par aati hai. Aap ye raqam kisi bhi ATM se nikal sakte hain — is ke liye kisi daftar jane ki zaroorat nahi. Raqam aane par aap ke registered mobile number par SMS bhi aata hai.

Kuch zaroori baatein:

- **Pehli qist** card milne ke baad agle quarter me aati hai — card milte hi foran raqam nahi aati, is liye sabr karein.
- **CNIC valid rakhein:** Agar aap ka CNIC expire ho jaye to raqam ruk sakti hai, kyun ke Bank of Punjab ki biometric tasdeeq CNIC se hoti hai. Expired CNIC foran renew karwayein.
- **Mobile number update rakhein:** Agar aap ne mobile number badla hai to DPMIS profile me naya number lazmi update karein, warna SMS aur ittila aap tak nahi pahunchegi.
- **Koi katoti nahi:** Ye raqam poori ki poori aap ki hai. Agar koi shakhs card se raqam nikalne ke badle "commission" mange to wo dhoka hai — foran helpline 1312 ya qareebi Social Welfare Office ko ittila dein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Rukwa Deti Hain

**1. Certificate ke baghair apply karna:** Sirf CNIC se Himmat Card nahi milta. Disability certificate ke baghair darkhwast aagay hi nahi barhti. Pehle certificate, phir registration — yehi tartib hai.

**2. Ghalat CNIC number likhna:** Status check ya registration me apne CNIC ki jagah sarparast ka CNIC likh dena sab se aam ghalti hai. Hamesha usi shakhs ka CNIC likhein jis ke naam par record hai.

**3. PMT score check kiye baghair darkhwast dena:** Agar aap ka PMT score 45 se zyada hai to darkhwast manzoor nahi hogi. Pehle apna score check karein — agar score zyada hai to [BISP 8171](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) par apna NSER record update karwayein.

**4. Jaali apps aur agents par bharosa:** "Himmat Card app download karein" ya "card ke liye 2,000 rupay fees dein" — ye sab dhoka hai. Registration muft hai aur sirf dpmis.punjab.gov.pk ya Social Welfare Office se hoti hai. Kisi ko apna CNIC, mobile number ya card ki tafseel na dein.

**5. Purani raqam par yaqeen karna:** Internet par ab bhi bohot si posts me 7,500 rupay ki raqam likhi hai. Maujooda raqam 10,500 hai — purani posts dekh kar apni umeedain us hisab se na lagayein.

**6. Mobile number update na karna:** Number badal gaya aur DPMIS me purana number reh gaya to raqam aur ittila ki khabar aap tak nahi pahunchegi. Number badalte hi profile update karein.

**7. Koi muqarrar aakhri tareekh samajhna:** Is scheme ki koi fixed last date nahi — registration rolling hai. Koi shakhs "aakhri tareekh qareeb hai, jaldi fees dein" kahe to wo jhoot bol raha hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Himmat Card par har teen mahine kitni raqam milti hai?**

Maujooda official maloomat ke mutabiq Himmat Card par har teen mahine **Rs 10,500** milte hain, yani saal bhar me Rs 42,000. Raqam Bank of Punjab ke Himmat ATM Card par aati hai. Pehle ye raqam Rs 7,500 thi, jo baad me barha di gayi — maujooda raqam apply karne se pehle DPMIS portal ya helpline 1312 par confirm kar lein.

**Himmat Card ke liye online apply kaise karein?**

Himmat Card ka koi alag online form nahi hai. Pehle apne zila Social Welfare Office se disability certificate hasil karein, phir apna record official portal **dpmis.punjab.gov.pk** par register ya update karein. Tamam sharaet poori honay par system aap ko khud shortlist karta hai aur Bank of Punjab ke zariye card jari hota hai. Registration bilkul muft hai.

**Himmat Card ke liye kaun eligible hai?**

Punjab ka rehne wala wo shakhs eligible hai jis ke paas Social Welfare Department ka disability certificate ho, disability board ne usay "Not Fit to Work" qarar diya ho, us ka PMT score 45 ya kam ho, wo kisi naukri me na ho aur pehle se BISP, Zakat, PSPA ya Bait-ul-Maal ki koi imdad na le raha ho.

**Himmat Card ka status kaise check karein?**

Official verification page **dpmis.punjab.gov.pk/himmatcard-verification** kholein aur us mazoor shakhs ka 13 hindson wala CNIC number baghair dash ke darj karein. Status screen par dikh jayega. Agar portal na khule to helpline **1312** par call kar ke CNIC number bata kar status maloom kar lein.

**Himmat Card ki registration ki koi fee ya aakhri tareekh hai?**

Nahi — registration bilkul muft hai aur is scheme ki koi muqarrar aakhri tareekh nahi hai, registration har waqt jari rehti hai. Jo shakhs fee mange ya aakhri tareekh ka dar dikhaye, wo dhoka de raha hai. Sirf official portal dpmis.punjab.gov.pk aur apne zila Social Welfare Office par bharosa karein.
