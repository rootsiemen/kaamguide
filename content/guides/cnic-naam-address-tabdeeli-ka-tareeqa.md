## Pehli Baat: CNIC Modification Kya Hoti Hai

Aap ke [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) par likha naam ya pata ghalat ho to us ko theek karwana **modification** kehlata hai. Ye naya card banwana nahi hai aur na hi renewal hai — NADRA aap ke record me sirf woh field badal kar naya card jari kar deta hai. Naam ki spelling ki chhoti ghalti ho, shadi ke baad surname badalna ho, ya aap kisi naye sheher shift ho gaye hon aur pata badalna ho — teeno kaam isi modification ke zariye hote hain.

Aik baat shuru me hi samajh lein: **naam aur pata dono aik hi modification application me badle ja sakte hain**, lekin in ke kagzaat alag hain aur in ke rules bhi alag hain. Naam badalna sab se sakht kaam hai — NADRA is ko sirf aik dafa karne deta hai aur is ke liye saboot lazmi hai. Pata badalna is ke muqable me asaan hai aur ye kaam PakID app se ghar baithe bhi ho sakta hai. Is guide me dono tareeqe alag alag samjhaye gaye hain.

**Aik zaroori wazahat:** Is guide ki tamam fees aur waqt NADRA ki official fee structure page (**nadra.gov.pk/feeStructure**) se liye gaye hain, aur kagzaat aur tareeqa NADRA ki policy aur is ki PakID FAQ ke mutabiq likha gaya hai. Fees waqt ke sath badal sakti hain, is liye apply karne se pehle NADRA ki fee page ya PakID app par raqam zaroor dobara check kar lein.

## Naam Ki Tabdeeli: Chhoti Ghalti Ya Pura Naam Badalna

NADRA naam ki tabdeeli ko do hisso me dekhta hai, aur in dono ka tareeqa aur kagzaat alag hain:

**1. Chhoti spelling ki ghalti (minor correction):** Agar aap ke naam ki spelling CNIC par ghalat chhap gayi hai — masalan "Muhammad" ki jagah "Mohammad" ya aik harf ka farq — to ye chhoti tabdeeli hai. Is ke liye aam taur par sirf aik saboot kafi hota hai: **computerized birth certificate** ya **matric ki sanad**, jis par naam sahi likha ho. Agar aap ki paidaish Union Council ke record me darj hai to pehle wahan birth certificate theek karwana parta hai — NADRA ki policy ke mutabiq birth certificate theek hue baghair CNIC ka naam nahi badla jata.

**2. Bara naam tabdeel karna (major name change):** Agar aap apna naam poora badalna chahte hain, naam me koi lafz jorna ya nikalna chahte hain, shadi ki wajah se surname badalna chahte hain, ya mazhab badalne ki wajah se naam badal rahe hain, to ye bari tabdeeli hai. Is ke liye **affidavit (halaf-nama)** lazmi hai. NADRA ka apna format hai — **CNIC ke liye Form C2** aur B-Form ke liye Form C1 — jo Pak-Identity ki website se milta hai. Is affidavit me purana naam aur naya naam dono saaf likhe hote hain.

**Kuch sakht rules jo yaad rakhne chahiye:**

- **Naam sirf aik dafa badla ja sakta hai.** Aik dafa naam tabdeel karne ke baad aap purane naam par wapas nahi ja sakte aur na hi dosra naya naam apna sakte hain. Is liye form bharne se pehle naya naam ki spelling soch samajh kar likhein.
- **Shadi ya talaq ke baad surname badalna** is rule se alag hai — khawateen shadi ke baad shohar ka naam aur talaq ke baad apna purana naam likhwa sakti hain, is ke liye nikah-nama ya talaq-nama saboot hota hai.
- **Mazhab badalne par naam tabdeel karne** ke liye Dar-ul-Ifta ka certificate chahiye hota hai.
- **Agar mamla jhagre wala ya qanooni pechida ho** — masalan koi aur shakhs aap ke naam par aitraz kare — to civil court ka order chahiye ho sakta hai. Aam cases me is ki zaroorat nahi parti.

**Aik aur ahem baat:** NADRA ki PakID app me naam aur tareekh-e-pedaish online badalne ka option nahi hai — app me sirf marital status, address, photo, signature, mother language, blood relative, qualification aur contact details badle ja sakte hain. Is liye **naam ki tabdeeli hamesha NADRA Registration Centre (NRC) par ja kar hi hoti hai**.

## Address Ki Tabdeeli: Mustaqil Aur Mojooda Pata

CNIC par do pate hote hain — **mustaqil pata (permanent address)** aur **mojooda pata (present address)**. Dono alag alag badle ja sakte hain, aur dono ke liye tareeqa aik jaisa hai. Aksar log tab pata badalte hain jab woh kiraye ke ghar se apne ghar shift hote hain, dosre sheher chale jate hain, ya shadi ke baad naye ghar me rehne lagte hain.

**Pata badalne ke do tareeqe hain:**

**Tareeqa 1 — PakID app se online:** NADRA ki PakID app me address un fields me shamil hai jo online badle ja sakte hain. App me Modification chunein, ID card par Update Card karein, aur address ka field add kar ke naya pata likhein. App aap se naye pate ka saboot bhi mang sakta hai. NADRA ki fee page ke mutabiq **non-printable fields** ki update NRC se Rs 100 me 2 din me hoti hai aur PakID app se USD 5 me 2 din me — lekin pata card par chhapta hai, is liye aam tor par is par modification ki aam fee lagti hai (neeche fee table dekhein). App par jo fee dikhaye, wahi hatmi hai.

**Tareeqa 2 — NADRA centre par:** Agar aap ke paas smartphone nahi hai, app kaam nahi kar raha, ya aap ko card par pata bhi tabdeel karwana hai, to qareebi NADRA Registration Centre par ja kar modification ki application dein. Wahan aap ka naya card bhi ban jaye ga.

**Naye pate ka saboot:** NADRA aam taur par naye pate ka saboot mangta hai. Is ke liye bijli ya gas ka bill, kiraya-nama, ya woh kagaz jo aap ke naye pate ko sabit kare, sath le jayein. Agar bill aap ke naam par nahi hai to ghar ke malik ka kiraya-nama ya affidavit kaam aa sakta hai. Centre par jaane se pehle NADRA helpline **1777** par call kar ke apne case ke liye sahi kagaz confirm kar lein — is se aik chakkar bach jata hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kis Haalat Me Kya Chahiye

Neeche di gayi table me har haalat ke kagzaat likhe hain. Centre jaane se pehle apni haalat wali line par nishan laga lein aur tamam kagzaat ki **asal (original)** copies sath le jayein — photocopy se kaam nahi chalta.

| Haalat | Zaroori kagzaat |
| --- | --- |
| Naam ki chhoti spelling ghalti | Original CNIC + computerized birth certificate ya matric ki sanad (jis par naam sahi ho) |
| Pura naam badalna | Original CNIC + computerized birth certificate + taleemi asnaad + **Form C2 affidavit** (NADRA ka format) |
| Shadi ke baad surname badalna | Original CNIC + nikah-nama + birth certificate / taleemi sanad + Form C2 affidavit |
| Mazhab badalne par naam badalna | Original CNIC + Dar-ul-Ifta ka certificate + birth certificate + Form C2 affidavit |
| Pata badalna | Original CNIC + naye pate ka saboot (bijli/gas ka bill ya kiraya-nama) |
| Tareekh-e-pedaish theek karwana | Original CNIC + computerized birth certificate ya matric ki sanad / passport / verified service record |

**Affidavit kaise banayein:** Form C2 NADRA ke Pak-Identity portal se download karein, us me purana naam, naya naam, walid ya shohar ka naam aur CNIC number likhein, phir is ko **Rs 50 ke stamp paper** par print karwa kar Oath Commissioner se tasdeeq karwayein. NADRA ki haaliya policy ke mutabiq CNIC ki tabdeeli ke liye affidavit ki alag se attestation ki zaroorat nahi bhi ho sakti — lekin stamp paper par sahi format me affidavit hona lazmi hai.

**Khoon ke rishtedar ki zaroorat:** Pehli dafa naam ki tabdeeli karwate waqt NADRA aap se **khoon ke rishtedar (blood relative)** ko sath lane ko keh sakta hai, jis ki biometric tasdeeq bhi hoti hai. Is liye walid, walida, bhai ya behn me se kisi aik ko apna CNIC le kar sath le jayein, taake centre par ye masla na bane.

## Fees Aur Waqt: Official Table

Ye fees **NADRA ki official fee structure page (nadra.gov.pk/feeStructure)** se li gayi hain. Naam ya pata badalna — dono hi **modification** hain, is liye dono ki fee aik hi hai.

| Card | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CNIC modification** | **Rs 400** (15 din) | **Rs 1,150** (12 din) | **Rs 2,150** (6 din) |
| **Smart NIC modification** | **Rs 750** (31 din) | **Rs 1,500** (23 din) | **Rs 2,500** (9 din) |

**Ahem note:** Ye waqt **fee jama karne ke baad** shuru hota hai, aur ye NADRA ka hadaf hai — chhuttiyan ya verification ki wajah se aik do din ka farq aa sakta hai. Smart NIC par chip ki wajah se is ka waqt aam CNIC se zyada hai: normal par 31 din. Agar aap ko card jaldi chahiye to executive behtar hai, lekin us ki fee bhi zyada hai.

**Doosri mutaliqa fees (NADRA fee page se):**

| Kaam | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Non-printable field ki update (NRC se) | Rs 100 (2 din) |
| Non-printable field ki update (PakID app se) | USD 5 (2 din) |
| Tareekh-e-pedaish ki tabdeeli (1 saal tak) | Rs 1,000 |
| Tareekh-e-pedaish ki tabdeeli (1–2 saal) | Rs 2,000 |
| Tareekh-e-pedaish ki tabdeeli (2–3 saal) | Rs 3,000 |
| Tareekh-e-pedaish ki tabdeeli (3 saal se zyada) | Rs 5,000 |
| Dosri dafa tareekh-e-pedaish ki tabdeeli | Rs 10,000 |

Tareekh-e-pedaish ki 5 saal se zyada tabdeeli ke liye NADRA ke Director General ki manzoori bhi chahiye hoti hai, aur 10 saal ya us se zyada ki tabdeeli ko NADRA ghair-haqeeqi samajhta hai — is liye is ki manzoori mushkil hai. **Delivery charges alag hain:** Pakistan ke andar card ki delivery par Rs 165 lagte hain.

## NADRA Centre Par Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Naam ki tabdeeli ho ya pata — centre par tareeqa yehi hai. Poora kaam aam taur par aik hi visit me ho jata hai, lekin subah jaldi jana behtar hai kyun ke token ki line lambi ho sakti hai.

**Step 1: Kagzaat tayyar karein.** Upar di gayi table se apni haalat ke tamam kagzaat ki asal copies sath rakhein. Agar birth certificate Union Council se theek karwana hai to woh kaam pehle kar lein — NADRA centre par is ka hal nahi hai.

**Step 2: Qareebi NADRA Registration Centre par jayein.** Apne sheher ka qareebi centre NADRA ki website ya helpline 1777 se maloom karein. Centre par ja kar **token lein** aur batayein ke aap ko naam ya pata ki tabdeeli (modification) karwani hai.

**Step 3: CNICF form lein aur bharein.** Counter se **CNICF (Computerized National Identity Card Form)** lein. Is me naya naam ya naya pata bilkul waisa likhein jaisa aap ke sabooti kagaz par hai — affidavit par likha naam aur form par likha naam aik jaisa hona chahiye, warna application atak sakti hai.

**Step 4: Biometric tasdeeq karwayein.** Aap ki ungliyon ke nishan (fingerprints) liye jayein ge, tasveer li jaye gi aur dastakhat liye jayein ge. Pehli dafa naam badal rahe hon to sath aaye khoon ke rishtedar ki biometric bhi hogi.

**Step 5: Form aur kagzaat jama karwayein.** Bhara hua form, tamam asal kagzaat aur affidavit counter par jama karwayein. Officer aap ke kagzaat check kare ga aur ho sakta hai ke aap se aap ke khandan ya kagzaat ke bare me chand sawal bhi pooche — ye aam procedure hai, ghabrayein nahi.

**Step 6: Fee jama karein aur receipt sambhalein.** Apni pasand ki category chunein — Normal, Urgent ya Executive — aur fee jama karein. **Tracking ID wali receipt sambhal kar rakhein** — isi se aap apne card ka status check karein ge. Purana CNIC NADRA zapt kar ke wapas kar dega ya naya card milne tak aap ke paas rehne de ga; centre ke rules ke mutabiq amal karein.

**Step 7: Naya card wasool karein.** Aap ko SMS se khabar mile gi ke card tayyar hai. Naya card **usi centre se** lena hota hai jis par aap ne apply kiya tha — is liye us centre ka naam aur apni receipt sambhal kar rakhein.

## PakID App Se Online Tabdeeli

Agar sirf **pata** badalna hai to aap ko centre jaane ki zaroorat nahi — NADRA ki **PakID app** se ye kaam ghar baithe ho sakta hai. Naam ki tabdeeli app se nahi hoti, ye yaad rakhein.

**Step 1: PakID app kholein.** Apne mobile par official PakID app kholein aur apne CNIC number aur mobile number se login karein. App sirf official store se download karein — kisi aur link se milne wali app par apna CNIC aur maloomat kabhi na dein.

**Step 2: Modification chunein.** App me **Modification** ka option chunein, phir ID Documents me ja kar **ID card** aur **Update Card** par tap karein.

**Step 3: Address ka field add karein.** Sabz **+** button se woh field add karein jo badalna hai — yahan **address**. Naya pata bilkul sahi likhein, sheher, gali/mohalla aur makan number samet.

**Step 4: Saboot upload karein.** App aap se naye pate ka saboot mang sakta hai — bijli ya gas ke bill ya kiraya-nama ki saaf tasveer upload karein.

**Step 5: Submit karein aur fee dein.** Form submit karne ke baad NADRA aap ki application check kare ga aur manzoori ke baad aap ko email ya SMS se fee dene ko kahe ga. Fee dene ke baad hi aap ke card ki tayyari ka waqt shuru hota hai. Status app me hi check hota rehta hai, aur naya card aap ke diye hue pate par bhej diya jata hai.

**Agar app me address ka option na mile:** Kuch purane cards ya khaas cases me app address ka field nahi dikhata — is soorat me pareshan na hon, qareebi NADRA centre par ja kar wohi kaam karwayein. App ka kaam na karna aap ki application ko nuksan nahi deta.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

**1. Pehle birth certificate theek kiye baghair NADRA chale jana:** Agar aap ka birth certificate Union Council ke record me ghalat hai to NADRA aap ka naam nahi badle ga — aap ko wapas bhej diya jaye ga. Pehle Union Council se birth certificate theek karwayein, phir NADRA jayein.

**2. Affidavit aur form par naam ka farq:** Affidavit par aik spelling aur form par dosri spelling likhna sab se aam ghalti hai. Dono par naam **harf ba harf aik jaisa** hona chahiye. Form jama karne se pehle dono ko mila kar parh lein.

**3. Naam do dafa badalne ki koshish:** Naam sirf aik dafa badla ja sakta hai. Agar aap ne pehle hi aik dafa naam badla hua hai to dosri tabdeeli ki koshish na karein — NADRA is ko reject kar de ga aur aap ki fee bhi zaya ho sakti hai.

**4. Ghalat category chunna:** Sirf is liye ke card jaldi mil jaye, executive fee de dena jab aap ko jaldi na ho — Rs 2,150 ki bajaye Rs 400 me bhi kaam ho sakta hai. Apni zaroorat ke mutabiq category chunein.

**5. Receipt ya Tracking ID kho dena:** Receipt ke baghair aap apne card ka status nahi check kar sakte aur na hi card wasool kar sakte hain. Receipt ki tasveer foran mobile me save kar lein.

**6. Kisi agent ko paisa dena:** NADRA ke kaam ke liye koi agent nahi chahiye. Jo shakhs "jaldi card" ya "bina kagaz ke naam tabdeel" karne ke naam par paisa mange, woh dhoka de raha hai. Sirf official fee dein — na zyada, na kisi shakhs ko.

**7. Purane pata par card mangwana:** Agar aap shift ho chuke hain to PakID app ya form me **naya pata** likhein. Purane pate par bheja gaya card wapas aa sakta hai aur aap ko dobara chakkar lagana pare ga.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CNIC par naam badalne ki fee kitni hai?**
NADRA ki official fee page ke mutabiq aam CNIC ki modification ki fee Normal par Rs 400 (15 din), Urgent par Rs 1,150 (12 din) aur Executive par Rs 2,150 (6 din) hai. Smart NIC ki modification Normal par Rs 750 (31 din), Urgent par Rs 1,500 (23 din) aur Executive par Rs 2,500 (9 din) hai. Naam ya pata — dono par yehi fee lagti hai, kyun ke dono modification hain.

**CNIC par naam online badla ja sakta hai?**
Nahi. NADRA ki PakID app me naam aur tareekh-e-pedaish online badalne ka option nahi hai — is ke liye aap ko NADRA Registration Centre par ja kar, kagzaat aur affidavit ke sath, application deni hoti hai. App se sirf pata, marital status, photo, signature aur contact details jaisi fields badli ja sakti hain.

**Naam ki tabdeeli ke liye kin kagzaat ki zaroorat hoti hai?**
Chhoti spelling ki ghalti ke liye computerized birth certificate ya matric ki sanad kafi hoti hai. Pure naam ki tabdeeli ke liye original CNIC, birth certificate, taleemi asnaad aur NADRA ke format par Form C2 affidavit chahiye hota hai. Shadi ke baad surname ke liye nikah-nama aur mazhab badalne par Dar-ul-Ifta ka certificate bhi lagta hai.

**CNIC par address tabdeel karne me kitna waqt lagta hai?**
Waqt wohi hai jo modification ki fee table me hai: aam CNIC ke liye Normal 15 din, Urgent 12 din aur Executive 6 din; Smart NIC ke liye Normal 31 din, Urgent 23 din aur Executive 9 din. Ye waqt fee jama karne ke baad shuru hota hai. PakID app se non-printable field ki update 2 din me hoti hai.

**CNIC par naam kitni dafa badla ja sakta hai?**
Sirf aik dafa. Aik dafa naam tabdeel karne ke baad aap purane naam par wapas nahi ja sakte aur na hi koi teesra naam apna sakte hain. Is rule me sirf shadi ya talaq ke baad surname ki tabdeeli alag hai, jis ke liye nikah-nama ya talaq-nama saboot hota hai.
