## Pehli Baat: Solar Tube Well Scheme Kya Hai

**Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme** — sarkari naam **CM Solarization Program for Agri Tubewells** — Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo programme hai jis ke tehet kisanon ke **diesel ya bijli par chalne wale tube wells ko solar energy par shift** kiya jata hai. Is ka maqsad kheti ko mehnge diesel aur bijli ke bill se aazad karna hai: solar lagne ke baad tube well din me suraj ki roshni se chalta hai aur kisan ko har fasal par diesel ya bijli ka kharch nahi uthana parta. Ye programme **Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif** ke Kissan Package ka hissa hai aur is ko **Agriculture Department Punjab** chalata hai.

Scheme ke pehle marhale (Phase 1) me **8,000 tube wells** ko solar par shift karna tha. Hukumat ne is ke liye **Rs 9 billion** ki subsidy rakhi thi. Programme ka iftitah **1 February 2025** ko hua tha. Punjab Portal ki launch report ke mutabiq pehle marhale ke liye **5,30,000 se zyada darkhwastein** aayi thin, jin me se taqreeban **3,85,000 kisan eligible** paye gaye, aur in me se **8,000 tube wells** ka intekhab **February 2025 ki qurra andazi (draw)** se hua tha.

**Sab se ahem taaza khabar — pehle ye samajh lein:** Phase 1 ki darkhwastein **band ho chuki hain**. Pehle aakhri tareekh 31 December 2024 thi, jo phir **6 January 2025** tak barha di gayi thi. **23 November 2026 tak naye marhale (Phase 2) ka koi sarkari elaan nahi hua** — na portal par, na Agriculture Department ki site par, na kisi bare akhbar me. Is waqt internet par jo websites "2026 me registration khuli hai — abhi apply karein" likh rahi hain, wo **sarkari websites nahi hain** aur un par bharosa nahi karna chahiye. Is guide me aap ko Phase 1 ki asal shartein, band hone ki wajah se kya karna hai, aur agle marhale ki tayyari ka poora tareeqa diya gaya hai.

**Aik ahem note:** official portal **cmstp.punjab.gov.pk** 23 November 2026 ko check karne par load nahi hua — text fetch aur live browser dono par connection reset ki ghalti aayi. Is liye is guide ke numbers aur shartein official **Punjab Portal (punjab.gov.pk)** ki shaya shuda scheme page se li gayi hain, jo 23 November 2026 ko parhi gayi, aur status ki tasdeeq 19 November 2026 ki taaza jaanch se ki gayi hai. Portal dobara khulne ya naye marhale ke elaan par yahi page hatmi zariya ho ga.

## Subsidy Kitni Milti Hai

Subsidy kisi percentage me nahi, **solar system ki taaqat (kilowatt) ke hisab se fixed raqam** me milti hai. Official Punjab Portal ki table ye hai:

| Solar system ki taaqat | Hukumat ki subsidy |
| --- | --- |
| 10 kW | Rs 5,00,000 |
| 15 kW | Rs 7,50,000 |
| 20 kW | Rs 10,00,000 |

Matlab agar aap 10 kilowatt ka solar system lagwate hain to hukumat Rs 5 lakh deti hai, 15 kilowatt par Rs 7.5 lakh aur 20 kilowatt par **Rs 10 lakh** tak subsidy milti hai. Baqi raqam — system ki kul qeemat me se subsidy minus kar ke — kisan ko khud apne pasandeeda vendor ko deni hoti hai. Portal kul system ki qeemat nahi batata, kyunke qeemat vendor aur zila ke hisab se mukhtalif hoti hai. Is liye muntakhib hone ke baad apne zila ke vendor se **likhit (written) quote** lein aur phir faisla karein.

**Ghalat figures se hoshiyar rahein:** Kuch websites likhti hain ke "80% subsidy" ya "75% se 80% tak subsidy" milti hai, ya "Rs 4 lakh se 8 lakh tak" milta hai. Ye **ghalat** hai — official shartein fixed raqam ki table deti hain, percentage ki nahi. Aisi hi aik aur ghalat baat ye phaili hui hai ke subsidy zameen ki miqdar par hai — nahi, subsidy sirf system ki taaqat (10, 15 ya 20 kW) par hai. Jo website official table ke baghair percentage likhe, us par bharosa na karein.

Subsidy sirf **pehle se maujood tube well ki solarization** par milti hai — naya tube well khodne ya naya connection lagwane par nahi. Aur aik khandan ko **sirf aik hi solar system** milta hai, chahe us ke naam par kitni hi zameen kyun na ho.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Official Punjab Portal ki shartein saaf hain. Aap apply kar sakte hain agar ye tamam shartein poori hoti hon:

**1. Punjab ka rehaishi hona lazmi hai:** Darkhwast guzar ke paas NADRA ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) hona chahiye aur us ki copy darkhwast ke sath lagani hoti hai. Punjab se bahir ke rehaishi is scheme ke eligible nahi hain.

**2. Kam az kam 1 acre zaraai zameen:** Aap ke naam par Punjab me kam az kam **1 acre** zameen honi chahiye. Zameen usi zila ki revenue haddood me honi chahiye jis zila me aap darkhwast de rahe hain. Malkiyat ka saboot **Fard-e-Malakiat** hai — ye darkhwast ke sath lagana lazmi hai. Agar aap ki zameen abhi tak apne naam par transfer nahi hui to pehle [fard-e-malkiat](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) ke zariye apne naam par karwa lein, phir apply karein.

**3. Sirf 10, 15 ya 20 kW ka system:** Solarization sirf inhi teen taaqaton ke system ke liye hoti hai. Is se chhota ya bara system is scheme me nahi ata.

**4. Bijli wale tube well ke liye khaas shartein:** Agar aap ka tube well bijli par chalta hai to aap ko **aakhri paid bijli bill** dena hota hai — ye aap ki tube well ki malkiyat ka saboot hai. Jo kisan bill ada nahi karte (non-payers/defaulters) wo eligible nahi hote. Bijli ka connection **30 October 2024 ya is se pehle** lagna chahiye tha — is tareekh ke baad lagne wale connection Phase 1 me eligible nahi the. Connection **3-phase** hona bhi lazmi hai.

**5. Diesel wala tube well bhi eligible hai:** Scheme sirf bijli wale tube wells ke liye nahi hai — **diesel par chalne wale tube wells** ki solarization bhi is me shamil hai. Diesel wale kisanon ko bijli bill ki shart nahi lagti, lekin zameen aur malkiyat ki shartein wohi rehti hain.

**6. Aik khandan, aik system:** Aik khandan ko sirf aik hi solar system milta hai. Aik hi ghar ke do afrad alag alag darkhwastein de kar do system nahi le sakte.

**Kisan Card ki shart nahi hai:** Kuch websites likhti hain ke apply ke liye [Punjab Kisan Card](/guides/punjab-kisan-card-apply-online) lazmi hai. Official Punjab Portal ki shartein me Kisan Card ka **koi zikr nahi** hai. Kisan Card aik alag scheme hai aur is solarization programme ki shart nahi hai — is par kisi ki baat par paise kharch na karein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Tayyari

Agle marhale ki window khulne se pehle ye kagzaat tayyar rakhein taake aakhri waqt me bhag-dor na karni pare:

**1. Valid CNIC ki copy:** Aap ka apna CNIC valid hona chahiye. Agar CNIC expire ho gaya hai ya abhi tak nahi bana to pehle CNIC renew karwa lein ya banwa lein — baghair valid CNIC ke portal par registration nahi hoti.

**2. Fard-e-Malakiat:** Aap ke naam par zameen ki malkiyat ka saboot. Fard taaza ho aur us me aap ka naam, zameen ki miqdar aur zila saaf darj ho. Purana ya ghalat fard darkhwast reject karwa sakta hai.

**3. Bijli wale tube well ka aakhri paid bill:** Sirf un kisanon ke liye jin ka tube well bijli par hai. Bill paid hona chahiye aur us par connection ki tareekh aur 3-phase connection saaf nazar ana chahiye. Agar bill gum ho gaya hai to apne bijli ke daftar se copy nikalwa lein. Naya [bijli connection](/guides/bijli-naya-connection-meter-lagwana) lagwane ka tareeqa alag hai — is scheme me purana connection hi kaam ata hai.

**4. Mobile number:** Aap ka apna active mobile number, jo aap ke naam par register ho. Portal par OTP aur status ki maloomat isi number par ati hai.

**5. Tube well ki tafseel:** Aap ke tube well ki qism (bijli ya diesel), us ki location aur system ki zaroorat (10, 15 ya 20 kW) — ye faisla pehle se kar lein ke aap ko kitne kW ka system chahiye, kyunke form me ye darj karna hota hai.

Kagzaat ki **saaf scan ya photo** tayyar rakhein — dhundli ya kati hui copy upload karne se darkhwast atak sakti hai.

## Online Apply Karne Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

Ye tareeqa **Phase 1 ka hai**, jo official portal par shaya hua tha. Is waqt window band hai, lekin jab naya marhala khule ga to tareeqa yahi hoga — tab portal par dobara confirm zaroor kar lein:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:** Apne mobile ya computer par **cmstp.punjab.gov.pk** kholein. Yaad rakhein: sirf isi address par bharosa karein. Milti-julti naam wali kisi aur website par form na bharein.

**Step 2 — Account banayein ya login karein:** Portal par apna CNIC number aur mobile number darj kar ke account banayein. Mobile par ane wala OTP likh kar tasdeeq karein.

**Step 3 — Application form bharein:** Form me apna naam, CNIC number, zila, zameen ki tafseel (acre aur Fard ke mutabiq), tube well ki qism (bijli ya diesel) aur chahiye gaye system ki taaqat (10, 15 ya 20 kW) darj karein. Portal se application form **download** bhi kiya ja sakta tha.

**Step 4 — Kagzaat upload karein:** CNIC ki copy, Fard-e-Malakiat aur bijli wale tube well ka aakhri paid bill upload karein. Har kagaz saaf aur mukammal ho.

**Step 5 — Form submit karein aur tracking number sambhal lein:** Submit karne ke baad aap ko aik tracking ya reference number milta hai. Is ko sambhal kar rakhein — isi se aap apni darkhwast ki status check kar sakte hain.

**Step 6 — Tasdeeq ka intezar karein:** Department aap ki zameen aur kagzaat ki tasdeeq karta hai. Eligible darkhwastein qurra andazi me shamil hoti hain aur muntakhib kisanon ko vendor chunne ka tareeqa bataya jata hai.

**Apply muft tha — koi fee nahi:** Phase 1 me darkhwast dene ki **koi fee nahi** thi. Jo shakhs form bharne, naam dalwane ya qurra andazi me naam nikalwane ke paise mange, wo **fraud** hai.

## Qurra Andazi, Result Aur Installation Ka Status

Phase 1 ka safar ab kis marhale par hai, ye samajhna zaroori hai taake aap ko pata ho ke aap kahan khare hain:

**Qurra andazi ho chuki hai:** February 2025 me pehle marhale ki qurra andazi hui thi, jis me 3,85,000 eligible kisanon me se **8,000 tube wells** muntakhib hue the. Intekhab computerized tha — kisi sifarish ya agent ka is me koi kirdar nahi tha.

**Result kaise check hota tha:** Muntakhib kisan apna result official portal **cmstp.punjab.gov.pk** par apna CNIC number darj kar ke check kar sakte the. Portal is waqt load nahi ho raha, is liye result check karne ke liye **helpline 0800-17000** par rabta karein ya apne zila ke Agriculture Extension daftar se maloom karein.

**Installation 2026 me jari hai:** Muntakhib tube wells par solar system lagane ka kaam 2026 me zila ba zila jari hai. February 2026 me Okara zila me 17 me se 12 tube wells ka saman deliver ho chuka tha (Business Recorder ki report ke mutabiq). Matlab programme khatam nahi hua — pehle marhale ki takmeel abhi chal rahi hai, aur isi liye naye marhale ka elaan abhi tak nahi hua.

**Aap ne Phase 1 me apply kiya tha?** Agar haan, to apna result helpline ya daftar se zaroor check karwa lein — ho sakta hai aap muntakhib hue hon aur aap ko khabar na mili ho. Agar aap eligible the lekin muntakhib nahi hue, to aap ki tayyari agle marhale ke liye kaam aaye gi.

## Agle Marhale Ki Tayyari: Abhi Kya Karein

Naya marhala kab khule ga, is ka koi sarkari elaan abhi nahi hai. Lekin jo kisan abhi se tayyar honge, wo window khulte hi sab se pehle apply kar sakein ge:

**1. Apne kagzaat abhi se durust kar lein:** CNIC valid rakhein, Fard-e-Malakiat taaza karwa lein aur bijli wale tube well ka bill waqt par ada karte rahein. Defaulter hone par aap agle marhale se bhi bahar ho sakte hain.

**2. Helpline par status poochte rahein:** **0800-17000** (subah 8 bajey se raat 8 bajey tak) par call kar ke poochein ke naya marhala kab khul raha hai. Yehi aik number hai jo department ne is scheme ke liye diya hai.

**3. Sirf sarkari zariye par nazar rakhein:** Naye marhale ka elaan **cmstp.punjab.gov.pk**, **agriculture.punjab.gov.pk** aur bare akhbaron par aaye ga. Kisi private website ke "apply now" button par click na karein.

**4. System ki taaqat ka faisla pehle se kar lein:** Apne tube well ki zaroorat dekh kar tay karein ke aap ko 10, 15 ya 20 kW ka system chahiye. Ghalat taaqat chunne se subsidy ki raqam bhi badal jaye gi.

**5. Vendor se qeemat pehle se maloom kar lein:** Apne zila ke solar vendors se 10, 15 aur 20 kW system ki likhit qeemat le lein taake window khulte hi aap ko pata ho ke subsidy ke baad aap ko kitne paise dene honge.

**6. Doosri solar scheme se na milayein:** Ghar ke liye **Free Solar Panel Scheme** (portal cmsolarscheme.punjab.gov.pk, SMS 8800) aik **alag** scheme hai. Is ke bare me tafseel [CM Punjab Free Solar Panel Scheme guide](/guides/cm-punjab-free-solar-panel-scheme-apply-online) me parhein. Tube well wali scheme ka us se koi talluq nahi — dono ke portal, shartein aur subsidy alag hain. Solar Panel Scheme ke Phase 2 ki manzoori 19 November 2026 ko hui hai (1,00,000 panels ka target), lekin us ki bhi abhi koi registration window nahi khuli.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Aur Fraud Se Bachao

Is scheme ke naam par fraud aur ghalat maloomat bohat phaili hui hai. In se bachein:

**1. "2026 registration khuli hai" wali websites:** 19 November 2026 ki jaanch ke mutabiq koi naya marhala elaan nahi hua. Jo website "abhi apply karein" keh kar aap se CNIC ya paise mange, wo sarkari nahi hai. Naya marhala sirf .gov.pk address par elaan ho ga.

**2. 80% subsidy ka jhoot:** Official subsidy fixed raqam hai (Rs 5 lakh / 7.5 lakh / 10 lakh). "80% subsidy" ya "75% tak" likhne wali websites official table ko nahi manti — un ki baat par qeemat ka faisla na karein.

**3. Zameen ki ghalat shart:** Kuch websites likhti hain ke 5 acre ya 25 acre zameen chahiye. Official shart **kam az kam 1 acre** hai. Is se zyada zameen ki shart Phase 1 me nahi thi.

**4. Kisan Card lazmi hone ka dawa:** Official shartein me Kisan Card ka zikr nahi hai. Jo shakhs Kisan Card banwane ke paise le kar solar scheme me naam dalwane ka wada kare, wo fraud hai.

**5. Agent ko paise dena:** Qurra andazi **computerized** hoti hai aur apply **muft** hota hai. Koi agent, sifarish ya rishwat aap ka naam nahi nikalwa sakti. Form bharne ke paise mangne wala shakhs fraud hai.

**6. CNIC aur kagzaat kisi ajnabi ko dena:** Portal ya department kabhi SMS ya WhatsApp par aap se CNIC ki copy ya bank ki tafseel nahi mangta. Aisi koi call ya message aaye to kabhi apne kagzaat na bhejein.

**7. Green Tractor se confusion:** [Green Tractor Scheme](/guides/punjab-green-tractor-scheme-apply-online) tractor ke liye hai, tube well ke liye nahi — dono ki subsidy, portal aur shartein alag hain. Aik scheme ki shartein doosri par lagu na karein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab Solar Tube Well Scheme me kitni subsidy milti hai?**
Official table ke mutabiq 10 kW system par Rs 5,00,000, 15 kW par Rs 7,50,000 aur 20 kW par Rs 10,00,000 subsidy milti hai. Ye fixed raqam hai, percentage nahi. Baqi raqam kisan ko khud vendor ko deni hoti hai.

**Kya abhi Solar Tube Well Scheme ke liye apply ho sakta hai?**
Nahi. Phase 1 ki darkhwastein 6 January 2025 ko band ho gayin theen aur 23 November 2026 tak naye marhale ka koi sarkari elaan nahi hua. Jo website ab apply karwane ke paise mange, wo fraud hai. Agle marhale ka intezar karein aur tab tak apne kagzaat durust kar lein.

**Solar Tube Well Scheme ke liye kam az kam kitni zameen chahiye?**
Kam az kam **1 acre** zaraai zameen jo aap ke apne naam par ho aur usi zila me ho jis me aap darkhwast de rahe hain. Malkiyat ka saboot Fard-e-Malakiat hai. 5 acre ya 25 acre ki shart official shartein me nahi hai.

**Bijli wale tube well ke liye kya shartein hain?**
Bijli ka connection **30 October 2024 ya is se pehle** lagna chahiye, connection **3-phase** hona chahiye aur aap ko **aakhri paid bijli bill** dena hota hai. Bill ada na karne wale (defaulters) eligible nahi hote. Diesel wale tube well ke liye bijli bill ki shart nahi lagti.

**Solar Tube Well Scheme ka result kaise check karein?**
Phase 1 ka result official portal cmstp.punjab.gov.pk par CNIC number se check hota tha. Portal 23 November 2026 ko load nahi ho raha, is liye result ke liye helpline **0800-17000** (subah 8 se raat 8 bajey tak) par call karein ya apne zila ke Agriculture Extension daftar se maloom karein.
