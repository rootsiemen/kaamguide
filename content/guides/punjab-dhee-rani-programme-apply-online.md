## Pehli Baat: Punjab Dhee Rani Programme Kya Hai

**Punjab Dhee Rani Programme** Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo programme hai jis ke tehet mustahiq aur kamzor tabqe ke walidain ki betiyon ki shadi **muft ijtimai shadi ki taqreeb** me karwai jati hai aur dulhan ko **Rs 2,00,000 salami** di jati hai. Ye programme **Chief Minister Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif** ne manzoor kiya aur is ko **Social Welfare and Bait-ul-Maal Department Punjab** chalata hai. Programme ka asal maqsad ye hai ke jo walidain mehengai aur maali tangi ki wajah se apni beti ki shadi izzat se nahi karwa sakte, un ki is samaji aur mazhabi zimmedari ko hukumat apne kharche par poora kare.

Programme ke tehet shadi **sarkari ijtimai taqreeb** me hoti hai — matlab aik hi waqt me bohat si dulhanein aik bare aur ba-izzat event me shadi karti hain, aur taqreeb ka tamam kharcha (venue, khana aur intezamaat) hukumat khud uthati hai. Dulhan ko salami ki raqam **Bank of Punjab ke ATM card** par milti hai, jo use taqreeb ke din hi diya jata hai.

**Sab se ahem taaza khabar:** Social Welfare Department ke mutabiq ijtimai shadiyon ki taqreebat **12 November 2026** se dobara shuru ho gayi hain aur mali saal **2026-27** me mazeed **5,000 mustahiq joron** ki shadiyan karwane ka hadaf hai. Is ka matlab ye hai ke registrations is waqt **khuli hui** hain — official portal [cmp.punjab.gov.pk](https://cmp.punjab.gov.pk) par Register ka button maujood hai aur form bhara ja sakta hai. Agar aap ki beti, behn ya koi qareebi mustahiq dulhan is programme ki shartein poori karti hai to ye apply karne ka behtareen waqt hai, kyun ke taqreebat ka schedule pehle se tay hai aur har taqreeb me mehdood jode shamil kiye jate hain.

Rs 2,00,000 ki salami dulhan ko **ATM card** par milti hai, jis ka matlab ye hai ke raqam seedha dulhan ke naam par khole gaye Bank of Punjab account me jati hai. Bank account aur card ki tayyari verification ke baad hoti hai — NADRA record aur biometric tasdeeq ke baad Bank of Punjab account kholta hai aur card taqreeb par dulhan ko diya jata hai. Card milne ke baad dulhan is se raqam ATM se nikal sakti hai.

Aik baat saaf samajh lein: portal ki FAQ me **Rs 1,21,000 ke tohfe** ka bhi zikr hai, lekin taaza elan aur portal ke home page me sirf **Rs 2,00,000 salami** ki tasdeeq hai. Ho sakta hai ke tohfe ki raqam marhale ke mutabiq badalti ho. Is liye apne card par kul kitni raqam hogi, ye **helpline 1312** par zaroor confirm kar lein — apply karne se pehle ye aik call aap ko ghalat umeed se bacha legi.

**Ahem shart:** ye Rs 2,00,000 ki madad **sirf un dulhanon ko milti hai jo hukumat ki ijtimai shadi ki taqreeb me shadi karti hain**. Agar khandan apni niji (private) shadi karwana chahta hai to wo is raqam ka haqdar nahi hoga. Ye shart programme ki bunyad hai — is liye apply karte waqt is par raazi hona lazmi hai.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Dhee Rani Programme ke liye dulhan ko ye shartein poori karni hoti hain:

**1. Umar 18 se 40 saal:** Dulhan ki umar kam se kam **18 saal** aur zyada se zyada **40 saal** honi chahiye. CNIC par darj tareekh-e-pedaish hi umar ka saboot hoti hai.

**2. Punjab ki rehaishi aur domicile:** Dulhan ke paas **Punjab ka domicile** hona lazmi hai. Agar domicile abhi tak nahi bana to pehle [domicile certificate banane ka tareeqa](/guides/domicile-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) parh kar use banwa lein, warna darkhwast aage nahi barhegi.

**3. Ghair-shadi-shuda hona:** Dulhan **kunwari** honi chahiye aur us ka **rishta tay** hona chahiye. Darkhwast ke sath ghair-shadi-shuda hone ka halafnama (affidavit) dena hota hai.

**4. Ijtimai shadi par raazi hona:** Dulhan aur us ke khandan ko hukumat ki taraf se mun'aqid ki jane wali **ijtimaai shadi ki taqreeb** me shirkat par raazi hona hoga. Jo khandan sirf raqam lena chahta hai aur taqreeb me shamil nahi hona chahta, wo eligible nahi hai.

**5. Mustahiq tabqe se taluq:** Programme **mustahiq** dulhanon ke liye hai. Tarjeeh un dulhanon ko di jati hai jo **yateem** hon, **nadhar/ghareeb** hon, **mazoor afrad (PWD)** hon ya **mazoor walidain ki betiyan** hon. Aam ghareeb khandan bhi apply kar sakte hain, lekin in tabqon ko pehle number par rakha jata hai. Agar aap ka khandan BISP ka mustahiq hai to [8171 par apni registration check](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) kar lein — is se aap ke mustahiq hone ki tasdeeq me madad mil sakti hai. Mazoor afrad ke liye [Himmat Card](/guides/himmat-card-apply-online) bhi aik alag sahulat hai, jo isi department ki scheme hai.

**6. Darkhwast kaun de sakta hai:** Darkhwast dulhan **khud**, ya us ke **walid, walida ya sarparast (legal guardian)** de sakta hai. Form bharte waqt darkhwast guzar ka CNIC aur mobile number darkar hota hai.

**7. Valid CNIC:** Dulhan ke paas NADRA ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) hona lazmi hai. Agar CNIC abhi tak nahi bana to pehle CNIC banwa lein — baghair CNIC ke portal par registration nahi hoti.

**8. Dulhe ki tafseel:** Form me dulhe ki maloomat bhi mangi jati hai, is liye dulhe ka CNIC number aur us ki buniyadi tafseel bhi tayyar rakhein.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Tayyari

Portal par form bharne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein taa ke darkhwast aadhi na reh jaye:

- **Dulhan ka valid CNIC** — number aur us ki saaf copy (JPG ya PNG format me, kyun ke portal par document upload karna hota hai).
- **Punjab ka domicile certificate** — dulhan ke Punjab ki rehaishi hone ka saboot.
- **Ghair-shadi-shuda hone ka halafnama (affidavit)** — ye portal ki shart hai; stamp paper par halafnama banwa lein.
- **Yateem / nadhar / mazoor hone ka saboot** — agar dulhan in me se kisi tabqe me aati hai to us ka saboot (masalan walid ka death certificate, ya disability certificate) tayyar rakhein, kyun ke is se tarjeeh milti hai.
- **Dulhe ka CNIC number** — form me dulhe ki tafseel bhi darj karni hoti hai.
- **Apne naam par registered mobile number** — tamam SMS aur updates isi number par aate hain; kisi aur ke naam wali SIM se register na karein.
- **Haaliya tasveer** — dulhan ki aik haaliya passport size tasveer bhi tayyar rakhein.
- **Ghar ka pata aur zila** — form me zila aur mukammal pata darj karna hota hai; apne zila ka naam durust likhein.

Sab se bara kaam **domicile aur halafnama** hai — bohat si darkhwastein sirf is liye ruk jati hain ke in me se aik kagaz tayyar nahi hota. Agar domicile nahi bana to use pehle banwa lein, aur halafnama kisi qareebi stamp vendor se banwa kar tayyar rakhein. Dono kaam aik din me ho jate hain.

## Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa: Step-by-Step

Dhee Rani Programme ke liye apply karne ka **official tareeqa** ye hai. Registration **muft** hai — kisi agent ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi:

**Step 1 — Official portal kholein:**
Apne mobile ya computer par official portal **[cmp.punjab.gov.pk](https://cmp.punjab.gov.pk)** kholein. Sirf yehi official website hai — social media par chalne wale "registration" links aur fake websites se bachein aur un par apna CNIC kabhi na dein.

**Step 2 — Account register karein:**
Portal par **Register** ka button dabayein. Registration ke liye apna **naam, CNIC number, zila aur mobile number** darj karein. Aap ke mobile par aik tasdeeqi code (OTP) aayega — use darj kar ke account mukammal karein.

**Step 3 — Application form bharein:**
Account me login karne ke baad application form kholein aur ye tafseel darj karein:
- Dulhan ki zaati maloomat (naam, CNIC, umar, pata)
- Dulhan ki ehliyaat ki tafseel (yateem / nadhar / mazoor tabqa, agar laagu ho)
- Dulhe ki buniyadi tafseel (naam, CNIC number)
- Zila aur rabte ka number

**Step 4 — Kagzaat upload karein:**
Form me mange gaye kagzaat — CNIC, domicile, halafnama aur deegar saboot — **JPG ya PNG** format me upload karein. Tasveer saaf aur parhne ke qabil honi chahiye; dhundli ya aadhi tasveer verification me reject ho sakti hai.

**Step 5 — Form submit karein aur reference number mehfooz rakhein:**
Form submit karne ke baad aap ko aik **reference ya tracking number** milega — use likh kar mehfooz rakh lein ya us ka screenshot le lein. Isi number se aap baad me apni darkhwast ka status check kar sakein ge.

**Step 6 — Verification ka intezar karein:**
Aap ki darkhwast ki **scrutiny aur verification** hoti hai — NADRA record check kiya jata hai, kagzaat ki tasdeeq hoti hai aur Bank of Punjab account kholne ke liye biometric tasdeeq bhi ho sakti hai. Verification mukammal hone par aap ko SMS se ittila milti hai ke aap muntakhib hui hain ya mazeed kagzaat darkar hain.

**Doosre tareeqe — helpline aur daftar:**
- **Helpline 1312** par call kar ke ehliyaat, registration ya status ke bare me pooch sakte hain.
- Apne zila ke **District Deputy (DD) office** ya Social Welfare Department ke help desk par ja kar bhi darkhwast ke bare me madad li ja sakti hai.
- Kuch zilon me call center ke zariye bhi registration me madad milti hai — tafseel helpline 1312 par maloom karein.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Apply karne ke baad poora process is tarah chalta hai:

- **Form bharna:** Portal par form bharna sirf **15 se 30 minute** ka kaam hai, agar tamam kagzaat pehle se tayyar hon.
- **Verification:** Kagzaat aur NADRA record ki tasdeeq me aam tor par **kuch hafte** lagte hain, kyun ke har darkhwast ki scrutiny hoti hai. Is doran department aap se mazeed kagzaat bhi maang sakta hai — apna mobile hamesha on rakhein.
- **Muntakhib hone ki ittila:** Verification mukammal hone par muntakhib dulhanon ko **SMS** se ittila di jati hai aur unhe aane wali taqreeb ki tareekh aur jagah batayi jati hai.
- **Taqreeb aur salami:** Salami ka ATM card **taqreeb ke din** dulhan ko diya jata hai. Portal ke dashboard ke mutabiq taqreebat ka schedule **12 November 2026 se 22 March 2027** tak hai — apne zila ki taqreeb ki tareekh portal ya helpline par check karein.

**Status check karne ke tareeqe:**
- **Portal:** [cmp.punjab.gov.pk](https://cmp.punjab.gov.pk) par apne account me login kar ke apni darkhwast ka status dekhein.
- **Helpline:** **1312** par call kar ke apna reference number ya CNIC bata kar status maloom karein.
- **SMS:** Registration ke baad aane wale tamam SMS mehfooz rakhein — har marhale ki ittila SMS se milti hai.

Yaad rakhein: **aakhri tareekh (last date)** portal par chhapi nahi hoti — registrations taqreebat ke schedule ke mutabiq chalti rehti hain. Is liye der na karein: form bharne ke baad verification me waqt lagta hai, aur jo darkhwastein pehle jama hoti hain unhe pehle ki taqreeb me shamil kiya jata hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Reject Karwa Deti Hain

Ye ghaltiyan har saal hazaron darkhwastein reject karwati hain — in se bachein:

**1. Fake ya unofficial website par form bharna:** Sirf **cmp.punjab.gov.pk** official portal hai. Social media par chalne wale "Dhee Rani registration" links par apna CNIC dena aap ki maloomat chori karwa sakta hai.

**2. Agent ko paise dena:** Registration **muft** hai. Jo shakhs card ya salami dilane ke badle paise maange, wo dhoka de raha hai. Sirf official portal, helpline ya zila daftar se apply karein.

**3. Domicile ya halafnama ke baghair apply karna:** Ye do kagzaat darkhwast ki bunyad hain. In ke baghair form ya to submit hi nahi hota, ya verification me reject ho jata hai.

**4. Ghalat CNIC ya mobile number:** Form me CNIC number likhte waqt aik aik digit check kar lein aur wo mobile number dein jo **aap ke apne naam** par registered ho. Ghalat number par OTP aur status SMS kabhi nahi pahunche ga.

**5. Niji shadi ki shart par raazi na hona:** Jo khandan ijtimai taqreeb me shamil hone ke bajaye sirf raqam lena chahta hai, us ki darkhwast is programme ke liye nahi hai. Ye shart apply karne se pehle samajh lein.

**6. Dhundli ya ghalat kagzaat upload karna:** Upload ki hui tasveer saaf aur mukammal honi chahiye. Aadhi, kati hui ya dhundli copy verification me reject ho jati hai — dobara upload karne ka mauqa shayad na mile.

**7. Umar ya tabqe ki ghalat maloomat:** Umar CNIC se match karni chahiye aur mustahiq tabqe ka dawa saboot ke baghair na karein. Ghalat dawa pakre jane par darkhwast hamesha ke liye reject ho sakti hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab Dhee Rani Programme ke tehet dulhan ko kitni raqam milti hai?**
Muntakhib dulhan ko **Rs 2,00,000 salami** milti hai, jo Bank of Punjab ke ATM card par taqreeb ke din di jati hai. Is ke sath ijtimai shadi ki taqreeb ka tamam kharcha bhi hukumat uthati hai. Portal ki FAQ me Rs 1,21,000 ke tohfe ka bhi zikr hai, lekin taaza elan me sirf Rs 2,00,000 salami ki tasdeeq hai — apne card ki kul raqam helpline 1312 par confirm kar lein.

**Dhee Rani Programme ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?**
Punjab ki rehaishi dulhan jis ki umar 18 se 40 saal ho, jis ke paas Punjab ka domicile aur valid CNIC ho, jo ghair-shadi-shuda ho aur jis ka rishta tay ho, wo apply kar sakti hai. Tarjeeh yateem, nadhar aur mazoor dulhanon ya mazoor walidain ki betiyon ko di jati hai. Darkhwast dulhan khud, ya us ke walid, walida ya sarparast de sakta hai.

**Dhee Rani Programme ke liye online apply kaise karein?**
Official portal [cmp.punjab.gov.pk](https://cmp.punjab.gov.pk) kholein, Register par click kar ke apna naam, CNIC, zila aur mobile number darj karein, phir login kar ke application form me dulhan aur dulhe ki tafseel bharein aur CNIC, domicile aur halafnama JPG ya PNG me upload kar ke form submit karein. Reference number mehfooz rakh lein. Madad ke liye helpline **1312** par call karein.

**Kya Dhee Rani ki salami niji shadi par bhi milti hai?**
Nahi. Rs 2,00,000 ki salami **sirf un dulhanon ko milti hai jo hukumat ki ijtimai shadi ki taqreeb me shadi karti hain**. Jo khandan apni niji taqreeb karwana chahta hai, wo is raqam ka haqdar nahi hoga.

**Dhee Rani Programme ki darkhwast ka status kaise check karein?**
Apne account se [cmp.punjab.gov.pk](https://cmp.punjab.gov.pk) par login kar ke status dekhein, ya helpline **1312** par apna reference number ya CNIC bata kar maloom karein. Verification ke baad muntakhib dulhanon ko SMS se taqreeb ki tareekh aur jagah ki ittila di jati hai. Taqreebat ka schedule 12 November 2026 se 22 March 2027 tak hai.

*Maloomat ka zariya: official portal cmp.punjab.gov.pk (programme ki tafseel aur helpline 1312), Social Welfare and Bait-ul-Maal Department Punjab ke dobara shuru hone wale ijtimai shadiyon ke elan ki khabrein (12 November 2026) — check date 18 November 2026. Raqam, hadood aur schedule har marhale me badal sakte hain — apply karne se pehle taaza tafseel portal ya helpline 1312 par zaroor confirm kar lein.*
