## Pehli Baat: CNIC Aap Ki Pehchan Hai, Isay Halka Mat Lein

Pakistan me CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) sirf aik card nahi — ye aap ki qanooni pehchan hai. Bank account khulwana ho, SIM lena ho, passport banana ho, vote dalna ho ya sarkari nokri ke liye apply karna ho — har jagah CNIC manga jata hai. 18 saal ki umar poori hote hi CNIC banwana qanooni zimmedari hai, aur is me deri karne se baad me kaam atke rehte hain.

Achhi khabar ye hai ke NADRA ne process kaafi asaan kar diya hai. Pehli dafa ka naya CNIC **normal processing par bilkul muft** hai, aur renewal ya duplicate ke liye bhi fees munasib hain. Online apply ka option bhi hai — Pak ID app ya id.nadra.gov.pk se ghar bethe kaam ho sakta hai. Is guide me hum teenon cheezen cover karenge: **naya CNIC, renewal, aur gumshuda/kharab card ka duplicate** — fees, kagzaat aur step-by-step tareeqa, taake aap ko aik dafa me kaam ho jaye.

> **Note:** Is guide me di gayi tamam fees NADRA ke published fee schedule se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. NADRA waqtan-fo-waqtan fees tabdeel karta rehta hai, is liye payment se pehle hamesha nadra.gov.pk ya Pak ID app par maujooda fee zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Kaunsa CNIC Lena Hai: Normal Ya Smart?

NADRA do qisam ke card jari karta hai — faisla apply karne se pehle kar lein:

1. **Normal CNIC:** Aam card hai, rozmarra ke tamam kamon ke liye kaafi hai. Pehli dafa muft milta hai aur renewal sasta hai.
2. **Smart CNIC (chip wala):** Is me electronic chip lagi hoti hai jis me biometric data mehfooz hota hai. Zyada mehfooz hai aur mustaqbil ki digital services ke liye behtar hai, lekin fee zyada hai.

**Ahem baat:** Aik dafa Smart CNIC le liya to wapas normal CNIC par nahi ja sakte — tamam mustaqbil ki renewals Smart CNIC ki fees par hongi. Agar aap ka kaam normal card se chal jata hai to paise bachane ke liye normal CNIC theek hai.

## 2026 Ki Official Fees: Mukammal Table

Fees teen processing speeds par munhasir hain — **Normal** (sasta, zyada waqt), **Urgent** (darmiyani), **Executive** (tez tareen, mehenga):

### Naya CNIC (pehli dafa, 18 saal par)

| Service | Normal (15 din) | Urgent (12 din) | Executive (6 din) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Naya CNIC | **Muft (Rs 0)** | Rs 1,150 | Rs 2,150 |
| Naya Smart CNIC | Rs 750 | Rs 1,500 | Rs 2,500 |

### Renewal, Duplicate (gumshuda/kharab) Aur Modification

| Service | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CNIC Renewal | Rs 400 | Rs 1,150 | Rs 2,150 |
| Duplicate CNIC (gumshuda) | Rs 400 | Rs 1,150 | Rs 2,150 |
| Modification (naam/pata tabdeeli) | Rs 400 | Rs 1,150 | Rs 2,150 |
| Smart CNIC (koi bhi service) | Rs 750 | Rs 1,500 | Rs 2,500 |

**Yaad rakhein:** Sirf pehli dafa ka naya CNIC normal processing par muft hai. Renewal hamesha fee ke saath hota hai — chahe pehli renewal hi kyun na ho. CNIC ki validity **10 saal** hoti hai, is ke baad renewal karwana parta hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Category Ke Mutabiq

Kagzaat na-mukammal hona sab se aam wajah hai jis se logon ka din zaya hota hai. Apni category ke kagzaat pehle se tayyar kar lein:

**Naya CNIC (pehli dafa):**

- B-Form (Child Registration Certificate) — agar bana hua hai
- Walid ya walida ka CNIC (tasdeeq ke liye)
- Matric ya school certificate (umar ke saboot ke liye, agar manga jaye)
- Union Council ka birth certificate (kuch centers mangte hain)

**CNIC Renewal (expiry se pehle ya baad):**

- Purana CNIC (chahe expire ho chuka ho)
- Agar pata ya koi maloomat tabdeel karni hai to mutaliqa saboot (bijli ka bill, nikah-nama waghera)

**Duplicate CNIC (gumshuda ya kharab card):**

- FIR ki copy (gumshuda card ki soorat me qareebi thane se katwa lein — ye zaroori hai)
- Kharab card (agar toot-phoot gaya hai to wohi le jayen)
- Kisi aik rishtedar ka CNIC tasdeeq ke liye

**Modification (naam, walid ka naam, pata ya marital status ki tabdeeli):**

- Maujooda CNIC
- Tabdeeli ka saboot: nikah-nama (shadi ki soorat me), court order (naam ki tabdeeli par), bijli/gas ka bill (pate ki tabdeeli par)

## Tareeqa 1: NADRA Center Ja Kar Apply Karna (Step-by-Step)

1. **Qareebi NADRA Registration Center (NRC) jayen.** Bara shehar hai to Mega Center ya Executive Center jayen — wahan bheer kam aur kaam tez hota hai.
2. **Token lein.** Counter par batayen ke naya, renewal ya duplicate chahiye — usi ke mutabiq token milega.
3. **Data entry.** Operator aap ki maloomat darj karega. **Screen par apna naam, walid ka naam, tareekh-e-pedaish aur pata ghor se parhein** — yehi maloomat card par chapegi, ghalti baad me theek karwana mushkil aur fee wala kaam hai.
4. **Biometric aur tasveer.** Fingerprints liye jayenge aur digital tasveer banegi. Tasveer ke waqt seedha dekhein — yehi tasveer 10 saal card par rahegi.
5. **Fee ada karein.** Counter par ya bank se challan ke zariye fee jama karwayen aur raseed sambhal kar rakhein.
6. **Tracking ID lein.** Aap ko aik tracking ID milegi — is se aap card ka status maloom kar sakte hain.

## Tareeqa 2: Ghar Bethe Online Apply (Pak ID App)

NADRA ka **Pak ID app** (Android aur iPhone dono par) se renewal, duplicate aur modification ghar bethe ho sakta hai:

1. Pak ID app download karke CNIC number, mobile number aur email se account banayen — OTP se verify karein.
2. Service chunein: **CNIC renewal, duplicate ya modification**.
3. Form bharein — tamam maloomat CNIC ke mutabiq darj karein.
4. App ke camera se **fingerprints** scan karein aur fresh tasveer upload karein (hidayat ke mutabiq).
5. Fee **debit/credit card, JazzCash, Easypaisa ya Raast QR** se ada karein.
6. Submit karke **tracking ID** mehfooz kar lein — app me hi status dekh sakte hain.

**Pehli dafa ka naya CNIC** online nahi ban sakta — us ke liye NADRA center jana zaroori hai kyunke pehli biometric enrollment wahan hoti hai.

## Card Ka Status Kaise Check Karein

- **SMS:** Apni tracking ID **8400** par bhej dein — status ka jawab aa jayega.
- **Pak ID app:** "Track Your Application" me tracking ID dalen.
- **NADRA helpline:** 1777 (NADRA ka official helpline number) par call karke bhi maloom kar sakte hain.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Ghalat spellings:** Naam ya walid ke naam ki spelling CNIC par ghalat chap jaye to har jagah masla hota hai — data entry ke waqt screen ghor se parhein.
2. **FIR ke baghair jana:** Gumshuda card ka duplicate banwane FIR lazmi hai — pehle thane jayen, phir NADRA.
3. **Expiry ka intezar:** Card expire hone se **6 mahine pehle** renewal ke liye apply kar sakte hain — aakhri waqt ka intezar na karein.
4. **Agenton ko paise dena:** NADRA centers ke bahir agent paise mangte hain — in ki koi zaroorat nahi, process seedha hai.
5. **Purana pata:** Agar aap shehar badal chuke hain to renewal ke waqt pata update karwa lein, warna card purane pate par bhej diya jayega.

## Apne CNIC Ki Hifazat Kaise Karein (Bohat Ahem)

CNIC ki copy har jagah mangi jati hai — SIM lene par, bank me, nokri ke form par. Isi liye CNIC ka ghalat istemal bhi aam hai. Chand ehtiyaten:

1. **Har copy par maqsad likhein:** Jahan CNIC ki copy dein, us par pen se likh dein ke "sirf ___ ke liye" — masalan "sirf bank account ke liye". Is se copy kahin aur istemal nahi ho sakti.
2. **NADRA ki biometric tasdeeq khud karein:** Kabhi kisi agent ya dukandar ko apne fingerprints "verification" ke naam par na dein — SIM aap ke naam par nikal sakti hai.
3. **Apne naam par kitni SIMs hain, check karte rahein:** PTA ki website ya 668 par SMS karke maloom karein ke aap ke CNIC par kitni SIMs registered hain. Anjaan SIM foran band karwayen.
4. **CNIC ki tasveer social media par na lagayen:** Card ki photo WhatsApp status ya Facebook par lagana seedha fraud ko dawat dena hai.
5. **Gumshuda card ki FIR foran:** Card ghumte hi FIR katwayen — ye aap ka qanooni tahaffuz hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal (Jari)

**Kya pehla CNIC waqai muft hai?**
Ji haan — 18 saal par pehli dafa normal processing par koi fee nahi. Urgent ya executive chahiye to fee deni hogi.

**CNIC kitne din me mil jata hai?**
Normal: 15 din, Urgent: 12 din, Executive: 6 din. Smart CNIC me zyada waqt lagta hai (normal 31 din).

**Gumshuda CNIC ka kya karun, kya koi ghalat istemal kar sakta hai?**
Foran qareebi thane me FIR katwayen aur NADRA se duplicate ke liye apply karein. FIR aap ko tahaffuz deti hai agar card ka ghalat istemal ho.

**Kya CNIC par pata tabdeel karwana zaroori hai?**
Zaroori to nahi lekin behtar hai — bank, passport aur degar kamon me maujooda pate ka saboot manga jata hai.

**Smart CNIC normal se behtar hai?**
Chip ki wajah se zyada mehfooz hai aur digital tasdeeq me kaam aata hai. Lekin fee dugni se zyada hai — faisla apni zaroorat par karein.

**Kya 18 saal se pehle CNIC ban sakta hai?**
Nahi — 18 saal se kam umar ke liye B-Form (CRC) banta hai, CNIC nahi.
