## Seedha Jawab: LAT Kya Hai Aur Kyun Lazmi Hai

**LAT (Law Admission Test)** Pakistan me **LLB (5-year programme)** me admission ke liye **lazmi entry test** hai. Ye test **HEC (Higher Education Commission)** ka idarah **ETC (Education Testing Council)** leta hai aur registration sirf **etc.hec.gov.pk** par hoti hai. Supreme Court of Pakistan ki 2018 ki hidayat ke baad ye usool tay hai: **LAT pass kiye baghair koi bhi HEC/PBC-recognized university ya law college aap ko LLB me admission nahi de sakta** — chahay sarkari ho ya private. Test kul **100 marks** ka hai, **passing marks 50** hain, aur result **2 saal** tak valid rehta hai.

Is guide me aap ko milega: eligibility, step-by-step online registration, fee, official paper pattern, roll number slip aur result ka tareeqa, aur woh tayyari jo waqai marks barhati hai. Aur admission se pehle apni pasandeeda university ki HEC recognition check karna na bhoolein — tareeqa hamari [HEC Recognized Universities Check guide](/guides/hec-recognized-universities-check) me hai.

> **Note:** LAT saal me **kai martaba** hota hai (ETC ka test calendar HEC ki website par publish hota hai) aur har cycle ki registration deadline aur test date alag hoti hai. Is guide me koi fixed date nahi di gayi — **current cycle ki dates hamesha etc.hec.gov.pk aur HEC ki announcement se confirm karein.** Fee bhi portal par consumer number generate hote waqt display hoti hai — wahi amount final hai.

## Eligibility — Kaun LAT De Sakta Hai

- **HSSC / Intermediate (FA, FSc, I.Com waghera) ya equivalent** 12 saal ki taleem pass kar chuke hon, **ya**
- Jinka **HSSC ka result aanay wala hai (result awaiting)** — woh bhi current rules ke mutabiq apply kar sakte hain (admission ke waqt result shart ke mutabiq pura karna hoga)
- **Age limit:** HEC ki taraf se LAT ke liye koi upper age limit nahi hai
- Apna **khud ka CNIC ya B-Form** lazmi hai — waledain ya kisi rishtedaar ke CNIC par registration nahi ho sakti; test centre par bhi yehi document check hota hai
- Naam aur tafseel **Matric certificate ke harf-ba-harf mutabiq** hon — mismatch par centre me daakhla ruk sakta hai

**Ahem:** LAT sirf **5-year LLB** programme ke liye hai. Jin universities/colleges me admission lena hai woh **HEC-recognized** aur **Pakistan Bar Council (PBC)** se manzoor-shuda honay chahiyein — ghair-recognized college ka LLB kahin qabool nahi hota.

## Fee

| Item | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| LAT test fee | **Rs 1,800** (haaliya ETC cycles ke mutabiq — portal par generated amount hi final hai) |
| Payment tareeqa | **1Link / 1Bill** invoice — bank branch, ATM, internet/mobile banking, Easypaisa, JazzCash, UPaisa |
| Refund | Fee **non-refundable aur non-transferable** hai |

Fee adaa karne ke baad ETC dashboard par **"Verify Online Payment"** par click karna **lazmi** hai — payment verify hue baghair application mukammal nahi samjhi jati. Consumer number apne application ke against generate hota hai; kisi aur ke number par paid fee aap ke kaam nahi ayegi.

## Step-by-Step Online Registration (etc.hec.gov.pk)

### Step 1: ETC Portal Par Profile Banayen

1. **etc.hec.gov.pk** kholein aur **Sign Up** karke apna account banayen — apna **khud ka CNIC/B-Form number**, active email aur mobile number use karein (roll number slip aur result ki ittila inhi par ayegi).
2. **"My Profile"** me tamam maloomat bharein: naam, walid ka naam, date of birth, Matric aur Intermediate ki tafseel — sab kuch documents ke mutabiq.

### Step 2: LAT Application Bharein

1. Menu se **"Law Admission Test (LAT)"** chunein.
2. Form mukammal karein aur apna **test centre** soch samajh kar select karein — **submit ke baad centre change nahi hota.**
3. Photo aur zaroori documents ki saaf images upload karein jo form mange.

### Step 3: Fee Pay Karein Aur Verify Karein

1. Application se **1Link/1Bill consumer number** generate karein.
2. Kisi bhi bank app, ATM, bank branch ya mobile wallet (Easypaisa/JazzCash/UPaisa) se fee ada karein.
3. Portal par wapas aakar **"Verify Online Payment"** click karein.

### Step 4: Application SUBMIT Karein

1. Ye sab se ahem step hai: sirf **SUBMITTED** applications hi process hoti hain. **SAVE ya INCOMPLETE** reh jane wali applications par koi karwai nahi hoti.
2. Submit ke baad confirmation sambhal kar rakhein.
3. Koi masla ya query ho to **registration ki closing date se pehle** hi onlinehelp.hec.gov.pk par resolve karwayen — deadline ke baad complaints logistics/security wajuhaat ki bina par qabool nahi hotin.

## Official Paper Pattern (100 Marks)

HEC/ETC ke published content weightages ke mutabiq LAT do hison me hota hai:

### Part I — MCQs (75 Marks, 75 Sawalat)

| Subject | Marks |
| --- | --- |
| English (synonyms, antonyms, prepositions) | 20 |
| General Knowledge | 20 |
| Islamic Studies | 10 |
| Pakistan Studies | 10 |
| Urdu (vocabulary) | 10 |
| Basic Mathematics | 5 |
| **Kul MCQs** | **75** |

### Part II — Subjective (25 Marks)

| Section | Marks | Tafseel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essay (English ya Urdu) | 15 | Maximum 200 words |
| Personal Statement (English ya Urdu) | 10 | Maximum 200 words — aap law kyun parhna chahte hain |

**Passing marks: 100 me se 50.** Essay aur personal statement English ya Urdu, kisi aik zuban me likh sakte hain. Negative marking ETC ki current policy ke mutabiq nahi hai — phir bhi test-day instructions ghor se parhein, kyunkay policy announcement ke zariye badal sakti hai.

## Roll Number Slip, Test Day Aur Result

- **Roll number slip:** Test se taqreeban **aik hafta pehle** ETC portal se download hoti hai — apne account me login karke print nikalein. Email/SMS par bhi ittila aati hai.
- **Test day par lazmi:** Printed roll number slip + **asal CNIC** (18 saal se kam ke liye asal B-Form/Juvenile Card ke sath photo wala Matric certificate). **Mobile phone, calculator aur har electronic device mamnoo** hai — question book ki photo lena ETC rules ke tehat punishable hai.
- **Answer keys:** Test ke baad ETC/HEC answer keys publish karta hai — apne paper ke booklet colour ke mutabiq key se marks ka andaza laga sakte hain.
- **Result:** ETC portal par announce hota hai; apne account se check karein. **50 ya zyada marks = pass**, aur score **2 saal** valid rehta hai.
- **Dobara koshish:** Score improve karne ke liye agle cycle me dobara test de sakte hain — universities aam tor par valid behtreen score ko dekhti hain.

## Tayyari Ke Tips — Jo Waqai Kaam Karte Hain

1. **English (20 marks) sab se bara MCQ hissa hai:** Synonyms/antonyms ki vocabulary rozana thori thori barhayen aur prepositions ki practice karein — ye pure 20 marks practice se pakke hote hain.
2. **GK aur Pakistan Studies:** Intermediate ki Pakistan Studies ki kitab dobara parhna sab se tez tayyari hai; sath current affairs ke liye rozana akhbar ki headlines lazmi dekhein.
3. **Essay ki practice likh kar karein:** 200 words ke andar mukammal essay (muqaddima, dalail, ikhtitam) time ke andar likhne ki mashq karein — law se mutaliq aam topics (adalat, insaf, qanoon ki hukmarani) par pehle se soch rakhein.
4. **Personal statement apna likhein:** Ratta hua statement nambar nahi deta — wazeh likhein ke law kyun, aur aap ki zindagi ka kaun sa tajurba/mashahida aap ko is taraf laya.
5. **Past papers aur mock tests:** Guzishta LAT papers ke pattern se practice karein; Part I ke 75 sawalat waqt ke andar hal karne ki speed banayen.
6. **Math se darna nahi:** Sirf 5 marks aur woh bhi basic (percentage, average, ratio jaisay topics) — aik hafta ki bunyadi practice kaafi hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan

1. **Application SAVE par chhor dena:** Registration mukammal nahi hota jab tak application **SUBMIT** na ho — sab se aam ghalti yahi hai.
2. **Fee pay karke verify na karna:** 1Bill se pays de kar "Verify Online Payment" na dabayen to application atki rehti hai.
3. **Doosre ke CNIC par apply karna:** Registration apne CNIC/B-Form par hi hogi — ghalti ki surat me centre se wapas bhej diya jata hai.
4. **Centre ghalat chunna:** Submit ke baad centre change nahi hota — apne sheher ka centre dhyaan se chunein. (Aik note: agar kisi centre par bohat kam candidates hon to ETC aap ko qareebi centre muntaqil kar sakta hai — portal par ittila check karte rahein.)
5. **Deadline ka intezar karna:** Closing date ke aakhri ghanton me portal par rush hota hai — kam az kam 2-3 din pehle registration mukammal kar lein.
6. **LAT ke baghair college me fee bhar dena:** Jo college "LAT baad me de dena" kahe, pehle us ki [HEC recognition check](/guides/hec-recognized-universities-check) karein — LAT pass kiye baghair diya gaya admission ghair-qanooni hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**LAT ki fee kitni hai aur kaise pay hoti hai?**
Haaliya ETC cycles me LAT fee **Rs 1,800** hai, jo application ke consumer number se **1Link/1Bill** ke zariye bank, ATM, mobile banking ya wallet (Easypaisa, JazzCash, UPaisa) se pay hoti hai. Fee non-refundable aur non-transferable hai. Portal par generate hone wala amount hi final hai — pay karne ke baad dashboard par "Verify Online Payment" lazmi click karein.

**LAT me passing marks kitne hain aur result kitne arse valid hai?**
Kul 100 marks me se **50 marks** lena lazmi hai. LAT ka score **2 saal** tak valid rehta hai — is arse me aap usi score par LLB admission le sakte hain.

**Kya result awaiting students LAT de sakte hain?**
Ji. Jin students ka HSSC/Intermediate ka result aanay wala hai (result awaiting), woh current rules ke mutabiq LAT ke liye apply kar sakte hain. LLB admission ke waqt university ki result wali shart pura karna hogi.

**LAT saal me kitni dafa hota hai aur next test kab hai?**
ETC saal me kai LAT cycles chalata hai. Har cycle ki registration deadline aur test date ETC apni announcement aur test calendar me deta hai — current dates ke liye **etc.hec.gov.pk** aur HEC ki website check karein; purani dates par bharosa na karein.

**Roll number slip kab aur kahan se milegi?**
Test se taqreeban aik hafta pehle ETC portal (etc.hec.gov.pk) par apne account se download hogi. Test day par printed slip aur asal CNIC/B-Form lazmi le kar jayen.

**LAT fail ho jaye to kya hoga?**
Agle cycle me dobara apply kar sakte hain — koi pabandi nahi. Tayyari is dafa essay aur English par zyada rakhein, kyunkay yehi do hisse sab se zyada marks dete hain. Jab tak LAT pass nahi, koi recognized university LLB admission nahi de sakti.

**Kya LAT private law colleges ke liye bhi lazmi hai?**
Ji, bilkul. LAT sarkari aur private — **har HEC/PBC-recognized** university aur affiliated law college ke 5-year LLB ke liye lazmi hai. Jo college LAT ke baghair admission de, us ki recognition pehle [check karein](/guides/hec-recognized-universities-check).

**Degree ke baad HEC se mutaliqa kaam kahan se hoga?**
LLB mukammal karne ke baad degree ki attestation online DAS se hoti hai — tareeqa [HEC Degree Attestation guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) me hai, aur bahir mulk jaane ki surat me [HEC Sealed Envelope guide](/guides/hec-sealed-envelope-wes) kaam ayegi.
