## E-Challan Kya Hai Aur Ye Kaise Banta Hai

E-challan wo traffic jurmana hai jo Safe City ke cameras ya traffic police ke digital nizam ke zariye automatic tor par banta hai. Jab koi gaari ya motorcycle signal torri hai, speed limit se zyada tez chalti hai, ghalat side par jati hai ya kisi aur traffic qanoon ki khilaf warzi karti hai to camera us ki tasweer aur number plate record kar leta hai. Us record ki bunyad par gaari ke malik ke naam aik challan jari ho jata hai, aur aksar malik ko SMS ke zariye bhi ittila mil jati hai.

Purane zamane ke haath se likhe challan ke bar-aks e-challan me violation ki tasweer, tareekh, waqt aur jaga sab kuch digital record me mehfooz hota hai. Is ka faida ye hai ke ap ghar baithe apna challan dekh sakte hain, us ki tasweer bhi check kar sakte hain aur baghair kisi office jaye jurmana ada kar sakte hain. Is ka nuqsan sirf ye hai ke log aksar SMS ya challan ko nazar andaz kar dete hain aur phir baqaya jurmane barhte chale jate hain.

Ye guide Punjab ke e-challan ke liye hai, jo Punjab Safe Cities Authority (PSCA) ke portal par check hota hai. Agar apki gaari Punjab me registered hai ya Punjab ke shehron me chalti hai to yehi tareeqa ap ke kaam aayega. Gaari ki maloomat aur registration ki tasdeeq ke liye [gaari verification MTMIS](/guides/gaari-verification-mtmis) wali guide bhi dekh lein, aur salana token tax ke liye [gaari ka token tax online jama karwana](/guides/gaari-token-tax-online-jama-karwana) wali guide mojood hai.

## Check Karne Se Pehle Kya Chahiye Hoga

E-challan check karne ke liye apko kisi account ya login ki zaroorat nahi hoti. PSCA ke portal par search baghair account ke hoti hai. Bas ye do cheezein tayyar rakhein:

- **Gaari ki number plate:** Portal plate number teen formats me qabool karta hai — ABC 000, ABC 0000 aur ABC-00-0000. Plate wahi likhein jo gaari par lagi hui hai. Agar plate par sheher ka code ya koi khaas nishan hai to usay durust tareeqe se likhna zaroori hai, warna challan nahi milega.
- **CNIC ya chassis number:** Plate ke saath doosri zaroori cheez apka 13 huroof ka CNIC number hai, baghair dash ke, ya gaari ka chassis number. Sirf CNIC se search nahi hoti — plate lazmi hai, aur CNIC ya chassis doosri key ka kaam karta hai. Is liye har gaari ko alag alag check karna hota hai; aik CNIC se apki tamam gariyon ke challan aik saath nahi nikalte.

Aik ahem baat: challan check karna bilkul muft hai. PSCA ke official portal par status dekhne ki koi fee nahi. Agar koi website ya banda challan check karne ke paisay mange to samajh jayen ke wo official portal nahi hai. Hamesha sirf official portal echallan.psca.gop.pk istemal karein — gop.pk domain Punjab hukumat ka hai, is se milti julti kisi aur website par apni maloomat na dein.

## Step-by-Step Tareeqa: PSCA Portal Se E-Challan Check Karein

Ab asli tareeqa, aik aik step:

1. Apne mobile ya computer par browser kholein aur **echallan.psca.gop.pk** par jayen. Ye Punjab Safe Cities Authority ka official e-challan portal hai.
2. Pehle khane me apni gaari ka **plate number** likhein, us format me jo portal batata hai.
3. Doosre khane me apna **13 huroof ka CNIC number** baghair dash ke likhein, ya agar CNIC na ho to **chassis number** likhein.
4. Captcha par tick lagayen aur **Search** ke button par click karein.
5. Agar apki gaari par koi baqaya challan hai to wo screen par aa jayega. Har challan me ye maloomat hoti hain: violation ki qisam, wo tareekh aur waqt jab camera ne pakra, jaga, camera ki tasweer aur jurmane ki rakam.
6. Har challan ke saath aik **PSID** bhi hota hai — yehi number payment ke liye kaam aayega, isay note kar lein ya challan ki copy download kar lein.
7. Agar koi challan nahi aata to pehle plate number aur CNIC dobara check karein. Aksar ghalat format ya aik huroof ki ghalti ki wajah se challan nahi milta. Agar sab durust hai aur phir bhi kuch nahi aata to is ka matlab hai ke is waqt koi baqaya challan mojood nahi hai.

PSCA ki mobile app bhi mojood hai — PSCA Public Safety app — jis me e-challan check karne ki sahulat bhi di gayi hai. Lekin website se check karna sab se seedha tareeqa hai kyunke is ke liye app install karne ya account banane ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

## PSID Kya Hai Aur Is Se Payment Kaise Karein

PSID ka matlab hai Payment Slip ID. Har e-challan ka apna aik unique PSID hota hai, aur yehi wo number hai jo payment ke har tareeqe me manga jata hai. Challan check karne ke baad PSID note kar lein — is ke baghair payment nahi ho sakti. Payment ke liye ye tareeqe mojood hain:

- **ePay Punjab app ya portal:** Punjab hukumat ka official payment platform. Is me Traffic Challan ka option chun kar PSID dalein, challan ki tafseel confirm karein aur debit card, credit card ya mobile banking se payment kar dein. Payment ke foran baad receipt download kar lein.
- **JazzCash app:** App me Bill Payments ya Government Payments me ja kar Punjab E-Challan ya PSCA ka option chunein, PSID dalein, rakam confirm karein aur PIN se payment mukammal karein. JazzCash retailer ki dukaan se bhi PSID de kar payment ho sakti hai.
- **Easypaisa app:** Bill Payments me Traffic Challan ka option chun kar PSCA select karein, PSID dalein aur payment kar dein. Payment ke baad SMS confirmation ka intezar karein.
- **Mobile ya internet banking:** Aksar banks ki apps me bhi government payments ya PSID payment ka option hota hai. Wahan bhi PSID hi kaam aata hai.
- **Bank branch ya ATM:** Jo log app istemal nahi karte wo PSID le kar bank ki branch ya ATM se bhi payment kar sakte hain.

Payment karne ke baad receipt zaroor sambhal kar rakhein — screenshot le lein ya PDF download kar lein. Agar baad me koi ghalat fehmi ho to yehi receipt apka saboot hogi. Payment ke baad challan ka status update hone me kuch waqt lag sakta hai, is liye foran dobara check karne par wo paid nazar na aaye to ghabrayen nahi; kuch ghanton baad dobara check karein.

## Jurmane Ki Raqam: Maujooda Fine Schedule

Jurmane ki rakam ke bare me sab se bara masla ye hai ke internet par purani schedule abhi bhi ghoom rahi hai — jis me signal torrne ka jurmana 200 ya 500 rupay likha hota hai. Wo purani rakamain ab lagu nahi hain. Punjab Traffic Police ki official website par mojood maujooda schedule (Twelfth Schedule me tarmeem ke baad) ke mutabiq ahem violations ke jurmane ye hain:

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speed limit se zyada tez chalana | 2,000 | 3,000 | 5,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 |
| Signal ki khilaf warzi | 2,000 | 3,000 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 15,000 |
| Ghalat side par gaari chalana | 2,000 | 3,000 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 15,000 |
| Raat ko baghair munasib lights ke chalana | 2,000 | 3,000 | 3,000 | 8,000 | 10,000 |

| Baghair registration ke gaari chalana | 2,000 | 3,000 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 15,000 |

Ye rakamain official schedule se li gayi hain, lekin aik baat zehen me rakhein: apke challan par jo rakam PSCA portal par likhi hai wahi hatmi hai. Agar schedule aur portal ki rakam me farq lage to portal wali rakam ko durust samjhein aur mutaliqa traffic office se tasdeeq kar lein, kyunke schedule me waqt ke saath tabdeeli ho sakti hai. ## Agar Challan Ghalat Lage To Kya Karein

Kabhi kabhi aisa hota hai ke challan ghalat ban jata hai — tasweer me gaari kisi aur ki ho, plate number ghalat parha gaya ho, ya us waqt gaari koi aur chala raha ho. Aisi surat me foran payment karne ki bajaye pehle challan ki tasweer ghor se dekhein. PSCA portal par har challan ke saath camera ki tasweer hoti hai; us se ye wazeh ho jata hai ke violation waqai hui thi ya nahi.

Agar apko yaqeen hai ke challan ghalat hai to PSCA portal par Online Review ya correction ka option mojood hai, jis ke zariye ap apni shikayat aur saboot jama kara sakte hain. Shikayat ke saath wo tasweer ya kagaz lagayen jo apke dawe ki tasdeeq kare — jaise us waqt gaari kisi aur sheher me hone ka saboot. Agar gaari us waqt koi aur chala raha tha to malik ke tor par apko mutaliqa traffic office se rabta kar ke ye sabit karna hoga ke violation ke waqt ap drive nahi kar rahe the; sabit ho jane par zimmedari us shakhs par muntaqil ho sakti hai jis ne gaari chalayi thi.

Ghalat challan ki review me waqt lag sakta hai, is liye shikayat jama karane ke baad us ka reference number sambhal kar rakhein aur waqfe waqfe se status check karte rahein. Jab tak review ka faisla na aaye, challan ko baqaya hi samjhein aur usay nazar andaz na karein.

## Challan Na Bharne Par Kya Hota Hai

Baqaya challan ko nazar andaz karna mehnga par sakta hai. Pehli baat to ye ke baqaya challan apke gaari ke record me rehta hai, aur traffic police ki checking ke doran ye samne aa sakta hai. Doosri baat ye ke musalsal violations aur baqaya jurmane apke driving licence ke record par bhi asar daal sakte hain — sakht cases me licence ki suspension tak ki naubat aa sakti hai. Is ke ilawa gaari bechne ya transfer karane ke waqt bhi baqaya challan rukawat ban sakta hai, is liye transfer se pehle tamam challan clear kar lena behtar hai. Gaari transfer ka tareeqa [gaari transfer karne ka tareeqa](/guides/gaari-transfer-karne-ka-tareeqa) wali guide me tafseel se diya gaya hai.

Is liye behtar tareeqa ye hai ke har mahine aik dafa apni gaari ka challan check kar lein, chahe koi SMS aaya ho ya na aaya ho. SMS kabhi kabhi nahi aata ya purane number par chala jata hai, lekin portal par record hamesha mojood hota hai. Monthly check ki adat apko achanak bare jurmane se bacha sakti hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jin Se Bachein

- **Sirf SMS ka intezar karna:** SMS na aane ka matlab ye nahi ke koi challan nahi hai. Portal par khud check karein.
- **Ghair official website par maloomat dena:** PSCA ke logo wali har website official nahi hoti. Sirf echallan.psca.gop.pk istemal karein.
- **Ghalat plate format likhna:** Plate me aik huroof ya digit ki ghalti bhi challan chhupa sakti hai. Plate ko gaari par lagi plate se mila kar likhein.
- **PSID ghalat likhna:** Payment se pehle PSID ko challan se mila kar dobara parhein. Ghalat PSID par payment kisi aur ke challan me ja sakti hai.
- **Receipt na sambhalna:** Payment ke baad receipt ka screenshot ya PDF zaroor rakhein. Yehi apka saboot hai.
- **Purani fine schedule par yaqeen karna:** Internet par 200 ya 500 rupay wali purani rakamain abhi bhi mojood hain. Hatmi rakam sirf PSCA portal wali hai.
- **Agent ko paisay dena:** Challan check karna muft hai aur payment bhi ap khud kar sakte hain. Kisi agent ko extra paisay dene ki zaroorat nahi hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Punjab ka e-challan online kaise check karein?**

PSCA ke official portal echallan.psca.gop.pk par jayen, apni gaari ka plate number aur 13 huroof ka CNIC ya chassis number dalein, captcha mukammal karein aur Search par click karein. Baqaya challan, us ki tasweer, tareekh aur rakam screen par aa jayegi. Ye search baghair account ke aur bilkul muft hai.

**E-challan pay karne ke liye PSID kya hai aur kahan se milega?**

PSID har challan ka unique Payment Slip ID hai jo challan check karne par us ki tafseel ke saath nazar aata hai. Payment ke har tareeqe — ePay Punjab, JazzCash, Easypaisa, mobile banking ya bank branch — me yehi PSID manga jata hai. Is liye payment se pehle PSID zaroor note kar lein.

**Kya e-challan sirf CNIC se check ho sakta hai, baghair plate number ke?**

Nahi. Plate number lazmi hai; CNIC ya chassis number sirf doosri key ka kaam karta hai. Sirf CNIC se apki tamam gariyon ke challan aik saath nahi nikalte, har gaari ko us ki plate se alag check karna hota hai.

**Agar challan ghalat lage to kya karein?**

Pehle PSCA portal par challan ki tasweer ghor se dekhein. Agar challan waqai ghalat hai to portal par Online Review ya correction ke zariye shikayat aur saboot jama karayen aur us ka reference number sambhal kar rakhein. Review ke doran challan ko baqaya hi samjhein aur nazar andaz na karein.

**Signal torrne ka maujooda jurmana kitna hai?**

Punjab Traffic Police ki maujooda schedule ke mutabiq signal ki khilaf warzi par motorcycle ka 2,000, three-wheeler ka 3,000, 2000cc tak ki car ka 5,000, 2000cc se zyada ki car ka 10,000 aur public service ya transport vehicle ka 15,000 rupay jurmana hai. Hatmi rakam wahi hai jo PSCA portal par apke challan par likhi ho.

Schedule me tabdeeli ki surat me PSCA portal par darj rakam hi hatmi samjhi jayegi.*
