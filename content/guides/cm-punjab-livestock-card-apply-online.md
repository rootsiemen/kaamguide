## Pehli Baat: CM Punjab Livestock Card Kya Hai

**CM Punjab Livestock Card** Hukumat-e-Punjab ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet chhote aur darmiyane livestock farmers ko **bila sood qarz** milta hai taa ke wo apne janwaron ke liye wanda (concentrated feed), silage, mineral mixture aur dawaaiyan waqt par khareed sakein. Ye scheme **Punjab Livestock and Dairy Development Department** aur **Bank of Punjab** ke zariye chalayi jati hai, aur is ka maqsad ye hai ke janwar palne wala kisan charay aur dawa ki kami ki wajah se na to mehengay qarze lene par majboor ho aur na hi us ke janwaron ki sehat aur doodh ki paidawar mutasir ho.

Card ki khaas baat ye hai ke is par milne wala qarz **mukammal tor par bila sood** hai — aap jitni raqam istemaal karte hain, utni hi wapas karte hain, us par koi markup nahi lagta. Phase II ka iftitah **14 October 2026** ko Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz ne kiya aur is marhale ke liye **16.2 arab rupay** ka fund mukhtas kiya gaya hai. Ye card cash nahi deta — ye aik **digital wallet** ki tarah kaam karta hai jis se aap sirf manzoor-shuda **registered dealers** se janwaron ka chara aur dawaaiyan khareed sakte hain, taa ke raqam seedha janwaron par lage.

**Sab se ahem taaza khabar:** official portal [plc.punjab.gov.pk](https://plc.punjab.gov.pk) par aaj, **17 November 2026** ko check karne par likha hai ke **"Current Application Season is Closed"** — maujooda season ki darkhwastein band ho chuki hain. Is ka matlab ye nahi ke scheme khatam ho gayi — scheme phases me chalti hai aur agla season jald elan hoga. Is guide me aap ko poora tareeqa milega taa ke agla season khulte hi aap ki darkhwast pehle din tayyar ho: kitna qarz milta hai, kaun apply kar sakta hai, janwaron ki tagging kaise karwate hain aur 8070 par SMS se apply kaise karte hain.

## Livestock Card Se Kitna Qarz Milta Hai

Phase II ke elan ke mutabiq Livestock Card par qarz ki hadood ye hain. Raqam aap ke **eligible janwaron ki tadaad** par munhasir hai — jitne janwar, utna qarz:

| Cheez | Tafseel |
|---|---|
| **Fi janwar qarz** | **27,000 rupay** |
| **Kam se kam janwar** | **5 janwar** |
| **Zyada se zyada janwar** | **20 janwar** |
| **Kam se kam qarz** | **1,35,000 rupay** (5 janwar) |
| **Zyada se zyada qarz** | **5,40,000 rupay** (20 janwar) |
| **Markup / sood** | **0% — mukammal bila sood** |
| **Qarz ki muddat** | **4 mahine**, mazeed **1 mahine ki grace period** |
| **Phase II ka kul fund** | **16.2 arab rupay** |
| **Card jari karne wala bank** | **Bank of Punjab** |

Misaal ke taur par, agar aap ke paas **5 janwar** hain to aap ko **1,35,000 rupay** tak ka qarz mil sakta hai (5 × 27,000). Agar janwar **10** hain to had **2,70,000 rupay** ho jayegi, aur **20 janwar** par poori had **5,40,000 rupay** milegi. 20 se zyada janwar hon to bhi qarz 5,40,000 se zyada nahi hoga, kyun ke ye card ki zyada se zyada had hai.

Qarz ki raqam **cash nahi milti**. Card aik digital wallet hai — is se aap registered dealers se wanda, silage, mineral mixture, veterinary dawaaiyan aur vaccination khareed sakte hain. Is tarah raqam seedha janwaron ki khoraak aur sehat par lagti hai, kahin aur kharch nahi hoti. Qarz ki wapsi **4 mahine** me karni hoti hai, aur agar kisi wajah se aik mahina der ho jaye to **1 mahine ki grace period** bhi di jati hai. Exact wapsi schedule aap ke card aur allocation letter par likha hota hai — usi ko final samjhein.

Aik ahem baat: pehle marhalon me qarz ki had aur janwaron ki had mukhtalif thi. **Maujooda Phase II (2026) ki hadood wohi hain jo upar table me likhi hain.** Agar koi purani khabar ya video aap ko mukhtalif raqam bataye to us par yaqeen karne se pehle **Livestock Helpline 0800-09211** par taaza had confirm kar lein.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

Livestock Card ke liye buniyadi shart **janwaron ki malkiat** hai. Phase II ke elan ke mutabiq ye shartein poori karne wala har Punjab ka livestock farmer apply kar sakta hai:

**1. Punjab ka rehaishi hona:** Ye scheme sirf **Punjab** ke farmers ke liye hai. Aap ka mustaqil pata Punjab me hona chahiye aur aap ke janwar bhi Punjab me hon.

**2. Valid CNIC:** Darkhwast guzar ke paas NADRA ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) hona lazmi hai. Agar CNIC expired hai to pehle [CNIC renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) karwa lein, warna darkhwast aage nahi barhegi.

**3. 5 se 20 eligible janwar:** Aap ke paas kam se kam **5 aur zyada se zyada 20** eligible janwar hone chahiye — male calves (narr bichhre), fattening animals, bail, heifers, gaay ya bhains. Aik se chaar janwar wale is scheme ke liye eligible nahi hote.

**4. Janwaron ki tagging (9211 system):** Aap ke janwar Livestock Department ke **9211 animal tagging system** me registered aur tag lage hon. Tagging muft hoti hai aur aap ke qareebi government veterinary hospital par karwai jati hai. Baghair tag wale janwar qarz ke hisaab me shamil nahi hote — is liye apply karne se pehle tagging lazmi karwa lein.

**5. SPMS-9211 me registration:** Darkhwast guzar ka **SPMS-9211 system** (Sustainable Poverty Management Survey / livestock registration) me registered hona zaroori hai. Ye registration bhi veterinary hospital ya facilitation desk par hoti hai.

**6. Apne naam par SIM:** Jis mobile number se aap apply karte hain, wo SIM **aap ke apne CNIC** par registered honi chahiye. Kisi aur ke naam wali SIM se bheja gaya SMS ya form verify nahi hota.

**7. Koi bank default nahi:** Agar aap ka kisi bank ya idare me pehle se koi qarz default hai to darkhwast mushkil ho sakti hai. Bank aap ki credit history zaroor check karta hai.

**8. Khawateen bhi eligible hain:** Khawateen livestock owners bhi poori tarah eligible hain — scheme me un ke liye koi alag pabandi nahi hai.

**9. Pehle faida na liya ho:** Agar aap ne isi scheme ke pehle marhale me card le kar faida utha liya hai to dobara apply karne se pehle portal ya helpline par apni eligibility zaroor check kar lein — purane mustafideen par pabandi lag sakti hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Tayyari

Apply karne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein taa ke darkhwast aadhi na reh jaye — khaas tor par ab jab ke season band hai, ye tayyari ka behtareen waqt hai:

- **Valid CNIC** — number aur us ki copy. CNIC number baghair dash ke likhna hota hai (masalan 3520212345678).
- **Apne naam par registered mobile SIM** — SMS aur tamam updates isi number par aate hain.
- **Janwaron ki tagging certificate** — 9211 system me registered janwaron ka record; agar tagging nahi hui to pehle qareebi Civil Veterinary Hospital se muft tagging karwa lein.
- **SPMS-9211 registration** — agar aap is system me registered nahi hain to veterinary hospital par registration karwa lein.
- **Zameen ya rehaish ka saboot** — Punjab ka rehaishi hone ka saboot; agar zameen hai to us ki [fard-e-malkiat](/guides/fard-malkiat-property-transfer-ka-tareeqa) bhi kaam aa sakti hai.
- **Bank of Punjab account** — qarz Bank of Punjab ke zariye diya jata hai, is liye account hona ya apply ke doran khulwana zaroori ho sakta hai. Card bhi isi bank se jari hota hai.
- **Tasveer** — darkhwast form ke liye aik haaliya passport size tasveer bhi tayyar rakh lein.

Sab se bara kaam **tagging** hai: bohat se farmers ki darkhwastein sirf is liye ruk jati hain ke un ke janwaron par government tag nahi lage hote. Tagging muft hai, is liye season khulne ka intezar kiye baghair aaj hi apne qareebi veterinary hospital se janwaron ki tagging karwa lein.

## Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa: Step-by-Step

Jab agla season khulega to apply karne ke **chaar official tareeqe** honge. Kisi agent ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi — registration **muft** hai:

**Tareeqa 1 — SMS se apply (sab se asaan):**
1. Apne mobile se SMS likhein: **PLC** (space) **apna 13-digit CNIC number** — masalan `PLC 3520212345678`
2. Ye SMS **8070** par bhej dein.
3. Aap ko confirmation SMS milega ke aap ki registration mil gayi hai ya mazeed maloomat darkar hai.
4. Verification ke baad card ki manzori ka SMS Bank of Punjab se aayega.

**Tareeqa 2 — Online portal:**
1. Official portal **[plc.punjab.gov.pk](https://plc.punjab.gov.pk)** kholein.
2. Registration form me apna CNIC number, mobile number, zila aur janwaron ki tadaad darj karein.
3. Form submit karne ke baad aap ko aik reference number milega — use mehfooz rakh lein.
4. Verification ke baad aap ko SMS se card milne ki ittila di jayegi.

**Tareeqa 3 — Veterinary hospital par:**
1. Apne qareebi **Civil Veterinary Hospital** ya CM Facilitation Desk par jayein.
2. Wahan mojood desk par apna CNIC aur janwaron ka record dikhayein.
3. Amle ki madad se form bharwayein aur jama karwayein.
4. Jin farmers ke paas internet ya smartphone nahi, un ke liye ye tareeqa sab se behtar hai.

**Tareeqa 4 — Helpline se madad:**
1. **0800-09211** (toll free) par call karein.
2. Apni eligibility aur registration ke bare me poochein.
3. Helpline aap ko qareebi registration point ka pata bhi bata sakti hai.

![Livestock Card ke liye mobile par SMS se registration — PLC likh kar 8070 par bhejein](/images/cm-punjab-livestock-card-apply-online-3.jpg)

Darkhwast jama karne ke baad **verification** hoti hai: veterinary officer aap ke janwaron ki malkiat aur tagging ki tasdeeq karta hai, aur Bank of Punjab aap ki credit history check karta hai. Verification mukammal hone par card jari hota hai aur aap ko SMS se ittila milti hai.

## Card Milne Ke Baad: Raqam Kaise Istemaal Hoti Hai

Card milne ke baad aap ko ye baatein maloom honi chahiye taa ke raqam zaya na ho:

**Sirf registered dealers se khareedari:** Card ki raqam se aap sirf un dealers se khareed sakte hain jo Livestock Department ke registered hain. In dealers se aap wanda, silage, mineral mixture, veterinary dawaaiyan aur vaccination khareed sakte hain.

**Cash nahi nikal sakte:** Ye card ATM se cash nikalne ke liye nahi hai. Raqam sirf khareedari ke liye istemaal hoti hai — ye shart is liye hai ke qarz seedha janwaron par lage.

**Wapsi 4 mahine me:** Qarz ki wapsi **4 mahine** ki muddat me karni hoti hai, aur zaroorat parne par **1 mahine ki grace period** bhi milti hai. Wapsi ka exact schedule aap ke allocation letter aur card ke sath milne wali hidayat par likha hota hai.

**Card sambhal kar rakhein:** Card aap ke naam par jari hota hai — use kisi aur ko na dein aur us ka PIN kisi se share na karein. Card gum ho jaye to foran **Bank of Punjab** ya helpline **0800-09211** par ittila dein.

**Kisan Card se farq:** [Punjab Kisan Card](/guides/punjab-kisan-card-apply-online) fasal ke liye beej aur khaad ka qarz deta hai, jab ke Livestock Card janwaron ke charay aur dawa ke liye hai. Dono alag schemes hain — aik ka card doosri cheez par istemaal nahi hota.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai Aur Status Kaise Check Karein

Season khulne ke baad poora process aam tor par **kuch hafte** me mukammal hota hai:

- **Registration:** SMS ya portal par form bharna sirf chand minute ka kaam hai.
- **Verification:** Janwaron ki malkiat aur tagging ki tasdeeq me aam tor par **1 se 3 hafte** lagte hain, kyun ke veterinary officer ko kabhi kabhi mauqa par ja kar janwar dekhne parte hain.
- **Card jari hona:** Verification mukammal hone ke baad Bank of Punjab card jari karta hai aur aap ko SMS se ittila milti hai.

**Status check karne ke tareeqe:**
- **SMS:** 8070 par bheje gaye SMS ka jawab aur us ke baad aane wale tamam SMS mehfooz rakhein — har marhale ki ittila SMS se milti hai.
- **Portal:** [plc.punjab.gov.pk](https://plc.punjab.gov.pk) par ja kar apna CNIC darj kar ke status check karein (season khulne par).
- **Helpline:** **0800-09211** par call kar ke apna CNIC bata kar status maloom karein.
- **Veterinary hospital:** Jis hospital se registration karwai thi, wahan ja kar bhi status pooch sakte hain.

Yaad rakhein: **maujooda season band hai**, is liye aaj status check karne par portal sirf "season closed" dikhayega. Agle season ka elan portal aur helpline par hoga — apni tayyari (tagging, SPMS-9211, SIM) abhi se mukammal kar lein taa ke season khulte hi foran apply kar sakein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Reject Karwa Deti Hain

Ye ghaltiyan har saal hazaron darkhwastein reject karwati hain — in se bachein:

**1. Baghair tagging apply karna:** Sab se aam ghalti — janwaron par 9211 tag nahi lage hote aur darkhwast verification me hi reject ho jati hai. Apply se pehle tagging lazmi karwa lein.

**2. Doosre ke naam wali SIM:** Jis SIM se apply karte hain wo aap ke apne CNIC par honi chahiye. Biwi, bhai ya dost ke naam wali SIM se bheja gaya SMS verify nahi hota.

**3. Ghalat CNIC number:** SMS me CNIC number likhte waqt dash na lagayein aur aik aik digit check kar lein. Aik ghalat digit bhi darkhwast ko phansa deta hai.

**4. Janwaron ki ghalat tadaad:** Apne eligible janwaron ki sahi tadaad likhein. Kam ya zyada tadaad likhne par verification me farq nikal aata hai aur darkhwast ruk jati hai.

**5. Agent ko paise dena:** Registration **muft** hai. Jo shakhs card dilane ke badle paise maange, wo dhoka de raha hai. Sirf official portal, 8070 SMS ya veterinary hospital se apply karein.

**6. Unofficial websites par form bharna:** Sirf **plc.punjab.gov.pk** official portal hai. Social media par chalne wale "registration" links aur fake websites se bachein — un par apna CNIC kabhi na dein.

**7. Season band hone par bhi agent ke peeche bhagna:** Jab season band ho to koi agent aap ki darkhwast jama nahi karwa sakta. Agle season ka intezar karein aur tayyari mukammal rakhein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**CM Punjab Livestock Card ke liye kaun apply kar sakta hai?**
Punjab ka rehaishi jis ke paas valid CNIC ho, apne naam par registered SIM ho, aur 5 se 20 eligible janwar (male calves, fattening animals, gaay ya bhains) hon jin par 9211 system ke tag lage hon, wo apply kar sakta hai. Khawateen livestock owners bhi poori tarah eligible hain.

**Livestock Card par kitna qarz milta hai?**
Phase II ke elan ke mutabiq fi janwar 27,000 rupay milte hain — 5 janwar par 1,35,000 rupay aur 20 janwar par zyada se zyada 5,40,000 rupay. Qarz mukammal bila sood hai aur wapsi 4 mahine me karni hoti hai, mazeed 1 mahine ki grace period ke sath.

**Livestock Card ke liye apply kaise karein?**
Chaar tareeqe hain: SMS me `PLC` (space) apna CNIC likh kar 8070 par bhejein, official portal plc.punjab.gov.pk par online form bharein, qareebi Civil Veterinary Hospital par ja kar form jama karwayein, ya helpline 0800-09211 par rabta karein. Registration muft hai.

**Kya abhi Livestock Card ke liye apply ho sakta hai?**
Nahi — official portal plc.punjab.gov.pk par 17 November 2026 ko check karne par "Current Application Season is Closed" likha hai. Agla season jald elan hoga. Is doran janwaron ki tagging aur SPMS-9211 registration mukammal kar lein taa ke season khulte hi foran apply kar sakein.

**Livestock Card ki raqam cash nikal sakte hain?**
Nahi. Ye card aik digital wallet hai — is se sirf registered dealers se wanda, silage, mineral mixture, veterinary dawaaiyan aur vaccination khareed sakte hain. Cash nikalne ki sahulat is card par nahi hai, taa ke raqam seedha janwaron par lage.

*Maloomat ka zariya: official portal plc.punjab.gov.pk (season status aur helpline 0800-09211 ki tasdeeq — check date 17 November 2026) aur Punjab Livestock and Dairy Development Department ke Phase II ke elan ki khabrein (14 October 2026). Raqam aur hadood har phase me badal sakti hain — apply karne se pehle taaza tafseel portal ya helpline par zaroor confirm kar lein.*
