## Pehli Baat: Asaan Karobar Card Kya Hai

**Asaan Karobar Card** Punjab hukumat ki wo scheme hai jis ke tehet chhote karobari afrad ko **10 lakh rupay tak ka bila sood (interest-free) loan** aik digital SME card ke zariye milta hai. Ye Chief Minister Punjab ka program hai, jisay **Punjab Small Industries Corporation (PSIC)** chalati hai, portal ki technology **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB)** ki hai aur loan **Bank of Punjab (BOP)** deta hai. Markup borrower ke liye **0%** hai — jo markup bank ko milta hai, wo Punjab hukumat subsidy ke tor par khud ada karti hai.

Ye scheme un logon ke liye hai jo dukaan, workshop, chhota business ya service ka kaam chalate hain, ya naya karobar shuru karna chahte hain, lekin bank ke aam loan ki sood wali sharait aur lambi paper-work ki wajah se peeche reh jate hain. Is program ke tehet **1 lakh se zyada chhote karobaro** ko madad dene ka hadaf hai aur kul raqam taqreeban **48 arab rupay** rakhi gayi hai.

Sab se ahem baat pehle samajh lein: ye loan **naqd cash ki surat me ghar le jane ke liye nahi** milta. Raqam card ke zariye sirf karobari maqasid par kharch hoti hai — mobile app, POS aur doosre digital channels se — taa ke paisa asal karobar hi me lage. Jo shakhs isay zaati kharchay ke liye istemal karne ka soche, us ke liye ye scheme nahi hai, aur aisi harkat par card block ho sakta hai.

## Card Aur Finance: Scheme Ke Do Hisse

Asaan Karobar program ke do hisse hain, aur apply karne se pehle ye tay kar lein ke aap ko kaunsa chahiye:

| Hissa | Kis ke liye | Raqam |
|---|---|---|
| **Asaan Karobar Card** | Chhote dukandaar, micro aur small entrepreneurs (mojooda ya naya karobar) | **Rs 10 lakh tak**, digital card par revolving credit |
| **Asaan Karobar Finance** | Wo SMEs jin ko bari financing chahiye (expansion, machinery, bara setup) | Card se bari raqmein — apni category ki exact limit portal par confirm karein |

Is guide ka markaz **Asaan Karobar Card** hai, kyun ke aksar log isi ke liye apply karte hain. Dono ke liye apply aik hi portal **akc.punjab.gov.pk** se hota hai aur buniyadi ehliyaat taqreeban aik jaisi hai. Agar aap ka karobar pehle se chal raha hai aur aap ko sirf stock, khaam maal ya rozmarra ke karobari kharcho ke liye raqam chahiye, to Card wala hissa aap ke liye hai.

## Ehliyaat: Kaun Apply Kar Sakta Hai

PSIC ki official scheme page ke mutabiq Asaan Karobar Card ke liye ye shartein hain:

**1. Umar:** Darkhwast guzar ki umar **21 saal se zyada aur zyada se zyada 57 saal** honi chahiye.

**2. Rehaish:** Pakistani shehri ho aur valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) ke mutabiq **Punjab ka rehne wala** ho.

**3. Karobar ki jagah:** Aap ka mojooda ya naya (prospective) karobar **Punjab ke andar** hona chahiye.

**4. CNIC:** NADRA record ke mutabiq valid CNIC ho — expired CNIC par darkhwast aage nahi barhti, pehle [CNIC renewal](/guides/cnic-renewal-ka-tareeqa) karwa lein.

**5. Mobile number:** Mobile phone **aap ke apne naam par registered** ho. Kisi rishtadar ke naam wali SIM se apply karenge to verification wahi ruk jayegi.

In ke ilawa do ghair-tehreeri lekin lazmi jaanchein hoti hain: aap ki **credit history saaf** honi chahiye (kisi bank ka overdue loan ya default na ho — ye e-CIB record se check hota hai) aur aap ko **credit aur psychometric assessment** me satisfactory ana hota hai. Aik shakhs ya aik karobar ke naam par **sirf aik darkhwast** di ja sakti hai.

## Loan Ki Sharait Aur Fees

| Cheez | Detail |
|---|---|
| Loan ki hadd | **Rs 10 lakh tak** |
| Markup (sood) | **0%** — borrower ke liye mukammal bila sood |
| Kul muddat | **3 saal** |
| Grace period | **3 mahine** (is dauran qist nahi deni) |
| Adaigi | Grace period ke baad **33 mahana qistein** |
| Processing fee | **Rs 500** (na-qabil-e-wapsi) |
| Card ki saalana fee | **Rs 25,000 + FED** (approved limit me se adjust hoti hai) |
| Kharch ka tareeqa | Sirf karobari maqasid — mobile app, POS waghera digital channels |

**Aik hisaab se samjhein:** Agar aap ko poora **Rs 10,00,000** approve ho jaye to 3 mahine ke grace ke baad aap ki mahana qist taqreeban **Rs 30,303** bane gi (10,00,000 ÷ 33), aur kul wapsi bilkul wohi 10 lakh hogi — aik rupiya sood nahi. Agar **Rs 5,00,000** mile to qist taqreeban **Rs 15,152** mahana hogi. Ye saalana card fee (Rs 25,000 + FED) is hisaab se alag hai — apply karte waqt isay zehen me rakhein, ye chhupa hua kharcha nahi, PSIC ki page par wazeh likha hai.

## Zaroori Kagzaat

![Asaan Karobar Card ke kagzaat: CNIC, mobile phone aur karobari documents desk par](/images/asaan-karobar-card-apply-online-2.jpg)

Apply karne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar kar lein taa ke form beech me na ruke:

- **CNIC** (aage aur peeche ki saaf tasveer/scan) — valid aur apne naam par
- **Apne naam par registered mobile number** — OTP isi par ayega
- **Karobar ka saboot:** dukaan/business ka address; mojooda karobar ho to us ki tafseel, naya karobar ho to mukhtasar business plan ya feasibility (kya karobar hai, raqam kahan lagegi, andaazan aamdani kitni hogi)
- **Bank statement** (agar portal mange) — credit jaanch ke liye
- Naya karobar shuru karne walo ke liye feasibility seedhi aur sach par mabni rakhein — barha charha kar likhe hue andaze verification me nakaam ho jate hain

Aik zaroori wazahat: apply ke waqt **tax registration lazmi nahi** hai, lekin card milne ke baad **6 mahine ke andar Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA) ya FBR me registration** karwana lazmi hai — warna card block ho sakta hai. FBR filer banne ka tareeqa hamari [FBR filer guide](/guides/fbr-filer-banne-ka-tareeqa) me maujood hai.

## Step-by-Step: Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

**Step 1 — Portal kholein:** Sirf official portal **akc.punjab.gov.pk** istemal karein. Is jaisi dikhne wali kai ghair-rasmi websites aur "agent" pages maujood hain — CNIC aur OTP sirf official .gov.pk portal par dein.

**Step 2 — Account banayein:** Apna naam, CNIC number aur apne naam par registered mobile number darj karke register karein. Mobile par OTP ayega, wo darj karke account confirm karein.

**Step 3 — Category chunein:** **Asaan Karobar Card** select karein (agar bari financing chahiye to Asaan Karobar Finance wala hissa chunein) aur darkhwast ka form kholein.

**Step 4 — Tafseel bharein:** Zaati maloomat, karobar ka naam/pata/qisam, mojooda karobar ki tafseel ya naye karobar ka plan, aur darkaar raqam (10 lakh ki hadd ke andar) darj karein. Jo raqam likhein, us ka karobari istemal bhi wazeh likhein — stock, khaam maal, machinery waghera.

**Step 5 — Documents upload karein:** CNIC aur baqi mange gaye documents ki saaf scans upload karein. Dhundli tasveer sab se aam rejection ki wajah hai.

**Step 6 — Processing fee ada karein:** **Rs 500** processing fee portal ke bataye hue tareeqe se jama karein. Is ke ilawa kisi ko koi "fee" na dein — na agent ko, na "approval karwane" wale ko.

**Step 7 — Jaanch ka intezar karein:** Jama karne ke baad CNIC verification, e-CIB se credit history ki jaanch, psychometric assessment aur phir karobar ki jagah ki tasdeeq (verification) hoti hai. Ye amal kuch haftay le sakta hai — is dauran portal par login karke status dekhte rahein.

**Step 8 — Card hasil karein:** Manzoori ke baad Bank of Punjab ke zariye aap ka Asaan Karobar Card issue hota hai aur approved limit us par active ho jati hai. Card milte hi 6 mahine wali PRA/FBR registration ki deadline note kar lein.

Aik ahem note: portal par registration mukhtalif phases me khulta aur band hota hai — 2026 ke dauran aik arsay ke liye nayi registration band bhi rahi. Agar portal par registration band ka notice aye to ghabrayein nahi; ye guide apne paas rakhein, portal waqtan fawaqtan check karte rahein, aur jaise hi agla phase khule, upar ke steps se apply kar dein. Status aur maloomat ke liye scheme ki helpline **1786** bhi maujood hai.

## Card Milne Ke Baad Ke Usool

Card milna manzil nahi, shuruaat hai. In usoolo ki khilaf warzi card band karwa sakti hai:

- **Sirf karobari kharcha:** Funds sirf business-related activities ke liye hain — supplier ko payment, stock, khaam maal, karobari services. Zaati kharchay, shadi, ghar ka kharcha is se nahi chalana.
- **Digital channels:** Kharch mobile app, POS waghera se hota hai taa ke record rahe. Funds ko ghalat simt modne (diversion) ki koshish system pakar leta hai.
- **Qistein waqt par:** 33 qistein grace period ke baad shuru hoti hain. Der se adaigi aap ki credit history kharab karegi, jis ka asar mustaqbil ke har bank loan par parega.
- **PRA/FBR registration:** Card milne ke **6 mahine ke andar** PRA ya FBR registration lazmi hai. Agar aap ko apni karobari aamdani ka saboot chahiye to [income certificate guide](/guides/income-certificate-banane-ka-tareeqa) bhi kaam ayegi.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Darkhwast Rukwa Deti Hain

**Kisi aur ke naam wali SIM:** Sab se aam ghalti. Mobile number darkhwast guzar ke apne CNIC par registered na ho to OTP aur verification wahi ruk jati hai. Apne naam par kitni SIMs hain, ye [SIM check guide](/guides/apne-naam-par-kitni-sim-hain-check-karne-ka-tareeqa) se pehle confirm kar lein.

**Purana default chhupana:** Agar kisi bank ka purana loan overdue hai to wo e-CIB me nazar aye ga. Pehle wo clear karein, phir apply karein — chhupane se sirf waqt zaya hota hai.

**Agent ko paise dena:** Is scheme ka koi agent nahi hota aur Rs 500 processing fee ke ilawa koi sarkari fee nahi. Jo shakhs "approval ki guarantee" ke badle paise mange, wo fraud hai.

**Ghalat portal:** Sirf **akc.punjab.gov.pk** official hai. Isi naam se milty julty ghair-rasmi pages par CNIC/OTP dena khatarnaak hai.

**Do darkhwastein:** Aik shakhs ya karobar ke liye sirf aik darkhwast — duplicate darkhwast dono ko rukwa sakti hai.

Ghareeb aur mustahiq gharano ke liye ye loan wala raasta hai, jab ke naqad imdad ke liye [BISP 8171 guide](/guides/bisp-8171-registration-check) dekhein, aur retirement ke baad ki pension ke liye [EOBI pension guide](/guides/eobi-pension-claim-karne-ka-tareeqa) aap ke kaam ki hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Asaan Karobar Card par kitna loan milta hai aur sood kitna hai?**
Rs 10 lakh tak ka loan milta hai aur borrower ke liye markup 0% hai — mukammal bila sood. Kul muddat 3 saal hai, pehle 3 mahine grace period, phir 33 mahana qistein.

**Apply karne ki fee kitni hai?**
Sirf Rs 500 processing fee hai, jo na-qabil-e-wapsi hai. Is ke ilawa approved card par Rs 25,000 + FED saalana card fee lagti hai. In ke ilawa kisi ko koi raqam na dein.

**Kya naya karobar shuru karne wala bhi apply kar sakta hai?**
Ji haan. Ehliyaat me mojooda aur prospective (naya) dono qisam ke karobar shamil hain — shart ye hai ke karobar Punjab me ho aur aap ke paas seedha, qaabil-e-amal business plan ho.

**Kya apply ke waqt FBR filer hona lazmi hai?**
Nahi. Apply ke waqt tax registration lazmi nahi, lekin card milne ke 6 mahine ke andar PRA ya FBR me registration karwana lazmi hai, warna card block ho sakta hai.

**Mera purana bank loan chal raha hai, kya main apply kar sakta hun?**
Agar wo loan regular hai aur koi qist overdue nahi, to credit assessment me masla nahi hona chahiye. Lekin agar koi default ya overdue raqam hai to pehle wo clear karein — credit history ki jaanch har darkhwast par hoti hai.
