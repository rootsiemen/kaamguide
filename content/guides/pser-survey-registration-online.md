## Pehli Baat: PSER Kya Hai

**PSER** ka matlab hai **Punjab Socio-Economic Registry** — Hukumat-e-Punjab ka wo province-wide programme hai jis ke tehet Punjab ke har gharane ki samaji aur maashi maloomat ka aik mukammal aur qabil-e-bharosa database banaya ja raha hai. Is database me aap ke gharane ki ye maloomat darj hoti hai: ghar me kitne afrad hain, un ki taleem kya hai, kaun kaam karta hai aur kitna kamata hai, rehne ki haalat kaisi hai, aur bunyadi sahulaton (bijli, pani, gas, sehat) tak rasai kya hai.

Official portal **pser.punjab.gov.pk** ke mutabiq PSER ka maqsad sirf maloomat jama karna nahi hai — ye maloomat is liye jama ki jati hain taake hukumat ko sahi taur par pata chal sake ke kis gharane ko madad ki zaroorat hai, aur sarkari programme ka faida waqai usi tak pahunche jo is ka mustahiq hai. Isi liye PSER ko Punjab ke welfare nizaam ki bunyaad kaha jata hai: jab aap ka record PSER me darj ho jata hai, to har scheme ke liye aap ko baar baar naye form bharne ya alag se apni ghareebi sabit karne ki zaroorat nahi parti.

PSER ki khaas baat ye hai ke ye aik **survey** hai, koi aik scheme ya aik dafa ka form nahi. Is ka matlab ye hai ke PSER me register hona khud koi inaam, card ya raqam nahi deta — ye aap ka record banata hai, jis ki bunyaad par mukhtalif schemes ki ehliyaat tay hoti hai. Ye farq samajhna bohat zaroori hai, kyun ke bohat se log samajhte hain ke PSER form bharte hi un ko koi card ya paisa mil jaye ga. Asal me PSER sirf darwaza hai; scheme ka faida us ke baad, us scheme ki apni shartein poori hone par milta hai.

**Aik ahem note:** is guide ke facts 26 November 2026 ko official portal **pser.punjab.gov.pk** se check kiye gaye hain. Portal ka login page (portal-pser.punjab.gov.pk) is din direct load nahi hua, is liye account banane aur form bharne ke steps aazad (independent) guides se mutabiq kar ke likhe gaye hain — apply karne se pehle portal par ja kar hatmi steps zaroor confirm kar lein. Guide ke aakhir me is ka zikr dobara hai.

## PSER Kyun Zaroori Hai: Kin Schemes Ke Liye Chahiye

PSER ki asal ahmiyat is liye hai ke Punjab ki bari welfare schemes ki ehliyaat ab isi record se tay hoti hai. Official portal ke mutabiq PSER ka data **PSPA (Punjab Social Protection Authority)** aur mutaliqa departments istemal karte hain taake social protection programmes ke liye ehliyaat ka faisla ho sake. Aasan lafzon me: agar aap Punjab ki kisi sarkari sahulat ke liye apply karna chahte hain, to pehle PSER me aap ka record hona lazmi hai.

**CM Punjab Rashan Card:** Is scheme ke tehet mustahiq gharano ko har mahine **Rs 3,000** ki khane-peene ki cheezon ke liye madad di jati hai. Rashan Card ke liye darkhwast ka rasta seedha PSER portal se guzarta hai — aap ko pehle apna household survey PSER par mukammal karna hota hai, phir hukumat aap ki aamdani, **PMT score**, [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur gharane ki maloomat check kar ke faisla karti hai. Sirf PSER mukammal karne se Rashan Card ki guarantee nahi milti, lekin PSER ke baghair Rashan Card ka rasta band hai. Isi liye hamari [Rashan Card guide](/guides/punjab-rashan-card-apply-online) bhi pehle PSER se shuru hoti hai.

**Apni Chhat Apna Ghar:** Punjab ka bila-sood housing loan (Rs 15 lakh tak) bhi unhi gharano ko milta hai jin ka record aur maashi haal PSER jaisi sarkari tehqeeq se tasdeeq shuda ho. Is scheme ki tafseel hamari [Apni Chhat Apna Ghar guide](/guides/apni-chhat-apna-ghar-loan-apply-online) me hai.

**Dosri schemes:** Himmat Card, taleemi wazaif aur Punjab ki dosri sahulat schemes me bhi PSER record ko bunyaad banaya jata hai, kyun ke hukumat ab har scheme ke liye alag survey karne ke bajaye aik hi verified record istemal karna chahti hai. Is liye agar aap Punjab me rehte hain aur mustahiq gharane se hain, to PSER me apna record darj karwana aik dafa ka kaam hai jo aane wali tamam schemes ke liye kaam aaye ga.

**PMT score kya hota hai:** PSER me aap ki maloomat jama hone ke baad PSPA aap ke CNIC mutaliqa departments ko bhejti hai, jo aap ki milkiyat aur aamdani ki tasdeeq karte hain. Is ke baad aap ka **PMT (Proxy Means Test) score** banta hai — aik number jo aap ke gharane ki maashi haalat ko zahir karta hai. Har scheme ka apna PMT score ki had hoti hai; jis gharane ka score us had se neeche ho, wo us scheme ka mustahiq mana jata hai. Aap ko apna PMT score khud nahi banana hota aur na hi is ka koi form hota hai — ye aap ki di hui maloomat se khud banta hai. Is liye form me sahi aur mukammal maloomat dena hi sab se bara kaam hai.

## Kaun Register Kar Sakta Hai: Ehliyaat

PSER aik survey hai, koi muqabla ya merit list nahi — is liye is ki ehliyaat seedhi aur wasee hai:

**1. Punjab ka rehne wala hona lazmi hai:** PSER sirf Punjab ke gharano ke liye hai. Aap ka mustaqil pata Punjab ke kisi zila me hona chahiye. Dosre subon ke rehne wale is registry me nahi aate, kyun ke ye Punjab hukumat ka programme hai.

**2. Gharane ka sarbarah (head of family) darkhwast de:** Form gharane ke sarbarah ki maloomat se shuru hota hai. Aam taur par ye ghar ka woh fard hota hai jis ke naam par ghar chal raha hai — aksar walid ya shohar. Sarbarah ka valid CNIC hona lazmi hai, kyun ke poora record usi CNIC se jura hota hai.

**3. Khaas aamdani ki had nahi:** PSER me register hone ke liye koi aik fix aamdani ki had nahi hai — survey ka maqsad hi ye hai ke har gharane ki asal haalat darj ho. Aamdani zyada ho ya kam, record banta hai; sirf ehliyaat ka faisla baad me PMT score se hota hai. Is liye "hamari aamdani zyada hai, hum register na karein" sochna ghalat hai — record sab ka banta hai.

**4. Har gharane ka aik hi record:** Aik gharane ke liye aik hi application honi chahiye. Ghar ke har fard ki alag application bharna ghalti hai — aik ghar, aik form. Gharane ke tamam afrad ki maloomat aik hi form me darj hoti hai.

**5. Mobile number aur email:** Online account ke liye aik active mobile number (jo aap ke CNIC par registered SIM ho) aur aik email address chahiye hota hai. Mobile number par aap ko ahem paighamat aur verification milti hai, is liye woh number dein jo aap ke paas rehta ho aur band na ho.

## Zaroori Kagzaat Aur Maloomat

Form bharne se pehle ye cheezein tayyar rakh lein taake aap ka kaam adhoora na reh jaye. Form me aik waqt me poori maloomat mangi jati hai, aur beech me rukna aap ko dobara shuru karne par majboor kar sakta hai:

| Kagaz / Maloomat | Kis liye chahiye |
| --- | --- |
| Ghar ke har bare fard ka valid [CNIC](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) | Sarbarah aur har bare fard ki shanakht |
| 18 saal se chhote bachon ka [B-Form](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) | Bachon ki maloomat aur shanakht |
| Active mobile number (apne CNIC par registered SIM) | Account verification aur ahem paighamat |
| Email address | Account login aur password bhoolne par recovery |
| Ghar ke tamam afrad ki tareekh-e-pedaish | Har fard ki personal maloomat |
| Taleemi maloomat (har fard ki taleem) | Education section |
| Rozgar aur aamdani ki tafseel | Employment aur income section |
| Rehne ki haalat (ghar apna hai ya kiraye ka, kamre kitne hain) | Housing section |
| Sehat ki maloomat (koi mazoor fard hai to us ki tafseel) | Health section |

**CNIC aur B-Form ki ahmiyat:** Form me naam aur tareekh-e-pedaish bilkul waisi hi likhni hai jaisi CNIC ya B-Form par darj hai. Agar CNIC ki miyaad khatam ho gayi hai ya us me ghalti hai, to pehle us ko durust karwa lein — hamari [CNIC guide](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) aur [B-Form guide](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa) me is ka tareeqa hai. Ghalat naam ya tareekh baad me aap ke record ki tasdeeq me rukawat ban sakti hai.

**Aamdani ki maloomat:** Apni asal mahana aamdani likhein — na kam kar ke, na barha kar. Kam likhne se aap ka PMT score ghalat ban sakta hai aur baad me tasdeeq ke waqt masla ho sakta hai; zyada likhne se aap mustahiq hone ke bawajood scheme se bahir ho sakte hain. Tankhwah ki parchi ya aamdani ka saboot ho to behtar hai, lekin form bharne ke liye asal raqam ka pata hona hi kafi hai.

## Online Registration Ka Tareeqa: Step-by-Step

Online tareeqa un logon ke liye behtareen hai jin ke paas mobile ya computer aur internet hai. Ye tareeqa bilkul muft hai aur aap ghar baithe kar sakte hain. Steps is tarteeb se karein:

**Step 1: Official website kholein.** Apne browser me **pser.punjab.gov.pk** type karein. Website ka pata (address) kholne se pehle dobara check kar lein — is naam se milti-julti nakli websites bhi hain jo aap ka CNIC aur maloomat chura sakti hain. Sirf isi official website par bharosa karein.

**Step 2: Account banayein (Register).** Homepage par **Register** ya **Sign Up** ka button mile ga. Us par click karein aur ye maloomat dein: apna poora naam, CNIC number, mobile network aur mobile number, aur email address. Phir aik mazboot password banayein aur us ko confirm karein. Password aisa rakhein jo aap ko yaad rahe — yehi password aap ko baad me login ke liye chahiye ho ga.

**Step 3: Login karein.** Account banne ke baad apne CNIC number aur password se login karein. Agar login me masla ho to password dobara check karein — aksar masla password ki spelling ya CNIC me dash (-) lagane se hota hai. CNIC baghair dash ke, sirf 13 hindse, likhein.

**Step 4: New Application shuru karein.** Login ke baad **New Application** ka button mile ga. Us par click karne se pehle aik declaration (halafnama) aaye ga ke aap jo maloomat de rahe hain woh sach aur mukammal hain — is ko parh kar confirm karein. Ghalat maloomat dena aap ko scheme se bahir kar sakta hai.

**Step 5: Sarbarah ki maloomat dein.** Pehle hisse me gharane ke sarbarah ka naam aur CNIC darj karein. Phir un ki personal maloomat: jins (gender) aur tareekh-e-pedaish. Ye maloomat CNIC ke mutabiq honi chahiye.

**Step 6: Sehat aur taleem ki maloomat dein.** Agle hisse me gharane ke afrad ki sehat ki haalat (koi bimari ya mazoor hon to us ki tafseel) aur har fard ki taleem darj karein. Jo fard parh raha hai, us ka idara aur class bhi likhein.

**Step 7: Rozgar aur aamdani likhein.** Har kamane wale fard ka pesha, rozgar ki qisam (sarkari, private, mazdoori, karobar) aur mahana aamdani darj karein. Jo fard be-rozgar hai, us ko bhi saaf likhein — khaali chhorna ya ghalat likhna theek nahi.

**Step 8: Gharane ke tamam afrad shamil karein.** Is hisse me ghar me rehne wale har fard ko aik aik kar ke shamil karein — naam, CNIC ya B-Form number, aur sarbarah se rishta. Koi fard reh na jaye, kyun ke gharane ka size hi aap ki ehliyaat ka aik ahem hissa hai.

**Step 9: Ghar aur bunyadi sahulaton ki maloomat dein.** Ghar apna hai ya kiraye ka, kitne kamre hain, bijli/pani/gas ki sahulat hai ya nahi — ye sab darj karein. Official portal ke mutabiq ye maloomat is liye mangi jati hain taake aap ke rehne ki haalat ka sahi andaza ho sake.

**Step 10: Form review kar ke submit karein.** Submit karne se pehle poora form aik dafa dobara parh lein. Koi spelling ki ghalti, ghalat number ya reh jane wala hissa ho to usi waqt theek kar lein. Jab sab theek lage to **Submit** par click karein. Submit ke baad aap ko aik confirmation ya reference number milna chahiye — us ko sambhal kar rakh lein ya screenshot le lein.

**Status kaise check karein:** Submit ke baad apna status dekhne ke liye dobara usi portal par apne CNIC aur password se login karein. Wahan aap ko apni application ki haalat nazar aani chahiye. PSER aik musalsal (ongoing) registry hai — is ki koi aik fix aakhri tareekh nahi hoti; registration aur updates marhalon me hoti rehti hain, is liye kisi bhi website par likhi "aakhri tareekh" par bharosa karne se pehle official portal par check kar lein.

## Baghair Internet Ke Registration: Centre Aur Door-to-Door

Agar aap ke paas internet nahi hai ya online form bharna mushkil lagta hai, to PSER ke liye do aur tareeqe hain — aur ye bhi bilkul muft hain:

**Registration centres:** Punjab bhar me **5,000 se zyada registration centres** banaye gaye hain, jin me **E-Khidmat Markaz** bhi shamil hain. Aap apna original CNIC, ghar ke afrad ke CNIC/B-Form aur apna registered mobile number le kar qareebi centre ja sakte hain. Wahan mojood amla aap ka form bharne me madad kare ga. Centre jane se pehle ye maloomat aik kagaz par likh kar le jayein: ghar ke har fard ka naam aur CNIC/B-Form number, mahana aamdani, aur rehne ki tafseel — is se aap ka waqt bache ga.

**Union Council office:** Agar qareebi registration centre door hai to apni Union Council ke office se bhi madad mil sakti hai. Wahan bhi wohi kagzaat le kar jayein jo upar likhe hain.

**Door-to-door survey:** Official portal ke mutabiq PSER bunyadi taur par **door-to-door survey** se ban raha hai — tarbiyat-yafta teams secure digital tools ke sath ghar ghar ja kar maloomat jama karti hain. Agar survey team aap ke ghar aaye to un se taawun karein aur apne CNIC aur B-Form tayyar rakhein. Team ka koi fard aap se **koi fee nahi maang sakta** — PSER ki registration har tareeqe se muft hai. Jo bhi shakhs PSER ke naam par paisa mange, woh dhoka de raha hai — us ki shikayat qareebi Union Council ya E-Khidmat Markaz par karein.

## Fees Aur Waqt: Kitna Kharcha, Kitna Time

**Fees: bilkul muft.** PSER ki registration aur survey ki **koi fee nahi hai** — na online, na centre par, na door-to-door survey me. Ye aik sarkari survey hai, koi paid service nahi. Agar koi agent ya website aap se PSER registration ke naam par paisa mange to woh fraud hai. Apna CNIC number sirf official portal ya sarkari centre par dein, kisi agent ko na dein.

| Cheez | Tafseel |
| --- | --- |
| Online registration ki fee | **Muft (Rs 0)** |
| Centre ya survey ki fee | **Muft (Rs 0)** |
| Online form bharne ka waqt | Kagzaat tayyar hon to **20–30 minute** |
| Centre par waqt | Rash ke hisab se **30 minute se 2 ghante** |
| Record ki tasdeeq aur PMT score | Maloomat ki tasdeeq departments se hoti hai; is me **kuch hafte** lag sakte hain |
| Scheme ka faida | PSER mukammal hone ke baad, us scheme ki apni shartein aur elaan ke mutabiq |

**Waqt kitna lagta hai:** Online form khud bharna, agar tamam kagzaat pehle se tayyar hon, to aam taur par 20–30 minute ka kaam hai. Centre par rash par munhasir hai. Lekin ye yaad rakhein: form submit karna hi aakhri step nahi hai — is ke baad aap ka data **PITB** ko jata hai, phir **PSPA** us ki safai aur tasdeeq karti hai, aur phir departments aap ki milkiyat aur aamdani ki tasdeeq karte hain. Ye amal kuch hafte le sakta hai, is liye submit ke foran baad kisi scheme ka faida milne ki umeed na rakhein. Sabr karein aur apna status portal par waqtan fawaqtan check karte rahein.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

**1. Nakli website par form bharna:** Sab se bari aur khatarnak ghalti ye hai ke log "PSER registration" search kar ke pehli milne wali website par apna CNIC daal dete hain. Sirf **pser.punjab.gov.pk** official hai. Kisi bhi dosri website par CNIC, password ya bank ki maloomat kabhi na dein.

**2. PSER ko scheme samajhna:** PSER register karne se koi card ya paisa foran nahi milta. Jo log samajhte hain ke form bharte hi Rashan Card ya koi raqam mile gi, woh ghalat-fehmi me hain. PSER sirf aap ka record hai; scheme ka faida us scheme ki apni shartein poori hone par milta hai.

**3. Gharane ke afrad chhupana ya ghalat aamdani likhna:** Kuch log sochte hain ke aamdani kam likhne se scheme jaldi mile gi. Asal me departments aap ki maloomat ki tasdeeq karte hain — ghalat maloomat pakri jane par aap ka record kharab ho sakta hai aur aap scheme se bahir ho sakte hain. Hamesha sach likhein.

**4. Aik gharane ki do applications:** Ghar ke do afrad alag alag form bhar dein to record duplicate ho jata hai aur tasdeeq me masla hota hai. Aik ghar, aik form — ye rule yaad rakhein.

**5. Password bhool jana:** Online account ka password kisi kagaz par likh kar sambhal lein ya apne mobile me save karein. Password bhool jane par dobara account banana mushkil ho sakta hai aur aap ka purana record alag ho sakta hai. Password recovery ke liye wohi email aur mobile number chahiye hota hai jo account banate waqt diya tha.

**6. Agent ko paisa dena:** PSER muft hai. Jo shakhs "jaldi registration" ya "guarantee card" ke naam par paisa mange, woh dhoka de raha hai. Kisi agent ki zaroorat nahi — online tareeqa ghar baithe ho sakta hai, aur centre par sarkari amla muft madad karta hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**PSER survey registration ki fee kitni hai?**
PSER ki registration bilkul muft hai — online, registration centre ya door-to-door survey, kisi bhi tareeqe se koi fee nahi li jati. Agar koi aap se is ke naam par paisa mange to woh fraud hai; us ki shikayat qareebi Union Council ya E-Khidmat Markaz par karein.

**PSER me register hone se kya Rashan Card ya koi scheme foran mil jaye gi?**
Nahi. PSER sirf aap ke gharane ka record hai. Scheme ka faida us scheme ki apni shartein (jaise PMT score aur aamdani ki had) poori hone par milta hai. PSER mukammal karna scheme ke liye zaroori pehla qadam hai, lekin is ki guarantee nahi ke scheme foran mile gi.

**PSER ke liye kaun se kagzaat chahiye hote hain?**
Ghar ke har bare fard ka valid CNIC, 18 saal se chhote bachon ka B-Form, aik active mobile number (apne CNIC par registered SIM) aur aik email address. Is ke ilawa ghar ke tamam afrad ki tareekh-e-pedaish, taleem, rozgar, aamdani aur rehne ki haalat ki maloomat chahiye hoti hai.

**Online form bharna mushkil ho to kya karein?**
Aap qareebi registration centre (Punjab bhar me 5,000 se zyada centres, jin me E-Khidmat Markaz bhi hain) ya apni Union Council ke office ja sakte hain — wahan sarkari amla aap ka form bharne me muft madad kare ga. Is ke ilawa PSER ki survey team ghar ghar ja kar bhi maloomat jama karti hai.

**PSER ki koi aakhri tareekh hai?**
PSER aik musalsal (ongoing) registry hai, koi aik dafa ka form nahi — registration aur updates marhalon me hoti rehti hain. Kisi bhi website par likhi "aakhri tareekh" par bharosa karne se pehle official portal pser.punjab.gov.pk par taaza haalat zaroor check kar lein.
