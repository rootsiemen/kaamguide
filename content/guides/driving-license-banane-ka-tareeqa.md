## Pehli Baat: License Ke Baghair Gaari Chalana Qanoon Shikni Hai

Punjab me baghair valid driving license ke gaari ya bike chalana qanooni jurm hai — pakre jane par **e-challan**, bhari jurmana aur gaari zabt tak ho sakti hai. Traffic police ab digital system (DLIMS) se foran check kar leti hai ke license asal hai ya nahi. Is liye license banwana "sahulat" nahi, **zaroorat** hai.

Achhi khabar ye hai ke Punjab me process ab taqreeban poora **online** ho gaya hai. Learner license ghar bethe 15 minute me ban jata hai, fee online ada hoti hai, aur permanent license ka test bhi manzam tareeqe se hota hai. Is guide me hum **learner se lekar permanent license tak** ka poora safar cover karenge — fees, test, kagzaat aur aam ghaltiyan.

> **Note:** Is guide ki fees Punjab Traffic Police ke DLIMS/Dastak fee schedule se li gayi hain aur **October 2026 me check ki gayi thin**. Fees waqtan-fo-waqtan revise hoti hain — payment se pehle hamesha dlims.punjab.gov.pk ya Dastak portal par maujooda fee confirm kar lein.

## Step 1: Learner License (Ghar Bethe, 15 Minute Me)

Permanent license se pehle **learner license** lena lazmi hai — ye aap ka "seekhne ka ijazat-nama" hai. Punjab me ye poora online banta hai:

### Online Apply Ka Tareeqa

1. **Dastak portal** (dastak.punjab.gov.pk) par jayen ya Dastak app download karein — apne CNIC aur mobile number se account banayen.
2. Service me **Learner License** chunein aur category batayen: **Bike, Car, ya dono** (har category ki fee alag hai).
3. Form bharein — naam, CNIC, pata waghera darj karein.
4. Zaroori documents upload karein: **CNIC ki front/back scan copy** aur **haaliya tasveer**.
5. **PSID** (Payment Slip ID) generate hoga — is ke zariye fee ada karein: **ePay Punjab, JazzCash, Easypaisa, ATM ya internet banking** se.
6. Payment confirm hote hi **e-license download** kar lein — PDF form me milega, print karke ya phone me rakhna **qanooni tor par valid** hai.

### Learner License Ki Fee

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Motorcycle / Bike | Rs 500 |
| Car / Jeep | Rs 500 |
| LTV | Rs 500 |
| Bike + Car (dono) | Rs 1,000 |

**Ahem:** Har category ki fee alag hai — bike aur car dono chalani hai to dono ki fee deni hogi. Learner license **6 mahine** ke liye valid hota hai.

### 42 Din Ka Lazmi Wait

Learner license milne ke baad **kam-az-kam 42 din** guzarna zaroori hai — is se pehle aap permanent license ke test ke liye eligible nahi hain. Ye waqt driving seekhne ke liye hai. Is doran learner license saath rakh kar gaari chala sakte hain (behtar hai ke saath koi tajurbakar driver ho).

## Step 2: Sign Test Aur Driving Test

42 din poore hone ke baad qareebi **Traffic Police Licensing Center** jayen. Wahan do test hote hain:

### 1. Sign Test (Theory)

- Traffic signs (isharay) ki pehchan ka test hai — computer ya zabani liya jata hai.
- Aam signs yaad kar lein: stop, give way, speed limit, no entry, U-turn waghera.
- Ye test aasan hai lekin tayyari ke baghair log fail bhi hote hain — aik din signs parh lein.

### 2. Driving Test (Practical)

- **Bike:** Mukarrar track par bike chala kar dikhani hoti hai — tawazun aur control dekha jata hai.
- **Car:** Parking, reverse aur mukarrar route par gaari chalana hota hai.
- **AI-based supervision:** Ab test ki nigrani camera/AI system se bhi hoti hai — sifarish ka koi chance nahi, driving aani chahiye.

### Test Ki Fee

| Category | Test Fee (har attempt par) |
| --- | --- |
| Motorcycle | Rs 50 |
| Car / Jeep | Rs 150 |
| LTV | Rs 150 |

**Fail ho jayen to?** Ghabrayen nahi — dobara test de sakte hain, lekin **test fee dobara** deni hogi aur kuch din ka wait bhi ho sakta hai. Is liye pehli dafa tayyari karke jayen.

## Step 3: Permanent License Ki Fee (2026)

Test pass karne ke baad permanent license ki fee ada karni hoti hai. Neeche **1 saal** ki validity ki fees hain (courier Rs 480 shamil):

| Category | Test Fee | License Fee | Courier | Kul (1 saal) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motorcycle | Rs 50 | Rs 450 | Rs 480 | **Rs 930** |
| Car / Jeep | Rs 150 | Rs 1,350 | Rs 480 | **Rs 1,830** |
| LTV | Rs 150 | Rs 1,850 | Rs 480 | **Rs 2,330** |
| Rickshaw | Rs 100 | Rs 400 | Rs 480 | **Rs 880** |

### Validity Kitne Saal Ki Lein?

Punjab me license **1 se 5 saal** tak ki validity par ban sakta hai. Fee usi hisab se multiply hoti hai (courier sirf aik dafa):

- **Masalan:** 5 saal ka car license = Rs 1,350 × 5 + Rs 480 = **Rs 7,230**
- **Mashwara:** Agar budget ijazat de to **5 saal** ka banwa lein — har saal renewal ki pareshani aur mustaqbil me fee barhne ka khatra, dono khatam.

## Zaroori Kagzaat (Test Aur License Ke Liye)

- Asal **CNIC**
- **Learner license** (42 din purana)
- 2-3 **passport-size tasveeren**
- **Medical certificate** — sirf 50 saal se zyada umar walon ke liye (sarkari doctor se)
- Fee ki **payment raseed** (PSID wali)

## E-License Download Karna

Physical card aane me hafte lag sakte hain — is doran **e-license** kaam aata hai:

1. **dlims.punjab.gov.pk/elicense** par jayen.
2. License type chunein, **CNIC aur date of birth** darj karein.
3. E-license generate karke **PDF download** kar lein.

Traffic police ne khud tasdeeq ki hai ke **e-license qanooni tor par valid** hai jab tak printed card post se na aa jaye. Phone me save rakhna kaafi hai.

## License Ki Online Verification

Kisi ka license check karna ho (masalan driver rakhne se pehle):

1. **dlims.punjab.gov.pk/verify** par jayen.
2. License type, **CNIC aur license number** darj karein (dono zaroori hain).
3. Verification **muft** hai, login ki zaroorat nahi.

## License Renewal Ka Tafseeli Tareeqa

License expire hone se pehle renewal karwa lein:

- **Expiry se pehle renewal:** Koi jurmana nahi
- **3 mahine tak late:** Mamooli jurmana
- **3-12 mahine late:** Zyada jurmana
- **1 saal se zyada late:** Maximum penalty — aur is doran gaari chalana ghair-qanooni hai

Renewal ki fee taqreeban naye license jitni hi hoti hai (category ke hisab se). **Online renewal** ka tareeqa:

1. Dastak portal ya DLIMS par apne account me login karein.
2. **License Renewal** service chunein — apna license number darj karein.
3. PSID generate karke fee online ada karein (ePay, JazzCash, Easypaisa).
4. Naya license **courier se ghar** aa jayega — purana card saath rakhein jab tak naya na aa jaye.

**Pro tip:** Expiry se **aik mahine pehle** renewal ke liye apply kar dein — taake card waqt par mil jaye aur beech me baghair license ke na rehna pare.

## Doosre Soobe Se Punjab Aaye Logon Ke Liye

Agar aap ka license **Sindh, KPK ya Balochistan** se bana hai aur ab Punjab me rehte hain to:

- Aap ka maujooda license **poore Pakistan me valid** hai — alag se Punjab ka license banwane ki zaroorat nahi.
- Lekin agar license **expire** ho gaya hai to renewal usi soobe se hoga jahan se bana tha, ya NOC le kar Punjab se renewal karwayen.
- Mustaqil Punjab me rehne walon ke liye behtar hai ke agla license Punjab se banwayen — local centers par asani hoti hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Baghair learner ke test ke liye jana:** Learner lazmi hai — 42 din poore hon.
2. **Agenton ko paise dena:** Test ab AI ki nigrani me hota hai — agent kuch nahi karwa sakta, paise zaya na karein.
3. **Expiry bhool jana:** Card par expiry date check karte rahein — expired license par challan pakka hai.
4. **Ghalat category:** Sirf bike ka license hai to car nahi chala sakte — har category ka alag license hota hai.
5. **E-license na rakhna:** Card na aaya ho to e-license phone me zaroor rakhein.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Kya learner license ke baghair gaari chala sakta hun?**
Nahi — kam-az-kam learner license lazmi hai, warna challan hoga.

**Test me fail ho jaun to kitne din baad dobara de sakta hun?**
Aam tor par kuch din ke waqfe ke baad dobara test diya ja sakta hai — center se tareekh le lein. Test fee dobara deni hogi.

**Kya doosre soobe ka license Punjab me valid hai?**
Ji haan — Pakistan ke kisi bhi soobe ka valid license poore mulk me chalta hai.

**International driving license kaise banta hai?**
Valid Pakistani license ke baad international permit ke liye apply kar sakte hain — process DLIMS par mojood hai.

**Kya 18 saal se kam umar me license ban sakta hai?**
Nahi — driving license ke liye 18 saal ki umar lazmi hai.

**License gum ho jaye to?**
DLIMS se **duplicate** ke liye apply karein — motorcycle ke liye Rs 100, car ke liye Rs 150 fee hai. Gumshudgi ki FIR bhi katwa lein.

## Pehli Dafa Test Dene Walon Ke Liye

- Track ki **shape (figure-8, parking box)** pehle din kisi khali ground par practice kar lein.
- Test wale din **apni hi practised gaari** le jayen — naayi gaari par haath set karne me fail hone ka chance hota hai.
