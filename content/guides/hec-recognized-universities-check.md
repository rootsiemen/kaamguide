## Seedha Jawab: Recognition Check Kyun Lazmi Hai

Pakistan me har saal hazaron students aisi universities aur campuses me admission le lete hain jo **HEC (Higher Education Commission) se recognized hi nahi hote** — aur chaar saal baad pata chalta hai ke un ki degree **kabhi attest hi nahi ho sakti**. HEC ka usool bilkul wazeh hai: jo university, campus ya programme HEC ki official lists me nahi, us ki degree HEC **recognize nahi karta** — woh degree sarkari nokri, bahir ke mulk ke visa, aur aagli taleem, kahin qabool nahi hoti. Fee bharne se pehle sirf **10 minute** ka check aap ke chaar saal aur lakhon rupay bacha sakta hai.

Khushkhabri ye hai ke HEC ne tamam lists apni official website **hec.gov.pk** par public kar rakhi hain aur check bilkul **free** hai. Is guide me hum step-by-step batayenge ke kaun si list kahan hai, university ke saath-saath us ke **campus** aur **programme** ki tasdeeq kaise hoti hai, aur kin red flags par admission foran rok dena chahiye. Aur agar aap ki degree pehle se Pakistani university ki hai aur us ki attestation chahiye, to tareeqa [HEC Degree Attestation guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) me hai.

> **Note:** HEC ki lists waqt ke saath update hoti rehti hain — naye campuses judte hain, kuch ke admissions band bhi hote hain. Is liye ye check **usi din** karein jis din fee bharne lagein, aur list ka screenshot mehfooz rakhein.

## HEC Ki Chaar Official Lists — Kaun Si Kab Dekhni Hai

HEC aik nahi, **chaar alag lists** publish karta hai. Sirf aik list dekhna kaafi nahi — university main list me ho sakti hai lekin aap wala campus list me na ho.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Recognized Universities / DAIs** | Pakistan ki tamam recognized universities aur degree awarding institutions | https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/pages/recognised.aspx |
| 2 | **HEC Recognized Campuses** | Kin universities ke **kaun se sub-campuses** recognized hain (public 84 + private 36 = 120 campuses) | https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/DAIs/HEC-recognized-Campuses.aspx |
| 3 | **Illegal / Fake Universities & Campuses** | Ghair-qanooni, jaali aur unrecognized idaron ki warning list | https://www.hec.gov.pk/Urdu/universities/Pages/Illegal-DAIs.aspx |
| 4 | **HEC Recognised TNE Institutions (Foreign Collaboration)** | Pakistan me **bahir ki degree** dene wale manzoor-shuda programmes | https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/Foreign-Collaboration-Institutions.aspx |

HEC apne official public notices me inhi lists ke short links bhi deta hai: **hec.gov.pk/site/HEIs** (recognized universities), **hec.gov.pk/site/recognizedCampuses** (campuses), **hec.gov.pk/site/FCI** (foreign collaboration) aur **hec.gov.pk/site/Illegal-DAIs** (illegal/fake list). Jo idarah in lists me na mile, HEC ke notice ke alfaz me woh **recognized nahi samjha jayega** — baat yahin khatam.

## DAI Kya Hota Hai — Aur Ye Lafz Kyun Ahem Hai

Lists me aap ko baar baar **DAI** likha milega: **Degree Awarding Institution**. Is ka matlab hai aisa idarah jis ko qanoon (charter) ke zariye **apne naam se degree dene ka ikhtiyar** mila ho. Har university DAI hoti hai, lekin har college DAI nahi hota:

- **University / DAI** — apni degree khud deta hai; HEC ki main list me hona lazmi hai
- **Affiliated college** — kisi university se affiliated hota hai, degree **university** deti hai. Public sector university ke affiliated colleges ki tasdeeq mutaliqa university se hoti hai
- **Sub-campus** — university ki branch; recognized campuses wali list me hona lazmi hai

**Ahem qanooni nuqta:** **Private universities ko doosre idaron ko affiliate karne ki ijazat nahi hoti.** Agar koi private "college" dawa kare ke woh kisi private university se affiliated hai aur us ki degree milegi, to ye pehla red flag hai.

## Step-by-Step: University Aur Campus Check Karne Ka Tareeqa

### Step 1: Main List Me University Ka Naam Talash Karein

1. **Recognized Universities** wali list kholein: https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/pages/recognised.aspx
2. University ka **poora official naam** talash karein — mukhtasar naam (abbreviation) se nahi, full naam se, kyunkay milte julte naam wale jaali idaray aam hain.
3. Naam mil jaye to us ka **sector** (public/private), **city** aur **chartering authority** note kar lein — yehi tafseel baad me degree par bhi ayegi.

### Step 2: Campus Alag Se Check Karein

1. Agar aap ka admission **main campus** ke ilawa kisi doosre sheher ke campus me hai, to **Recognized Campuses** list kholein: https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/DAIs/HEC-recognized-Campuses.aspx
2. University ke naam ke neeche apne sheher ka campus talash karein. HEC ka wazeh usool hai: **sirf wohi campuses recognized hain jo is list me hain.**
3. List me kuch campuses ke sath notes bhi hote hain — masalan "admissions stopped" ya kisi specific programme ki ijazat. Ye notes ghor se parhein; campus recognized honay ke bawajood **aap wala programme** band ho sakta hai.

### Step 3: Illegal/Fake List Se Cross-Check Karein

1. **Illegal/Fake Universities & Campuses** list kholein: https://www.hec.gov.pk/Urdu/universities/Pages/Illegal-DAIs.aspx
2. Apne idare ka naam is list me talash karein — naam yahan milna matlab **foran admission rok dein**, koi wazahat qabool na karein.
3. Ye list province ke hisab se hai aur is me woh chhote "institutes" bhi shamil hain jo computers ya management ke naam par **degrees baant rahe hain** halankay un ke paas degree dene ka koi ikhtiyar nahi.

### Step 4: Foreign Degree Programme Ho To TNE List Lazmi

1. Agar idarah dawa karta hai ke "UK/USA ki degree Pakistan me baithe milegi", to **TNE (Transnational Education) list** kholein: https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/universities/Pages/Foreign-Collaboration-Institutions.aspx
2. Is list me har idare ke sath **foreign university ka naam, programmes, aur recognition ki muddat (duration)** likhi hoti hai. Aap wala programme aur aap ke admission wale saal ka is muddat me hona lazmi hai.
3. Muddat khatam ho chuki ho ya programme list me na ho, to woh foreign degree baad me **Pakistan me recognize nahi hogi** — aur us ki [HEC equivalence](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate) bhi nahi banegi.

### Step 5: Programme Ki Accreditation Bhi Dekhein (Jahan Applicable Ho)

University ki recognition ke ilawa kuch professions me **programme ki alag accreditation** bhi lazmi hai — masalan Engineering ke liye **PEC**, Law ke liye **Pakistan Bar Council**, Medical ke liye mutaliqa council. University recognized ho lekin programme accredited na ho, to degree ke bawajood aap us profession ki practice/license nahi le sakte. LLB ke liye to admission se pehle [HEC LAT test](/guides/hec-lat-test-guide) bhi pass karna lazmi hai.

## Ghair-Recognized Degree Ka Nuqsan — Haqeeqat Samajh Lein

- **HEC attestation kabhi nahi hogi:** Attestation ka pehla usool hi ye hai ke degree HEC-recognized idare ki ho. Ghair-recognized degree par HEC ki mohar nahi lagti — chahay degree bilkul "asal" hi kyun na ho.
- **Sarkari nokri zero:** Har sarkari advertisement me HEC-recognized degree shart hoti hai.
- **Bahir ka visa/ECA zero:** WES aur doosre ECA idaray bhi Pakistani degree ko HEC recognition se jor kar dekhte hain; [sealed envelope](/guides/hec-sealed-envelope-wes) to door ki baat, application hi aage nahi barhti.
- **Waqt aur paisa wapas nahi aata:** HEC aisi degrees ke liye koi "special case" nahi chalata. Chaar saal aur fee, dono zaya.

## Admission Se Pehle Ke Red Flags

1. **"HEC se recognition under process hai"** — recognition ya hoti hai ya nahi hoti; "process me hai" ka matlab hai **nahi hai**. Fee na bharein.
2. **Idarah list me hai lekin campus nahi** — aap ki degree campus ki hogi, main university ki nahi. Campus list hi faisla hai.
3. **Bohat sastti fee aur "baghair attendance degree"** — recognized idaray attendance aur examination ke baghair degree nahi dete; ye jaali setup ki pehchan hai.
4. **Foreign degree ka dawa, TNE list me naam nahi** — seedha fraud samjhein.
5. **Naam me halka sa farq** — jaali idaray mashhoor universities se milta julta naam rakhte hain. Daftar ke board par likha naam lists ke naam se **harf-ba-harf** mila kar dekhein.
6. **Affiliation ka jhoota dawa** — college kahe ke "hum falan university se affiliated hain" to university ki official website/registrar se khud confirm karein; private university ki affiliation to qanoonan hoti hi nahi.

## Agar Degree Bahir Ki University Se Karni Hai

Jo students bahir ja kar parhne ka plan bana rahe hain, un ke liye usool ye hai: jis mulk me parhna hai, wahan ki university **us mulk ke apne system me recognized/accredited** honi chahiye. Wapas aane par HEC usi surat me [equivalence certificate](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate) deta hai jab foreign university apne mulk me recognized ho aur degree ki tasdeeq university khud HEC ko kare. Admission se pehle us mulk ki official accreditation body ki list se university check karna aap ki apni zimmedari hai — HEC bahir ki har university ki list pehle se publish nahi karta, equivalence application ke waqt case-to-case tasdeeq hoti hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**University HEC recognized hai ya nahi, sab se tez check kya hai?**
HEC ki Recognized Universities list (hec.gov.pk/english/universities/pages/recognised.aspx) me poora naam talash karein, phir Recognized Campuses list me apna campus, aur aakhir me Illegal/Fake list me cross-check. Teeno checks me kul 10 minute lagte hain aur bilkul free hain.

**University list me hai lekin mera campus list me nahi — kya degree chalegi?**
Nahi. HEC sirf un campuses ko recognize karta hai jo Recognized Campuses list me hain. Ghair-listed campus ki degree HEC attest nahi karega, chahay university khud recognized ho.

**"Recognition under process" wale idare me admission le loon?**
Bilkul nahi. Jab tak idarah HEC ki published list me na aa jaye, woh unrecognized hi hai. Us arse ki degree baad me bhi tasdeeq nahi hoti.

**Affiliated college ki degree kaun deta hai aur check kaise ho?**
Degree hamesha **university** deti hai, college nahi. Public university ke affiliated colleges ki list mutaliqa university se confirm karein. Private university kisi college ko affiliate nahi kar sakti — aisa dawa red flag hai.

**Foreign (UK/USA) degree Pakistan me baithe kar sakte hain?**
Sirf wohi programmes jo HEC ki **TNE / Foreign Collaboration list** me us programme aur muddat ke sath darj hon. List se bahir ke foreign collaboration programmes ki degree Pakistan me recognize nahi hoti aur us ki equivalence bhi nahi banti — tareeqa [HEC Equivalence guide](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate) me dekhein.

**Maine pehle se ghair-recognized idare se degree le li hai — ab kya ho sakta hai?**
Sach ye hai ke HEC aisi degree ko attest ya recognize nahi karta, aur koi agent ye kaam nahi kar sakta — jo agent "paison se attestation" ka wada kare woh fraud hai. Mustaqbil ka nuqsan rokne ke liye aage ki taleem hamesha recognized idare se karein, aur apni attested documents ki hifazat aur verification ka tareeqa [HEC verification guide](/guides/hec-degree-verification-online) me dekhein.

**HEC ki lists kab update hoti hain?**
HEC lists ko waqtan fawaqtan update karta hai — naye charters, naye campuses aur band kiye gaye programmes shamil hote rehte hain. Is liye admission ke **usi season** me dobara check karna lazmi hai; purana screenshot hamesha ka saboot nahi.
