## Pehli Baat: Equivalence Kya Hai Aur Kis Ko Chahiye

**Equivalence certificate** wo sarkari dastavez hai jo ye tasdeeq karta hai ke aap ki bahir ki taleem Pakistan ki kis class ke barabar hai. Isay **Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC)** jaari karta hai — ye idara Wafaqi Wazarat-e-Taleem ke tehet kaam karta hai aur Pakistan ke tamam taleemi boards ke darmiyan rabta rakhta hai.

Agar aap ne **O Levels** kiye hain to IBCC unhe **SSC (Matric)** ke barabar qarar deta hai, aur agar **A Levels** kiye hain to unhe **HSSC (Inter)** ke barabar qarar deta hai. Ye certificate un sab ko chahiye hota hai jo:

- **Pakistan ki university me admission** lena chahte hain — universities O/A Levels ke number seedhe nahi letin, unhe IBCC equivalence par diye gaye marks chahiye hote hain.
- **Pakistan me sarkari ya private nokri** ke liye apply kar rahe hain jahan Matric/Inter ki qualification mangi jati hai.
- **Bahir mulk (WES waghera) evaluation** ke liye documents jama kar rahe hain — aksar evaluation bodies IBCC equivalence mangti hain.
- **Medical ya engineering college** me admission lena chahte hain — PMDC/PMC aur engineering universities equivalence ke baghair application qabool nahi kartin.

Ahem farq yaad rakhein: **equivalence** aur **attestation** do alag kaam hain. Equivalence ka matlab hai "aap ki bahir ki taleem Pakistan ki kis class ke barabar hai", jabke attestation ka matlab hai "aap ke asal certificate par tasdeeq ki mohar". Attestation ke liye alag process hai — pehle equivalence lein, phir zaroorat ho to attestation karayen.

## Kaunsi Equivalence: SSC, HSSC Ya Dono

IBCC do qisam ki equivalence deta hai, aur aap apni zaroorat ke mutabiq aik ya dono ke liye apply kar sakte hain:

| Equivalence | Kis Ke Liye | Pakistan Me Barabar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSC Equivalence | O Levels (Grades 9-10) | Matric — Science ya Arts group |
| HSSC Equivalence | A Levels (Grades 11-12) | Inter — Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, Computer Science ya Humanities |

**Group ka intekhaab** aap ke subjects par munhasir hai. SSC ke liye **Science group** tab milta hai jab aap ne science subjects parhe hon, warna **Arts group**. HSSC ke liye: **Pre-Medical** ke liye Biology, Chemistry aur Physics; **Pre-Engineering** ke liye Mathematics, Physics aur Chemistry; **Computer Science** ke liye Computer Science ke saath Physics aur Mathematics; **Humanities** ke liye koi bhi teen A Level subjects. Ghalat group chunne se admission me masla ho sakta hai, is liye form bharne se pehle apni target university ya college ke required group ko confirm kar lein.

Aik aur ahem shart: **HSSC equivalence ke liye SSC ya us ke barabar qualification pehle se hona lazmi hai**. Yani A Levels ki equivalence tabhi milegi jab O Levels ki equivalence (ya us ke barabar) mojood ho. Dono ke liye aik saath bhi apply kiya ja sakta hai — form me "Both" ka option mojood hai.

## Official Fee: Har Certificate Rs 6,000

IBCC ki fee IBCC ke official equivalence form par darj hai — ye rakam andaze par nahi, balkay IBCC ki apni published fee hai:

| Kaam | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Equivalence certificate — SSC (O Levels) | Rs 6,000 |
| Equivalence certificate — HSSC (A Levels) | Rs 6,000 |
| Dono (SSC + HSSC) aik saath | Rs 12,000 |
| Return courier charges (andar-e-mulk, optional) | Rs 300 |
| Bahir mulk se apply karne walon ke liye | $100 per certificate |
| Duplicate / Revised certificate | Rs 8,000 |
| Triplicate / 2nd revision | Rs 10,000 |
| Quadruplicate / 3rd revision | Rs 12,000 |

Fee **non-refundable** hai — aik dafa jama ho jaye to wapas nahi milti, chahe application me kami ki wajah se ruk jaye. Is liye form jama karne se pehle documents ki list dobara check kar lein. Teesri dafa revision (quadruplicate) ke baad IBCC koi mazeed certificate jaari nahi karta, is liye duplicate/revision ka kaam ehtiyat se karein.

Fee jama karne ka tareeqa form par darj hota hai — challan ya bank ke zariye. Fee receipt application ke saath lagana lazmi hai; baghair receipt ke incomplete application process nahi hoti.

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kya Kya Lagana Hai

IBCC ki official requirements ke mutabiq ye kagzaat darkar hain — **incomplete application process nahi hoti**, is liye har cheez mukammal kar ke jayen:

- **Mukammal equivalence application form** — tamam tafseel capital letters me, saaf aur durust.
- **Asal certificates aur transcripts** — O Levels aur A Levels ke asal result certificates; Grades 9 se 12 tak ka mukammal academic record. Asal certificates wapas kar diye jate hain, lekin transcripts IBCC ke record me reh jati hain.
- **School/institution ki accreditation ka saboot** — ye tasdeeq ke aap ka school/board kisi tasleem-shuda authority (masalan Cambridge) se manzoor-shuda hai.
- **CNIC ya Form-B ki copy** — Pakistani students ke liye; walid/walida ya guardian ka CNIC bhi lagta hai.
- **Passport ki copy (agar bahir parha ho)** — visa details aur entry/exit stamps ke saath, jo ye sabit kare ke aap ne wo muddat bahir guzari.
- **Translation (agar zaroorat ho)** — jo documents English ya Urdu me na hon, un ki certified translation mutaliqa embassy ya NUML se karwani hoti hai.
- **Syllabus/curriculum ki copy** — khaas tor par naye subjects ya nayi qualifications ke liye, taake IBCC course ki noiyat samajh sake.
- **Passport-size tasveerain** — form par mangi gayi tadaad me, haal ki tasveerain.
- **Fee payment receipt** — challan ya transaction ki raseed.

Aik khaas ehtiyat: **laminated certificates** IBCC ko pasand nahi — hard lamination wale certificate par mohar lagane me masla hota hai. Agar aap ka certificate laminated hai to jama karne se pehle IBCC office se mashwara kar lein.

## Pakistani Students Ke Liye Khaas Shart: Urdu, Islamiat, Pak Studies

Ye wo shart hai jo sab se zyada students ko hairan karti hai, is liye isay achi tarah samajh lein. IBCC ke rules ke mutabiq:

- **Pakistan se O/A Levels karne wale Pakistani students** ko equivalence ke liye **Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies aur Mathematics** pass karna lazmi hai — ye subjects un ke O Level record me hone chahiye.
- **Bahir mulk se O Levels karne wale students** SSC equivalence ke liye in teen subjects se mustasna (exempt) hain.
- **A Levels ke liye** exemption sirf un Pakistani students ko milti hai jin hon ne **O Levels aur A Levels dono bahir mulk** se kiye hon. Agar aik Pakistan se aur aik bahir se kiya ho to ye teen subjects phir bhi lazmi hain.
- **Ghair-mulki (foreign nationals) aur dual nationals** in subjects se mustasna hain.

Agar aap ke paas ye subjects nahi hain to ghabrayen nahi — IBCC ke rules me in subjects ko baad me pass kar ke equivalence mukammal karne ka tareeqa mojood hai. Apni surat-haal IBCC office ya helpline par bata kar sahi rasta maloom karein, aur form jama karne se pehle ye shart zaroor check karein.

## Grade Conversion: Aap Ke Marks Kaise Bante Hain

IBCC aap ke Cambridge grades ko Pakistani marks me is tarah badalta hai — ye IBCC ka apna grade table hai:

| Grade | Marks (100 me se) |
| --- | --- |
| A\* | 90 |
| A | 85 |
| B | 75 |
| C | 65 |
| D | 55 |
| E | 45 |

Is ka hisaab is tarah hota hai:

- **O Levels (SSC equivalence):** 8 subjects ke marks jama hote hain — kul 800 me se. Masalan agar aap ke 8 subjects ke grades ke marks 750 bante hain to aap ki SSC equivalence 750/800 hogi.
- **A Levels (HSSC equivalence):** 3 subjects ke marks jama hote hain — kul 300 me se. Masalan A\*, A, B grades ke marks 90 + 85 + 75 = 250/300 honge.
- **Mukammal HSSC equivalence:** O Levels ke 800 aur A Levels ke 300 mila kar kul 1100 me se percentage nikali jati hai. Aap ki final equivalence certificate par jo marks aur percentage likhi hogi, wohi universities istemal karengi.

Yaad rakhein: IBCC sirf **A\* se E tak ke grades** qabool karta hai. U (ungraded) ya is se kam grade wala subject equivalence me shumaar nahi hota, aur required subject me E se kam grade ho to wo group ki equivalence nahi ban sakti. Apne grades ki tafseel IBCC ke grade table se khud bhi mila lein — final certificate par likhi rakam hi hatmi hai.

## Step-by-Step: Apply Karne Ka Tareeqa

IBCC me apply karne ka tareeqa ye hai:

1. **Form hasil karein** — IBCC ki website **ibcc.edu.pk** se equivalence form download karein ya IBCC office se muft hasil karein. Form muft hai, is ki koi qeemat nahi.
2. **Form mukammal bharein** — naam, walid ka naam, date of birth, CNIC, address, school/institution ki tafseel, subjects aur group — sab capital letters me aur certificate ke mutabiq. Naam aur date of birth tamam certificates aur CNIC par aik jaisi honi chahiye.
3. **Documents jama karein** — oopar wali list ke tamam kagzaat, asal aur copies, form ke saath lagayen. Copies par apne dastakhat karein.
4. **Fee jama karayen** — Rs 6,000 per certificate (ya dono ke liye Rs 12,000) form par darj tareeqe se jama karayen aur receipt form ke saath lagayen.
5. **IBCC office me jama karayen** — IBCC Secretariat, Plot 25, Street 38, Mauve Area, G-10/4, Islamabad, ya qareebi regional office me jama karayen. Online portal par bhi apply kiya ja sakta hai — portal par account bana kar form aur documents upload karein.
6. **Receipt mehfooz rakhein** — jama karne par jo receipt ya diary number mile, usay sambhal kar rakhein; isi se status maloom hoga aur certificate lene ke waqt yehi dikhana hoga.
7. **Certificate hasil karein** — tay shuda tareekh par IBCC office se asal certificates aur equivalence certificate wapas lein. Courier ka option chuna ho to certificate aap ke address par bhej diya jayega.

**Provisional equivalence** saaf cases me sirf **7 working days** me Islamabad Secretariat se mil sakti hai — ye aik aarzi letter hota hai jo admission ke kaam aa sakta hai. **Proper (mustaqil) equivalence certificate** us waqt banta hai jab IBCC aap ke documents ki verification mutaliqa authority/institution se kar le — is me zyada waqt lag sakta hai, is liye admission ki aakhri tareekh se kam az kam aik mah pehle apply karein.

## Time Kitna Lagta Hai

- **Provisional equivalence (saaf cases, Islamabad Secretariat):** 7 working days.
- **Proper equivalence:** verification par munhasir — aam tor par kuch haftay lagte hain. Agar aap ka school/board verification me der kare to waqt barh sakta hai.
- **Courier delivery:** andar-e-mulk return courier Rs 300 me hoti hai; bahir mulk ke liye apna prepaid courier envelope lagana hota hai.

Agar aap ko admission ki aakhri tareekh qareeb hai to provisional letter le kar university me jama karayen aur proper certificate aane par usay update kar dein — kai universities provisional letter par aarzi admission de deti hain.

## Duplicate Ya Revised Certificate Kaise Lein

Agar aap ka equivalence certificate gum ho jaye ya is me ghalti ho to:

- **Duplicate ke liye:** akhbaar me gumshudgi ka ishtehaar, Rs 100 ke stamp paper par notarized affidavit, purane certificate ki photocopy (agar mojood ho) aur Rs 8,000 fee darkar hai.
- **Revision ke liye:** saada kagaz par darkhwast jis me tabdeeli ki noiyat likhi ho, asal purana certificate wapas karna hoga, aur Rs 8,000 fee darkar hai.
- **Triplicate (2nd revision):** Rs 10,000; **Quadruplicate (3rd revision):** Rs 12,000. Is ke baad IBCC koi mazeed certificate jaari nahi karta.

Is liye asal certificate milte hi is ki 2-3 attested copies banwa kar mehfooz rakh lein — har kaam ke liye asal certificate baar baar nikalna khatarnak hai.

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

- **Incomplete form jama karna** — IBCC incomplete application process hi nahi karta; fee bhi zaya hoti hai kyunke fee non-refundable hai.
- **Naam/date of birth me farq** — certificate, CNIC aur form par naam aur date of birth aik jaisi honi chahiye; farq ho to pehle usay durust karayen.
- **Urdu/Islamiat/Pak Studies ki shart nazar-andaz karna** — Pakistan se parhne walon ke liye ye lazmi hai; baad me pata chalne par waqt zaya hota hai.
- **Laminated certificate jama karna** — hard lamination par mohar nahi lagti; pehle mashwara karein.
- **Aakhri waqt par apply karna** — verification me waqt lagta hai; admission se aik mah pehle apply karein.
- **Equivalence aur attestation ko aik samajhna** — pehle equivalence lein, phir zaroorat ho to attestation ka alag process karein.
- **Agent ko paise dena** — IBCC ka form muft hai aur fee sirf official tareeqe se jama hoti hai; jo shakhs "jaldi certificate" ke naam par zyada paise mange, wo fraud hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**IBCC equivalence ki fee kitni hai?**
Official fee Rs 6,000 per certificate hai — SSC (O Levels) ke liye Rs 6,000 aur HSSC (A Levels) ke liye Rs 6,000, yani dono ke liye Rs 12,000. Andar-e-mulk return courier Rs 300 aur bahir mulk se apply karne walon ke liye $100 per certificate hai. Duplicate ya revised certificate ki fee Rs 8,000 hai. Fee non-refundable hai.

**O Levels ki equivalence kis class ke barabar hoti hai?**
O Levels ki equivalence SSC (Matric) ke barabar hoti hai — Science ya Arts group, aap ke subjects ke mutabiq. A Levels ki equivalence HSSC (Inter) ke barabar hoti hai — Pre-Engineering, Pre-Medical, Computer Science ya Humanities group. HSSC equivalence ke liye SSC ya us ke barabar qualification pehle se hona lazmi hai.

**Pakistani students ko Urdu, Islamiat aur Pak Studies kyun lazmi hain?**
IBCC ke rules ke mutabiq Pakistan se O/A Levels karne wale Pakistani students ko Urdu, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies aur Mathematics pass karna lazmi hai. Bahir mulk se dono levels karne wale Pakistani students aur foreign/dual nationals is se mustasna hain. Apni surat-haal IBCC helpline par confirm karein.

**Equivalence certificate kitne din me milta hai?**
Saaf cases me provisional equivalence 7 working days me Islamabad Secretariat se mil sakti hai. Proper (mustaqil) certificate ke liye IBCC aap ke documents ki verification mutaliqa authority se karta hai, is liye is me kuch haftay lag sakte hain. Admission ki aakhri tareekh se kam az kam aik mah pehle apply karein.

**Equivalence aur attestation me kya farq hai?**
Equivalence ye batati hai ke aap ki bahir ki taleem Pakistan ki kis class ke barabar hai, jabke attestation aap ke asal certificate par tasdeeq ki mohar hai. Pehle equivalence lein, phir agar university ya nokri ke liye attestation darkar ho to IBCC attestation ka alag process karein.
