## HEC Degree Verification Kya Hai — Seedha Jawab

**HEC degree verification** ka matlab hai kisi bhi HEC-attested degree ki asliyat online check karna. 2026 se HEC ka attestation system **fully online aur paperless** hai: degree attest hone par HEC aik **e-Attestation Certificate** jari karta hai jis par mojood **QR code aur certificate number** se koi bhi — employer, university ya embassy — **eservices.hec.gov.pk** par degree ki tasdeeq kar sakta hai. Agar QR scan ya portal check par degree ka record match ho jaye, to degree asal hai; record na mile to woh attestation HEC ki nahi hai.

Verification attestation se alag cheez hai:

- **Attestation** — degree holdar apni degree HEC se attest karwata hai ([HEC Degree Attestation guide](/guides/hec-degree-attestation))
- **Verification** — koi **teesra fard** (employer/university) us attestation ko check karta hai ke asal hai ya nahi (ye guide)
- **Equivalence verification** — bahir ki degree ke equivalence letter ki tasdeeq bhi isi portal se hoti hai ([HEC Equivalence guide](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate))

## Verification Ki Zaroorat Kab Parti Hai

1. **Nokri ki joining par:** Sarkari aur baray private idaray HEC attestation khud verify karte hain — jaali degree pakarna ab QR scan jitna asan hai.
2. **Bahir admission/visa par:** Universities aur embassies Pakistani degree ki HEC tasdeeq mangti hain.
3. **Apni degree check karne ke liye:** Attestation ke baad khud verify karke tasalli kar lein ke aap ka record system me sahi darj hai — naam ki spelling waghera.
4. **Purani attestation par shak ho to:** Kisi candidate ki purani (manual daur ki) attestation ki tasdeeq ke liye bhi HEC ka system istemal hota hai.

## Step-by-Step: QR Code Se Verification

1. Degree ke sath mila **e-Attestation Certificate** lein — us par QR code aur certificate number chhapa hota hai.
2. Mobile camera ya kisi QR scanner se **QR code scan** karein — woh aap ko HEC ke verification page par le jayega.
3. Page par degree holdar ka **naam, degree, university aur attestation ki tafseel** zahir hogi.
4. Tafseel ko asal degree se **harf-ba-harf match** karein — naam, university, session aur degree title sab aik jaisa hona chahiye.

QR code sab se tez aur sab se reliable tareeqa hai — type karne ki ghalti ka imkan nahi hota.

## Step-by-Step: Portal Se Verification (Baghair QR Ke)

1. **eservices.hec.gov.pk** kholein.
2. **Degree Verification** wala section chunein (portal par attested degrees aur equivalence letters dono ki verification mojood hai).
3. Certificate number / darj shuda maloomat enter karein jo e-attestation certificate par di gayi hain.
4. Result screen par record zahir ho jayega — match ho to degree verified hai.

Portal par apne account se login karke aap **apni attested degrees ki list** bhi dekh sakte hain aur e-attestation certificate dobara download bhi kar sakte hain — certificate kho jaye to yehi rasta hai, daftar jane ki zaroorat nahi.

## Verification Fail Ho To Kya Karein

1. **Spelling check karein:** Naam ki spelling CNIC, degree aur certificate — teeno par aik jaisi honi chahiye. Mismatch ho to pehle ye farq wazeh karein.
2. **Certificate number dobara dekhein:** Aik digit ki ghalti bhi "record not found" de sakti hai — QR scan isi liye behtar hai.
3. **Purani manual attestation:** Purane daur ki physical stamp wali attestation ka record online system me foran zahir na ho to HEC ki helpline/portal support se rabta karein — degree holdar ko apni attestation ki record updation karwani par sakti hai.
4. **Degree hi record me na ho:** Agar degree kabhi attest hi nahi hui, to verification mumkin nahi — pehle [HEC attestation](/guides/hec-degree-attestation) karwani hogi (fee Rs 3,000 per document, poora process online hai).
5. **Jaali degree ka shak:** Record bilkul match na ho aur certificate bhi mashkook lage, to employer ko chahiye ke woh degree qabool na kare aur HEC ko report kare.

## Employers Aur Universities Ke Liye

Pakistan me hiring ke waqt HEC verification ab aik **standard step** ban chuka hai — khaas tor par sarkari idaron, banks, universities aur multinational companies me. Aam practice ye hai:

- Candidate se **e-Attestation Certificate** ki copy mangi jati hai
- HR QR code scan karke ya portal par number dal kar record check karta hai
- Verification ka screenshot/print file me lagaya jata hai

Isi tarah foreign degree walon se **equivalence letter** manga jata hai aur us ki verification bhi portal se hoti hai — equivalence ka poora process [is guide](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate) me hai. School level (Matric/Inter) documents ki tasdeeq IBCC ki attestation se hoti hai — us ka tareeqa [IBCC guide](/guides/ibcc-attestation-ka-tareeqa) me diya hai.

## Aam Sawalat Ke Short Jawab

- **Verification ki fee?** E-attestation certificate ka QR/portal check aam verification ke liye system ka hissa hai — kisi ko degree check karne ke liye daftar jane ki zaroorat nahi.
- **Certificate kho gaya?** Portal account se dobara download ho jata hai.
- **Transcript verify ho sakta hai?** Jo document attest hua hai (degree ya transcript), usi ka record verify hota hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**HEC degree verification online kaise hoti hai?**
E-Attestation Certificate par mojood QR code scan karein ya eservices.hec.gov.pk ke Degree Verification section me certificate number darj karein — degree ka record foran zahir ho jata hai.

**Kya employer meri degree khud verify kar sakta hai?**
Jee haan. Yahi is system ka maqsad hai — employer, university ya embassy QR code se ya portal par aap ki attestation check kar sakta hai, aap ki ijazat ke baghair daftar jane ki zaroorat nahi.

**Meri degree ka record online nahi mil raha, kya karoon?**
Pehle certificate number aur naam ki spelling check karein. Purani manual attestation ho to HEC support se record ki updation karwayen. Aur agar degree attest hi nahi hui, to pehle online attestation karwani hogi.

**QR code scan karne par kya maloomat nazar ati hain?**
Degree holdar ka naam, degree ka unwan, university ka naam aur attestation ki tafseel — jo asal degree se match karke tasdeeq ki jati hai.

**How can I verify an HEC attested degree online?**
Scan the QR code printed on the HEC e-Attestation Certificate, or enter the certificate number in the Degree Verification section of eservices.hec.gov.pk. If the portal shows the degree holder's name, degree and university matching the document, the attestation is genuine.

**Equivalence letter ki verification bhi yahin se hogi?**
Jee — foreign degree ke equivalence letters ki tasdeeq bhi HEC ke e-services portal se hoti hai. Letter banwane ka process [HEC Equivalence guide](/guides/hec-equivalence-certificate) me hai.
