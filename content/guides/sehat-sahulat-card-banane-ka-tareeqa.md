## Pehli Baat: Sehat Card Kya Hai Aur Ye Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

**Sehat Sahulat Card** (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa me **Sehat Card Plus**) hukumat ka health insurance program hai jis ke tehet mustahiq khandan **empanelled (registered) hospitals me muft ilaj** karwa sakte hain. Ilaj **cashless** hota hai — hospital aap se paise nahi leta, balke bill seedha insurance company (State Life Insurance) ko bhejta hai. Program me bari surgeries (dil, cancer, kidney transplant), maternity, emergency aur indoor ilaj shamil hain.

Sab se ahem baat: **card muft hai, koi premium nahi, aur koi agent ya middleman ki zaroorat nahi.** Jo shakhs card dilane ke badle paise mange, wo fraud hai.

**Wazeh intebah:** Purana official domain **sehatsahulat.com.pk** October 2026 me check karne par **official program ki website nahi dikha raha** (wahan ek ghair-mutaliqa website nazar aayi). Is liye us purane link par bharosa na karein — ehliyaat check aur hospital ki list ke liye neeche diye gaye **SMS codes aur official helplines** use karein.

## Aap Ke Sube Ke Mutabiq Program (KP vs Punjab)

2026 me Sehat Card suba ke hisaab se alag manage hota hai — apne sube ka tareeqa samajh lein:

### Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: Sehat Card Plus

- KP ke **tamam permanent residents jo NADRA me registered hain, auto-covered** hain — alag se apply ya register karne ki zaroorat nahi.
- Agar aap ke CNIC ya B-Form par **permanent KP ka pata** hai to aap enrolled hain (jab tak NADRA family record me koi kharabi na ho).
- Coverage: **Rs 1,000,000 (10 lakh) tak per family per year** — secondary care (aam ilaj, maternity, choti surgeries) per family member had tak, aur tertiary care (dil ki surgery, cancer, transplant, ICU) ke liye bari had + critical illnesses ke liye extra reserve.
- **Helpline: 0800-89898** (muft) — ehliyaat, hospital ki list aur shikayat ke liye.

### Punjab: PHIMC Ke Tehet

- Punjab me program **Punjab Health Initiative Management Company (PHIMC)** chalati hai.
- Aap ka **CNIC hi aap ka Sehat Card** hai — alag physical card ki zaroorat nahi (bachon ke liye B-Form istemal hota hai).
- Coverage (published reports, October 2026 ke mutabiq): **priority healthcare services ke liye Rs 400,000 tak aur secondary services ke liye Rs 60,000 tak per year**, khaas surat me Rs 1 million tak extendable.
- 2026 me program me tabdeeliyan hui hain — ilaj se pehle **empanelled hospitals ki maujooda list** zaroor confirm karein.
- **Helpline: 0800-09009** (muft).

> **Note:** Coverage ki haden waqtan-fo-waqtan revise hoti hain. Oopar di gayi rakamain **October 2026 me published reports se li gayi** hain — ilaj plan karne se pehle apne sube ki helpline (KP: 0800-89898, Punjab: 0800-09009) par maujooda limit zaroor confirm kar lein.

## Ehliyaat Check Karne Ke Tareeqe

### Tareeqa 1: SMS (Sab Se Tez)

1. Mobile ke SMS app me apna **13 hindsyon wala CNIC number baghair dashes ke** likhein.
2. **Punjab / Islamabad / AJK / GB:** **8500** par bhejein.
3. **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa:** **8500 ya 9780** par bhejein.
4. Reply me aap ke khandan ki enrollment status aajayegi.

Ye SMS muft hai aur tamam networks par chalta hai. Do rishtedaron ke jawab mukhtalif aa sakte hain — kyunkay faisla **permanent address (NADRA record)** par hota hai, maujooda rehaish par nahi.

### Tareeqa 2: Helpline Call

- **KP:** 0800-89898
- **Punjab:** 0800-09009

Helpline par CNIC batane se ehliyaat, qareebi empanelled hospital aur coverage ki had confirm ho jati hai.

### Tareeqa 3: Mobile App

**Qaumi Sehat Card / Sehat Sahulat mobile app** (Android aur iOS dono par) se card status check, empanelled hospitals ki GPS location aur ilaj ki history dekhi ja sakti hai.

## Hospital Me Muft Ilaj Ka Step-by-Step Tareeqa

1. **Empanelled hospital chunein:** Helpline ya app se confirm karein ke hospital ab bhi program me shamil hai — list waqtan-fo-waqtan update hoti hai.
2. **Kagzaat saath le jayen:** Apna **asal CNIC**, aur bachon ke ilaj ke liye **B-Form**. (KP me smart/physical card ho to saath le jayen, lekin aksar hospitals me biometric verification kaafi hoti hai.)
3. **Hospital ke Sehat Sahulat counter par jayen:** Reception par batayen ke aap Sehat Card par ilaj karwana chahte hain. Counter aap ki ehliyaat State Life ke system se verify karega (aam tor par 2–5 minute).
4. **Admit hon:** Coverage confirm hote hi ilaj shuru — **koi advance payment nahi**.
5. **Ilaj ke dauran:** Covered ilaj, tests aur dawayan bilkul muft. **Koi bhi cash demand ghair-qanooni hai** — agar hospital paise mange to foran helpline par shikayat karein.
6. **Discharge:** Farigh hote waqt satisfaction form par sign karein — case State Life ke system me close ho jata hai.

**Emergency me:** 24 ghante ke andar admission par card istemal ho sakta hai chahe card saath na ho — hospital CNIC number se verify kar lega, aur card agle din pesh kiya ja sakta hai.

## Kaunsi Bimariyan Covered Hain (Aur Kaunsi Nahi)

**Covered (indoor ilaj):**
- Dil ki surgery (bypass, angioplasty), cancer ka ilaj, kidney transplant, dialysis
- Normal aur C-section delivery (maternity)
- Emergency aur accident ka ilaj
- General aur specialized surgeries, ICU services
- Admission ke dauran tests aur dawayan

**Covered NAHI:**
- **OPD (outpatient) ilaj** — sirf admit hone wale (inpatient) ilaj par coverage hai
- Cosmetic/plastic surgery
- Ilaj ke baad bahir se khareedi gayi dawayan (kuch cases me)
- Mulk se bahir ilaj

## Zaroori Kagzaat: Kya Tayyar Rakhein

- **Asal CNIC** (valid ho — expired CNIC par verification atak sakti hai; [CNIC renewal ka tareeqa](/guides/nadra-cnic-banane-ka-tareeqa) yahan dekhein)
- Bachon ke liye **B-Form** ([B-Form banane ka tareeqa](/guides/b-form-banane-ka-tareeqa))
- **NADRA family record durust** hona chahiye — shadi-shuda afraad apne walidain ke record me nahi, apne khandan ke record me covered hote hain ([FRC guide](/guides/nadra-frc-banane-ka-tareeqa))

## Aam Ghaltiyan Jo Log Karte Hain

1. **Purani hospital list par bharosa:** 2026 me empanelled hospitals ki list badli hai — admit hone se pehle helpline se confirm karein, warna bill apni jeb se dena par sakta hai.
2. **OPD ka kharcha card par dalne ki koshish:** OPD covered nahi — sirf indoor/admission wale ilaj par card chalta hai.
3. **Agent ko paise dena:** Card muft hai; "card banwa deta hoon" kehne wala har shakhs fraud hai.
4. **Expired CNIC lekar jana:** Verification NADRA data se hoti hai — expired CNIC par case atak sakta hai.
5. **NADRA family record update na karna:** Nayi shadi ya bachay ki paidaish ke baad family record update karwayen, warna coverage me masla ho sakta hai.
6. **Hospital ki cash demand par chup rehna:** Covered ilaj par paise mangna SOP ke khilaf hai — helpline par shikayat darj karwayen, aksar masla foran hal ho jata hai.

## Aksar Poche Jane Wale Sawal

**Sehat Card ke liye apply kaise karun? Koi form hai?**
Alag se apply karne ki zaroorat nahi. KP me tamam NADRA-registered permanent residents auto-enrolled hain; Punjab me aap ka valid CNIC hi card hai. Bas apni ehliyaat **8500** (KP: 8500 ya 9780) par CNIC SMS karke confirm karein — agar enrolled hain to seedha empanelled hospital ja sakte hain.

**Sehat Card par saal me kitne ka ilaj muft hai?**
KP (Sehat Card Plus) me **Rs 1,000,000 tak per family per year** (secondary aur tertiary care ki alag hadon ke saath). Punjab (PHIMC) me published reports ke mutabiq **priority services Rs 400,000 aur secondary Rs 60,000 per year**, khaas cases me Rs 1 million tak. Haden revise hoti rehti hain — ilaj se pehle helpline par confirm karein (KP: 0800-89898, Punjab: 0800-09009).

**Kya Sehat Card par OPD ya dawai milti hai?**
Nahi. Sirf **hospital me admit hone wala (inpatient) ilaj** covered hai — OPD checkup aur aam tor par bahir se li gayi dawayan covered nahi hain.

**Hospital ne paise mange to kya karun?**
Covered ilaj par koi cash demand **ghair-qanooni** hai. Paise na dein aur foran apne sube ki helpline par shikayat karein — **KP: 0800-89898, Punjab: 0800-09009**. Aksar shikayat par masla foran hal ho jata hai.

**Kya card ki expiry hoti hai? Renew karna parta hai?**
Card ki koi fixed expiry nahi — jab tak aap eligible hain, coverage **har saal auto-renew** hoti hai. Bas CNIC valid aur NADRA family record updated rakhein.
